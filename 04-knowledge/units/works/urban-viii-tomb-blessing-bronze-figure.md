## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 在乌尔班八世墓葬叙述中称为“the gigantic bronze figure of the blessing Pope”的雕像。印刷页42称该像随后以青铜铸成，并描述教宗高坐于朝圣者之上、右手抬起。该像按原书叙述作为墓葬纪念碑的一部分登记。Haskell没有指明此像的铸造者；本卡不由墓葬总工程的受托关系推定该像由 Bernini 铸造。作者对姿势的象征性阐释仍归于 Haskell。[第2章 pp.41–42](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_ii.md)（L125、128–129；PDF物理页27）

**English:** This card records the statue Haskell calls “the gigantic bronze figure of the blessing Pope” in his account of Urban VIII’s tomb. On p. 42 he says it was subsequently cast in bronze and describes the Pope seated high above a pilgrim, with his right hand raised. The figure is registered as a component of the tomb monument in the book’s account. Haskell does not name its caster; this card does not infer that Bernini cast it from his responsibility for the overall tomb project. The author’s symbolic interpretation of the pose remains attributed to Haskell. [第2章 pp.41–42](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_ii.md)（L125、128–129；PDF物理页27）
