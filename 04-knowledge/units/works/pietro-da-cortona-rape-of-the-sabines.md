## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 皮耶特罗·达·科尔托纳于1630年创作的布面油画《劫夺萨宾妇女》，来自萨凯蒂收藏，现登记为卡比托利欧馆藏PC 137。

**English:** Pietro da Cortona’s Rape of the Sabines is an oil painting on canvas dated 1630, from the Sacchetti collection and catalogued by the Capitoline Museums as PC 137.
