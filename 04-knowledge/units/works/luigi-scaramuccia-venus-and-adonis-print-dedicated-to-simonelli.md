## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第5章第124页注1提到的《Venus and Adonis》版画。哈斯克尔称Luigi Scaramuccia将该版画题献给Niccolò Simonelli，并转述版画题词称Simonelli钟爱Carracci的作品。S3将正文候选`cand-6018`与索引子项`cand-2391`归入同一work身份组。本卡与Sebastiano Ricci的同名绘画保持区分；题献关系不证明Scaramuccia是版画的绘制者或刻印者。原版画及题词原件未查阅。

**English:** This card records the *Venus and Adonis* print noted in Haskell’s Chapter 5, p. 124 n. 1. Haskell says Luigi Scaramuccia dedicated the print to Niccolò Simonelli and reports the inscription’s praise of Simonelli’s love of works by the Carracci. S3 maps body-derived candidate `cand-6018` and index subentry `cand-2391` to one work identity group. This print remains distinct from the painting of the same title by Sebastiano Ricci; the dedication does not establish Scaramuccia as its maker. The print and original inscription were not consulted.
