## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔注2所称Carlo Maratta绘制的《The Death of St Francis Xavier》。与Gaulli另一幅同题祭坛画分别识别；作品实物、收藏地与年代未由本来源确认。题名OCR异文“0/St”经印刷页图核对。

**English:** This card records the painting titled *The Death of St Francis Xavier* that Haskell note 2 attributes to Carlo Maratta. It remains distinct from a similarly titled altarpiece by Gaulli; the object, location, and date are not established by this source. The OCR variant “0/St” was checked against the printed page image.
