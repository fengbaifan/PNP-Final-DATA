### 描述

**中文：** 比萨坎波桑托内纪念弗朗切斯科·阿尔加罗蒂的墓，由泰西设计，比安科尼负责建造。

**English:** A tomb commemorating Francesco Algarotti in the Camposanto, Pisa, designed by Tesi and constructed under Bianconi’s responsibility.
