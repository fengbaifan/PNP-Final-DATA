### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔称Georg Friedrich Schmidt为Francesco Algarotti蚀刻的Felice Salimbeni肖像。来源称Salimbeni为歌手；1753年是依“1752年之后一年”的措辞推算，并非本段另给的明确年月。本卡不据此推定正式委托合同、现存状态或外部归属已核。

**English:** This unit records the portrait etching that Haskell says Georg Friedrich Schmidt made for Francesco Algarotti, depicting Felice Salimbeni. Haskell calls Salimbeni a singer. The date 1753 is derived from the wording “a year after” the 1752 print; it is not separately stated as an explicit year in this clause. This card does not infer a formal commission contract or claim that survival or external attribution has been verified.
