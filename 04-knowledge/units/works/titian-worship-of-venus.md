## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 记载提香《维纳斯崇拜》曾是费拉拉埃斯特家族艺术收藏的一部分，并于1598年前后由 Aldobrandini 枢机带回罗马；1637年又被送往西班牙。第2章叙述其在罗马的接受，第4章记录版画传播及 Padovanino 前往临摹。本卡只登记提香原作，相关临摹、后出版本和其他提香画作另行区分。

**English:** Haskell reports that Titian’s Worship of Venus belonged to the Este art collection at Ferrara, was brought to Rome by Cardinal Aldobrandini around 1598, and was sent to Spain in 1637. Chapter 2 discusses its reception in Rome; Chapter 4 records the print after it and Padovanino’s journey to copy it. This unit records Titian’s painting alone, distinct from copies, later versions, and other Titian works.

### 作品信息

- 对象类型：提香《维纳斯崇拜》绘画原作；此处采用第4章正确拼写的作品名。第2章相应 OCR 将 Venus 误读为“os”，不沿用该误字。
- 收藏与转移：Haskell 将其列为16世纪末从埃斯特家族艺术收藏中被带离的作品之一，称 Aldobrandini 枢机将它与另外两幅提香画一并带回罗马。
- 罗马接受：Haskell 称这些画在罗马一度少有人注意，直到 Maffeo Barberini 成为教宗近四分之一世纪后，才帮助奠定一种全新艺术风格的基础；这是作者对接受史的评价。
- 后续流转与传播：第4章称该画于1637年送往西班牙；Andrea Podesta 为 Cassiano dal Pozzo 制作并题献一幅取自该画的版画，Alessandro Varotari（Padovanino）前往罗马临摹。
- 复制史的限定：第2章注释提到 d’Arpino 曾复制相关画作、Albani 可能研究过它们；John Walker 认为 Rubens 不太可能在罗马访问期间复制《维纳斯崇拜》，其斯德哥尔摩版本年代更晚，可能依据另一位艺术家的复制品。保留注释范围与作者转述，不据此补定作品版本链。

- Object type: Titian’s original painting, titled Worship of Venus here following the correctly spelled title in Chapter 4. Chapter 2’s OCR renders Venus as “os”; the OCR error is not adopted.
- Collection and transfer: Haskell includes it among the Este art treasures removed from Ferrara and says Cardinal Aldobrandini brought it to Rome with two other Titian paintings.
- Reception in Rome: Haskell says the paintings attracted little attention there until nearly a quarter century later, when Maffeo Barberini became Pope; he judges that they then helped lay the foundations for a wholly new artistic style.
- Later movement and circulation: Chapter 4 says the painting was sent to Spain in 1637. Andrea Podesta dedicated to Cassiano dal Pozzo a print taken from it, and Alessandro Varotari (Padovanino) travelled to Rome to copy it.
- Copying qualification: Chapter 2’s note says d’Arpino copied the paintings and Albani must have studied them; John Walker considered it unlikely that Rubens copied the Worship of Venus during his visit to Rome. His Stockholm versions were much later and probably derived from another artist’s copies. The note’s qualifications are retained, and no complete version history is inferred.

### 身份边界

中文：本卡只指提香原作。与同批带回罗马的《安德里安人》和《巴克斯与阿里阿德涅》分开；d’Arpino 的临摹、Albani 的研究、Rubens 后出的斯德哥尔摩版本及 Podesta 版画均为各自对象，不合并为原作。Haskell 注释中的复数代词不据此断言每条复制史都只对应本画。接受史与版画关系候选留待 S6逐条核验。

English: This unit is Titian’s original painting alone. It remains distinct from The Andrians and Bacchus and Ariadne, which were brought to Rome with it; from d’Arpino’s copies and Albani’s study; from Rubens’s later Stockholm versions; and from Podesta’s print. The plural references in Haskell’s note are not used to claim that every copying statement applies exclusively to this painting. Reception and print-related relation candidates remain for S6 review.
