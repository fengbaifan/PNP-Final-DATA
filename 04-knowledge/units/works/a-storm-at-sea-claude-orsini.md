## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所称 Paolo Giordano II 委托 Claude 绘制的《A Storm at Sea》。Haskell 只推测作品“或许”旨在说明 Paolo 的格言；注7转述 Röthlisberger 称画作已失佚、见于 *Liber Veritatis* 第33号，年代可能为1638–39。后述状态、图册记录与年代均是 Haskell 对被引研究的转述，本卡未独立查阅该研究或图册。[第4章 p.97](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_i.md)（L29–30、注7 L58）

**English:** This unit records the painting Haskell calls *A Storm at Sea*, commissioned by Paolo Giordano II from Claude. Haskell only suggests that it may have been designed to illustrate Paolo's motto. Note 7 relays Röthlisberger's report that the picture was lost, is recorded in *Liber Veritatis*, no. 33, and probably dates from 1638–39. These later status, catalogue, and dating claims are reported by Haskell from a cited study; that study and the album have not been independently consulted here. [Chapter 4, p. 97](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_i.md) (L29–30, n. 7 at L58)
