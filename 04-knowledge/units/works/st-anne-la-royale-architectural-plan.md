## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell所述St Anne-la-Royale建筑方案，与教堂建筑本身及其弯曲立面分开。Haskell称方案没有迁就当时法国实践，并把它描述为Grand Siècle时期巴黎孤立的意大利巴洛克建筑实例；这是作者的历史评价，不外推为普遍共识。

**English:** This card records the architectural plan for St Anne-la-Royale as described by Haskell, separate from the church building and its curving façade. Haskell says the plan made no concession to current French practice and describes it as a wholly isolated example of Italian Baroque architecture in Paris during the Grand Siècle. This is the author’s historical assessment, not a general consensus claim.
