## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所述、为圣母无原罪始胎堂提出的金属多枝烛台方案。原文将其与 Barberini 家族圣安德烈亚·德拉瓦莱堂的陈设作比较，并称该方案后来可以省略；不据此认定其制作或安装。

**English:** This card records the proposed metal candelabra design Haskell describes for S. Maria della Concezione. The passage compares it with furnishings in the Barberini family chapel at S. Andrea della Valle and later says the candelabra could be omitted. It does not establish fabrication or installation.

### 本章相关内容

方案与 Urban VIII 的家族礼拜堂作比较；Haskell 随后称，作为让步可以省略这组金属烛台。该陈述保留“可省略”的来源限定，不表示其曾被制作或安置。

The scheme is compared with Urban VIII’s family chapel; Haskell later says the metal candelabra could be omitted as a concession. This card preserves the source’s qualification and does not imply that the group was made or installed.
