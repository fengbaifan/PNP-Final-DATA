## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell第3章p.93所述S. Ignazio左耳堂内的1697年祭坛。原文称Andrea Pozzo设计该祭坛，Pierre Legros以一件描绘Blessed Luigi Gonzaga升天主题的浅浮雕完成该祭坛。此处仅记录书内叙述，不据此推断祭坛现存状态或独立核实其作品身份。 [第3章 p.93](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md)。

**English:** This unit records the 1697 altar in the left transept of S. Ignazio described by Haskell in Chapter 3, p. 93. The passage says Andrea Pozzo designed it and Pierre Legros completed it with a bas-relief representing the Assumption of Blessed Luigi Gonzaga. This records the book's account and does not establish the altar's present condition or an independently verified work identity. [Chapter 3, p. 93](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md).
