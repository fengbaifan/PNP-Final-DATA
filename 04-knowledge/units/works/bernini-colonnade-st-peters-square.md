## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 称 Bernini 在圣彼得大教堂外建造了大型柱廊，并描述其向外环抱广场。第9章还称Passeriano的Manin乡间宅邸入口有两座仿自Bernini为St Peter’s所作porticos的柱廊翼；候选cand-8218与本卡为同一柱廊作品，但具体仿制对象或现存构件未能确认，关系仍待证。原书另转述威尼斯使节对柱廊费用的观察、Niccolò Sagredo 于1661年关于工程约完成一半且预计三年后完成的估计，以及 Giacomo Querini 对柱廊的政治性批评。本卡不推定准确施工年月或实际竣工状态。

**English:** Haskell says Bernini built a great colonnade outside St Peter’s that stretches out to embrace the square. Chapter 9 also says that two wings at the Manin country house in Passeriano were adapted from Bernini’s porticos for St Peter’s. Candidate cand-8218 refers to this same colonnade; the exact feature or surviving design is not identified, so the relation remains pending. Haskell also reports Venetian ambassadors’ observations on its expense, Niccolò Sagredo’s 1661 estimate that the work was about half finished with three years to go, and Giacomo Querini’s political criticism. This unit does not infer exact construction dates or completion status.

### 作品信息

- 对象类型：围绕圣彼得广场展开的柱廊建筑作品；本卡不把广场或圣彼得大教堂合并为作品组成部分。
- 作者归属与位置：Haskell 将柱廊归于 Bernini，称其建于教堂外并延伸环抱广场。
- 工程进度报告：Niccolò Sagredo 转述的1661年估计称工程约完成一半，预计三年后完成；这是当时的估计，不是竣工记录。
- 费用与政治评价：Haskell 称威尼斯使节注意到 Bernini 柱廊的巨大开支；Giacomo Querini 后来批评圣彼得柱廊和其建筑政策，称三百根柱子按椭圆形布局形成教堂的建筑界域，并预测对 Leonine City、Vatican 及 conclave 会造成影响。后几项均记录为 Querini 的预测和论辩，不当作实际后果。

- Object type: an architectural colonnade extending around St Peter’s Square; the square and St Peter’s Basilica are not merged into the work.
- Attribution and location: Haskell attributes the colonnade to Bernini and says it was built outside the church, stretching out to embrace the square.
- Reported progress: an estimate relayed by Niccolò Sagredo in 1661 put the work at about half complete, with three years remaining. This is a contemporary estimate, not a completion record.
- Expense and political criticism: Haskell says Venetian ambassadors noticed the ruinous expense of Bernini’s portico. Giacomo Querini later criticized the colonnades and papal building policy, describing three hundred columns in an oval arrangement as an architectural precinct for the church and predicting effects on the Leonine City, the Vatican, and conclaves. These are recorded as Querini’s arguments and predictions, not established outcomes.
