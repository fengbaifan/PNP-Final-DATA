## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell所述的Giovanni del Pian九幅大版画系列，题材为Carpaccio《圣乌尔苏拉生平》场景。Haskell称Padre Giuseppe Toninotto于1785年负责出版该系列。原书此句未逐一列出版画题名；1785年是该出版责任的来源记载，不据此推定每幅版画的创作时间、印刷者或出版机构。

**English:** This card records Haskell’s series of nine large prints by Giovanni del Pian, with scenes from Carpaccio’s Life of St Ursula as their subject. Haskell says Padre Giuseppe Toninotto was responsible for publishing the series in 1785. The passage does not title the individual prints; 1785 dates the reported publication responsibility, not necessarily the creation of each print, and the passage does not identify a printer or publishing house.
