## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第10章称 Luca Carlevarijs 为 Frederick IV 绘制向其致敬的赛舟场景；注5以《Regatta in honour of Frederick IV of Denmark (1709)》指称该画。作品卡仅表达书内可识别的作品身份。

**English:** Chapter 10 says Luca Carlevarijs painted a regatta for Frederick IV, offered in his honour; note 5 names the painting Regatta in honour of Frederick IV of Denmark (1709). This card records only the work identity established in the book.

The bilingual display names appear in the card title. The Chinese name is a transliteration or descriptive rendering for readability; the book's wording is preserved in the source quotations.

### 本书语境

正文将作画置于 Frederick IV 1709年访威尼斯的庆典语境中。注2报告作品当时位于“the Fredericksborg, Copenhagen”，并引 Mauroner 1945；本批未独立查阅所引文献。

The narrative places the painting in the context of Frederick IV’s 1709 visit to Venice. Note 2 reports the picture at “the Fredericksborg, Copenhagen,” citing Mauroner 1945; that reference was not independently consulted in this batch.

English: The p. 277 narrative and note 5 support the work title, painter, and year 1709; creation, honour, and location relations remain for item-level S6 review.
