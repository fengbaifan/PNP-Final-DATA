## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Tiepolo 为维琴察的 Valmarana 家族别墅绘制了一组表现 Homer、Virgil、Tasso 与 Ariosto 浪漫场景的壁画。第20章后记转述 Puppi 的研究，明确更正委托者为 Conte Giustino Valmarana，而非 Leonardo。

**English:** Haskell says Tiepolo painted a cycle of frescoes for the Valmarana family villa in Vicenza, illustrating romantic scenes from Homer, Virgil, Tasso, and Ariosto. The Chapter 20 postscript reports Puppi's research and corrects the commissioner to Conte Giustino Valmarana rather than Leonardo.

Chapter 9 (p. 258) gives the work type, subject range, and family context; Chapter 20 (p. 405) refers back to the same cycle and reports Puppi's correction of the commissioner.

The postscript's reported research identifies Giustino as commissioner; the Chapter 9 family-head report about Leonardo is not treated as a commission. The book gives no individual titles, technique, or survival status.
