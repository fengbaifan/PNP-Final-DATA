## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 1659年描绘马扎然坐于画廊中的雕刻版画，由南特伊及协作者制作，人物面貌取自米尼亚尔的肖像。

**English:** A 1659 engraving depicting Mazarin seated in his gallery, made by Nanteuil and collaborators with the sitter’s likeness based on a portrait by Mignard.
