## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔称位于友谊祭坛下方、献给吉罗拉莫·阿斯卡尼奥·朱斯蒂尼亚尼的一则铭文。原书没有转录铭文内容；本卡不据此补写文本或断言现存状态。

**English:** This unit records an inscription that Haskell says was below the Altar of Friendship and dedicated to Girolamo Ascanio Giustiniani. The book does not transcribe its wording; this card does not supply a text or claim present survival.
