## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell所述Pietro da Cortona为耶稣新堂穹顶开始绘制的图稿。“Cartoons”在此按绘图／图稿理解；卡片名称为描述性工作题名，不是原作正式题名。Haskell将开始时间定为1646年11月，并在后文另述穹顶穹隅壁画的进度；本卡不把图稿与后来的壁画合并，不声称图稿数量、完成状态、存世情况或现藏地。

**English:** This unit records the cartoons Pietro da Cortona began painting for the Chiesa Nuova cupola, as reported by Haskell. “Cartoons” is understood here as preparatory drawings; the card title is descriptive, not an original title. Haskell dates the beginning to November 1646 and later separately discusses the progress of the cupola pendentive frescoes. This card does not merge the cartoons with those later frescoes or assert a number of cartoons, completion, survival, or present location.
