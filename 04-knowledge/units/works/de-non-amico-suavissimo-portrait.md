## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称多米尼克·德农绘制了一幅肖像，并在其上题签“amico suavissimo”。原文“his”所指坐者不确定；此卡不将肖像认定为奎里尼肖像，也不补写作品原题、日期或现藏地点。

**English:** Haskell says Dominique De Non drew a portrait and signed it “amico suavissimo.” The sitter referred to by “his” in the passage is unresolved; this unit does not identify the portrait as one of Querini or supply an original title, date, or present location.
