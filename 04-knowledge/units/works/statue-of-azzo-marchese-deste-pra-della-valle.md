## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本知识元记录 Haskell 所述由格洛斯特公爵竖立、表现 Azzo, Marchese d’Este 的一尊雕像。注1称该像至1777年10月已竖立，并将其描述为位于 Prato della Valle；该注转引的书信未独立查阅，不能据此推定具体安装日或现今存续状态。

**English:** This unit records the statue that Haskell says the Duke of Gloucester erected to represent Azzo, Marchese d’Este. Note 1 says it had been erected by October 1777 and describes it as being in the Prato della Valle. The cited letters were not independently consulted; no exact installation day or present survival is inferred.
