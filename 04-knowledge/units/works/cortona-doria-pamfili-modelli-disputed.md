## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Grassi 于 1957 年发表为科尔托纳画廊天顶所作 modelli；Haskell 说归属未普遍获认。本卡记录争议对象而非确认作者，不能写 created_by Cortona 正式边。

**English:** Grassi published these attributed sketches in 1957, but Haskell notes that the attribution had not won general acceptance. They remain a disputed group and do not support an unqualified creation relationship with Cortona.
