## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Bologna 同名善会要求 Ricci 表现斩首场面并加入所需人物；本章支持委托题材，不确认今日流传的具体同题作品版本。

**English:** A Bologna confraternity's 1682 instructions required Ricci to depict the beheading of John the Baptist with the figures needed to express it. The contract reference does not independently identify a specific present-day version.
