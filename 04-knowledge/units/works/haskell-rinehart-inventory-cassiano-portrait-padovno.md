## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第4章 p.100 注2所述的一条目录项目：一幅卡西亚诺肖像，归属栏写作“Padov.no”。这是哈斯克尔对 Haskell 与 Rinehart 目录的转述；原文章未独立查阅。该肖像与图版17a的贝尔尼尼讽刺像分开，是否就是 Dati 颂诗集的扉页像仍未确定。

**English:** This card records an inventory item reported in Haskell’s chapter 4, p.100, note 2: a portrait of Cassiano with the attribution “Padov.no.” This is Haskell’s report of the Haskell and Rinehart inventory; the article has not been independently consulted. The portrait remains distinct from Bernini’s Plate 17a caricature, and its possible identification with the frontispiece to the Dati panegyric is unresolved.
