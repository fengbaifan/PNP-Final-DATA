**English:** This unit records the group of paintings Haskell describes on the pendentives of the Chiesa Nuova cupola: four prophets who had foretold the coming of the Virgin and the Redeemer—Isaiah, Jeremiah, Ezekiel, and Daniel. The source’s shared number, location, and subject define one group here; the four figures are not split into separate work units, and this descriptive name is not treated as an original title.
