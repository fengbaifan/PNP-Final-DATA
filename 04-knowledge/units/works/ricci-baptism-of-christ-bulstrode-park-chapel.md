**English:** Haskell lists The Baptism of Christ as one of four paintings for Portland’s chapel; the title continues across a printed line break. This card identifies the final painting in the group, distinct from the sketch reported as sold at Sotheby’s in note 3. That sale does not establish the final painting’s present location.
