## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本书称 Louis XIV 在 Bernini 的路易十四胸像完成后订制其骑马像。作品于 Bernini 返回罗马四年后开工，1684年抵达巴黎，后置于凡尔赛园林偏远角落；Louis XIV 曾要求砸毁，随后改变主意，Girardon 作必要改造，形象被称为 Marcus Curtius。本卡记录同一雕像的委托、迁置与改造叙述。

**English:** Haskell says Louis XIV ordered an equestrian statue after Bernini completed the king’s bust. Work began four years after Bernini’s return to Rome; the statue reached Paris in 1684 and was later placed in a remote corner of the Versailles grounds. Louis first wanted it broken up, then relented; Girardon made alterations and the figure was called Marcus Curtius. This unit records the book’s account of one statue’s commission, relocation, and alteration.

### 作品信息

- 作品类型：骑马像。\n- 创作者：Gian Lorenzo Bernini；委托者：Louis XIV。\n- 开工：Bernini 返回罗马四年后；本段没有直接给出开工的公历年份。\n- 抵达巴黎：1684年，晚于 Bernini 去世。\n- 后续安置：Louis XIV 改变砸毁雕像的决定后，将其置于凡尔赛园林最偏远处。\n- 改造与称名：Girardon 作必要改造，之后形象被称为 Marcus Curtius。\n- 后续报价：Haskell 转述有人传言 Del Carpio 曾愿以30,000 scudi 求购，且该报价未获接受；不记录为实际所有权转移。

- Work type: equestrian statue.\n- Creator: Gian Lorenzo Bernini; commissioner: Louis XIV.\n- Begun: four years after Bernini’s return to Rome; the passage does not directly supply a calendar year for the start of work.\n- Arrival in Paris: 1684, after Bernini’s death.\n- Later placement: after Louis XIV relented from his wish to break it up, the statue was banished to a remote corner of the Versailles grounds.\n- Alteration and later name: Girardon made necessary alterations, after which the figure was called Marcus Curtius.\n- Later offer: Haskell reports a rumor that Del Carpio offered 30,000 scudi for the statue and says the offer was not accepted; this is not recorded as a transfer of ownership.

### 身份边界

中文：此候选与既有《路易十四肖像》（Bernini, Louis XIV）是不同作品：后者为胸像，本卡为骑马像。书中把后来的改造与 Marcus Curtius 称名接在同一雕像叙述中，故暂按同一作品的变化史登记；不据此扩写改造前后完整实物状态或外部现存位置。

English: This candidate is distinct from the existing Portrait of Louis XIV by Bernini, which is a bust; this unit is the equestrian statue. Haskell narrates the later alteration and the name Marcus Curtius as the history of this statue, so they are recorded here as changes to one work. The card does not add an external account of the object’s present state or location.
