## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡识别 Haskell 第7章所述贝尼尼为查理一世雕制的胸像。原文称该像于1637年7月抵达英格兰，并以范·戴克的三重肖像为图像依据。此处题名为描述性暂题，原文未给出正式题名。

**English:** This card identifies the bust of Charles I that Haskell attributes to Bernini. The text says it reached England in July 1637 and was based on Van Dyck’s triple portrait. The title here is descriptive; Haskell gives no formal title for the bust.
