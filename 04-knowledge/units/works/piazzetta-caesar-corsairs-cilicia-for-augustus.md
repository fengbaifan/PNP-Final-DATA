## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡表示Haskell第14章p.355所述、由Algarotti委托为Augustus绘制的Caesar与基利西亚海盗题材画。Haskell将这幅委托画列入Piazzetta仅有的三件大型古典历史画之一。此中文名称为据书内题材拟定的描述性译名，不是已核实的原题或馆藏题名；原书未给确切绘制日期或现藏地点。

**English:** This unit represents the painting on the subject of Caesar and the Cilician corsairs that Haskell says Algarotti commissioned for Augustus. Haskell includes it among the only three large-scale classical-history pictures in Piazzetta’s oeuvre. The English label follows Haskell’s wording and is descriptive, not a verified original or collection title; the page gives no exact date or present location.
