## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell注2称“这幅壁画的草图”当时藏于Orleans Museum。该注释紧随图版39，正文将图版39对应于皮蒂宫《维纳斯与阿多尼斯》场景；因此本卡以此作为描述性工作名并与该场景关联，但不声称草图的具体媒材、版本、完成状态或现今位置。

**English:** Haskell’s note 2 says that “the sketch for this fresco” was then in the Orleans Museum. The note follows Plate 39, which the text identifies with the Pitti Venus and Adonis scene. This descriptive unit is linked to that scene; the medium, version, completion status, and present location of the sketch are not asserted.
