## 内容

### 作品描述

**中文：**本卡记录哈斯克尔第6章第166页所称的《忏悔》之后“余下六件圣事”作品组。克雷斯皮从博洛尼亚寄给奥托博尼的《忏悔》与这六件作品分开登记；六件单幅作品的题名在本页未载。本卡只代表该六件子组，不与《忏悔》或完整七件圣事系列合并。馆藏机构所载1712年创作时间适用于完整系列，不能据此为每件作品分别定年。

**English:** This unit records the “remaining six Sacraments” described on p. 166 of Haskell. The Confession, which Crespi sent from Bologna to Ottoboni, is registered separately; this page supplies no individual titles for the six works. This unit represents only that six-work subset and is not merged with The Confession or the complete Seven Sacraments series. The museum’s 1712 date applies to the complete series and does not date each work individually.
