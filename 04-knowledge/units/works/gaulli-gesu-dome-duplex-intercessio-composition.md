## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell所述Gaulli最终为耶稣圣名堂（Gesù）穹顶采用的Duplex Intercessio图像构图方案。Haskell称该方案与较早的Blood of Christ主题不同；注4又把两种方案的图像联系解释为Adam与Eve常见于Blood of Christ图像，并说明Gaulli改编了Bernini此前处理的Duplex Intercessio。本卡保存这些来源限定的对象边界和作者解释，不与Bernini的先行构图、整个穹顶及拱顶装饰委托或未指明的具体预备图稿合并。

**English:** This card records the Duplex Intercessio composition scheme that Haskell identifies as Gaulli’s eventual subject for the Gesù dome. Haskell distinguishes it from the earlier Blood of Christ theme; footnote 4 explains an iconographic link between the programmes through the appearance of Adam and Eve in versions of the Blood of Christ, and says Gaulli adapted Bernini’s earlier Duplex Intercessio. This card preserves those source-bounded object boundaries and the author’s interpretation. It does not merge the scheme with Bernini’s earlier composition, the whole dome and vault decoration commission, or an unspecified preparatory drawing.
