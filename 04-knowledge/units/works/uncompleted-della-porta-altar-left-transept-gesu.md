## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所称、由 Giacomo della Porta 设计但未完成的耶稣堂圣依纳爵小堂祭坛。小堂原奉献于 Crucifixion 是空间背景，不将该奉献对象误作此祭坛的独立主题或作品题名。后由 Cortona 所建的新祭坛替代；两件作品保持分立。[第3章 p.91](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md)

**English:** This unit records the altar that Haskell says Giacomo della Porta designed but never completed for the Gesù's Chapel of St Ignatius. The chapel's original dedication to the Crucifixion is contextual information, not a separate subject or title for this altar. Cortona's later new altar replaced it; the two works remain distinct. [Chapter 3, p. 91](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md)

### 本章相关内容

Haskell说该小堂原奉献于 Crucifixion，继而称祭坛由 Della Porta 设计但未完成。前句的奉献对象是小堂；本卡只把设计与未完成状态归于祭坛。

**English:** Haskell first says the chapel was originally dedicated to the Crucifixion, then says its altar was designed but never completed by Della Porta. The dedication belongs to the chapel; the design and incomplete status belong to the altar.
