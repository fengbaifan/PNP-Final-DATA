### 描述

**中文：** 博塔拉为萨凯蒂家族画廊创作的布面油画，收藏于卡皮托利欧美术馆；本书列为图版11a。

**English:** An oil painting on canvas made by Bottalla for the Sacchetti picture collection, held in the Capitoline Picture Gallery and listed as plate 11a in the book.
