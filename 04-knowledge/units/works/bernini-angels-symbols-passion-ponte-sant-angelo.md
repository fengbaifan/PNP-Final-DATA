## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 记录哈斯克尔所述由克雷芒九世委托贝尔尼尼制作、原拟安置于 Ponte S. Angelo 的受难象征天使群。原书没有在此确认安装、完成状态、现存版本或单件数量。中文题名为描述性译名。

**English:** This unit records the group of angels carrying the Symbols of the Passion that Haskell says Clement IX commissioned from Bernini for intended placement on Ponte S. Angelo. The passage does not confirm installation, completion, surviving versions, or the number of individual figures. The Chinese title is descriptive.
