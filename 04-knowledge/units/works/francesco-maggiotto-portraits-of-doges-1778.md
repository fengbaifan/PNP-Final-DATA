## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 弗朗切斯科·马焦托于1778年为藏书家与艺术品收藏者Maffeo Pinelli绘制的168幅油画铜板肖像组，题材包括威尼斯总督、总督夫人、威尼斯宗主教、枢机与教宗。该组于1787年售予Pisani家族圣斯德望支系。来源另记1791年补入总督Ludovico Manin及其妻的两幅肖像；目前不判断两幅补作的作者、实物身份及其与原168幅作品的具体目录关系。

**English:** A group of 168 oil-on-copper portraits of Venetian doges, dogaresse, patriarchs, cardinals, and popes, painted by Francesco Maggiotto for the bibliophile and collector Maffeo Pinelli in 1778. The group was sold in 1787 to the Pisani di Santo Stefano branch. The source also records portraits of Doge Ludovico Manin and his wife added in 1791; their authorship, object identities, and catalogue relationship to the original 168 are not established here.
