## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell在同一句中记载枢机S. Onofrio订置十二支烛台和两件木制十字架，并将它们送往该教堂。Capuchin修士将物件退回。后文只明确烛台被接受，没有说明十字架后续去向；不推定其制造者、安装或现存地点。

**English:** In the same sentence, Haskell reports that Cardinal S. Onofrio ordered a dozen candlesticks and two wooden crucifixes and sent them to the church. The Capuchin monks returned the objects. The later acceptance is stated only for the candlesticks; the passage does not give the crucifixes' later location. No maker, installation, or present location is inferred.
