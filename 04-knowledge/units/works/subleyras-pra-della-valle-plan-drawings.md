## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本知识元表示 Haskell 所述、由 Giuseppe Subleyras 为 Andrea Memmo 的 Prà della Valle 方案绘制的图稿组。该段使用复数 plans，但未给出数量、题名或单张图稿身份；后文所说供 Piranesi 刻制的单数 drawing 尚未能对应到该组中的具体一张。

**English:** This unit represents the group of plans that Haskell says Giuseppe Subleyras drew for Andrea Memmo’s Prà della Valle scheme. The passage uses the plural “plans” but gives no count, title, or individual sheet identity. The singular drawing later mentioned as engraved by Piranesi has not been identified as a particular member of this group.
