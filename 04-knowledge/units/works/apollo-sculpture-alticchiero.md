## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔在阿尔蒂基耶罗花园祭坛语境中提及的阿波罗雕塑。原文称其以“高贵的静穆、青春的完整之美”呈现；雕塑家仅被称为当时仍在世的现代雕塑家，本段没有姓名。卡片不将作品归给 Canova 或其他雕塑家，也不补写现存地点。

**English:** This unit records the Apollo sculpture Haskell mentions in the context of the Alticchiero garden altars. He describes it as rendered “in noble repose in the full beauty of his youth” and identifies its sculptor only as a modern artist still living at the time of the account. This unit assigns no name to the sculptor and does not attribute the work to Canova or another artist, nor does it supply a present location.
