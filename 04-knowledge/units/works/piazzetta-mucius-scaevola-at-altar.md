## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell在注2中将《祭坛前的穆基乌斯·斯凯沃拉》列为Piazzetta两幅后续古典历史画之一；正文将这两幅画纳入Piazzetta的作品范围。中文名称为忠实暂译。注2转述Pallucchini称该画在Algarotti委托之后不久绘成；原注并列Palazzo Barbaro Curtis与Cà Rezzonico两个威尼斯地点，却未明确逐一配对，本卡不指定其位置。Pallucchini原文未独立查阅。

**English:** Haskell’s note 2 names *Mucius Scaevola at the Altar* as one of two further classical-history paintings; the main text places those paintings in Piazzetta’s oeuvre. The Chinese title is a descriptive translation. Note 2 reports Pallucchini’s claim that the picture was painted shortly after the Algarotti commission. It lists Palazzo Barbaro Curtis and Cà Rezzonico in Venice without an explicit one-to-one assignment, so no individual location is assigned here. Pallucchini’s cited work was not independently consulted.
