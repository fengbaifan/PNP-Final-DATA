## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 贝加莫圣母大殿的南入口。Haskell称其较1355年建成的哥特门廊更为简朴，并说 Giovanni da Campione 于数年后建造该入口；原书没有给出南入口的确切年份。本知识元将入口构件与教堂整体分别登记。

**English:** The south entrance of Santa Maria Maggiore in Bergamo. Haskell describes it as more restrained than the Gothic portico erected in 1355 and says Giovanni da Campione built it a few years later; the book gives no exact year for the entrance. This unit records the architectural component separately from the church as a whole.
