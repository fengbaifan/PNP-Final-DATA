## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 引举枢机为 Bernini 作 David 自我形象时举镜的故事，并提醒此类逸事常重写 Charles V/Titian 母题；本卡不把举镜当成确定发生。

**English:** Haskell recounts a story of a Cardinal Barberini holding a mirror while Bernini represented himself in David. He compares such stories with the Charles V–Titian motif; the anecdote is not treated here as a verified event.
