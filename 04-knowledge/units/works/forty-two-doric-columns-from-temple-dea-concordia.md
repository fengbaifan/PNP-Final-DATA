## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡表示哈斯克尔所述的一组四十二根多立克柱，不拆分为单柱实体。原书称其来自罗马的德阿·协和神庙，并称教皇将柱群移交给法尔塞蒂；其在别墅中的位置记为哈斯克尔所述的下层拱廊安置，不推定目前存续或位置。 [第15章来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_i.md)

**English:** This card represents Haskell's source-defined group of forty-two Doric columns, not forty-two separately identified units. He says they came from the Temple of the Dea Concordia in Rome and were ceded to Farsetti by the Pope. Their villa placement is recorded as Haskell's account of installation in the lower-storey arcades; present survival or location is not inferred. [Chapter 15 source](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_i.md)
