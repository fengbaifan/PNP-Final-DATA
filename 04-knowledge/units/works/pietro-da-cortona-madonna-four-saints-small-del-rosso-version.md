## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell所称科尔托纳《Madonna and Four Saints》的德尔·罗索家族较小版本。Haskell说该画是圣奥古斯丁堂较早画作的较小版本，并由Luca Giordano在Naples为德尔·罗索家族取得。来源没有给出取得日期或该较小版本的现今位置；本卡只登记书内对象区分与叙述。

**English:** This card records the smaller Del Rosso version of Pietro da Cortona’s *Madonna and Four Saints* as described by Haskell. He identifies it as a smaller version of an earlier picture at S. Agostino, Cortona, and says Luca Giordano obtained it for the Del Rosso family in Naples. The source gives neither an acquisition date nor a present location for the smaller version; this card records the book’s object distinction and report only.
