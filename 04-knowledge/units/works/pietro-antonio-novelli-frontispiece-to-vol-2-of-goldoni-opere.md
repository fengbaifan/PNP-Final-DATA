## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 帕斯夸利版戈尔多尼《喜剧集》第二卷的卷首图，设计者为皮埃特罗·安东尼奥·诺韦利；《赞助人与画家》将其列为图版57b，载体标年1761。

**English:** The frontispiece to the second volume of the Pasquali edition of Goldoni’s Comedies, designed by Pietro Antonio Novelli; Patrons and Painters lists it as plate 57b and dates the containing volume to 1761.
