## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell描述的贝加莫圣母大殿哥特式门廊。原书称其面向Palazzo della Ragione、由两座石狮支撑，并于1355年由Giovanni da Campione建成。本知识元只指门廊，与教堂整体、两座石狮及数年后建造的南入口分别区分。 [第8章 p.215；OCR L13](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md)

**English:** Haskell’s Gothic portico of Santa Maria Maggiore in Bergamo. The book says that it faces the Palazzo della Ragione, is supported on two stone lions, and was erected by Giovanni da Campione in 1355. This unit refers only to the portico, distinct from the church as a whole, the two lions, and the south entrance built a few years later. [Chapter 8, p.215, OCR L13](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md)
