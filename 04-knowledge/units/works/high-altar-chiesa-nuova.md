## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 在 Chiesa Nuova 叙述中称为“High Altar”的对象，按既有 S3 身份决定登记为 work。第69页称主祭坛分配予圣母诞生；第70页称其原奉献于圣母诞生，装饰曾由原文所称“Cardinal Angelo Cesi”承诺。约1605年预留给“其装饰”的款项据称被立面的意外费用吸收。“其”的先行词依 S2 暂指主祭坛；本卡不把它等同于后文的祭坛画，也不将枢机头衔冲突改写为已核实身份。[第3章 pp.69–70](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)

**English:** This unit records the object Haskell calls the “High Altar” in his account of Chiesa Nuova, following the existing S3 identity decision to register it as a work. Haskell says on p. 69 that the High Altar was assigned to the Nativity of the Virgin; on p. 70 he says it was originally dedicated to the Nativity and that decoration had been promised by the person he calls “Cardinal Angelo Cesi.” Funds set aside for “its decoration” were reportedly absorbed by unexpected façade expenses around 1605. S2 provisionally resolves “its” to the High Altar. This unit keeps the altar distinct from the later altarpiece and does not treat the disputed cardinal title as verified identity. [第3章 pp.69–70](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)

### 本章相关内容

Haskell 将装饰承诺、约1605年的款项状况及立面费用分别叙述。款项与立面费用之间的具体机制未明；本卡保留原书报告，不补作资金转移解释。Cardinal Angelo Cesi 的人物身份沿用 S3 指向 Angelo Cesi, Bishop of Todi 的映射，枢机称谓冲突及承诺事实本身均保持原有来源限定。
