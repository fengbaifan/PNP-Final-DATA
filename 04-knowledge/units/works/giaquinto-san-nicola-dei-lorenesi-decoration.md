## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 贾昆托于1731年签约承担的罗马洛林人圣尼古拉堂绘画装饰，涉及中殿拱顶、祭坛后区及穹顶。

**English:** Painted decoration of San Nicola dei Lorenesi in Rome contracted to Giaquinto in 1731, covering the nave vault, tribune and dome.
