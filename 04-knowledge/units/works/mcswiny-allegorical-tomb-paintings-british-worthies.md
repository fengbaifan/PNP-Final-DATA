## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 记 Owen McSwiny 代表 Lord March（后为 Duke of Richmond）提出委托意大利画家绘制一组寓意墓葬画，以纪念英国近代显要人物。原书称1722年前已有十五幅开工；之后既评述该组画未能实现原定纪念目的，也记录其收藏和版画出版情况。本卡指绘画系列，不指实际墓葬；具体单幅作品、收藏清单及1741年版画书均保持各自边界。

**English:** Haskell reports that Owen McSwiny, acting for Lord March (later the Duke of Richmond), proposed commissioning Italian painters to make a series of allegorical tomb pictures commemorating England’s recent great men. The book says fifteen pictures had been started by 1722; it later judges that the paintings failed their declared commemorative aim and recounts their acquisition and engraved publication. This unit is the painting series, not physical tombs; individual pictures, uncertain ownership lists, and the 1741 book remain distinct.

### 作品信息

- 作品类型：以寓意墓葬为题材的一组绘画；原书交替称其为 tombs、monuments、pictures 和 paintings。
- 提议者与委托语境：Haskell 称 McSwiny 代表 Lord March（后为 Duke of Richmond）提出由意大利画家绘制该系列；正式委托关系候选留待 S6。
- 预定纪念对象：英国君主、指挥官及其他显要人物。Haskell 判断项目开始时几乎可以确定没有确定名单；具体人物和单幅作品另行登记。
- 形式与分工：画幅拟采用统一竖式及半圆形顶部；McSwiny 要求每幅由人物、风景、建筑与装饰分工绘制。
- 实施情况：原书称截至1722年已有十五幅开工，不表示十五幅均完成。Haskell 认为这组画未能达到其宣称的纪念英国名人的目标。
- 收藏与版画：Haskell 称 Richmond 至少购入十幅，William Morice 于1730年买下其余已完成画作；其注释同时指出具体归属清单存在混乱。拟议的五十幅版画卷于1741年出版时仅有十八幅版画，其中九幅为寓意墓画。

- Work type: a series of paintings depicting allegorical tombs; Haskell also calls them monuments, pictures, and paintings.
- Proposal and commission context: Haskell says McSwiny proposed the series on behalf of Lord March, later the Duke of Richmond, for execution by Italian painters; the formal commission relation remains for S6.
- Intended subjects: British monarchs, commanders, and other illustrious figures. Haskell infers that no definite list had probably been drawn up at the outset; named subjects and individual pictures are kept distinct.
- Format and roles: the pictures were intended to share a vertical format with semicircular tops; McSwiny required separate artists for figures, landscape, buildings, and ornaments.
- Execution: the book reports that fifteen pictures had been started by 1722; this does not mean all fifteen were completed. Haskell judges that the series failed its declared aim of commemorating British Worthies.
- Ownership and engravings: Haskell says Richmond bought at least ten paintings and William Morice bought the remaining completed pictures in 1730; his note also records confusion over the precise acquisition list. The proposed fifty-plate volume appeared in 1741 with eighteen plates, only nine of them allegorical tombs.

### 身份边界

中文：本卡登记系列层级，区别于其中任何一幅具名纪念画或版本；“tomb/monument”是绘画所表现的寓意墓葬，不据词义登记实体墓地或纪念建筑。1741年《Tombeaux des Princes》是版画出版物，不并入绘画系列。后记候选 cand-11080 仅称“为 Owen McSwiny 绘制的纪念画”并延续到作品目录，尚未证明与本系列完全对应，故其 S3 对应关系保持 undecided，不纳入本卡映射。

English: This unit records the series as a whole and remains distinct from every named commemorative painting and version within it. “Tomb” and “monument” refer to depicted allegorical tombs, not a physical burial site or monument. The 1741 Tombeaux des Princes is a printed publication, not part of the painting series. Postscript candidate cand-11080 refers only to commemorative pictures painted for Owen McSwiny and continues into a catalogue discussion; its correspondence with this series is not established, so its S3 status remains undecided and it is not mapped here.
