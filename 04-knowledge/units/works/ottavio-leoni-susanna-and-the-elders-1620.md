## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 底特律艺术学院藏《苏珊娜与长老》，编号41.89；馆方与传记辞典均将其归于奥塔维奥·莱奥尼。

**English:** Detroit Institute of Arts object 41.89, *Susanna and the Elders*, attributed to Ottavio Leoni by the museum and the biographical dictionary.
