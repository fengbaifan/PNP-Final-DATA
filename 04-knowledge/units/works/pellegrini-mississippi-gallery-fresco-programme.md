## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell所述、Giovanni Antonio Pellegrini受命装饰Banque Royale内Mississippi Gallery的壁画装饰方案。Haskell称方案结合路易十四时期凡尔赛式王权颂扬与商业寓意，并认为此类商业寓意至18世纪下半叶才普遍；他还称壁画在Système崩溃后不久被毁。原文未提供作品题名，本卡名称为描述性暂名；相关内容及任务归属按书内记载，不作外部身份、完成状况或存世现状核验。

**English:** This unit records the fresco programme that Haskell says Giovanni Antonio Pellegrini was assigned to create for the Mississippi Gallery in the Banque Royale. Haskell describes a combination of royal glorification in the manner of Louis XIV's Versailles and allegories of commerce, which he says became general only in the second half of the eighteenth century. He reports that the frescoes were destroyed soon after the collapse of the Système. The book gives no title; this unit uses a descriptive provisional name. Its content and assignment remain attributed to the book, without external verification of identity, completion, or survival.

### 本章相关内容

Haskell称该画廊位于Banque Royale，该银行是John Law金融方案的总部。装饰方案颂扬国王、摄政与银行利益，并用商业寓意组织图像；壁画在该金融方案崩溃后不久被毁，原文未给具体日期。本卡不补入方案中各寓意人物的实体端点。
