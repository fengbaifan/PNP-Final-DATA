## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本作品项记录哈斯克尔第9章 p.275 所称 Carità 处的大理石立面。原文报告 Corner 曾以建议鼓励兴建，并以“若非资金支持”（if not with money）保留资金关系的不确定性；Carità 所指地点身份、实际出资和立面完工情况均未确定。

**English:** This work unit records the great marble façade at the Carità named by Haskell on p.275. He reports that Corner encouraged its construction with advice and leaves possible funding uncertain (“if not with money”). The site identity, any direct funding, and completion of the façade remain unresolved.

### 名称

来源所述名称：Great marble façade at the Carità。对象范围为 Carità 处的立面建筑作品，具体地点未定（第9章，印刷页275，L81）。
