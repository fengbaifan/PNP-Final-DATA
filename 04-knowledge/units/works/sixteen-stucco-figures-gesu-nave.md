## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记 Haskell 所述耶稣堂中殿窗龛中的一组十六尊灰泥像。Haskell 将设计归于 Gaulli，称其追随者以灰泥执行并置于中殿窗龛；本卡不把群组拆作十六件独立作品，也不据此断定现状。

**English:** This card records the group of sixteen stucco figures that Haskell places in the Gesù nave window niches. Haskell attributes their design to Gaulli and says his followers executed them in stucco. The group is not split into sixteen individual works, and the account does not establish their present condition.
