## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Valguarnera 看见已勾画的画布并请求完成；交易发生在初稿存在之后，不能改为最初题材委托。

**English:** Valguarnera encountered the Magdalene as an existing canvas in Lanfranco's studio and requested its completion. The evidence distinguishes the purchase and completion of studio stock from an initial subject commission.
