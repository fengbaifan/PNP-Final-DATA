## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录为 S. Ignazio 规划的透视帆布穹顶幻象。Haskell 称 Pozzo 提出在平面帆布上绘制透视图以造成穹顶效果，其计划获采纳；装置于1685年6月向公众展示，原文随后称“他的装置”。来源没有明确给出公开展示的具体地点、实际绘制过程或当前存世状态。[第3章 p. 89](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md#L136-L137)

**English:** This unit records the perspective-painted canvas illusion proposed for S. Ignazio. Haskell says Pozzo proposed painting a flat canvas in perspective to create the appearance of a dome and that his plan was accepted. The device was shown to the public in June 1685 and then called “his device.” The passage does not specify the exact venue of the public display, the execution process, or the object’s present survival status. [Chapter 3, p. 89](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md#L136-L137)

### 作品边界与状态

- 帆布幻象与教堂原拟建造的实体穹顶、后来绘制的拱顶壁画及该壁画的 modello 分立。
- Haskell 称该帆布作品在绘制后数年内“几乎不可见”；本卡不推断其准确移除时间、毁损方式或现存地点。[第3章 p. 89](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md#L137)
- Pozzo 的作者身份按 Haskell 对“his device”的归属记录；不补写工作室、协作者或制作细节。
