## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell在第20章后记中提到的一件小型青铜半身像。Haskell称该像在本书第一版出版后进入Cyril Humphris之手，并将其称为Paolo Giordano II Orsini的半身像。Haskell还报告Wittkower于1966年提出Bernini归属，但表示他所理解的文献理由支持拒绝该归属；本卡不把Bernini列为已确认创作者。该像与下句所说、位于普利茅斯City Museum and Art Gallery的另一版本分开登记。持有、版本身份及归属均仅按Haskell的转述记录，未查核相关文献或外部馆藏资料。

**English:** This card records a small bronze bust mentioned in Haskell’s postscript. Haskell says it came into Cyril Humphris’s possession after the first edition of the book and describes it as a bust of Paolo Giordano II Orsini. He also reports Wittkower’s 1966 proposal of a Bernini attribution, while stating that the documentary reasons he understands support rejecting that attribution; Bernini is not recorded here as a confirmed maker. This bust is kept separate from the other version said in the next clause to be at the City Museum and Art Gallery, Plymouth. Possession, version identity, and attribution are recorded only as Haskell reports them; the cited documents and external collection records were not consulted.
