## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 《赞助人与画家》图版02a所列马费奥·巴贝里尼肖像，与2024–2025年在Palazzo Barberini及2026年在Palazzo della Minerva展出的同一作品相符。原书保留“归于卡拉瓦乔”的判断；2026年馆方公告称该归属已获主要研究者一致认可。作品描绘时任教士、后为教宗乌尔班八世的马费奥·巴贝里尼。

**English:** The Maffeo Barberini portrait listed in plate 02a corresponds to the work exhibited at Palazzo Barberini in 2024–2025 and at Palazzo della Minerva in 2026. The book's wording, “attributed to Caravaggio,” is retained; the 2026 museum announcement reports unanimous agreement among leading scholars. The sitter is Maffeo Barberini, then a cleric and later Pope Urban VIII.
