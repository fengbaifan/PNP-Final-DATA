## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡按哈斯克尔第10章 p.286 的叙述，将 Amigoni 在 Powis House 所绘的季节与犹滴故事记录为一个装饰方案。原文提及天花板、墙面和楼梯，但没有将其列成单件画布。哈斯克尔说此处无法确定赞助人；Lord Powis 只是条件性可能。

**English:** This card records the Seasons and the story of Judith and Holofernes that Haskell says Amigoni painted at Powis House as one described decorative programme. The passage names the ceiling, walls, and staircase but does not list separate canvases. Haskell says the patron cannot be identified with certainty; Lord Powis remains only a conditional possibility.
