## 内容

### 作品身份

**中文：** 本卡记录 Circignani 为 S. Stefano Rotondo 创作的殉道题材壁画组。第3章称一位未具名校长委托其创作，画面以早期基督教圣徒殉道为题材，事件大多发生在晚期罗马帝国时期，且每幅壁画附有酷刑说明。哈斯克尔在后记 p.399 再次以 Circignani 和 S. Stefano Rotondo 指称该组壁画；他转述 Thomas Buser 将图像的教诲意图与形式同耶稣会 Jerome Nadal 的福音默想图书相比较，同时明确不是具体构图的影响。此处是哈斯克尔对 Buser 的转述，未独立核阅 Buser 原文。壁画组为 work，与[圣斯特凡诺圆形堂建筑](../places/s-stefano-rotondo-rome.md)分开。

**English:** This unit records Circignani’s martyrdom fresco cycle at S. Stefano Rotondo. Chapter 3 says an unnamed rector commissioned the cycle; its subjects are early Christian martyrs, mostly in the Late Roman Empire, and each fresco had an explanation of its torture. In the postscript, p. 399, Haskell again identifies the cycle through Circignani and S. Stefano Rotondo. He reports Thomas Buser’s comparison of its didactic intent and format with Jerome Nadal’s Jesuit book of gospel meditations, while explicitly excluding influence on the actual compositions. This is Haskell’s report of Buser; Buser’s original text was not independently consulted. The frescoes are a work, separate from the [church building](../places/s-stefano-rotondo-rome.md).

### 对象边界

**中文：** 壁画组为 work；教堂建筑、晚期罗马帝国政治实体及各条正式关系均分别记录。

**English:** The fresco cycle is a work; the church building, Late Roman Empire, and formal relations are recorded separately.
