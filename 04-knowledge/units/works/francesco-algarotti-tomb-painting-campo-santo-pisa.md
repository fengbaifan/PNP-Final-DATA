## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell讨论的阿尔加罗蒂墓画。原文称其可能取法于Volpato图版60，并记录1963年的John Bryson收藏及牛津地点、后来的夏洛滕堡宫地点。第410页保留两种未解决的作者归属意见；本文不据此指定创作者。

**English:** The painting of Francesco Algarotti’s tomb discussed by Haskell. He describes it as probably derived from Volpato’s Plate 60 and records the John Bryson collection and Oxford location in 1963, followed by Schloss Charlottenburg. The competing attributions on p. 410 remain unresolved; no creator is asserted here.
