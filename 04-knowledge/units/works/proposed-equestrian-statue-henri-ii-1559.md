## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 描述性暂题，非原书题名。Haskell称Catherine de’ Medici于1559年从Michelangelo处委托一尊表现Henri II的骑马像，并明确说明该方案从未执行。原文“from Michelangelo”保留为委托来源表述；未据此认定已完成作品的创作者。

**English:** Descriptive title, not a title supplied by the source. Haskell says Catherine de’ Medici commissioned an equestrian statue of Henri II from Michelangelo in 1559 and explicitly states that the proposal was never executed. “From Michelangelo” is retained as the wording of the commission; it is not treated as proof of a completed work or a completed-work creator attribution.
