## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell第3章描述Gesù后殿的作品，并称Gaulli在该处复兴《羔羊崇拜》题材；同段把这一题材与数百年前S. Prassede和S. Maria Maggiore的马赛克区分为不同作品。中文题名为依地点和题材拟定的描述性暂题，不声称是原题。本卡只指Gesù后殿的壁画，不与Gaulli在该教堂的穹顶／拱顶作品、旧马赛克或图像题材本身合并。[第3章书内来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md)

**English:** Chapter 3 describes the work in the apse of the Gesù and says Gaulli revived the subject of the Adoration of the Lamb there; the same passage distinguishes this work from mosaics at S. Prassede and S. Maria Maggiore made centuries earlier. The Chinese title is a descriptive working title based on location and subject, not an asserted original title. This card identifies only the apse fresco, separate from Gaulli’s dome or vault works in the church, earlier mosaics, and the iconographic subject itself. [第3章书内来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md)
