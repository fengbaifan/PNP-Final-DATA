## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 弗朗切斯科·法内利约1635–1640年在伦敦创作的查理一世青铜肖像胸像，以失蜡法铸造，现藏维多利亚与阿尔伯特博物馆，馆藏号A.3-1999。

**English:** A bronze portrait bust of Charles I by Francesco Fanelli, made in London around 1635–1640 using lost-wax casting. It is now held by the Victoria and Albert Museum (accession A.3-1999).
