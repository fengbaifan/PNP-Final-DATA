## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 卡洛·多尔奇约1665–1670年在佛罗伦萨绘制的托马斯·贝恩斯肖像，由约翰·芬奇委托，现藏菲茨威廉博物馆，编号PD.13-1972。

**English:** Carlo Dolci’s portrait of Thomas Baines, painted in Florence around 1665–1670 and commissioned by John Finch; held by the Fitzwilliam Museum as PD.13-1972.
