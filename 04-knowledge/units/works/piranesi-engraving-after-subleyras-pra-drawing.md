## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 称 Francesco Piranesi 刻制了 Subleyras 的一幅图稿。紧随其后的1786年修饰 Radicchio 写作说明的时间，不能转作版画日期。该段未给出版画正式题名或版本信息，也未能将所据单张图稿对应到前文计划图稿组中的特定成员。

**English:** Haskell says Francesco Piranesi engraved a drawing by Subleyras. The following 1786 date modifies Radicchio’s writing of an accompanying account, not the engraving. The passage gives no formal title or edition details for the engraving and does not identify the specific drawing as a particular member of the earlier plan group.
