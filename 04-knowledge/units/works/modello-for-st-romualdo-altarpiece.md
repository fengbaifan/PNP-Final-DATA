## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell记载Cassiano取得一件为Sacchi的《圣罗穆阿尔多》祭坛画准备的modello。模型稿与祭坛画作为两个作品对象登记；原文没有明确指出模型制作者。

**English:** Haskell reports that Cassiano obtained a modello for Sacchi’s St Romualdo altarpiece. The modello and the altarpiece are registered as distinct works; the passage does not identify the modello’s maker.
