**中文：** 佩莱格里尼以普法尔茨选侯继承人约翰·威廉的教育为题创作的寓意画，现属老绘画陈列馆，馆藏号4666。

**English:** An allegorical painting by Pellegrini on the education of Johann Wilhelm, heir to the Elector Palatine, in the Alte Pinakothek collection, inventory 4666.
