## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称 Henry, Duke of Portland 委托 Sebastiano Ricci 为其布尔斯特罗德乡间宅邸礼拜堂绘制画作，并将《The Ascension》《The Last Supper》《The Baptism of Christ》《The Visitation》列为一个 programme。本卡记录四幅画的来源限定组，不是第五件独立画作；原书未确认完成、存世或现址。

**English:** Haskell says Henry, Duke of Portland, commissioned paintings from Sebastiano Ricci for the chapel at his country house at Bulstrode Park and lists The Ascension, The Last Supper, The Baptism of Christ, and The Visitation as one programme. This card records the source-bounded group, not a fifth independent painting; the book does not establish completion, survival, or present location.

### 本章相关内容

第10章 p.280 将四幅画称为一个 programme，并报告 George Vertue 约二十年后看见这些画作并予以称赞。脚注3所述《The Last Supper》与《The Baptism》的素描是另外的对象，不能据其馆藏或拍卖记录推断这四幅最终画作的现址。书中把 programme 比作可置于天主教堂的内容，不表示该礼拜堂属于天主教机构。

On p. 280, Haskell calls the four paintings a programme and reports that George Vertue saw and praised them about twenty years later. The sketches for The Last Supper and The Baptism mentioned in note 3 are separate objects; their reported location or sale does not establish where the final paintings are. Haskell’s comparison with a programme suitable for a Catholic church does not identify this chapel as a Catholic institution.
