### 身份边界

该 work 是来源所描述的壁画组，不是图书馆空间，也不是可识别的单幅壁画或封闭系列。它与 Niccolo Bambini 的其他作品及后文那幅作者、年代未明的天花壁画保持分立。

This work is the source-described group of frescoes, not the library space, an identified individual fresco, or a closed series. It remains distinct from Bambini's other works and from the later ceiling fresco whose artist and date Haskell leaves uncertain.
