## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔书内索引所称的查理一世三重肖像；第7章 p.179 将其识别为 Bernini 胸像的图像依据，并称 Thomas Baker 将 Van Dyck 的肖像带到 Rome。此处采用描述性暂题，不将其表述为原作正式题名。

**English:** This card records the Charles I triple portrait identified in Haskell’s index. Chapter 7, p. 179 identifies Van Dyck’s portrait as the pictorial model for Bernini’s bust and says Thomas Baker took it to Rome. The title here is descriptive, not presented as the painting’s formal original title.
