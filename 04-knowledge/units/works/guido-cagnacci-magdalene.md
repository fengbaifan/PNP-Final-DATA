## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 在1706年佛罗伦萨展览叙述中称一幅“Guido Cagnacci Magdalene”，并称斐迪南曾出借该画。脚注1另报告该画当时位于 Pitti、编号75。现有证据仅支持这条书内作品指称及其被报告的展览、馆藏语境，未据此确认其为外部馆藏中的唯一实物。

**English:** Haskell names a “Guido Cagnacci Magdalene” in his account of the 1706 Florentine exhibition and says Ferdinand lent it. Note 1 separately reports that the picture was then at the Pitti, no. 75. The available evidence supports this source-local work reference and its reported exhibition and location context; it does not identify a unique object in an external collection.

### 本章相关内容

Haskell 将该画列入斐迪南借展的作品，并在脚注1报告 Pitti 编号75。借展者与馆藏地点均以 Haskell 的叙述为依据；脚注所引书信及馆藏记录未在本卡中独立核验。

**English:** Haskell includes the painting among works lent by Ferdinand and reports Pitti no. 75 in note 1. The lender and location are recorded as Haskell's statements; neither the cited letter nor a collection record has been independently checked here.
