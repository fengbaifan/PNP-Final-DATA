## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡仅指 Haskell 所述由 Domenichino 绘于 S. Carlo ai Catinari 穹顶穹隅的复杂枢德寓意壁画。原书另称穹顶本身由 Guido Reni 的一名学生绘制；该穹顶装饰与穹隅壁画分开。Cardinal Borghese 的个人身份未确定，不能据“for”推断为已证委托关系。

**English:** This unit refers only to the complex allegories of the Cardinal Virtues that Haskell attributes to Domenichino on the pendentives of S. Carlo ai Catinari. The dome itself, which Haskell attributes to a pupil of Guido Reni, is distinct. The identity of Cardinal Borghese is unresolved, and “for” is not treated as proof of a formal commission.
