## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡以描述性题名登记哈斯克尔提及的版画：他称该版画由 Huber 创作，表现伏尔泰头像的多个角度，并将其列为挂在“cabane de la folie”墙壁的版画之一。原书未提供正式题名、创作日期或可识别的存世印张。

**English:** This card uses a descriptive title for the engraving Haskell mentions. He attributes it to Huber, describes it as showing Voltaire’s head from multiple angles, and names it among the engravings hung on the walls of the “cabane de la folie.” The book gives no formal title, date, or identifiable surviving impression.
