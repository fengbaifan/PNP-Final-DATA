## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第3章描述的一幅祭坛画：圣多米蒂拉神情沉醉、仰目、微张口，位于圣涅柔斯与圣阿基琉斯之间。原书没有给出独立正式题名；卡名是描述性名称。原文将作品归于 Pomerancio，并称它位于 Cardinal Baronio 的同名领衔教堂；具体作者关系与教堂身份仍留在 S2 候选中待审。[第3章 p.69](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md#L50)

**English:** This card records an altarpiece described in chapter 3: St Domitilla appears rapt, with raised eyes and a half-open mouth, between Sts Nereus and Achilleus. The book supplies no separate formal title; the card title is descriptive. Haskell attributes the work to Pomerancio and places it in Cardinal Baronio’s titular church “of that name”; the creator relation and church identity remain S2 candidates for review. [Chapter 3, p. 69](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md#L50)
