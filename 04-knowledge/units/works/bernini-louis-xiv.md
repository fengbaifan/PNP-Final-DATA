## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 《赞助人与画家》图版27b目录所记的伯尼尼《路易十四》胸像。第7章 p.188 将这件胸像与伯尼尼1665年的制作及从真人描绘世俗君主的经历相联系；本卡只记录哈斯克尔书中的对象与说法。

**English:** Bernini’s bust of Louis XIV, listed as plate 27b in Patrons and Painters. Chapter 7, p. 188 connects this bust with Bernini’s work in 1665 and his first opportunity to portray a secular prince from life. This card records Haskell’s account of the object.
