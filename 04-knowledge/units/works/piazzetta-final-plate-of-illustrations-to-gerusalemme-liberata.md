## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 《耶路撒冷解放》1745年阿尔布里齐版的末幅插图，由皮亚泽塔设计，含皮亚泽塔和阿尔布里齐的肖像；本书列为图版57a。

**English:** The final illustration in Albrizzi’s 1745 edition of Jerusalem Delivered, designed by Piazzetta and containing portraits of Piazzetta and Albrizzi; it is listed as plate 57a in this book.
