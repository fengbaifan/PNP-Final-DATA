## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 1624 年 Piacenza S. Antonino 工程，委托人给定题材并准予添加透视、天使、女先知与先知等；合同亦要求上等颜料。未给统一作品名，本标题为定位性称呼。

**English:** The 1624 Piacenza commission prescribed subjects while allowing Gavasetti to add perspective, angels, sibyls and prophets as the art required. The contract also specified fine pigments. The title here descriptively identifies the project.
