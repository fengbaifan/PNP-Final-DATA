## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 卡米洛·加瓦塞蒂依据1624年6月30日委托装饰皮亚琴察圣安东尼诺教堂司祭席并制作祭坛画。原定1626年完成，现存署年显示工程延至1628年；条款允许画家在给定题材内按艺术需要扩充构图。

**English:** A commission dated 30 June 1624 engaged Camillo Gavasetti to decorate the presbytery and make an altarpiece for Sant’Antonino in Piacenza. Completion was planned for 1626, but the surviving date shows work continuing to 1628; the terms allowed the painter to expand the prescribed subject as artistic practice required.
