## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应哈斯克尔第3章称为耶稣堂最醒目特征的 Gaulli 中殿壁画。作者说该壁画原本只是祭坛区高潮的前奏，并将其图像构想置于耶稣会宣教信息的语境中；由于中殿拱顶比穹顶更便于公众观看，叙述强调其象征表达较为直接。原书此处未给该壁画正式作品名，本卡使用描述性题名。

**English:** This card represents the Gaulli fresco in the nave that Haskell calls the most striking feature of the Gesù. The author says it was originally intended as a prelude to the climax in the tribune and places its imagery in the context of the Jesuits’ missionary message; because the nave vault was more accessible to the public than the dome, the passage stresses a more direct symbolism. The text does not supply a formal title here, so the card uses a descriptive title.
