## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所述 Tiepolo 为 Scuola del Carmine 主厅提出的第一套天顶装饰方案。方案沿用两案共有的中央画面，并拟以八幅画布环绕；Haskell 将其说明为表现兄弟会所享特权与相应教义主题，包括天使拯救炼狱灵魂。原文没有说明第一方案是否被正式否决或实施，也没有列出八幅画布的完整题材。

**English:** This card records the first ceiling-decoration scheme that Haskell says Tiepolo proposed for the main room of the Scuola del Carmine. It shares the central image planned for both alternatives and places eight surrounding canvases around it. Haskell describes these as illustrating privileges enjoyed by the Confraternity and related doctrinal subjects, including angels rescuing souls in purgatory. The passage does not say whether the first scheme was formally rejected or executed, and it does not list the complete subjects of all eight canvases.

### 本章相关内容

Haskell distinguishes this first alternative from the second scheme of virtues, which he says was selected and carried out with modifications. This card preserves the proposal-level distinction and does not identify the first scheme with the later executed frescoes.
