## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔在阿尔蒂基耶罗花园提到的友谊祭坛。他称祭坛有伊壁鸠鲁与福基翁的巨型半身像。本卡不声称祭坛现存，也不补作者、年代或现状。

**English:** This unit records the Altar of Friendship that Haskell mentions in the Alticchiero garden. He says it had colossal busts of Epicurus and Phocion. The card does not claim that the altar survives or supply a maker, date, or present condition.
