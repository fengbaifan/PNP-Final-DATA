## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本知识元登记 Haskell 第8章 p.208 所述的版画组。Haskell称其中有描绘农民生活的场景，并称Jean-Baptiste de Wael将版画组题献给Gaspar Roomer。Paris Musées 将 Bartsch 1 题献页归入一套十四幅版画，并提供该系列的法文题名；Catalogo Generale 记录一件版画上的题献铭文，题献对象为Roomer、署名为De Wael。原书没有给出各幅版画的完整清单或制作年代；目录中的单件版画记录不等于整套实物，也不据此推定英国博物馆的现时馆藏状态。

**English:** This unit records the print series described by Haskell in Chapter 8, p. 208. Haskell says that it includes scenes of peasant life and that Jean-Baptiste de Wael dedicated the set to Gaspar Roomer. Paris Musées places the Bartsch 1 dedication title page within a suite of fourteen prints and supplies the series title in French. The Italian national catalog records a dedicatory inscription on a catalogued print naming Roomer and signed by De Wael. Haskell does not provide a complete list of the prints or a production date; a catalog record for one impression does not represent the entire physical set or establish the British Museum’s current holdings.

### 证据边界

Haskell 第8章 p.208 L55及注3 L140经页图核对；注3所引 Bartsch 卷册页与巴黎目录的 Bartsch 1 题献页支持版画组身份关联。题献者和受献者边由版画题记目录记录与 Haskell 的组级叙述共同支持。英国博物馆关系只记录 Haskell 的报告；没有核实该馆当前目录或具体藏品号。没有推定版画组的原始题名、制作日期、单幅清单、委托关系、Roomer所有权或De Wael对全套版画的创作／刻印责任。
