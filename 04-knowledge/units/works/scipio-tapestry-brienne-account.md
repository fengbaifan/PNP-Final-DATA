## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Brienne回忆中Mazarin住所小画廊的Scipio题材羊毛挂毯。引文称其曾属Saint-André元帅，并把设计归于Jules Romain；这些均是来源记载，不构成对具体存世版本或创作者身份的独立核验。Brienne还称它是Cardinal最美的挂毯，这是原文评价而非核定排名。

**English:** This card records the wool tapestry depicting Scipio that appears in Brienne’s account of the small gallery in Mazarin’s residence. The quotation says that it had belonged to the Marshal of Saint-André and attributes its designs to Jules Romain; neither statement independently identifies a surviving version or verifies the maker. Brienne also calls it the Cardinal’s finest tapestry, a reported evaluation rather than an established ranking.
