## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡以描述性暂题指称哈斯克尔第3章所述的圣像。原书称画作描绘圣方济各·沙勿略，由范戴克绘制，并在沙勿略1623年册封时安置于耶稣堂右横翼礼拜堂。原书未给正式题名、具体版本或现存位置；本卡不补推这些信息。

**English:** This unit uses a descriptive title for the picture mentioned in Haskell’s Chapter 3. The book identifies it as an image of St Francis Xavier, painted by Van Dyck and placed in the Gesù’s right-transept chapel at the time of Xavier’s canonisation in 1623. Haskell gives no formal title, specific version, or present location; none is inferred.
