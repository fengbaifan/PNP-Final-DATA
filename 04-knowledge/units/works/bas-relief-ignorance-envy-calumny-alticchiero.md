## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell记载，Ignorance、Envy与Calumny被描绘在一件浮雕中，并称该浮雕依据Pliny所记的Apelles著名构图。本卡只登记书内指称的浮雕；不声称其存世、正式题名或复制链已确定。所据Apelles构图仍为独立待证候选。

**English:** Haskell reports that Ignorance, Envy, and Calumny were depicted in a bas-relief and says it was based on a famous composition by Apelles as recorded by Pliny. This card registers only the relief described in the book; it does not assert a surviving object, formal title, or established copy history. The Apelles composition remains a separate unresolved candidate.
