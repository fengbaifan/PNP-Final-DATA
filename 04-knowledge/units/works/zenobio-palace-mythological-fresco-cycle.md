## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell所述泽诺比奥宫一组神话题材壁画。Dorigny约在该宫殿接近1703年竣工状态之前十五年受请绘制；原书没有列出单幅题名或准确创作年份。本卡不把装饰框架或其中的裸像合并为壁画组本身。[第9章 p.250](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)（L92）

**English:** This card records the mythological fresco cycle Haskell describes at Palazzo Zenobio. Dorigny was called in to paint it some fifteen years before the palace was nearing completion by 1703; Haskell gives neither individual titles nor an exact year. The architectural framework and its nude figures are kept distinct from the fresco cycle. [Chapter 9, p. 250](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md) (L92)
