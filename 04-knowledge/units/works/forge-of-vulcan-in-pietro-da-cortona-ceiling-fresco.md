## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第2章脚注中称为“Forge of Vulcan”的绘画场景。该脚注明示其位于Pietro da Cortona在Palazzo Barberini的天花壁画中。本卡将场景与已登记的整幅[《乌尔班八世统治的荣耀》](pietro-da-cortona-glorification-of-the-reign-of-urban-viii.md)区分，不另称其为独立载体或已确认版本。印本p.33拼写为“Forge of Vulcan”；S0 OCR误作“forge os Vulcan”。中文题名为描述性译名；脚注所引Pastor第XIII卷第865页未查阅。

**English:** This card records the pictorial scene Haskell calls “Forge of Vulcan” in a Chapter 2 footnote. The note places it within Pietro da Cortona’s ceiling fresco at Palazzo Barberini. The scene is kept distinct from the registered whole fresco, [Glorification of the Reign of Urban VIII](pietro-da-cortona-glorification-of-the-reign-of-urban-viii.md), without asserting a separate support or confirmed version. The print on p. 33 reads “Forge of Vulcan”; the S0 OCR reads “forge os Vulcan.” The Chinese title is descriptive. Pastor, vol. XIII, p. 865, cited in the footnote, has not been consulted.
