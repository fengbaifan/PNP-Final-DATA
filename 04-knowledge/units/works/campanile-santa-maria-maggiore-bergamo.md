## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本知识元记录 Haskell 第8章 p.215 所述贝加莫 Santa Maria Maggiore 教堂的钟楼。原书在教堂历次营建变化中称钟楼于1425年完成。本卡将钟楼作为独立建筑构件，与教堂整体、祭衣间、穹顶及南入口分开；不据此补写钟楼设计者或建造者。

**English:** This unit records the campanile of Santa Maria Maggiore in Bergamo, as described on p. 215 of Haskell’s Chapter 8. In the church’s construction history, the book says that the campanile was completed in 1425. This unit treats it as a separate architectural component, distinct from the church as a whole, the sacristy, the dome, and the south entrance; it does not identify a designer or builder.
