## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称La Vrillière随后委托Guercino绘制该作品；p.184注4将“Guercino’s Cato”报告在Marseilles。 本卡限原书所述，具体实物、版本与现藏未独立核实。

**English:** Haskell reports that La Vrillière subsequently commissioned this work from Guercino; note 4 on p.184 reports “Guercino’s Cato” at Marseilles. The specific physical object, version, and present location have not been independently verified.
