## 内容

### 描述
**中文：** Haskell记载的一幅1665年版画，描绘圣雅努阿里乌斯，并题献给Gaspar Roomer；脚注称其底本为Ribera作品，但未识别该底本。

**English:** A print recorded by Haskell as dated 1665, depicting Saint Januarius and dedicated to Gaspar Roomer; the note attributes its model to Ribera but does not identify that underlying work.
