## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本知识元记录 Haskell 所述 Andrea Memmo 为 Prà della Valle 绘制的椭圆规划。原文称该规划以罗马斗兽场为依据，并受 Bologna 与 Vicenza 的实例启发；没有给出正式题名，也未在此识别一张现存图稿。第10章提到的1782年方案只被称为相似，不与本规划合并。

**English:** This unit records the oval plan that Haskell says Andrea Memmo drew up for Prà della Valle. Haskell says it was based on the Colosseum and inspired by examples from Bologna and Vicenza. The passage gives no formal title and identifies no surviving drawing. The scheme mentioned in Chapter 10 is described as similar; it is not merged with this plan.
