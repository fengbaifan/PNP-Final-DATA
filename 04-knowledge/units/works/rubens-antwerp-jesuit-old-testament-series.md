## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本作品组记录哈斯克尔第9章 p.272 注4所述的一系列旧约场景：他称这些场景预示基督生平，由鲁本斯为安特卫普耶稣会教堂绘制。原书未在此列出系列中的单件画作；本卡不把系列拆成未经识别的单幅，也不把注4开头代词 It 的先行词并入此组。

**English:** This work group records the series of Old Testament scenes described in Haskell's Chapter 9, p.272 note 4. He says Rubens painted the scenes for the Jesuit Church at Antwerp and that they prefigured the Life of Christ. The passage does not list individual paintings; this card does not invent member works or identify the antecedent of the note's opening pronoun It with this series.
