## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所述、为圣母无原罪始胎堂设计的青铜圣龛方案。原方案材质为青铜；书中随后称 Urban VIII 同意改用 pietre fine。该记述不证明方案实际制作、安装或现存，也未指名设计者。

**English:** This card records the bronze tabernacle design Haskell describes for S. Maria della Concezione. The initial design called for bronze; Haskell then says Urban VIII agreed to pietre fine. This account does not establish that the design was built, installed, or survives, and it does not name the designer.

### 本章相关内容

Haskell称 Urban VIII 曾命人为主祭坛设计青铜圣龛，后来作为让步同意采用 pietre fine。原文未给出设计者姓名；扫描印刷页将材质写作两词“pietre fine”，来源 OCR 转录连写为“pietrefine”。

Haskell says Urban VIII had a bronze tabernacle designed for the High Altar and later agreed, as a concession, to the use of pietre fine. The designer is unnamed. The printed scan reads “pietre fine” as two words, while the source OCR joins them as “pietrefine.”
