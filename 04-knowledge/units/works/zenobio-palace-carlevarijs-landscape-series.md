## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell所述由Luca Carlevarijs为泽诺比奥宫绘制的一组风景画。原书称其为“romantic landscapes”，并说这些作品嵌入通往Salone的走廊画框中；“romantic”按本段语境译为风景描述，不据此归入现代浪漫主义流派。原书未给数量、单幅题名或创作年份。[第9章 p.250](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)（L93）

**English:** This card records the landscape series Haskell says Luca Carlevarijs painted for Palazzo Zenobio. Haskell calls them “romantic landscapes” and says they were inserted into frames along the corridor leading to the Salone. The wording is kept within this passage and does not assign the works to the modern Romantic movement. No count, individual titles, or date is supplied. [Chapter 9, p. 250](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md) (L93)
