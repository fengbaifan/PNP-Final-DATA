## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔所述耶稣堂圣依纳爵小堂大祭坛内部、表现圣依纳爵生平与奇迹的浅浮雕组。书中没有为单幅浮雕提供名称、材质或独立版本信息。

**English:** This card records the group of bas-reliefs inside the great altar of the Chapel of St Ignatius in the Gesù, described by Haskell as depicting St Ignatius’s life and miracles. The passage does not name individual reliefs or supply their material or separate versions.
