## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡表示乡间别墅连接主楼与端部亭体的两层低矮翼部。翼部是被单独描述的建筑构件，归 work；原书还称其下层由矩形拱廊构成并插有柱群。没有尺寸数据超出层数与形状描述。 [第15章来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_i.md)

**English:** This card represents the low, two-storey wings that connect the villa's main block to its end pavilions. The wings are separately described architectural components and are classified as work. Haskell says their lower storeys contain rectangular arcades with the column group; no dimensions beyond the stated storeys and form are supplied. [Chapter 15 source](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_i.md)
