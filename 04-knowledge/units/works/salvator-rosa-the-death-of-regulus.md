**中文：** 罗萨约1650–1652年创作的布面油画，藏于弗吉尼亚美术馆，馆藏号59.15；普林斯顿另藏其准备稿。

**English:** An oil painting on canvas by Rosa, dated about 1650–1652 and held by the Virginia Museum of Fine Arts as 59.15; a preparatory drawing is held at Princeton.
