## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 1639至1641年间，弗朗切斯科·阿尔巴尼为博洛尼亚圣母忠仆大殿的Gozzadini家族礼拜堂创作《圣安德烈朝拜殉道十字架》。1639年通信所载画布与群青供应属于这项委托，但不能与同页另一价格案例合并。

**English:** Between 1639 and 1641, Francesco Albani painted Saint Andrew Adoring the Cross of Martyrdom for the Gozzadini family chapel in Santa Maria dei Servi, Bologna. The 1639 provision of canvas and ultramarine belongs to this commission and must not be merged with the separate price example on the same page.
