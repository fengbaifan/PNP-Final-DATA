## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Bonifazio Gozadini 承诺供应画布和群青；不能无证地与同页 Albani 450/1000 lire 的另一脚注例合并。

**English:** In the 1639 agreement cited by Haskell, Bonifazio Gozadini promised canvas and ultramarine for Albani's altarpiece for the Chiesa de' Servi in Bologna. The chapter does not establish that this was the separate Albani commission priced at 1,000 lire with a 450-lire advance.
