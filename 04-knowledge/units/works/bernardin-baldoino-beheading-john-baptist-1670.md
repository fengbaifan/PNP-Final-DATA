## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Thevenon列出的Bernardin Baldoino作品；来源称画作于1670年在罗马为尼斯黑衣忏悔者制作。媒材、尺寸、现藏地及存续状态未由本次读取的来源说明。（S1）

**English:** A work listed by Thevenon among Bernardin Baldoino’s paintings. The source states that it was executed in Rome in 1670 for the Black Penitents of Nice. The medium, dimensions, present location, and survival status are not specified in the source consulted here. (S1)
