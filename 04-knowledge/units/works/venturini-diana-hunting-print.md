## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 乔万尼·弗朗切斯科·文图里尼据多梅尼基诺《狄安娜狩猎》制作的蚀刻与雕版印刷品；本卡以Rijksmuseum藏印本RP-P-OB-38.357为对象。

**English:** An etching and engraving by Giovanni Francesco Venturini after Domenichino’s The Hunting of Diana; this record describes the Rijksmuseum impression RP-P-OB-38.357.
