## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本书图版26b所列的罗马内利顶画局部，题材为罗慕路斯与雷穆斯，书中地点记为原马扎然宫的画廊。

**English:** The Romanelli ceiling detail listed as plate 26b, depicting Romulus and Remus and located by the book in a gallery of the former Palais Mazarin.
