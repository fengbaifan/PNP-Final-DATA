## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 朱塞佩·佐基绘制的扎内蒂与安德烈亚·杰里尼双人肖像，约1750年的铜板油画；本书列为图版58a。

**English:** Giuseppe Zocchi’s double portrait of Zanetti and Andrea Gerini, an oil painting on copper dated to about 1750; listed as plate 58a in this book.
