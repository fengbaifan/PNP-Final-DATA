### 本书相关内容

作品见第5章 p.127 对Orazio Gentileschi 1603年证言的转述。Haskell称Michelangelo da Caravaggio创作Earthly Love，并将其与Baglione的Divine Love并列于艺术竞争语境；原证言及作品实物未在本轮独立核阅。

The work appears in Haskell’s account of Orazio Gentileschi’s 1603 testimony on p. 127. Haskell names Earthly Love as a work by Michelangelo da Caravaggio and places it alongside Baglione’s Divine Love in an account of artistic rivalry; the testimony and the physical painting were not independently consulted in this registration.
