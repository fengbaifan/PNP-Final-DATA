## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡以描述性暂题识别Haskell第4章p.116所述的Donna Olimpia肖像：Camillo Massimi从画家处取得该像。第7章p.190把它列为Velasquez绘制的两幅肖像之一，并记Del Carpio取得该像；Haskell书内索引将两处页码列在同一作品子目下。原书没有给出正式题名、创作日期或现藏地点。虽然p.190紧接Massimi遗产处置的叙述，但没有明确说明这幅肖像来自其遗产。

**English:** This card uses a descriptive working title for the portrait of Donna Olimpia described in chapter 4, p. 116, where Camillo Massimi acquired it from the painter. Chapter 7, p. 190 lists it among two portraits by Velasquez and records Del Carpio's acquisition; Haskell's index places both passages under the same work subentry. The book gives no formal title, date, or present location. Although p. 190 follows the account of the disposal of Massimi's property, it does not explicitly state that this portrait came from his estate.
