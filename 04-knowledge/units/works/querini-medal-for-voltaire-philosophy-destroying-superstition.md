## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡将Haskell在第15章印刷页369所述的单枚奖章作为来源限定的作品对象登记。Haskell称安杰洛·奎里尼特铸此章并赠予伏尔泰，描述其图像为“哲学摧毁迷信”，并称其周围文字出自卢克莱修。此处记录的是原书报告；原书未指出铸币厂、奖章制作者、材质、尺寸或现存状况，也未识别可核对的存世实物。

**English:** This card registers the single medal reported on printed page 369 of Chapter 15 as a source-bounded work. Haskell says Angelo Querini had it specially coined and presented it to Voltaire, describes its image as Philosophy destroying Superstition, and attributes the surrounding text to Lucretius. This records Haskell's account; the source does not name a mint or medallist, give material or dimensions, or establish the survival of an identifiable object.
