## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本知识元记录 Haskell 对贝加莫 Santa Maria Maggiore 教堂穹顶内部灰泥装饰的描述。原书称装饰丰富而厚重，逐渐覆于穹顶内侧；没有给出装饰者、精确年代或具体单件题名。本卡与穹顶建筑构件分开登记，并以关系表示其装饰部位。

**English:** This unit records Haskell’s description of the stucco ornamentation over the interior of the dome of Santa Maria Maggiore in Bergamo. The book characterizes it as rich and heavy but supplies no decorator, precise date, or individual title. This unit is registered separately from the dome as an architectural component, with their connection expressed as a relation.
