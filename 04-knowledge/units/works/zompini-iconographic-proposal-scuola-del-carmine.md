## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 第9章 p.273–274 所述佐姆皮尼为 Scuola del Carmine 后续装饰竞赛提出的图像方案。Haskell 报告佐姆皮尼的 idea 获得接受，并把该方案描述为颂扬圣母，特别以 Rebecca、Abigail、Esther、Judith 与 Mother of the Maccabees 说明她在旧约中的预表。原文未说明“被接受”的具体对象，也未说明完整方案最终获选或实施；本卡不将其与 Tiepolo 的两套天顶方案合并。书中称方案据教父阅读而来，并明确保留“as he claimed”的归属限定，此处只记录 Haskell 的转述。

**English:** This card records the iconographic proposal that Haskell says Zompini put forward for the Scuola del Carmine's further-decoration competition in chapter 9, pp. 273–274. Haskell reports that Zompini's idea was accepted and describes the proposal as a glorification of the Virgin, particularly through her Old Testament prefiguration by Rebecca, Abigail, Esther, Judith, and the Mother of the Maccabees. The passage does not specify what “accepted” refers to or say that the complete proposal was selected or executed; this card keeps it distinct from Tiepolo's two ceiling schemes. Haskell reports the claimed basis in readings of the Church Fathers with the qualification “as he claimed”; this card records that report only.
