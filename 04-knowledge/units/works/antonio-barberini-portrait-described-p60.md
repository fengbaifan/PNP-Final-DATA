### 本章相关内容

**中文：** Haskell描述画像中的人物身着枢机礼服，并称其为“衰落权力的化身”。注3将这幅文中所述画像定位到Galleria Nazionale di Arte Antica in Rome，使用的是Haskell出版语境中的“now”，不表示当前馆藏；该注引用的Lombardo 1959 p.2未独立查阅。段落位于有关Carlo Maratta肖像的语境中，但本批不把上下文自动转写为这幅具体画像的创作者归属。

**English:** Haskell describes the sitter in Cardinal's regalia and calls the image an embodiment of declining power. Note 3 locates the portrait at the Galleria Nazionale di Arte Antica in Rome using “now” relative to Haskell's publication context; this is not a claim about present custody. Lombardo 1959, p. 2, cited in the note, was not independently consulted. The passage occurs in a discussion of Carlo Maratta's portraits, but this batch does not turn that context into a creator attribution for this specific picture.
