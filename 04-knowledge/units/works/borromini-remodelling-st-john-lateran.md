## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本作品知识元记录 Haskell 所述博罗米尼接手的 St John Lateran 整体改建。作者称博罗米尼被要求保留旧大殿基本结构，因此将设计才能用于装饰，而非设计一座全新的主教座堂。标题是描述性工作名，不是原书或外部目录确认的正式题名；书中未给出准确开工、完工日期或委托者。

**English:** This work unit records the complete remodelling of St John Lateran that Haskell says Borromini took over. Haskell reports that Borromini was instructed to preserve the old basilica’s essential structure, so he applied his design abilities to decoration rather than to a wholly new cathedral. The title is descriptive, not a verified title from the book or an external catalogue. The source gives no precise start or completion date or commissioning party.

### 身份边界

本卡记录建筑改建项目，与 St John Lateran 教堂建筑分开；不把建筑本体、被毁的 Gentile da Fabriano 与 Pisanello 壁画组 `cand-6278` 或木质天花板计划混成同一作品。Haskell 的“接手”和装饰表述不在本卡中扩写为唯一创作者或全程负责。

This card denotes the remodelling project, distinct from the St John Lateran building. It does not merge the building, the destroyed fresco group by Gentile da Fabriano and Pisanello (`cand-6278`), or the planned wooden ceiling into one work. Haskell’s statements about taking over the project and applying design work to decoration are not expanded into sole authorship or responsibility for every phase.
