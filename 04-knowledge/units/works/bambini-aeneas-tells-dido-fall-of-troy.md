## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 班比尼为布奥纳科尔西宫埃涅阿斯画廊绘制的宴会场景，表现狄多邀请埃涅阿斯讲述往事。作品为布面油画，馆藏号131a。

**English:** A banquet scene painted by Bambini for the Aeneid Gallery in Palazzo Buonaccorsi, showing Dido inviting Aeneas to recount his story. The work is an oil painting on canvas, inventory 131a.
