## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell记载费迪南德于1704年委托Sebastiano Ricci创作的一件受难题材绘画，称名包含圣母、圣约翰与圣嘉禄·鲍罗梅奥。这里采用描述性暂题，不声称是作品原题；Haskell说Ricci“似乎”曾赴佛罗伦萨为这件画作现场创作，不能据此确认完成、安装或存世版本。

**English:** A Crucifixion picture that Haskell says Ferdinand commissioned from Sebastiano Ricci in 1704, naming the Madonna, St John and St Charles Borromeo. The English wording is used as a descriptive working title, not asserted as an original title. Haskell says Ricci “seems” to have visited Florence to paint it on site; this does not establish completion, installation, or a surviving version.
