## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 鲁本斯于1606年为罗马新教堂主祭坛受托创作《教皇圣格里高利与诸圣朝拜瓦利切拉圣母像》，1607年完成，1608年撤下并带回安特卫普。该布面油画现藏格勒诺布尔博物馆，与后来的石板方案分开。

**English:** Rubens received the 1606 commission for Saint Gregory with Saints Venerating the Madonna della Vallicella for the high altar of the Chiesa Nuova in Rome. Completed in 1607 and removed in 1608, the canvas is now in the Musée de Grenoble and is distinct from the later slate solution.
