## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 皮亚泽塔为舒伦堡创作的田园题材画作，不晚于1745年，科隆瓦尔拉夫—里夏茨博物馆藏品2806。

**English:** A pastoral painting made by Piazzetta for Schulenburg no later than 1745, catalogued as 2806 at the Wallraf-Richartz Museum in Cologne.
