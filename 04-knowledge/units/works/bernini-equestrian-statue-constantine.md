## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 这是一件在哈斯克尔脚注中被列为英诺森十世时期伯尼尼重要委托的作品；第七章又称路易十四的骑马像曾被要求以它为参照。书内未证明君士坦丁骑马像的完成状态，也未证明路易十四骑马像最终采用了这一设计。

**English:** Haskell’s footnote lists this as an important Bernini commission from Innocent X. Chapter 7 says the Louis XIV equestrian statue had been requested to follow it. The book does not establish the Constantine statue’s completion or prove that the later statue adopted its design.
