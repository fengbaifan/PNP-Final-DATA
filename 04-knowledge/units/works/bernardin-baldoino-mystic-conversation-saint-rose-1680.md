## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Bernardin Baldoino所作《圣罗撒与圣母的神秘交谈》，与其背面签名并纪年1680年的《利马圣罗撒神魂超拔》成对；研究将两件作品定位于尼斯圣雷帕拉塔主教座堂，并据配对关系将本件定为1680年。（S1）

**English:** *The Mystical Conversation of Saint Rose with the Virgin* by Bernardin Baldoino, paired with his signed and dated 1680 *Ecstasy of Saint Rose of Lima*. The study locates both works in Nice Cathedral and dates this companion painting to 1680 by association. (S1)
