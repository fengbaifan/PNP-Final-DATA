## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell记载，Sebastiano Ricci为费迪南德在皮蒂宫一间小室绘制的壁画装饰仍然存世，并在下一页描述天花板上的《维纳斯与阿多尼斯》场景、四幅墙面神话场景、四角人物胸像和门上构图。此中文题名为描述性工作名；原书未给整套装饰的正式题名、确切房间名称或创作日期。它与同页提及、报告已毁的《艺术寓意》及1704年受难像分开登记。

**English:** Haskell reports that a fresco decoration Ricci painted for Ferdinand in a small room at the Pitti survived, and describes its ceiling scene, four mythological wall scenes, corner busts, and overdoor composition on the following page. This is a descriptive working title; Haskell supplies no formal title for the whole decoration, exact room name, or creation date. It is kept distinct from the destroyed Allegory of the Arts and the 1704 Crucifixion.
