## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 萨索费拉托为罗马圣撒比纳堂创作的宗教画，1643年获付40斯库多。

**English:** A religious painting made by Sassoferrato for Santa Sabina in Rome, for which he was paid 40 scudi in 1643.
