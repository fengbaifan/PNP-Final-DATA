## 内容

### 描述

**中文：**德·罗西斯1663年6月2日致鲁福信附有罗萨制作的待售画单，记一件5×5 palmi见方、人物约3 palmi的萨提尔与女子画作，标价100 scudi。Ruffo 1916年刊本脚注称该画由Antonio Ruffo购得，后列作“Due Satiri e tre Ninfe che scherzano”，但未给精确购藏日期。Bonhams将其与一件106.8×106.8厘米、署名“Rosa”的布面油画联系起来；刊本的历史清单信息尚不能独立确认该现代拍卖画布就是该作，Volpi 2014年第553页仍待直接核读。（S1–S2）

**English:** The sales note attached to De Rosis's letter to Ruffo of 2 June 1663 lists a 5-by-5-palmi painting with figures about 3 palmi high, depicting satyrs with women, priced at 100 scudi. Ruffo's 1916 footnote says Antonio Ruffo bought this picture and lists it as “Due Satiri e tre Ninfe che scherzano,” without giving an exact acquisition date. Bonhams links the description to a 106.8 × 106.8 cm oil painting on canvas signed “Rosa.” The 1916 inventory reference does not independently establish that the modern auction canvas is the same object; Volpi 2014, p. 553, still needs direct reading. (S1–S2)
