## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第2章以 *The Andrians* 称名、列为 Titian 绘画的一件作品。中文题名“安德罗斯人”为工作译名。第25页将其列入 Cardinal Aldobrandini 从 Ferrara 带回 Rome 的提香绘画；此处只记录哈斯克尔的叙述，不据此补写创作年代、原作现藏地或转移后的完整所有权链。第129页注4另述 Cavaliere d’Arpino 的复制、Albani 可能研究原作，以及 Rubens 在 Stockholm 的后期版本；它们均作为原作以外的复制／版本记录。Cavaliere d’Arpino 的身份仍待证；所引 Baglione 手稿与 John Walker 原文均未独立查阅。[第2章 p.25](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_i.md)（L49–50） [第2章 p.129 注4](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_i.md)（L129）

**English:** This unit records the work Haskell calls *The Andrians* and lists among Titian’s paintings in Chapter 2. The Chinese title is a working translation. On page 25 Haskell includes it among Titian paintings Cardinal Aldobrandini brought from Ferrara to Rome; this records Haskell’s account and does not add a creation date, present location, or complete ownership chain. Note 4 on page 129 separately discusses a copy by the Cavaliere d’Arpino, Albani’s possible study of the original, and later Rubens versions in Stockholm. These remain copies or versions distinct from the original painting. The identity behind “Cavaliere d’Arpino” remains unresolved; neither the cited Baglione manuscript nor John Walker’s original text was consulted.
