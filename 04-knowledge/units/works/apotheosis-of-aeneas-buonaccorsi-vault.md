## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell记述的一幅长廊低桶形拱顶壁画，题材为《埃涅阿斯神化》。他称Raimondo Buonaccorsi于1707年召Carlo Antonio Rambaldi与Antonio Dardani绘制；这说明画家受召承担的任务，不独立证明壁画由二人完成。

**English:** A fresco on the low barrel vault of the long gallery, depicting the Apotheosis of Aeneas in Haskell’s account. Haskell says Raimondo Buonaccorsi summoned Carlo Antonio Rambaldi and Antonio Dardani to paint it in 1707; this records their assigned task and does not independently establish that either completed the fresco.
