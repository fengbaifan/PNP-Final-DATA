## 内容

### 作品身份

**中文：** 本卡记录原书所称耶稣堂 High Altar 这一独立祭坛结构。哈斯克尔称 Farnese 将其装饰保留给自己及家族，并称 Muziano 受委托为其绘制《The Circumcision》。祭坛结构按 S3 类型作为 work 记录，与祭坛画及耶稣堂建筑分别处理；本卡不补写形制、材料或现存状况。

**English:** This card records the Gesù High Altar as a distinct altar structure. Haskell says Farnese reserved its decoration for himself and his family and that Muziano was commissioned to paint The Circumcision for it. Following the S3 classification, the altar structure is recorded as a work, separate from the painting and the church building; no form, material, or present condition is added.

### 对象边界

**中文：** 祭坛结构为 work；《The Circumcision》为另一件绘画 work，教堂整体为 place。

**English:** The altar structure is a work; The Circumcision is a separate painting work, and the church as a whole is a place.
