## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell第10章注5所指、Sebastiano Ricci为伯灵顿勋爵绘制并于1713年纪年的《苏珊娜与长老》。Haskell报告该画当时在Chatsworth。Chatsworth House Trust的2022年年报以相同作者、题名和年份记录此画并说明曾借展；该记录支持本卡身份匹配，不表示作品目前实际陈列或存放于Chatsworth。

**English:** This unit records the *Susanna and the Elders* that Haskell identifies in chapter 10, note 5, among Sebastiano Ricci’s pictures for Lord Burlington dated 1713. Haskell reports the painting at Chatsworth. The Chatsworth House Trust’s 2022 annual review records a loan of a painting with the same artist, title, and date; this supports the identity match and does not establish its present display or physical storage at Chatsworth.
