## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡识别哈斯克尔第八章正文及索引中与Crespi相连的《无辜者屠杀》作品条目。原书称Crespi于1708年初在佛罗伦萨将该画呈给Ferdinand；注2称Silva曾委托此画作为赠画，但后来撤回赠予意图。此描述性题名只指书内作品身份，不识别存世实物、具体版本、材质或现藏。

**English:** This card identifies the Crespi-linked Massacre of the Innocents work entry in Haskell’s Chapter 8 text and index. The book says Crespi presented the picture to Ferdinand in Florence in early 1708; note 2 reports Silva’s commission as an intended gift and his later withdrawal of that intention. This descriptive title refers only to the work identity in the book, without identifying a surviving object, specific version, medium, or present location.
