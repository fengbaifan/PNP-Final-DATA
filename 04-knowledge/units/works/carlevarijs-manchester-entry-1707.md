## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第10章正文称 Lord Manchester 于1707年第二次正式访问威尼斯时委托 Luca Carlevarijs 记录其入城；注5以《Lord Manchester’s Entry into Venice (1707)》指称该画。此卡登记书内可识别的作品，不据旧馆藏报告确认现存版本。

**English:** Chapter 10 says Lord Manchester commissioned Luca Carlevarijs to record his entry into Venice during a second official visit in 1707; note 5 names the painting Lord Manchester’s Entry into Venice (1707). This card records the work identifiable in the book and does not use the older holding report to verify a surviving version.
