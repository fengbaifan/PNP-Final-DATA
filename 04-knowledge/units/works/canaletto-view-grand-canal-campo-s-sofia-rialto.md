## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔描述的第一幅施特赖特生活场景：从圣索菲亚广场望向里亚托的大运河全景。书中没有提供正式题名或现藏信息。

**English:** The first of the two life-related views described by Haskell: a sweep of the Grand Canal from Campo S. Sofia toward the Rialto. No formal title or present location is supplied.
