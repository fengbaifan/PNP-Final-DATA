## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第13章关于 Pietro Antonio Novelli 为 Pasquali 出版的 Goldoni 作品设计的一组插图：原书称需要一百多幅图稿，由不同版画家复制；卷首图表现 Goldoni 生平片段，剧作插图则延续其亲密、面向公众的呈现方式。本卡登记整组插图方案，不逐件命名未识别图稿。

**English:** This card records Haskell’s account in Chapter 13 of a body of illustrations Pietro Antonio Novelli designed for Pasquali’s edition of Goldoni’s works. The text says more than one hundred drawings were needed and that various engravers reproduced them; frontispieces depicted episodes from Goldoni’s life, while the play illustrations extended the undertaking’s intimate address to readers. The card records the programme as a group and does not name unidentified individual designs.
