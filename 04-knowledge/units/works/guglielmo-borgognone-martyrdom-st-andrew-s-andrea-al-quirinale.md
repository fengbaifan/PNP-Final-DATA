## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称名为《圣安德烈殉难》的祭坛画于1668年委托Guglielmo Borgognone绘制，并说明其目的地为奎里纳尔圣安德烈堂高祭坛。本卡记录书内所述作品与委托，不据此断言作品已完成、存世或现藏于该堂。

**English:** Haskell names an altarpiece The Martyrdom of St. Andrew and reports that Guglielmo Borgognone was commissioned to paint it in 1668 for the High Altar of S. Andrea al Quirinale. This unit records the work and commission as reported in the book; it does not assert completion, survival, or present location.
