## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell以《Madonna and Four Saints》称呼、并作为德尔·罗索家族较小版本参照对象的圣奥古斯丁堂早期画作。Haskell将其与较小版本区分，并把早期画作定位于S. Agostino, Cortona；未给出该早期画作的现今位置或更精确日期。

**English:** This card records the earlier picture that Haskell calls *Madonna and Four Saints* and uses as the reference for the smaller Del Rosso version. Haskell distinguishes the two versions and locates the earlier picture at S. Agostino, Cortona; he gives no present location or more precise date for the earlier picture.
