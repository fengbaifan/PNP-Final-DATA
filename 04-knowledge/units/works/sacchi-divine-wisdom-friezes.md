### 描述

**English:** The painted and stucco decorative friezes that Haskell describes around the main fresco of Sacchi’s Divine Wisdom. The source says the bee and laurel emblems feature prominently there; it does not establish a separate maker, date, or the friezes’ exact boundary relative to the main fresco.
