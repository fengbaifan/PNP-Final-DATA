## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录卡列拉1726年账目中以 Le Quattro Stagioni 命名的作品组。条目称其拟寄往伦敦给 Smith；是否实际寄出、付款，以及它与第10章 p.295 泛称的“四季寓意画”是否同组，均未由当前证据确定。

**English:** This unit records the work group named Le Quattro Stagioni in Carriera’s 1726 account. The entry says it was intended for dispatch to London to Smith; shipment, payment, and identity with the general “Four Seasons” allegories on Chapter 10 p.295 remain unestablished.
