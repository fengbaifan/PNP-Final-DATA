## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡以描述性名称登记Haskell所述、Bernini为Countess Matilda设计的墓葬纪念碑。书中把该墓置于圣彼得大殿右侧走道，并称其主浮雕呈现亨利四世在坎诺莎跪于教皇格里高利七世面前的场景。该段未给墓的正式题名、建造或完成状态、确切日期，也未说明主浮雕的材质或制作者。这里的Countess Matilda人物身份仍未与其他人物KU对齐；墓、主浮雕和邻近段落所述的另一件大理石浮雕是不同对象。

**English:** This card uses a descriptive title for the tomb monument that Haskell says Bernini designed for Countess Matilda. The book places it in the right aisle of St Peter’s Basilica and says its principal relief showed Henry IV kneeling before Pope Gregory VII at Canossa. The passage gives no formal title, construction or completion status, exact date, or material and maker for the principal relief. Countess Matilda is not aligned here to another person KU. The tomb, its principal relief, and the separate marble relief discussed nearby remain distinct objects.
