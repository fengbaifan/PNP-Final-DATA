### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称弗朗索瓦·迪凯努瓦雕刻了一尊 St Andrew 像，并将其置于圣彼得大殿四位圣人壁龛雕像的委托叙述中。卡片采用来源限定的描述性名称，原文没有给出正式题名、确切壁龛或实际安装情况。

**English:** Haskell says François Duquesnoy carved a St Andrew and places the work within his account of the commission for the four saint statues at St Peter’s. This card uses a source-scoped descriptive name; the passage gives no formal title, exact niche, or installation status.
