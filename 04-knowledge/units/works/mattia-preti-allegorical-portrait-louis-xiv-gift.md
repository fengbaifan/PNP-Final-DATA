## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell第七章印刷页189所述、Mattia Preti为Louis XIV绘制的寓意肖像。Haskell称Preti原拟将画作作为礼物赠予Louis XIV，但因法西敌对行动及其在Malta的处境认为此举不审慎，后来将画卖往别处。书中未说礼物已经交付，也未指明买家、出售目的地、出售日期或存世版本。注6引用De Dominici卷IV第50页；该被引页尚未独立查阅。

**English:** This unit records the allegorical portrait of Louis XIV that Haskell says Mattia Preti made. Haskell reports that Preti intended to present it to Louis XIV as a gift, but judged the gesture imprudent because of hostilities between France and Spain and his situation in Malta; Preti later sold the picture elsewhere. The book does not say that the gift was delivered and does not identify a buyer, destination, sale date, or surviving version. Footnote 6 cites De Dominici, vol. IV, p. 50; that cited page has not been independently consulted.
