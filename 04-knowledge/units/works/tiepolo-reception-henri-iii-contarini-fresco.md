## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell第14章所称提埃坡罗的《亨利三世受迎》壁画，并将其与第9章对提埃坡罗绘制Contarini家族迎接亨利三世场景的描写归为同一件作品。两章在创作者、家族、事件与Mira别墅语境上相合；馆方作品记录提供独立的题材与作者身份核对。来源称谓为描述性题名，不主张是经核实的原题。

**English:** This card records the fresco Haskell calls Tiepolo’s Reception of Henri III and identifies it with the work described in Chapter 9 as Tiepolo’s painting of the Contarini welcome of Henri III. The artist, family, event, and Mira villa context align across the two passages, and the museum record independently matches the subject and attribution. The source wording is used as a descriptive title, not asserted as a verified original title.
