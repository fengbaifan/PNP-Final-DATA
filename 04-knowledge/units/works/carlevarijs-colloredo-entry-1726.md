## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔注5称 Carlevarijs 绘制《科洛雷多伯爵入城图》，并将人物称为帝国大使、作品定年1726年；注释报告该画当时位于 Dresden。作品身份以这条书内描述为限。

**English:** Haskell’s note 5 says Carlevarijs painted the Entry of the Count of Colloredo, identifies the Count as an Imperial ambassador, and dates the work to 1726; the note reports the picture in Dresden at the time of its cited source. The work identity is limited to this source-defined description.
