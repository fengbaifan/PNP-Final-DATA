## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 将圣安德烈堂 S. Andrea della Valle 的穹顶壁画归于 Giovanni Lanfranco，并称其于1627年完成。书中描述灯笼中的基督为光源，光线逐渐充满穹顶表面，周围有诸圣、先知、使徒与天使班列。本卡是作品，不是穹顶建筑空间；图名为描述性工作译名。

**English:** Haskell attributes the dome fresco at S. Andrea della Valle to Giovanni Lanfranco and says it was completed in 1627. He describes Christ in the lantern as the source of light filling the dome surface, surrounded by choirs of saints, prophets, apostles, and cherubs. This unit is the fresco, not the architectural dome; its title is descriptive rather than a formal work title.

### 作品信息

- 作品类型：圣安德烈堂穹顶的壁画装饰；不等同于穹顶这一建筑空间。
- 作者归属：Haskell 称为 Lanfranco 的穹顶壁画；本卡按原书表述记录，未作外部归属核验。
- 完成时间：Haskell 记为1627年；该日期随作者引注记载，不据此补出更精确的完工日。
- 可能开工时间：第3章称画中的圣安德鲁·阿韦利诺于1624年列福，“壁画可能在那时开始”；保留作者的可能性表述。
- 构图描述：灯笼中基督为光源，光线向外扩展并充满画面；周围诸圣、殉道者、先知、使徒及 cherubs 合唱赞颂。圣母注视光线，并指向两侧的圣安德鲁、圣加耶塔诺、圣彼得及圣安德鲁·阿韦利诺。人物及集体形象分别作为图像端点处理。
- 比较与影响：Haskell 称该穹顶是帕尔马 Correggio 穹顶之后一百余年间同类穹顶的首例，并称其影响后来的艺术家约一百年；这些是作者的历史评价，不是独立验证的影响范围。第6章另泛称 Lanfranco 在 S. Andrea 的早期壁画已被视为经典，本卡不把这一复数表述缩限为只指本穹顶。

- Work type: a fresco decorating the dome of S. Andrea della Valle; it is not the architectural dome itself.
- Attribution: Haskell identifies the dome fresco as Lanfranco’s; this card records the book’s attribution without external authorship verification.
- Completion: Haskell dates it to 1627; no more precise completion date is inferred.
- Possible start: Chapter 3 says the fresco was “probably begun” when St Andrew Avellino was beatified in 1624; the qualification is retained.
- Composition: Christ in the lantern is described as the source of light spreading across the surface, surrounded by choirs of saints, martyrs, prophets, apostles, and cherubs. The Virgin gazes toward the light and gestures to St Andrew, S. Gaetano, St Peter, and St Andrew Avellino. The depicted figures and group remain distinct iconographic endpoints.
- Comparison and influence: Haskell calls it the first dome of its kind since Correggio’s at Parma more than a century earlier and says it served as a model to artists for the next hundred years. These are the author’s historical assessments, not independently verified measures of influence. Chapter 6 separately refers broadly to Lanfranco’s earlier frescoes at S. Andrea as having come to be regarded as classics; this plural statement is not narrowed to this dome alone.

### 身份边界

中文：本卡指穹顶内的绘画作品，不指 S. Andrea della Valle 教堂或穹顶建筑空间；与 Domenichino 的后殿／tribune 装饰及其横翼帆拱四福音作者组分开。候选 cand-1360 只是索引中的“dome of S. Andrea della Valle”子项，没有 S2 正文锚点，继续保持 undecided，不据此映射本卡。诸圣群 cand-5071、基督 cand-4141及具名圣徒仍是独立图像端点；关系候选待 S6。

English: This unit is the painting inside the dome, not the church or architectural dome space. It is distinct from Domenichino’s tribune decoration and his Evangelists in the pendentives. Index candidate cand-1360, “dome of S. Andrea della Valle,” has no S2 body anchor and remains undecided; it is not mapped here. The choir group cand-5071, Christ cand-4141, and the named saints remain distinct iconographic endpoints; relation candidates are for S6.
