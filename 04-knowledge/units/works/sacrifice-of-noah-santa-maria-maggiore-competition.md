## 内容

### 描述
**中文：** 哈斯克尔将一幅《挪亚献祭》画作描述为圣母玛利亚大殿门上画作竞赛的对象，用以替代 Ciro Ferri 未成功完成的画作。Federico Cervelli 赢得竞赛；原书称他访问贝加莫作出修改后获付370杜卡特。本卡依原书内容标识该画作，不据此断言现存作品、当前地点或外部归属记录。

**English:** Haskell identifies a Sacrifice of Noah painting as the subject of a competition for a door picture at Santa Maria Maggiore, replacing an unsuccessful Ciro Ferri effort. Federico Cervelli won the competition and, after visiting Bergamo to make alterations, was paid 370 ducats. This card follows the source’s identification and does not assert the present survival, location, or an externally verified attribution.

### 名称
- 描述性题名：《挪亚献祭》画作；原书未提供更完整的正式题名。
- 作品语境：1677年竞赛获选对象；与其他同题材作品按来源语境区分。
