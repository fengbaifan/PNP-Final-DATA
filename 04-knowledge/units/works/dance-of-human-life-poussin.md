## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell称为《The Dance of Human Life》的一幅普桑绘画。正文将其列为诠释Rospigliosi道德诗作的三幅画之一；是否受Rospigliosi委托仍不确定。注3报告作品在Wallace Collection，并称其约作于1637年。创作者、收藏位置与年代均限于Haskell书内表述。

**English:** This card records a painting by Nicolas Poussin that Haskell calls *The Dance of Human Life*. He lists it among three paintings interpreting Rospigliosi's morality poems; whether Rospigliosi commissioned it remains uncertain. Note 3 reports it in the Wallace Collection and dates it to about 1637. The creator, collection location, and date are recorded only as Haskell's account.
