## 内容

### 描述

**中文：**《还愿祭坛画》（Pala del Voto）是萨尔维奥·萨维尼为古比奥共同体承诺给守护圣人圣乌巴尔多的祭坛绘制的布面油画。画面上部为圣母子与施洗者圣约翰、圣乌巴尔多，下部表现信使宣告费代里科·乌巴尔多·德拉·罗韦雷出生。指南记载1608年3月25日委托，1610年交付；2022年圣殿指南列其位于还愿小堂。（S1–S2）

**English:** The *Pala del Voto* is an oil-on-canvas altarpiece by Salvio Savini, commissioned by the Gubbio community for the altar it had vowed to its patron saint, Ubaldo. The upper scene shows the Madonna and Child with Saints John the Baptist and Ubaldo; below, a messenger announces the birth of Federico Ubaldo della Rovere. The guide dates the commission to 25 March 1608 and delivery to 1610; a 2022 basilica guide places the painting in the votive chapel. (S1–S2)
