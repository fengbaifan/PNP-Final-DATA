## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所称 Pietro Liberi 为贝加莫 Santa Maria Maggiore 绘制的首幅画布《The Flood》。原书称作品抵达后遭委员会拒绝，Liberi 按要求重绘，随后被安置在教堂南门上方。中文显示名是对 Haskell 英文称谓的描述性译名；原题、现代馆藏身份及当前存放状态未在本卡核实。[第8章 p.217](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md)（L71–72）

**English:** This unit records the first canvas Haskell calls *The Flood*, painted by Pietro Liberi for Santa Maria Maggiore in Bergamo. The account says that the committee rejected the canvas after its arrival; Liberi repainted it as requested, and it was then placed above the church’s south door. The Chinese display name is descriptive. The original title, present-day object identity, and current location have not been independently established here. [第8章 p.217](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md)（L71–72）

### 身份范围与书内记录

- **作品识别：** Haskell identifies this as Liberi’s first canvas in the church commission and names it *The Flood*. This descriptive unit does not assert a verified original title or an externally confirmed surviving painting. [第8章 p.217](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md)（L71–72）
- **委托语境：** The 1660 contract covered sixteen pictures for the church. Its total price and advance payment apply to the whole commission, not solely to this canvas. [第8章 p.217](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md)（L71–72）
- **拒绝与重绘：** Haskell reports that the committee rejected the canvas and demanded a satisfactory repainting. The letter is known here through Haskell’s quotation, not an independent inspection of the document. [第8章 p.217](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md)（L71–72）
- **安置报告：** Haskell says that after repainting, the work was placed above the south door and “still” was there at the time of his account. The passage supplies no exact installation date and does not establish the present location. [第8章 p.217](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md)（L71–72）
