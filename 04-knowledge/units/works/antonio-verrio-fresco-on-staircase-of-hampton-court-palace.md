## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录《赞助人与画家》图版29目录所记的《汉普顿宫楼梯壁画》。第7章将图版29与一幅复杂寓意壁画相连，称其表现William III战胜以法国为首的天主教势力，题材取自Julian the Apostate的著作。此处只记录Haskell的书内描述，不补入外部实物身份。[第7章 p.196](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_iv.md#L59)

**English:** This card records the Fresco on staircase of Hampton Court Palace listed as plate 29 in *Patrons and Painters*. Chapter 7 links plate 29 to a complex allegorical fresco depicting William III’s triumph over the Catholic powers led by France, with a subject drawn from the writings of Julian the Apostate. This records Haskell’s description and adds no external object identification. [Chapter 7, p. 196](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_iv.md#L59)
