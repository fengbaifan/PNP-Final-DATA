## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 莱奥纳迪斯依据提埃坡罗设计制作的《梅塞纳斯向奥古斯都呈献艺术》版画，本书图版68b所列对象。

**English:** A print of Maecenas presenting the Arts to Augustus, made by Leonardis after Tiepolo and listed as plate 68b in Patrons and Painters.
