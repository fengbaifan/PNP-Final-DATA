## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 提埃坡罗于1742–1743年绘制的布面油画，现藏巴黎科涅克—杰博物馆，编号J 104，是墨尔本《克娄巴特拉的宴会》的预备版本。

**English:** An oil painting on canvas by Tiepolo, dated 1742–1743 and held by Musée Cognacq-Jay in Paris as J 104; a preparatory version for The Banquet of Cleopatra in Melbourne.
