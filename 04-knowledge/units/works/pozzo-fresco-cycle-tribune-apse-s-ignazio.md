## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 称 Andrea Pozzo 在1685年后若干年间于圣依纳爵堂的 tribune 与 apse 绘制壁画，表现耶稣会早期历史中的重要事件，并以基督在 La Storta 向圣依纳爵作出的应许为高潮。本卡记录这组叙事壁画及 Haskell 对其风格的评价，不并入同堂另一项拱顶壁画。

**English:** Haskell says Andrea Pozzo painted frescoes in the tribune and apse of S. Ignazio some years after 1685. They represented principal events in the Society’s early history and culminated in Christ’s promise to St Ignatius at La Storta. This unit records that narrative cycle and Haskell’s stylistic assessment, separately from another fresco on the church vault.

### 作品信息

- 对象类型：圣依纳爵堂 tribune 与 apse 内的一组叙事壁画；原书将两处空间分别列出，但本卡只登记壁画组。
- 作者与年代：Haskell 将壁画归于 Andrea Pozzo，称其在1685年后若干年间绘制；未给出确切开工或完工年份。
- 题材：表现耶稣会早期历史中的若干主要事件，并以基督在 La Storta 向圣依纳爵作出的应许为叙事高潮。
- 风格评价：Haskell 认为这些作品在题材与处理上均不同于耶稣堂 Gaulli 壁画，某些方面标志着对 Baroque 的反动；这是作者的风格史判断。

- Object type: a narrative fresco group in the tribune and apse of S. Ignazio. Haskell names the two spaces separately; this card records the paintings, not the rooms.
- Attribution and date: Haskell attributes the frescoes to Andrea Pozzo and dates them to some years after 1685; he gives no exact start or completion year.
- Subject: the pictures represent principal events in the early history of the Society and culminate in Christ’s promise to St Ignatius at La Storta.
- Stylistic assessment: Haskell says they differ in subject and treatment from Gaulli’s frescoes in the Gesù and, in some ways, mark a reaction against the Baroque. This is the author’s art-historical judgment.

### 身份边界

中文：本卡是 tribune 与 apse 中的壁画组，不是这两处建筑空间。既有[圣依纳爵堂拱顶壁画](pozzo-fresco-vault-s-ignazio.md)及其模型[波佐圣依纳爵堂拱顶壁画模型](andrea-pozzo-modello-for-fresco-on-vault-of-s-ignazio.md)属于不同作品及媒介，不并入本组；Gaulli 在耶稣堂的壁画亦为比较对象。La Storta 应许属于画面叙事内容；本卡不把其地点、基督显现或圣依纳爵另行折入作品身份。

English: This unit is the fresco group in the tribune and apse, not those architectural spaces. The existing [Fresco on the Vault of S. Ignazio](pozzo-fresco-vault-s-ignazio.md) and [Pozzo’s modello for the vault fresco](andrea-pozzo-modello-for-fresco-on-vault-of-s-ignazio.md) are different works and media and are not included; Gaulli’s Gesù frescoes are a comparison. The La Storta promise is narrative content and does not merge its place, Christ’s apparition, or St Ignatius into the identity of this work.
