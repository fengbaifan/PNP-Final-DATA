## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔所称贝尔尼尼曾处理的Duplex Intercessio构图，并作为Gaulli后来改编对象。来源未识别或定位存世图稿；本卡限于构图身份，不推断具体实物、收藏或年代，并与Gaulli最终用于Gesù穹顶的构图分开。

**English:** This card records the Duplex Intercessio composition that Haskell says Bernini had treated and that Gaulli later adapted. The source does not identify or locate a surviving drawing. This card is limited to the composition identity, makes no claim about a specific object, collection, or date, and keeps it distinct from Gaulli’s final composition for the Gesù dome.
