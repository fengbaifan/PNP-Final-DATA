## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔第10章印刷页316注2转引 Zimmermann pp. 199–203，将一件称作 “Sala del Maggior Consiglio” 的画作归于 Gianantonio 与 Francesco Guardi。现有 S2 校读记录采用印本名，OCR 作“Maxtor”；本批未重新核看页图。此卡记录来源所指作品，不将其与同名大议会厅地点合并，也不声称已确认作品的外部目录身份、年代或现藏地点。

**English:** Haskell’s Chapter 10, p. 316 n. 2, cites Zimmermann, pp. 199–203, for a painting referred to as “Sala del Maggior Consiglio” and attributed to Gianantonio and Francesco Guardi. The existing S2 collation follows the printed name while the OCR reads “Maxtor”; the page image was not rechecked in this batch. This card records the source-referred work, keeps it distinct from the same-named hall, and does not establish a catalogue identity, date, or present location.
