## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录连接圣依纳爵生前居所小室的走廊装饰，而非走廊建筑空间本身。Haskell 称 Giacomo Borgognone 开始了这项装饰工作，但在其去世时尚未完成；随后 Andrea Pozzo 被要求绘制走廊，Haskell 又称“艺术家”将墙壁与拱顶划分为由框景和 putti 隔开的场景，并描绘圣依纳爵此世与来世的经历。原书没有说明最终完成范围或现存状态。[第3章 p. 89](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md#L131-L132)

**English:** This unit records the decoration of the corridor linking the small rooms where St Ignatius had lived, rather than the corridor’s architecture. Haskell says Giacomo Borgognone began the work but left it incomplete at his death. Andrea Pozzo was later required to paint the corridor; Haskell then says the artist divided its walls and vault into scenes framed by putti and depicted aspects of Ignatius’s sojourn in this world and the next. The book does not state the final extent of completion or the work’s survival status. [Chapter 3, p. 89](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md#L131-L132)

### 作品边界与创作过程

- 装饰作品与走廊建筑空间分立；本卡不把空间本身作为作品。
- Haskell 将 Borgognone 的初始工作、去世时未完成的状态，以及 Pozzo 后续的分场与绘制记作不同阶段。来源没有给出完整创作者名单或完成日期。[第3章 p. 89](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md#L131-L132)
- 框景 putti 是场景之间的装饰母题，不在本卡中拆成独立人物或作品。
