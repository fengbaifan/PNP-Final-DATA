## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 记载 Oliva 于1672年8月与 Gaulli 签订合同，委托其绘制并装饰耶稣堂穹顶、主拱顶及横翼拱顶。原约将穹顶与其余绘饰分列工期，并排除预留给 Borgognone 的后殿拱顶。本卡表示这项整体装饰委托及其约定范围，不指单幅壁画，也不表示所有项目均按期完成。

**English:** Haskell reports that Oliva and Gaulli signed a contract in August 1672 for the decoration of the Gesù’s dome, main vault, and transept vaults. The contract set separate deadlines for the dome and the remaining work and excluded the tribune vault reserved for Borgognone. This unit records the overall decoration commission and its agreed scope, not an individual fresco or proof that every element was completed on schedule.

### 作品信息

- 对象类型：耶稣堂内部穹顶、主拱顶及横翼拱顶的大型绘饰工程；本卡按原书所述整体委托范围登记。
- 委托语境与日期：Haskell 称 Oliva 与 Gaulli 于1672年8月签订合同。
- 合同范围：穹顶及其灯笼、帆拱；含窗龛的主拱顶；以及圣依纳爵和圣方济各·沙勿略礼拜堂上方的横翼拱顶。后殿拱顶预留给 Borgognone，不在此约范围内。
- 工期：穹顶约定两年内完成，其余绘画、镀金和灰泥工程约定于八年内、即1682年底前完成。Haskell 另记穹顶于1675年4月已就绪，主拱顶在此后四年完成；不将合同期限写成所有部分的实绩日期。
- 合同条件：Gaulli 自付绘画和镀金费用；若专家认为作品不够完善，他承诺免费修正。顺利完成时约定报酬为14,000 scudi，脚手架等费用另计。
- 展示评价：Haskell 记受邀专家对私人预展不热衷，而公众赞赏绘画与灰泥组合；这是作者报告的不同反应。

- Object type: an extensive decorative programme for the Gesù’s dome, main vault, and transept vaults; this card records the overall commission as Haskell describes it.
- Commission context and date: Haskell says Oliva and Gaulli signed a contract in August 1672.
- Contracted scope: the dome with its lantern and pendentives; the main vault including window recesses; and the transept vaults above the chapels of St Ignatius and St Francis Xavier. The tribune vault was reserved for Borgognone and excluded from this contract.
- Schedule: the dome was due within two years; the remaining painting, gilding, and stucco work was due within eight years, by the end of 1682. Haskell separately reports that the dome was ready by April 1675 and the main vault four years later; the contractual deadline is not treated as a completion date for every element.
- Contract terms: Gaulli was to pay the painting and gilding expenses and correct work judged imperfect by experts without charge. If all went well, his fee was 14,000 scudi, with scaffolding and related expenses additional.
- Reception: Haskell reports that invited experts were not enthusiastic at a private showing, while the public admired the painting and stucco arrangement; these are the author’s reports of differing reactions.

English: This unit records the decorative work defined by the 1672 contract, not the contract document itself; archive candidate cand-5227 remains a distinct archive object, pending S4 registration and later relation review. The existing [Gaulli’s vault fresco project at the Gesù](gaulli-gesu-vaults.md) records the nave and transept vault work and its eight-year allowance as described in Chapter 1; it does not include the dome and its separately stated two-year deadline listed here. The two work identities are therefore kept distinct, and the larger commission is not treated as one individual fresco. Plate 14, Triumph of the Name of Jesus, is a separate nave fresco; the tribune vault and Courtois’s earlier proposal are also excluded.
