## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell注1所转述的拍卖目录题名“A pair of Triumphal Mausoleums”。中文“一对凯旋陵墓”为描述性译名，不是经核定的正式中文题名。归属形式Petoni仅见于Haskell转述Sir Anthony Blunt所述拍卖目录；原目录未独立查阅，不把该拼写与Paltronieri或Pittoni合并。

**English:** This card records the sale catalogue title “A pair of Triumphal Mausoleums” as Haskell reports it in note 1. The Chinese display name is descriptive, not an established formal Chinese title. The attribution form “Petoni” is reported second-hand by Haskell from Sir Anthony Blunt; the catalogue was not independently consulted, and the name is not identified with Paltronieri or Pittoni.
