## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 将 Joseph Smith 为宣扬帕拉第奥及其建筑原则而策划的门上画委托描述为连续系列：Canaletto 于1744年绘制十三幅门上画，离开威尼斯后 Smith 又请 Visentini 延续系列建筑画，并由 Zuccarelli 添加装饰风景。本卡登记系列与委托计划，不把不同阶段画作合成一幅作品，也不推定系列总件数。

**English:** Haskell describes Joseph Smith’s overdoor commission, intended to glorify Palladio and his architectural principles, as a continuing series: Canaletto painted thirteen door pieces in 1744, and after he left Venice Smith had Visentini continue the architectural views, with Zuccarelli adding decorative landscapes. This unit records the series and commission, not a single painting or an inferred total number of works.

### 作品信息

- 对象类型：为门上装饰设计的一组建筑画，包含1744年及1746年后的委托阶段。
- 委托语境：第20章后记称其为 Smith 精心设计的委托，旨在彰显帕拉第奥及其建筑原则；这是 Haskell 对系列意图的概括。
- 1744年阶段：Canaletto 绘制十三幅以帕拉第奥主要建筑为题的门上画；Haskell 同时指出并非所有画中建筑都出自帕拉第奥。
- 后续阶段：Canaletto 离开后，Smith 转请 Visentini 延续系列。Haskell 描述十一幅后续建筑画取材于英国乡间别墅：五幅涉及 Inigo Jones，两幅分别涉及 Burlington 与 Colen Campbell，另有 Roger Morris 和 Vanbrugh 各一幅。
- 分工及形式：Zuccarelli 为 Visentini 的建筑画添加威尼斯式装饰风景；原书称其参与较次要。后续画作主要依据 Colen Campbell《Vitruvius Britannicus》的版画，建筑细节大多准确，部分画面组合彼此分离的建筑。

- Object type: a series of architectural pictures designed as overdoors, with commission phases described in 1744 and after 1746.
- Commission context: the postscript calls it a carefully devised commission by Smith to glorify Palladio and his architectural principles; this is Haskell’s characterization of its purpose.
- 1744 phase: Canaletto painted thirteen door pieces depicting Palladio’s principal buildings. Haskell notes that not all buildings represented were by Palladio.
- Later phase: after Canaletto’s departure, Smith turned to Visentini to continue the series. Haskell describes eleven later architectural pictures based on English country houses: five portray works by Inigo Jones; two each by Burlington and Colen Campbell; and one each by Roger Morris and Vanbrugh.
- Division and style: Zuccarelli added decorative Venetian landscapes to Visentini’s architectural views; Haskell calls his role minor. The later pictures were based primarily on engravings from Colen Campbell’s Vitruvius Britannicus, their architectural details were mostly accurate, and some combined geographically separate buildings.

### 身份边界

中文：本卡登记系列层级。1744年 Canaletto 的十三幅门上画与1746年后 Visentini、Zuccarelli 参与的十一幅后续建筑画是不同阶段和贡献，不合成单幅作品；现有 S2 证据不支持逐件目录、全部存世情况或系列总件数。后记称 William Barcham 分析过此委托，但该研究未在本轮独立查阅，不据其增补内容。索引候选 cand-2785、cand-2883 仍待证其艺术家子项是否代表可独立识别作品；cand-2453 的“委托”事件索引项因缺少对应 S2 身份锚点保持 undecided，均不强行并入本卡。

English: This card records the series level. Canaletto’s thirteen 1744 door pieces and the eleven later architectural pictures involving Visentini and Zuccarelli are distinct phases and contributions, not one individual work; the available S2 evidence does not support a complete item list, survival account, or total series count. The postscript says William Barcham analyzed the commission, but that study was not independently consulted here and no details are added from it. Index candidates cand-2785 and cand-2883 remain pending as to whether the artists’ subentries denote separately identifiable works; commissioning-event candidate cand-2453 also remains undecided because it lacks a corresponding S2 identity anchor. They are not forced into this card.
