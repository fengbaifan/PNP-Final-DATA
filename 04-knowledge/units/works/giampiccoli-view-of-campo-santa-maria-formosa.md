## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第10章 p.327 所述一幅圣玛丽亚·福尔莫萨广场景观版画，中文题名为描述性工作译名，原书未给正式题名。书中称 Giorgio Pisani 委托 Giuliano Giampiccoli 制作该景观；版画基部有赞颂 Pisani 的拉丁题铭，随后因自我宣传和自由言论相关措辞被视为危险而仓促撤下。原书未识别存世印张、版本、制作日期或具体宫邸；题铭释义保留为哈斯克尔的转述，未经独立翻译核验。

**English:** This unit records the view-print of Campo di S. Maria Formosa described on p. 327 of Haskell's chapter 10; the displayed title is descriptive, not a title supplied by the book. Haskell says Giorgio Pisani commissioned the view from Giuliano Giampiccoli. A Latin inscription at the base praised Pisani, and Haskell says the print was hurriedly withdrawn after its self-advertisement and references to public liberty were considered dangerous. The source identifies no surviving impression, edition, production date, or particular palace; the inscription's meaning is retained as Haskell's account, not an independently verified translation.
