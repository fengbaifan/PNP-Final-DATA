## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记 Haskell 在第4章 p.113 称为《The Tempest with Pyramus and Thisbe》的作品。Haskell 将其归于普桑，并报告该画与《绘画论》中风暴段落有关；有关杜盖风景的可能影响仍保持“或许”限定。

**English:** This card records the work Haskell calls The Tempest with Pyramus and Thisbe on p.113. Haskell attributes it to Poussin and reports a connection to the storm passage in the Trattato; the possible influence of Dughet’s landscapes remains qualified as “perhaps.”
