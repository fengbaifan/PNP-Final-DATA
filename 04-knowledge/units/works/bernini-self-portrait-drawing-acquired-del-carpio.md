## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell第七章印刷页191所述的一幅Bernini自画像素描。Haskell称Del Carpio在Bernini的工作室从其子Domenico处取得该画。原书没有提供作品题名、日期或可对应的现存版本；本卡仅登记这一书内描述的单件作品，不指认任何已知馆藏对象。

**English:** This unit records one of Bernini’s self-portrait drawings described on page 191 of Haskell’s chapter 7. Haskell says Del Carpio acquired it from Bernini’s son Domenico during a visit to the sculptor’s studio. The book supplies no title, date, or identifiable surviving version; this record is limited to the single work described in the source and does not identify a known collection object.
