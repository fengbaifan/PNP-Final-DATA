## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔叙述的谷地圣安德烈堂祭坛席与穹顶装饰项目。约1616年，枢机 Montalto 曾承诺把整个祭坛席与穹顶交由 Lanfranco；后来 Domenichino 争取并获得装饰工作，Montalto 又坚持至少将穹顶交给 Lanfranco，最终两位画家分担委托。哈斯克尔称两人至1628年已完成各自任务。此记录呈现委托范围和分配变化，不把初始承诺写成最终安排，也不把整项委托等同于 Lanfranco 的单件穹顶壁画。哈斯克尔称圣安德烈堂装饰为当时最重要的巴洛克成就，这是作者的评价。[第3章 p.72](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L76–81）

**English:** This unit records Haskell’s account of the decoration project for the tribune and dome of S. Andrea della Valle. Around 1616, Cardinal Montalto promised the entire tribune and dome to Lanfranco; Domenichino later sought and received the decorative commission, after which Montalto insisted that Lanfranco receive at least the dome. The two painters ultimately shared the commission, and Haskell says both had completed their respective tasks by 1628. This record preserves the changing scope and allocation: the initial promise is not treated as the final arrangement, and the broader commission is not equated with Lanfranco’s individual dome fresco. Haskell calls the church’s decoration the most important Baroque achievement to that point; this is the author’s evaluation. [第3章 p.72](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L76–81）
