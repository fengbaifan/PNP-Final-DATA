## 内容

### 作品身份

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第4章印刷页118所列的《珀耳修斯与珊瑚起源的海景》。该段称Claude在1673年和1674年为Massimi创作两幅大型画作，但没有把其中某一年分配给本画。作者归属仅按书内叙述记录，未作外部身份核验。

**English:** This unit records A Coast View with Perseus and the Origin of Coral, named on p. 118 of Haskell’s chapter 4. Haskell says Claude painted two large pictures for Massimi in 1673 and 1674, but does not assign either year to this individual picture. The creator attribution is recorded as reported in the book and has not been independently verified.
