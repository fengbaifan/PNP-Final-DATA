## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应哈斯克尔所述 Angelo Querini 委托 Antonio Canova 为其友人 Paolo Renier 制作的胸像。作者称 Renier 于1779年成为总督后，后来与自己曾反对的势力妥协，Querini 因失望而把胸像砸向园中复仇女神祭坛。书下注又转述一件可能为此胸像的陶土像据称存于帕多瓦市立博物馆，并提到其后续用途的传闻；这些识别与保管说法在本项目中未核实。

**English:** This card represents the bust Angelo Querini commissioned from Antonio Canova of his friend Paolo Renier. Haskell says that after Renier became Doge in 1779 and later compromised with forces he had once opposed, Querini, disappointed, flung the bust against the Altar of Furies in his garden. A note then reports that a terracotta bust thought to be this object was said to survive in the Museo Civico at Padua and mentions a story about its later use; these identifications and custody claims remain unverified in this project.
