## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 在祭坛画委托叙述中称为“other paintings”的未具名单件作品组。原文另列 Domenichino、Lanfranco、Pietro da Cortona、Baccio Ciarpi 与 Andrea Sacchi 的贡献，但没有给出这些作品的具体题名、数量或逐件作者对应。

**English:** This card records the unidentified group Haskell calls “other paintings” in the altar-picture commission narrative. He also names Domenichino, Lanfranco, Pietro da Cortona, Baccio Ciarpi, and Andrea Sacchi as contributors, but supplies no titles, count, or individual work-to-maker assignments.

### 本章相关内容

该未识别作品组是祭坛画委托叙述中区别于 Reni 具名作品的“other paintings”。来源虽点名多位贡献者，但没有把特定画作逐件对应给某一人；不把此组改称“后代画家作品”或扩展为完整清单。

This unidentified group is the “other paintings” distinguished from Reni’s named work within the commission narrative. Although several contributors are named, Haskell does not assign particular pictures to individual makers. This card does not recast the group as a later-generation set or as a complete inventory.
