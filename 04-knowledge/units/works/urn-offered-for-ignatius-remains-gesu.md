## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所述、被提供给耶稣会士以供安放圣依纳爵遗骨的瓮，并保留其归于 Alessandro Algardi 的来源说法。原文未说明耶稣会士是否接受，也未交代瓮后来所在、遗骨是否实际安放其中或该器物现存状况。[第3章 p.91](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md)

**English:** This unit records the urn that Haskell says was offered to the Jesuits for St Ignatius's remains, preserving the source's attribution to Alessandro Algardi. The passage does not say whether the offer was accepted, where the urn later went, whether the remains were placed in it, or whether the object survives. [Chapter 3, p. 91](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md)

### 本章相关内容

Haskell称，遗骨移入小堂十五年后，耶稣会士获提供一只由 Algardi 造型的瓮。该相对时间沿用原文，不换算为精确年份；提供者、接受情况及器物后续均未说明。

**English:** Haskell places the offer fifteen years after the remains were moved to the chapel. This relative interval is retained without conversion to a calendar year; the offerer, acceptance, and later history are not identified.
