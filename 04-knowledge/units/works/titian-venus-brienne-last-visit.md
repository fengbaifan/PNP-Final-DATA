### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Brienne回忆中Mazarin告别时提到的“Titian的Venus”。引述将作品归于Titian，但没有指出版本、确切题名或收藏编号；不与前页所述Venus del Pardo合并。

**English:** This card records the “Venus of Titian” mentioned in Brienne’s account of Mazarin’s farewell. The quoted wording attributes the painting to Titian but gives no version, exact title, or collection number. It is not merged with the Venus del Pardo mentioned on the preceding page.
