## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell称为Carpaccio《圣乌尔苏拉生平》场景的对象组。Haskell将这些场景称为Carpaccio的著名场景，并说Giovanni del Pian据此制作的九幅大版画以其为题材。该句未逐幅指认场景，也未提供各场景的独立题名；作者归属仅记录Haskell的表述。

**English:** This card records the group of scenes that Haskell refers to as Carpaccio’s scenes from the Life of St Ursula and identifies as the subject of Giovanni del Pian’s nine large prints. The passage does not identify or title the scenes individually; the attribution records Haskell’s wording only.
