## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本知识元记录 Haskell 第8章 p.215 所述贝加莫 Santa Maria Maggiore 教堂穹顶。Haskell称建筑师 Francesco Maria Ricchini 于1612年规划该穹顶；书页另称穹顶内部后来覆有灰泥装饰。本卡只记录穹顶这一建筑构件，不把规划时间等同于实际建造或完工时间。

**English:** This unit records the dome of Santa Maria Maggiore in Bergamo, as described on p. 215 of Haskell’s Chapter 8. Haskell says that the architect Francesco Maria Ricchini planned the dome in 1612; the page separately reports later stucco ornamentation over its interior. This unit refers to the architectural component and does not treat the planning date as a construction or completion date.
