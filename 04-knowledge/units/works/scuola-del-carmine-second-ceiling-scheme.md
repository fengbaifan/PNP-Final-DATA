## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所述 Tiepolo 为 Scuola del Carmine 主厅装饰提出的第二套方案。两种方案共用拟置于天顶中央的圣母像：圣母与先知以利亚、以利沙及天使同在，并将肩衣交给圣西门·斯托克。第二套方案把周围画布设想为启迪兄弟会成员的德性形象，包括信、望、爱及其他未具名德性。Haskell 称第二方案被选中并经少量修改后实施。此卡登记的是书内可辨认的设计对象，不把其内容逐项等同于存世画作，也不重建未详述的最终配置。[第9章 p.273](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md)（L67）

**English:** This card records the second scheme that Haskell says Tiepolo proposed for decorating the main room of the Scuola del Carmine. Both alternatives shared a planned central ceiling panel showing the Virgin with Elijah, Elisha, and angels handing the scapular to St Simeon Stock. In the second scheme, the surrounding canvases were to represent virtues that should inspire the Brethren, including Faith, Hope, Charity, and other unnamed virtues. Haskell says this second plan was selected and carried out with a few modifications. This unit identifies the design described in the book; it does not equate each planned subject with a surviving canvas or reconstruct the unspecified final arrangement. [第9章 p.273](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md)（L67）

### 本章相关内容

第一方案的周围画布表现兄弟会成员获救的特权，第二方案则改为德性主题；两个提案共用中央构图。Haskell 另称 Tiepolo 对德性的描述取自 Ripa 的图像志手册插图，并以“must have known by heart”作作者推断。本卡保留方案的书内语境，不据此补写外部作者关系或存世作品身份。[第9章 p.273](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md)（L67）
