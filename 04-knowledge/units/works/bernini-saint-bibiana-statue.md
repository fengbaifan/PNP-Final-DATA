## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称Bernini在S. Bibiana教堂重建后制作了圣比比亚娜像，并称其原定安置在主祭坛后。这里的安置是原定位置，不证明作品最终或现存于该处。雕像、圣人、主祭坛和教堂分别指向不同对象；本卡不作外部身份或作者归属核验。

**English:** Haskell says Bernini made a statue of Saint Bibiana after the rebuilding of S. Bibiana and that it was intended to stand behind the High Altar. This is an intended placement, not evidence of its final or present location. The statue, saint, altar, and church are distinct objects. The work’s identity and attribution have not been checked against external records.

### 本章相关内容

Haskell将该像置于教堂重建后的叙述，并称Bernini收到与该像有关的第一笔款项。原文没有给出付款人、金额和确切付款日期。
