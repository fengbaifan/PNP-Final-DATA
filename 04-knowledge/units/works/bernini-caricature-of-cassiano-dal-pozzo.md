**中文：** 贝尔尼尼描绘卡西亚诺·达尔·波佐的纸本笔绘棕墨漫画肖像，由博伊曼斯—范伯宁恩博物馆保管。

**English:** A caricature portrait of Cassiano dal Pozzo drawn by Bernini in pen and brown ink on paper, held by Museum Boijmans Van Beuningen.
