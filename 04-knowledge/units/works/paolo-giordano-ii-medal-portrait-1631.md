## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 在谈及保罗·焦尔达诺二世时提到的1631年肖像奖章。原文称“we see him once more”于奖章中出现，语境将被表现者指向 Paolo Giordano II。Haskell称奖章“superbly spirited”，并以“perhaps”表示可能由 Giulio della Greca 制作；此制作归属尚非确定事实。该奖章与随后提及的 Paolo 及其妻 Isabella 大理石胸像组为不同对象。[第4章 p.97](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_i.md)（L28；PDF物理页4）

**English:** This card records the 1631 medal Haskell mentions while discussing Paolo Giordano II. The phrase “we see him once more” in the medal identifies Paolo as its represented subject in context. Haskell calls it “superbly spirited” and qualifies Giulio della Greca as its possible maker with “perhaps”; that attribution is not treated as certain. This medal is distinct from the marble busts of Paolo and his wife Isabella mentioned later in the passage. [第4章 p.97](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_i.md)（L28；PDF物理页4）
