## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell所述、由Algarotti取得的《亨利三世受迎》壁画modello。模型稿与壁画是两个不同作品对象；本书没有指出模型稿的制作者或其现存地点。

**English:** This card records the modello of the Reception of Henri III fresco that Haskell says Algarotti obtained. The modello and fresco are distinct work entities; the book does not identify the modello’s maker or present location.
