## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell将《The Last Supper》列为 Portland 礼拜堂四幅画作之一。本卡标识的是 programme 中的画作；第10章注3另提一幅《The Last Supper》素描，两者须保持区分。原书未说明该画的现址、存世或完成情况。

**English:** Haskell lists The Last Supper as one of four paintings for Portland’s chapel. This card identifies the painting in the programme; note 3 separately mentions a sketch for The Last Supper, which remains distinct. The book does not state the painting’s present location, survival, or completion status.

正文将此题名列入四画组；注3所称华盛顿国家美术馆素描是独立草图，不能作为最终礼拜堂画作现藏的证据。

The title appears in the four-painting group in the text. The sketch reported in note 3 at the National Gallery of Art in Washington is a separate drawing and is not evidence for the final chapel painting’s present custody.
