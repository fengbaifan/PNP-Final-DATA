## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称奎里尼拥有大量埃及人物像。此卡保留来源的复数描述，不指定数量、单件身份、材质或现藏位置，也不将其等同于某一完整馆藏。

**English:** Haskell says Querini owned a large number of Egyptian figures. This unit preserves the source’s plural description without specifying a count, individual identities, material, or present location, and does not equate the group with a complete collection.
