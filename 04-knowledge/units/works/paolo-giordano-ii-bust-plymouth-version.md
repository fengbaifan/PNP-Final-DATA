## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell称作前句半身像“另一版本”的作品。原书称该版本位于普利茅斯City Museum and Art Gallery，并由Anthony Radcliffe于1978、1979年讨论；本批未查阅被引研究或馆藏目录。它与Haskell所述Cyril Humphris持有版本分开建档；除原书明确的版本关系外，不推断两件实物的制作关系或其后流转。

**English:** This card records the work Haskell calls “another version” of the preceding bust. He says this version was at the City Museum and Art Gallery, Plymouth, and was discussed by Anthony Radcliffe in 1978 and 1979; the cited studies and collection catalogue were not consulted in this batch. It is recorded separately from the version Haskell says came into Cyril Humphris’s possession. Beyond the version relationship stated in the source, no production relationship between the physical objects or later movement is inferred.
