## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本作品项记录威尼斯 Pietà 教堂的建筑立面。哈斯克尔说，若 Massari 知道该立面还要约150年才完成，他会更加懊恼；原文没有给出确切完工年份，本卡不据此推算日期或现状。

**English:** This work unit records the architectural façade of the Pietà church in Venice. Haskell remarks that Massari would have been more rueful had he known the façade would take another 150 years to complete. The passage supplies no exact completion year, and this card does not infer one or claim a current state.

### 本章相关内容

The façade is kept distinct from the church as a place. The formal part-of relation records only that the façade belongs to the church building; the reported delay remains a source-qualified statement.
