## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell注5所述Canaletto于1725年绘制的《德·热尔吉伯爵入城图》。原书注5引用Haskell 1956 p.298，报告作品“now in the Hermitage”；本卡将其作为来源所载位置事实记录，不声称2026年现址已核验，也未查阅被引的1956年版本。原书未提供Comte de Gergy的名字或作品委托事实；本卡不补造人物身份或委托关系。该画与Count of Bolagno入城图（1729）及Carlevarijs绘制的Count of Colloredo入城图（1726）分别识别。

**English:** This card records the Entry of the Comte de Gergy, which Haskell’s note 5 says Canaletto painted in 1725. The note cites Haskell 1956, p. 298, for the report that the painting was “now in the Hermitage”; this card records that source-reported location without claiming a verified 2026 location or independent consultation of the cited 1956 edition. The book does not give the Comte de Gergy’s first name or state that he commissioned the painting; no personal identity or commission is inferred. This work remains distinct from the Count of Bolagno entry (1729) and Carlevarijs’s Count of Colloredo entry (1726).
