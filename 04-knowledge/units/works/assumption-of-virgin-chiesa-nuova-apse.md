## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔所称Pietro绘于耶稣新堂后殿的《圣母升天》。原文还称该作品受到多位圣徒称颂，并以圣菲利普·内里为其中最显著者。本卡将作品与“圣母升天”这一题材或奉献称谓区分；不把原文对圣徒的称颂改写为人物必然出现在画面中的断言，也不补写精确年代、存世状态或当前馆藏。

**English:** This unit records the Assumption of the Virgin that Haskell says Pietro painted in the apse of the Chiesa Nuova. The source also says that a number of saints acclaimed the work, with St Philip Neri the most prominent among them. This work remains distinct from the subject or dedication “Assumption of the Virgin”; the source’s statement about acclaim is not rewritten as a claim that these figures appear in the painting. No exact date, survival claim, or present collection is supplied.
