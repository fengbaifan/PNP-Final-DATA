**English:** This card records Haskell's named Moses striking the Rock. He identifies it as one of four large Old Testament scenes produced by Ottavio Vannini for the Del Rosso family house and describes the four as among Vannini's finest works. The attribution is limited to Haskell's account; no independent catalogue or surviving object has been verified. Haskell also reports that this picture was published in R. Longhi's 1956 article; that article was not independently consulted. The source gives no present location or identifiable surviving version.
