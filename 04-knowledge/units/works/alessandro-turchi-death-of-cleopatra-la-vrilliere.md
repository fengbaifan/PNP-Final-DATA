## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第7章所列、归于亚历山德罗·图尔基的《克娄巴特拉之死》。原书将其列入 La Vrillière 在 1640 年代增添的一组画作；本对象与 Guido Cagnacci 的同名图版作品分别识别，未确定具体版本或现藏地点。

**English:** This unit records *The Death of Cleopatra* attributed to Alessandro Turchi in Haskell’s Chapter 7. The book lists it among paintings added by La Vrillière in the 1640s. It is distinguished from Guido Cagnacci’s work of the same title illustrated in the book; a specific version and present location have not been established.
