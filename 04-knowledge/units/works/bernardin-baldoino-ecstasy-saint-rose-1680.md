## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Bernardin Baldoino创作的《利马圣罗撒神魂超拔》，背面有签名并纪年1680；研究将其与《圣罗撒与圣母的神秘交谈》列为一对，并称二者位于尼斯圣雷帕拉塔主教座堂。（S1）

**English:** *The Ecstasy of Saint Rose of Lima* by Bernardin Baldoino, signed and dated 1680 on the reverse. The study pairs it with *The Mystical Conversation of Saint Rose with the Virgin* and locates both paintings in Nice Cathedral. (S1)
