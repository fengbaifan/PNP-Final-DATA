## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本作品项记录哈斯克尔第9章 p.272 注3所述、题材为圣路易吉·贡扎加荣耀的构图模型。该注转述1959年 Seilern 目录第170号，并报告 Wilde 的意见：它可能是为1726年封圣而作，且可能拟作一幅未实现祭坛画的草图。被引目录与 Wilde 的著作均未在本批独立查阅；原文的推测语气保留，不把假说写成已证用途。

**English:** This work unit records the modello for the Glory of S. Luigi Gonzaga mentioned in Haskell's Chapter 9, p.272 note 3. The note cites no. 170 of a 1959 Seilern catalogue and reports Wilde's suggestion that it may have been made for the 1726 canonization and may have been intended as a sketch for an unrealized altarpiece. Neither cited work was independently consulted in this batch; the hypothesis remains qualified rather than an established purpose.

### 本章相关内容

The work is distinct from Tiepolo's Pietà dome painting. Haskell's cited catalogue attribution does not yet support a formal ownership edge because Count Seilern remains an unresolved person endpoint.
