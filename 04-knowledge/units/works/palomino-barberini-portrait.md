## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell正文所述的Francesco可能肖像，以及注5所转述的Palomino关于Antonio Barberini肖像的说法。Haskell推断Palomino可能混淆了两位枢机；原书没有确认肖像实际绘成，Velasquez是否为创作者仍待证。

**English:** This card records the possible portrait of Francesco in Haskell’s narrative together with Palomino’s reported account of a portrait of Antonio Barberini in note 5. Haskell infers that Palomino may have confused the two cardinals. The book does not confirm that the portrait was executed, and Velasquez’s authorship remains unresolved.
