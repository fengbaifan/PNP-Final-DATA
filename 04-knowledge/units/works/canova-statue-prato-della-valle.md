**English:** This card uses a descriptive working title for the statue of Antonio Canova mentioned in Chapter 15. Haskell says Antonio Cappello commissioned it from Giovanni Ferrari and that, “within fifteen years,” it found a place among the statues of heroes at Prà della Valle in Padua, as the only contemporary so honoured.
