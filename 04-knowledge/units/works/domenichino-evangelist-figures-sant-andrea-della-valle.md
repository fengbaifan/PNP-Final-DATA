## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所述 Domenichino 在 S. Andrea della Valle 穹隅制作的四位纪念性福音书作者像，按来源共同描述保留为一个作品组，不拆分为四件作品，也不把描述性名称当作原题。Haskell 称这些形象“明显源自 Michelangelo”；这是作者的艺术史判断，本卡不将其表述为已独立验证的影响事实。[第3章 p.72](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L80；PDF物理页10）

**English:** This unit records the group of four monumental Evangelist figures that Haskell says Domenichino produced in the pendentives of S. Andrea della Valle. The figures remain one source-defined group rather than four separate works, and the descriptive title is not treated as an original title. Haskell describes them as “clearly derived from Michelangelo”; this is the author's art-historical interpretation, not an independently verified influence claim. [第3章 p.72](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L80；PDF物理页10）
