## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 巴托尼1737年创作的布面油画，由威尼斯驻教廷大使马尔科·福斯卡里尼委托；后入藏北卡罗来纳艺术博物馆。

**English:** An oil painting made by Batoni in 1737 for Marco Foscarini, the Venetian ambassador to the Holy See, and later acquired by the North Carolina Museum of Art.
