## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔所述的一组巨型半身像，来源分别将两尊像指认为伊壁鸠鲁和福基翁。该描述性暂题不表示作品有独立原题；来源未给出创作者、制作年代或现今地点。

**English:** This unit records the paired colossal busts that Haskell reports and identifies as Epicurus and Phocion. The descriptive title is not presented as an independent original title. The source gives no maker, date, or present location.
