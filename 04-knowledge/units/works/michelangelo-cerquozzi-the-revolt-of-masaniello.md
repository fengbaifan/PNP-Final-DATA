## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 切尔科齐受维尔吉利奥·斯帕达委托绘制的布面油画，描绘1647年那不勒斯起义，藏于斯帕达美术馆。

**English:** An oil painting commissioned from Cerquozzi by Virgilio Spada, depicting the 1647 uprising in Naples and held by Galleria Spada.
