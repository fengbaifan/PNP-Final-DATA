## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔所述Memmo家族宫邸中的壁画廊饰项目：以古典演说家、诗人与哲学家为表现对象，并在各自基座上题写姓名。来源将装饰行为归于Andrea Memmo，但未指明画家、确切年代或现存状况。本卡指装饰作品，不把宫邸空间本身合并为作品。

**English:** This unit records the frescoed gallery decoration in the Memmo family palace described by Haskell. Its subjects are classical orators, poets, and philosophers, with each name inscribed on a pedestal. The source attributes the act of having the palace decorated to Andrea Memmo but names no painter or exact date and gives no survival status. This unit denotes the decorative work, not the palace space itself.
