### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所称《The Archangel Michael trampling on the Devil》这件祭坛画。原文称有人在 Bologna 写信给 Guido Reni，并由 Reni 送出该画；本卡不把寄送者关系扩展为已独立核实的创作归属，也不识别其现存实物。

**English:** This card records the altar picture Haskell calls *The Archangel Michael trampling on the Devil*. He says Guido Reni was written to in Bologna and sent the picture. This card records the reported sending without treating it as independently verified authorship or identifying a surviving object.

### 本章相关内容

Haskell称此画被送入 Urban VIII 与其兄弟的祭坛画委托语境，并称其在当时受到广泛赞赏。该句未指明写信者或赞赏者；作品的外部身份与创作归属未在本批核验。

Haskell places the picture within the altar-picture commission of Urban VIII and his brother and reports that it was widely admired at the time. The passage does not name the correspondent or admirers; external object identity and authorship were not verified in this batch.
