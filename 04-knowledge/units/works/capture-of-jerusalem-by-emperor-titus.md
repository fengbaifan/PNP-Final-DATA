## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 第2章第44页所称的一幅《The Capture of Jerusalem by the Emperor Titus》。中文题名“《提图斯皇帝攻占耶路撒冷》”为工作译名，只转述题名，不验证相关历史事件或画面内容。Haskell 称 Francesco Barberini 从年轻的 Nicolas Poussin 处买得此画；原文明确的是卖方，不据此认定 Poussin 为创作者。附注4报告一位 Barberini 红衣主教后来将此画赠出，收受者“也许是 Richelieu”，并称画作当时已散佚；人物与收受者均按原文保留不确定性。第45页另提约十二年后制作的第二版本；对应索引候选 `cand-2001` 仍为 S3 undecided，本卡不将其并入本 KU 或建立版本关系。作品及版本未独立查阅。[第2章 p.44](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_iv.md)（L13–14、注4 L194）；[第2章 p.45](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_iv.md)（L29）

**English:** This card records the picture that Haskell identifies in Chapter 2, p.44 as *The Capture of Jerusalem by the Emperor Titus*. The Chinese title is a working translation that reports the wording of the title; it does not verify the historical event or the picture’s subject. Haskell says Francesco Barberini bought the picture from the young Nicolas Poussin. The passage identifies Poussin as the seller and does not establish him as the painter. Note 4 says a Cardinal Barberini later gave the picture away, “perhaps to Richelieu,” and reports it as lost at the time of Haskell’s account; both the Cardinal’s identity and the recipient remain unspecified. Page 45 separately mentions a second version made roughly a dozen years later; the corresponding index candidate `cand-2001` remains S3 undecided, so this card does not merge it into this KU or assert a version relation. Neither the work nor its versions have been independently consulted.
