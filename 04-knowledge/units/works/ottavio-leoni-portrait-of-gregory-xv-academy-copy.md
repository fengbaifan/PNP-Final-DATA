## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 奥塔维奥·莱奥尼受万神殿Virtuosi学院委托绘制的格列高利十五世布面肖像，现列于学院绘画收藏。Treccani记为1628年绘制；学院官网著录1628–1630年。此为约1621年佚失原作的后续复制品，二者年代与存世状态分别保留。

**English:** A canvas portrait of Gregory XV by Ottavio Leoni, commissioned by the Academy of the Virtuosi al Pantheon and listed in its painting collection. Treccani dates it to 1628; the Academy lists 1628–1630. This is a later copy of the lost original portrait of c. 1621.
