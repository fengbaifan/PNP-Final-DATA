## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 称 Pietro da Cortona 在耶稣堂圣依纳爵小堂建造、并用以替代 Della Porta 未完成前身的新祭坛。正文将它与后续更宏大的纪念工程分开；p.81 的“圣依纳爵祭坛”提及及 Postscript p.400 的研究文献提示均不足以独立说明其后续改建或现存身份。[第3章 pp.81、91](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md)；[Postscript p.400](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/20_CHP-20Postscript.md)

**English:** This unit records the new altar that Haskell says Pietro da Cortona built in the Gesù's Chapel of St Ignatius to replace Della Porta's uncompleted predecessor. It remains distinct from the later, more ambitious memorial project. The p. 81 reference to the “altar of St Ignatius” and the Postscript's p. 400 bibliographic mention do not independently establish later alterations or a surviving object identity. [Chapter 3, pp. 81, 91](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md); [Postscript, p. 400](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/20_CHP-20Postscript.md)

### 本章相关内容

原文称该祭坛建于提供 Algardi 所造瓮的同一时期，但没有给出精确年份。Haskell称其替代了 Della Porta 未完成的祭坛；本卡不据此断言现存状态，也不把二者合并为一个作品。

**English:** The source places the altar's construction at the same time the Algardi-modelled urn was offered but gives no exact year. Haskell says it replaced Della Porta's uncompleted altar. This unit does not infer survival or merge the two works.
