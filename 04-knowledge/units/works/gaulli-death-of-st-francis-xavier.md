## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第3章印刷页88所称Gaulli于1676年绘制的《Death of St Francis Xavier》祭坛画。页图印作“Giacomo. Gaulli”；S3依全书索引将姓氏Gaulli对齐至既有Giovanni Battista Gaulli人物KU，但这不表示外部作品目录核验。此处称名沿用哈斯克尔叙述，不声称为馆藏正式题名，也不确认现存实物或今藏地点。

**English:** This card records the altarpiece that Haskell says Gaulli painted in 1676 under the name *Death of St Francis Xavier*. The printed page reads “Giacomo. Gaulli”; S3 maps the surname Gaulli through the book index to the existing Giovanni Battista Gaulli person KU. This is not an external catalogue identification. The descriptive title follows Haskell’s account and does not establish a formal collection title, surviving object, or present location.
