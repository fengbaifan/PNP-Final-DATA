## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell第7章所列、归于Guercino的《科里奥兰纳斯与其母亲》。原书将其列入La Vrillière在1640年代增添的一组画作；具体版本和现藏地点尚未确定。

**English:** This unit records *Coriolanus and His Mother*, attributed to Guercino in Haskell’s Chapter 7. The book lists it among paintings added by La Vrillière in the 1640s. A specific version and present location have not been established.
