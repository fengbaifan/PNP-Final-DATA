## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell第3章记述耶稣堂圣依纳爵小堂的大祭坛方案在Tirso Gonzalez于1687年接任耶稣会总会长后重新推进，竞赛中选择Pozzo的方案；1695年5月Gonzalez作出最终决定，Pozzo全面负责该祭坛。第92–93页还描述祭坛的工艺与表现圣依纳爵生平、奇迹的浅浮雕。本卡用描述性暂题指向这一特定项目；原书没有在本处给出完工日期、现存状态或馆藏定位。[第3章书内来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md)

**English:** Chapter 3 says that the great altar project for the Chapel of St Ignatius in the Gesù resumed after Tirso Gonzalez became Jesuit General in 1687; Pozzo’s plan was selected in the competition. Gonzalez made a final decision in May 1695 and Pozzo was put in charge of the altar. Pages 92–93 also describe its craftsmanship and bas-reliefs of St Ignatius’s life and miracles. This card uses a descriptive working title for that specific project; this passage supplies no completion date, survival status, or collection location.
