### 本书相关内容

Haskell在第3章 p.66称Baglione绘制一幅The Resurrection画作，五年后因耶稣会未足额付款而投诉；p.84称他于1602年为耶稣堂右横翼礼拜堂绘制祭坛画，并说明礼拜堂曾奉献于Resurrection。S3据共同画家、题材、教堂语境和时间相容将两条候选合并。两处未明确说明作品位置与题名的对应细节，相关限制仍保留。

On p. 66 Haskell says Baglione produced a picture called The Resurrection and complained five years later about incomplete payment by the Jesuits. On p. 84 Haskell says Baglione painted an altarpiece for the Gesù’s right transept chapel in 1602, noting that the chapel had been dedicated to the Resurrection. S3 groups the two candidates using the shared painter, subject, church context, and compatible chronology. The passages do not explicitly state the location-title link, so that limitation remains recorded.
