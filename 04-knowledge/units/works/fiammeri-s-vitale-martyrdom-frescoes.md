## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 记述圣维塔莱堂以早期基督教殉道者受刑为主题的一组壁画：Jesuit 画家 Giovanni Battista Fiammeri 负责装饰，Claudio Aquaviva 总会长直接监督并参与构思。壁画多以田园景致呈现酷刑场面，唱诗班区域则更直接描绘施刑过程。后记又转述 Thomas Buser 对这些壁画及其可能“古代感”风格来源的讨论。本卡登记绘画组，不指教堂空间或其中任何单幅场景。

**English:** Haskell describes a group of frescoes at S. Vitale depicting the tortures of early Christian martyrs. The Jesuit painter Giovanni Battista Fiammeri directed the decoration under the immediate control of Superior General Claudio Aquaviva, who took a personal interest and offered suggestions. Most scenes presented torture within pastoral landscapes, while those in the choir showed the mechanisms more directly. The postscript also reports Thomas Buser’s discussion of the frescoes and the possible sources of their “antique” style. This unit is the group of paintings, not the church space or an individual scene.

### 作品信息

- 对象类型：圣维塔莱堂内一组以早期基督教殉道者受刑为主题的壁画；原书未给出确切绘制、完成日期或逐幅清单。
- 装饰统筹：Haskell 称 Jesuit 画家 Giovanni Battista Fiammeri 负责装饰，Claudio Aquaviva 总会长直接监督，对方案有强烈个人兴趣并提出建议。
- 题材与呈现：受刑是主要题材；一般画面以牧歌式风景表现酷刑，观看者需结合画面和题铭辨认受难者。唱诗班中的画家被鼓励减少克制，更直接展示施刑方式。
- 归属限定：Haskell 说这些作品在19世纪曾被归于 Gaspard Poussin；本卡不将此历史误归属当作认可归属。
- 后记讨论：Haskell 转述 Thomas Buser 对 S. Vitale“牧歌式”酷刑场景的评论，并报告 Buser 推测其风格可能受罗马壁画启发、意在营造古代感；这是被转述的解释，不是本轮独立核验的结论。

- Object type: a group of frescoes at S. Vitale depicting the tortures of early Christian martyrs; Haskell supplies no exact production or completion dates and no itemized list.
- Direction: Haskell says the Jesuit painter Giovanni Battista Fiammeri directed the decoration under the immediate control of Superior General Claudio Aquaviva, who took a strong personal interest and made suggestions.
- Subjects and presentation: torture predominates. The scenes often place it in pastoral landscapes, with inscriptions helping identify the victims; in the choir, painters were encouraged to show the mechanisms of torture more directly.
- Attribution qualification: Haskell says the works were attributed to Gaspard Poussin in the nineteenth century; this historical misattribution is not adopted as the card’s attribution.
- Postscript discussion: Haskell reports Thomas Buser’s comments on the “pastoral” torture scenes and his suggestion that their style may have been influenced by Roman wall painting to give the victims’ suffering an antique feeling. This is a reported interpretation, not independently verified here.

### 身份边界

中文：本卡是壁画组，不是圣维塔莱堂建筑、唱诗班空间 cand-5023 或单幅殉道场景；第3章的作品与第20章后记对该组壁画的评论相互指认，但不因此添加逐幅作品。圣斯德望圆厅 Circignani 壁画是原书用于比较的前一组作品，不与本组合并。Thomas Buser 为独立人物端点，后记所转述的论述不等于已查阅其1976年文章。

English: This unit is the fresco group, not the church building, the choir space candidate cand-5023, or any individual martyrdom scene. Chapter 3’s account and the postscript’s later comments refer to the same S. Vitale frescoes, but do not supply an itemized list. Circignani’s earlier frescoes at S. Stefano Rotondo are a separate comparison and are not merged here. Thomas Buser remains a distinct person endpoint; Haskell’s report of his interpretation does not mean that his 1976 article was consulted directly.
