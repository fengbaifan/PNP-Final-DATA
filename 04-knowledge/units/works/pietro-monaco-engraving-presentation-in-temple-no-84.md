## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell第13章注5所述的第84号《圣殿献礼》版画。Haskell将其版画归于Pietro Monaco，并称其收入《Raccolta di Opere scelte》。本卡登记书内所述版画对象，不代表已查阅原版画、确认出版版本或识别现存印张。Haskell所称底稿《圣殿献礼》与第10章的Chatsworth作品是否为同一件仍待证，本卡不将两者合并。

**English:** This unit records the no. 84 engraving of *The Presentation in the Temple* mentioned in Haskell’s chapter 13, note 5. Haskell attributes the engraving to Pietro Monaco and says it appeared in *Raccolta di Opere scelte*. This unit records the print described in the book; the print, its edition, and any surviving impression have not been consulted or identified. Whether Haskell’s source painting is the same work as the painting at Chatsworth in chapter 10 remains unresolved, so this card does not merge them.
