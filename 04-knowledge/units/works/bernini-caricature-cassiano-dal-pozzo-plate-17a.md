## 内容

### 作品身份

**中文：** 本卡记录图版17a的卡西亚诺·达尔·波佐讽刺像。哈斯克尔第100页称该讽刺像与唯一已知肖像形成的印象相符；图版17题注将作品署为Bernini并标注Cassiano dal Pozzo。题注是本卡作者与对象信息的依据，不构成独立作者核验。脚注2讨论的肖像与图版17a讽刺像保留为两件作品；哈斯克尔的视觉评述不据此转写为正式关系。

**English:** This unit records the caricature of Cassiano dal Pozzo in Plate 17a. On p. 100 Haskell says it confirms the impression made by the only known portrait; the Plate 17 caption credits Bernini and names Cassiano dal Pozzo. The caption supports only a source-attributed creator and subject identification, not independent authorship verification. The portrait discussed in note 2 remains distinct from the Plate 17a caricature. Haskell’s visual assessment is not converted into a formal graph relation.
