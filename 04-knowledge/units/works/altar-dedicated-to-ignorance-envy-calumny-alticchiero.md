## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称，一座献给Ignorance、Envy与Calumny的祭坛体现了他此前讨论的Querini性格特征，并报告这些恶德在一件浮雕中得到表现。本卡保留来源叙述；未将祭坛与浮雕合并为同一知识元，也未补作者、独立题名或现存状态。

**English:** Haskell describes an altar dedicated to Ignorance, Envy, and Calumny as expressing an aspect of Querini’s temperament, and reports that these vices were depicted in a bas-relief. This card preserves the source account; it does not merge the altar with the relief or supply a maker, independent title, or present survival claim.
