## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡以描述性名称记录Haskell所称玛蒂尔达墓的主浮雕。原书说该浮雕呈现亨利四世在坎诺莎跪于教皇格里高利七世面前，以说明玛蒂尔达遗赠的领土政治意涵。图像记述不是对历史事件细节的独立核验；原文也未在此给出浮雕材质、作者或正式题名。不得将前段另一件由Bernini与助手于1633年底开始制作的大理石浮雕的材质或作者归属转移到本作品。

**English:** This card uses a descriptive title for the principal relief that Haskell places on Matilda’s tomb. He says it showed Henry IV kneeling before Pope Gregory VII at Canossa to make the territorial implications of Matilda’s bequest understood. This account of the image does not independently verify the historical episode. The passage supplies no material, maker, or formal title for this relief. The material and work-start attribution attached to the separate marble relief mentioned immediately before it are not transferred to this work.
