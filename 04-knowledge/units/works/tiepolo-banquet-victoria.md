## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 提埃坡罗于1743–1744年绘制的布面油画，现藏墨尔本维多利亚国家美术馆，编号103-4；馆方于1933年取得。

**English:** An oil painting on canvas by Tiepolo, dated 1743–1744 and held by the National Gallery of Victoria in Melbourne as 103-4; acquired by the gallery in 1933.
