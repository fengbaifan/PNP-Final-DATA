### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell所称的Bernini《Neptune》，并按原文列为Farsetti选出的四尊代表“modern sculpture”的雕像之一。原文未给正式题名、年代或当前地点；本卡不作外部作品核验，并与Mattielli在布吕尔住所群中的《Neptune》喷泉作品分开。

**English:** This card records the *Neptune* Haskell names as Bernini’s and lists among the four statues Farsetti chose to represent “modern sculpture.” The passage supplies no formal title, date, or present location. This is a source-bound record without external catalogue verification and remains distinct from Lorenzo Mattielli’s *Neptune* fountain at the Brühl residences.
