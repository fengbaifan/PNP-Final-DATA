## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第4章脚注所列的作品题名“Funeral of Phocion”；中文题名为描述性暂译。脚注称Cassiano的一组未定数量普桑风景画中有一件版本在Lord Plymouth收藏，但作品版本与具体复制品对应仍未解决，被引文献也未查阅。

**English:** This unit records the composition title “Funeral of Phocion” as it appears in Haskell’s chapter 4 footnote; the Chinese rendering is descriptive. The note reports a version among Cassiano’s unresolved group of Poussin landscapes in Lord Plymouth’s collection, but the version and individual-copy mapping remain unresolved, and the cited publication was not consulted.
