## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell称Pietro Tacca寄给Paolo Giordano II、与拟议骑马像有关的一件modello。Haskell在附录转录的1624年书信称马模型已经装箱寄出，并将增添铜像装饰写成条件计划。这里的模型、拟议铜像和其余装饰分别处理；来源不证明铜像后来制作完成，也不证明模型现存。附录书信原件未独立查阅。

**English:** This unit records the modello that Haskell says Pietro Tacca sent to Paolo Giordano II in connection with a proposed equestrian statue. A letter transcribed by Haskell in the appendix says the horse model had been packed and sent, and describes additional bronze fittings conditionally. The model, proposed bronze statue, and fittings remain distinct; the source does not establish that the bronze statue was made or that the model survives. The manuscript letter has not been independently consulted.

### 本章相关内容

附录书信中的“若决定制作铜像”是条件语气，后续装饰仅作计划。Haskell对原信的转录不是本次对档案原件的独立核验。本卡不与相关的初步油画草图候选或其他媒介版本合并。
