## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称《Bath of Diana》是Algarotti为自己委托Tiepolo绘制的一幅画，并描写为轻松、感官性的裸体女仙场景。本卡按书内称名登记，不据此主张现存原件或外部归属核验。

**English:** Haskell identifies the Bath of Diana as a picture Algarotti commissioned from Tiepolo for himself and describes it as a relaxed, sensual scene of naked nymphs. This card records the book’s name and claims without asserting a surviving original or external attribution verification.
