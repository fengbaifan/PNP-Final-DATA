## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 提埃坡罗为皮萨尼家族创作的寓意壁画，列于本书图版44。第9章第254页称其为“the Apotheosis of the Pisani”，第255页以“for it”续指该作；该章将寓意场景置于1762年。这里的1762年指场景所述年份，不作为新的创作日期。

**English:** Tiepolo’s allegorical fresco for the Pisani family, listed as plate 44 in the book. Chapter 9, p. 254, calls it “the Apotheosis of the Pisani,” and p. 255 continues with “for it.” The chapter places the allegory’s setting in 1762; this is the setting’s stated year, not a new creation date.
