## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 提埃坡罗于1761至1762年绘于斯特拉皮萨尼别墅舞厅天顶的家族颂扬壁画，本书图版44所列对象。

**English:** Tiepolo’s fresco glorifying the Pisani family, painted in 1761–1762 on the ballroom ceiling of Villa Pisani at Stra and listed as plate 44 in Patrons and Painters.
