## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 将此 Aurora 描述为 Guercino 绘于 Pincio 赌场天顶的壁画，并将其作为 Ludovisi 赞助家乡 Emilia 艺术家的例子。原书没有确认这件作品的现存版本或现状。

**English:** Haskell identifies this Aurora as a fresco by Guercino on the ceiling of the casino on the Pincio, citing it as an example of Ludovisi’s patronage of artists from Emilia. The source does not identify a surviving version or establish the work’s present status.
