## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 1757年冬绘于威尼斯雷佐尼科宫的婚姻寓意顶画，由乔万巴蒂斯塔·提埃坡罗主绘，门戈齐·科隆纳绘建筑幻景，乔万多梅尼科·提埃坡罗绘萨堤尔。

**English:** A nuptial allegory ceiling painted at Ca’ Rezzonico in Venice in winter 1757, principally by Giambattista Tiepolo, with fictive architecture by Mengozzi Colonna and satyrs by Giandomenico Tiepolo.
