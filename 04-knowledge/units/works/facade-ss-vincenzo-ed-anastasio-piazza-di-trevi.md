## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔在第7章所述、增建于 SS. Vincenzo ed Anastasio 教堂的立面。原书称 Mazarin 经 Paolo Maccarani 委托建筑师 Martino Longhi the Younger 增建立面；本卡保留作者所述委托关系，不据此补写实际施工分工或其他身份信息。哈斯克尔还称工程延宕、Mazarin 受到经济困难困扰，并把立面解释为彰显枢机权威的视觉表达。后一判断是作者的解释。[第7章 p.183](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md)（L163–165）

**English:** This unit records the façade described in Chapter 7 as an addition to the church of SS. Vincenzo ed Anastasio. Haskell says Mazarin commissioned the architect Martino Longhi the Younger through Paolo Maccarani to add it; this card preserves the reported commission without inferring the division of actual construction work or adding biographical claims. Haskell also says that the work dragged on as Mazarin faced economic difficulties, and interprets the façade as proclaiming the Cardinal’s authority. The last point is the author’s interpretation. [第7章 p.183](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md)（L163–165）
