## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔在讨论雕塑家弗朗索瓦·迪凯努瓦时，提到其 St Susanna 位于 S. Maria di Loreto 教堂，并转述同时代人认为它可与古人制作的任何作品相媲美。此处中文名称是来源限定的描述性题名。

**English:** In a passage about the sculptor François Duquesnoy, Haskell places his St Susanna in the church of S. Maria di Loreto and reports that contemporaries judged it equal to anything produced by the ancients. The title here is a descriptive label scoped to the source.
