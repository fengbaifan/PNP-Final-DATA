## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 奇罗·费里1657年为科尔托纳圣方济各教堂拉帕雷利祭坛创作《荣耀中的圣母与圣路易国王、圣路易主教、科尔托纳的圣玛格丽特及真福圭多》。祭坛奉献于无染原罪，画作后来移至邻墙。

**English:** In 1657 Ciro Ferri painted a Virgin in Glory with Saint Louis the King, Saint Louis the Bishop, Saint Margaret of Cortona, and Blessed Guido for the Laparelli altar in San Francesco, Cortona. The altar was dedicated to the Immaculate Conception, and the painting was later moved to an adjacent wall.
