## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 卡洛·多尔奇于1665–1670年在佛罗伦萨绘制的约翰·芬奇肖像，与贝恩斯肖像配对，现藏菲茨威廉博物馆，编号PD.12-1972。

**English:** Carlo Dolci’s portrait of John Finch, painted in Florence in 1665–1670 and paired with his portrait of Thomas Baines; held by the Fitzwilliam Museum as PD.12-1972.
