## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔在第13章 p.343 所述的 Zanetti 明暗木刻组。原文称其约有五十幅，复兴了三至四色明暗木刻技法；大部分据 Zanetti 的 “Parmigianino drawings” 摹制或改编。哈斯克尔还称版片被毁、整组题献 Liechtenstein 亲王、单幅题献英、法、威尼斯友人。本卡只表示文本所述的组作，不将单幅识别为现存作品，不识别亲王或友人，也不推定作品现存、收藏地点或确切创作日期。[第13章 p.343](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md)（L112–113）

**English:** This card registers the group of Zanetti chiaroscuro woodcuts described in Haskell’s Chapter 13, p. 343. The passage reports about fifty cuts and says Zanetti revived the three- or four-colour chiaroscuro technique; most were copied or adapted from his “Parmigianino drawings.” Haskell also says the plates were destroyed, the set as a whole was dedicated to the Prince of Liechtenstein, and individual sheets to English, French, and Venetian friends. The card represents only the group described in the text. It does not identify surviving individual prints, the prince or friends, a present collection, or an exact date of production. [Chapter 13, p. 343](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md) (L112–113)
