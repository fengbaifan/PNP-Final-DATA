## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 1670 年委托要求，获批准后不得未经许可更改；稿本与实际穹顶壁画是不同阶段的对象。

**English:** The 1670 agreement required a coloured modello and prohibited unauthorized alterations after approval. This preliminary design is distinguished from the executed cupola frescoes; its present physical identity remains to be established.
