## 内容

### 描述
**中文：** 本卡记录伯尔尼尼圣彼得大殿青铜华盖的巨型螺旋柱这一建筑构件组。哈斯克尔分别叙述其柱式传统和宗教图像语境；所称源自所罗门圣殿属于书中记载的传统，不在此确认为实际历史源流。
**English:** This unit records the group of giant twisted columns in Bernini’s bronze baldacchino at St Peter’s Basilica. Haskell discusses the column form’s tradition and religious context; the claimed derivation from Solomon’s Temple remains a reported tradition, not an independently established origin.
