## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称Sebastiano Ricci随后抵达，为费迪南德在其最喜爱的别墅及画廊绘制《艺术寓意》，并称这件作品不幸已毁。中文及英文标题是识别性描述，不声称为原题。该段没有重申别墅名称，故不将作品地点登记为Poggio a Caiano，也不与皮蒂宫小室装饰合并。

**English:** Haskell says Sebastiano Ricci later arrived to paint an Allegory of the Arts for Ferdinand in his favourite villa and gallery, and reports that the work was destroyed. The bilingual title is descriptive, not asserted as the original title. The passage does not repeat the villa’s name, so this work is not assigned to Poggio a Caiano or merged with the Pitti-room decoration.
