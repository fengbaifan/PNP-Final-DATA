## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 奇罗·费里自1670年起绘制罗马纳沃纳广场圣阿涅塞教堂穹顶的《圣阿涅塞被引入天堂荣耀》。工程多次延期；费里1689年去世时尚未完成，1693年由塞巴斯蒂亚诺·科尔贝利尼续成。

**English:** Beginning in 1670, Ciro Ferri painted Saint Agnes Introduced to the Glory of Paradise in the dome of Sant’Agnese in Agone, Rome. The project was repeatedly delayed; it remained unfinished at Ferri’s death in 1689 and was completed by Sebastiano Corbellini in 1693.
