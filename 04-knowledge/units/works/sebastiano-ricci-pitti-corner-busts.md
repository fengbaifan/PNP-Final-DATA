## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell记载皮蒂宫小室壁画装饰的四角绘有人物胸像，男女形象含蓄地唤起古代风格。此卡仅代表原文明确描述的四角胸像装饰组，不识别坐像者，也不将其拆分为四件独立作品。

**English:** Haskell reports painted busts of men and women in the four corners of the Pitti-room decoration, vaguely evoking antiquity. This unit represents the bounded group of corner-bust decoration described in the source; it does not identify the sitters or split them into four individual works.
