### 描述

**中文：** 米开朗基罗·切尔科齐描绘画家及友人聚会的布面油画，卡塞尔馆藏号GK 554。

**English:** An oil painting by Michelangelo Cerquozzi depicting a gathering of the artist and friends, catalogued in Kassel as GK 554.
