## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔第9章 p.252 以十幅从 L. Annaeus Florus 的罗马史叙述中选取的场景，指称蒂埃波罗为多尔芬家族绘制的画布组；第253页的 “Roman epics” 回指该组。本卡仅表示该来源界定的十幅作品集合，不将未列名的单幅画扩展为独立对象。哈斯克尔注3报告这些画布分藏于 Leningrad 与 Vienna，但没有给出每幅画的分配或两地数量。该组与同页注5所称、归于 Niccolò Bambini 的 Vendramin-Calergi 组画分开。

**English:** In Chapter 9, p. 252, Haskell identifies a group of ten canvases depicting scenes selected from L. Annaeus Florus's Roman history and says Tiepolo painted them for the Dolfin family; “Roman epics” on p. 253 refers back to this group. This unit represents only the ten-work group defined by Haskell and does not expand the unnamed individual canvases into separate units. Note 3 reports that the canvases were divided between Leningrad and Vienna but gives neither each canvas's allocation nor a count for either city. The group remains distinct from the Vendramin-Calergi cycle attributed to Niccolò Bambini in note 5 on the same page.

### 题名与身份范围

本卡标题为描述性名称，不是来源中出现的正式题名。来源将画作称为十幅场景，但未逐幅列出题名、媒材细节、制作日期或完整存世范围。
