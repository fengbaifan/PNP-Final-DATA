## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡表示乡间别墅被描述为凸湾部突出于其上的主楼建筑构件。其与别墅整体、两翼及端部亭体分别建模；原书未给楼体尺寸或建造者。 [第15章来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_i.md)

**English:** This card represents the main block from which the villa's convex bay projects. It is modeled separately from the villa as a whole, the wings, and the end pavilions; the passage gives no dimensions or builder. [Chapter 15 source](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_i.md)
