## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔书中称为 Guglielmo della Porta 作品的保罗三世墓葬纪念碑。第2章称该墓从一根中央支柱移到圣彼得大殿后殿左侧的对应壁龛；没有给出移置日期。第4章记 Cassiano 在1647年的比较性报告，但该段是嵌套引述，不据此建立墓葬之间的声誉关系。本卡题名为描述性工作名。

**English:** This unit records the funerary monument of Paul III described in Haskell’s book as a work by Guglielmo della Porta. Chapter 2 says it was moved from a central pillar to a corresponding niche on the left side of the apse at St Peter’s Basilica; it gives no relocation date. Chapter 4 recounts Cassiano’s comparative report from 1647 as a nested quotation; this does not establish a reputation relation between the tombs. The title is descriptive.
