## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡指哈斯克尔所述耶稣堂后殿的 conch（后殿半穹顶内表面）绘饰。原文仅称 Gaulli 绘制了上方拱顶的一小部分及后殿 conch；未说明此处的具体图像、材料、完成时间或存世情况。描述性名称不视为原作题名。

**English:** This unit refers to the painted conch (the semi-dome surface) of the Gesù apse, as described by Haskell. The source says only that Gaulli painted a small fraction of the vault above and the apse conch; it does not specify the imagery, material, completion date, or survival of this part. The descriptive name is not treated as an original title.
