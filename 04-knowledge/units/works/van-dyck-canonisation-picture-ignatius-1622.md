## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所述、为圣依纳爵1622年册封礼绘制并于册封时安置在耶稣堂圣依纳爵礼拜堂的 Van Dyck 图像。此为描述性名称，不是原题；原书未给出图像题名、明确描绘题材、作品版本或现藏地。本卡与另有记载的 Van Dyck 圣方济各·沙勿略图像为不同作品。[第3章 p.91](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md)（L154–156；PDF物理页33）

**English:** This card records the Van Dyck picture Haskell says was painted for Ignatius's 1622 canonisation and placed in the Chapel of St Ignatius in the Gesù on that occasion. This is a descriptive name, not an original title. Haskell supplies no title, explicit depicted subject, version, or present location. This is distinct from the separately reported Van Dyck picture of St Francis Xavier. [第3章 p.91](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md)（L154–156；PDF物理页33）
