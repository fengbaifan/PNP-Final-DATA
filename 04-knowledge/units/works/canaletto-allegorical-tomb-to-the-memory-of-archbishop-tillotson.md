## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 以约翰·蒂洛特森为纪念对象的寓意绘画，由卡纳莱托、奇马罗利和皮托尼合作，分别绘制建筑、风景和人物。

**English:** An allegorical painting commemorating John Tillotson, made collaboratively by Canaletto, Cimaroli and Pittoni, who painted the architecture, landscape and figures respectively.
