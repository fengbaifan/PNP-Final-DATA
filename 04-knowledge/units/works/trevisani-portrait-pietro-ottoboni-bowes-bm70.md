## 内容

### 描述
**中文：** 本卡对应 Haskell 所称 Trevisani 为 Pietro Ottoboni 所作、现藏 Bowes Museum 的肖像。Treccani 的 Trevisani 词条将 B.M.70 与该肖像相连；馆藏页及两篇 DBI 词条提供不同年代估计，均保留为异文，不在本批裁定。
**English:** This card represents Haskell’s portrait of Pietro Ottoboni by Trevisani, now at the Bowes Museum. Trevisani’s DBI entry connects the portrait to inventory B.M.70; the museum page and two DBI entries give differing date estimates, retained without adjudication in this batch.
