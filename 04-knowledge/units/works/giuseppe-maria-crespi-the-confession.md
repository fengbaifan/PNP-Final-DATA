## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第6章 p.166 明确题名为 *The Confession* 的一件克雷斯皮绘画。原文称克雷斯皮从博洛尼亚将此画寄给枢机，并称其为“他的一幅画”；画中题材没有具名，哈斯克尔将其描述为以教士生活为背景的亲切而略带讽刺的场景。赞美性用语与题材说明均按原书归属保留。

**English:** This card records the Crespi painting explicitly titled *The Confession* on p. 166. Haskell says Crespi sent it from Bologna to the Cardinal and calls it one of his pictures. The subject is unnamed; Haskell describes an affectionate, slightly ironic scene from clerical life. The evaluative language and subject description remain attributed to Haskell.

### 身份与范围

本卡只登记具名的单件作品，不把随后提到的六幅圣事画并入本作品。原文没有给出题材名称、创作日期、媒材或原始委托信息。中文题名《忏悔》是便于识别的描述性译名，不声称为正式馆藏题名。

Footnote 1 says “These pictures are now at Dresden” and cites L. Crespi, p. 213. The S2 record links the plural note to this painting and the adjacent six Sacraments group. This is Haskell's report, not an independent confirmation of the cited page or a claim about the present-day location.
