## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 记录 Haskell 转录的 Lorenzo del Rosso 遗嘱所述一幅 Caravaggio 作品，描绘 S. Gio. Decollato。来源称作品遗赠给 Coriolano Montemagni，并给出保留古代单位的近似尺寸；不等同于已知存世作品。

**English:** Records the Caravaggio work in Haskell's transcription of Lorenzo del Rosso's will, depicting S. Gio. Decollato. The source reports a bequest to Coriolano Montemagni and approximate historic-unit dimensions; no surviving work is identified.

**English:** Braccia are not converted, the measurement convention for the ornament is not inferred, and the work is not merged with another painting.
