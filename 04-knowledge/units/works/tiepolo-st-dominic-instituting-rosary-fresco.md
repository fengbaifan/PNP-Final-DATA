## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell所述、描绘圣道明设立玫瑰经的天顶壁画。Haskell将创作归于Tiepolo，日期为1737–1739年；作品身份限于书内称述，不声称已核对外部目录或现存状况。

**English:** This unit records Haskell's ceiling fresco depicting St Dominic instituting the Rosary. Haskell attributes it to Tiepolo and dates it to 1737–1739. The work identity is limited to the book's account; no external catalogue or present condition has been checked.
