## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所述、位于 Fontenelle《Eloge》摘录上方的 Newton 肖像章与黄道十二宫图案设计。Haskell 称该设计绘于单独一页并由 François Boucher 绘制；具体版页未独立识别。

**English:** This unit records the Newton medallion and Zodiac design above extracts from Fontenelle’s Eloge. Haskell says it was drawn by François Boucher on a separate page; the specific printed page or design was not independently identified.
