## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 布面油画，描绘圣埃洛伊与圣若翰洗者、圣安德烈，尺寸280×200厘米。Thevenon研究与法国文化部遗产目录记录签名及1661年；目录将其列于尼斯圣雷帕拉塔主教座堂Sainte-Rose-de-Lima小堂。教堂官网记该堂Saint Éloi画作署名B. Baldoino、年代1646；意大利语主教座堂条目把同一说法的参考来源指向Palissy PM06000568。因此本卡按一个目录对象处理，保留年代和作者归属异文，不另建第二件作品。（S1–S3、S5）

**English:** An oil painting on canvas depicting Saint Eloi between Saint John the Baptist and Saint Andrew, measuring 280 × 200 cm. Thevenon’s study and the French Ministry of Culture’s heritage record give a signature and date of 1661 and locate it in the Sainte-Rose-de-Lima chapel of Nice Cathedral. The cathedral website gives “B. Baldoino, 1646”; the Italian cathedral entry cites the same Palissy record number, so this card treats the conflicting claims as referring to one catalogued object while leaving the date and attribution discrepancy unresolved. (S1–S3, S5)
