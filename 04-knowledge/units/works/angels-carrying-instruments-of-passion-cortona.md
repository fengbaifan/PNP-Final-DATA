## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 将此作品称为 Pietro da Cortona 的一幅小型壁画，并与《圣斐理伯神魂超拔》并列。原书认为两幅壁画对其后来与奥拉托利会的密切联系具有意义；该概括不另转写为不精确的正式关系。

**English:** Haskell describes this as a small fresco by Pietro da Cortona and lists it alongside St Philip in Ecstasy. He says the two frescoes mattered to Cortona’s later close association with the Oratorians; that broad statement is not converted into an imprecise formal relation.
