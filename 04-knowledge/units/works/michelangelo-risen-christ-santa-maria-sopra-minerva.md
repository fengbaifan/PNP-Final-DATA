## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell在Farsetti所选四尊“代表近代雕塑”的雕像中列出的《Risen Christ》，印本称其为“Michelangelo’s”并以Santa Maria sopra Minerva作地点限定。题名与归属均按该书所载，不代表独立的作品目录或当前安置核验；也不与Bernini为圣彼得巴尔达基诺计划、后改为十字架的未执行方案合并。

**English:** This card records the *Risen Christ* that Haskell includes among the four statues Farsetti chose to represent “modern sculpture.” The printed wording calls it “Michelangelo’s” and identifies it with Santa Maria sopra Minerva. The title and attribution remain source-bound and are not an independent catalogue or present-location verification. This is distinct from the unexecuted *Risen Christ* plan for Bernini’s baldachin, later changed to a Cross.
