## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 瓦朗坦·德·布洛涅1631年受法布里齐奥·瓦尔瓜内拉委托创作《有算命人的音乐聚会》。审判证词所述算命女性、士兵、其他女性和乐师与现属列支敦士登王室收藏的作品相符，并记录其在复活节前完成。

**English:** In 1631 Fabrizio Valguarnera commissioned Valentin de Boulogne to paint A Musical Company with a Fortune-Teller. The gypsy woman, soldiers, other women, and musicians described in the trial testimony match the work now in the Princely Collections of Liechtenstein, which was completed by Easter.
