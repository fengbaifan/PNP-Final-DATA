## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Urban VIII 将圣比比亚娜小教堂的重建与装饰交给贝尔尼尼，并称这是贝尔尼尼的首个纯建筑性工作；同段称贝尔尼尼在“数年内”完成任务，但未给出确切年份。此卡记录该书内工程指称，不将其扩展为对全部工程参与者、完工状态或外部项目身份的核验。

**English:** Haskell says Urban VIII entrusted Bernini with the reconstruction and decoration of the small church of S. Bibiana, describing it as Bernini’s first purely architectural work. The same passage says Bernini finished the task within a couple of years but gives no exact year. This unit records the project as described in the book and does not establish all participants, the complete state of the works, or an externally verified project identity.
