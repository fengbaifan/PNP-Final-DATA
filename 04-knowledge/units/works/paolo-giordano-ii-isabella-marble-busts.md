## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡按Haskell第4章印刷页97记录Paolo Giordano II与其妻Isabella的大理石半身像组。正文将其置于1631年奖章后一两年；注3转引Faldi称约1630年。两种年代依据并列保留，未作调和。原文未在该段具名雕刻者，也未识别具体存世实物。

**English:** This card records the marble busts of Paolo Giordano II and his wife Isabella described in chapter 4, printed page 97. Haskell's narrative places them a year or two after the 1631 medal, while note 3 reports Faldi's c. 1630 date. Both source accounts are retained without reconciliation. The passage does not name a sculptor or identify a surviving object.
