## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所述 S. Ignazio 拱顶此前的灰泥装饰。作者称这项装饰后来被 Pozzo 毁去；Haskell 又转引 Pozzo 贬称它“更适合厨房，而非教堂”。来源没有给出灰泥装饰的作者、具体日期、造型细节或现存状态。该装饰是作品候选，不是拱顶建筑空间或后来绘制的壁画。[第3章 p. 90](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md#L147)

**English:** This unit records the earlier stucco decoration described by Haskell on the vault of S. Ignazio. Haskell says Pozzo later destroyed it and quotes Pozzo disparaging it as “more suitable for a kitchen than for a church.” The source gives no maker, precise date, formal description, or survival status. This is a work candidate, distinct from the architectural vault and the later fresco. [Chapter 3, p. 90](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md#L147)

### 作品边界与状态

- 仅记录原文称为此前存在的 stucco work；不推测灰泥层的单件数量、作者或艺术质量。
- Haskell称 Pozzo 毁去该装饰，未说明所用方式或具体日期；卡片不推断现状。
