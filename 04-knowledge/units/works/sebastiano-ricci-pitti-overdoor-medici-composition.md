## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell描述小室主门上方的装饰构图：天使托举盾牌，盾上绘有美第奇家族纹章，putti与冠冕位于上方。此卡将该门上构图作为壁画装饰中的独立构件；不把人物个体或徽章另拆为作品。

**English:** Haskell describes an overdoor composition in which angels support a shield bearing the Medici arms, with putti and a crown above. This card treats the bounded overdoor composition as a decorative component; it does not split the figures or arms into separate works.
