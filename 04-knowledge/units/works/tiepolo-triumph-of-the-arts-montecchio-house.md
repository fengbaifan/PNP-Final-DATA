## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第9章称为“The Triumph of the Arts”的作品。原文将其列为提埃坡罗在科代利纳蒙泰基奥乡间住宅天顶绘制的壁画。中文题名为暂译；书内称名和位置不指认某一存世版本或外部目录条目。

**English:** This card records the work Haskell names “The Triumph of the Arts” in Chapter 9. He describes it as a fresco Tiepolo painted on the ceiling of Carlo Cordellina’s country house at Montecchio. The Chinese title is provisional; the book’s wording and location do not identify a surviving version or catalogue record.

### 本章相关内容

**中文：** 哈斯克尔把《艺术的凯旋》与两件墙面壁画分开，并明确置于天顶。本卡只记录这件具名作品，不将其与不同宅邸的《雄辩之力》天顶画合并。

**English:** Haskell distinguishes The Triumph of the Arts from the two wall frescoes and places it on the ceiling. This card records the named work separately from the Powers of Eloquence ceiling in a different palace.
