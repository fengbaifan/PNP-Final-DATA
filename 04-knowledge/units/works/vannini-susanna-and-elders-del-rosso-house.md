## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell列出的Susanna and the Elders。Haskell称其属于Ottavio Vannini为Del Rosso家族住宅制作的四幅大型旧约场景，并将四幅作品称为Vannini最出色的作品；本卡将作者归属限定为Haskell的书内表述，不表示独立作品目录或实物已核验。来源未给出现今位置或可确认的存世版本。

**English:** This card records Haskell's named Susanna and the Elders. He identifies it as one of four large Old Testament scenes produced by Ottavio Vannini for the Del Rosso family house and describes the four as among Vannini's finest works. The attribution is limited to Haskell's account; no independent catalogue or surviving object has been verified. The source gives no present location or identifiable surviving version.
