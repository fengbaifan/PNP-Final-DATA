## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell第8章所称的《Portrait of a Cook》，即Cassana为Ferdinand所作的一幅厨师肖像。题名采用原书英文称呼的描述性中文译名，不声称是作品的正式原题。Haskell称该画于1707年9月抵达Florence；这不是创作日期或现藏地。原文没有识别画中人物，也没有证明Ferdinand计划中的展出实际发生。

**English:** This card records the painting Haskell calls *Portrait of a Cook*, a portrait associated with Cassana’s work for Ferdinand. The English wording follows Haskell; the Chinese is a descriptive translation, not an asserted formal title. Haskell says the picture reached Florence in September 1707, which is neither a creation date nor a present-location claim. The sitter is not identified, and the planned display is not shown to have taken place.
