## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 马扎然宫上层画廊的穹顶壁画与镀金灰泥装饰整体，绘画由罗马内利及作坊于1646–1647年完成。

**English:** The ensemble of vault frescoes and gilded stucco in the upper gallery of the Palais Mazarin, painted by Romanelli and his workshop in 1646–1647.
