## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记 Haskell 第8章 pp.218–219 记述的 Antonio Zanchi《Moses striking the Rock》画作。书中称 Zanchi 获选绘制该画，在威尼斯完成后由 Bergamo 的委托方接受，并获付825杜卡特；书后索引将题名与其在 S. Maria Maggiore 的工作、付款条目定位于同一页段。原书未确认作品此后的保存地点或现存状态。题名中文为项目工作译名。[第8章 pp.218–219](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md)（L84、86–87）；[书后索引](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-1-Index-MD/UVWXYZ.md)（UVWXYZ.csv#172–174）

**English:** This card records Antonio Zanchi’s painting *Moses striking the Rock* as described on pp.218–219 of Chapter 8. Haskell says Zanchi was selected to paint it, completed it in Venice, and received 825 ducats after the Bergamo committee accepted it. The index locates the title, his work at S. Maria Maggiore, and the payment entry in the same page range. The book does not establish the work’s later location or present state. The Chinese title is a working translation. [Chapter 8, pp.218–219](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md) (L84, 86–87); [Index](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-1-Index-MD/UVWXYZ.md) (UVWXYZ.csv#172–174)
