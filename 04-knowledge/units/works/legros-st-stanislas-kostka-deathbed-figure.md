## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第3章称为圣斯坦尼斯劳斯·科斯特卡临终时的多彩人物像。原书没有提供正式作品题名；卡名是描述性名称。哈斯克尔称该像由 Pierre Legros 制作，并称它曾存放在耶稣会初学院一间光线昏暗的房间。[第3章 p.93](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md#L181)

**English:** This card records the polychrome figure of St Stanislas Kostka on his deathbed described in chapter 3. The book provides no formal title; the card title is descriptive. Haskell attributes the figure to Pierre Legros and says it was kept in a badly lit room in the Jesuit novitiate. [Chapter 3, p. 93](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md#L181)
