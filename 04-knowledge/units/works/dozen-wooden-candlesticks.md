## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell记载，枢机S. Onofrio订置一打木制烛台并送到该教堂。Capuchin修士将烛台退回；随后Urban VIII坚持接收，原文称烛台被接受。接受不证明完成安装或现存地点；本书此处未给制造者或独立正式题名。

**English:** Haskell reports that Cardinal S. Onofrio ordered a dozen wooden candlesticks and sent them to the church. The Capuchin monks returned the candlesticks; Urban VIII then insisted, and the text says they were accepted. Acceptance does not establish installation or a present location. This passage gives no maker or formal individual title.
