## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell所述Bernini于1623年6月为Paolo Giordano II的头像制作的蜡模型。页96的句子在页97续完；来源称当时Bernini“已经在制作”模型，不据此补写完成日期、后续铸造版本或该蜡模型的存世状态。作者归属沿用原书记述，未作外部核验。

**English:** This unit records the wax model of Paolo Giordano II’s head that Haskell says Bernini was modelling in June 1623. The sentence continues from p. 96 to p. 97. The wording reports that the modelling was already under way; it does not establish a completion date, a later cast as the same object, or the wax model’s survival. The attribution follows Haskell and has not been checked against external records.
