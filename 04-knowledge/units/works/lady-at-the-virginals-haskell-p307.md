## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell第10章p.307所称《Lady at the Virginals》。Haskell在同段称它为“Vermeer’s”作品，同时报告当时它被归为Frans van Mieris；两种表述均按书内语境保留，不裁定作品的现行作者。Haskell还称该画当时位于Buckingham Palace，并推测Joseph Smith“almost certainly”从Pellegrini遗孀处取得，随后又将来源写成条件句。这里记录的是该书的来源限定主张；项目未独立核对作品、流传记录或现址。中文名称为描述性译名。

**English:** This card records the work Haskell calls *Lady at the Virginals* on p.307. In the same passage he calls it “Vermeer’s” painting, while also reporting that it was then attributed to Frans van Mieris; both statements are retained in their book context and do not determine the work’s present authorship. Haskell also places it in Buckingham Palace at the time of his account and says Joseph Smith “almost certainly” acquired it from Pellegrini’s widow, then qualifies the provenance conditionally. These are source-qualified claims; the work, its provenance, and its present location have not been independently checked in this project. The Chinese name is a descriptive translation.
