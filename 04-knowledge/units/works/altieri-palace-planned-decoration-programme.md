## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 记录哈斯克尔所述由 Carlo Maratta 及其学生为 Altieri 宫殿规划、但从未完成的装饰计划。原书将未完成归因于与 Don Gasparo Altieri 之间未具体说明的分歧；此卡不把含混的“the work dragged on”年限归给该计划。

**English:** This unit records the decorative programme that Haskell says Carlo Maratta and his pupils planned for the Altieri palace but never completed. Haskell attributes its non-completion to unspecified differences with Don Gasparo Altieri. The ambiguous statement that “the work dragged on” is not assigned a duration for this programme.
