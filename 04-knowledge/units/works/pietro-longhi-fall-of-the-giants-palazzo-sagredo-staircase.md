## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Pietro Longhi 在 Palazzo Sagredo 楼梯绘制了这幅壁画，并称画上标注1734年；他认为该年份应指作品完成。委托人Zaccaria Sagredo的说法仍是哈斯克尔明确标为“just possible”的推测。中文题名为便于识别的工作暂译，不代表已核实的正式题名；Palazzo Sagredo楼梯是原书所报位置，具体建筑身份未定。

**English:** Haskell says Pietro Longhi painted this fresco on the staircase of Palazzo Sagredo and dated it 1734; he considers that date to refer to completion. A commission by Zaccaria Sagredo remains Haskell's explicitly tentative suggestion (“just possible”). The Chinese display title is a working translation, not a verified formal title; the staircase is Haskell's reported location, and the building's identity remains unresolved.
