## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 在讨论耶稣堂穹顶穹隅图像时所述的人物组。作者称 Oliva 决定采用惯常图像群组并加入一组此前未在该处表现的图像；随后将单一穹隅中四个人物的构图解决方案归于 Gaulli。此卡只表示 Haskell 所述的穹隅人物组，不推定整座穹顶的总人物数，也不补出个体身份。[第3章 p.83](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md)（L70；PDF物理页25）

**English:** This card records the figure group discussed by Haskell in connection with the Gesù dome pendentives. He says Oliva chose the customary iconographic groups and added a further group not previously represented in that setting; he then attributes the compositional solution for presenting four figures in a single pendentive to Gaulli. This card represents Haskell’s source-defined pendentive group; it does not infer the total number of figures across the dome or identify individual figures. [第3章 p.83](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md)（L70；PDF物理页25）
