## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本书将 Nicolas Poussin 为 Cassiano dal Pozzo 创作的《七件圣事》称为一组绘画，并称他于17世纪30年代后半开始绘制。本卡登记系列层级，不指某一幅单作或未经逐件核定的完整作品清单。

**English:** Haskell identifies the Seven Sacraments as a series of paintings made by Nicolas Poussin for Cassiano dal Pozzo and says Poussin began painting it in the second half of the 1630s. This unit records the series as a whole, not an individual painting or an independently verified complete item list.

### 名称

中文显示名及英文显示名见卡片标题；中文名为描述性工作译名，非原书正式题名。

The bilingual display names appear in the card title; the Chinese name is a descriptive working title, not a formal title from the book.

### 作品信息

- 作品类型：绘画组／系列。
- 创作者：Nicolas Poussin（原书称 Poussin）。
- 开始绘制：17世纪30年代后半；原文未在此给出确切首作日期。
- 委托语境：Haskell 将其置于 Poussin 为 Cassiano 工作的语境，并将 Cassiano 的委托与系列构想作为作者判断；正式人物—作品关系仍留待 S6。

- Work type: a series of paintings.
- Creator: Nicolas Poussin (named as Poussin in the cited passage).
- Begun: the second half of the 1630s; the passage gives no exact date for the first painting.
- Commission context: Haskell places the series within Poussin’s work for Cassiano and presents Cassiano’s commission and role in the series as the author’s interpretation; the formal person–work relation remains for S6.

### 身份边界

中文：系列身份不等于单幅作品、版本或现存实物；此卡不据“七件”补出逐件清单、现藏地点或完整存世状态。后记所引1664年报告只说 Cassiano 曾考虑将该系列赠给一位未具名友人，不表示赠与实际发生。

English: The series identity is distinct from any individual painting, version, or surviving object. The card does not infer an itemized list, present location, or complete survival status from the title. The postscript’s report that Cassiano once considered giving the series to an unnamed friend does not establish a completed gift.
