### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 在第10章注1所描述的牛顿纪念碑铭文牌。注释将设计归于 Joseph Perrot；牌面及纪念碑实体未在本批独立识别。

**English:** This unit records the inscription plate to Sir Isaac Newton’s monument described in Chapter 10 note 1. Haskell attributes its design to Joseph Perrot; the plate and monument were not independently identified in this batch.
