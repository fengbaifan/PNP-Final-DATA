## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本知识元只表示Haskell在第8章p.222所述、大主教宫顶层另一枚描绘克雷芒十一世的奖章。印本称其为全浮雕；原书没有说明作者、材质、具体年代或现存版本。它与前句所述楼梯墙上的六枚教皇奖章及楼梯灰泥装饰分开。

**English:** This unit represents only the additional medallion on the top floor of the Archbishop's Palace described by Haskell in Chapter 8, p. 222, as depicting the reigning Pope Clement XI. The print describes it as being in full relief. The source does not identify its maker, material, date, or a surviving version. It is separate from the six papal medallions on the staircase wall and from the staircase stucco decoration.
