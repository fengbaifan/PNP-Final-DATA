## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔第10章印刷页313注1称，威尼斯 Museo Correr 有一幅归于 Gian Antonio Guardi 的舒伦堡肖像。此卡以来源描述名记录该对象；书中未将其认定为图版53a所列的骑马肖像。

**English:** Haskell’s Chapter 10, p. 313 n. 1, refers to a portrait of Schulenburg at the Museo Correr in Venice and attributes it to Gian Antonio Guardi. This card records the source-described work; Haskell does not identify it with the equestrian portrait listed in plate 53a.
