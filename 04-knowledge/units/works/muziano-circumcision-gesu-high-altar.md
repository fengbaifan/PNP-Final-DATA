## 内容

### 作品身份

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔称为《The Circumcision》的绘画。原书称 Cardinal Alessandro Farnese 委托 Girolamo Muziano 为耶稣堂主祭坛绘制此画。中文题名为描述性译名；原书所述委托目的地是已单独登记的[耶稣堂主祭坛结构](gesu-high-altar.md)。原书此处未说明画作是否完成、材质或现存地点。

**English:** This card records the painting Haskell calls The Circumcision. The book says Cardinal Alessandro Farnese commissioned Girolamo Muziano to paint it for the Gesù High Altar. The Chinese title is descriptive; the altar structure is separately registered as the [Gesù High Altar](gesu-high-altar.md). The passage does not say whether the painting was completed or identify its medium or present location.

### 对象边界

**中文：** 祭坛画为 work；主祭坛结构是另一件 work，教堂建筑为 place。

**English:** The painting is a work; the altar structure is a separate work, and the church building is a place.
