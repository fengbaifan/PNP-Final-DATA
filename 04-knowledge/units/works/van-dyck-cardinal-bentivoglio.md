## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 范·戴克在罗马受本蒂沃利奥枢机委托绘制的坐姿肖像，现属皮蒂宫帕拉蒂纳画廊。

**English:** A seated portrait commissioned from Van Dyck by Cardinal Bentivoglio in Rome, now held by the Galleria Palatina at Palazzo Pitti.
