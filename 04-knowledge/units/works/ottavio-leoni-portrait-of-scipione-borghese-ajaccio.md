## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 奥塔维奥·莱奥尼创作的希皮奥内·博尔盖塞枢机布面油画肖像，现藏于法国阿雅克肖费施博物馆，馆藏号MFA 852.1.265。此卡仅指该馆藏画布；Louvre另藏一件1617年同题人物纸本素描。

**English:** An oil-on-canvas portrait of Cardinal Scipione Borghese by Ottavio Leoni, held by Musée Fesch in Ajaccio, France, inventory MFA 852.1.265. This record identifies the museum’s canvas; the Louvre holds a separate 1617 drawing of the same sitter.
