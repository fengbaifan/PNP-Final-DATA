## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔将阿尔蒂基耶罗图书室中的一件雕像称为培根胸像，并以“神庙偶像”（“idol of the temple”）作修辞称呼；这不是经证实的作品题名。原文未说明创作者、具体实物身份或现今地点。

**English:** Haskell identifies a bust in the library at Alticchiero as a bust of Bacon and calls it the “idol of the temple.” This is a metaphor in his prose, not an evidenced title. The passage does not identify the maker, establish a more specific object identity, or give a present location.
