## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应 Haskell 第15章注2所列的 Carlo de’ Conti Lodoli《Apologhi》1787年版。正文称 Andrea Memmo 为纪念其就任 Procuratore di San Marco 而出版了五十八则寓言，并将其概述为讽刺威尼斯社会人物的短篇。卡片记录的是书中所引书目身份；所引印本未在本轮独立查阅。[第15章 p.367](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_ii.md)（L32）；[第15章 p.367 注2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_ii.md)（L94）

**English:** This unit identifies the 1787 edition of Carlo de’ Conti Lodoli’s *Apologhi* listed in Haskell’s chapter 15 note 2. The text says Andrea Memmo published fifty-eight fables to mark his assumption of the Procuratore di San Marco office and describes them as satirizing leading figures in Venetian society. This card records the bibliographic identity cited in the book; the cited edition was not independently consulted in this pass. [第15章 p.367](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_ii.md)（L32）; [第15章 p.367 注2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_ii.md)（L94）
