## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell第8章第222页注1所引的Collezione Antonelli MS. 610手稿定位。Haskell称关于费拉拉大主教宫的这段叙述取自该手稿，并将其题名引作《Galleria di Pitture raccolte, ed esposte nel Palazzo Vescovale di Ferrara…》，题名末尾注明“descritta da Girolamo Baruffaldi”。这只是Haskell所报告的书目身份；本任务未查阅手稿或馆藏目录。中文题名为工作译名，不声称是原件目录题名。

**English:** This card records the manuscript reference cited in note 1 on page 222 of Chapter 8: Collezione Antonelli MS. 610. Haskell says that his account of the Archbishop’s palace is taken from this manuscript and cites its title as *Galleria di Pitture raccolte, ed esposte nel Palazzo Vescovale di Ferrara…*, ending with “descritta da Girolamo Baruffaldi.” This is the bibliographic identity reported by Haskell; the manuscript and catalogue were not consulted in this task. The Chinese rendering is a working translation, not a claim about the manuscript’s catalogue title.
