**English:** Haskell cites a document from the Sagredo archives published by Anthony Blunt in 1957, page 24, concerning Joseph Smith’s purchases from Zaccaria’s heirs in 1751–1752. Neither the document nor the published page was independently consulted.

Haskell cites a document from the Sagredo archives published by Anthony Blunt in 1957, page 24, concerning Joseph Smith’s purchases from the heirs in 1751–1752.
