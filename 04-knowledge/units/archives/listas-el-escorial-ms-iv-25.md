### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第7章注11所标识的手稿清单。注中将其定位为 El Escorial MS. &-IV-25，并称 Warburg Institute 有一份影印本；手稿与影印本均未查阅。

**English:** This card records the manuscript list identified in Haskell’s chapter 7 note 11. The note gives El Escorial MS. &-IV-25 and reports a photocopy at the Warburg Institute; neither copy has been consulted.
