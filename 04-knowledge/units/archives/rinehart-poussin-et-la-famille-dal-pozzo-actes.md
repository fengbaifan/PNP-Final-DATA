## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡依据哈斯克尔第4章脚注和第21章书目登记S. Rinehart的会议论文集文章。第4章脚注称1961年，第21章书目经页面校读记为1960年卷I，年份差异保留待证；没有独立查阅该文章、论文集目录或原件。作者名在脚注与书目中的形式不同，个人身份未在本卡消歧。

**English:** This unit records S. Rinehart’s conference-proceedings contribution as cited in Haskell’s chapter 4 footnote and listed in chapter 21. The footnote gives 1961, while the page-checked bibliography gives 1960, volume I; the discrepancy remains unresolved. The article, proceedings catalogue, and original were not independently consulted. The differing author forms in the footnote and bibliography are not resolved here as a personal identity.
