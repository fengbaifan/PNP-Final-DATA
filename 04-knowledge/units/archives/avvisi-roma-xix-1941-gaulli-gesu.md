## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本知识元指 Haskell 第6章 p.160 注1所引的 avvisi；它们是独立于期刊卷册的被引材料，具体篇名、日期及作者未载。Haskell 给出 Roma 第十九卷（1941）第392页定位，未因此视为已查阅告示原文。

**English:** This unit represents the avvisi cited in Haskell’s chapter 6, page 160, note 1. They are cited materials distinct from the periodical volume; their individual titles, dates, and authors are not supplied. Haskell gives a locator at page 392 of Roma, volume XIX (1941), but the notices themselves were not consulted.
