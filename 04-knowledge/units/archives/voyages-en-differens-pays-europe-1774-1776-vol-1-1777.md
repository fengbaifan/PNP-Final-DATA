## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第15章 p.369 注1所引《Voyages en differens pays de l'Europe en 1774, 1775, et 1776》第一卷。哈斯克尔称该书于1777年在海牙出版、引第一卷第211页，并称作者匿名。CiNii将作者归属为Pilati de Tassulo；e-rara的相关1778年瑞士版第一卷记录以方括号列出Carlo Antonio Pilati。两项书目记录均不足以确定哈斯克尔所引1777年海牙版的作者；该版及引文页未独立查阅。原书提及的1764年作者传闻仅作为该来源报告的传闻保留，不视为事实。

**English:** This unit registers the first volume of *Voyages en differens pays de l'Europe en 1774, 1775, et 1776*, cited in Haskell's Chapter 15, p. 369, note 1. Haskell gives The Hague, 1777, volume I, p. 211, and calls the author anonymous. CiNii attributes authorship to Pilati de Tassulo; an e-rara record for a related 1778 Swiss first-volume edition lists [Carlo Antonio Pilati]. Neither record settles the authorship of the 1777 Hague edition cited by Haskell; that edition and cited page have not been independently consulted. The reported 1764 authorship story remains a rumour attributed to this source, not an established fact.
