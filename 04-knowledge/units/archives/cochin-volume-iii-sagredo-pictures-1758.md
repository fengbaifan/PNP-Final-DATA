## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡按 Haskell 的引文定位记录 [Cochin](../persons/charles-nicolas-cochin.md) 第三卷中关于 Sagredo 宫绘画的记述。第9章 p.263 指向第三卷第143–150页；p.265 注2称 Cochin 于1758年记述当时位于 Sagredo 宫中的绘画，并称[《Angelo Custode》](../works/piazzetta-angelo-custode-altarpiece.md)为“tableau fort beau”。完整书名、版本及引文上下文在此处没有独立核验；本卡不将 Haskell 的引文定位扩展为已读原书。〔[第9章 p.263](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)（L237）；[p.265 注2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)（L297）〕

**English:** This unit records the citation locator in Haskell for [Cochin](../persons/charles-nicolas-cochin.md)'s third volume and its account of paintings in the Sagredo palace. Chapter 9, p. 263 points to volume III, pages 143–150; note 2 on p. 265 says Cochin wrote an account in 1758 of pictures then in the Sagredo palace and called the [*Angelo Custode*](../works/piazzetta-angelo-custode-altarpiece.md) “tableau fort beau.” The full title, edition, and cited context have not been independently verified here; Haskell's citation is not treated as evidence that the original volume was read. ([Chapter 9, p. 263](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md) (line 237); [p. 265, note 2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md) (line 297).)

### 文献定位

- **文献类型：** Cochin 著作中的第三卷段落；此处按作者与卷页定位。
- **年代语境：** Haskell 称该记述作于1758年。
- **页码：** Haskell 引为第143–150页；注2另指第150页。
