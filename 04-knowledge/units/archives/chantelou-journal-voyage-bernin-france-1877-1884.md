## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本 KU 登记Haskell书目所列Chantelou日记的1877–1884年刊本：原题名为 *Journal du voyage du chev. Bernin en France*，L. Lalanne在 *Gazette des Beaux-Arts* 刊布并加注。第7章p.187注6列出Chantelou作为参考来源。日记手稿与刊本均未独立查阅；“刊布并注释”不自动建立未经裁定的责任关系。

**English:** This unit records the 1877–1884 publication of Chantelou’s journal listed in Haskell’s bibliography: *Journal du voyage du chev. Bernin en France*, published and annotated by L. Lalanne in *Gazette des Beaux-Arts*. Chapter 7, p.187 n.6 names Chantelou as a reference. Neither the manuscript journal nor the printed publication was independently consulted; “published and annotated” does not by itself create an unadjudicated responsibility relation.


### 身份边界

身份限于Haskell书目所指刊本；出版年段和Lalanne责任形式按书目原文保留，不推断手稿馆藏或刊本内容。
