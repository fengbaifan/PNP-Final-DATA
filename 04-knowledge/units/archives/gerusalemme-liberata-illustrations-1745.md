## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 1745年由阿尔布里齐在威尼斯出版、皮亚泽塔设计插图的托尔夸托·塔索《耶路撒冷解放》版本。《赞助人与画家》图版57a采用其插图末页。

**English:** The 1745 Venice edition of Torquato Tasso’s Jerusalem Delivered, published by Albrizzi with illustrations designed by Piazzetta. Its final illustration appears as plate 57a in Patrons and Painters.
