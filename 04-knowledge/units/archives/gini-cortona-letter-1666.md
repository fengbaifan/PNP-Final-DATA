### 描述

**中文：** 萨伏依驻罗马代表 Gini 的信，由 Claretta 1885 p.516 概述。科尔托纳拒绝自行提出画题，并称以往未这样做；收信人、月日及原件档号未知。

**English:** Claretta 1885, page 516, summarizes this letter by the Savoy resident in Rome, Gini. Cortona refuses to propose subjects himself and says he has never done so; the recipient, exact date and archival reference remain unknown.
