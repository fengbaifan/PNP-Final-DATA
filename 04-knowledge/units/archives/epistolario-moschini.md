## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔称为Epistolario Moschini的手稿书信系列。第16章注2和第19章附录7将相关书信定位于威尼斯Biblioteca Correr；第17章注6写作Epistolario Meschini并回指附录7。保留书内拼写差异；本系列及其馆藏目录未独立查阅。 [第16章 p.373](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/16_CHP-16_intro.md#L71)。

**English:** This unit records the manuscript correspondence series that Haskell calls the Epistolario Moschini. Chapter 16, note 2, and Chapter 19, Appendix 7, place related letters at Biblioteca Correr, Venice; Chapter 17, note 6 spells the name Epistolario Meschini and refers to Appendix 7. The source variants are retained, and neither the series nor its catalog has been independently consulted. [Chapter 16, p. 373](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/16_CHP-16_intro.md#L71).
