## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔所述科雷尔于1787年写给威尼斯总督的一封信：他因经济拮据无法就任特雷维索总督，并申述其处境。第381页注5以“ibid.”回指同页注4的档案定位，并列出1468/10号单元中的第5项。原信、档案目录和当时总督的个人身份均未查明。

**English:** This card records Haskell’s report of a 1787 letter from Correr to the Doge of Venice explaining that lack of means prevented him from taking up the Treviso governorship. Note 5 on p. 381 refers back to note 4’s archival locator and identifies item 5 in 1468/10. The original letter, archival catalogue, and identity of the Doge in office were not checked.
