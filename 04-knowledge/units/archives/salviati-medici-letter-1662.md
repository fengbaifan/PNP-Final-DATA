## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 有关向 Claude 购画及其不向高身份买家预定价格的报告，来源为 Boyer 刊引。

**English:** This report concerns purchases from Claude and his practice of not fixing prices in advance for buyers of high standing. It is cited from Boyer's publication.
