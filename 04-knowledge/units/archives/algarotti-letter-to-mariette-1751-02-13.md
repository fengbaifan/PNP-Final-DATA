## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡按Haskell第14章p.351注2登记Algarotti致Pierre-Jean Mariette、日期为1751年2月13日的书信。注释给出 *Opere, VW, pp. 15–40* 及Posse（1931）的书内定位；OCR转录中的“VW”照录，未凭推测改成“VIII”。这里只记录Haskell的书内引注，未查阅信件原件、*Opere*刊本或Posse文献；题名为描述性暂题。

**English:** This card records the letter from Francesco Algarotti to Pierre-Jean Mariette dated 13 February 1751, as cited in Haskell’s Chapter 14, p. 351, note 2. The note gives the in-book locator “Opere, VW, pp. 15–40” and also cites Posse (1931); the OCR reading “VW” is retained rather than silently normalized to “VIII”. The letter, the *Opere* edition, and Posse were not independently consulted. The title is descriptive.
