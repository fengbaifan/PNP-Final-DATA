## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本知识元指 Francesco Marucelli 的书目手稿 Mare Magnum，与 1888 年印行的索引及 Haskell 所称 Guido Biagi 导论分别建模。

**English:** This unit represents Francesco Marucelli’s manuscript bibliography, the Mare Magnum. It remains distinct from the printed index published in 1888 and from Haskell’s cited introduction by Guido Biagi.
