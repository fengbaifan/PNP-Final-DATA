## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell第2、3、6章均以Pastor卷XIV为引文对象；第3章明确标为 `parte II`。S3结合卷册书目结构，将第3章第二部分和第6章所引同卷页码归入卷XIV第一、二部分构成的一个书目身份组。第21章另列该系列的Pio Cenci意大利译本及Roma 1943，但未给卷号，因此不将1943年、出版地或具体印次套用于本卷。本卡的中英文题名为描述性工作题名；被引原页未独立查阅。

**English:** Haskell cites Pastor's volume XIV in Chapters 2, 3, and 6; Chapter 3 explicitly identifies `parte II`. Based on the bibliographic structure reviewed at S3, the Chapter 3 citation and the Chapter 6 citations to the same volume are grouped as the two-part volume XIV publication identity. Chapter 21 separately lists Pio Cenci's Italian version of the series and Rome, 1943, without a volume number, so that date, place, and printing are not assigned to this volume. The Chinese and English titles are descriptive working titles; the cited pages were not independently consulted.
