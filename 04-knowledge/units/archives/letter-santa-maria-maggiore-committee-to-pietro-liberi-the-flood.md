## 内容

### 描述
**中文：** Haskell 记述圣母玛利亚大殿委员会在 Pietro Liberi 的《洪水》画布抵达后致信画家，表示画作未达到其预期，并引录信件中的一段话。本卡描述的是 Haskell 所转引的书内文献对象；未核阅或识别信件原件、档号、日期或完整文本。

**English:** Haskell reports that the Santa Maria Maggiore committee wrote to Pietro Liberi after the arrival of his canvas The Flood, saying it did not meet expectations, and quotes part of the letter. This card records the document as cited in Haskell; the original letter, archival identifier, date, and complete text have not been established here.
