## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell第21章书目所列的1664年《Nota delli Musei》附录项目。原书将责任者写作带方括号的“[Bellori, G. P.]”；Treccani指出该题名见于1663、1664、1667年的刊本，且归属Bellori尚未完全证实。因此，本卡将身份范围限定为Haskell所列的1664年书目项目，不确认Bellori著者身份，也不表示本项目已读刊本或被引页。中文题名为描述性工作译名。

**English:** This card records the 1664 *Nota delli Musei* appendix item listed in Haskell’s chapter 21 bibliography. Haskell prints the responsibility form in brackets as “[Bellori, G. P.]”; Treccani records manifestations in 1663, 1664, and 1667 and says attribution to Bellori is not fully proven. This card is limited to Haskell’s 1664 bibliographic item; it does not confirm Bellori’s authorship or claim the publication or cited pages were read here. The Chinese title is a descriptive working translation.
