## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 发给未具名公国大臣，强调及时向罗马画家支付定金；未知收信人不虚构。

**English:** This letter to an unnamed ducal minister stresses prompt payment of deposits to painters in Rome. The recipient's name is not supplied.
