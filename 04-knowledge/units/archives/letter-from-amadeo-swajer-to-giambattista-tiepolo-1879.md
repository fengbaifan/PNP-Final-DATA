## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡标识哈斯克尔第16章注3所述、由阿马德奥·斯瓦耶尔寄给乔万巴蒂斯塔·提埃坡罗的一封信。注释称该信刊于 Urbani de Ghelthof 1879 年出版物第19页；这里登记的是被书内引注识别的信件文本，不据此断定原件的形态或馆藏。信中提到一幅尚未识别的提埃坡罗绘画。原信和被引刊本页未独立查阅；卡名为描述性工作题名，并非原件题名。

**English:** This card identifies the letter from Amadeo Swajer to Giambattista Tiepolo cited in Haskell’s Chapter 16, note 3. The note locates it on p. 19 of Urbani de Ghelthof’s 1879 publication. This unit represents the letter text identified by that citation and does not establish the form or custody of an original manuscript. The letter refers to an unidentified Tiepolo picture. Neither the letter nor the cited publication page was independently consulted. The card title is descriptive, not an original title.
