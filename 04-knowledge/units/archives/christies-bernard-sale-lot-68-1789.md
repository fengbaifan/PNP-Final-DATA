## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell注1转述的拍卖目录条目：据称Sir Anthony Blunt提醒Haskell，Sir Robert Bernard于1789年5月9日在Christie’s举行的拍卖中，第68号拍品被描述为“Petoni—A pair of Triumphal Mausoleums”。本卡只标识Haskell转述的第68号条目；原拍卖目录未独立查阅，不把Petoni与Paltronieri或Pittoni等同。

**English:** This card records the catalogue entry as reported by Haskell in note 1: he says Sir Anthony Blunt pointed out that lot 68 in Sir Robert Bernard’s sale at Christie’s on 9 May 1789 was described as “Petoni—A pair of Triumphal Mausoleums.” It identifies the reported lot entry only. The sale catalogue was not independently consulted, and Petoni is not equated with Paltronieri or Pittoni.
