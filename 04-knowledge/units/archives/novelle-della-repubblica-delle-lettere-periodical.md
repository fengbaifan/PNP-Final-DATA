## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell第13章印刷页334所称的周刊《Novelle della Repubblica delle Lettere》。书中称该刊评论并评介全欧洲近期出版的图书，注5说其出版时间“as from 1729”；这不是精确创刊日期。本卡中文名为项目描述性工作译名。期刊本身、具体刊期与其文章均未独立查阅。

**English:** This unit registers the weekly bulletin *Novelle della Repubblica delle Lettere* identified on printed page 334 of Haskell’s chapter 13. Haskell says it commented on and reviewed recent books published across Europe; note 5 says it was published “as from 1729,” which is not an exact founding date. The English title here is a descriptive working translation. Neither the periodical itself nor any particular issue or article has been independently consulted.
