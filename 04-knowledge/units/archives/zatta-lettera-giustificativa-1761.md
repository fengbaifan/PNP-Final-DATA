## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记 Haskell 第13章第338页注2所列的 *Lettera Giustificativa di Antonio Zatta*。Librinlinea 与 ADABI 的书目记录将其识别为 Antonio Zatta 署名、1761 年在威尼斯出版的专著；ADABI 另列完整题名及 83 页、八开本。该刊本题名还注明附有另一封答复信；S6 中的作者关系仅指题名所称的辩护信，不宣称 Zatta 独自撰写整册或附信。该出版物与同一注中的 *Lettera del Magnifico Signor Antonio Zatta a Sua Eccellenza il Signor Duca di...* 是两项不同书目对象。当前依据书目记录核实了出版身份，尚未阅读该刊本正文。

**English:** This card records the *Lettera Giustificativa di Antonio Zatta* cited in Haskell’s Chapter 13, p. 338, note 2. Librinlinea and ADABI catalogue it as a monograph under Antonio Zatta, published in Venice in 1761; ADABI also gives the full title and an octavo extent of 83 pages. The catalogue title says that another letter of response is included. The S6 authorship relation is limited to the justificatory letter named in the title; it does not claim that Zatta alone wrote the entire volume or the appended response. This publication is distinct from the *Lettera del Magnifico Signor Antonio Zatta a Sua Eccellenza il Signor Duca di...* listed in the same note. Its publication identity has been checked against catalogue records; the printed text has not been read.
