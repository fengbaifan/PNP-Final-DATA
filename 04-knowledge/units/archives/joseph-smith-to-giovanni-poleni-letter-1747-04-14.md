## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 报告 Joseph Smith 于1747年4月14日答复 Giovanni Poleni 4月9日的来信，并转录其中一句意大利文。脚注将两封 Poleni 来信定位于 Biblioteca Marciana, MSS. Ital:Cl.X, Cod CCLXXXVIII–6580；信件及原件未独立查阅。

**English:** Haskell reports that Joseph Smith answered Giovanni Poleni’s letter of 9 April 1747 on 14 April and transcribes a phrase from Smith’s reply. The note gives the Biblioteca Marciana shelfmark MSS. Ital:Cl.X, Cod CCLXXXVIII–6580; neither the letter nor manuscript was independently consulted.
