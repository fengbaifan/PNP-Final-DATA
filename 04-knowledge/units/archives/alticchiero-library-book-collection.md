## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔在第15章所述阿尔蒂基耶罗图书室的藏书集合。该段称图书室藏有重要经典，以及大量农业、哲学和神学类图书；原书未列具体题名或数量。本对象是藏书集合，不是图书室空间本身。

**English:** This unit records the book collection that Haskell describes as held in the library at Alticchiero. He says it contained the great classics and a large collection of books on agriculture, philosophy, and theology; the passage provides no itemised titles or count. The collection is distinct from the library room.
