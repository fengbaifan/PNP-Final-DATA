## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 乔万尼·洛多维科·比安科尼从德累斯顿写给菲利波·埃尔科拉尼侯爵的旅行与艺术见闻信。信中在描述奥格斯堡的古迹和艺术后，以约翰·埃万格利斯特·霍尔策为例反驳将杰出画家归因于疯狂、恶习或放荡生活的传闻；Haskell 截取的是这段批评艺术家逸闻传统的结语。（S1–S2）

**English:** Giovanni Ludovico Bianconi wrote this travel-and-art letter from Dresden to Marchese Filippo Hercolani. After describing Augsburg's antiquities and art, he used Johann Evangelist Holzer to reject popular stories that explained great painting through madness, vice, or libertine living; Haskell cites the concluding criticism of that biographical tradition. (S1–S2)
