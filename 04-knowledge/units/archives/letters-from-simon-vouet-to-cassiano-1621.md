## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第4章印刷页102注2所引的1621年西蒙·武埃致卡西亚诺书信。脚注列出 Bottari 卷 I 第331–333页及 Demonts 第322页作为刊录定位；该对应关系按哈斯克尔的书目指引记录，原信及两个被引版本均未独立查阅。卡名为描述性工作题名，不是原件题签。

**English:** This card records the 1621 letters from Simon Vouet to Cassiano cited in Haskell’s Chapter 4, p. 102, note 2. The note gives Bottari, vol. I, pp. 331–333, and Demonts, p. 322, as publication locators. These are recorded as Haskell’s references; neither the correspondence nor the cited editions was independently consulted. The card title is descriptive, not an original archival title.
