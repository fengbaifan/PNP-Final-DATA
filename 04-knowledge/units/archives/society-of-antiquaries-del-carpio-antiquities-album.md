## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应哈斯克尔第7章注5所述、并在注2再次指引的古物图绘册。哈斯克尔称图册属于 Society of Antiquaries，并转录其扉页文字；来源未给出分会、馆藏号或日期。该册与 cand-6955 的三十卷素描收藏保持区分。

**English:** This card represents the antiquities drawings album mentioned in Haskell’s chapter 7 note 5 and cited again in note 2. Haskell reports its association with the Society of Antiquaries and transcribes its frontispiece; no branch, shelfmark or date is supplied. It remains distinct from the thirty-volume drawing collection in cand-6955.
