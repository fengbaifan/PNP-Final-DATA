## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 指出 Saverio Savini 1608 年在 Gubbio 的委托条款由 Gualandi 卷 IV p.60 刊布。条款内容并未在本章展开，不能将邻接例子的自由度或题材复制过来，也不声称已见双方签字合同。

**English:** Haskell identifies terms for Saverio Savini's 1608 commission at Gubbio as published by Gualandi, volume IV, page 60. Their contents are not detailed here; the freedoms or subjects of neighbouring examples must not be transferred to them, and no signed original has been examined.
