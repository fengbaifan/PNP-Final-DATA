## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡标识 Robert Enggass 的期刊文章《The Altar-rail for St. Ignatius’ Chapel in the Gesù di Roma》。Haskell 第20章称其为讨论 Gesù 圣依纳爵祭坛的文章；注1为“Enggass, 1974”，第21章书目列出文章题名、期刊和页码。中文题名为项目工作译名；文章未独立查阅。

**English:** This unit identifies Robert Enggass's journal article *The Altar-rail for St. Ignatius’ Chapel in the Gesù di Roma*. Haskell describes it in chapter 20 as an article on the altar of St Ignatius in the Gesù; note 1 reads “Enggass, 1974,” and the chapter 21 bibliography supplies the title, journal, and pages. The Chinese title is a working translation. The article has not been independently consulted.
