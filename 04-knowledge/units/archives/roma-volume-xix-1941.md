## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应 Haskell 第6章脚注所引 *Roma* 第十九卷（1941）：注3称 1679年1月28日 avviso 刊于 p.161，注4称 2月18日 avviso 刊于 ibid.，未另列页码。此卡登记被引期刊卷册，不把两则 avvisi 合并为卷册本身，也不表示期刊或通知原文已查阅。 [第6章 p.163 注3–4](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_v.md#L57)

**English:** This unit represents the *Roma* periodical volume XIX (1941) cited in Haskell’s chapter 6 notes. Note 3 locates the avviso of 28 January 1679 at p. 161; note 4 says the avviso of 18 February was published ibid. and gives no separate page. The unit is the cited volume, not either notice, and neither the volume nor the avvisi was consulted. [第6章 p.163 注3–4](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_v.md#L57)
