## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔注释所引、经Pastor转引的1679年8月12日告示。注释转录对拟议讲坛画作的批评，并定位至Pastor《教宗史》第十四卷第二部分第26页。本卡不把未实施方案建为作品，也不表示已查阅告示或Pastor原书。

**English:** This card records the Avviso of 12 August 1679 cited through Pastor in Haskell’s note. The note quotes criticism of the proposed tribune paintings and locates the notice in volume XIV, part II, page 26 of Pastor’s history of the popes. This card does not treat the unrealized scheme as a work and does not imply consultation of the notice or Pastor’s volume.
