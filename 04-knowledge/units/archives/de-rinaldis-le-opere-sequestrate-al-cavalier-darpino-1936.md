## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应Haskell书目所列、刊于Archivi的1936年论文《Le opere d’arte sequestrate al Cavalier d’Arpino》。Haskell记录作者形式A. de Rinaldis和页码110–118；Galleria Borghese馆藏目录给出题名、作者形式A. de Rinaldis、刊名、卷期形式“2”、年份和页码。其卷期形式与Haskell所写“III”不同，当前保留两种原样著录，不作统一。论文正文和被引页未独立查阅。

**English:** This card represents the 1936 Archivi article Le opere d’arte sequestrate al Cavalier d’Arpino listed in Haskell’s bibliography. Haskell gives the author form A. de Rinaldis and pages 110–118; the Galleria Borghese collection bibliography provides a matching citation for the title, author form A. de Rinaldis, journal, volume notation “2”, year and page range. Its journal citation form differs from Haskell’s “III”; both forms are retained without normalization. The article and cited pages were not consulted. The journal citation form remains unresolved without checking the issue.
