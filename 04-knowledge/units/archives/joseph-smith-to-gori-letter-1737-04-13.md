## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 在第10章第306页注6中引用 Joseph Smith 于1737年4月13日致 A. F. Gori 的信，并称其见于 Biblioteca Marucelliana，回指第299页注4。手稿未独立查阅；信中提及的 Hadrian 头像对象未在本卡识别。

**English:** Haskell’s Chapter 10 note 6 quotes a letter from Joseph Smith to A. F. Gori dated 13 April 1737 and locates it at Biblioteca Marucelliana, referring back to note 4 on page 299. The manuscript was not independently consulted; the Head of Hadrian mentioned in the letter is not identified here.
