**English:** This unit records the four letters from Sasso to John Strange that Haskell reports in Chapter 16, p. 373, note 2, at the British Museum under Add. MSS. 23,730, folios 359–363. The manuscripts have not been independently consulted. This group remains distinct from the Strange-to-Sasso group in the Epistolario Moschini and from the separately registered 1777 letter.
