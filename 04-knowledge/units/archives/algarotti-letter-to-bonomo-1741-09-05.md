## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell第14章p.350正文所引的书信：作者当时在柏林写到一幅正在绘制的自己的肖像；注1将该信指向Bonomo并标注日期1741年9月5日和Treviso MSS. 1256。这里记录的是哈斯克尔书中报告的文书身份与引注，不表示信件原件、书写地点或档号已独立核查；该条目与第14章p.349的1734–1736年巴黎书信子集分开。

**English:** This card records the letter cited in Haskell’s Chapter 14, p. 350: while in Berlin, Algarotti wrote about a portrait of himself then being painted. Note 1 identifies the addressee as Bonomo, dates the letter 5 September 1741, and gives the in-book citation Treviso MSS. 1256. This is Haskell’s reported document identity and citation; the manuscript, place of writing, and shelfmark have not been independently checked. The item remains distinct from the Paris-letter subset described on p. 349.
