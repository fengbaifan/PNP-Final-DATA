**English:** This card records the Mazarin exhibition catalogue listed in Haskell’s chapter 21 bibliography. The original title is transcribed from that entry, which describes an exhibition organized for the third centenary of Mazarin’s death and lists Bibliothèque Nationale, Paris, 1961. The Chinese title and English catalogue wording are descriptive working titles, not verified published translations. The chapter 7 locators record Haskell’s citations only; neither the catalogue nor its page xxxiii was independently consulted.
