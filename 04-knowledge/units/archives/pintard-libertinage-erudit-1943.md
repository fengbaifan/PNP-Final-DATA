## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第21章书目所列 *Le libertinage érudit dans la première moitié du XVII siècle*（Paris，1943）。中文名与英文名为对原法文题名的描述性工作译名；书目中的责任者形式为“Pintard, R.”，其对应人物身份仍待证。第4章两条脚注分别引至本书第2章及第238页；本书和被引页均未独立查阅。[第21章书目 p.433](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/21_CHP-21Bibliography.md#L966)；[第4章 p.107、108 脚注](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md#L261)

**English:** This unit records *Le libertinage érudit dans la première moitié du XVII siècle* (Paris, 1943), as listed in Haskell’s chapter 21 bibliography. The Chinese and English names are descriptive working translations of the French title. The bibliography gives the responsibility form “Pintard, R.”; that form’s person identity remains unresolved. Two chapter 4 notes point to chapter 2 and page 238. Neither the book nor the cited passages was independently consulted. [Chapter 21 bibliography, p. 433](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/21_CHP-21Bibliography.md#L966); [Chapter 4 notes, pp. 107–108](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md#L261)
