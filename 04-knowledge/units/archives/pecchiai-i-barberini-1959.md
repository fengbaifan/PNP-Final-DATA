## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔书目所列P. Pecchiai著 *I Barberini*（Roma 1959）。第2章在开篇及后续脚注多次引用该书，涉及巴贝里尼与乌尔班八世的一般参考，以及Pecchiai所刊文件。第47页脚注称这些文件足以反驳Pastor关于Taddeo道德操守的说法；这是哈斯克尔对所引材料的评价，本项目未查读该书或独立判断争论。中文和英文展示题名为工作译名。[第2章开篇注1 p.24](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_intro.md#L7)；[第2章注3 p.41](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_ii.md#L187)；[第2章注2 p.47](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_iv.md#L206)；[第2章注1 p.54](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_iv.md#L232)；[第21章书目 p.433](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/21_CHP-21Bibliography.md#L940)

**English:** This card records P. Pecchiai’s *I Barberini* (Rome, 1959), as listed in Haskell’s bibliography. Chapter 2 cites the book several times, both as a general reference on the Barberini and Urban VIII and for documents published by Pecchiai. A note on page 47 says those documents effectively dispose of Pastor’s claim about Taddeo’s moral character. That is Haskell’s assessment of the cited material; the book has not been read here and the dispute has not been independently adjudicated. The Chinese and English display titles are working translations. - **英文工作译名：** *The Barberini Family*；不声称为正式英文题名。
