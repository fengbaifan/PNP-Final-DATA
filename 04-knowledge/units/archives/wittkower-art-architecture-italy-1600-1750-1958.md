## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell第21章书目列出R. Wittkower的 *Art and architecture in Italy 1600 to 1750*，出版地为London，年份为1958。OCR将书目年份写成“195 8”；第7章印刷页187注6的OCR又将“1958”误作“1938”。对应校读记录分别保存在S2 statement `st-chp21-bib-l1261-1299-entry-07` 与 `st-chp7-p187-n6-cite-wittkower` 的 qualifiers 中，见[原书断言记录](../../tables/book-statements.jsonl)。第2至7章的年份与引文定位共同支持本身份组。中文题名为工作译名；本卡只登记书目身份，没有独立查阅该书或被引页。正式作者关系留待S6。

**English:** Haskell’s Chapter 21 bibliography lists R. Wittkower’s *Art and architecture in Italy 1600 to 1750*, published in London in 1958. The OCR spaces the bibliography year as “195 8”; in Chapter 7, printed page 187, note 6, OCR misreads “1958” as “1938”. The qualifiers of S2 statements `st-chp21-bib-l1261-1299-entry-07` and `st-chp7-p187-n6-cite-wittkower` preserve the page-image readings in the [book statement records](../../tables/book-statements.jsonl). Citations across Chapters 2–7 support this publication identity. The Chinese title is a working translation. This card records the bibliographic identity only; neither the book nor its cited pages were independently consulted. The formal authorship relation remains for S6.
