## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 托马斯·科克于1620年10月8日致阿伦德尔伯爵托马斯·霍华德的信。本章经Hervey刊本转引其中对罗马教皇久任与家族机会的评论。刊本将它置于帕多瓦家户通信中，信中罗马来访者的消息不能证明发信地为罗马。

**English:** A letter from Thomas Coke to Thomas Howard, Earl of Arundel, dated 8 October 1620. Haskell quotes its remarks on papal longevity and family opportunities in Rome through Hervey's edition. The edition places it within correspondence concerning the household at Padua; news brought by a Roman visitor does not establish Rome as the place of writing.
