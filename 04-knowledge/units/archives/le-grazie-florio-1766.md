## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell在第9章p.251注2所引Daniele Florio《Le Grazie》的书目对象；所引诗句被用于说明Manin家族Passeriano乡间宅邸与St Peter’s之间的联系。卡片只记录书内引文所给信息，未声称查阅了1766年刊本或p. xli全文。

**English:** This card registers the bibliographic object cited by Haskell in chapter 9, note 2, as Daniele Florio’s *Le Grazie*. The cited verse is used to discuss a connection between the Manin country house at Passeriano and St Peter’s. The card records the citation only; it does not claim that the 1766 edition or page xli was consulted.

### 本章相关内容

印本页图将S0转录中的“Le Crazie”校读为“Le Grazie”；S0来源文本未改。Haskell引用该诗描写宅邸房间的彩色大理石，并称十八世纪诗人注意到St Peter’s的影响。诗歌原文未独立查阅，卡片不扩写为建筑实测或确定的设计来源。

The printed page image supports reading the S0 transcription “Le Crazie” as “Le Grazie”; the S0 source text remains unchanged. Haskell cites the poem for its description of the country-house rooms in coloured marble and says eighteenth-century poets noted St Peter’s influence. The poem itself was not independently consulted, and this card does not turn the citation into a building survey or a confirmed design source.
