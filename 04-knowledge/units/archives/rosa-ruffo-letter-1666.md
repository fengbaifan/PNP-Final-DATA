## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 罗萨自述只为满足自己、受热情驱动才作画；自我宣称不等于无经济依赖。

**English:** Rosa says that he paints only to satisfy himself and when moved by enthusiasm. This self-presentation does not establish freedom from economic dependence.
