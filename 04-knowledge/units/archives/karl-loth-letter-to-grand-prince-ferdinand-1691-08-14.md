### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记 Haskell 在第8章注5中提及的一封具体书信：Karl Loth 于1691年8月14日写给 Prince；篇章上下文将其指向 Grand Prince Ferdinand。Haskell称信中感谢对方赠送油和酒，并说自己将开始临摹提香《圣彼得殉难》。卡片标题为便于识别而拟定的描述性标题，不是原件题名。Haskell经 Fogolari 1937 转述；该刊本与原信均未在本任务中独立查阅，因而卡片只记录书内可追溯的文献身份和转述范围，不声称已读信件全文。[第8章 p.233 注5](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md)（L423）

**English:** This card registers a specific letter mentioned in Chapter 8 note 5: Karl Loth wrote to the Prince on 14 August 1691; the chapter context identifies the recipient as Grand Prince Ferdinand. Haskell says the letter thanks him for a gift of oil and wine and reports Loth’s intention to begin a copy after Titian’s St Peter Martyr. The bilingual title is descriptive and is not presented as an original title. Haskell reports the letter through Fogolari 1937; neither that publication nor the letter was independently consulted for this task. The card therefore records the document identity and the scope of the book’s report, not a claim to have read the letter in full.
