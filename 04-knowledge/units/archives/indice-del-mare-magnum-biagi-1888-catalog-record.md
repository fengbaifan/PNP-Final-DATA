## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本知识元指 Francesco Marucelli 的 Mare Magnum 索引印本。Haskell 第21章书目印作 Rome 1882；Biblioteca Marucelliana 的馆藏目录和 SBN 记录均著录为 Rome 1888。该年份差异保留为来源分歧，不改写 Haskell 原文，也不把它与 Mare Magnum 手稿或 Biagi 导论合并。

**English:** This unit represents the printed index to Francesco Marucelli’s Mare Magnum. Haskell’s chapter 21 bibliography prints Rome 1882, while the Biblioteca Marucelliana inventory and the SBN record catalogue the publication as Rome 1888. The date discrepancy is preserved as a source disagreement; the index remains distinct from the manuscript and Biagi’s introduction.
