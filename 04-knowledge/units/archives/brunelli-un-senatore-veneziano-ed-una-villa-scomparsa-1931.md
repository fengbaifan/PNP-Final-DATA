## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第21章书目所列 Bruno Brunelli 的文章 *Un Senatore Veneziano ed una villa scomparsa*。该书目将文章列于 *Le Tre Venezie*，1931年，第4–11页；第15章 p.369 注4以相同作者、年份及页码指向一篇讨论并图示 Alticchiero 遗址的文章。题名据书目页图核对；文章和期刊原件均未独立查阅。卡名的中文部分为工作翻译。

**English:** This card records Bruno Brunelli’s article *Un Senatore Veneziano ed una villa scomparsa*, listed in Haskell’s Chapter 21 bibliography in *Le Tre Venezie* (1931), pp. 4–11. Chapter 15, p. 369, note 4 points to an article on the remains of Alticchiero with the same author, year, and pages. The title was checked against the bibliography page image; neither the article nor the journal issue was independently consulted. The Chinese display title is a working translation.
