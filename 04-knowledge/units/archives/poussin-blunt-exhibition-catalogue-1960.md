## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡按Haskell第21章书目登记巴黎1960年的尼古拉·普桑展览目录，书目注明由Sir Anthony Blunt编制。第2、4、7章出现的展览目录引注按同一出版物组记录；本卡只确认书目对象与引注定位，未查阅目录或第46、65页。

**English:** This card records the Paris 1960 Nicolas Poussin exhibition catalogue listed in Haskell’s chapter 21 bibliography as prepared by Sir Anthony Blunt. The chapter 2, 4, and 7 citations are grouped as references to this publication. The catalogue and its cited pages 46 and 65 were not consulted.
