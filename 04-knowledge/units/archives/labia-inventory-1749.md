## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔引用1749年未刊的拉比亚绘画清单，称其中两项提埃波罗作品仅记为“Ritratto con cristallo”和“Una Palla”，并另引 Cignaroli。第250页注8续列若干题名；这些清单条目尚不能对应到已识别的存世作品。

**English:** Haskell cites an unpublished Labia picture inventory of 1749. Two Tiepolo entries are described only as “Ritratto con cristallo” and “Una Palla,” and a separate Cignaroli reference is cited. A continuation on page 250 lists further titles; these entries are not identified with surviving works.
