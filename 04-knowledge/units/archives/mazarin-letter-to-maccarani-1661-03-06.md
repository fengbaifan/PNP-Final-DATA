## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应既有候选 cand-6798。哈斯克尔第7章 p.183 正文引述 Mazarin 写给 Maccarani 的信，注3将其定位为1661年3月6日、收于《Lettres pendant son ministère》第IX卷第693页。书信及该页未独立查阅；正式关系仅记录书内所述作者、收信人和引注定位。

**English:** This card registers the existing candidate cand-6798. Haskell’s Chapter 7, p.183 quotes a letter Mazarin wrote to Maccarani; note 3 identifies it as dated 6 March 1661 and locates it at p.693 of vol. IX of *Lettres pendant son ministère*. Neither the letter nor that page was independently consulted; relations record only Haskell’s account and locator.
