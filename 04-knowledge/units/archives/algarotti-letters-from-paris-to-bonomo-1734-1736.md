## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell所述特雷维索书信汇集中的一部分：阿尔加罗蒂致Bonomo、来自巴黎、日期范围为1734年11月至1736年1月的若干书信。原文没有提供单封信清单，故本卡表示书信子集，不将其拆作未经识别的单件文书。“来自巴黎”保留为来源用语；本批没有将其解释为经确认的写信地或寄发地。

**English:** This card records a subset of the Treviso correspondence described by Haskell: several letters from Algarotti to Bonomo, from Paris, dated November 1734 to January 1736. The note gives no inventory of individual letters, so this remains a letter subset rather than a set of identified documents. “From Paris” is retained as the source wording; this batch does not normalize it as a verified place of composition or dispatch.
