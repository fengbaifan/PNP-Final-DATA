## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本知识元指 Haskell 第6章 p.160 注2所列的 1670年9月20日书信。双语标题是描述性题名，不冒充原件题签；Haskell称信件刊于 V. Ruffo 1916 年出版物第290页，该信件及刊载页未独立查阅。

**English:** This unit represents the letter dated 20 September 1670 cited in Haskell’s chapter 6, page 160, note 2. The bilingual title is descriptive, not an archival title. Haskell says the letter was published by V. Ruffo in 1916, page 290; neither the letter nor that publication page was independently consulted.
