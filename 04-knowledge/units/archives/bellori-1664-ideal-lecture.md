## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所述 Gian Pietro Bellori 于1664年在 Accademia di S. Luca 讲授的文本。第6章 p.159 又称该讲演后来作为 Bellori《Lives》的序言收入1672年出版物。Haskell 未在本段给出讲演的正式题名；“《艺术中的理想》讲演”为描述性工作名，不表示已查阅独立讲稿或某一现存版本。

**English:** This card records the lecture text that Haskell says Gian Pietro Bellori delivered to the Accademia di S. Luca in 1664. Chapter 6, p. 159, adds that the lecture was later included as a preface to Bellori's *Lives* published in 1672. Haskell does not give a formal title here; “lecture on the Ideal in art” is descriptive and does not imply consultation of a separate manuscript or a surviving edition.
