## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡按Haskell第14章p.347注1对一套阿尔加罗蒂著作十七卷本作描述性登记。注释将Michelessi的*Memorie*指向其中第一卷，并记该卷于1791年刊行、由Carlo Palese在Venice出版。本注未提供该套书的正式题名；此卡不据此合并本章其他*Opere*引注。具体卷册及版本未独立核查。

**English:** This card descriptively registers the seventeen-volume edition of Algarotti’s works cited in Haskell’s Chapter 14, p. 347 n. 1. The note places Michelessi’s *Memorie* in its first volume and reports that volume as published in 1791 by Carlo Palese in Venice. The note does not supply the edition’s formal title; this card does not use it to merge other *Opere* citations in the chapter. The volumes and edition have not been independently checked.
