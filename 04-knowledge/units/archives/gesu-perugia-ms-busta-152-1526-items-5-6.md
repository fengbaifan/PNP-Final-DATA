## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔脚注所载的佩鲁贾Gesù手稿定位“Busta 152/1526—5 and 6”。脚注将其用于Carlone在耶稣会教堂tribune作画及Negroni完成学业的叙述。手稿、档案层级和保管机构未独立查验；本卡只保存来源给出的定位。

**English:** This card records the manuscript locator “Busta 152/1526—5 and 6” given in Haskell’s note for Gesù in Perugia. The note uses it in connection with Carlone’s painting of the Jesuit church tribune and Negroni’s studies. The manuscript, archival hierarchy, and holding institution were not independently checked; the card preserves only the source-provided locator.
