## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称一份1762年绘画清单存于 Biblioteca Correr 的萨格雷多档案中，并称清单记为 Zuane Udni（英国领事）所寻。p.264扫描页将档号读作 MSS. P.D. C2193/VII；该定位本身不证明完成购买。

**English:** Haskell locates a 1762 picture list among the Sagredo papers at Biblioteca Correr and describes the pictures as sought by Zuane Udni, English consul. A scan of page 264 reads MSS. P.D. C2193/VII. The locator alone does not establish a completed purchase.

### 本章相关内容

Haskell locates a 1762 picture list in the Sagredo archives at Biblioteca Correr; the print scan reads MSS. P.D. C2193/VII. The locator says the pictures were sought by Zuane Udni.
