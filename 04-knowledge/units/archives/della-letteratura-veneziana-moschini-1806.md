## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell第21章书目所列1806年四卷本。p.379注1与p.381注1引第II卷第107页，p.380注4亦引同一卷页并转录对Manfrin画廊的描述。书名与责任者的印本形式据页图校读；原书及被引页未独立查阅。 [第17章p.379注1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/17_CHP-17_intro.md#L8)；[第21章p.431书目](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/21_CHP-21Bibliography.md#L849)

**English:** This unit records the four-volume 1806 work listed in Haskell’s chapter 21 bibliography. Chapters 17, notes 1 and 4 on pp. 379–380 and note 1 on p. 381 cite volume II, p. 107; note 4 quotes a description of Manfrin’s gallery. The printed name form is retained from the page image. Neither the book nor the cited page was independently consulted. [第17章p.379注1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/17_CHP-17_intro.md#L8); [第21章p.431书目](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/21_CHP-21Bibliography.md#L849)
