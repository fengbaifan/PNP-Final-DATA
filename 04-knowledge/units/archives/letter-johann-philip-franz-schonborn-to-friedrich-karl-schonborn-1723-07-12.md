## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第10章印刷页293注4所引、日期为1723年7月12日的 Johann Philip Franz 致 Friedrich Karl Schönborn 书信。该注称书信旨在介绍 Giovanni Antonio Pellegrini，并提及其在伦敦、巴黎和 Düsseldorf 的成功；注释将信息指向 Von Freeden（1955，第848页）。原信和被引页均未在本批独立查阅。中文显示名依人物卡工作音译，描述性题名并非原件题签。

**English:** This card records the letter from Johann Philip Franz to Friedrich Karl Schönborn, dated 12 July 1723, cited in Haskell’s Chapter 10, p. 293, note 4. The note says the letter was intended to introduce Giovanni Antonio Pellegrini and mentions his success in London, Paris, and Düsseldorf; it points to Von Freeden (1955, p. 848). Neither the letter nor the cited page was independently consulted in this batch. The descriptive title is not an original archival title.
