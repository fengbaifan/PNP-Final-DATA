## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡按 Haskell 第21章书目登记《La carta del navegar pittoresco》（书目署名 Marco Boschini，Venezia，1660）。中文和英文显示题名是工作译名，意大利文原题按书目保留。第7章 p.193 注2以 Boschini p.44 引用该书，并报告其题献 Archduke、讨论其威尼斯古代大师收藏；被引页及该书本身未由本项目独立查阅。题献与收藏对象关系仍按当前 S3/S6 类型边界待证，不据此建立超出来源范围的正式关系。

**English:** This card registers *La carta del navegar pittoresco* as listed in Haskell’s Chapter 21 bibliography under Marco Boschini (Venezia, 1660). The Chinese and English display titles are working translations; the Italian title is retained as printed. Chapter 7, p. 193 n. 2 cites Boschini p. 44 and reports a dedication to the Archduke and discussion of his Venetian old-master collection. The cited page and the publication itself have not been independently consulted. The dedication and collection relations remain subject to the current S3/S6 type boundaries; no wider claim is made.
