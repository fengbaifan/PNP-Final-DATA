### 描述

**English:** This unit records the 1958 published book Diario Romano listed under Giacinto Gigli in Haskell’s chapter 21 bibliography, which identifies Giuseppe Ricciotti as editor. The unit is the cited publication, not a diary manuscript. The four chapter 5 and 6 locators record Haskell’s in-book citations only; neither the volume nor its cited pages was consulted.
