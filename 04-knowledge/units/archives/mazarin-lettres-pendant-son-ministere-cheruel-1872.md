## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第21章书目所列的《Lettres pendant son ministère》：书目列 Mazarin, G.，称由 M. A. Chéruel 汇编并刊行，共9卷，Paris 1872–1906。第7章 p.183 注3 引第IX卷第693页，称其为1661年3月6日致 Maccarani 的信。该书与所引页未独立查阅。

**English:** This card records *Lettres pendant son ministère* as listed in Haskell’s Chapter 21 bibliography: the entry names Mazarin, G., says M. A. Chéruel collected and published it, and lists nine volumes, Paris 1872–1906. Chapter 7, p.183 n.3 cites vol. IX, p.693 for a letter to Maccarani dated 6 March 1661. Neither the edition nor the cited page was independently consulted.
