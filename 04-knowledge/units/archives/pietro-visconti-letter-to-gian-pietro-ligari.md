## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔第10章注6将这封彼得罗·维斯孔蒂致吉安·皮耶特罗·利加里的信定为1749年12月19日，并称刊于 Arslan 1952年论文第63页；发信人、收信人及刊录页码与第9章注3相合。信件及被引论文未独立查阅。

**English:** Haskell's Chapter 10 note 6 dates the letter from Pietro Visconti to Gian Pietro Ligari to 19 December 1749 and says it was published on page 63 of an Arslan article (1952). The sender, recipient and page locator match Chapter 9 note 3. The letter and cited article were not independently consulted.

### 本章相关内容

Haskell identifies the same letter in Chapter 9 note 3 and Chapter 10 note 6: it is dated 19 December 1749 and cited to Arslan (1952), p.63. Neither the letter nor the cited page was independently consulted.
