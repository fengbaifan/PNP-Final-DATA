## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记《Inventaire de tous les meubles du Cardinal Mazarin》的1861年伦敦刊本。第21章书目将其列为“d’Aumale, duc”名下，称该清单于1653年编制、依据Condé档案所藏原件刊行，并记录Londres 1861；书目OCR把 *meubles* 转录为 *tneubles*，此处保留原转录而按BnF Catalogue général的FRBNF30039849规范题名。BnF记录的出版项为Londres: Whittingham et Wilkins, 1861。第7章p.183注2与p.184注2分别引用该刊本p.8及pp.293 ff.，这些页面未独立查阅。此KU是1861年印刷刊本，不是其所据1653年清单原件；该原件已另行登记为[1653年清单原件KU](mazarin-inventory-manuscript-1653.md)，来自独立S3组 `archive-group-mazarin-inventory-1653`。作者形式“d’Aumale, duc”由BnF人物权威记录FRBNF12375307识别为[Henri d’Orléans, duc d’Aumale](../persons/henri-dorleans-duc-aumale.md)，人物知识元保持独立；正式作者关系留待S6审查。中文题名为项目工作译名。

**English:** This card records the 1861 London edition of *Inventaire de tous les meubles du Cardinal Mazarin*. Chapter 21’s bibliography lists it under “d’Aumale, duc,” describes the inventory as drawn up in 1653 from an original held in the Condé archives, and gives London, 1861. The bibliography OCR reads *tneubles*; that reading is preserved here, while the normalized title follows BnF Catalogue général record FRBNF30039849. The BnF imprint is Londres: Whittingham et Wilkins, 1861. Chapter 7, p. 183, note 2, and p. 184, note 2, cite pp. 8 and 293 ff. of this edition; those cited pages were not independently consulted. This KU is the 1861 printed edition, not the underlying 1653 inventory, which is separately registered as the [1653 inventory manuscript KU](mazarin-inventory-manuscript-1653.md) from the distinct S3 group `archive-group-mazarin-inventory-1653`. BnF person authority FRBNF12375307 identifies the responsibility form “d’Aumale, duc” as [Henri d’Orléans, duc d’Aumale](../persons/henri-dorleans-duc-aumale.md); the person KU remains separate, and the formal authorship relation awaits S6. The Chinese title is a project working translation.

### 身份边界

本KU表示1861年出版物，不把刊本与其所据的1653年清单原件合并。BnF书目记录支持刊本身份与出版项，不核验Haskell关于原件编制、保管沿革或所引页内容的陈述。作者形式已映射至独立人物KU；正式作者关系保持pending。
