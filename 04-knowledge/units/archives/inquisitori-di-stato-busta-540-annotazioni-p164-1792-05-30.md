## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡标识哈斯克尔第15、16章两处注释共同指向的档案定位：Archivio di Stato, Venice 的 Inquisitori di Stato 案卷540、Annotazioni第164页，日期为1792年5月30日。第16章注3称该记录提及Urbani de Ghelthof的文件与手稿；“His”按前句回指Urbani。原始档案记录未独立查阅，本卡不据此确认其内容、作者、所有权或保管状态。

**English:** This card identifies the archival locator cited in Haskell's notes in chapters 15 and 16: Archivio di Stato, Venice, Inquisitori di Stato, Busta 540, Annotazioni, p. 164, dated 30 May 1792. Note 3 in chapter 16 says that the record mentions Urbani de Ghelthof's papers and manuscripts; “His” refers to Urbani in the preceding sentence. The archival record itself was not consulted, so this card does not establish its contents, authorship, ownership, or custody.
