### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应Haskell第21章书目所列的Hinks 1953年Caravaggio专著。Haskell采用短题名“Michelangelo Merisi da Caravaggio”；Rijksmuseum目录所列全题名为“Michelangelo Merisi da Caravaggio: his life - his legend - his works”，责任者为Hinks, Roger (Roger Packman)，出版地London、出版年[1953]。作者、主题和出版年支持两条记录指向同一出版物。只记录书目身份；该书及Haskell引用的第104页未独立查阅。

**English:** This card represents the 1953 Caravaggio monograph listed in Haskell’s chapter 21 bibliography. Haskell gives the shortened title “Michelangelo Merisi da Caravaggio”; the Rijksmuseum catalogue records the full title “Michelangelo Merisi da Caravaggio: his life - his legend - his works,” author Hinks, Roger (Roger Packman), London, [1953]. The author, subject and publication year support the bibliographic match. This records publication identity only; the book and Haskell’s cited page 104 were not consulted.
