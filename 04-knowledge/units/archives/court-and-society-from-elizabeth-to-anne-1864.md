## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell第21章书目所列《Court and Society from Elizabeth to Anne》：两卷本，伦敦，1864年。书目责任者形式为“Manchester, Duke of”，但本批未识别具体作者，也不将其与第10章所称Lord Manchester（Charles Montagu，约1662–1722）等同。Haskell第10章p.278注5、注6分别以卷II通引和第140页引用该书；被引内容未查。中文题名为项目工作译名。

**English:** This unit records *Court and Society from Elizabeth to Anne* as listed in Haskell’s chapter 21 bibliography: two volumes, London, 1864. The bibliography gives the responsibility form “Manchester, Duke of”; this batch does not identify that person or equate the author with the Lord Manchester in chapter 10 (Charles Montagu, circa 1662–1722). Haskell’s chapter 10, p. 278, notes 5 and 6 cite volume II, including p. 140; the cited contents were not consulted. The Chinese title is a project translation.
