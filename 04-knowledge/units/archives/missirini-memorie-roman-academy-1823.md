## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记 Haskell 第21章书目列出的 *Memorie per servire alla storia della Romana Accademia di S. Luca*（Roma，1823）。第1章两条脚注分别引用第81、87页及第115页；第6章注3引用第116页。中文题名为描述性工作译名。Haskell的书目和脚注只证明本书中的题录与引注；本卡不声称查阅Missirini原书或这些被引页面。

**English:** This archive unit records the book listed in Haskell’s chapter 21 bibliography as *Memorie per servire alla storia della Romana Accademia di S. Luca* (Roma, 1823). Two chapter 1 notes cite pages 81 and 87, and page 115; chapter 6 note 3 cites page 116. The English title is a descriptive working translation. Haskell’s bibliography and notes document the entry and citation paths in his book; neither the Missirini volume nor its cited pages has been consulted here.
