## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第21章书目所列的1661年罗马出版物；书目将 Elpidio Benedetti 列为责任者。第7章 p.183 注3 引用第5页。原书及所引页未独立查阅，卡片只记录哈斯克尔的书目信息与引注。

**English:** This card records the 1661 Rome publication listed in Haskell’s Chapter 21 bibliography, which names Elpidio Benedetti as responsible author. Chapter 7, p.183 n.3 cites p.5. The publication and cited page were not independently consulted; the card records Haskell’s bibliographic entry and citation only.
