### 描述

**中文：** 本章只说 a Virgil with a commentary，用于选择四元素装饰神话；未给书名、注释者和版本。保留语境文献身份，不直接补成某版《埃涅阿斯纪》。

**English:** The chapter mentions only a Virgil with a commentary, used in choosing myths for the Four Elements decoration. No title, commentator or edition is supplied; it is not automatically identified as an edition of the Aeneid.
