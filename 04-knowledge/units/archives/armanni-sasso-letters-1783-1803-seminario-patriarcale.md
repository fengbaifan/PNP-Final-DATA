## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 第16章注3所称 G. A. Armanni 与 G. M. Sasso 之间的两卷书信材料，时间范围据该注为1783–1803，馆藏号为MSS. 565、566。中文标题为描述性译名；`Lettere pittoriche` 保留作来源称谓，不冒称经目录确认的原件题名。手稿未独立查阅。该对象是未刊／档案卷册，不同于既有 Vincenzo Armanni 17世纪刊本《Delle lettere...》。

**English:** This card records the two-volume correspondence described in Haskell, Chapter 16, note 3, between G. A. Armanni and G. M. Sasso. The note gives 1783–1803, a broadly chronological arrangement, and shelfmarks MSS. 565 and 566. The Chinese title is descriptive; `Lettere pittoriche` is retained as Haskell’s wording, not asserted as a catalog-verified manuscript title. The manuscripts were not independently consulted. This archival corpus is distinct from the existing seventeenth-century printed collection by Vincenzo Armanni, *Delle lettere...*.
