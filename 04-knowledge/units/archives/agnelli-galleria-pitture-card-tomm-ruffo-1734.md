## 内容

### 描述
**中文：** Haskell书目著录J. Agnelli于1734年在费拉拉出版的《Galleria di pitture del Card. Tomm. Ruffo vescovo di Ferrara》。第8章注4以Ruffo为短引，指向该书第122–123页；第8章注2还称Ruffo的画作清单与J. Agnelli的诗体评注一同刊行。此卡记录书内书目和引文定位，未核读该书内容；作者身份仍仅有首字母。

**English:** Haskell’s bibliography lists J. Agnelli’s Galleria di pitture del Card. Tomm. Ruffo vescovo di Ferrara, published at Ferrara in 1734. Chapter 8 note 4 resolves the short form Ruffo to this publication and gives pages 122–123; note 2 says the list of Ruffo’s pictures was published with verse commentaries by J. Agnelli. This card records the book bibliography and citation locator only; the publication itself was not read, and the contributor’s identity remains at initial level.
