## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡标识 Haskell 第4章 p.112 注6 所引的一组支付记录。注释用语为“appear to be dated 1656”；印本页图补足 OCR 截断的档号为 Biblioteca Vaticana, Barb. Lat. 5635。记录未实际查阅，Cardinal Barberini 的具体身份及 Cassiano 的岗位均未确定。中文题名为描述性工作题名。

**English:** This card identifies payment records cited in Haskell’s Chapter 4, p. 112, note 6. The note says they “appear to be dated 1656”; the print scan supplies the shelfmark omitted by OCR as Biblioteca Vaticana, Barb. Lat. 5635. The records were not consulted. The Cardinal Barberini’s identity and Cassiano’s specific post remain unresolved. The Chinese title is descriptive, not an archival title.
