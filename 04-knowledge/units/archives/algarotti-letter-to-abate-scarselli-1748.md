## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 第14章 p.357 注2所述的1748年书信。注2中的“he”沿用前文明确谈论的 Francesco Algarotti；收信人依原文保留为 Abate Scarselli，个人身份仍待消歧。标题是项目描述性题名，不是原信题名。Haskell所引 *Opere*, XIII, p.207 与信件原件均未独立查阅。

**English:** This unit records the 1748 letter reported in Haskell’s Chapter 14, p.357, note 2. The note’s “he” continues the preceding account of Francesco Algarotti; the recipient is retained as “Abate Scarselli,” whose personal identity remains unresolved. The title is descriptive and is not presented as the letter’s original title. Neither the cited *Opere*, XIII, p.207 nor the original letter was independently consulted.
