## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell第10章注1所述、由James致Robert Adam且日期为1760年8月20日的书信。Haskell感谢John Fleming向其展示副本；第19章附录再引其中关于Joseph Smith财务状况的评述，并标注转引自Fleming 1962年版第270页。原信与Fleming刊本均未查阅；现有材料没有给出手稿馆藏或编号。中文和英文题名为描述性题名，不是原信题名。

**English:** This card records the letter from James to Robert Adam dated 20 August 1760, identified in Haskell’s Chapter 10, note 1. Haskell thanks John Fleming for showing him a copy; Appendix 5 later quotes the letter’s assessment of Joseph Smith’s finances and cites Fleming 1962, p. 270. Neither the original letter nor Fleming’s publication has been consulted, and no manuscript repository or identifier is supplied. The bilingual title is descriptive, not an original title.
