## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell第8章注4所引的Fogolari 1937年Letter 120（1707年9月3日）。Haskell正文称Ferdinand于1707年写信给Cassana；p.240注4用“ibid.”指回Fogolari的书目来源并给出具体信件编号，p.234注4也引用同一编号。题名是便于识别的描述性名称，不是原信题名。当前记录依据Haskell的叙述和引注，不表示已查阅Fogolari文章或原信；原件保管机构及档号未由这些引注确定。

**English:** This card records Fogolari’s 1937 Letter 120 (3 September 1707) as cited in Haskell’s Chapter 8, note 4. Haskell’s body text says Ferdinand wrote to Cassana in 1707; note 4 on p. 240 uses “ibid.” to refer to the Fogolari source and supplies the specific letter number, which is also cited in note 4 on p. 234. The title is descriptive, not the original letter title. This record follows Haskell’s report and citation; neither the Fogolari article nor the original letter was consulted, and these notes do not identify the repository or shelfmark.
