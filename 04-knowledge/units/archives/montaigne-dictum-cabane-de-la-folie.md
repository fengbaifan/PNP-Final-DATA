## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔称固定于“cabane de la folie”门上的法文格言。哈斯克尔将其归于蒙田；本页没有给出文本版本或可供独立核对的出处，因此卡片仅记录该书内归属表述。

**English:** This card records the French dictum that Haskell says was fixed to the door of the “cabane de la folie.” Haskell attributes it to Montaigne; this page supplies no textual edition or source for independent checking, so the card preserves only that attribution as reported in the book.
