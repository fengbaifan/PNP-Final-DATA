**English:** This unit represents the second edition of *La letteratura artistica* (Florence, 1956) cited in Haskell’s chapter 6 note and listed in chapter 21. The SBN record gives the authorized main-author form Julius von Schlosser and names Julius Schlosser Magnino in the title statement; the authority match resolves this book’s author only and adds no broader biography.
