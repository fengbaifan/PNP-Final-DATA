## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记 Haskell 第21章书目所列的 *Voyage d’Italie . . . fait aux années 1675 et 1676*：书目列 Jacob Spon 与 George Wheler 为责任者，2卷，La Haye，1724。第6章 p.157 注1与第7章 p.183 注3分别将第一卷 p.232、p.236 列为引文位置；这两个被引页均未独立查阅。第6章 OCR 将 Wheler 误作 Wilder，书目及页图读作 Wheler；第7章注3的 OCR 读作 Span，S2 页图校读为 Spon。书名中的间隔省略号按来源保留；不据书目记录声称已核验版本或内容。

**English:** This card records *Voyage d’Italie . . . fait aux années 1675 et 1676* as listed in Haskell's Chapter 21 bibliography, which names Jacob Spon and George Wheler as responsible authors and gives two volumes, La Haye, 1724. Chapter 6, p.157 n.1, and Chapter 7, p.183 n.3, separately cite volume I, pp.232 and 236; neither cited page was independently consulted. Chapter 6 OCR reads “Wilder” for Wheler; the bibliography and page image read “Wheler.” Chapter 7 note 3 OCR reads “Span”; S2 records the page-image correction to “Spon.” The spaced ellipsis is retained from the source. The bibliography alone does not independently verify the edition or contents.
