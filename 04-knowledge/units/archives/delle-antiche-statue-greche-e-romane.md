## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记 Haskell 第13章所述 *Delle Antiche Statue Greche e Romane*（工作译名《古希腊与罗马雕像》）。Haskell 称这部作品始于1725年，并在十五年后由 Albrizzi 出版；原文没有在该处直接给出出版年，因此本卡不作年份换算。书末索引把同一题名列在 Zanetti, A. M., the Elder 子目下，与正文候选共同指向这一文献身份。Haskell 还称书中把幻想从属于学术要求，其版画标示新古典趣味传入威尼斯的重要阶段。原书与具体版本均未独立查阅。[第13章 p.343](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md)（L114）；[第13章 p.344](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md)（L117）；[书末索引](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-1-Index-MD/UVWXYZ.md)（L188）

**English:** This card records *Delle Antiche Statue Greche e Romane*, described in Haskell’s Chapter 13 (working English title: *On Ancient Greek and Roman Statues*). Haskell says the work was begun in 1725 and published fifteen years later by Albrizzi; the passage does not state a publication year directly, so no year is calculated here. The book index lists the same title under the subentry for Zanetti, A. M., the Elder, aligning the index and body candidates as one documentary identity. Haskell also says that the work subordinated fantasy to scholarly demands and that its plates marked an important stage in the arrival of neo-classical taste in Venice. The book and any particular edition have not been independently consulted.
