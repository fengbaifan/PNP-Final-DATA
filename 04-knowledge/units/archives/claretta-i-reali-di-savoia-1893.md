## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应哈斯克尔第21章书目所列 Gaudenzio Claretta 的1893年论文。中文名与英文名是对意大利文原题的工作译名，并非已知正式译名。第3章 p.65 注2引 p.33；第7章 p.202 注1引 pp.1–309。论文及被引页面未独立查阅。

**English:** This unit records Gaudenzio Claretta’s 1893 article listed in Haskell’s chapter 21 bibliography. The Chinese and English titles are working translations of the Italian title, not established published translations. Chapter 3, p. 65 n. 2 cites p. 33; chapter 7, p. 202 n. 1 cites pp. 1–309. Neither the article nor its cited pages was independently consulted.
