## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell第2、3、6章多次以 Pastor 卷XIII为引文对象；S3确认这些跨章引用指向同一卷册身份组。外部书目支持本卷的题名和范围，但不能确定 Haskell 所引的具体印次。第21章列出的 Pio Cenci 意大利译本、Roma 1943 条目没有卷号，不据以推定本卷的出版年、出版地或版本。此卡登记书目身份而非所引页内容；中英文题名为描述性工作题名，原书及被引页面未独立查阅。

**English:** Haskell repeatedly cites Pastor's volume XIII in Chapters 2, 3, and 6; S3 identifies these cross-chapter references as one volume-level identity group. External bibliographies support the volume's title and scope but do not identify the printing cited by Haskell. The Chapter 21 entry for Pio Cenci's Italian version, Rome 1943, gives no volume number and is not used to infer this volume's publication year, place, or edition. This card records bibliographic identity, not the contents of the cited pages; both titles are descriptive working titles, and neither the volume nor its cited pages was independently consulted.
