**中文：** 帕斯夸利版卡洛·戈尔多尼《喜剧集》的第二卷，原书图版目录标年1761；本书图版57b采用该卷的诺韦利卷首图。

**English:** The second volume of the Pasquali edition of Carlo Goldoni’s Comedies, dated 1761 in the source book’s plate list; its frontispiece by Novelli appears as plate 57b.
