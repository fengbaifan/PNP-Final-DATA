## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记《赞助人与画家》第六章第154页注4所引、并见于第21章书目的1925年《Roma》论文。哈斯克尔书目题名为 *Il museo di curiosità del Cardinale Flavio Chigi Seniore*；作者1966年论文中的自引和梵蒂冈宗座档案馆的相关书目均支持这一题名及页码539–544。Treccani条目出现另一种1925年题名措辞，作为异文保留；现有证据不足以据此改写本卡对应的引文身份。被引论文及其页码未独立阅读。

**English:** This card records the 1925 article in *Roma* cited in chapter 6, page 154, note 4, and listed in chapter 21 of *Patrons and Painters*. Haskell gives the title *Il museo di curiosità del Cardinale Flavio Chigi Seniore*. The author’s 1966 self-reference and the Vatican Apostolic Archive bibliography support this title and pages 539–544. A Treccani entry gives different wording for a 1925 title; it is retained as a variant and does not replace the identity supported by the book, the author’s self-reference, and the Vatican listing. The cited article and pages were not independently read.
