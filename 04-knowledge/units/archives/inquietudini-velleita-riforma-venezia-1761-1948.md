**English:** This card records the 1948 journal article cited in Haskell’s Chapter 15, page 368, note 7. The Chapter 21 bibliography lists *Inquietudini e velleità di riforma a Venezia nel 1761-2*, under the printed author form “Bozzòla, A.,” in *Bollettino Storico-Bibliografico Subalpino*, pages 93–116. The Chinese title is a working translation. The bibliography page image was checked for the author form and entry layout; neither the article nor the cited content was independently consulted. This article remains separate from the 1956 *Casanova illuminista*, which has a different title and year; the author’s personal identity was not resolved in this batch.
