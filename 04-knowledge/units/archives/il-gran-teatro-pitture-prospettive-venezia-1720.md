## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第13章所述的1720年出版物 *Il Gran Teatro delle Pitture e Prospettive di Venezia*。书内称其由 Domenico Lovisa 出版，载有著名纪念建筑的图绘及多位艺术家的画作，包括年轻时的 Giambattista Tiepolo，并称两种威尼斯出版物面向旅行者；第333页将本书回指为 Lovisa 资助的出版项目。上述信息均为哈斯克尔的书内记载，未独立查阅该出版物。中英文显示名均为描述性工作译名。[第13章 p.332](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md)（L12） [第13章 p.333](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md)（L16）

**English:** This card records the 1720 publication *Il Gran Teatro delle Pitture e Prospettive di Venezia* described in Haskell’s Chapter 13. Haskell says that Domenico Lovisa published it, that it contains drawings of famous monuments and pictures by several artists, including the young Giambattista Tiepolo, and that the two Venetian publications discussed there were intended for travellers. On p. 333 he refers back to this book as a publishing venture sponsored by Lovisa. These are statements in Haskell’s book; the publication was not independently consulted. The Chinese and English display names are descriptive working translations.
