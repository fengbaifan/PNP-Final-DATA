## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第8章注6所引的 Archivio Mediceo, Filza 5904, No. 272 来信。哈斯克尔称该信报告克雷斯皮回到博洛尼亚；此处仅记录书内转述，未查阅信件原件，也不将引文日期直接当作克雷斯皮返程日期。

**English:** This card records the letter cited in Haskell’s chapter 8, note 6: Archivio Mediceo, Filza 5904, No.272. Haskell says it reports Crespi’s return to Bologna. This records Haskell’s account only; the original letter was not consulted, and its cited date is not treated as Crespi’s return date.
