## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记 Haskell 第4章所称 Marino 最著名的诗 *Adone*；“《阿多涅》”为中文工作题名。书末索引将该题名列在 Marino, Giambattista 名下。Haskell 另说 Poussin 的相关图像“主要”取材于神话主题，并“松散地”受 *Adone* 启发；两项限定均保留，图像与诗作是不同知识元。原诗及具体版本未独立查阅，本卡不补充出版年、版本或存本。[第4章 p.115](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md)（L186）；[书末索引](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-1-Index-MD/M.md)（L93）

**English:** This card records *Adone*, which Haskell calls Marino’s most famous poem in Chapter 4; 《阿多涅》 is a working Chinese title. The book index lists the title under Marino, Giambattista. Haskell also says that Poussin’s related drawings consist “mainly” of mythological subjects and are “loosely” inspired by *Adone*; both qualifications are retained, and the drawings remain a separate knowledge unit. The poem and any particular edition have not been independently consulted. No publication year, edition, or copy is supplied here.
