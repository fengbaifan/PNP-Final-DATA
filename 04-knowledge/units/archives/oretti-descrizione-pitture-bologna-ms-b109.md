## 内容

### 描述
**中文：** Haskell第8章印刷页203注5称，博洛尼亚市立图书馆藏手稿B.109是有关Belloni、Bellucci及博洛尼亚家族收藏的来源，并把题名归于Marcello Oretti。扫描页确认题名中的“che”与“Città”；Markdown OCR转录有误。此卡记录Haskell对手稿的描述，不表示已查阅手稿，也不据“essential”断言其内容完整。

**English:** Haskell’s Chapter 8, printed p.203 note 5 describes manuscript B.109 in the Biblioteca Comunale, Bologna as a source for the collections of Belloni, Bellucci, and Bolognese families, and attributes the title to Marcello Oretti. The page image confirms “che” and “Città” in the title where the Markdown OCR is faulty. This card records Haskell’s description only; the manuscript was not consulted, and “essential” is not treated as an independent completeness claim.
