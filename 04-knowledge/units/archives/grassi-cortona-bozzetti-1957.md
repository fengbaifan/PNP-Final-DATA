## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 路易吉·格拉西的论文研究彼得罗·达·科尔托纳为多利亚潘菲利宫画廊壁画所作的油画草稿。作者在讨论科尔托纳的装饰性空间语言后，逐一比较壁画与罗马帕特里齐收藏的七件草稿，主张它们约作于1651年末至1652年初并出自科尔托纳本人；这是一项有论证的归属意见，不应转写为无争议事实。（S1–S3）

**English:** Luigi Grassi's article studies oil sketches associated with Pietro da Cortona's frescoes in the Doria Pamphili palace gallery. After framing Cortona's decorative conception of space, Grassi compares the frescoes with seven sketches in the Patrizi collection, dates them to about late 1651–early 1652, and argues that they are autograph works. This remains an argued attribution rather than an uncontested fact. (S1–S3)
