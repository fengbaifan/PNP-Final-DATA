## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 卡洛·夸里斯米尼写给文图拉·卡拉拉伯爵，报告彼得罗·保罗·拉吉正在为阿扎诺圣马蒂诺教堂创作的画，并请伯爵促使教堂管理者依惯例提供群青、已打底画布和预付款。信中明确预付款应在最终价款中扣计，既是工作激励，也是委托方应履行的常规条件。（S1–S2）

**English:** Carlo Quarismini wrote to Count Ventura Carrara about a painting Pietro Paolo Raggi was making for San Martino at Azzano. He asked Carrara to have the church deputies supply ultramarine, a primed canvas, and an advance, describing these as customary conditions and stating that the advance should be deducted from the final payment. (S1–S2)
