**English:** This card records Michelessi’s *Memorie* as cited in Haskell’s Chapter 14, p. 347 n. 1. This is the short title in the note; no full title, edition, or separate bibliographic record is given here, and the author’s identity is not further specified. Haskell says the work appeared six years after Algarotti’s death and was included in 1791 in the first volume of the seventeen-volume edition of Algarotti’s works.
