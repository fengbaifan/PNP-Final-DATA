## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell第16章注3所定位的一封1789年7月7日书信：注释称其属于G. A. Armanni致G. M. Sasso的通信材料，并称Mr Poore在信中被讨论。Haskell同时报告该通信材料为两卷、收藏号MSS. 565与566；注释未说明这封信所在的具体一卷。原件与档案目录未独立查阅。本卡标题为描述性译名，不冒充原件正式题名。

**English:** This card records a letter dated 7 July 1789 identified in Haskell’s Chapter 16, note 3. The note places it in the correspondence from G. A. Armanni to G. M. Sasso and says that Mr Poore is discussed. Haskell describes the correspondence as two volumes, MSS. 565 and 566, but does not assign this letter to a specific volume. The manuscript and archival catalog were not independently consulted. The title is descriptive, not asserted as an original title.
