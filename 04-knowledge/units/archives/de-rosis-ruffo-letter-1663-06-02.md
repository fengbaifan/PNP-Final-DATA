## 内容

### 描述

**中文：**1916年刊本收录一封日期为1663年6月2日、由朱塞佩·德·罗西斯签署并致安东尼奥·鲁福的信；刊文脚注说明所附待售画单由萨尔瓦多·罗萨制作。这里依据刊本文字转录，尚未定位或查阅原信手稿，也没有其原件保管地或档号。

**English:** A 1916 printed transcription gives a letter dated 2 June 1663, signed by Giuseppe de Rosis and addressed to Antonio Ruffo; its footnote identifies the attached sale note as prepared by Salvator Rosa. The manuscript itself has not been located or examined, and its present repository and archival reference remain unidentified.
