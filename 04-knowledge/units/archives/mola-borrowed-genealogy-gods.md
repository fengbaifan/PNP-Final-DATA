## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 记 Mola 向当地律师借阅 a genealogy of the gods，用于选择 Valmontone 的图像内容。可保留该借阅语境中的文献指代，但不能擅定为 Boccaccio 或其他作者某书；不与任意同类神谱合并。

**English:** Haskell records Mola borrowing a genealogy of the gods from a local lawyer to choose imagery for Valmontone. The documentary referent can be retained, but cannot be identified with Boccaccio or any other author without evidence.
