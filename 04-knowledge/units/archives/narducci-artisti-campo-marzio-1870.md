### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell书目所列E. Narducci关于1656年居住在罗马战神广场区艺术家的文章。第一章第20页注1引用其pp.122–126，讨论许多画家的贫困处境。书目OCR将期刊名转录为“II Buonarotti”，本卡保留该字形问题，不作刊名校勘。文章及被引页未在本批独立查阅。

**English:** This card records E. Narducci’s article on artists residing in Rome’s Campo Marzio district in 1656, as listed in Haskell’s bibliography. Chapter 1, note 1 cites pages 122–126 in discussing painters’ poverty. The bibliography OCR reads the periodical as “II Buonarotti”; the reading remains unresolved here. The article and cited pages were not independently consulted in this batch.
