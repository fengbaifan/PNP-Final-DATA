## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔第15章 p.366 注1称 John Strange 于1777年10月8日致信 G. M. Sasso，请其让 Mingardi 为格洛斯特公爵的雕像绘图。第16章 p.373 注2将 Strange 致 Sasso 的信件总体定位于 Epistolario Moschini、威尼斯 Biblioteca Correr，但未给这封信的单件索书号。个别信件及该馆藏记录未独立查阅。

**English:** Haskell’s Chapter 15, p. 366, note 1 says that John Strange wrote to G. M. Sasso on 8 October 1777, asking him to get Mingardi to draw the Duke of Gloucester’s statue. Chapter 16, p. 373, note 2 places the Strange-to-Sasso letters generally in the Epistolario Moschini at Biblioteca Correr, Venice, but gives no item-level shelfmark for this letter. The individual letter and the collection record have not been independently consulted.
