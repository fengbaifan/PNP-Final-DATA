## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记 Haskell 所述 Giambattista Marino 描写主要艺术家作品的一组诗作。书末索引以“poems on works by leading artists”子目列于 Marino 名下，与第2章正文相互对应。Haskell 称这些诗作曾流行，并特别称赞相关作品的异教性质；此处保留为 Haskell 的书内叙述。正文没有列出诗组的单篇题名、各自对象或版本，本卡不补造具体作品。该诗组与 Marino 的单篇长诗 *Adone* 及其致 Marcello Sacchetti 的未具名歌曲分开。[第2章 p.38](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_ii.md)（L77）；[书末索引](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-1-Index-MD/M.md)（L95）

**English:** This card records the group of poems by Giambattista Marino on works by leading artists described by Haskell. The book index lists the subentry “poems on works by leading artists” under Marino, matching the Chapter 2 passage. Haskell says the poems were popular and singled out the pagan qualities of the works they praised; this is retained as Haskell’s account. The passage names no individual poem, subject, or edition, and none is supplied here. This group is kept distinct from Marino’s individually titled *Adone* and his untitled song dedicated to Marcello Sacchetti. [Chapter 2, p. 38](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_ii.md) (L77); [book index](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-1-Index-MD/M.md) (L95)
