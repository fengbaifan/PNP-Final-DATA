## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第10章所述、1741年出版的 *Tombeaux des Princes grands capitaines et autres hommes illustres, qui ont fleuri dans la Grande-Bretagne vers la fin du XVII et le commencement du XVIII siècle*。第291页给出完整题名，称该卷有18幅版画，其中9幅寓意墓葬图被刻印；第289页注2称McSwiny在书中刊出九幅方案图，并转述其均属Richmond公爵，同时明确提醒实际购藏经过存在混乱。因此，版画数和归属均作为Haskell的书内报告保留，未独立核验。索引短题名及页码与正文身份锚点相合。身份入口： [Owen McSwiny](../persons/owen-mcswiny.md)。征订小册子和拟议的50幅版画卷册分别留在其S3对象边界内；本卡只表示1741年实际出版卷册。原刊未独立查阅。[第10章 p.291](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L258–260）[第10章 p.289 注2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L544）[书末索引](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/22_CHP-22Index_intro.md)（L1853）

**English:** This card records the volume Haskell says appeared in 1741 under the title *Tombeaux des Princes grands capitaines et autres hommes illustres, qui ont fleuri dans la Grande-Bretagne vers la fin du XVII et le commencement du XVIII siècle*. On p. 291 he reports eighteen plates, including nine engraved allegorical tombs. Note 2 on p. 289 says McSwiny published nine scheme pictures in the volume and repeats its claim that they all belonged to the Duke of Richmond, while warning that the acquisition history is confused. The plate count and ownership remain Haskell’s reports and were not independently verified. The index short title and page references match the body identity anchors. Identity entry: [Owen McSwiny](../persons/owen-mcswiny.md). The subscription pamphlet and its proposed fifty-plate volume remain separate S3 objects; this card covers only the actual 1741 publication. The printed volume was not independently consulted.
