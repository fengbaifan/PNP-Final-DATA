## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell第13章p.341注4所述的一份收据，日期为1736年8月10日、地点为维也纳；该注称收据由Anne Marie de Savoye签署，并涉及售予A. M. Zanetti的物品。本卡登记书中描述的文书，不表示已查阅收据原件。

**English:** A receipt described in note 4 on p.341 of Haskell’s chapter 13, dated in Vienna on 10 August 1736. The note says it was signed by Anne Marie de Savoye and refers to items sold to A. M. Zanetti. This card registers the document as described in the book; the receipt itself has not been consulted.

### 本书相关内容

**中文：** 注4将该收据置于A. M. Zanetti的Biblioteca Marciana卷册著录语境中；现有描述没有提供叶码或收据专属档号。Haskell所报买方为Zanetti，卖方未具名。两幅绘画只按该注中的描述保留，十五件宝石与浮雕宝石未逐件识别。

**English:** Note 4 describes the receipt in the context of the Biblioteca Marciana volume associated with A. M. Zanetti; it supplies no folio or receipt-specific shelfmark. Haskell names Zanetti as the buyer but does not identify the seller. The two paintings remain at the descriptive level used in the note, and the fifteen gems and cameos are not individually identified.
