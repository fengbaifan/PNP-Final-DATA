## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 代理人赞赏 Rosa 宁愿挨饿也不降低质量；评价不直接证实画家真的挨饿。

**English:** The agent praises Rosa for preferring hunger to diminished quality. That appraisal does not independently establish that the painter actually starved.
