## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 关于 Maratta 接受委托的讽刺性抱怨，不能按字面解释为所有委托永久被拒。

**English:** This is a sarcastic complaint about Maratta accepting a commission, not evidence that he permanently refused every commission.
