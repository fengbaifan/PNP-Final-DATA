**English:** This card records the 1960 journal article cited in chapter 6, page 151, note 4, and listed in chapter 21 of *Patrons and Painters*. The book bibliography gives the form “N. Wibiral”; the official *Bollettino d’Arte* contents identifies the author as Norbert Wibiral, supplies the full Italian title, and gives its starting page as 123.
