## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第4章注7所引的新闻信，书内定位为Vallicelliana—Fondo Orsini 170, c. 529，日期为1623年2月10日。哈斯克尔称作者仅以“Il solito humil. mo Ser.re”签署；该签名未能识别具体人物。题名为描述性名称，未独立查阅刊本或档案原件。

**English:** This card records the newsletter cited in Haskell’s Chapter 4, note 7, with the book locator Vallicelliana—Fondo Orsini 170, c. 529, dated 10 February 1623. Haskell says the writer signs only as “Il solito humil. mo Ser.re”; this signature does not identify a specific person. The title is descriptive, and neither a printed edition nor the archival item was independently consulted.
