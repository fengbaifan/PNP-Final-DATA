## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** S. Antonino 壁画由执行委员给定题材，准画家增添透视、天使、女先知和先知等，并要求上等颜料。正文所谈作品与条款载体分开；材料条款、图像条款可交叉定位，但原合同本件尚未直接检验。

**English:** The commissioners prescribe subjects for the S. Antonino frescoes while allowing additions such as perspective, angels, sibyls and prophets, and require high-quality pigments. The artwork and documentary terms are distinct. The original contract has not been examined.
