## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 1624年6月30日，皮亚琴察圣安东尼诺教堂的委托文书约定卡米洛·加瓦塞蒂装饰司祭席并制作祭坛画，原定在1626年7月4日圣安东尼诺节前完成。条款在给定题材的同时允许画家依艺术与专业判断加入透视、天使合唱、女先知与先知等内容，并要求使用优质颜料；现存作品上的1628年署年表明实际工期延后。（S1–S4）

**English:** The commission document of 30 June 1624 engaged Camillo Gavasetti to decorate the presbytery and execute an altarpiece at Sant'Antonino in Piacenza, with completion planned for the saint's feast on 4 July 1626. While prescribing a subject, the terms allowed him to add perspective, choirs of angels, sibyls, prophets, and other elements required by artistic practice, and specified fine pigments. A date of 1628 uncovered on the work shows that execution ran late. (S1–S4)
