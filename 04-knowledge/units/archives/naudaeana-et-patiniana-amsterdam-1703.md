## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡指 Haskell 第21章书目所列的《Naudaeana et Patiniana》修订第二版，书目注明阿姆斯特丹、1703年。中文显示名为便于阅读的描述性译名，不主张为正式中译题名。第4章两处脚注分别引到该书第29页与第8页；本卡只记录 Haskell 的书内书目和引文信息，未独立查阅1703年版本，也不据此验证关于 Cassiano dal Pozzo 的相邻陈述。

**English:** This card identifies the revised second edition of *Naudaeana et Patiniana* listed in Haskell’s chapter 21 bibliography as Amsterdam, 1703. The Chinese display title is descriptive, not asserted as an official translation. Two chapter 4 footnotes refer to pp. 29 and 8 of the publication. This card records Haskell’s in-book bibliography and citations only; the 1703 edition has not been independently consulted, and these references do not verify adjacent claims about Cassiano dal Pozzo.
