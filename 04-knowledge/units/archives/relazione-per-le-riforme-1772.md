## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡仅记录Haskell第10章注2所列的1772年8月18日《Relazione per le riforme》。注释称该报告由Agostino Sagredo于1857年出版物第208页转引；本项目未查阅报告或Sagredo出版物。中文题名为描述性工作译名。

**English:** This unit records only the report identified in Haskell’s chapter 10 note 2 as Relazione per le riforme, dated 18 August 1772. The note says Agostino Sagredo quoted it in a 1857 publication, page 208. Neither the report nor Sagredo’s publication was independently consulted. The Chinese title is a descriptive working translation.
