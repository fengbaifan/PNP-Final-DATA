## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡标识 Haskell 第4章 p.115 注2 所述 Capponi Lat. 260 原始清单的摄影副本。注释称副本在 Warburg Institute Library；原始清单与该副本是两个不同对象，现行保管状态未独立核验。题名为描述性工作题名。

**English:** This card identifies the photographic copy of the Capponi Lat. 260 original inventory described in Haskell’s Chapter 4, p. 115, note 2. The note places the copy in the Warburg Institute Library. The original inventory and its photographic copy are distinct objects; present custody was not independently verified. The title is descriptive.
