## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第8章注2所引的1708年介绍信；第19章附录4给出该信的题头、日期及Archivio Mediceo, Filza 5897, No. 183定位。附录称信来自温琴佐·拉努齐，并以边注识别其介绍的西班牙画家为朱塞佩·马里亚·克雷斯皮。此卡仅记录哈斯克尔书内所载；档案原件及保管目录未独立查阅。

**English:** This unit records the 1708 letter of introduction cited in Haskell’s chapter 8, note 2. Appendix 4 in chapter 19 supplies its heading, date, and locator, Archivio Mediceo, Filza 5897, No. 183. The appendix names Vincenzo Ranuzzi as sender, and its marginal note identifies the Spanish painter as Giuseppe Maria Crespi. This records Haskell’s account only; neither the archival manuscript nor a repository catalogue was independently consulted.
