## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所述 Bellori 的《Le Vite de’ Pittori . . . Moderni》（1672年出版物）。第6章称 Bellori 正在撰写该书，并称其于1672年出版；第7章给出书名形式并报告题献 Colbert。这里保留原书省略号和来源归属，不补写未由本批来源确认的出版地、出版者、物理副本或完整题名；未独立查阅1672年原版。

**English:** This card records Haskell's account of Bellori's *Le Vite de’ Pittori . . . Moderni* as a 1672 publication. Chapter 6 says Bellori was writing the book and that it was published in 1672; Chapter 7 gives this title form and reports its dedication to Colbert. The printed ellipsis and source attribution are retained. This batch does not supply an unverified place, publisher, physical copy, or expanded title, and the 1672 publication was not independently consulted.
