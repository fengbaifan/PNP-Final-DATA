## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第21章书目列出的 1879 年出版物 *Tiepolo e la sua Famiglia*。第16章注3将 Amadeo Swajer 致 Giambattista Tiepolo 的信和 Gian Domenico Tiepolo 致 Swajer 的信分别定位在该书第19页与第32页；第9章注释另以第100–117、123–126页作引注定位。上述被引页及刊本均未独立查阅。本卡只据本书书目和引注记录该出版物身份及页码，不主张已核读其内容。

**English:** This card records the 1879 publication *Tiepolo e la sua Famiglia* listed in Haskell’s Chapter 21 bibliography. Chapter 16, note 3 locates the letter from Amadeo Swajer to Giambattista Tiepolo on p. 19 and the letter from Gian Domenico Tiepolo to Swajer on p. 32. Chapter 9 notes also cite pp. 100–117 and 123–126. The publication and all cited pages were not independently consulted. This card records the publication identity and page locators found in the book; it does not claim that the cited contents were read.
