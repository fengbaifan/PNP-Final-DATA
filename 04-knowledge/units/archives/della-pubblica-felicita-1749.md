### 描述

**English:** This card records the treatise *Della pubblica felicità, oggetto de’ buoni principi* named in Haskell’s Chapter 10. The passage says Muratori published it in 1749 and comments that the title itself expressed advanced ideas in its thesis. Markdown OCR line 136 reads `Della puhblica felicita, oggetto de’buoni principi`; S2 compared Haskell’s scanned page and recorded the reading as *Della pubblica felicità, oggetto de’ buoni principi*. This card records Haskell’s account and does not imply consultation of Muratori’s work. The Chinese and English display titles are descriptive working translations.
