## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡按Haskell第13章p.345正文及注2登记一封Pietro Monaco致John Strange的书信。正文称信写于1763年；注2日期排作“4 February 1763/4”，故保留原有歧义。Haskell称信中附有莫纳科拥有并希望出售的17幅作品清单。此处仅记录书内报告的文书身份和档案引注，未查阅信件原件或核实馆藏号；题名为描述性暂题。

**English:** This card records the letter from Pietro Monaco to John Strange cited in Haskell’s Chapter 13, p. 345. The narrative calls it a 1763 letter, while note 2 prints the date as “4 February 1763/4”; the ambiguity is retained. Haskell says it enclosed a list of seventeen pictures owned by Monaco and offered for sale. This records the document identity and archival citation as reported in the book; the letter and shelfmark have not been independently checked. The title is descriptive.
