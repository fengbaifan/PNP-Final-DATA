## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应 Haskell 第21章书目所列 A. Ilg 的《Prinz Eugen von Savoyen als Kunstfreunde》（维也纳，1889），并记录第7章 p.201 注2 对 Ilg 的引注。Haskell 书目题名为复数形式 *Kunstfreunde*，TU Wien 馆藏记录则以单数形式 *Kunstfreund* 著录，并给出作者 Albert Ilg、Graeser 出版社、维也纳和1889年。两种书名形式及注释 OCR 中的 “Ug” 均保留为来源差异；跨章条目和馆藏记录支持其为同一书目对象。该书及 Haskell 所引内容未独立查阅。

**English:** This card represents the book listed in Haskell’s chapter 21 bibliography as A. Ilg’s *Prinz Eugen von Savoyen als Kunstfreunde* (Vienna, 1889), and records the citation to Ilg in chapter 7, p. 201 n.2. Haskell’s bibliography gives the plural form *Kunstfreunde*, while the TU Wien catalog records the singular *Kunstfreund* and identifies the author as Albert Ilg, the publisher as Graeser, Vienna, and the publication year as 1889. Both title forms and the note’s OCR reading “Ug” are retained as source variants; the cross-chapter entry and catalog record support treating them as one bibliographic object. Neither the book nor the cited content was independently consulted.
