## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第4章注6与第19章附录2所引Fondo Orsini档案系列。第4章称该系列包含与Paolo Giordano II有关的文书，包括致Barberini的若干信；附录2将相关委托文书定位于罗马Biblioteca Vallicelliana的Fondo Orsini。中文题名为描述性名称，不冒称目录正式题名；第4章抽取文本作“Fonda Orsini”，第19章作“Fondo Orsini”，本批未据页图判定其拼写差异。

**English:** This card records the Fondo Orsini archival fonds cited in Haskell’s Chapter 4, note 6, and Chapter 19, Appendix 2. Chapter 4 describes documents relating to Paolo Giordano II, including some letters to the Barberini; Appendix 2 locates related commission documents in the Fondo Orsini at the Biblioteca Vallicelliana in Rome. The Chinese title is descriptive, not a verified catalogue title. The Chapter 4 extracted text reads “Fonda Orsini” and Chapter 19 reads “Fondo Orsini”; this batch does not resolve the spelling difference from page images.
