## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 第4章 p.97 脚注1称，附录2刊出四封致 Paolo Giordano、涉及 Bernini 的未刊书信。此卡只指该四封书信的集合。现有阅读依据是 Haskell 的书内说明和附录转录，未独立查阅原始档案书信。

**English:** Chapter 4, p. 97, note 1, says that Appendix 2 reproduces four unpublished letters to Paolo Giordano relating to Bernini. This card identifies that four-letter group only. The evidence is Haskell’s printed account and transcription; the original archival letters have not been independently consulted.
