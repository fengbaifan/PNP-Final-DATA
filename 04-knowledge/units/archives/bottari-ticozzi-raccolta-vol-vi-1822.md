### 描述

**中文：** 本知识元登记Bottari–Ticozzi《Raccolta di lettere sulla pittura, scultura ed architettura》1822年米兰版第VI卷。Haskell第21章书目列出该系列题名、Giovanni Bottari与Stefano Ticozzi及Milano 1822；Internet Archive卷册记录标识该item为第6卷，出版者G. Silvestri，语种为意大利语。Haskell第1章和第4章分别以本卷第147页和第121页定位两条引文。相关卷册正文及被引页未在本项目独立阅读。中文题名为描述性工作译名。

**English:** This knowledge unit records volume VI of the 1822 Milan edition of Bottari and Ticozzi’s *Raccolta di lettere sulla pittura, scultura ed architettura*. Haskell’s Chapter 21 bibliography gives the series title, names Giovanni Bottari and Stefano Ticozzi, and lists Milano 1822; the Internet Archive item identifies this as volume 6, published by G. Silvestri, in Italian. Chapters 1 and 4 of Haskell cite pages 147 and 121 of this volume. The volume text and cited pages have not been independently consulted here. The Chinese title is a descriptive working translation.
