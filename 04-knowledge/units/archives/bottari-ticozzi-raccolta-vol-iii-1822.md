**English:** This unit records volume III of the Ticozzi-continued edition of *Raccolta di lettere sulla Pittura, Scultura ed Architettura* listed in Haskell’s bibliography. Chapter 3 explicitly locates a letter from Resta to Giuseppe Ghezzi in Bottari’s volume III, pages 490–491. Chapter 8 contains further volume-page citations in the OCR, with title and Roman-numeral variants. This card records the volume-level bibliographic identity and in-book citation locations; it does not indicate that this edition, a particular copy, the cited letter, or the volume pages were consulted. The English display title is a descriptive project translation, not an original English title.
