## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第16章印刷页373注2所述John Strange致G. M. Sasso的复数书信。原书将其定位于Epistolario Moschini、威尼斯Biblioteca Correr，但没有给出封数、日期或单件索书号。书信及档案目录未独立查阅；本卡不将其与已登记的1777年10月8日单封书信自动等同。 [第16章 p.373](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/16_CHP-16_intro.md#L71)。

**English:** This unit records the plural group of letters from John Strange to G. M. Sasso described in Haskell's Chapter 16, p. 373, note 2. The book places them in the Epistolario Moschini at Biblioteca Correr, Venice, but gives no count, dates, or item-level shelfmarks. The letters and archival catalog have not been independently consulted; this group is not automatically identified with the separately registered letter of 8 October 1777. [Chapter 16, p. 373](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/16_CHP-16_intro.md#L71).
