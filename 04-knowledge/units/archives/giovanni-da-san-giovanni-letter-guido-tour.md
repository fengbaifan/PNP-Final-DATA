## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell第2章p.49提到的一封来自画家Giovanni da San Giovanni的信。Haskell将Cardinal Guido带画家巡视宫殿底层房间、并要求其装饰未完工拱顶的叙述归于该信。此题名为描述性题名；原书在该处未给出书信日期、收信人、原件馆藏或正式题名。本项目未独立找到或阅读该信件原件。

**English:** This unit records a letter from the painter Giovanni da San Giovanni mentioned on p. 49 of chapter 2 of Haskell’s book. Haskell attributes to the letter the account of Cardinal Guido showing the painter the ground-floor apartments and asking him to decorate unfinished vaults. The displayed title is descriptive; the passage supplies no date, recipient, manuscript repository, or formal title. The original letter has not been independently located or read for this project.
