## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 记录 Haskell 所述由 Andrea del Rosso 编制的德尔罗索家族收藏完整清册。原书说其在 Cinelli 1677年记述十二年后编制；保留相对年代，不补成精确年份。

**English:** Records the complete inventory of the del Rosso family collection attributed by Haskell to Andrea del Rosso. The book says it was drawn up twelve years after Cinelli's 1677 account; the relative chronology is preserved without assigning an exact year.

### 本书语境

Haskell 另称其刊于 Gualandi 第二卷；页码依 p.211 页图校读为115–128页。清册未独立查阅。

Haskell says it was published in volume II of Gualandi; the printed pages were visually corrected to 115–128. The inventory was not independently consulted.

### 身份边界

**中文：** 不把十二年间隔静默转换为精确编制日期，不把刊本等同原件。

**English:** The twelve-year interval is not converted to an exact compilation date, and the published text is not equated with the original.
