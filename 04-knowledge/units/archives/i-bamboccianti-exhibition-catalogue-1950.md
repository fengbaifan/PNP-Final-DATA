## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡按 Haskell 第21章书目所载登记 I Bamboccianti 展览图录。书目记载展览由 Alessandro Morandotti 组织、图录由 Giuliano Briganti 编辑，并列有 “Organizzazione Mostre d’Arte ‘Antiquaria’, Roma 1950”。第5章注2建议读者参见 Briganti 所作引言作为 general survey。中文题名为据书目原题拟定的工作译名。本卡仅记录 Haskell 的书目与引注信息；图录及其引言未独立查阅。

**English:** This card registers the I Bamboccianti exhibition catalogue as listed in Haskell’s Chapter 21 bibliography. The entry names Alessandro Morandotti as exhibition organizer and Giuliano Briganti as catalogue editor, and lists “Organizzazione Mostre d’Arte ‘Antiquaria’, Roma 1950.” Chapter 5, note 2, directs readers to Briganti’s introduction as a general survey. The Chinese title is a descriptive working translation of the bibliographic title. This card records Haskell’s bibliography and citation only; the catalogue and its introduction were not independently consulted.
