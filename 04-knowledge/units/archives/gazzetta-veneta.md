## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本档案项记录哈斯克尔所引的期刊标题 Gazzetta Veneta。第9章 p.272 转述该刊在1760年对 Pietà 教堂的描述，但未给出期号或页码；第10章 p.328 注7另明确标出1760年7月26日的一条引用，涉及不同的书内论述。现有材料不足以证明两处引文出自同一期，故本卡只表示期刊层级，不合并为同一篇文章或期号。

**English:** This archive unit records the periodical title Gazzetta Veneta cited by Haskell. Chapter 9, p.272, reports a 1760 description of the Pietà church but supplies no issue or page; Chapter 10, p.328 note 7, separately cites an item dated 26 July 1760 in a different discussion. The available text does not establish that the two citations refer to the same issue, so this card represents the periodical title rather than a single article or issue.

### 本章相关内容

Haskell says the periodical described the Pietà as nearly completed and praised its architectural richness. The scope of the formal subject relation is limited to this 1760 report as quoted in the book; the periodical issue and underlying report have not been independently consulted.
