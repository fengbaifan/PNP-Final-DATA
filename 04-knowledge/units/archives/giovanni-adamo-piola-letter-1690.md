## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 列支敦士登家族摄政亲王约翰·亚当·安德烈亚斯委托保罗·吉罗拉莫·皮奥拉绘制一幅含三至四个真人大小人物的叙事画。信件给定另附尺寸，却把宗教或世俗题材、男性或女性角色留给画家，并要求画家说明完成时间；价格则表示可以另行议定。（S1–S2）

**English:** Johann Adam Andreas, the ruling prince of the House of Liechtenstein, commissioned Paolo Girolamo Piola to paint a narrative picture with three or four life-size figures. The letter supplied dimensions but left sacred or secular subject matter and male or female figures to the painter, asked for a completion date, and left the price open to agreement. (S1–S2)
