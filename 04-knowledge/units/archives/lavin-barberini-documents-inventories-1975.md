## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 后记称 Marilyn Lavin 关于巴贝里尼家族清单的出版物改变了他对家族赞助与收藏的认识；第21章书目给出该1975年出版物的完整题名。这里记录 Haskell 的书内说明，不将出版物与其所刊清单原件混同；被引出版物未在本项目独立阅读。中文题名为工作译名。 [S1–S2]

**English:** Haskell’s postscript says Marilyn Lavin’s publication of Barberini family inventories transformed his understanding of the family’s patronage and collecting; Chapter 21 supplies the full title of the 1975 publication. This records Haskell’s account and keeps the publication distinct from the underlying inventories. The cited publication was not independently consulted; the Chinese title is a working translation. [S1–S2]
