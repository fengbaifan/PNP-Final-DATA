## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称拉扎罗·里维耶拉（Lazzaro Riviera）曾发行解释其委托制作的四幅寓意画版刻的小册子。第10章 p.327 注3将两种语言版本称为其自己的说明性小册子（*La Educazione Virile*），分别为法文和意大利文，并称其于1773年在维罗纳出版。该注还报告 Correr 图书馆藏有两版，索书号为 Op.P.D. 3577-8。小册子、目录及当前馆藏状态未独立查阅；本卡不推断具体版次或两版之间的翻译关系。

**English:** Haskell says Lazzaro Riviera issued a pamphlet to explain the engravings of four allegorical pictures he had commissioned. Chapter 10, note 3 calls two language versions his own explanatory pamphlets (*La Educazione Virile*), one in French and one in Italian, and says they were published in Verona in 1773. The note reports copies of both at the Biblioteca Correr under shelfmark Op.P.D. 3577-8. The pamphlets, catalogue record, and current holdings were not independently consulted; this card does not infer exact editions or a translation relationship between the two versions.
