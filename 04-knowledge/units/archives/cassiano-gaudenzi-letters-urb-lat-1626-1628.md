### 描述

**中文：** 本卡标识 Haskell 第4章 p.109 注2 所定位的一组书信：注释称 Cassiano 致 Gaudenzi，日期在1641至1647年间，定位于 Biblioteca Vaticana 的 Urb. Lat. 1626、1628。各书信与叶码对应关系未说明，手稿未独立查阅。中文题名为描述性工作题名，不是档案原题。

**English:** This card identifies the group of letters located in Haskell’s Chapter 4, p. 109, note 2: the note says they are from Cassiano to Gaudenzi, dated between 1641 and 1647, and located in Biblioteca Vaticana, Urb. Lat. 1626 and 1628. The note does not map individual letters to folios, and the manuscripts were not independently consulted. The title is descriptive, not an archival original title.
