## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第21章书目所列Rapparini《Die Rapparini-Handschrift》1958年版。第10章注1称Rapparini文稿于1958年在Düsseldorf出版，并感谢Alessandro Bettagno提供线索；此卡不表示项目已查阅该版或其中手稿，也不与Rapparini的1709年颂词混为一项。

**English:** This card records the 1958 edition of Rapparini’s *Die Rapparini-Handschrift* listed in Haskell’s chapter 21 bibliography. Chapter 10, note 1 says Rapparini’s manuscripts were published in Düsseldorf in 1958 and thanks Alessandro Bettagno for indicating them. The edition and manuscripts have not been consulted here, and this item is distinct from Rapparini’s 1709 eulogy.
