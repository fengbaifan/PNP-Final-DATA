## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔所述科雷尔向新民主政府执政者申请免除市民卫队义务的通信。书中称该通信涉及其身体不适，并提到随信附有医生和牙医证明；第383页注2将相关材料定位至 Archivio Correr 1468/10 第9a项。具体日期、收件人身份和原件内容均未独立核验。

**English:** This card records Haskell’s report of Correr’s correspondence to the new democratic rulers requesting exemption from the Civic Guard. The account cites ill health and certificates from his doctors and dentist; note 2 on p. 383 locates related material at Archivio Correr 1468/10, item 9a. The date, recipients’ identities, and original document have not been independently checked.
