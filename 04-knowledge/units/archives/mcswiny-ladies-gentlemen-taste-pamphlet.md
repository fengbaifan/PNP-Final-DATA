## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔所述、面向“To the Ladies and Gentlemen of Taste”而写的征订小册子。第10章称Owen McSwiny在1730年代某时发行此册，并邀请读者为一部计划中的大型卷册订阅；同章p.288注1称该小册子无日期，并报告哈斯克尔写作时大英博物馆藏有一份。前者是约略刊行时期，后者是注释对册上日期的说明，两者均按原文范围保留；没有核验当前馆藏。小册子与计划卷册是不同对象；哈斯克尔称1741年实际出版的 *Tombeaux des Princes* 只有18幅版画，而非征订说明中的50幅，本卡不把计划规格当作已出版版本。刊行者身份入口：[Owen McSwiny](../persons/owen-mcswiny.md)。小册子与被引版本均未独立查阅。[10_CHP-10_intro.md](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L247–248、L259–260、L540）

**English:** This card records the subscription pamphlet addressed “To the Ladies and Gentlemen of Taste” in Haskell’s account. Chapter 10 says Owen McSwiny issued it sometime in the 1730s and invited subscriptions for a proposed large volume; note 1 on p. 288 calls the pamphlet undated and reports a copy at the British Museum at Haskell’s source time. The approximate issue period and the note’s statement about the pamphlet’s date are kept within their source scope; present-day holdings were not checked. The pamphlet and proposed volume are distinct objects. Haskell says the actual *Tombeaux des Princes* published in 1741 had eighteen plates, not the fifty in the subscription proposal; this card does not treat the proposal as a completed edition. Publisher identity entry: [Owen McSwiny](../persons/owen-mcswiny.md). Neither the pamphlet nor the cited edition was independently consulted. [10_CHP-10_intro.md](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md) (L247–248, L259–260, L540)
