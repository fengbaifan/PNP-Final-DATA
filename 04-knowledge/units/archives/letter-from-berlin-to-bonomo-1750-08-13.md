## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡按Haskell第14章p.357注4登记一封自柏林致Bonomo Algarotti、日期为1750年8月13日的书信。正文称Algarotti委托Pannini绘制《万神殿室内景》，并请Bonomo将画留在房间内；注4给出该信由Campori于1866年刊布、p.201。作者归属取自正文与脚注的语境联系；原信、Campori刊本及画作均未独立查阅。题名为描述性暂题，不是原信题名。

**English:** This card records the letter from Berlin to Bonomo Algarotti dated 13 August 1750, cited in Haskell’s Chapter 14, p. 357, note 4. The narrative says that Algarotti commissioned Pannini’s *Interior of the Pantheon* and asked Bonomo to keep the painting in his rooms; the note cites Campori’s 1866 publication, p. 201. Authorship follows the contextual link between the narrative and note; neither the letter, Campori’s publication, nor the painting was independently consulted. The title is descriptive.
