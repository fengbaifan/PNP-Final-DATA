## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Comte Gabriel-Jules de Cosnac《Les richesses du Palais Mazarin》的1885年第二版。Haskell书目列题名、责任者和Paris 1885；Persée书目确认Renouard出版的第二版，Smithsonian目录确认1885年书籍记录及实体描述。中文题名为描述性工作译名，不是官方译名。目录副题称该书涉及1661年Mazarin死后清单；具体引文页和书中清单条目未独立查阅，人物责任关系及刊本与清单条目的正式关系留待S6。[第21章书目 p.420](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/21_CHP-21Bibliography.md)（L380） [Smithsonian Libraries and Archives](<https://www.si.edu/object/siris_sil_278901>) [Persée authority record 727409](https://www.persee.fr/authority/727409)

**English:** This card records the 1885 second edition of *Les richesses du Palais Mazarin* by the Comte Gabriel-Jules de Cosnac. Haskell’s bibliography gives the title, responsibility form, and Paris 1885; Persée identifies the second edition as published by Renouard, and the Smithsonian catalog confirms an 1885 book record and physical description. The Chinese title is a descriptive working translation, not an official title. The catalog subtitle says the book concerns an inventory made after Mazarin’s death in 1661. The cited pages and the inventory entry itself have not been independently consulted; the formal author relation and the relation between this publication and the cited inventory remain for S6 review.
