## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡依哈斯克尔第2章p.50的称名记录《所罗门智训》。他称萨基《神圣智慧寓意》壁画主题源自该文本，并转述其劝勉王侯尊崇智慧以永享王权。哈斯克尔没有提供章节或经节；本卡只记录书中对文本及其与壁画主题关系的叙述，未独立核对原典或具体版本。

**English:** This card records the text named by Haskell on p. 50 of Chapter 2 as the Wisdom of Solomon. He says the subject of Sacchi’s *Divine Wisdom* fresco derives from this text and reports its exhortation that princes honour Wisdom so that they may reign for ever. Haskell supplies no chapter or verse; this card records his account of the text and its relation to the fresco subject, without independent identification of the source passage or a particular edition.
