## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第9章第265页所引、并节录一段意大利文的克雷斯皮致孔蒂书信。书内写明日期为1729年7月4日，定位为 Biblioteca Governativa, Lucca, MSS. 3299；信件原件和保管目录未独立核查。本卡与同一馆藏号下另外两封较早书信分别建档。

**English:** This card records Crespi’s letter to Conti cited and excerpted in Italian in Haskell’s chapter 9, page 265. The book gives the date 4 July 1729 and the locator Biblioteca Governativa, Lucca, MSS. 3299. Neither the letter nor a repository catalogue was independently checked. It remains a distinct item from the two earlier letters cited at the same shelfmark.
