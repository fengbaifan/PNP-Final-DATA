## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔第3章称法国耶稣会士 Louis Richeôme 于1611年写作一部献给 Aquaviva 的书，并说明其讨论 S. Andrea 与 S. Vitale 的绘画；第21章书目列题名 *La peinture spirituelle*、出版地 Lyon、出版年1611。“《灵性绘画》”是描述性中文暂译，不宣称为正式译名。本卡仅记录哈斯克尔书内书目与叙述信息；未查阅该书、原件或外部目录。

**English:** Chapter 3 says that the French Jesuit Louis Richeôme wrote a book in 1611, dedicated it to Aquaviva, and discussed paintings in S. Andrea and S. Vitale. Chapter 21 lists the title *La peinture spirituelle*, Lyon, 1611. “Spiritual Painting” is a descriptive English rendering, not an asserted authorized title. This card records Haskell's bibliographic and narrative information only; the book, original, and external catalogues were not consulted.
