### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔所称 Goldoni 戏剧《Il Filosofo Inglese》。第300页注2称 Goldoni 将该剧题献给 Joseph Smith，并引《Opere》第五卷第259页；被引版本未在本批独立查阅。

**English:** This unit records the Goldoni play Il Filosofo Inglese named by Haskell. Chapter 10 note 2 says Goldoni dedicated it to Joseph Smith and cites Opere, volume V, page 259; the cited edition was not independently consulted.
