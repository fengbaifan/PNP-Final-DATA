### 描述

**中文：** 本条目记录哈斯克尔第10章p.278引述的一封谈音乐的书信。哈斯克尔称其为Marlborough公爵夫人致Lord Manchester的信，并以注6将读者引至《Duke of Manchester, Court and Society from Elizabeth to Anne》卷II第140页。原信、刊本及所引第140页未在本批中独立查阅；书信日期和原件保管信息均未见于该段。中文题名为项目描述性题名，不是原件题名。

**English:** This unit records a letter about music quoted in Haskell, chapter 10, p. 278. Haskell describes it as a letter from the Duchess of Marlborough to Lord Manchester and note 6 directs readers to volume II, p. 140 of *Duke of Manchester, Court and Society from Elizabeth to Anne*. The letter, a printed edition, and the cited page were not independently consulted in this batch. The passage gives no letter date or repository. The title is a project description, not a title found on the original document.
