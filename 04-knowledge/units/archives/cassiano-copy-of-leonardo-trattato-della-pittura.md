## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所称卡西亚诺亲手抄写的《绘画论》版本。脚注将其与安布罗修图书馆 H 228 inf 相联系，并称这一识别“普遍被接受”；本卡保留该来源归属，不表示已核对手稿或馆藏目录。

**English:** This card records the version of the Trattato that Haskell says Cassiano copied in his own hand. Haskell’s footnote associates it with Ambrosiana MS H 228 inf and calls that identification generally accepted; this card preserves the report and does not indicate that the manuscript or catalogue was checked.
