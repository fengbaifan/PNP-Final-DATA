## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔第15章 p.366 注1将 British Museum: Egerton MSS. 1969, no. 80 识别为一封来自帕多瓦的 Memmo 致 John Strange 的信，日期为 18 October [1775]，并称信中提及格洛斯特公爵的健康。方括号中的年份照录哈斯克尔的标记。此卡仅记录书内的档案定位和信件描述；未查阅手稿，也不独立确认其现藏地点。

**English:** In Chapter 15, p. 366, note 1, Haskell identifies British Museum: Egerton MSS. 1969, no. 80 as a letter from Memmo in Padua to John Strange, dated 18 October [1775], concerning the Duke of Gloucester’s ill-health. The year is retained in Haskell’s brackets. This card records the locator and description reported in the book; the manuscript has not been consulted, and its present repository is not independently confirmed.
