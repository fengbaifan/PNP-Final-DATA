### 名称、责任与出版

**中文：** 本卡登记 Haskell 第21章书目所列出版物第VI卷。书目将责任者著录为“G. Hanotaux”，本批不据缩写推定全名或建立人物知识元。卷题标示 Rome 1648–1687，出版项为 Paris 1888。Haskell 第2章注4引用第8页及第12–13页，作为其转述1654年法国部长评论的书目路径；被引页与底层部长文件均未独立查阅。中文题名为项目工作译名。

**English:** This card records volume VI of the publication listed in Haskell’s chapter 21 bibliography. The responsibility form “G. Hanotaux” is retained without expanding the initials or creating a person unit. The volume title gives the coverage “Rome 1648–1687”; the publication statement is Paris, 1888. Haskell’s chapter 2, note 4 cites pages 8 and 12–13 as the bibliographic path for its report of the French ministers’ 1654 comments. Neither those pages nor the underlying ministerial document were independently consulted. The Chinese title is a project translation.
