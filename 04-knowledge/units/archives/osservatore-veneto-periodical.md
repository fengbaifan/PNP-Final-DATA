## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell所称由Gasparo Gozzi编辑的《Osservatore Veneto》期刊题名。第10章p.323将该刊与The Spectator相类比；p.324注2引用1761年2月14日一期，作为Gozzi评论Pietro Longhi的出处。卡片表示题名层级的期刊对象，不代表已查阅该期原件，也不据引文另建期号对象。中文说明保留刊名原文。

**English:** This card records the periodical title *Osservatore Veneto* as named by Haskell and described as edited by Gasparo Gozzi. Chapter 10 p. 323 compares it with English journals such as *The Spectator*; note 2 on p. 324 cites an issue dated 14 February 1761 for Gozzi’s remarks on Pietro Longhi. The card represents the periodical at title level. It does not claim that the cited issue was consulted and does not create a separate issue object from the citation alone.
