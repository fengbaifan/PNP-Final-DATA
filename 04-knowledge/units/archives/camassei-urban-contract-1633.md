## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本章以1633年卡马塞伊与乌尔班八世的约定说明画家承担材料费用。外部刊本已发现载同类条款的教皇付款令，但尚未核定本章两处所谓“合同”和“收据”是否是同一原档的转述，暂不宣称存在独立签署合同。[S1–S3]

**English:** Chapter 1 refers to a 1633 arrangement between Camassei and Urban VIII to illustrate the artist's responsibility for materials. A published papal payment order contains corresponding terms, but the chapter's references to a contract and a receipt have not yet been established as either one original record or separate documents. A separately signed contract is not claimed. [S1–S3]
