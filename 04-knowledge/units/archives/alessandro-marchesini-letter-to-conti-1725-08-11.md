## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔引用1725年8月11日亚历山德罗·马尔凯西尼致斯特凡诺·孔蒂的信，并称其刊于哈斯克尔1956年论文第298页；第270页另一条注释将这封信定位于卢卡 Biblioteca Governativa。信件及刊载页未独立查阅。

**English:** Haskell cites a letter from Alessandro Marchesini to Stefano Conti dated 11 August 1725 and says it was published in his 1956 article, page 298. A separate note on page 270 locates it in the Biblioteca Governativa, Lucca. Neither the letter nor page 298 was independently consulted.
