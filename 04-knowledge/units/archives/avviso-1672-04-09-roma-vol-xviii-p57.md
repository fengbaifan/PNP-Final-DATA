## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡只登记 Haskell 第6章注2所称的“1672年4月9日avviso”，以及注中给出的 *Roma* 第十八卷（1940）第57页定位。avviso原件和期刊卷册均未独立查阅；来源所指的“耶稣会士对宫殿建造的愤怒”仅保留为Haskell注释的语境，不作为本卡已核实内容。

**English:** This unit records only the “avviso of 9 April 1672” cited in Haskell’s chapter 6 note 2 and its locator in *Roma*, volume XVIII (1940), p. 57. Neither the notice nor the periodical volume was independently consulted. The preceding statement about Jesuit indignation is retained as the context of Haskell’s note, not as independently verified content of the notice.
