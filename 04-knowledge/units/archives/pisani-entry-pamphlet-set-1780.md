## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称乔治·皮萨尼1780年当选圣马可检察官时曾有宣传册为其祝贺，其中包含引发保守派反感的寓意版画；第十章注1又称 Biblioteca Marciana 所藏的一组宣传册有十八世纪手写批语。此卡只登记书中作为整体提及的宣传册集，不将选举事件并入文献，也不把集合拆成已核实的单件清单。

**English:** Haskell says pamphlets were printed in Giorgio Pisani’s honour after his 1780 election as Procuratore di San Marco, and that they included allegorical prints which aroused hostility among the bienpensants. Chapter 10 note 1 also describes a set at the Biblioteca Marciana bearing eighteenth-century annotations. This card records the collective pamphlet-set reference, keeps the election event distinct, and does not turn the set into a verified item-level inventory.

### 本书相关内容

**中文：** 第十章 p.326 将这些宣传册置于皮萨尼当选后的政治反应中；注1把一组宣传册定位于 Biblioteca Marciana, Misc. 212，并转述其中的手写批语。

**English:** Chapter 10 (p. 326) places the pamphlets in the political response to Pisani’s election; note 1 locates a set at Biblioteca Marciana, Misc. 212 and reports its handwritten comments.
