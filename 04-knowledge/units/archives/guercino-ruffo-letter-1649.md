## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 就每位主要人物 125 ducats 与对方提出 80 ducats 交涉的信件，编辑 V. Ruffo 与收信人 Antonio 分开。

**English:** The letter negotiates Guercino's rate of 125 ducats per principal figure against the recipient's offer of 80. The editor V. Ruffo is distinguished from the recipient Antonio Ruffo.
