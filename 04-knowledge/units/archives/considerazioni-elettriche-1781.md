## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记意大利语题名 *Considerazioni elettriche*。中文题名“电学考论”为项目暂译，不声称是原刊所题。Treccani DBI将其列为Francesco Fedeli（别名Maggiotto）名下、威尼斯1781年出版的印刷小册子。Haskell第10章p.325称Francesco Maggiotto写有一篇关于电学机器的论著；该页注1引用同名题名，并报告一份[威尼斯Correr图书馆](../institutions/correr-library.md)本，定位为Misc. 105113，且称所据本未标日期或出版者。Treccani的书目记载支持题名、作者和出版地／年份，不核实Haskell所引具体副本或该馆当前保管状态；原刊及机器陈述本轮未独立查阅。作者身份入口：[Francesco Fedeli, detto il Maggiotto](../persons/francesco-maggiotto.md)。[第10章 p.325](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_sec_ii.md)（L205–206、L329）[书末索引](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/22_CHP-22Index_intro.md)（L1869）

**English:** This card records the Italian title *Considerazioni elettriche*. The Chinese title “电学考论” is a project translation, not a claimed title of the original publication. Treccani’s DBI lists it among the printed booklets published under Francesco Fedeli, known as Maggiotto, in Venice in 1781. Chapter 10, p. 325, says that Francesco Maggiotto wrote a treatise on an electrical machine; note 1 cites the same title and reports a copy at the [Correr Library in Venice](../institutions/correr-library.md), shelfmark Misc. 105113, while stating that the cited copy has no date or publisher indicated. Treccani supports the bibliographic title, author, place, and year; it does not verify Haskell’s specific Correr copy or its present custody. The original publication and the machine-related account were not independently consulted. Identity entry: [Francesco Fedeli, detto il Maggiotto](../persons/francesco-maggiotto.md).
