### 描述

**中文：** 本卡以日期和哈斯克尔的刊录定位标识一封1646年1月21日书信。第2章注2将其与 Pollak 1913 年版第50页相连；第21章书目将该年份的 Pollak 记录为《Italienische Künstlerbriefe aus der Barockzeit》补编。注释未注明发信人、收信人或原件档号。书信、刊本及被引页均未独立查阅；卡名是描述性工作题名，不是原件题名。

**English:** This card identifies a letter dated 21 January 1646 by Haskell’s publication locator. Chapter 2, note 2 links it to Pollak’s 1913 edition, p. 50; Chapter 21 identifies Pollak’s 1913 item as the *Italienische Künstlerbriefe aus der Barockzeit* supplement. The note does not name the sender or recipient or give an archival reference. Neither the letter, the edition, nor the cited page was independently consulted. The card title is descriptive, not an original title.
