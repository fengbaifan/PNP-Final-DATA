## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔在书目中列出的意大利语书籍 *Storia segreta dello stile dei Gesuiti*。第3章开篇脚注将其作为讨论耶稣会与艺术争议的参考，并称它没有涉及下一注所引著作及19世纪以前的类似论述；后一判断是哈斯克尔的书目评价，本项目未独立核查。中文与英文展示题名为工作译名。[第3章开篇脚注](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_intro.md#L8)；[第21章书目 p.423](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/21_CHP-21Bibliography.md#L506)

**English:** This card records the Italian-titled book *Storia segreta dello stile dei Gesuiti* listed in Haskell’s bibliography. The opening note to chapter 3 cites it for the controversy about the Jesuits and the arts and says that it does not refer to the work in the following note or to similar passages before the nineteenth century. That assessment is Haskell’s bibliographic report and has not been independently checked here. The Chinese and English display titles are working translations. [第3章开篇脚注](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_intro.md#L8); [第21章书目 p.423](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/21_CHP-21Bibliography.md#L506)
