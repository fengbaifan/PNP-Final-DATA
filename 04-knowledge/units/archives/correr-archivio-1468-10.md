## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第17章三条脚注共同给出的书内档案定位：Biblioteca Correr—Archivio Correr，1468/10。第381页注4列6 a-d，注5回指同一定位并列第5项；第383页注2列第9a项。本卡仅把书内相同编号作为来源限定的定位组，不判断档案目录中的正式层级、机构关系或当前物理保管情况；原件与目录均未查。

**English:** This card records the same book-reported locator in three Chapter 17 notes: Biblioteca Correr—Archivio Correr, 1468/10. Note 4 on p. 381 lists items 6 a-d; note 5 refers back to the same locator and lists item 5; note 2 on p. 383 lists item 9a. The repeated identifier is grouped as a source-limited locator only. The catalogue hierarchy, institutional relationship, current custody, manuscripts, and catalogue were not checked.
