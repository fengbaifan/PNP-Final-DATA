## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell第21章书目所列的Palomino多卷出版物。该书目著录将作品列为马德里出版，三卷分两册，出版期为1715–1724。Haskell第2章与第8章分别引用第三卷第336页和第311页；被引原书及页码均未独立查阅。

**English:** This card records the multi-volume Palomino publication listed in Haskell’s Chapter 21 bibliography. The entry gives Madrid, three volumes in two books, and the publication span 1715–1724. Haskell cites volume III, pages 336 and 311 in Chapters 2 and 8; neither the cited work nor those pages has been independently consulted.
