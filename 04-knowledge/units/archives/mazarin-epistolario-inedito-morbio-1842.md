## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第21章书目所列的《Epistolario inedito》：书目列 Mazarin, G. 为责任者，并称由 Carlo Morbio 编刊，Milano 1842。第7章 p.183 注3 引第119页，称该处为1650年6月3日致 Maccarani 的信。该书与第119页未独立查阅。

**English:** This card records *Epistolario inedito* as listed in Haskell’s Chapter 21 bibliography: the entry names Mazarin, G. as responsible author and says it was published by Carlo Morbio, Milano 1842. Chapter 7, p.183 n.3 cites p.119 for a letter to Maccarani dated 3 June 1650. Neither the edition nor p.119 was independently consulted.
