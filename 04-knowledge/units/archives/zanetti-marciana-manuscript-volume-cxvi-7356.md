### 描述

**中文：** 第十三章注4将 Biblioteca Marciana, MSS. Italiani, Cl. XI, Cod. CXVI, 7356 描述为属于 A. M. Zanetti 的卷册，其中有他的部分笔记及写给他的多封书信，卷首由 Zanetti 编有索引和摘要。此卡以书目定位和书中描述识别该卷册；手稿和目录未独立查阅。

**English:** Chapter 13 note 4 describes Biblioteca Marciana, MSS. Italiani, Cl. XI, Cod. CXVI, 7356 as a volume belonging to A. M. Zanetti, containing some of his notes and a number of letters addressed to him, with an index and summaries prepared by Zanetti at the beginning. This card identifies the volume by the book’s locator and description; neither the manuscript nor its catalogue was independently consulted.
