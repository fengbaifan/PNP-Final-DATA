## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第13章所述 Boschini《Ricche Miniere》1733年修订本。正文称 Antonio Maria the Younger 作了该文本的修订，并将修订与致其年长堂兄的友好献辞相联系；书末索引也把此修订项列于 Zanetti, A. M., the Younger 名下。显示题名为描述性工作题名；本卡只记录书内所述文献版本，不将修订者或献辞收受者扩展为已独立核实的个人身份。[S1][S2]

**English:** This unit records the 1733 revision of Boschini’s *Ricche Miniere* described in Haskell’s Chapter 13. The text attributes the revision to Antonio Maria the Younger and associates it with a friendly dedication to his elder cousin; the book index also lists the revision under Zanetti, A. M., the Younger. The display title is descriptive. This card records the bibliographic version as presented in the book without treating the reviser or dedication recipient as independently verified personal identities. [S1][S2]
