## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 在第10章注5中提到 Joseph Smith 于1730年7月17日致 Samuel Hill 的信，并称该信由 W. H. Chaloner 刊出。信件和论文页未独立查阅；不与同章另引的1729年11月26日信件合并。

**English:** Haskell’s Chapter 10 note 5 identifies a letter from Joseph Smith to Samuel Hill dated 17 July 1730 and says it was published by W. H. Chaloner. The letter and article page were not independently consulted; this is kept distinct from another Smith–Hill letter dated 26 November 1729.
