## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本知识元记录哈斯克尔第4章注5列出的Paolo Giordano II诗作出版物。注5写作“Rime, e Satire di Paolo Giordano II, Duca di Bracciano”，并记为Bracciano于1649年印行；第21章书目则写作“Rime e Satira”。两种题名形式并列保留，尚未据原刊本校定。哈斯克尔称相关诗作具有Marino风格。

**English:** This unit records the poetry publication cited in Haskell’s Chapter 4, note 5. The note gives “Rime, e Satire di Paolo Giordano II, Duca di Bracciano” and identifies Bracciano, 1649; Chapter 21’s bibliography gives “Rime e Satira.” Both title forms are retained without adjudicating them against an original copy. Haskell describes the poems as being in Marino’s style.
