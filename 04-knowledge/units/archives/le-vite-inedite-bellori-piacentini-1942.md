## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记 Giovanni Pietro Bellori 的 *Le Vite inedite (Guido Reni, Andrea Sacchi, Carlo Maratta)*，即 M. Piacentini 编、1942年于罗马出版的版本。Haskell 第1、2、6、7章注释多次引用该版不同页码；这只证明该版本是本书引用的文献对象，不表示已独立查阅 Bellori 原书或验证这些引文。中文名《未刊传记》为工作译名。

**English:** This card records Giovanni Pietro Bellori’s *Le Vite inedite (Guido Reni, Andrea Sacchi, Carlo Maratta)*, the edition edited by M. Piacentini and published in Rome in 1942. Notes in Chapters 1, 2, 6, and 7 cite different pages of this edition. Those citations identify the bibliographic object referenced by Haskell; they do not indicate independent consultation of Bellori’s book or verification of the cited passages. The Chinese title 《未刊传记》 is a descriptive working translation.
