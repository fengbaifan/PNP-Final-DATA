## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell第21章书目所指的《Relatione della corte di Roma》题名，并保留其作为《Nota delli Musei》附录所属主标题的书内语境。Treccani《意大利人传记辞典》将该作品归于Girolamo Lunadoro；LILLIT列有1664年威尼斯刊本，Google Books另列Brigonci的1664年记录。目录记录没有证明Haskell所指的附录一定装订在这些具体副本中，因此本卡不声称已确认某一物理副本。中文与英文标题为描述性译名，原题见下表。

**English:** This card records the *Relatione della corte di Roma* title identified in Haskell’s chapter 21 bibliography and retains its source context as the host title for the *Nota delli Musei* appendix. Treccani’s Italian biographical dictionary identifies the work as by Girolamo Lunadoro; LILLIT lists a 1664 Venetian edition, and Google Books separately lists a Brigonci 1664 record. These catalog records do not prove that Haskell’s appendix is bound in either specific copy, so this card does not claim a verified physical copy. The Chinese and English titles are descriptive translations; the original title appears below.
