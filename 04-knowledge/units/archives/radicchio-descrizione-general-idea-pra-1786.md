## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 第21章书目列出 Don Vincenzo Radicchio 的 1786 年出版物，题名从 “quando fu” 后以省略号保留。第15章 p.365 注2以 Radicchio, 1786 引作 Prà della Valle 历史来源；p.366 称 Radicchio 写有随 Piranesi 版画附带的说明。本项目依据作者、年份及同一 Prà 主题将该说明与书目出版物对齐；未独立查阅该出版物，也未确认说明与版画实际装订或发行关系。

**English:** Haskell’s Chapter 21 bibliography lists a 1786 publication by Don Vincenzo Radicchio, retaining an ellipsis after “quando fu” in the title. Chapter 15, p. 365 note 2 cites Radicchio, 1786, for the history of Prà della Valle; p. 366 says Radicchio wrote an account to accompany Piranesi’s engraving. This project aligns the account with the bibliographic publication on the basis of author, year, and the same Prà subject. The publication was not independently consulted, and its physical or publishing relationship to the engraving has not been established.
