**English:** Chapter 4 note 3 in Haskell refers to Pietro Testa’s prison letters from September 1637 and says Bottari published them. This unit represents the cited group of letters; it does not establish their number, the publication title, or the original holding, and neither the letters nor Bottari’s edition was consulted.
