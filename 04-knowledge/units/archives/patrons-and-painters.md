## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 《赞助人与画家》是Francis Haskell研究巴洛克时期意大利艺术与社会关系的著作。本项目采用1980年修订扩充版的2006年印刷本，当前语义处理范围为第一章及必要的书目前置页、书目和索引。

**English:** Patrons and Painters is Francis Haskell’s study of the relations between Italian art and society in the Baroque period. The project uses the 2006 printing of the revised and enlarged edition of 1980; current semantic work covers chapter 1 and the necessary preliminary, bibliography and index pages.
