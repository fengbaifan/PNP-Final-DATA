**English:** This card records the *Guida Spirituale* text cited in Haskell’s Chapter 3, p. 82; the Chinese and English display titles are working translations. The passage names Miguel de Molinos’s *Guida Spirituale*, and the book index also lists the title under Molinos, Miguel de. S3 aligns the candidates at the text level only; no particular edition, copy, or original was identified. Haskell presents the passage as saying that before entering into divinity the soul must wash itself in the Redeemer’s blood and adorn itself with the richness of Christ’s Passion.
