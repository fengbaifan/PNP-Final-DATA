## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell第8章注1给出一封1708年2月26日来信的档案定位：Archivio Mediceo, Filza 5904, No. 22，并称全文收入附录4。附录4将其署为Crespi之信，置于涉及G. M. Crespi受托斯卡纳大公费迪南德雇用的文件组中。本卡据书内标引登记该信；原件及全文均未独立查阅。

**English:** Haskell’s Chapter 8 note 1 gives the archival locator for a letter dated 26 February 1708—Archivio Mediceo, Filza 5904, No. 22—and says it is published in Appendix 4. The appendix labels it a letter from Crespi and places it in a group concerning G. M. Crespi’s employment by Grand Prince Ferdinand of Tuscany. This unit records Haskell’s locator; neither the manuscript nor the full letter was independently consulted.
