**English:** Chapter 17, p.379 note 3 cites a pamphlet printed in Venice in 1797, with the locator Biblioteca Marciana 183.C.89, p. 321, as an example of controversial material. Haskell uses such material to comment on Manfrin’s reputation while leaving the extent of dishonesty uncertain.
