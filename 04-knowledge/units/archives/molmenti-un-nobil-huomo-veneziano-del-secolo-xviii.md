## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第15章注释所引 P. Molmenti 的文章 *Un nobil huomo veneziano del secolo XVIII*。第21章书目将其列于 *Epistolari veneziani del secolo XVIII*，地点为 Milano，出版年记作 n.d.；没有据此补定年份。哈斯克尔分别以该文作为 Andrea Memmo 的简要传记来源，并引用其第143–144页和第137页及后续页。文章及被引页均未独立查阅。

**English:** This card records P. Molmenti’s article *Un nobil huomo veneziano del secolo XVIII*, cited in Haskell’s Chapter 15 notes. Chapter 21 lists it in *Epistolari veneziani del secolo XVIII*, Milano, n.d.; no publication year is inferred. Haskell cites it for a brief biography of Andrea Memmo and gives locators to pp. 143–144 and p. 137 ff. The article and cited pages were not independently consulted.
