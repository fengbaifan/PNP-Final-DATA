### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔在第4章脚注中报告一封皮雷斯克致圣索尔沃尔先生的信，日期为1635年1月9日，并将其定位到 Peiresc 卷III第252页。此卡记录哈斯克尔的书内报告；被引版本与原手稿均未查阅。哈斯克尔指出信中“libertines”一词的所指及归属并不明确。

**English:** Haskell's Chapter 4 note reports a letter from Peiresc to Monsieur de Saint-Saulveur dated 9 January 1635 and cites Peiresc, volume III, page 252. This card records Haskell's report; neither the cited edition nor the manuscript has been consulted. Haskell notes that the reference to “libertines” is ambiguous in meaning and attribution.
