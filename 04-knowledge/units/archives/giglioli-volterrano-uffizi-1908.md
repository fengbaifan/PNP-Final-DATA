## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell第8章p.231注2所引、并在第21章书目中列明的O. H. Giglioli 1908年文章。Haskell书目记作pp.335–358；官方《Bollettino d’Arte》扫描从印刷页355开始，Treccani书目列pp.355–358，页码差异保留为来源差异。官方文章首页和Treccani词条支持文献身份及其与Piovano Arlotto题材的相关性；文章全文未完整阅读，本卡不据此确认具体画作身份、委托史或现藏状态。

**English:** This card records O. H. Giglioli’s 1908 article cited in Haskell’s Chapter 8, p. 231, note 2 and listed in his Chapter 21 bibliography. Haskell gives pp. 335–358; the official *Bollettino d’Arte* scan begins at printed p. 355, and Treccani cites pp. 355–358. The pagination discrepancy is retained as a source difference. The official first page and Treccani entry support the publication identity and its relevance to the Piovano Arlotto subject; the full article has not been read, and this card does not use it to confirm a particular painting’s identity, commission history, or present location.
