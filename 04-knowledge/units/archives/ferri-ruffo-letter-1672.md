## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 拒收预付以保自由，但后接事务繁多的理由；必须保留整句而非仅摘前半。

**English:** Ferri refuses an advance to retain his freedom, then explains that he is occupied with many commitments. The whole statement must be retained rather than only its first part.
