## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第8章第227页注2所引的书信。该注本身未署寄件人；它所指向的第19章附录3第389页标题明确写明此信来自朱塞佩·托雷利、致亚历山德罗·马尔凯西尼，日期为1705年2月24日。题头中的点号保留在来源转录；MS. 3299 为书内定位，原件未查。

**English:** This card records the letter cited in Haskell’s chapter 8, page 227, note 2. The note does not name its writer; the cross-referenced heading in chapter 19, Appendix 3, page 389 identifies it as a letter from Giuseppe Torelli to Alessandro Marchesini, dated 24 February 1705. The source transcription’s punctuation before Marchesini is preserved; MS. 3299 is a book-reported locator, and the manuscript was not consulted.
