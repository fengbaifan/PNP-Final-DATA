### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell书目所列Bottari书信集Ticozzi续编本第五卷。第一章第13页注2引用第186页；第10章第294页注3引用pp.27–28。卷次由两处Haskell脚注确定，系列出版信息据书目；本卡不表示已查阅该版、特定印本或被引页。中文题名为描述性工作译名。

**English:** This card records volume V of the Ticozzi-continued Bottari letter collection listed in Haskell’s bibliography. Chapter 1, note 2 cites page 186; Chapter 10, note 3 cites pages 27–28. The volume number is established by the two Haskell notes and the series publication details by the bibliography. This card does not indicate that the edition, a particular copy, or the cited pages were consulted. The Chinese display title is descriptive.
