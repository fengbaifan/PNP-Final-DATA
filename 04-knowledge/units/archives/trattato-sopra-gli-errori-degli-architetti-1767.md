## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第10章所述的1767年刊本，题名为 *Trattato sopra gli errori degli architetti*。哈斯克尔称该书依据Smith收藏的一份Teofilo Gallacini手稿刊行；第10章正文与书末索引的同题子目均指向印刷页309。S2依据该页图将正文OCR的连写“sopragli”读作“sopra gli”，索引转录保留连写形式。这里的手稿是刊本来源说明，原文没有给出可独立识别的手稿馆藏号或存世本，本卡只登记1767年刊本。原刊未独立查阅。[第10章 p.309](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L473–474） [书末索引](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/22_CHP-22Index_intro.md)（L1407–1408）

**English:** This card records the 1767 publication described in Haskell’s Chapter 10 under the title *Trattato sopra gli errori degli architetti*. Haskell says Smith published it from a manuscript in his collection by Teofilo Gallacini; the Chapter 10 passage and the book index entry both point to printed p. 309. S2 read the page image as “sopra gli,” while the index transcription retains the joined OCR form “sopragli.” The manuscript is described as the publication’s source; Haskell gives no separately identifiable manuscript holding or surviving copy, so this card covers only the 1767 publication. The printed work was not independently consulted.
