## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记 Haskell 所称马真塔的重要列奥纳多手稿收藏。来源称收藏在他去世前已散佚，后来由 Galeazzo Arconato 大部分重建；本卡不据此推定全部单件文献或完整收藏清单。

**English:** This card records the collection of Leonardo manuscripts that Haskell attributes to Ambrogio Mazenta. Haskell says it had dispersed before Mazenta’s death and was later largely reconstructed by Galeazzo Arconato; this card does not infer an itemized or complete collection.
