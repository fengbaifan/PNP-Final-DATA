## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔书目所列S. Fraschetti著 *Il Bernini*（Milano 1900）。该书在第1、2、5章多次被引用，包括第2章注中所称刊载一则1633年埃斯特使节报告的第107页。书籍作者只以首字母著录；本卡不扩展Fraschetti全名，也不把引文内容提升为已独立查核。[第2章注1 p.37](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_ii.md#L167)；[第1章注1 p.17](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/01_CHP-1.md#L700)；[第5章注3 p.123](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_i.md#L140)；[第21章书目 p.422](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/21_CHP-21Bibliography.md#L493)

**English:** This card records S. Fraschetti’s *Il Bernini* (Milan, 1900), as listed in Haskell’s bibliography. The book is cited several times in chapters 1, 2, and 5, including page 107 in chapter 2, which Haskell identifies as the publication location for a 1633 notice from the Este resident. The author is listed only by an initial; this card does not expand Fraschetti’s name or treat the cited contents as independently checked. [第2章注1 p.37](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_ii.md#L167); [第1章注1 p.17](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/01_CHP-1.md#L700); [第5章注3 p.123](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_i.md#L140); [第21章书目 p.422](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/21_CHP-21Bibliography.md#L493)
