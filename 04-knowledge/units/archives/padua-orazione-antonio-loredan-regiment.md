## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔引用一篇未注明年份、以帕多瓦城名义向安东尼奥·洛雷丹致辞的颂词，主题涉及其军团离任。引文末尾的“In Padova”是刊行地；原句未说明军团从帕多瓦出发。

**English:** Haskell cites an undated oration delivered in the name of Padua to Antonio Loredan concerning the departure of his regiment. “In Padova” at the end is the publication place; the text does not say that the regiment departed from Padua.
