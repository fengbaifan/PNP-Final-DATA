## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡标识 Haskell 第4章 p.115 注2 所述马西米收藏原始清单，印本页图所示档号为 Biblioteca Vaticana, Capponi Lat. 260。注释另称该清单部分内容由 Orbaan 1920 年刊布，并称 Warburg Institute 保存摄影副本；原件、刊布页与现行馆藏均未独立核验。原件与摄影副本分立登记。

**English:** This card identifies the original inventory of Massimi’s collections described in Haskell’s Chapter 4, p. 115, note 2, with the shelfmark shown in the print scan as Biblioteca Vaticana, Capponi Lat. 260. The note also says parts were published by Orbaan in 1920 and that a photographic copy is at the Warburg Institute; neither the original, published pages, nor present custody was independently checked. The original and photographic copy are registered separately.
