## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第21章书目将此文献著录为 Lione Pascoli 的 *Vite de’ Pittori, Scultori ed Architetti moderni* 第二卷（1736）；同条另列第一卷（1730），并称影印版由 R. Istituto d’Archeologia e Storia dell’Arte 于1933年在罗马出版。Haskell 在多章脚注中引用第二卷，例如第1章 p.18 注4和第7章 p.187 注4。本卡只记录 Haskell 所载书目及引文定位，未查阅 Pascoli 原书或影印本；中英文展示题名为描述性工作译名，不是书目原载英文题名。

**English:** Chapter 21’s bibliography lists this work by Lione Pascoli as volume II (1736) of *Vite de’ Pittori, Scultori ed Architetti moderni*; the same entry separately lists volume I (1730) and says that a facsimile edition was published in Rome in 1933 by R. Istituto d’Archeologia e Storia dell’Arte. Haskell cites volume II in notes across several chapters, including chapter 1, p. 18, note 4, and chapter 7, p. 187, note 4. This card records Haskell’s bibliographic entry and citation locators only; neither Pascoli’s original book nor the facsimile was consulted. The Chinese and English display titles are descriptive working translations, not English titles printed in the bibliography.
