## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所引 [Francesco Algarotti](../persons/francesco-algarotti.md) 致 [Pierre-Jean Mariette](../persons/pierre-jean-mariette.md) 书信中的展览比较。书中称，至迟在1751年，Algarotti 曾将[圣罗科展览](../events/s-rocco-annual-exhibition.md)比作“在某些方面”如同绘画的 tribunal 的巴黎 [Salon](../events/salon-in-paris-exhibition-series.md)。此为 Haskell 对书信的转述和引文；书信原件未在本任务中独立查阅，不能据此指定更精确日期或档案馆藏。〔[第10章 p.331](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_sec_ii.md)（L270）〕

**English:** This unit records the comparison in a letter from [Francesco Algarotti](../persons/francesco-algarotti.md) to [Pierre-Jean Mariette](../persons/pierre-jean-mariette.md) cited by Haskell. The book says that by 1751 Algarotti had described the [S. Rocco exhibition](../events/s-rocco-annual-exhibition.md) as, “in some ways,” the tribunal of Venetian painting like the [Paris Salon](../events/salon-in-paris-exhibition-series.md). This is Haskell's report and quotation; the letter itself was not independently consulted here, so no more precise date or repository is assigned. ([Chapter 10, p. 331](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_sec_ii.md) (line 270).)
