## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第13章以 Carlevarijs’ Le Fabriche e Vedute di Venetia (1703) 为例，称其为面向旅行者的图书插图出版物。此档案卡指该书目对象，不把其中每幅图版拆成已核实作品，也不判断 Carlevarijs 在设计、制版、印刷或出版中的具体角色。

**English:** Chapter 13 cites Carlevarijs’ Le Fabriche e Vedute di Venetia (1703) as an illustrated book designed for travellers. This archive unit represents the publication as cited; it does not inventory individual plates or assign Carlevarijs a specific design, engraving, printing, or publishing role.

### 名称

中文显示名及英文显示名见卡片标题；中文名为便于识别所用音译或描述性暂译，原书用词按来源引文保留。

The bilingual display names appear in the card title. The Chinese name is a transliteration or descriptive rendering for readability; the book's wording is preserved in the source quotations.

### 本书语境

第13章 p.332 将该书与 Domenico Lovisa 于1720年出版的另一部威尼斯图像书并列，讨论18世纪前期艺术家参与书籍插图的现象。

Chapter 13 p. 332 places the book alongside Domenico Lovisa’s 1720 Venetian publication in a discussion of artists’ work in book illustration during the early eighteenth century.

### 类型记录

中文：对象类型：archive（具体出版物）；原书称其为 books，并与面向旅行者的图像出版物相连。

English: Object type: archive (a specific publication); Haskell calls it a book and associates it with illustrated publications for travellers.

### 身份边界

中文：标题与年份按原书；Haskell 的所有格写法不区分绘图、雕版、印刷或出版责任，本卡不据此建立创作者关系。

English: The title and year follow the book. Haskell’s possessive wording does not distinguish drawing, engraving, printing, or publishing responsibility, so this card does not assert a creator relation.
