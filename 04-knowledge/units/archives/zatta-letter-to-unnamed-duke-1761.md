## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记 Haskell 第13章第338页注2所列的 Zatta 致未具名公爵书信（1761）。注2保留未完题名，并在刊地处先印 Fiorenza、再以方括号更正为 Venice；这只是 Haskell 的引文形式与更正。第339页注3引用同一题名并交叉指回注2，给出第23页，因而与本候选组中“载有喷泉与海豚小图像的书”候选指向同一被引书信。原信、题名完整形式与刊地均未独立核实；公爵仍未具名。注2另列《Lettera Giustificativa di Antonio Zatta》，该文献不属于本卡身份组。[第338页注2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md)（L210）；[第339页注3](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md)（L214）

**English:** This card records the 1761 letter by Zatta to an unnamed Duke cited in Haskell’s Chapter 13, p. 338, note 2. The incomplete title is retained. The note prints Fiorenza and then corrects it in brackets to Venice; this records Haskell’s citation and correction. Note 3 on p. 339 repeats the title, cross-references note 2, and supplies p. 23, linking the cited letter to the book candidate for the fountain-and-dolphin vignette in this identity group. The letter, its complete title, and its imprint have not been independently verified; the Duke remains unnamed. Note 2 separately lists *Lettera Giustificativa di Antonio Zatta*, which is outside this identity group. [p. 338, note 2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md) (L210); [p. 339, note 3](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md) (L214)
