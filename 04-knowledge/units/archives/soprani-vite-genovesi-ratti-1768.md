## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记 Raffaello Soprani 的 *Vite de’ Pittori, Scultori ed Architetti Genovesi* 1768 年第二版。第21章参考书目说明该版由 Carlo Giuseppe Ratti 修订并增注，共两卷，出版地为 Genova。第1、3、6、7、8章注释多次引用该版不同卷页，确认这些引文指向同一书目对象；原书及各被引页均未在本轮独立查阅。中文标题为据原题所作的描述性工作译名。

**English:** This card records the 1768 second edition of Raffaello Soprani’s *Vite de’ Pittori, Scultori ed Architetti Genovesi*. Chapter 21’s bibliography identifies Carlo Giuseppe Ratti as reviser and annotator, gives two volumes, and names Genova as the place of publication. Notes in Chapters 1, 3, 6, 7, and 8 cite different volume and page locators for this bibliographic object. The edition and cited pages were not independently consulted in this pass. The Chinese title is a descriptive working translation.
