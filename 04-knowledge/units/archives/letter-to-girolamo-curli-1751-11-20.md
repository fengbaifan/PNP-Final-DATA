## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡按Haskell第14章p.357注1登记一封致Girolamo Curli、日期为1751年11月20日的书信。注释称该信由A. Neri于1885年刊布；前接p.356–357的正文称Algarotti写信给意大利熟人索取热那亚建筑版画，因此本卡按该语境记录作者归属。Haskell引用的书信及Neri刊本均未独立查阅；注释没有提供刊本题名和页码，题名为描述性暂题。

**English:** This card records the letter to Girolamo Curli dated 20 November 1751 cited in Haskell’s Chapter 14, p. 357, note 1. The note says it was published by A. Neri in 1885. The preceding text on pp. 356–357 says that Algarotti wrote to acquaintances in Italy for prints of Genoese buildings, so authorship is recorded from that context. Neither the cited letter nor Neri’s publication was independently consulted; the note supplies no publication title or page number. The title is descriptive.
