## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 在1980年修订版后记中称，“本书初版”曾将 Corsini 收藏的一幅肖像重印并描述为 Caravaggio 所作的 Maffeo Barberini 肖像。此卡仅记录该版本指称；初版的出版年份、版本细节和肖像个体身份均未由该段确认。中文名称为描述性工作译名。 [S1–S2]

**English:** In the 1980 revised-edition postscript, Haskell says that “the first edition of this book” reproduced and described a portrait in the Corsini collection as a portrait of Maffeo Barberini by Caravaggio. This card records only that edition reference; the first edition’s publication details and the portrait’s individual identity are not established by the passage. The Chinese title is a descriptive working translation. [S1–S2]
