### 描述

**中文：** 本知识元记录Haskell第3章pp.80–81所引的一段书信节录。Haskell称Gian Paolo Oliva于1679年9月写信给帕尔马公爵；节录中Oliva描述罗马Gesù及未完成的tribune，并请求公爵支持。Haskell随后记述Ranuccio的回应。此卡表示Haskell所引节录，不代表已查阅原信、抄本或完整刊本。

**English:** This unit records a letter excerpt quoted in Haskell’s chapter 3, pp. 80–81. Haskell says Gian Paolo Oliva wrote to the Duke of Parma in September 1679; the excerpt describes the Gesù in Rome and its unfinished tribune and appeals for the Duke’s support. Haskell then reports Ranuccio’s response. This card represents Haskell’s quoted excerpt and does not imply consultation of the original letter, a manuscript, or a complete printed version.
