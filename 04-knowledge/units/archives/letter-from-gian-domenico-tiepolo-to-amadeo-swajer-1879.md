## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡标识哈斯克尔第16章注3所述、由乔万多梅尼科·提埃坡罗寄给阿马德奥·斯瓦耶尔的一封信。正文将 G. D. Tiepolo 对应为 Giambattista Tiepolo 之子 Gian Domenico；注释称该信刊于 Urbani de Ghelthof 1879 年出版物第32页。这里登记的是被书内引注识别的信件文本，不据此断定原件的形态或馆藏。原信与被引刊本页未独立查阅；卡名为描述性工作题名，并非原件题名。

**English:** This card identifies the letter from Giandomenico Tiepolo to Amadeo Swajer cited in Haskell’s Chapter 16, note 3. The surrounding text identifies G. D. Tiepolo as Giambattista Tiepolo’s son, Gian Domenico; the note locates the letter on p. 32 of Urbani de Ghelthof’s 1879 publication. This unit represents the letter text identified by that citation and does not establish the form or custody of an original manuscript. Neither the letter nor the cited publication page was independently consulted. The card title is descriptive, not an original title.
