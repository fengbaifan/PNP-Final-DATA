## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡按Haskell第14章p.357注7登记一封致Prospero Pesci、日期为1759年9月28日的书信。正文将Canaletto大运河景观委托置于Algarotti的叙述中，注7给出该信的 *Opere, VHI, pp. 89–100* 书内定位并报告Parma画廊有一版本；作者归属按正文与注释的语境联系记录。“VHI”按OCR转录保留，未推测改为“VIII”。书信、刊本和画作均未独立查阅；题名为描述性暂题。

**English:** This card records the letter to Prospero Pesci dated 28 September 1759 cited in Haskell’s Chapter 14, p. 357, note 7. The narrative places the Canaletto Grand Canal commission within its account of Algarotti; note 7 gives the in-book locator “Opere, VHI, pp. 89–100” and reports a version in the Parma gallery. Authorship is recorded from the contextual link between the narrative and note. The OCR reading “VHI” is retained rather than silently normalized to “VIII”. The letter, edition, and painting were not independently consulted. The title is descriptive.
