## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记 Carlo Cesare Malvasia 的 *Felsina Pittrice* 1678年作品身份。Haskell 第7章称该作品题献给 Louis XIV。第21章书目另列两卷本 Bologna 1841 年版，该具体出版版本现单独登记为[1841年版档案记录](felsina-pittrice-bologna-1841-edition.md)。第1章 p.16、第7章 pp.176、178、182 和第8章 p.203 的短式引注均按这条书目记录映射至1841年版，不再指向本作品候选。被引卷页及原书均未独立查阅。中文题名为据 Haskell 英文释义所作的描述性工作译名。

**English:** This card records the 1678 work identity of Carlo Cesare Malvasia’s *Felsina Pittrice*. Haskell’s Chapter 7 reports that the work was dedicated to Louis XIV. The Chapter 21 bibliography separately lists a two-volume Bologna 1841 publication, now recorded as a distinct [1841 edition archive](felsina-pittrice-bologna-1841-edition.md). Haskell’s abbreviated references in Chapter 1, pages 16, Chapter 7, pages 176, 178, and 182, and Chapter 8, page 203 are mapped to that bibliography-listed edition rather than this work candidate. Neither the cited pages nor the original work was independently consulted. The Chinese title is a descriptive working translation of Haskell’s English gloss.

### 作品身份

- **原题名：** *Felsina Pittrice*；Haskell glosses it as “lives of the Bolognese artists.”
- **作者：** Carlo Cesare Malvasia；姓名见第21章书目。
- **原作时间与题献：** Haskell p.189 gives 1678 and reports a dedication to Louis XIV.
- **版本边界：** The two-volume Bologna 1841 publication is a separate archive KU. The volume-II locators in Chapters 1 and 7, and the passim reference in Chapter 8, point to that bibliography-listed publication; its pages have not been consulted.
