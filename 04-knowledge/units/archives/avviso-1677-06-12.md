## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应哈斯克尔注中所引的 1677 年 6 月 12 日 Avviso。注文明示该告示刊于 Roma 第十九卷（1941）第308页；告示与卷册原件均未独立查阅。

**English:** This card represents the Avviso dated 12 June 1677 cited in Haskell’s note. The note places it in Roma, volume XIX (1941), p.308; neither the notice nor the volume has been independently consulted.
