## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell第20章提到Bettagno为扎内蒂相册赠予纪念展览所编目录，并评价其总结了扎内蒂的生平与活动。第21章书目给出目录原题和1969年出版项。本卡仅依据Haskell书内记载，目录本身未独立查阅。

**English:** Haskell’s Chapter 20 refers to Bettagno’s catalogue for the exhibition marking the gift of Zanetti’s album and says it summarizes Zanetti’s life and activities. The Chapter 21 bibliography supplies the title and 1969 publication details. This card is based on Haskell’s citations; the catalogue itself was not independently consulted.
