### 本书相关内容

Haskell用此信讨论Algarotti关于绘画的观点，并称信中暗示白底绘画的想法刚刚出现；此处仅记录Haskell的转述，未把它当作对原信的直接查阅。

Haskell cites the letter in discussing Algarotti’s ideas on painting and reports that it implies the white-ground proposal had only just occurred. This records Haskell’s account; it does not represent direct consultation of the letter.
