## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell第9章注5将其著录为Giambattista Tiepolo于1743年10月26日致Francesco Algarotti的信，并指向Fogolari 1942年刊本第34页。第14章正文也记述同年10月Tiepolo从Villa Cordellina致信Algarotti，希望与他讨论绘画；注3给出的日期和刊引与第9章注5相同。两章候选据发送人、收信人、日期及刊引归为同一封书信。原信及Fogolari刊本未在本批独立查阅。[第9章书内来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md) [第14章书内来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/14_CHP-14_intro.md)

**English:** Chapter 9 note 5 identifies this as Giambattista Tiepolo’s letter to Francesco Algarotti dated 26 October 1743 and points to Fogolari’s 1942 publication, page 34. Chapter 14 also reports that Tiepolo wrote Algarotti from Villa Cordellina in October of that year, wishing to discuss painting; note 3 gives the same date and publication locator as Chapter 9. The two chapter candidates are grouped as one letter based on the correspondents, date, and publication reference. Neither the letter nor Fogolari’s publication was independently consulted in this batch.
