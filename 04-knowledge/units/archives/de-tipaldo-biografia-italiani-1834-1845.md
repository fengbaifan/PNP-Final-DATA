**English:** Haskell’s Chapter 21 bibliography lists Emilio de Tipaldo’s ten-volume *Biografia degli Italiani illustri nelle scienze, lettere ed arti del secolo XVIII, e de’ contemporanei*, published in Venice in 1834–1845. Chapter 15 note 2 directs readers to de Tipaldo concerning the relationship between De Non and Querini. This card records the in-book bibliographic identity and citation pointer; the cited work was not independently consulted, and the historical relationship is not verified here.

### 身份边界

本书与de Tipaldo 1833年关于Sala别墅的出版物分属不同书目对象。

This work is distinct from de Tipaldo’s 1833 publication about the villa at Sala.
