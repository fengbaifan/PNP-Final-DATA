## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡标识 Robert Enggass 的《Early Eighteenth-Century Sculpture in Rome—an illustrated catalogue raisonné》。Haskell 第20章称该书增进了对 Gesù 堂内 Théodon 与 Legros 雕像及耶稣会赞助的认识；注2为“Enggass, 1976”，书目列其两卷本、费城和1976年。中文题名为项目工作译名；原书未独立查阅，未据此识别具体雕像。

**English:** This unit identifies Robert Enggass's *Early Eighteenth-Century Sculpture in Rome—an illustrated catalogue raisonné*. Haskell says in chapter 20 that the book added to knowledge of statues by Théodon and Legros in the Gesù and of Jesuit patronage; note 2 reads “Enggass, 1976,” and the bibliography records two volumes, Philadelphia, and 1976. The Chinese title is a working translation. The book has not been independently consulted, and no individual statue is identified from this citation alone.
