## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 引用一部1776年5月16日 Christie’s 拍卖目录，并称其中另列十四幅画。目录及各拍品未独立查阅；十四幅画仍未形成可识别的作品组。

**English:** Haskell cites a Christie’s sale catalogue dated 16 May 1776 and says it lists fourteen further pictures. The catalogue and individual lots were not independently consulted; the fourteen pictures remain unidentified as a stable work group.
