## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell第3章p.85在叙述Negroni礼拜堂争执时所引的一封致Gian Paolo Oliva的信，并按书内责任者形式对应Cardinal Giovan Francesco Negroni。Haskell先叙述Negroni命人在祭坛正面雕刻Clement IX和Innocent XI的徽章，随后引用他致Oliva的信，称已命石工开始制作纪念两位教皇的记录。前者是Haskell的叙述，后者是Haskell所引信文；本卡不表示项目已查阅原信或确认手稿身份。本处未载信件日期与保管信息。中英文名称为描述性题名，不是原信题名。

**English:** This card records a letter to Gian Paolo Oliva quoted in Haskell’s chapter 3, p. 85, in his account of Negroni’s chapel dispute, and identifies its writer from Haskell’s responsibility statement as Cardinal Giovan Francesco Negroni. Haskell first narrates that Negroni had the arms of Clement IX and Innocent XI carved on the altar front, then quotes his letter to Oliva saying that he had ordered masons to begin a memorial record for the two popes. The first point is Haskell’s narration; the second is the quoted letter text. The original letter has not been consulted and its manuscript identity is not established here. This passage supplies no date or repository. The bilingual title is descriptive, not an original title.
