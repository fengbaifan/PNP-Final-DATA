## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 后记称该两卷研究汇集17世纪罗马节庆的视觉与文献材料，并评价其有助于理解节庆与艺术、赞助、政治及思想环境的关系。书目将其署为 Maurizio Fagiolo dell’Arca 与 Silvia Carandini 合著；被引出版物未在本项目独立阅读。中文题名为工作译名。 [S1–S2]

**English:** Haskell’s postscript says this two-volume study assembled visual and documentary material on seventeenth-century Roman festivals and discusses its value for understanding their relations to art, patronage, politics, and intellectual life. The bibliography names Maurizio Fagiolo dell’Arca and Silvia Carandini as coauthors. The cited publication was not independently consulted; the Chinese title is a working translation. [S1–S2]
