## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔所述、科雷尔于1768年在所属俱乐部朗读的一篇关于“古人的献祭”的论文。书中原文称其为 *paper on* 该主题；此处为描述性题名，不确认为原稿正式题名，也不推定科雷尔是作者。第381页注4给出 Archivio Correr 1468/10 的6 a-d号档案定位，但该条目与这篇论文是否为同一件文献尚未证实；原稿和档案目录未查。

**English:** This card records Haskell’s report that Correr read a paper on “The Sacrifices of the Ancients” to his club in 1768. The descriptive title follows the wording *paper on* this subject; it is not asserted as the manuscript’s formal title, and Correr’s authorship is not inferred. Note 4 on p. 381 gives the Archivio Correr 1468/10 locator, items 6 a-d, but does not establish which item, if any, is this paper. Neither the manuscript nor the archival catalogue was consulted.
