## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡标识 Robert Enggass 的《The Painting of Baciccio》。Haskell 第20章称该书为 Gaulli 专著，第21章书目列其题名、费城和1964年。中文题名为项目工作译名；该书未独立查阅，所述壁画研究不作本项目的独立核验结论。

**English:** This unit identifies Robert Enggass's *The Painting of Baciccio*. Haskell calls it a monograph on Gaulli in chapter 20; the chapter 21 bibliography lists its title, Philadelphia, and 1964. The Chinese title is a working translation. The book has not been independently consulted, and Haskell's account of its fresco study is not presented as an independently verified finding.
