## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第2章 p.59 注3所指的1646年3月9日致 Cassiano dal Pozzo 的书信。脚注称该信刊于 Lumbroso 第312页；本书第21章唯一的 Lumbroso 书目项为 *Notizie sulla vita di Cassiano dal Pozzo*（Torino，1874），据此将简称对应到[既有文献卡](lumbroso-notizie-cassiano-dal-pozzo-1874.md)。脚注没有明示发信人；书信、刊本及第312页均未独立查阅。卡名是描述性工作题名，不是原件题名。

**English:** This card records the letter to Cassiano dal Pozzo dated 9 March 1646, identified in Haskell’s Chapter 2, p. 59, note 3. The note says it was published by Lumbroso, p. 312; Chapter 21’s sole Lumbroso bibliography entry is *Notizie sulla vita di Cassiano dal Pozzo* (Torino, 1874), which identifies the abbreviated reference with the existing publication card. The note does not name the sender. Neither the letter, the edition, nor p. 312 was independently consulted. The card title is descriptive, not an original title.
