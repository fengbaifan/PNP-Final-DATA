### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 转引的外层来信中提到的另一封信：总督致画家 Giordani。来源仅以“Viceroy”称呼发信人，并称该信 directed to painter Giordani；没有提供这封内层信件的日期或档案定位。本文只据 Haskell 的转述，不表示已查阅内层信件原件。

**English:** This card records a distinct letter mentioned in the outer letter quoted by Haskell: a Viceroy’s letter directed to painter Giordani. The source identifies the sender by office only and gives no date or archival locator for the inner letter. The card is based on Haskell’s report and does not imply consultation of the inner letter itself.
