## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记拉丁文题名 *Beatae Mariae Virginis Officium* 的1740年威尼斯刊本。中文题名“圣母玛利亚日课”为项目描述性暂译，不声称是原刊自题。Haskell第13章p.337以该题名指称一部1740年出版的宗教插图书；OPAC SBN记录PUVE008510将同题名著录为印刷单行本，出版项为Venetiis: Apud Jo. Baptistam Pasquali, 1740。该目录记录支持文献身份及刊本书目项，不核实Haskell关于插图委托、资助人Caime或艺术评价的叙述；本轮未独立查阅原刊。出版者入口：[Giovanni Battista Pasquali](../persons/pasquali.md)。[第13章 p.337](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md)（L57）[书末索引](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/22_CHP-22Index_intro.md)（L2308）

**English:** This card records the 1740 Venice edition under the Latin title *Beatae Mariae Virginis Officium*. The Chinese title “圣母玛利亚日课” is a descriptive project translation, not a claimed title of the original publication. Chapter 13, p. 337, uses the title for a religious illustrated book published in 1740; OPAC SBN record PUVE008510 catalogs the same title as a printed monograph and gives the imprint Venetiis: Apud Jo. Baptistam Pasquali, 1740. The catalog record supports the publication identity and bibliographic imprint; it does not verify Haskell’s statements about illustration commissions, the financier Caime, or artistic evaluation. The printed volume was not independently consulted. Publisher entry: [Giovanni Battista Pasquali](../persons/pasquali.md).
