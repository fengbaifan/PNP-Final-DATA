## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell将扎内蒂1752年的第一人称引文系于注6所列书信；注6称其为12月22日致A. F. Gori，并列出佛罗伦萨Biblioteca Marucelliana, MSS. B. VIII, 13, p. 400。原信与馆藏目录未独立查阅。题名为依据原书所载通信双方和日期拟定的描述性工作题名，不是原信题名。本信与第13章p.344注8所列1752年8月26日书信为不同文献对象。本卡只记录哈斯克尔的书内引文与定位，不表示本项目已查阅原信或馆藏目录。

**English:** Haskell links Zanetti’s 1752 first-person quotation to the letter cited in note 6, addressed to A. F. Gori on 22 December and located at Biblioteca Marucelliana, MSS. B. VIII, 13, p. 400. The letter and repository catalogue were not independently consulted. The title is a descriptive working title based on the correspondents and date reported in the book, not an original letter title. This is a distinct document from the letter of 26 August 1752 cited in Chapter 13, note 8. This card records Haskell’s report only; it does not indicate that the manuscript or catalogue was consulted.
