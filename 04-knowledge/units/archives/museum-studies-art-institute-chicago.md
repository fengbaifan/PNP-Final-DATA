## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 芝加哥艺术博物馆出版的研究期刊，内容涉及馆藏、馆史和特展；本书引用其1971年刊载的沃特豪斯论文。

**English:** A research periodical published by the Art Institute of Chicago, addressing its collections, history and special exhibitions; the book cites Waterhouse’s article published in it in 1971.
