## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔注释引用1724年威尼斯刊行、为彼得罗·洛雷丹所作的《La Rappresentanza del vero merito》。注释称其职务为 Legnano 堡垒的 provveditore 与 captain；这不表示洛雷丹曾前往 Legnano。

**English:** Haskell cites the 1724 Venetian oration *La Rappresentanza del vero merito* for Pietro Loredan. The note gives his offices as provveditore and captain of the fortress of Legnano; it does not say that Loredan travelled there.
