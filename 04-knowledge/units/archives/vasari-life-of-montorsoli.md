## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记 Haskell 第10章脚注所称的 Vasari《蒙托尔索利传》这一书内文献指称。脚注称该 Life 包含佛罗伦萨 Accademia di Disegno 成立的记述。第19章附录转录一张归于 Andrea Memmo 的便笺，其中有阅读 Vasari 所载 Montorsoli 生平的指示并标记“Letta”。这只记录 Haskell 的叙述与转录；便笺原件、所指作品版本和阅读者均未独立核实。中文题名为工作译名，不代表确认了正式书名或具体版本。

**English:** This card records the source-local work reference called Vasari's Life of Montorsoli in Haskell's Chapter 10 note. The note says that the Life contains an account of the formation of the Accademia di Disegno in Florence. Chapter 19's appendix transcribes a note attributed to Andrea Memmo that directs the reader to the life of Montorsoli in Vasari and marks it “Letta.” This records Haskell's account and transcription only; the note, the work's edition, and the reader have not been independently verified. The Chinese display title is a working translation, not a confirmed formal title or edition.
