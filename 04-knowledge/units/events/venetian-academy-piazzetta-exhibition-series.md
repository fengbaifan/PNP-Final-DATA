### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔所述威尼斯学院在皮亚泽塔设立年度展览安排的系列：1777年一群未具名贵族请求学院成员每年在一处展台展示作品；艺术家须承担高额开销，学院还需借画补足数量。作者说抱怨频繁，到1787年费用已难以承受，展览遂停止；原文没有给出末届的确切年份。“国家干预艺术”的句子是作者对这一变化的总结，不作为独立机构或关系事实。第10章 p.331 尚未完成本轮独立 S2 审读。〔[第10章 p.331](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_sec_ii.md)（L271）〕

**English:** This unit records the annual exhibition arrangement described by Haskell for the Venetian Academy at a stand in the Piazzetta. In 1777 a group of unnamed nobles asked Academy members to exhibit there each year; artists had to meet heavy expenses, and the Academy resorted to loans to make up the required number of pictures. Haskell says complaints were frequent and that the shows had been discontinued by 1787 because costs had become crippling; the exact final year is not stated. His concluding phrase about State interference in the arts is retained as the author’s interpretation, not made into a separate institutional or relational fact. Chapter 10, p. 331, has not yet been covered by this round of independent S2 review. [第10章 p.331](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_sec_ii.md)（L271）
