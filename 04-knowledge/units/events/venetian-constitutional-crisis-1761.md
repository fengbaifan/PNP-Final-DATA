### 描述

**中文：** 第15章在安杰洛·奎里尼小节中称，他于1761年宪政危机期间首次受到广泛注意，并在危机中发挥主导作用。此卡仅标识该书内称谓的事件，年代限于1761年；Haskell所引 Bozzola 研究未独立查阅。

**English:** In the section on Angelo Querini, Haskell says that Querini first achieved notoriety during the constitutional crisis of 1761 and played the leading part in it. This card identifies only the event as described in the book and limits its date to 1761. Haskell’s cited Bozzola study was not independently consulted.
