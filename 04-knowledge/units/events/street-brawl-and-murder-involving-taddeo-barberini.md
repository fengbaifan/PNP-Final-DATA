### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称塔代奥·巴贝里尼在“不到三年”的时间内牵涉一次街头斗殴和一起凶杀，随后提到一项措辞宽泛的教皇赦免。原文没有在此处指明受害者、责任性质或精确日期，本卡不补作判断。

**English:** Haskell says that within three years Taddeo Barberini was involved in a street brawl and a savage murder, then mentions a broadly worded papal pardon. The passage does not identify the victim, the nature of Taddeo’s responsibility, or a precise date; this card adds none of those details.
