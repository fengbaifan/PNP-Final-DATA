## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡仅记录哈斯克尔在第6章所述的教宗克勉九世之死。该页没有给出死亡日期、地点或经过；死亡在该段中只是改建与墓地方案被放弃的时间界标，不据此认定二者存在因果关系。

**English:** This unit records only Pope Clement IX’s death as mentioned in Haskell’s chapter 6 account. The page gives no date, place, or circumstances of death; the death serves as the temporal boundary after which the proposed remodelling and burial scheme was dropped, without establishing causation.
