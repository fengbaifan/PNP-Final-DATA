### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记 Haskell 所述 Andrea Memmo 在家族宫邸举行的反复开放聚会。原文称他与兄弟们一起接待当时“更为进步的知识界人士”（the more advanced spirits of the day），直至 Memmo 于1792年去世。原文未提供聚会的具体日期或频率、宫邸的可识别名称、兄弟姓名或来客名单；不据此推定。该 KU 表示反复发生的社会活动，不代表单次聚会。[S1：第15章 p.368](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_ii.md)（L41）

**English:** This unit records Haskell’s account of recurring open-house gatherings held by Andrea Memmo in his family palace. The text says he would receive his brothers and “the more advanced spirits of the day” until his death in 1792. It gives no specific dates or frequency, identifiable palace name, brothers’ names, or guest list; none are inferred here. The KU represents a recurring social activity, not one individual gathering.
