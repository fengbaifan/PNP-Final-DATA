## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔所述、归于教宗克勉九世的圣母大殿祭坛席改建与墓地方案：计划为本人提供一处比同一教堂中西克斯图斯五世和保罗五世墓地更宏大的安葬地点。哈斯克尔说方案在克勉九世去世后被放弃；这只说明先后顺序，不证明死亡导致方案中止。方案未实施，不能据此建立一件已建成的墓碑或建筑作品。

**English:** This unit records Haskell’s account of a plan attributed to Pope Clement IX to remodel the tribune of Santa Maria Maggiore as a burial place for himself, more imposing than the tombs of Sixtus V and Paul V in the same church. Haskell says the plan was dropped after Clement IX’s death; this establishes sequence, not causation. The proposal was not carried out, so it does not establish a completed tomb or architectural work.
