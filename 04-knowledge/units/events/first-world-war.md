## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称一座与索德里尼壁画周期相关但未具名的别墅在第一次世界大战期间被毁。本卡只记录该书的时间参照，不确定别墅身份或毁坏地点。

**English:** Haskell’s note says an unnamed villa associated with the Soderini fresco cycle was destroyed during the First World War. This card records the book’s time reference; it does not identify the villa or site.
