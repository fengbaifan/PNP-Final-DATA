## 内容

### 事件身份与范围

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔叙述的格列高利十五世颁令册封依纳爵、方济各·沙勿略与腓力·内利这一事件。作者称该册封时机之后举行盛大庆典，并接着提到委托 Van Dyck 为两位耶稣会圣人绘制画作。该段未给出册封的精确日期或庆典地点；本卡不补入外部日期、地点或仪式细节。[第3章 p.64](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_i.md#L18)（L17–18）

**English:** This unit records Haskell’s account of Gregory XV decreeing the canonisation of Ignatius, Francis Xavier, and Philip Neri. Haskell says spectacular ceremonies followed the occasion and then mentions a commission to Van Dyck for paintings of the two Jesuit saints. The passage gives no exact date for the decree or location for the ceremonies; this card adds no external date, location, or ceremonial detail. [第3章 p.64](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_i.md#L18) (L17–18)
