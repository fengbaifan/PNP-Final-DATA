## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本事件记录哈斯克尔所述巴贝里尼宫内最初举行的歌剧演出：正文记为1632年2月，并称数年后演出才从宫内大房间迁入 Pietro da Cortona 专门建造的剧场。这不是关于歌剧在欧洲或罗马首次出现的主张。

**English:** This event records the first operatic performances held in the Barberini palace in Haskell’s account. The text dates them to February 1632 and says they moved from a large room in the palace to a purpose-built theatre by Pietro da Cortona only a few years later. It is not a claim about the first opera in Europe or Rome.

### 时间、地点与过程

**中文：**

**事件类型：** 宫廷宫邸内的歌剧演出
- **哈斯克尔所记日期：** 1632年2月；注3记录1634、1633与1632等异说，并称1632年获 Bouchard 证词与一则 avviso 支持，后续重演造成混淆。
- **起初场地：** 巴贝里尼宫内大房间。
- **后续场地：** 数年后迁入 Pietro da Cortona 建造、可容纳约3,000人的专用剧场。
- **证据边界：** 日期及迁移叙述均按哈斯克尔正文与注3登记，本轮未另行核对注中所引文献。

**English:**

**Event type:** Operatic performances in a court palace
- **Date in Haskell:** February 1632; footnote 3 records competing dates of 1634, 1633, and 1632, and endorses 1632 on Bouchard’s evidence and an avviso; later repeat performances caused confusion.
- **Initial venue:** A large room in the Barberini palace.
- **Later venue:** A purpose-built theatre by Pietro da Cortona, seating about 3,000, to which the performances moved a few years later.
- **Evidence boundary:** Dates and transfer are recorded from Haskell’s text and note 3; the cited works have not been independently checked in this registration.

中文：**身份边界：** 本卡是宫内演出事件组，不是剧场建筑或某一部歌剧作品；演出参与者、剧目与相关人员仍按各自候选处理。正文称“最初”演出，范围限于巴贝里尼宫的叙述语境。日期采用哈斯克尔在注3中的裁决，同时保留其列出的史料分歧，不扩展为独立验证结论。

English: **Identity boundary:** This card records the palace performance event, not the theatre building or a particular opera. Participants, works, and related people remain separate candidates. Haskell’s wording about performances “for the first time” is limited to this palace context. The date follows his conclusion in note 3 while retaining the cited disagreement; it is not presented as an independent verification.
