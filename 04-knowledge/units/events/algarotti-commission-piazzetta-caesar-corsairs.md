## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡表示Haskell第14章p.355所述的Algarotti委托绘画事件：委托绘制的题材是Caesar与基利西亚海盗，为Augustus而作。段落将此委托置于Piazzetta的作品讨论中；原书没有给出事件日期、委托文书或具体存世版本。本卡不据委托推断画作完成或仍有存世实物。

**English:** This unit records Haskell’s account of Algarotti’s commission for a painting of Caesar and the Cilician corsairs for Augustus. The passage places the commission in its discussion of Piazzetta’s works, but gives no date, commission document, or identified surviving version. The commission is not treated as proof that a particular physical painting was completed or survives.

### 本章相关内容

Haskell称Algarotti为国王委托的画作已经失佚；本页没有给出此委托的确切日期，也没有指出某一存世实物或现藏地点。脚注2另报告Mucius Scaevola与The Death of Darius两幅画在该委托之后不久绘成；该时间说法归于Pallucchini，经Haskell转述。
