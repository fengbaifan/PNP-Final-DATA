## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本事件卡记录哈斯克尔在第5章提及、并在第6章明确置于1644年3月和约签订后不久的教皇乌尔班八世之死。来源未给出具体死亡日期；卡片不从相对时间推定某一日。

**English:** This event card records the death of Pope Urban VIII, which Haskell mentions in Chapter 5 and places in Chapter 6 shortly after the March 1644 peace. The sources provide no exact date, so the card does not infer one from this relative chronology.
