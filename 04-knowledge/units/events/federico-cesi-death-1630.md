## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔在第4章注释中称，林琴科学院创办人费德里科·切西亲王于1630年去世。脚注还说科学院在切西去世后很快瓦解；这只是先后关系，不能据此断言其死亡是瓦解的原因。

**English:** In a Chapter 4 footnote, Haskell says that Prince Federico Cesi, founder of the Accademia dei Lincei, died in 1630. The note also says the academy quickly disintegrated after his death; this establishes sequence in the account, not that his death caused the disintegration.
