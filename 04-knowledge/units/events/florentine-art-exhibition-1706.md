## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 记录哈斯克尔所述佛罗伦萨1706年圣路加节绘画展览。第20章后记将其与1705年展览区分，并修正斐迪南在展览史中的先驱作用；本卡不把他写成唯一组织者。展览地点按书内称谓链接到[圣路加礼拜堂](../places/annunziata-chapel-of-saint-luke.md)及[圣母领报修道院回廊](../places/ss-annunziata-cloister-florence.md)。

**English:** This unit records Haskell's Florentine picture exhibition associated with St Luke's Day in 1706. The postscript distinguishes it from the 1705 exhibition and qualifies Ferdinand's pioneering role; this card does not make him its sole organizer. The reported venue is linked to the [Chapel of St Luke](../places/annunziata-chapel-of-saint-luke.md) and the [SS Annunziata cloister](../places/ss-annunziata-cloister-florence.md).
