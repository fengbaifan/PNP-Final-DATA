## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔列为圣母奥迹之一的“圣母奉献”，并保留他将这一主题指定给 Chiesa Nuova 横臂祭坛的说法。这里指宗教图像与礼仪语境中的奉献主题，不代表某一有确定日期的历史事件。第3章同段另提 Barocci 的《圣母往见》祭坛画；稍后又写到另行委托的《献耶稣于圣殿》，后者不是本卡所指的圣母奉献主题。[第3章 p.69](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L46–52）

**English:** This unit records the Presentation of the Virgin as a Marian mystery named by Haskell and assigned in his account to the transept altars at Chiesa Nuova. It denotes a devotional and iconographic subject, not a dated historical occurrence. The same chapter separately discusses Barocci’s Visitation altarpiece and a later commission of the Presentation of Jesus in the Temple; the latter is distinct from this Marian subject. [Chapter 3, p. 69](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md) (L46–52)

- 地点语境：Haskell 将其与 Chiesa Nuova 的横臂祭坛联系起来；对应关系候选仍待 S6 裁决。
