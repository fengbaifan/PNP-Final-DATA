## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 在既定题材和尺寸之外追问衣色、会规载体、人物左右及照明，说明画家会主动要求更具体的指示。

**English:** Beyond prescribed subjects and dimensions, Guercino asks about clothing colours, the form of the rules, the placement of figures and lighting, showing an artist actively seeking more detailed instructions.
