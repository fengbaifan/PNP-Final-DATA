## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔记载的1697年展览场次。Prince Pio 同意自费出借画作，条件是只展出他的画；为满足其他收藏者，另有画作陈列于回廊上层画廊。作者称该场次成为一场社会仪式，包含圣彼得教堂一位传道者的布道及枢机主教集会。此处没有给其他收藏者、传道者或枢机主教具名；本卡不为这些群体补造身份。〔[第5章 p.128](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_i.md)（L97）〕

**English:** This unit records the 1697 exhibition described by Haskell. Prince Pio agreed to lend pictures at his own expense on the condition that only his pictures be shown; further pictures were displayed in the upper gallery of the cloisters to accommodate other collectors. Haskell says the occasion became a social ceremony with a sermon by a preacher of St Peter’s and an assembly of cardinals. The passage does not name the other collectors, preacher, or cardinals; this unit does not supply identities for them. [第5章 p.128](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_i.md)（L97）
