## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记 Haskell 所述罗马贝加莫社群的瞻礼画展系列：有时在每年8月24日圣巴托洛梅奥瞻礼举办较有组织的展览，地点为 S. Bartolomeo 教堂庭院，由 Guardiano 支付费用。Haskell称展览敬奉圣人及其信众，教堂立面和周边住宅悬挂挂毯，画作陈列于庭院；起始年份不详，至迟在1650年已兴盛。此卡的中英文题名为描述性名称，不是原书给出的正式系列标题。[第5章 p.125](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_i.md)（L56–57）

**English:** This card records the exhibition series Haskell describes for the Bergamasque community in Rome. More organised exhibitions were sometimes held on the feast day of St Bartholomew, 24 August, in the courtyard of S. Bartolomeo and paid for by the Guardiano. Haskell says the event honoured the saint and his devotees, with tapestries hung on the church façade and nearby houses and pictures displayed in the courtyard. The start date is unknown; Haskell says the exhibitions were flourishing by 1650. The bilingual card title is descriptive and is not presented as a formal title from the book. [第5章 p.125](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_i.md)（L56–57）
