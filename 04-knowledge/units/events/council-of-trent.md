## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔在叙述保罗三世的墓葬时称，保罗三世曾召集特伦托会议。本卡保留该处对会议的指称；该段没有进一步说明会议的日期或过程。

**English:** While discussing Paul III’s tomb, Haskell says that Paul III had summoned the Council of Trent. This card preserves the book’s reference to the Council; the passage gives no further date or account of its proceedings.
