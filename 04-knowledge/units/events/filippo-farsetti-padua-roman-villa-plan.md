## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡表示原拟在帕多瓦实施而未能建成的罗马式别墅计划。哈斯克尔将受阻原因归于与当地土地所有者的争议；此计划不代表帕多瓦存在一座已建成的法尔塞蒂别墅。 [第15章来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_i.md)

**English:** This card represents the unrealized plan for a Roman-type villa at Padua. Haskell attributes its non-realization to a dispute with local landowners; the plan does not identify a completed Farsetti villa at Padua. [Chapter 15 source](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_i.md)
