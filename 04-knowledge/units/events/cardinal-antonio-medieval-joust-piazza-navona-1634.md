## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本事件记录哈斯克尔所述1634年卡迪纳尔安东尼奥在罗马纳沃纳广场组织的中世纪式骑士比武。原计划是迎接一位波兰亲王来访的娱乐活动；亲王取消访问后，安东尼奥仍决定举行，并以复兴青年骑士精神为由。Bentivoglio 负责安排，Andrea Sacchi 绘制了比武高潮场面。

**English:** This event records the medieval-style joust that Haskell says Cardinal Antonio organised in Rome’s Piazza Navona in 1634. It was first planned as entertainment for a visiting Polish prince; after the visit was cancelled, Antonio continued with the event, invoking the revival of chivalry among Rome’s young men. Bentivoglio arranged it, and Andrea Sacchi painted its culminating moment.
