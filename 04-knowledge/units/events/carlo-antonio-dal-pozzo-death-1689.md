## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔脚注以1689年为卡洛·安东尼奥·达尔·波佐的去世年份，并说画作收藏“可能”在其死后“不久”开始散佚。后一个判断的概率和时间模糊性均予保留；本卡不将收藏散佚写成其死亡的确定结果或直接原因。

**English:** Haskell's note dates Carlo Antonio dal Pozzo's death to 1689 and says the painting collection “probably” began to be dispersed “not very long after” his death. Both the uncertainty and the vague interval are retained; the collection's dispersal is not treated as a certain result or direct cause of his death.
