## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔叙述的耶稣会奎里纳尔山初学院教堂重建工程，地点为罗马的 S. Andrea al Quirinale。书中称 Bernini 于1658年开始建造；工程前身包括1649年 Cardinal Ceva 同意建新教堂与宿舍、选择 Borromini 后绘图未获 Innocent X 执行，以及七年后院长向 Alexander VII 上诉成功。第3章另记 Prince Camillo Pamfili 于1663年查看施工。以上日期与进程均按原书叙述保留；所引段落没有给出工程完工日期。中文题名为工作译名。[第3章 p.85](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md)（L90–91） [第3章 p.86](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md)（L97–101） [第3章 p.87](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md)（L107–108）

**English:** This unit records Haskell’s account of the Jesuit rebuilding project for the novitiate church of S. Andrea al Quirinale in Rome. The book says Bernini began building in 1658. The preceding project history includes Cardinal Ceva’s 1649 agreement to build a new church and lodgings, his selection of Borromini, the prohibition by Innocent X against executing the drawings, and the Rector’s successful appeal to Alexander VII seven years later. Chapter 3 also reports Prince Camillo Pamfili’s visit to the work in progress in 1663. Dates and stages follow Haskell’s account; the cited passage does not give a completion date. The Chinese title is a working translation.
