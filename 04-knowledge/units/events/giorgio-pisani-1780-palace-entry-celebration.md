## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所述 Pisani 入城游行当晚在宫邸举行的大型庆祝会。书中将其作为游行当晚的另一场事件叙述，且叙事时间顺序不能单独证明庆祝会导致其后审讯行动。

**English:** This unit records the large palace celebration that Haskell places on the evening of Pisani’s entry procession. The book narrates it as a distinct event that evening; the narrative sequence alone does not establish that the celebration caused the later inquisitorial action.

- Event type: a palace celebration on the evening of the entry procession; the title is descriptive.
- Date: linked to Haskell’s account of Pisani’s 1780 entry; no exact date is supplied.
- Place: the palace; the passage does not restate the palace’s proper name here.
- Participants: guests and spies. Haskell reports a picture in the main reception room that was read as politically charged and French lines printed on the menus.

### 身份边界

中文：本事件与 Pisani 当选及公开入城仪式候选 cand-9792 分开：它发生在游行当晚。四天后审讯机构采取行动、随后 Pisani 被捕是原书接续叙述的后续事态；本卡不将叙述顺序提升为因果结论。

English: This event is separate from candidate cand-9792, Pisani’s election and public entry: it is the celebration held on the evening of the procession. Haskell next narrates inquisitorial action four days later and Pisani’s arrest; this card does not turn narrative sequence into a causal conclusion.
