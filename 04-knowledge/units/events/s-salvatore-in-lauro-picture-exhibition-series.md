## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔称为“一项常规艺术展览”的 S. Salvatore in Lauro 画展系列。第5章将其置于罗马的 Marchigian colony 语境：该群体在12月10日庆祝 Loreto 圣屋到达周年，庆典包括音乐会和画展；书中说画作在10日和11日展出，并记载自1682年起由画家 Giuseppe Ghezzi 负责安排。来源只说该系列“在本世纪晚些时候开始”，没有给出确切首年。展场称为教堂回廊；对应 place 候选 cand-6053 的身份与粒度仍待证，不据此添加正式地点边。第8章另报告一幅 Juan de Pareja 肖像于1704年借展至同一地点的一场展览；该场是否属于本常规系列仍留待 S6。[第5章 p.128](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_i.md)（L91–95） [第8章 p.222 注4](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md)（L136–137）

**English:** This unit records the S. Salvatore in Lauro picture-exhibition series that Haskell calls “one more regular art exhibition.” Chapter 5 places it in the Roman Marchigian-colony context: the group celebrated the arrival anniversary of the Holy House in Loreto on 10 December with festivities including concerts and a picture exhibition. Haskell says the paintings remained on view on the 10th and 11th, and that painter Giuseppe Ghezzi was in charge of arrangements from 1682. The source says the series began “much later in the century” but gives no exact first year. The venue is described as the church cloisters; place candidate cand-6053 remains unresolved in identity and granularity, so no formal place edge is added. Chapter 8 separately reports that a Juan de Pareja portrait was lent to an exhibition at the same venue in 1704; whether that occurrence belonged to this regular series remains for S6.
