## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔列为圣母奥迹之一的“圣母加冕”，并保留他将该主题指定给 Chiesa Nuova 横臂祭坛的说法。它指宗教图像与礼仪语境中的主题，不代表某一有确定日期的历史事件。第3章稍后提到曾未能从 Barocci 取得同名祭坛画；该画作与本卡所指主题节点区分，不推定画作已经完成或进入教堂。[第3章 p.69](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L46–52）

**English:** This unit records the Coronation of the Virgin as a Marian mystery named by Haskell and assigned in his account to the transept altars at Chiesa Nuova. It denotes a devotional and iconographic subject, not a dated historical occurrence. Chapter 3 later mentions an unsuccessful attempt to obtain an altarpiece of the same subject from Barocci; that work is distinct from this subject node, and the passage does not establish that the painting was completed or installed. [Chapter 3, p. 69](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md) (L46–52)
