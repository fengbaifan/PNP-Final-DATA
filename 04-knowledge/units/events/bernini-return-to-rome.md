## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本事件记录伯尼尼1665年巴黎之行后的返罗马行程。哈斯克尔只说到10月时他已在返罗马途中；出发日和抵达日均未给出。

**English:** This event records Bernini’s return journey from Paris to Rome after his 1665 visit. Haskell says that by October he was on his way back; the departure and arrival dates are not specified.
