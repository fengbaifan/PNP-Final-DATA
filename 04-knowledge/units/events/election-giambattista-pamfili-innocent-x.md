## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 记录哈斯克尔第6章所指吉安巴蒂斯塔·潘菲利当选英诺森十世这一事件。原书将此当选与法国利益及艺术整体遭受的变化相联系；本卡保留作者的叙述范围，不补出该段未载的选举日期、程序或独立因果结论。随后叙述的教宗选举会议另作区分。

**English:** This unit records the election of Giambattista Pamfili as Innocent X as named in Chapter 6. Haskell connects the election with changes affecting French interests and the arts as a whole; this card preserves the author's account without supplying a date, procedure, or independently established causal conclusion absent from the passage. The conclave described later is treated as a separate event.
