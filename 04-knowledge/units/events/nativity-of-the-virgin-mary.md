## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第3章所列圣母奥迹之一“圣母诞生”。他称 Chiesa Nuova 高坛被指定给圣母诞生，并在约1605年的叙述中称高坛原本奉献给 Nativity。后一说法不证明奉献状态始终未变。本卡指宗教图像与礼仪语境中的主题，不指某一可定年的圣母诞生历史事件。[第3章 p.69](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L46–48）；[第3章 p.70](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L58）

**English:** This unit records the Nativity of the Virgin as one of the Marian mysteries named by Haskell in Chapter 3. He says the High Altar at Chiesa Nuova was assigned to the Nativity and, in his account of events around 1605, calls the altar originally dedicated to the Nativity. The latter statement does not establish an unchanged dedication. This unit denotes a devotional and iconographic subject, not a dated historical occurrence of the Virgin’s birth. [Chapter 3, p. 69](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md) (L46–48); [Chapter 3, p. 70](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md) (L58)

### 活动性质与时间

- 性质：圣母奥迹及祭坛奉献主题；不是本书所定年的单次历史事件。
- 时间：来源未给出所描绘诞生事件的日期。
- 书内语境：高坛的原奉献关系见下方记录。
