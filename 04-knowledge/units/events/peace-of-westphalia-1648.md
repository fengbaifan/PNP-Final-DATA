## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本知识元将第6章所称“1648年和约”与第7章明名的《威斯特伐利亚和约》归为同一事件。哈斯克尔称该和约结束了三十年战争，并称其巩固了 Wittelsbach 王朝的地位。本卡仅记录原书叙述及其称名，不补充签约方、签署日期与地点、条约条款或外部核实。

**English:** This unit groups the “peace settlement of 1648” in Chapter 6 with the Peace of Westphalia explicitly named in Chapter 7 as one event. Haskell says the settlement ended the Thirty Years’ War and strengthened the position of the Wittelsbach dynasty. This card records the book’s account and naming only; it does not add signatories, an exact signing date or place, treaty terms, or independent verification.
