## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell称Poussin画作题材所取的故事：Camillus围攻Falerium时拒绝教师提出交出学生为人质，将教师交还学生。Haskell称题材取自Livy；本卡不认定事件实际发生，也不补充年代或Livy具体作品。

**English:** This unit records the story Haskell identifies as the subject of a Poussin painting: while besieging Falerium, Camillus refuses a schoolmaster’s offer to deliver pupils as hostages and hands the teacher over to them. Haskell attributes the subject to Livy. This record does not establish that the event occurred or supply a date or a specific work by Livy.
