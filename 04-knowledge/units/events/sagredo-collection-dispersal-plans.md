## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称格拉尔多·萨格雷多于1738年去世后，藏品处置计划随即开始酝酿；他另称藏品后来经过较长时间分批散出。计划与实际分散保持区别。

**English:** Haskell reports that plans to disperse the collection began after Gherardo Sagredo died in 1738. He separately says that the collection was later dispersed piecemeal over a long period; the plan and later dispersal remain distinct.

### 本章相关内容

Haskell reports that plans to disperse the collection began after Gherardo Sagredo died in 1738; he distinguishes these plans from the later piecemeal dispersal.
