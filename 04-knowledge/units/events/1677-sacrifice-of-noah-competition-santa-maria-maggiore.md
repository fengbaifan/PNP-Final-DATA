## 内容

### 描述
**中文：** 哈斯克尔称委员会于1677年决定为圣母玛利亚大殿的《挪亚献祭》画作举办竞赛。三位画家提交草图，Federico Cervelli 获胜，之后经一次贝加莫访问和修改后获付370杜卡特。此卡只登记该书内可识别的竞赛事件，不补造具体举行日期或场地。

**English:** Haskell reports that in 1677 the committee organized a competition for a Sacrifice of Noah painting for Santa Maria Maggiore. Three painters submitted sketches; Federico Cervelli won and was paid 370 ducats after a visit to Bergamo and alterations. This card identifies the event as described in the book without adding an exact event date or venue.

### 活动与变更
- 1677年：委员会决定举办竞赛；三名画家参与并提交草图。
- 结果：Federico Cervelli 获胜；Haskell 称其后来获付370杜卡特。
