## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 围绕 Giorgio Pisani 于1780年当选 Procuratore di San Marco 及相关公开入城、游行仪式的叙述。原书分别叙述当选和入城过程；不推定二者发生在同一天。

**English:** This unit records Haskell’s account of Giorgio Pisani’s 1780 election as Procuratore di San Marco and the associated public entry and procession. The book narrates the election and entry separately; this card does not assume that they occurred on the same day.

### 名称

中文显示名及英文显示名见卡片标题；中文名为描述性工作译名，非原书正式题名。

The bilingual display names appear in the card title; the Chinese name is a descriptive working title, not a formal title from the book.

### 事件信息

- 事件性质：当选及相关公开入城仪式；本卡标题为描述性工作题名。
- 时间：当选发生于1780年；原书对图像／版画在入城仪式中展出的判断为“有充分理由相信”其发生在1780年，未给出具体日期。
- 地点：威尼斯；原书以城市宫殿沿线及入城游行为语境。
- 参与者：Giorgio Pisani（当选者）；Gozzi 写有颂词。
- 经过：当局为新任 Procuratore 的游行路线悬挂画作是原书所述习惯；相关具体图像是否专为 Pisani 此次入城而委托，Haskell 明确提出反证并限制判断。

- Event type: election and associated public entry; the card title is descriptive.
- Date: the election took place in 1780. Haskell says there is good reason to believe that pictures or prints were displayed at the entry in 1780, without giving an exact day.
- Place: Venice, in the context of palaces along the procession route and the public entry.
- Participant: Giorgio Pisani, the elected Procuratore; Gozzi wrote a tribute.
- Course: Haskell describes pictures hung along the route as a custom for a newly elected Procuratore. He also qualifies whether the specific images were commissioned for Pisani’s entry and does not establish that they were.

### 身份边界

中文：当选与公开入城作为同一叙述组登记，但原书没有说二者同日。游行当晚的宫邸庆祝会另为事件候选 cand-9812，不并入本卡；不将所有沿线画作写成此次专门委托。

English: The election and public entry are registered as one source-defined narrative group, while the book does not say they occurred on the same day. The palace celebration on the evening of the procession is a separate event candidate, cand-9812, and is not merged here. The card does not treat all route pictures as commissions made specifically for this entry.
