## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔于第3章先明确说明，雅克·库尔图瓦即意大利人所称的贾科莫·博尔戈尼奥内；随后记述博尔戈尼奥内于1676年去世，当时已有初步草图，但他未能完成其杰作。哈斯克尔称此事使耶稣堂祭坛区的装饰事务停滞。本卡将死亡事件与库尔图瓦本人、未完成的装饰项目分别记录；项目停滞不表示项目已经完成。

**English:** Haskell explicitly identifies Jacques Courtois with Giacomo Borgognone, the name used by Italians, and later says that Borgognone died in 1676 after preliminary sketches had been made but before he could achieve his intended masterpiece. Haskell says that the Gesù tribune affair then came to a standstill. This card distinguishes the death event from [Jacques Courtois](../persons/jacques-courtois.md) and from the unfinished decoration project; the project's stoppage does not mean it was completed.

- 结果与后续：哈斯克尔称耶稣堂祭坛区装饰事务随后停滞；不据此推断完整项目的最终取消或完成。
