## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔在比较[圣罗科展览](../events/s-rocco-annual-exhibition.md)时提到巴黎的 Salon：他转述 Algarotti 至迟在1751年将[圣罗科展览](../events/s-rocco-annual-exhibition.md)称为“在某些方面”如同绘画的 tribunal，随后指出圣罗科展示在组织复杂程度和吸引关注方面都未达到 Salon 的程度。本卡只登记被明确点名的比较对象，不从这段文字补写巴黎展览的起讫时间、组织细节或历史沿革；二者不视为同一展览。〔[第10章 p.331](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_sec_ii.md)（L270）〕

**English:** Haskell mentions the Salon in Paris while comparing it with the [S. Rocco exhibitions](../events/s-rocco-annual-exhibition.md). He reports that by 1751 Algarotti described the S. Rocco exhibition as, “in some ways,” the tribunal of Venetian painting, then says its organization and public attention never approached those of the Salon. This unit records the explicitly named comparison object without adding dates, organizational details, or a history of the Paris exhibition; the two exhibitions are not treated as identical. ([Chapter 10, p. 331](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_sec_ii.md) (line 270).)
