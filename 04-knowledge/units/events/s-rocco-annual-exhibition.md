## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔所述的圣罗科年度绘画展览系列。第10章称，圣罗科的展览较有组织，每年都有若干画家参加；作者认为它们可能从[威尼斯总督职务](../institutions/doge-of-venice-office.md)与[威尼斯元老院](../institutions/senate-of-venice.md)每年8月16日前往该教堂的[巡行](../events/doge-senate-procession-to-s-rocco-august-16.md)中非正式发展而来，此解释保留“可能”限定。第9章记载[萨格雷多](../persons/zaccaria-sagredo.md)曾在某一年度展览中购入 Piazzetta 的[《守护天使》](../works/piazzetta-angelo-custode-altarpiece.md)，未给出年份。展览系列不等同于某一已定年的场次。〔[第10章 p.331](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_sec_ii.md)（L270）；[第9章 p.265](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)（L292）〕

**English:** This unit records the annual painting-exhibition series at S. Rocco described by Haskell. Chapter 10 calls the exhibitions more systematic and says that several artists took part each year. The author says they probably developed informally from the procession by the [Doge](../institutions/doge-of-venice-office.md) and [Senate](../institutions/senate-of-venice.md) to the church on 16 August; the qualification “probably” is retained. Chapter 9 says that [Sagredo](../persons/zaccaria-sagredo.md) bought Piazzetta's [*Angelo Custode*](../works/piazzetta-angelo-custode-altarpiece.md) at one annual exhibition but gives no year. The series is distinct in identity from any dated occurrence. ([Chapter 10, p. 331](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_sec_ii.md) (line 270); [Chapter 9, p. 265](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md) (line 292).)

### 基本信息

- **事件性质：** 年度绘画展览系列；不是单一场次。
- **周期：** Haskell称每年举行；未给系列起始年、结束年或完整场次数。
- **书内场所称谓：** 第10章称 Scuola di S. Rocco。具体场所对象身份仍按现有对齐记录保留，不由本卡标准化为教堂建筑或机构。
