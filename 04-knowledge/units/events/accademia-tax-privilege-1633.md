## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Urban VIII 时学院获对全体艺术家和画商征税及公共委托排他安排，受到强烈反对、未完全执行并撤回。获得法令、实际施行和撤销是不同状态。

**English:** Under Urban VIII, the Academy obtained taxation powers over artists and dealers and exclusive arrangements for public commissions. Strong opposition prevented full enforcement, and the measures were withdrawn. Enactment, implementation and withdrawal are distinct states.
