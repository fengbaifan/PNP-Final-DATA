## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本事件知识元记录 Haskell 所述 Santa Maria Maggiore 装饰计划的启动：教堂治理机构 Consiglio del Consorzio 于1653年决定开始一项大型装饰计划，主要目的是为教堂中央交叉空间提供绘画。本卡记录计划及其启动决定，不声称计划中的作品全部完成；它与1695年记载的装饰完成事件及后续各项委托分别保持身份。

**English:** This event unit records the initiation of the Santa Maria Maggiore decoration programme described by Haskell: in 1653 the church’s governing body, the Consiglio del Consorzio, decided to begin an extensive programme, chiefly to provide pictures for the church’s central crossing. This card records the programme and its initiating decision; it does not claim that all planned works were completed. It remains distinct from the later completion event recorded for 1695 and from individual commissions.
