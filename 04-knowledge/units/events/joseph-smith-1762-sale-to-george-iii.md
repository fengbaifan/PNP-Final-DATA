## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本事件知识元记录Haskell所述的Joseph Smith于1762年向George III出售藏品的交易。第10章称Smith经艰难谈判，将其最佳藏品中的大部分绘画、书籍、素描和宝石售予George III；附录5称交易包括大量绘画和素描，并在另一处再次叙述同一笔交易。原书没有给出准确数量，也没有列出完整的单件作品清单；本卡不把该交易扩大为Smith出售了全部收藏。附录5称Smith曾为相关作品编目，并推测作品选择可能有意；编目内容与选择过程均保留为Haskell的书内叙述或推论。

**English:** This event unit records Haskell's account of Joseph Smith's 1762 sale of collection holdings to George III. Chapter 10 says that, after difficult negotiations, Smith sold most of his best pictures, books, drawings, and gems to the King. Appendix 5 describes a very large number of paintings and drawings and recounts the same transaction again. Haskell gives no exact count or complete list of individual works; this card does not expand the sale to the whole collection. Appendix 5 says Smith catalogued the works and infers that their selection may have been deliberate; both points remain Haskell's report or interpretation.
