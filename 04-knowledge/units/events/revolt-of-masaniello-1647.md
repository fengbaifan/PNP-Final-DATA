## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本知识元记录哈斯克尔在第8章 p.205 所称的1647年马萨涅洛起义。第5章 p.139 说切尔阔齐的画作描绘了1647年7月7日的事件；该日期指画中表现的具体事件，不在此推断为整场起义的开始、结束或持续时间。第8章还叙述 Gaspar Roomer 在起义期间遇险并脱身；这是哈斯克尔的书内叙述，不构成对脱身原因的独立核验。

**English:** This unit records the 1647 revolt of Masaniello as identified by Haskell on Chapter 8, p. 205. On Chapter 5, p. 139, Haskell says that Cerquozzi's painting portrays events of 7 July 1647; that date identifies the depicted episode and does not establish the revolt's beginning, end, or duration. Chapter 8 also narrates Gaspar Roomer facing danger and surviving during the revolt; this remains Haskell's account and does not independently verify the reported explanation for his survival.
