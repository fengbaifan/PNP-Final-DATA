## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所称的威尼斯元老院这一政治机构。第9章提到其在国家财政资源消耗时被迫接纳新贵族家族，并称其与[总督职务](../institutions/doge-of-venice-office.md)共同庇护 Pietà 避难机构；第10章称元老院与[总督职务](../institutions/doge-of-venice-office.md)每年8月16日前往圣罗科堂[巡行](../events/doge-senate-procession-to-s-rocco-august-16.md)。上述记载保留各自章节语境，不把 Senate 与威尼斯共和国、城市或其他行政机构等同。〔[第9章 p.247](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)（L28）；[第9章 p.272](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md)（L56）；[第10章 p.331](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_sec_ii.md)（L270）〕

**English:** This unit records the political institution called the Senate of Venice in Haskell's account. Chapter 9 says it was compelled to admit new noble families as State resources were depleted and that it shared patronage of the Pietà refuge with the [Doge](../institutions/doge-of-venice-office.md). Chapter 10 says the Senate and Doge took part in the [procession to S. Rocco each 16 August](../events/doge-senate-procession-to-s-rocco-august-16.md). These statements retain their separate contexts; the Senate is not equated with the Republic of Venice, the city, or another administrative body. ([Chapter 9, p. 247](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md) (line 28); [p. 272](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md) (line 56); [Chapter 10, p. 331](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_sec_ii.md) (line 270).)
