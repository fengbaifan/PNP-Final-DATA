**English:** This card registers only the committee called “SS. i Deputati” in Haskell’s Chapter 1, note 2. The note says that it supplied the subject for Gavasetti’s fresco commission, while the commission text allowed the painter to invent and expand on that subject. The OCR form is retained; no formal name, legal identity, or organization beyond this commission is asserted.
