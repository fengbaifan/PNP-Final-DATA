## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell在第9章 p.260 描述的Pisani家族私人学院。原书未给出专名；中文与英文标题均为描述性暂名。Haskell称Pietro Longhi负责该院，学院似乎主要向Pisani之子Almorò提供绘画课，并在其去世时结束。

**English:** This card records the Pisani family’s private academy described by Haskell in Chapter 9, p.260. The source supplies no proper name; both display titles are descriptive labels. Haskell says Pietro Longhi was in charge, that the academy seems to have mainly provided drawing lessons to the Pisani’s son Almorò, and that it ended at his death.

### 本书语境

**中文：** Treccani的Pietro Longhi条目记载，Marina Sagredo为儿子Almorò Pisani设立绘画与雕刻学院，Longhi于1763年受命主持，并任至Almorò于1766年去世。该交叉语境支持本候选对应这所私人学院；原书“seems”仍保留，不扩大其活动范围。它与Accademia Albrizziana及佛罗伦萨Accademia del Disegno分别登记。

**English:** Treccani’s Pietro Longhi entry says Marina Sagredo established an academy of drawing and engraving for her son Almorò Pisani, that Longhi was appointed director in 1763, and that he held the post until Almorò died in 1766. This context supports identifying the candidate with that private academy; Haskell’s “seems” is retained and the institution’s activity is not expanded. It remains distinct from the Accademia Albrizziana and Florence’s Accademia del Disegno.
