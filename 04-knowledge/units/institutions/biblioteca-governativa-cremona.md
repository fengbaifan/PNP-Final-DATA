### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔在第9章与第10章将Biblioteca Governativa, Cremona列为Biffi书信及往来材料的保存处，并给出MSS. aa.I.4.1与aa.I.4.定位。书页读作Governativa，OCR一处作Govcrnativa；本卡仅记录书内具名机构及地名，不声称独立核查手稿、馆藏目录或机构现行状态。 [来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md#L229)；[来源2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_sec_ii.md#L341)

**English:** Haskell identifies Biblioteca Governativa, Cremona as the repository for Biffi correspondence and an unpublished 1773 letter, giving the locators MSS. aa.I.4.1 and aa.I.4. The printed reading is Governativa; one OCR transcription has Govcrnativa.
