## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 威尼斯共和国是历史政治实体，1797年5月12日将统治权移交临时市政政府；哈斯克尔以共和国覆亡界定其巴洛克研究时段的末端。

**English:** The Republic of Venice was a historical political entity that transferred governmental power to a provisional municipality on 12 May 1797; Haskell uses its fall as the endpoint of his working Baroque periodization.
