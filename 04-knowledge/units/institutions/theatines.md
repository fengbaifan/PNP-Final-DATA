## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 戴蒂尼会是1524年在罗马成立的天主教男性会规教士修会，以共同生活、牧灵服务和神职生活改革为目标。教皇批准与首批成员发愿分别发生于当年六月和九月。第一章将其列为产生新教堂需求的修会；圣安德烈亚德拉瓦莱教堂与该会有明确联系，但修会、建筑和具体艺术委托仍须分开。（S1、S4、S6–S7）

**English:** The Theatines are a Catholic male order of clerics regular founded in Rome in 1524, devoted to communal life, pastoral ministry and the reform of clerical life. Papal approval and the first companions’ profession occurred in June and September respectively. Chapter 1 lists them among the orders generating demand for new churches. Sant’Andrea della Valle is explicitly associated with the order, while the institution, building and individual art commissions remain distinct. (S1, S4, S6–S7)
