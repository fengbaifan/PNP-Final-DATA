## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔所称的 Carmelites 修会组织。第9章称其为 Scalzi 的母 Order，并分别叙述修会在 Carmini 教堂及其他场所的赞助活动；本卡只表示修会身份，不把具体教堂、Scuola 或作品并入机构。

**English:** This unit records the religious institution Haskell calls the Carmelites. Chapter 9 describes it as the mother Order of the Scalzi and recounts its patronage at the Carmini and elsewhere. This card represents the Order only; it does not merge churches, Scuole, or artworks with the institution.

### 本章相关内容

Haskell compares the Order's patronage with that of the Scalzi and says that the Elijah subject of a chariot of fire had previously been associated almost exclusively with the Carmelites. The comparison and iconographic statement retain their source wording and scope.
