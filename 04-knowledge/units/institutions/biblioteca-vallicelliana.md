**English:** This card records the Biblioteca Vallicelliana named in Haskell’s archival citations. Chapter 19, Appendix 2 gives the name with Rome and locates the Fondo Orsini there. The card records only the institutional name and archival citation context in the book; no authority record, institutional history, or present-day custody relationship was independently checked.
