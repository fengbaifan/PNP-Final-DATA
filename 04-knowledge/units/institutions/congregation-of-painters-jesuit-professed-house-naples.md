## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** DBI所称与耶稣会密切相关、驻于那不勒斯耶稣会专业会院的画家会众；此双语名称是来源描述性称谓，未确认为正式专名。

**English:** The painters’ congregation described by the DBI as closely linked to the Jesuits and based at their professed house in Naples; this bilingual label is descriptive, not established as a formal proper name.
