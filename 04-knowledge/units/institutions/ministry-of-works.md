**中文：** 英国负责历史建筑照护、公共工程及政府地产维护的历史政府部门；本书列其为图版29的供片者。

**English:** A historical UK government department responsible for historic buildings, public construction and the government estate; the book credits it as the photographic supplier for plate 29.
