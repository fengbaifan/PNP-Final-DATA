## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 在跨章叙述中以一般职务称谓提到的威尼斯总督。第9章称 Pietà 避难机构受到总督与[元老院](../institutions/senate-of-venice.md)的直接庇护；第10章称总督与[元老院](../institutions/senate-of-venice.md)每年8月16日赴圣罗科堂[巡行](../events/doge-senate-procession-to-s-rocco-august-16.md)。本卡表达制度性职务，不指向某一位总督；具名任职者另立人物 KU 并以 `held_office` 连接。〔[第9章 p.272](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md)（L56）；[第10章 p.331](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_sec_ii.md)（L270）〕

**English:** This unit records the Doge of Venice as a general institutional office in Haskell's cross-chapter account. Chapter 9 says the Pietà refuge came under the direct patronage of the Doge and Senate; Chapter 10 says the Doge and [Senate](../institutions/senate-of-venice.md) took part in the [procession to S. Rocco each 16 August](../events/doge-senate-procession-to-s-rocco-august-16.md). The unit denotes the office, not a particular officeholder; named officeholders remain distinct persons connected through `held_office`. ([Chapter 9, p. 272](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md) (line 56); [Chapter 10, p. 331](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_sec_ii.md) (line 270).)
