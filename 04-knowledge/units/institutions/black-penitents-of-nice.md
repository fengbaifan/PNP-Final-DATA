## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 尼斯黑衣忏悔者团体（Pénitents Noirs de Nice），Thevenon所引研究将其列为Bernardin Baldoino 1670年《施洗者圣若翰斩首》在罗马制作时的服务对象；现有来源未给正式法定名称、创立年代或具体礼拜堂。（S1）

**English:** The Black Penitents of Nice (Pénitents Noirs de Nice), identified by Thevenon’s cited scholarship as the intended recipient of Bernardin Baldoino’s *Beheading of Saint John the Baptist*, executed in Rome in 1670. The source does not provide a formal legal name, foundation date, or specific chapel. (S1)
