## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 罗马长官职位是Haskell在第2章讨论的历史职位。他称Taddeo Barberini于1631年获此称号，并叙述该职位权力的恢复。中文名称为描述性工作译名。

**English:** The office of Prefect of Rome is a historical office discussed by Haskell in Chapter 2. He reports that Taddeo Barberini received the title in 1631 and describes the revival of the office's authority. The Chinese name is a descriptive working translation.
