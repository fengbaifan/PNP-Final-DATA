## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡以 Haskell 的复数称呼 Dominicans 登记多明我会这一修会层级的机构身份。S3 将索引 D.csv#43（印刷页270–272）与第5章“Dominicans of Santa Sabina”、第9章威尼斯语境中的 Dominicans／the Order、以及第13章 local-Dominicans 归入同一 institution 组。书内还区分了具体地方团体（如 Santa Sabina 与 Zattere 的 Observant Dominicans）及其所在教堂；本卡记录修会层级名称，不把地方修院、教堂、个别成员或某一次行动合并为修会事实。各项转让、委托、募捐及作者概括均保留在对应 S2 候选的原限定内。[S1：第5章，印刷页131，正文行119–120](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_i.md) [S3：第9章，印刷页270，正文行35](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md) [S4：第9章，印刷页270，注7，正文行36](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md) [S8：第13章，印刷页338，正文行67–68](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md)

**English:** This unit records the institutional identity of the Dominican religious order under Haskell’s plural source form *Dominicans*. S3 grouped the D.csv#43 index entry (printed pp.270–272) with “Dominicans of Santa Sabina” in Chapter 5, the Dominicans / “the Order” in the Venetian context of Chapter 9, and “local-Dominicans” in Chapter 13. The book also distinguishes specific local communities, including Santa Sabina and the Observant Dominicans on the Zattere, and their churches. This unit records the order-level name; it does not attribute a local convent, church, individual member, or single action to the entire order. Transfers, commissions, alms reports, and authorial characterizations retain the qualifications of their S2 candidates.
