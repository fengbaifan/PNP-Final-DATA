## 内容

### 政治实体身份

**中文：** 本卡按 S3 的 institution 分类，记录原书明确命名的晚期罗马帝国政治实体。哈斯克尔将壁画组描绘的早期基督教殉道事件限定为“大多”发生于该语境；本卡保留这一范围限定，不补写政体边界、年代分期或个别殉道事件。

**English:** This card records the source-named Late Roman Empire under the S3 institution classification. Haskell places the early Christian martyrdoms depicted in the cycle mostly in this setting. The qualification “mostly” is retained; no political boundaries, period dates, or individual martyrdoms are added.

### 对象边界
