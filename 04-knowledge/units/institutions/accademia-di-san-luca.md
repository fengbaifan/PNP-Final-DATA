## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 罗马艺术家专业组织。祖卡里 1593–1594 年尝试振兴而非本章确认的首次创立；Paul V 授予年度赦免权，Gregory XV 于 1621 年确认章程，Francesco Barberini 任保护人。

1633 年征税和公共委托排他措施受阻、未全面施行、后撤回。早期并非仅收历史画家，1645 年后镀金工转为次等会员；Van Laer、Cerquozzi 的成员事实用于说明边界。

**English:** This is a professional organization of artists in Rome. Zuccari's 1593–1594 efforts were a revival, not an established founding date. Paul V granted an annual pardon privilege, Gregory XV confirmed the statutes in 1621, and Francesco Barberini was protector. The 1633 taxation and public-commission measures were opposed, incompletely enforced and withdrawn. Early membership was not restricted to history painters; gilders received secondary status after 1645. Van Laer and Cerquozzi illustrate membership boundaries.
