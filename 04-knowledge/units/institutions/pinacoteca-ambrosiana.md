## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 安布罗西亚纳画廊是米兰艺术博物馆。与同机构图书馆相连，但本卡以画廊保管角色为范围。

**English:** The Pinacoteca Ambrosiana is an art museum in Milan. It belongs to the Ambrosian institution, but this unit is limited to the gallery’s custodial role.
