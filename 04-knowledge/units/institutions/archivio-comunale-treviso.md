## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡按Haskell书中的称谓登记特雷维索的市档案馆。第14章p.348注2将一封致Bonomo的书信引至Archivio Comunale、MSS. 1256；p.349注1又把阿尔加罗蒂致兄长Bonomo的书信汇集称为保存在Archivio Comunale in Treviso。本卡保留来源中的机构称谓，不据此声称已确认该机构的现行正式名称、组织沿革或当前馆藏状态。

**English:** This card records the municipal archive as named in Haskell’s book. Chapter 14, p. 348, note 2 cites a letter to Bonomo as Archivio Comunale, MSS. 1256; p. 349, note 1 places a collection of Algarotti’s letters to his brother Bonomo in the Archivio Comunale in Treviso. The source-local designation is retained; this entry does not claim to establish the institution’s current official name, organizational history, or present holdings.
