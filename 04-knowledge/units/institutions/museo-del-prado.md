## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 普拉多国家博物馆是西班牙国家艺术博物馆。2024年官方展览确认并展示马德里《看这个人》；保管范围不等于私有产权。

**English:** The Museo Nacional del Prado is a Spanish national art museum whose 2024 official exhibition confirmed and displayed the Madrid Ecce Homo; custody does not by itself establish private ownership.
