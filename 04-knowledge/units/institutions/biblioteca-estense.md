## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔在第9、10、13章多次称摩德纳的 Biblioteca Estense 为 Gherardi 与 Muratori 相关书信的保存处。本卡按其书内用名登记机构和所载城市；图书馆目录、这些信件及机构沿革未在本批独立查阅。中文名为项目描述性译名。

**English:** Haskell repeatedly names the Biblioteca Estense in Modena as the repository for letters involving Gherardi and Muratori in Chapters 9, 10, and 13. This unit records the institution and city as named in the book; the library catalogue, the letters, and the institution’s history were not independently consulted in this batch.
