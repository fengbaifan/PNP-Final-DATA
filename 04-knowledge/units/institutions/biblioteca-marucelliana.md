## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔在第10章和第13章多处将佛罗伦萨Biblioteca Marucelliana作为Joseph Smith、A. F. Gori及Girolamo Zanetti相关书信的保存处，并列出MSS. B. VIII, 13等定位。该馆藏记录未在本任务中独立查阅；本卡仅登记书内明确命名的机构身份与地点。 [来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md#L581)；[来源2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md#L613)；[来源3](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_sec_ii.md#L339)；[来源4](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md#L233)

**English:** Haskell repeatedly names the Biblioteca Marucelliana in Florence as the repository for letters involving Joseph Smith, A. F. Gori, and Girolamo Zanetti, including references to MSS. B. VIII, 13. The manuscripts and repository catalogue were not independently consulted for this task; this card records only the institution and locality explicitly named in the book.
