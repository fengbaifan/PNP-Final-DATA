## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 1542年在万神殿成立的罗马艺术团体，原名Compagnia，后称万神殿Virtuosi学院；奥塔维奥·莱奥尼自1621年起为其成员。学院官网收藏记录列有莱奥尼受托绘制的格列高利十五世肖像。

**English:** A Roman artistic association founded at the Pantheon in 1542, originally called the Compagnia and later known as the Academy of the Virtuosi al Pantheon. Ottavio Leoni was a member from 1621. The academy’s collection lists his commissioned portrait of Gregory XV.
