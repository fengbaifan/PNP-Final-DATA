## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 卡萨纳滕塞图书馆是罗马的历史图书馆，1701 年在道明会圣母米涅瓦修院的环境中向公众开放，其早期核心来自吉罗拉莫·卡萨纳特的藏书。第一章把它列为阿梅登 1642 年罗马记述手稿 MS.5001 的保存机构，现又有官方修复项目记录支持该馆号与题名的对应。

**English:** The Biblioteca Casanatense is a historic library in Rome, opened to the public in 1701 in the Dominican setting of Santa Maria sopra Minerva, with Girolamo Casanate's collection as its early core. Chapter 1 identifies it as the repository of Ameyden's 1642 account of Rome, MS.5001; an official conservation-project record also matches that shelfmark and title.
