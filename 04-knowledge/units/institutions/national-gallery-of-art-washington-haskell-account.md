## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第10章注3所称华盛顿的National Gallery of Art。该注将一幅《最后的晚餐》素描与该机构联系起来，但未提供作者、媒材、馆藏编号或地点报告日期；素描对象身份仍未解决，亦未核实现今保管状态。

**English:** This card records the National Gallery of Art in Washington named in Haskell’s chapter 10, note 3. The note associates a sketch for the Last Supper with the institution but gives no artist, medium, inventory number, or date for the location report. The sketch’s identity remains unresolved, and present-day custody has not been checked.
