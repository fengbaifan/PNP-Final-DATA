**中文：** 柏林绘画馆是柏林艺术博物馆。现藏作品与1945年毁失作品的历史保管期分别表达。

**English:** The Gemäldegalerie in Berlin is an art museum; present holdings and the historical custody of works destroyed in 1945 are recorded separately.
