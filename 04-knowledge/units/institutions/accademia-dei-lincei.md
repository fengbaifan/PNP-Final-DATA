## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 在第2章与第4章所称的 Accademia dei Lincei。第2章将其与 Galileo 并列为罗马语境中的科学实验活动；第4章脚注99以 the Accademia 和 the Lincei 简称该机构，并讨论其成员组织。中文显示名“林琴科学院”为项目工作译名，原文名称保留为 Accademia dei Lincei。第4章将其比作欧洲科学社团的“先驱”是作者的比较性表述；本卡不据此声称存在已核实的正式制度传承，也不把本书中的历史对象与任何后来的同名机构合并。[第2章 p.33](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_ii.md)（L27）；[第4章 p.99 注99](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md)（L233）

**English:** This card records the Accademia dei Lincei named by Haskell in Chapters 2 and 4. Chapter 2 places it alongside Galileo in the context of scientific experiments in Rome; Chapter 4, footnote 99, refers to it as “the Accademia” and “the Lincei” and discusses its membership. The Chinese display name is a project working translation; the Italian name is retained. Chapter 4’s comparison of the institution to an “ancestor” of European scientific societies is recorded as the author’s characterization, not as a verified institutional succession. This card does not merge the historical subject in the book with any later institution of the same name. [第2章 p.33](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_ii.md)（L27）; [第4章 p.99 注99](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md)（L233）
