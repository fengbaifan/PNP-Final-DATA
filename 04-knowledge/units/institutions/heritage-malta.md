### 描述

**中文：** 马耳他遗产局是国家文化遗产机构。Wikidata将瓦莱塔《书写中的圣哲罗姆》列入该机构收藏；实际安置另记。

**English:** Heritage Malta is the national cultural heritage agency; Wikidata assigns Saint Jerome Writing in Valletta to its collection, while the work’s physical installation is represented separately.
