## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell跨第2、6、7、9章以France指称的国家政治实体。第2章称France为与哈布斯堡帝国竞争的天主教大国，并提到Urban VIII对法方联盟的倚重；第6章将France列为反巴贝里尼扩张的联合力量之一；第7章分别在Richelieu的称谓、Mazarin接掌France政府及一幅寓意画的政治描述中提及France；第9章将France与Austria并称为威尼斯贵族感到不适的邻国。这里仅归并原书中作为国家政治参与者或国家性参照的France，不补写政体沿革、具体政府机构、历史疆域或原书未述的外交关系。[第2章，印刷页33](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_ii.md#L23)；[第6章，印刷页146](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md#L6)；[第7章，印刷页175](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md#L67)；[第7章，印刷页180](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md#L130)；[第7章，印刷页196](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_iv.md#L59)；[第9章，印刷页254](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md#L142)

**English:** This unit records France as a state or political entity in Haskell’s accounts across Chapters 2, 6, 7, and 9. Chapter 2 calls France a great Catholic power rivaling the Hapsburg Empire and refers to Urban VIII’s reliance on an alliance with France. Chapter 6 names France among the powers acting against the Barberini plan to extend their dominions. Chapter 7 refers to France in Richelieu’s description, Mazarin’s assumption of its government, and an allegorical account of political powers. Chapter 9 names France alongside Austria as an uncomfortable neighbour to Venetian nobles. The unit groups only source references to France as a national political participant or national referent; it adds no constitutional history, specific government institutions, historical borders, or diplomatic relations absent from the source.
