### 描述

**中文：** 博尔盖塞美术馆是罗马艺术博物馆。机构与博尔盖塞家族、历史收藏分别表达。

**English:** The Galleria Borghese is an art museum in Rome; the museum, the Borghese family, and historical ownership are represented separately.
