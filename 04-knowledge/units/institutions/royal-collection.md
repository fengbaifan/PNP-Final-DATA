### 描述

**中文：** 英国皇家收藏是英国王室艺术收藏。保管《削水果的男孩》和《圣彼得与圣安德烈蒙召》。

**English:** The Royal Collection is the art collection of the British monarchy and holds Boy Peeling Fruit and The Calling of Saints Peter and Andrew.
