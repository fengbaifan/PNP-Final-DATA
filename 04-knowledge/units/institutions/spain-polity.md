## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 跨章叙述中作为国家／政治实体出现的 Spain。第2章把 Spain 放在战争威胁语境中；第3章称 Ludovisi 为 Spain 的利益行事；第5章把 Cerquozzi 的事业与 Spain 及其在 Rome 的支持者联系起来；第7章称 Spain 仍关注意大利画家及其作品；第8章提及 Spain 的衰落与 Naples 的 Spanish rule；第9章把威尼斯政府促使 Tiepolo 前往 Madrid 称为对 Spain 的绥靖；第10章又把 Spain 列为与 Venice 政体有相似之处的国家。各章指向同一政治实体，同时保留原文角色与限定。第2章的战争威胁不表示战事已经发生。[S1：第2章，印刷页28](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_iv.md#L4)；[S2：第3章，印刷页73](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md#L104)；[S3：第5章，印刷页136](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_iii.md#L43)；[S4：第5章，印刷页137](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_iii.md#L71)；[S5：第7章，印刷页189](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md#L256)；[S6：第8章，印刷页204](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i.md#L20)；[S7：第8章，印刷页209](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i.md#L63)；[S8：第9章，印刷页246](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md#L24)；[S9：第10章，印刷页276](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md#L7)

**English:** This unit records Spain as a state or political entity in Haskell’s cross-chapter account. Chapter 2 places Spain in a threatened-war context; Chapter 3 says Ludovisi acted in Spain’s interests; Chapter 5 links Cerquozzi’s career with Spain and its supporters in Rome; Chapter 7 says Spain retained an interest in Italian painters and their works; Chapter 8 refers to Spain’s decline and Spanish rule in Naples; Chapter 9 calls the Venetian government’s mission sending Tiepolo to Madrid an act of appeasement towards Spain; and Chapter 10 lists Spain among countries with regimes somewhat similar to Venice. These passages refer to the same political entity while retaining each source context and qualification. The threatened war in Chapter 2 does not establish that hostilities occurred.
