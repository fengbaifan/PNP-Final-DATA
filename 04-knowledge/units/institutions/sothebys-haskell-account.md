## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第10章注3中被点名为《洗礼》素描交易场所的Sotheby’s。注释称该素描于1960年12月7日售出；本项目未独立查阅拍卖记录，且未解决该素描的作者、拍品编号或后续所有者。

**English:** This card records Sotheby’s as the venue named in Haskell’s chapter 10, note 3 for the reported sale of a sketch for The Baptism. The note dates the sale to 7 December 1960. No auction record was independently consulted, and the sketch’s maker, lot number, and subsequent owner remain unidentified.
