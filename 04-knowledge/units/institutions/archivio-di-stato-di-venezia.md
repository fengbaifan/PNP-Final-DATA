## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔在第9章将 Archivio di Stato 列为巴尔巴罗家谱记录的保管机构，并另称扎卡里亚·萨格雷多遗嘱存于该馆。本卡限于书中所引机构名称及档案语境，不扩展为档案层级清单。

**English:** In chapter 9, Haskell names the Archivio di Stato as repository for the Barbaro genealogy and for Zaccaria Sagredo’s will. This card is limited to the institution name and archival context cited in the book.

### 本章相关内容

Haskell names the Archivio di Stato as repository for the Barbaro genealogy at MSS. VI, c. 507 and for Zaccaria Sagredo’s will; neither record was independently consulted.
