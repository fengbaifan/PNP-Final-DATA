## 内容

### 描述

**中文：**Gubbio市政共同体是《还愿祭坛画》的集体委托方；1606年档案编目记录当地执政官委托Gentile Carbonana向Federico Barocci转达制作提议，2015年圣殿指南则称社区为守护圣人许愿的祭坛委托了Salvio Savini的作品。此卡仅表示本项目中的历史委托角色，不主张其等同于现代Comune di Gubbio或某一已确认的法定机构。（S1–S2）

**English:** The civic community of Gubbio is recorded here as the collective commissioner of the *Pala del Voto*. A catalogued 1606 letter records a local magistrate asking Gentile Carbonana to approach Federico Barocci about the votive painting; a 2015 basilica guide says the community commissioned Salvio Savini's painting for its vowed altar. This record denotes that historical commissioning role and does not identify it with the present-day Comune di Gubbio or a specifically named legal body. (S1–S2)
