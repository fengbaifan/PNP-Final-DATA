## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第4章所称的乌莫里斯蒂学院。哈斯克尔称它为罗马最著名的学院，并记述该组织一度衰落、后由Massimi重组。此处保留原书的历史叙述，不延伸为对其组织沿革的独立核验。

**English:** This unit records the Umoristi academy as named in chapter 4. Haskell calls it the most famous of Roman academies and reports that it had fallen into decay before Massimi reorganised it. This preserves the book's historical account and does not independently verify the academy's institutional history.
