## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 佛罗伦萨的历史美术馆监管机构，本书列其为图版38a、38b、39和40a的供片者。

**English:** A historical galleries supervisory institution in Florence, credited as the photographic supplier for plates 38a, 38b, 39 and 40a in the book.
