## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔来源中以 City of Padua 或 municipal authorities 表述的帕多瓦市政主体。第9章注释称一篇致 Antonio Loredan 的演说以该城名义发表；第15章记载市政当局请求 Memmo 协助农业集市，随后城市提出一尊雕像方案，Memmo拒绝了代表自己的提议。此卡只记录原书中的市政行动主体，不推定其法定组织结构。

**English:** This card records the civic actor that Haskell calls the City of Padua or the municipal authorities. A chapter 9 note describes an oration to Antonio Loredan as delivered in the city’s name. Chapter 15 says the municipal authorities asked Memmo to assist with the agricultural fair, after which the city proposed a statue and Memmo declined the proposal to represent him. This card records the civic actor in Haskell’s account without inferring its legal organization.

### 名称与性质

- **原文称谓：** City of Padua；municipal authorities；Città di Padova。证据：第9章注释行379；第15章 pp.365–366。
- **机构性质：** 来源语境中的市政行动主体；本项目工作名用于与帕多瓦地点实体区分。

### 身份边界

本卡表示市政主体，不表示帕多瓦城市地点。雕像的具体作品身份及 proposal 的受事关系仍未解决。
