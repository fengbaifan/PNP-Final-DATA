## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 剑桥的摄影商号，本书以Turners of Cambridge署名提供图版3a、17c和18a。

**English:** A photographic firm in Cambridge, credited as Turners of Cambridge for plates 3a, 17c and 18a in the book.
