## 内容

### 描述
**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell书内以“Biblioteca Comunale, Bologna”标示的图书馆机构。第8章p.203将其列为手稿B.109的所在机构；第8章p.225、第14章p.358、第19章p.392及第21章书目也出现博洛尼亚的同一书内名称形式。此身份分组限于书内地点与名称线索，不映射到未经核验的当代机构名称，也不宣称目前保管状态。

**English:** This card records the library institution named in Haskell as “Biblioteca Comunale, Bologna.” Chapter 8 p.203 gives it as the location of manuscript B.109; Chapter 8 p.225, Chapter 14 p.358, Chapter 19 p.392, and the Chapter 21 repository list also use the Bologna library form. The identity grouping is limited to the name and locality in the book; it is not mapped to an unchecked present-day institution name and makes no claim about current custody.
