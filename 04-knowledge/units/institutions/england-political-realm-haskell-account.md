## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录《赞助人与画家》中作为政治实体提及的英格兰。第10章将英格兰写作贵族统治及艺术家赞助活动所涉及的政治共同体；本卡只表达哈斯克尔的书内范围，不提供英格兰的一般政治史。

**English:** This card records England as a political entity in *Patrons and Painters*. Chapter 10 places England within an account of aristocratic rule and artistic patronage. The card is limited to Haskell’s usage in the book and does not provide a general political history of England.
