## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell书内称作“court of Turin”的雇主机构。第9章称Sebastiano Ricci比威尼斯贵族更常受该宫廷雇用；第10章称Ricci在威尼斯以外主要受都灵宫廷雇用。身份限定于书内机构称名与雇主语境；来源未说明统治者、具体办公室、正式机构全称、聘期或委托数量。本卡不与都灵城市或萨伏依家族合并。

**English:** This card records the employer institution called the “court of Turin” in Haskell’s account. Chapter 9 says Sebastiano Ricci was employed by this court more than by the Venetian aristocracy; Chapter 10 says the court was his principal employer outside Venice. The identity is limited to the book’s institutional name and employer context; the source does not specify a ruler, office, formal institutional name, dates of employment, or number of commissions. This card remains distinct from the city of Turin and the House of Savoy.
