## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡按 S3 的 `institution` 裁决，记录 Haskell 第9章 p.268 所称在威尼斯承担艺术赞助角色的“the Church”。作者将其描述为当代艺术的领先赞助者、祭坛画需求的来源，并将教会装饰与贵族理想相联系。这里是书内叙述中的集体性机构主体；来源段没有界定其法律组织边界、具体分支或跨时期连续性。本卡不把它等同于威尼斯共和国、教宗职位、教廷国家或任何一座教堂建筑，也不以这些段落确立外部机构身份。[第9章 p.268，L9](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md#L9)；[第9章 p.268，L10](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md#L10)；[第9章 p.268，L11](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md#L11)

**English:** Following the S3 `institution` decision, this card records “the Church” as Haskell uses it on p. 268 of Chapter 9 for the institutional patron of art in Venice. The author describes it as a leading patron of contemporary art, a source of demand for altarpieces, and an institution whose church decoration reflected patrician ideals. The passage treats it as a collective institutional actor but does not define its legal organization, specific branches, or continuity across periods. This card does not equate it with the Venetian Republic, the papal office, the Papal States, or any individual church building, and the passage does not establish an external institutional identity. [第9章 p.268，L9](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md#L9); [第9章 p.268，L10](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md#L10); [第9章 p.268，L11](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md#L11)

### 书内角色

- 艺术赞助：Haskell称教会保有或增强其在威尼斯当代艺术中的领先赞助地位。[第9章 p.268，L9](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md#L9)
- 委托与市场语境：Haskell将持续的祭坛画需求与现代绘画市场相联系；未据此推定具体委托或画家。[第9章 p.268，L10](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md#L10)
- 装饰与社会理想：Haskell将威尼斯教堂装饰与贵族理想并置，并称许多贵族直接负责委托、在宫殿和教堂雇用偏爱的画家。[第9章 p.268，L11](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md#L11)；[第9章 p.268，L13](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md#L13)
- Haskell关于历史画家为教会创作比例的说法是作者的概括与估计，本卡不将其转写为已独立核验的统计。[第9章 p.268，L9](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md#L9)
