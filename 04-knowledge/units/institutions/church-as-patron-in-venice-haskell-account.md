**中文：** 本卡按 S3 `same` 复用，记录 Haskell 全书相关段落中的机构性“the Church”。第9章 p.268 将教会写作威尼斯当代艺术的领先赞助者，第10章再次称其为传统赞助来源；第4章和第8章分别提及林采学院与教会的冲突语境、Ottavio及Ruffo进入教会。第1至3章还涉及教会领土、遗产与教阶等语境。本卡只统一本书中这些机构性指称，不主张外部法律身份、组织边界或跨时期连续性；Church的领土、政治实体、教宗职位、城市、宗教修会及具体教堂建筑仍须分别识别。[第4章 p.99，L233](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md#L233)；[第8章 p.211，L86–87](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i.md#L86)；[第8章 p.221，L116](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md#L116)；[第9章 p.268，L9–13](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md#L9)；[第10章 p.302，L396](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md#L396)

**English:** This card reuses the source-local institutional referent “the Church” across Haskell’s relevant passages. Chapter 9, p. 268 calls the Church Venice’s leading patron of contemporary art; Chapter 10 again names it as a traditional source of patronage. Chapter 4 describes trouble between the Lincei and the Church, and Chapter 8 says that Ottavio and Ruffo entered the Church. Chapters 1–3 also refer to Church territory, inheritance and hierarchy. The card aligns these institutional references within the book only; it does not establish an external legal identity, organizational boundary or continuity across periods. The Church’s territory, political entities, papal office, cities, religious orders and individual church buildings remain distinct entities where separately identified. [Chapter 4, p. 99, L233](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md#L233); [Chapter 8, p. 211, L86–87](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i.md#L86); [Chapter 8, p. 221, L116](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md#L116); [Chapter 9, p. 268, L9–13](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md#L9); [Chapter 10, p. 302, L396](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md#L396)

### 书内角色

- 艺术赞助：Haskell称教会保有或增强其在威尼斯当代艺术中的领先赞助地位。[第9章 p.268，L9](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md#L9)
- 委托与市场语境：Haskell将持续的祭坛画需求与现代绘画市场相联系；未据此推定具体委托或画家。[第9章 p.268，L10](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md#L10)
- 装饰与社会理想：Haskell将威尼斯教堂装饰与贵族理想并置，并称许多贵族直接负责委托、在宫殿和教堂雇用偏爱的画家。[第9章 p.268，L11](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md#L11)；[第9章 p.268，L13](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md#L13)
- Haskell关于历史画家为教会创作比例的说法是作者的概括与估计，本卡不将其转写为已独立核验的统计。[第9章 p.268，L9](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md#L9)
