## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称威尼斯方面早在1724年计划设立绘画与雕塑学院，以吸引经威尼斯领土前往佛罗伦萨、博洛尼亚和罗马的外国旅行者；他将该学院的实际成立时间记为1756年。书中进一步讨论学院的科目等级、奖项活动及其社会语境。

**English:** Haskell says a plan was made in 1724 to found an Academy of Painting and Sculpture in Venice to attract foreign travellers passing through Venetian territory toward Florence, Bologna, and Rome; he dates the Academy’s establishment to 1756. The book also discusses its hierarchy of subjects, prize-giving sessions, and social context.

### 本书语境

第10章 p.329 称 Senate 希望该安排带来商业收益，并记述历史画在科目等级中居首、风景画等被置于较低位置。p.330 记 Andrea Memmo 曾询问威尼斯学院的徽记、名称与守护圣人；第15章称 Pittoni 为 Accademia 主席。后记附录6刊出 Memmo 的相关笔记，并保留 OCR 原文形式。

Chapter 10 p. 329 says the Senate hoped the arrangement would yield commercial benefit and describes history painting as dominant in the Academy’s subject hierarchy, with view painting ranked lower. Page 330 records Andrea Memmo’s questions about the Venetian Academy’s device, name, and patron saint; Chapter 15 calls Pittoni president of the Accademia. Appendix 6 reproduces related notes by Memmo, preserving the source OCR form.

### 类型记录

中文：原语索引形式：Accademia di Pittura e Scultura；书中英文称 Academy of Painting and Sculpture、Venetian Academy。Haskell 所述的1724年计划与1756年成立属于同一机构叙述。

English: Indexed Italian form: Accademia di Pittura e Scultura; Haskell also calls it the Academy of Painting and Sculpture and the Venetian Academy. The 1724 plan and 1756 establishment are presented as phases in the same institutional history.

### 身份边界

中文：此卡对应原书所称威尼斯绘画与雕塑学院／Venetian Academy；不与同段作为比较对象的帕尔马学院、巴黎学院或其他城市的学院合并。Memmo 对徽记、名称与守护圣人的问题是其笔记内容，不表示学院已实施相应改革。

English: This card represents the Venetian Academy of Painting and Sculpture named in the book. It is distinct from the Parma, Paris, and other city academies mentioned for comparison. Memmo’s questions about the emblem, name, and patron saint are recorded as notes, not as evidence that reforms were implemented.
