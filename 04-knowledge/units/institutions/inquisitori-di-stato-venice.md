## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 威尼斯国家机关 Inquisitori di Stato。哈斯克尔第10章称 Carlo Lodoli 曾因使用国家文件而与该机构发生冲突；第15章脚注又以 Inquisitori di Stato 指称 Busta 540 的档案层级。威尼斯国家档案总局的官方指南也列有同名机构及其档案。本卡只登记该机构身份，不据这些名称推定其完整职权、具体办案行为或未查阅档案的内容。

**English:** The Venetian state body known as the Inquisitori di Stato. Haskell’s chapter 10 says Carlo Lodoli had a brush with the body over his use of state documents; a chapter 15 note also names Inquisitori di Stato in a citation to the Busta 540 archival series. The official guide to the Archivio di Stato di Venezia lists the body and its records under this name. This card records the institution’s identity only; it does not infer its full jurisdiction, specific proceedings, or the contents of archival records not consulted here.

### 名称

- **原语名：** Inquisitori di Stato。证据：第10章 p.320、第15章 p.364注1及威尼斯国家档案官方指南。
- **英文描述名：** Inquisitori di Stato of Venice。本项目显示名，对应上述原语名及威尼斯语境。

### 基本信息

- **机构性质：** 威尼斯国家机关；此处不细分具体权限或组织结构。证据：第10章 p.320及威尼斯国家档案官方指南。
- **档案指称：** 第15章注1将 Busta 540、Annotazioni 列在 Archivio di Stato, Venice—Inquisitori di Stato 名下。证据：第15章 p.364注1；此项为书中档案引用，不表示已查阅该档案。

### 本章相关内容

第10章称 Lodoli 坚持以国家文件作语法与文学研究材料，因此“had a brush with” Inquisitori di Stato。此处不将表述扩张为某项正式调查、裁决或持续关系。第15章注1则在 Farsetti 的叙述附近引用 Busta 540 中一条1792年记录；该引用与第10章的机构名称相互印证，但不能证明第10章所述冲突与该条记录有关。

Chapter 10 says Lodoli “had a brush with” the Inquisitori di Stato because he used state documents for grammatical and literary investigation. This wording is not expanded into a formal inquiry, ruling, or ongoing relationship. A note in chapter 15 cites a 1792 item in Busta 540 near the account of Farsetti; it supports the same institutional name but does not connect that record to Lodoli’s episode.

第15章 p.368–369 将 Querini 与 State Inquisition 的权力争议相连，称他希望削减该机构的权力；哈斯克尔把其改革解释为统治阶层内部权力平衡变化，并未说明具体法律机制。第16章 p.376 称 Toninotto 因出售不当版画而“失宠于”该机构，未记正式处分；p.377 又称 Inquisitors of State 收取了 Swajer 全部“secret” manuscripts。后一组稿件的身份和粒度仍待裁决，本卡不据此推定档案内容或正式处置程序。

Chapter 15 (pp. 368–69) links Querini to a dispute over the State Inquisition’s powers and says he wished to reduce them. Haskell characterizes the proposed reform as a shift in the balance of power within the ruling class, without specifying a legal mechanism. Chapter 16 (p. 376) says Toninotto was “in disgrace with” the institution for selling improper prints but records no formal proceeding; p. 377 says the Inquisitors of State acquired all of Swajer’s “secret” manuscripts. The manuscripts’ identity and granularity remain unresolved; this card does not infer their contents or a formal proceeding.

### 身份边界

此卡仅代表 Inquisitori di Stato 机构。它与 Archivio di Stato di Venezia（保存机构）及 Busta 540 / Annotazioni（档案层级与记录）分开；也不与候选 cand-9851 Inquisitori alle Arti 合并。档案指南用于身份与名称核对，不代替对具体档案原件的查阅。

This card represents the Inquisitori di Stato as an institution. It is distinct from the Archivio di Stato di Venezia as repository and from Busta 540 / Annotazioni as archival records; it is also distinct from candidate cand-9851, Inquisitori alle Arti. The archival guide supports identity and naming, not consultation of the underlying records.
