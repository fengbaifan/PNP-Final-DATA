## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 卡比托利欧博物馆是罗马市立博物馆。保管《算命者》第一版和《施洗者约翰与公羊》。

**English:** The Capitoline Museums are civic museums in Rome and hold the first version of The Fortune Teller and John the Baptist with a Ram.
