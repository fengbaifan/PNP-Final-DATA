### 本书语境

**中文：** 本卡指文学与科学学会，不是既有Albrizziana印刷所 units/institutions/albrizziana-printing-house-venice。它也不同于Pisani家族为Almorò设立、由Pietro Longhi主持的私人绘画学院，以及佛罗伦萨的Accademia del Disegno。候选 st-chp13-p334-almoro-organized-academy 留待S6。

**English:** This card refers to the literary and scientific society, not the existing Albrizziana printing house, units/institutions/albrizziana-printing-house-venice. It is also distinct from the private drawing academy established for the Pisani family’s Almorò under Pietro Longhi and from Florence’s Accademia del Disegno. Candidate st-chp13-p334-almoro-organized-academy remains for S6.
