## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 多利亚·潘菲利美术馆是罗马艺术博物馆。机构与所在宫殿、潘菲利家族所有权分开。

**English:** The Galleria Doria Pamphilj is an art museum in Rome; the institution is distinguished from its palace building and from Pamphilj family ownership.
