## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称本蒂沃利奥家族来自 Ferrara，并记述其家族长期显赫以及后来为 Taddeo Barberini 提供建议。本卡不把家族整体行为归给 Enzo、Guido 或 Ippolito 中的任何一人。

**English:** Haskell says the Bentivoglio came from Ferrara and had long been one of its most distinguished families; he also describes the family as advising Taddeo Barberini. This card does not assign the family-level account to Enzo, Guido, or Ippolito individually.

### 名称

中文名为本蒂沃利奥家族；英文名为 Bentivoglio family。原书中的家族名称为 Bentivoglio。

### 本书语境

第2章以家族层级描述本蒂沃利奥的 Ferrara 出身、历史地位及其与 Barberini 宫廷的联系。

Chapter 2 describes the Bentivoglio at family level: their Ferrara origin, standing, and connection with the Barberini court.

### 身份边界

中文：不与分别识别的 Enzo、Guido、Ippolito 人物候选合并。

English: Do not merge with the separately identified people Enzo, Guido, or Ippolito Bentivoglio.
