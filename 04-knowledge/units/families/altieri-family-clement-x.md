## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称克勉十世的家族为 Altieri，并说其最紧迫的顾虑之一是在教皇去世前完成新家族宫邸。此卡保留家族集体指称，不把家族等同于宫邸或 Don Gasparo Altieri。

**English:** Haskell identifies Clement X's family as the Altieri and says one of its pressing concerns was completing a new family palace before the pope died. This card preserves the collective family reference and keeps the family distinct from the palace and Don Gasparo Altieri.

Chapter 6 (p. 161) places the family's concern about completing its palace within the account of Clement X's advanced age and the ongoing building work.

### 身份边界

中文：只登记本书明确称为 Altieri 的家族集体；不据姓氏或宫邸活动推定全部成员、支系或个人关系。

English: Register only the collective family Haskell calls the Altieri; do not infer all members, branches, or individual relationships from the surname or palace activity.
