### 描述

**中文：** 奥德斯卡尔基家族是意大利贵族家族。与《圣保罗归化》第一版的历史收藏及现存保管相联。

**English:** The Odescalchi family is an Italian noble family linked to the historical ownership and present custody of the first version of The Conversion of Saint Paul.
