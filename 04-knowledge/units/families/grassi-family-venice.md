## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Grassi 家族曾在宫殿或乡间宅邸设置家族神化题材装饰，并将其描述为1699年付费进入贵族阶层的新贵家族；他还称该家族在18世纪建立了贵族家族中少见的绘画收藏。

**English:** Haskell says the Grassi had family-apotheosis decoration in a palace or country house and describes them as a newly ennobled family that bought entry into the aristocracy in 1699; he also says they built a rare eighteenth-century picture collection among patrician families.

### 名称

中文显示名为格拉西家族；英文名及原书所用姓氏形式为 Grassi。中文显示名为便于阅读所用的姓氏音译，不表示书中另有中文规范名。

The Chinese display name is a transliteration of the surname for readability. The English name and surname form used in the book are Grassi family.

### 本书语境

第9章第254—255页将 Grassi 列为家族神化装饰的赞助者，并称这类历史题材超出 Widmann 或 Grassi 所能负担的范围。

Chapter 9 (pp. 254–255) includes the Grassi among patrons of family-apotheosis decoration and says historical subjects of this kind were beyond the means of a Widmann or a Grassi.

### 身份边界

中文：不把集体收藏或赞助陈述归给某一位 Grassi 成员，也不补写家族谱系。

English: The collective collection and patronage statements are not assigned to an individual Grassi member, and no genealogy is supplied.
