## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Pommersfelden 的 Schönborn 家族委托多地意大利画家创作，并将该家族列为资助 Lateran 圣殿艺术的德意志赞助者。第10章后记段落回指其既述赞助，并称该主教区曾属 Schönborn 家族。OCR 拼写包括 `Schônborn` 和不带变音符号的 `Schonborn`。

**English:** Haskell says the Schönborn family of Pommersfelden commissioned Italian painters in several cities and lists the family among German patrons who paid for art at the Lateran basilica. A later Chapter 10 passage refers back to the family's previously discussed patronage and says the bishopric had belonged to the Schönborn family. OCR forms include `Schônborn` and `Schonborn` without the umlaut.

### 名称

中文显示名为舍恩博恩家族；英文名及原书所用名称为 Schönborn family。中文显示名为便于阅读所用的音译，不表示书中另有中文规范名。

The Chinese display name is a transliteration for readability. The English name and form used in the book are Schönborn family.

### 本书语境

第6章第163页与第7章第194页记载家族为 Lateran 圣殿及 Pommersfelden 委托艺术；第10章第293、296页回顾其赞助与主教区归属。

Chapter 6 (p. 163) and Chapter 7 (p. 194) describe the family's Lateran and Pommersfelden patronage; Chapter 10 (pp. 293, 296) revisits that patronage and the bishopric's association with the family.
