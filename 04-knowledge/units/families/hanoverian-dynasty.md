## 内容

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Schulenburg 与 Hanoverian 王朝有密切亲属关系，但没有指明具体亲属或谱系。

**English:** Haskell says Schulenburg was closely related to the Hanoverian dynasty but identifies no particular relative or line of descent.

The Chinese display name is a transliteration for readability. The English name and form used in the book are Hanoverian dynasty.
