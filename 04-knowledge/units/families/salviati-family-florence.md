## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔将 Salviati 与 Corsini 并列为佛罗伦萨 Cerquozzi 的热心欣赏者。原书此处没有点名具体家族成员，也没有把欣赏者表述为委托人。

**English:** Haskell names the Salviati alongside the Corsini as keen admirers of Cerquozzi in Florence. The passage names no individual family member and does not describe the admirers as commissioners.

### 名称

中文显示名为萨尔维亚蒂家族；英文名及原书所用家族名称为 Salviati family。中文显示名为便于阅读所用的音译，不表示书中另有中文规范名。

The Chinese display name is a transliteration for readability. The English name and family form used in the book are Salviati family.

### 本书语境

第5章第142页讨论 Cerquozzi 在佛罗伦萨的声望及其作品的欣赏者。

Chapter 5 (p. 142) discusses Cerquozzi's reputation in Florence and those who admired his work.

### 身份边界

中文：不从家族集体欣赏者身份推断赞助、委托或成员关系。

English: The collective reference as admirers does not establish patronage, commissions, or individual membership relations.
