## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Filippo Farsetti 于1703年出生于一个极其富裕的家族，该家族到1664年才取得威尼斯贵族身份。本文候选索引页码为362，而对应的章节来源文件页眉标作302；卡片依实际引用的原文行及页眉定位，不把页码差异当作身份依据。

**English:** Haskell says Filippo Farsetti was born in 1703 into an exceedingly rich family that acquired Venetian patrician status only in 1664. The candidate index gives p. 362, while the linked chapter source marks the passage as p. 302; the card cites the source text and page marker without using the page discrepancy as identity evidence.

The Chinese display name is a transliteration for readability. The English name and family form used in the book are Farsetti family.

Chapter 15 (p. 302), while introducing Filippo's artistic patronage, also states the wealth and ennoblement date of his birth family.
