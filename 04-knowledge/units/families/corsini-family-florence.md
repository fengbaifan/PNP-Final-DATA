## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔将 Corsini 与 Salviati 并列为佛罗伦萨 Cerquozzi 的热心欣赏者。另一处提到 Carmine 的 Corsini 礼拜堂，并仅称 del Rosso 兄弟可能促成 Luca Giordano 获得该委托；该段没有指出委托家族中的具体人物。

**English:** Haskell names the Corsini alongside the Salviati as keen admirers of Cerquozzi in Florence. Another passage mentions the Corsini chapel at the Carmine and says only that the del Rosso brothers may have helped secure Luca Giordano's commission; it does not identify a specific Corsini family member as the commissioner.

### 名称

中文显示名为科西尼家族；英文名及原书所用家族名称为 Corsini family。中文显示名为便于阅读所用的音译，不表示书中另有中文规范名。

The Chinese display name is a transliteration for readability. The English name and family form used in the book are Corsini family.

### 本书语境

第5章第142页将 Corsini 列为 Cerquozzi 的欣赏者；第8章第212页提及 Corsini 礼拜堂及 del Rosso 兄弟可能参与委托的说法。

Chapter 5 (p. 142) names the Corsini as admirers of Cerquozzi; Chapter 8 (p. 212) mentions the Corsini chapel and the possibility that the del Rosso brothers helped secure the commission.

### 身份边界

中文：保留原书的家族指称；不将礼拜堂名称当作独立家族成员，也不把可能的中介行为改写为已确认关系。

English: This preserves the book's family reference; the chapel name is not treated as a family member, and the possible intermediary role is not rewritten as a confirmed relation.
