## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Raimondo Buonaccorsi 出生于一个古老且重要、在半岛各地有广泛支系的家族；又称家族成员多次分散其藏画，且没有清单留存。本卡只登记书中明确称作 Buonaccorsi 的家族集体，不宣称完整谱系、成员名录或对全部财产的归属。

**English:** Haskell says Raimondo Buonaccorsi was born into an old and important family with branches across the peninsula. He also says pictures were repeatedly split among family members and no inventory survived. This card registers only the collective family named Buonaccorsi in the book; it does not claim a complete genealogy, membership list, or ownership of all property.

### 本书语境

第8章 p.223 以家族背景介绍 Raimondo；p.225 讨论其藏画散入家族成员手中及清单缺失。原文关于 Corrado Giaquinto 为家族工作的说法带有 reported 转述限定。

Chapter 8 (p. 223) places Raimondo in the family context; p. 225 discusses pictures dispersed among family members and the absence of an inventory. Haskell qualifies the report that Corrado Giaquinto worked for the family as reported rather than established here.
