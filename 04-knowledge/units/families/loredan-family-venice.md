## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Antonio Loredan 是 Loredan 家族的另一成员，并记其离开帕多瓦总督任期时被演说者比作 Pompey。该卡仅登记家族集体，不延伸为完整家族成员表。

**English:** Haskell calls Antonio Loredan another member of the Loredan family and says an orator compared him to Pompey as he left the governorship of Padua. This card registers only the family collective, not a complete membership list.

The bilingual display names appear in the card title. The Chinese name is a transliteration or descriptive rendering for readability; the book's wording is preserved in the source quotations.

### 本书语境

第9章印刷页256将 Antonio 的离任演说与威尼斯贵族公共荣誉叙述相连。

Chapter 9 (p. 256) places Antonio's departure oration within the account of public honors for Venetian nobles.

### 身份边界

中文：不将 Antonio 的政治任职或演说比较转写为其他 Loredan 家族成员的事实。

English: Do not transfer Antonio's office or the orator's comparison to other members of the Loredan family.
