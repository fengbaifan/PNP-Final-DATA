## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Marco Foscarini 出身威尼斯最古老的家族之一；第9章 p.259 另引述一位1749年在家族宫殿工作的建筑画家对家族财富的描述。后者是 Haskell 保存的嵌套转述，不作为独立核实的财产或收入数据。

**English:** Haskell says Marco Foscarini descended from one of Venice's oldest families. Chapter 9 (p. 259) also quotes an architectural painter working at the family palace in 1749 on the family's wealth. That passage is Haskell's nested report, not independently verified property or income data.
