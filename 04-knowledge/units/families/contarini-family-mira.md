## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Tiepolo 曾以 Contarini 家族1574年迎接法国国王 Henri III 访问 Mira 别墅的场景，表现该家族的辉煌时刻。此卡只登记书中所指家族集体。

**English:** Haskell says Tiepolo represented the Contarini family's 1574 welcome to Henri III of France at their villa in Mira as a moment of the family's greatest glory. This card registers only the collective family named in the book.
