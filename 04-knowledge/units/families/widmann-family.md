## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Widmann 家族源自 Carinthia，在威尼斯 S. Canciano 附近拥有宫殿，并装饰其乡间宅邸；他还将该家族列入拥有家族神化题材装饰的家族。第8章注释另记 Widmann 持有那不勒斯画家的作品。

**English:** Haskell says the Widmann originated in Carinthia, had a palace near S. Canciano in Venice, and decorated a country house; he also lists the family among those with apotheosis decoration. A Chapter 8 note separately records Widmann ownership of works by Neapolitan painters.

### 名称

中文显示名为维德曼家族；英文名及原书所用姓氏形式为 Widmann。中文显示名为便于阅读所用的姓氏音译，不表示书中另有中文规范名。

The Chinese display name is a transliteration of the surname for readability. The English name and surname form used in the book are Widmann family.

### 本书语境

第9章第250页叙述其来源地、宫殿及乡间宅邸；第254页提及家族神化装饰；第8章第214页注释将其列为作品持有人。

Chapter 9 (p. 250) describes the family's origin, palace, and country house; page 254 mentions its apotheosis decoration; a Chapter 8 note on p. 214 lists the family as a picture owner.

### 身份边界

中文：不由居所或作品持有事实推断完整产权链、家族成员或支系。

English: The residence and picture-ownership references do not establish a complete title chain, membership list, or branch structure.
