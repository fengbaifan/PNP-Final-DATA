## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔在亚历山大七世亲属的叙述中将马里奥及其子侄置于基吉家族语境，并称家族势力日益增长；在克勉十世时期的比较中，他又以“a Chigi”说明奢侈规模。此条保留集体家族身份，不将比较语句归给某个成员。

**English:** In his account of Alexander VII’s relatives, Haskell places Mario and his nephews in the Chigi family context and describes the family as growing in wealth and influence; in the Clement X passage he uses “a Chigi” as a comparison for the scale of luxury. This record preserves the collective family identity and does not assign the comparison to an individual member.

### 名称

中文名为基吉家族；英文名为 Chigi family。原书使用的姓氏形式为 Chigi。

### 本书语境

cand-6329 的第6章第154页来源与 cand-6484 的第162页跨页比较共同指向基吉家族；两者没有给出不同支系。

The chapter-six source for cand-6329 on page 154 and the continuation for cand-6484 on page 162 both refer to the Chigi family; neither distinguishes a separate branch.
