## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔将 Labia 描述为具有加泰罗尼亚渊源、财富丰厚的威尼斯商人家族，记载其兴建大型宫殿并以显赫装饰展示财富；第8章注释也将 Labia 列入拥有那不勒斯画家作品的家族。

**English:** Haskell describes the Labia as wealthy Venetian businessmen of Catalan origin who built a large palace and displayed their wealth through its decoration. A Chapter 8 note also lists the Labia among families who owned works by Neapolitan painters.

### 名称

中文显示名为拉比亚家族；英文名及原书所用姓氏形式为 Labia。中文显示名为便于阅读所用的姓氏音译，不表示书中另有中文规范名。

The Chinese display name is a transliteration of the surname for readability. The English name and surname form used in the book are Labia family.

### 本书语境

第9章第250页记载 Labia 的宫殿建设、装饰与绘画收藏；第257页称该家族因奢华宴会而闻名。第8章第214页注释将 Labia 与其他威尼斯藏家并列。

Chapter 9 (p. 250) describes the Labia palace, its decoration, and picture holdings; page 257 says the family became legendary for lavish banquets. A Chapter 8 note on p. 214 lists the Labia with other Venetian picture owners.

### 身份边界

中文：书中另提家族成员 Maria Labia；本卡不据此扩展成员清单，也不将个人珠宝或身份自动归为家族财产。

English: The book separately names the family member Maria Labia; this card does not expand a membership list or automatically treat her personal jewels or status as family property.
