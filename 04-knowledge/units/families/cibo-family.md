## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称枢机 Tommaso Ruffo 晚年在罗马居住，并从 Cibo 家族购得 Trevi 喷泉对面的宫殿。书中未指出具体出售者或家族支系。

**English:** Haskell says Cardinal Tommaso Ruffo lived in Rome in old age and bought the palace opposite the Trevi Fountain from the Cibo family. The book does not identify an individual seller or family branch.

### 名称

中文显示名为奇博家族；英文名及原书所用家族名称为 Cibo family。中文显示名为便于阅读所用的音译，不表示书中另有中文规范名。

The Chinese display name is a transliteration for readability. The English name and family form used in the book are Cibo family.

### 本书语境

第8章第221页将 Cibo 家族列为 Ruffo 购置罗马宫殿时的前持有方。

Chapter 8 (p. 221) names the Cibo family as the former holder from whom Ruffo acquired a Roman palace.

### 身份边界

中文：不从宫殿转手推断卖方个人、确切支系或完整产权链。

English: The palace transfer does not establish an individual seller, exact branch, or complete chain of title.
