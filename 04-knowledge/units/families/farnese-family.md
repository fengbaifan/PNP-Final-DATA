## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Farnese 为其家族保留耶稣教堂祭坛区及主祭坛的装饰权。本卡只记录家族层级的保留安排，与 Cardinal Alessandro Farnese 个人分开。

**English:** Haskell says Farnese reserved decoration of the Gesù tribune and High Altar for his family. This record captures the family-level reservation and remains distinct from Cardinal Alessandro Farnese as an individual.

### 名称

中文名为法尔内塞家族；英文名为 Farnese family。原书中的家族名称为 Farnese。

### 本书语境

第3章称 Farnese 家族保留了耶稣教堂祭坛区和主祭坛的装饰权。

Chapter 3 says the Farnese reserved the Gesù tribune and High Altar decoration for their family.

### 身份边界

中文：不把家族身份与 Cardinal Alessandro Farnese 个人身份混为一项。

English: Keep the family identity separate from Cardinal Alessandro Farnese as a person.
