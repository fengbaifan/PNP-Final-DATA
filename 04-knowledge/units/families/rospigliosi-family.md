## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Rospigliosi 家族于1668年负责组织罗马圣若望斩首堂的年度展览，并以陈设的华丽程度超过此前主办者。文中接着说明教宗之弟 Camillo Rospigliosi 与其四子当时成为管理该堂的慈善团体的新成员。

**English:** Haskell says the Rospigliosi were responsible for the 1668 annual exhibition at San Giovanni Decollato and surpassed earlier organizers in the lavishness of the display. He adds that Camillo Rospigliosi, the Pope's brother, and his four sons had just become novices of the confraternity that administered the church.

### 名称

中文显示名为罗丝皮廖西家族；英文名及原书所用家族名称为 Rospigliosi family。中文显示名为便于阅读所用的音译，不表示书中另有中文规范名。

The Chinese display name is a transliteration for readability. The English name and family form used in the book are Rospigliosi family.

### 本书语境

第5章第127页以 Rospigliosi 家族的展览组织和教会活动为例，讨论罗马家族对年度展览的支持。

Chapter 5 (p. 127) uses the Rospigliosi exhibition and church activity as an example of Roman family involvement in the annual event.

### 身份边界

中文：原文没有列出 Camillo 四子的姓名；本卡不据此补写家族成员清单，也不新增展览或慈善团体关系。

English: The source does not name Camillo's four sons; this card does not add a membership list or create exhibition or confraternity relations.
