## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Johann Matthias Schulenburg 曾为 House of Savoy 作战。原书此处指王朝／家族，不指地理对象 Savoy；两类对象分别保留。

**English:** Haskell says Johann Matthias Schulenburg fought for the House of Savoy. The passage refers to the ruling house, not the geographic place Savoy; the two entity types remain separate.

### 名称

中文显示名为萨伏依王室；英文名及原书所用名称为 House of Savoy。中文显示名为便于阅读所用的音译，不表示书中另有中文规范名。

The Chinese display name is a transliteration for readability. The English name and form used in the book are House of Savoy.

第10章第311页在叙述 Schulenburg 的军事经历时列出 House of Savoy。来源 OCR 页面标记异常读作 `1661`，候选过程定位为第311页；本卡保留该定位差异供后续原件核对。

Chapter 10 (p. 311) names the House of Savoy in its account of Schulenburg's military service. The OCR page marker reads `1661`, while the candidate process locates the passage at p. 311; this discrepancy is retained for later source-image review.

### 身份边界

中文：不把 House of Savoy 与地理候选 `cand-2379` Savoy 合并。

English: Do not merge the House of Savoy with the geographic candidate `cand-2379` for Savoy.
