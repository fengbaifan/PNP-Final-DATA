## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称，1794年 Pietro Novelli 记录了 Collalto 家族的伟大；他还称 Novelli 在回忆录中说明，记录该家族伟大是他自己选择的题材。

**English:** Haskell says that Pietro Novelli recorded the greatness of the Collalto family in 1794, and reports Novelli's own memoirs as saying that he chose the subject himself.

### 名称

中文显示名为科拉尔托家族；英文名及原书所用家族名称为 Collalto family。中文显示名为便于阅读所用的音译，不表示书中另有中文规范名。

The Chinese display name is a transliteration for readability. The English name and family form used in the book are Collalto family.

### 本书语境

第9章第254—255页将 Collalto 家族置于威尼斯家族史题材及画家选择题材的讨论中。

Chapter 9 (pp. 254–255) places the Collalto family in a discussion of Venetian family-history subjects and painters' choices of subject.

### 身份边界

中文：不据家族题材扩展成员名单、谱系或具体委托关系。

English: The family-history subject does not establish a membership list, genealogy, or specific commission relation.
