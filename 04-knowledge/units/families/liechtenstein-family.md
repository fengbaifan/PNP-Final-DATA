## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称，哈布斯堡领地内新教势力失败后发生的没收使列支敦士登家族大为富裕，并称该家族仅向博洛尼亚画家 Marcantonio Franceschini 订购了42幅画。

**English:** Haskell says confiscations following Protestant defeats in the Habsburg dominions greatly enriched the Liechtenstein family, which ordered forty-two paintings from the Bolognese painter Marcantonio Franceschini alone.

### 名称

中文显示名为列支敦士登家族；英文名及原书所用家族名称为 Liechtenstein family。中文显示名为便于阅读所用的音译，不表示书中另有中文规范名。

The Chinese display name is a transliteration for readability. The English name and family form used in the book are Liechtenstein family.

### 本书语境

第7章第194页将列支敦士登家族置于德意志诸侯委托意大利艺术家的赞助语境。

Chapter 7 (p. 194) places the Liechtenstein family in the context of German princely patronage of Italian artists.

### 身份边界

中文：保留 Haskell 对家族财富来源及委托数量的叙述，不据此展开支系、成员或所有权史。

English: This preserves Haskell's account of the family's wealth and commission count without expanding its branches, membership, or property history.
