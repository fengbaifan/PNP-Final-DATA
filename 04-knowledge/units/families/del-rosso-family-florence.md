## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔将德尔·罗索描述为富有的佛罗伦萨商人家族，列出 Andrea、Lorenzo、Ottavio 及兄弟 Nicola；另称 1706 年佛罗伦萨展览中的那不勒斯作品主要由 del Rosso 兄弟借出。此条将两处集体指称合为同一书内家族身份，不推定各幅作品由哪位兄弟出借。

**English:** Haskell describes the Del Rosso as a wealthy Florentine merchant family and names Andrea, Lorenzo, Ottavio, and their brother Nicola; he later says the del Rosso brothers lent most of the Neapolitan contingent at the 1706 Florentine exhibition. These collective references are grouped as one family identity in the book, without assigning individual loans to a particular brother.

### 名称

中文名为佛罗伦萨德尔·罗索家族；英文名为 Del Rosso family of Florence。原书使用的姓氏形式为 Del Rosso family of Florence。

### 本书语境

第8章第211页列出 Del Rosso 家族及兄弟，第241页在佛罗伦萨展览语境中再次称 del Rosso brothers；书中没有提示另一个同名家族或不同支系。

Chapter 8, page 211, identifies the Del Rosso family and brothers; page 241 again refers to the del Rosso brothers in the context of a Florentine exhibition. The book does not indicate a separate family or branch.
