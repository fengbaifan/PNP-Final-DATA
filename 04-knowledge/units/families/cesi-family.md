## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Pier Donato Cesi 及其家族取得新教堂会务中的优先权，并说 Angelo Cesi 后来延续家族赞助。本卡保持家族与两位个人的层级区分。

**English:** Haskell says Pier Donato Cesi and his family received overriding privileges in the congregation’s affairs, and that Angelo Cesi later continued the family patronage. This card keeps the family distinct from both people.

### 名称

中文名为切西家族；英文名为 Cesi family。原书中的家族名称为 Cesi。

### 本书语境

第3章将家族特权和赞助延续分别关联于 Pier Donato 与 Angelo Cesi。

Chapter 3 links the family’s privileges and continued patronage with Pier Donato and Angelo Cesi respectively.

### 身份边界

中文：不与 Cardinal Pier Donato Cesi 或 Angelo Cesi 人物候选合并。

English: Do not merge with the person candidates Cardinal Pier Donato Cesi or Angelo Cesi.
