## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 威尼斯皮萨尼家族的贵族支系；本章来源记载该支系于1787年购得Maffeo Pinelli收藏的一组168幅肖像。购买者没有具体具名成员。

**English:** A patrician branch of the Venetian Pisani family. The chapter’s source records that this branch acquired a group of 168 portraits from Maffeo Pinelli’s collection in 1787; no individual buyer is named.
