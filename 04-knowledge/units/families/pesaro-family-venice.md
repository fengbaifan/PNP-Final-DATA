## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔以 Pesaro 为例，称威尼斯旧家族拥有政治特权，并将其与以财富进入贵族阶层的新商人群体相对照。

**English:** Haskell cites the Pesaro as an old Venetian family with political privileges, contrasting them with new mercantile groups entering the aristocracy through wealth.
