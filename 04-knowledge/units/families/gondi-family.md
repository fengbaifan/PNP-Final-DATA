## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称贡迪家族源自佛罗伦萨，约在其叙述所涉时期前50年迁居巴黎；Marie de Medici 抵达巴黎后曾短暂居于贡迪家族的宫殿。

**English:** Haskell describes the Gondi as a Florentine family that had settled in Paris about fifty years earlier; Marie de Medici stayed briefly in the family's palace after arriving in Paris.

### 名称

中文显示名为贡迪家族；英文名及原书所用家族名称为 Gondi family。中文显示名为便于阅读所用的音译，不表示书中另有中文规范名。

The Chinese display name is a transliteration for readability. The English name and family form used in the book are Gondi family.

### 本书语境

第7章第174页将贡迪宫殿、家族的佛罗伦萨渊源及其定居巴黎的记载联系起来。

Chapter 7 (p. 174) connects the Gondi palace with the family's Florentine origin and settlement in Paris.
