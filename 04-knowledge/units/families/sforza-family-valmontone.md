## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔在 Barberini 宫殿叙述中称斯福尔扎家族曾持有该宫殿，后来被迫出售瓦尔蒙托内财产。本卡与个人 Alessandro Sforza 及宫殿地点候选分别处理。

**English:** In his account of the Barberini palace, Haskell says the Sforza family held the palace and later had to sell property at Valmontone. This family record remains distinct from Alessandro Sforza as a person and from the palace as a place.

### 名称

中文名为斯福尔扎家族（瓦尔蒙托内语境）；英文名为 Sforza family (Valmontone account)。原书中的家族名称为 Sforza family (Valmontone account)。

### 本书语境

第2章把宫殿原有持有者表述为 Sforza 家族，并另提其瓦尔蒙托内财产。

Chapter 2 identifies the former palace holders as the Sforza family and separately mentions their Valmontone property.

### 身份边界

中文：不把家族指称并入个人 Alessandro Sforza 或地点候选。

English: Do not merge the family reference with the person Alessandro Sforza or the place candidate.
