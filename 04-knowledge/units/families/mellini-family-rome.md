## 内容

### 描述

**English:** Haskell lists this among Rome’s older aristocratic families. The OCR reads Meliini, while the S3 candidate note records Mellini as the print-scan reading; no branch is inferred.

The family list appears in Chapter 3’s discussion of the Gesù side chapels.
