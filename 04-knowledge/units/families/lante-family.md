## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔在宫殿转手叙述中将兰特家族列为本蒂沃利奥之后、Mazarin 之前的持有方。本卡不把后续取得者 Mazarin 并入家族。

**English:** In the palace transfer account, Haskell places the Lante family between the Bentivoglio and Cardinal Mazarin as successive holders. This record does not merge Mazarin into the family.

### 名称

中文名为兰特家族；英文名为 Lante family。原书中的家族名称为 Lante。

### 本书语境

第2章脚注指出本蒂沃利奥将宫殿售予兰特家族，Mazarin 后来从兰特家族取得。

Chapter 2’s note says the Bentivoglio sold the palace to the Lante, from whom Mazarin later acquired it.

### 身份边界

中文：与后续个人取得者 Cardinal Mazarin 区分。

English: Keep distinct from the later individual acquirer, Cardinal Mazarin.
