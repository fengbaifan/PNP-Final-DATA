## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Soderini 家族曾为其家族历史绘制提埃波罗的装饰画，并指出其具有悠久的佛罗伦萨渊源。原书此处 OCR 作 `Sodcrini`；候选索引记录作 `Soderini`，本卡保留这一文字差异，不把 OCR 读法称作扫描校勘结果。

**English:** Haskell says the Soderini commissioned Tiepolo to depict their family history and notes their long Florentine ancestry. The OCR reads `Sodcrini` at this passage, while the candidate index records `Soderini`; this card preserves the textual difference without presenting the OCR reading as a scan-based correction.

### 名称

中文显示名为索德里尼家族；英文名及原书所用姓氏形式为 Soderini。中文显示名为便于阅读所用的姓氏音译，不表示书中另有中文规范名。

The Chinese display name is a transliteration of the surname for readability. The English name and surname form used in the book are Soderini family.

### 本书语境

第9章第254页将 Soderini 列为曾绘制家族神化题材的家族；第255页称其于1656年获封贵族，并拥有悠久而知名的佛罗伦萨祖先渊源。

Chapter 9 (p. 254) includes the Soderini among families with apotheosis paintings; page 255 says they were ennobled in 1656 and had a long and renowned Florentine ancestry.

### 身份边界

中文：名称采用候选索引中的 Soderini 形式；不据单处 OCR 文字差异推定支系或谱系。

English: The name follows the candidate index form Soderini; the single OCR spelling difference is not used to infer a branch or genealogy.
