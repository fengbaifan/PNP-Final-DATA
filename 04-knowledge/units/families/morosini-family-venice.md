## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Morosini 是威尼斯十二个最古老家族之一，并说 Zenobio 购得的 Carmini 宫殿此前属于 Morosini。此处按书中集体家族称谓登记。

**English:** Haskell identifies the Morosini as one of Venice's twelve oldest families and says the Carmini palace bought by the Zenobio had previously belonged to the Morosini. The card registers the collective family named in the book.
