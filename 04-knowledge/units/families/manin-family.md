## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称来自 Friuli 的 Manin 家族持续装饰威尼斯及周边的宫殿、乡间宅邸和教堂；他还记载该家族参与 Udine 主教座堂与威尼斯教堂的装饰工程。

**English:** Haskell says the Manin from Friuli repeatedly decorated palaces, country houses, and churches in and around Venice; he also records the family's involvement in decoration projects at Udine Cathedral and Venetian churches.

### 名称

中文显示名为马宁家族；英文名及原书所用姓氏形式为 Manin。中文显示名为便于阅读所用的姓氏音译，不表示书中另有中文规范名。

The Chinese display name is a transliteration of the surname for readability. The English name and surname form used in the book are Manin family.

### 本书语境

第9章第251页概述 Manin 家族的装饰赞助；第268页与第271页进一步记载其教堂工程及其作为耶稣会教堂主要资源来源之一。

Chapter 9 (p. 251) outlines the Manin family's decorative patronage; pages 268 and 271 further describe its church projects and its role as a major source of resources for a Jesuit church.

### 身份边界

中文：本卡保留原书的 Manin 家族集体指称。相似拼写的 Manini brothers 候选 `cand-8780` 仍未决，不据姓名相近并入本卡。

English: This card preserves the book's collective reference to the Manin family. The similarly spelled Manini brothers candidate `cand-8780` remains undecided and is not merged on the basis of name resemblance.
