## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第8章注4将“the Giovanelli”列为拥有 Giordano 或 Solimena 画作的威尼斯群体之一；第9章又称该家族于1668年获授贵族身份，并购买 Tiepolo、Piazzetta、Canaletto、Zuccarelli 及古代大师的作品。本卡保留书中的家族集体称谓和两处不同语境。

**English:** Chapter 8, note 4, lists 'the Giovanelli' among Venetian groups that owned pictures by either Giordano or Solimena. Chapter 9 says the family was ennobled in 1668 and bought works by Tiepolo, Piazzetta, Canaletto, Zuccarelli, and old masters. This card preserves the book's family-collective reference across the two contexts.

### 本书语境

第8章印刷页214注4与第9章 p.262 分别提供画作所有者群体及购买作品的家族比较；后者明确使用 family 称谓。

Chapter 8 (p. 214 n. 4) names an owner group, while Chapter 9 (p. 262) compares the family's purchases and explicitly calls it a family.

### 身份边界

中文：p.214 的群体性所有权表述不指明具体画作或个人；不把家族与另一条尚未定型的 Giovanelli collection 候选视为同一对象，也不据此确认收藏的完整范围。关系候选留待 S6。

English: The group-level ownership statement on p. 214 identifies no particular picture or individual. Keep the family distinct from the separate, still-untyped Giovanelli collection candidate and do not infer the collection's full extent. Relation candidates remain for S6.
