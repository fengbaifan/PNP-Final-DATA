## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Giovanni Battista Crescenzi 出身罗马贵族家族，其兄为枢机；两人都是艺术赞助者与收藏者，并较早委托 Claude 创作宫殿壁画。该段未说明其兄的姓名。

**English:** Haskell says Giovanni Battista Crescenzi came from a noble Roman family and that his brother was a cardinal. The two were patrons and collectors who were among the early employers of Claude for frescoes in their palace; the passage does not name the brother.

### 名称

中文显示名为克雷申齐家族；英文名及原书所用家族名称为 Crescenzi family。中文显示名为便于阅读所用的音译，不表示书中另有中文规范名。

The Chinese display name is a transliteration for readability. The English name and family form used in the book are Crescenzi family.

### 本书语境

第7章第173页以 Giovanni Battista Crescenzi 及其兄弟的艺术赞助活动说明西班牙宫廷与意大利艺术家的联系。

Chapter 7 (p. 173) uses Giovanni Battista Crescenzi and his brother's patronage to discuss links between the Spanish court and Italian artists.

### 身份边界

中文：不补写兄长姓名、家族支系或未经该段直接支持的成员关系。

English: No name for the brother, family branch, or membership relation beyond what this passage directly supports is added.
