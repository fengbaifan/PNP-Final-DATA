## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔将 St Ignatius 礼拜堂所在地及原有赞助权归于萨韦利家族，并称 Prince Giulio 以家族首领身份放弃该权利。本卡不确定精确产权名称或家族结构。

**English:** Haskell says the St Ignatius chapel site officially still belonged to the Savelli and that Prince Giulio, as family head, gave up the family patronage. This card does not resolve the exact property title or family structure.

### 名称

中文名为萨韦利家族；英文名为 Savelli family。原书中的家族名称为 Savelli。

### 本书语境

第3章将场地、礼拜堂赞助权与 Prince Giulio 的家族首领身份相连。

Chapter 3 links the site, chapel patronage, and Prince Giulio’s role as family head.

### 身份边界

中文：精确产权名称及完整家族结构仍未核定。

English: The exact property title and full family structure remain unresolved.
