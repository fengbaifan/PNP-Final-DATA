## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Bernardino 于1632年从米尼亚内利家族购得宫殿。本卡只记录家族作为书中集体前持有方，不补写具体出售者或完整产权史。

**English:** Haskell says Bernardino acquired a sixteenth-century palace from the Mignanelli in 1632. This card records the family as the collective former holders and does not supply an individual seller or full title history.

### 名称

中文名为米尼亚内利家族；英文名为 Mignanelli family。原书中的家族名称为 Mignanelli。

### 本书语境

第3章以 Mignanelli 家族为宫殿先前持有方，并另述 Bernardino 在宫中集画。

Chapter 3 names the Mignanelli as prior holders of the palace and separately describes Bernardino’s picture collection there.

### 身份边界

中文：不据此推定具体出售者或完整产权链。

English: Do not infer an individual seller or a complete chain of title.
