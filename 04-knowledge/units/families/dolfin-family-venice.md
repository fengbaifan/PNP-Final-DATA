### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Dolfin 是“老式”的显赫家族，并将 Tiepolo 为其绘制的十幅罗马史场景称作该家族在1720年代的赞助活动。本卡登记书中所指的家族集体，不把家族活动直接分配给某一位成员。

**English:** Haskell describes the Dolfin as a prominent family of the old style and places Tiepolo's ten Roman-history scenes for them among the family's patronage in the 1720s. This card registers the family collective named in the book without assigning the family's activity to a particular member.
