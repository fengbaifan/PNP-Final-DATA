## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 潘菲利家族（亦作Pamphilj）是意大利贵族家族，在17世纪罗马政治与艺术赞助中活动。本章将该家族明确列为圣阿涅塞教堂穹顶委托的责任方。

**English:** The Pamphili family, also spelled Pamphilj, was an Italian noble family active in seventeenth-century Roman politics and artistic patronage. Chapter 1 explicitly identifies the family as responsible for the commission of the Sant’Agnese dome.
