## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔第9章称 Tiepolo 为维琴察的 Valmarana 家族绘制一组壁画；第20章后记转述 Puppi 的研究，明确更正此前的委托归属，称委托者是 Conte Giustino Valmarana，而非 Leonardo Valmarana。

**English:** In Chapter 9, Haskell says Tiepolo painted a fresco cycle for the Valmarana family in Vicenza. The Chapter 20 postscript reports Puppi's research and explicitly corrects the earlier commission attribution: the commissioner was Conte Giustino Valmarana, not Leonardo Valmarana.

The bilingual display names appear in the card title. The Chinese name is a transliteration or descriptive rendering for readability; the book's wording is preserved in the source quotations.

### 本书语境

第9章 p.258 将壁画与家族相连，并谨慎称 Leonardo 似在1757年为家族首领；第20章 p.405 回指同一组作品并将委托归于 Giustino。

Chapter 9 (p. 258) connects the frescoes with the family and cautiously says Leonardo seems to have headed it in 1757; Chapter 20 (p. 405) refers back to the same works and attributes the commission to Giustino.

### 身份边界

中文：家族身份、Leonardo 的来源限定头衔与 Giustino 的委托角色分开。p.405 是 Haskell 转述 Puppi；该文章未独立查阅。

English: Keep the family identity, the qualified report of Leonardo's family role, and Giustino's commissioning role distinct. The p. 405 account is Haskell reporting Puppi; the article was not independently consulted.
