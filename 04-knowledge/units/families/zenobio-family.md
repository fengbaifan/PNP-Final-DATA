## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Zenobio 是威尼斯富裕家族之一，并记述 Carmini 宫殿的改建及其对美术的保护；第9章注释还提到 Carlevarijs 曾受雇于 Zenobio。后记再次回顾该家族的艺术赞助。

**English:** Haskell describes the Zenobio as a wealthy Venetian family, records the rebuilding of its palace at the Carmini and its protection of the arts, and notes that Carlevarijs worked for the family. The postscript revisits its artistic patronage.

### 名称

中文显示名为泽诺比奥家族；英文名及原书所用姓氏形式为 Zenobio。中文显示名为便于阅读所用的姓氏音译，不表示书中另有中文规范名。

The Chinese display name is a transliteration of the surname for readability. The English name and surname form used in the book are Zenobio family.

### 本书语境

第9章第250页记载 Zenobio 宫殿及其艺术赞助；第260页注8提到 Carlevarijs 受雇于该家族；后记第405页再次讨论 Zenobio 的赞助。

Chapter 9 (p. 250) describes the Zenobio palace and artistic patronage; note 8 on p. 260 mentions Carlevarijs's employment by the family; the postscript on p. 405 revisits Zenobio patronage.

### 身份边界

中文：名称与描述限于书中的集体家族指称，不补写成员、支系或家族财产清单。

English: The name and description are limited to the book's collective family reference; no members, branches, or family-property inventory are added.
