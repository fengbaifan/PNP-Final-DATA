## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔将 Prefect of Rome 一职的历史归属追溯到德拉·罗韦雷家族，并称其家族在灭绝前拥有该职位。本卡不延伸为完整家谱。

**English:** Haskell traces the former Prefect of Rome office to the Della Rovere family and says the family held it until its extinction. This card does not supply a full genealogy.

### 名称

中文名为德拉·罗韦雷家族；英文名为 Della Rovere family。原书中的家族名称为 Della Rovere。

### 本书语境

第2章将该家族与罗马 Prefect 职位及乌尔比诺公爵身份相联系。

Chapter 2 connects the family with the Prefect of Rome office and the dukes of Urbino.

### 身份边界

中文：家族与个别 Della Rovere 统治者保持区分。

English: Keep the family distinct from individual Della Rovere rulers.
