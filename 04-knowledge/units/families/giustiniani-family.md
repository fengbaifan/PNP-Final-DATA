## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Giustiniani 家族曾在其宫殿或乡间宅邸中设置家族神化题材的装饰画。该卡只登记原书明确提及的集体家族及其装饰语境。

**English:** Haskell says the Giustiniani had family-apotheosis paintings in their palaces or country houses. This card records only the collective family and decorative context explicitly named in the book.

### 名称

中文显示名为朱斯蒂尼亚尼家族；英文名及原书所用姓氏形式为 Giustiniani。中文显示名为便于阅读所用的姓氏音译，不表示书中另有中文规范名。

The Chinese display name is a transliteration of the surname for readability. The English name and surname form used in the book are Giustiniani family.

### 本书语境

第9章第254页将 Giustiniani 与 Grassi、Widmann、Soderini 并列，称这些家族都曾在宫殿或乡间宅邸绘制家族神化题材。

On page 254, Chapter 9 names the Giustiniani alongside the Grassi, Widmann, and Soderini as families with apotheoses painted in their palaces or country houses.

### 身份边界

中文：不推断具体成员、支系、谱系或相关装饰的具体画家。

English: No individual members, branches, genealogy, or specific painter for these decorations is inferred.
