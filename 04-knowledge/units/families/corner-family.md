## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔将 Corner 列为声称拥有罗马祖先的威尼斯家族之一，并称他们曾委托提埃波罗和拉扎里尼创作家族历史题材作品。这里保留原书的集体家族指称，不把未具名家族成员识别为具体人物。

**English:** Haskell lists the Corner among Venetian families claiming Roman ancestry and says they commissioned Tiepolo and Lazzarini to paint family-history subjects. This record preserves the book's collective family reference and does not identify an unnamed member as a specific person.

### 名称

中文显示名为科尔纳家族；英文名及原书所用姓氏形式为 Corner。中文显示名为便于阅读所用的姓氏音译，不表示书中另有中文规范名。

The Chinese display name is a transliteration of the surname for readability. The English name and surname form used in the book are Corner family.

### 本书语境

第9章第252页称提埃波罗在1722年前曾为 Corner 工作；第255页称 Corner 于1700年委托拉扎里尼绘制一位曾在康布雷同盟战争时期建立功绩的家族成员。

Chapter 9 says Tiepolo had worked for the Corner before 1722 (p. 252), and that in 1700 the Corner commissioned Lazzarini to depict a family member distinguished during the War of the League of Cambrai (p. 255).

### 身份边界

中文：不据此确定 Corner 的完整谱系，也不把家族委托指派给某一位未具名成员。

English: This account does not establish a complete Corner genealogy or assign the family commission to an unnamed individual member.
