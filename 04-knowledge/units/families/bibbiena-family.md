## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称，Ferdinand 在 Pratolino 的剧院曾雇用领先的舞台设计师，其中包括 Bibbiena 家族成员。该段没有点名具体设计师。

**English:** Haskell says Ferdinand employed leading stage designers for his theatre at Pratolino, including members of the Bibbiena family. The passage does not name a specific designer.

### 名称

中文显示名为比比耶纳家族；英文名及原书所用家族名称为 Bibbiena family。中文显示名为便于阅读所用的音译，不表示书中另有中文规范名。

The Chinese display name is a transliteration for readability. The English name and family form used in the book are Bibbiena family.

### 本书语境

第8章第230页将 Bibbiena 家族成员置于 Ferdinand 剧院舞台设计工作的语境。

Chapter 8 (p. 230) places members of the Bibbiena family in the context of stage-design work for Ferdinand's theatre.

### 身份边界

中文：不根据家族集体称谓推定具体成员身份或设计项目清单。

English: The collective family reference does not establish individual identities or a list of design projects.
