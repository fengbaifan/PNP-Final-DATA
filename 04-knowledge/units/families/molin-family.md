## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称，Teodoro Correr 的许多绘画来自包括 Molin 在内的威尼斯显贵商人家族。原文未指定具体家族成员或逐件作品来源。

**English:** Haskell says many of Teodoro Correr's pictures came from prominent patrician and trading families, including the Molin. The passage identifies neither individual family members nor the source of each work.

### 名称

中文显示名为莫林家族；英文名及原书所用家族名称为 Molin family。中文显示名为便于阅读所用的音译，不表示书中另有中文规范名。

The Chinese display name is a transliteration for readability. The English name and family form used in the book are Molin family.

### 本书语境

第17章第382页将 Molin 与 Orsetti、Pellegrini 并列为 Correr 收藏的来源家族。

Chapter 17 (p. 382) names the Molin alongside the Orsetti and Pellegrini among sources of Correr's collection.

### 身份边界

中文：“来自该家族”只按原文宽泛来源语句保留，不改写为赠与、借展或确定的所有权链。

English: The source's broad statement that pictures came from the family is preserved without recasting it as a gift, loan, or established chain of ownership.
