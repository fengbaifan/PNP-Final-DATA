## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell第8章点名的Giovanni da Campione。第215页称他于1355年建造Santa Maria Maggiore的哥特式门廊，数年后又建造南侧较为简朴的入口；后一个时间仍只是相对表述。索引以“Campione, Giovanni da”列出该人物并指向同页。中文名为本项目工作音译。 [第8章 p.215；OCR L13](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md) [索引 C.csv#18](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/C.csv)

**English:** This card records Giovanni da Campione named in Chapter 8. Page 215 says that he erected the Gothic portico of Santa Maria Maggiore in 1355 and built a more restrained south entrance a few years later; the latter date remains relative. The index lists “Campione, Giovanni da” at the same page. The Chinese name is a working transliteration. [第8章 p.215；OCR L13](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md) [索引 C.csv#18](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/C.csv)

### 本书相关内容

**中文：** 门廊与南入口是两项不同建造陈述。Haskell只称南入口建于门廊之后数年，未给出确切年份；南入口建造关系已记录，门廊建造关系仍待S6裁决。 [第8章 p.215；OCR L13](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md) [索引 C.csv#18](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/C.csv)

**English:** The portico and south entrance are separate construction claims. Haskell dates the south entrance only to a few years after the portico, with no exact year; its builder relation is recorded, while the portico relation remains for S6 review. [Chapter 8, p.215, L13](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md) [Index C.csv#18](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/C.csv)
