### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell第8章点名的Giovanni da Campione。第215页称他于1355年建造Santa Maria Maggiore的哥特式门廊，数年后又建造南侧较为简朴的入口；后一个时间仍只是相对表述。索引以“Campione, Giovanni da”列出该人物并指向同页。中文名为本项目工作音译。 [第8章 p.215；OCR L13](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md) [索引 C.csv#18](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/C.csv)

**English:** This card records Giovanni da Campione named in Chapter 8. Page 215 says that he erected the Gothic portico of Santa Maria Maggiore in 1355 and built a more restrained south entrance a few years later; the latter date remains relative. The index lists “Campione, Giovanni da” at the same page. The Chinese name is a working transliteration.
