## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记书后索引“Piccino, Jacopo”所指人物。Postscript第408页转述Parker的判断，称Maggiotto的狗总椭圆肖像可能改编自Matina卷中的Piccino版画；原文保留probably的限定。本卡记录该书内称名，不把可能的版画归属写成确定事实。中文显示名为项目工作音译。

**English:** This card records the person indexed as “Piccino, Jacopo”. On page 408 of the Postscript, Haskell reports Parker's view that Maggiotto's oval Doge portraits were probably adapted from engravings by Piccino in Matina's volume; the source retains the qualification “probably”. This card records the source-local name and does not turn the possible print attribution into a certainty. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration.
