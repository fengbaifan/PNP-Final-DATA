### 本书相关语境

索引主项及两个子项分别指向第9、10、13、14章的不同页段。第10章p.291称Boucher为《Tombeaux des Princes》英雄铭牌的装饰设计者，并记其为Newton纪念铭牌绘制奖章图案；第10章p.293将其列入Tessin收藏语境。第13章p.335以其艺术作比较，第14章pp.351、354记载其被纳入画廊题材计划，并以其风格讨论Tiepolo画作。以上具体创作、委托、收藏及合作关系均留在原S2 statements，待S6逐条审查。[全书索引 B.csv#268–270](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/B.csv) [第9章 p.257](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)（L177） [第10章 p.291](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L253–266、L551） [第10章 p.293](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L279–290） [第13章 p.335](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md)（L35–37） [第14章 p.351](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/14_CHP-14_intro.md)（L50–51） [第14章 p.354](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/14_CHP-14_intro.md)（L77–78）

The index main entry and its two subentries point to different passages in Chapters 9, 10, 13, and 14. Chapter 10, p. 291, names Boucher as the designer of decorative friezes for the hero plates in *Tombeaux des Princes* and says he drew the medallion for Newton’s monument; p. 293 places him in the context of Tessin’s collection. Chapter 13, p. 335, uses his art as a comparison, and Chapter 14, pp. 351 and 354, discusses his inclusion in a gallery plan and uses his style to describe a Tiepolo painting. These specific creation, commission, collection, and collaboration claims remain in the original S2 statements for item-by-item S6 review.
