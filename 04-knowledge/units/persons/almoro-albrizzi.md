## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡指索引所列、并在第13章 p.334 被称为 Albrizzi 之兄的 Almorò Albrizzi。Treccani《意大利人传记辞典》以 Almorò（Ermolao）Albrizzi 为条目名，并记载他创办 Accademia Albrizziana。该身份与第9章所述 Pisani 家族之子 Almorò 不同；不因同名而合并。

**English:** This card identifies the Almorò indexed under Albrizzi and described on p.334 as Albrizzi’s brother. Treccani’s *Dizionario Biografico degli Italiani* gives the entry name Almorò (Ermolao) Albrizzi and records his founding of the Accademia Albrizziana. He is distinct from the Pisani family’s son Almorò discussed in Chapter 9; the shared given name does not imply identity.

**English:** Chapter 13 says Almorò organised the Accademia Albrizziana, described as a society concerned with literary and scientific matters. Treccani records the same-named academy as founded in 1724 and governed by Almorò until 1744.
