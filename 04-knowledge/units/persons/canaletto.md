## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 乔瓦尼·安东尼奥·卡纳尔，惯用名卡纳莱托，是画家，在《蒂洛特森大主教寓意纪念墓》中负责建筑绘制。

**English:** Canaletto is the name by which Giovanni Antonio Canal is commonly known. He painted the architecture in the Allegorical Tomb of Archbishop Tillotson.
