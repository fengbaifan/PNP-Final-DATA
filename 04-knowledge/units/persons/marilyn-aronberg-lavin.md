**English:** A chapter 2 footnote says the Barberini inventories were published by Marilyn Aronberg Lavin; the chapter 20 postscript refers to a later publication by Marilyn Lavin of inventories the author had not found while working in Rome. The chapter 2 OCR reads “Lavili”; the existing S2 candidate note records “Lavin” in print. This entry preserves the OCR and print-reading difference.
