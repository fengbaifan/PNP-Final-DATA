## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 书中称 Bellotto 为 Canaletto 之侄：他于1747年抵达德累斯顿，不到一年获任宫廷画家，任至七年战争后该宫廷解体。1765年他返回德累斯顿，短暂停留期间绘制十字教堂废墟，随后离开。此后他原拟前往圣彼得堡，途中受波兰新王 Stanislas Poniatowski 劝留而转往华沙；书中称他为该王绘制大量城市景观，持续十二年直至1780年去世。以上仅记录原书叙述，未作外部身份核验；中文名为工作音译。

**English:** Haskell calls Bellotto a nephew of Canaletto. He arrived in Dresden in 1747, was appointed court painter within a year, and held the post until that court broke up after the Seven Years War. He returned to Dresden in 1765, stayed briefly to paint the ruins of the Church of the Cross, and then left. He later planned to travel to St Petersburg but was persuaded to stop in Warsaw by the new King Stanislas Poniatowski; Haskell says Bellotto produced many views for the king for twelve years until his death in 1780. This card records the book's account only; the Chinese display name is a working transliteration.
