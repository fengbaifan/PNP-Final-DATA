## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡将哈斯克尔第5章的“Cardinal Azzolini”对应到Decio Azzolino Junior（Treccani作Decio Azzolini）。权威传记与2024年研究支持其红衣主教身份、1623–1689年生卒及其与1669年Natio Picena迁址S. Salvatore in Lauro的关联。此身份比对不裁定他是教堂的法律买受人、代理人或出资者；购置关系仍待S6。〔[第5章 p.128](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_i.md)（L91–93）〕

**English:** This card maps Haskell’s Chapter 5 form “Cardinal Azzolini” to Decio Azzolino Junior (spelled Decio Azzolini by Treccani). The biographical sources support his cardinalate, dates (1623–1689), and connection with the 1669 relocation of the Natio Picena to S. Salvatore in Lauro. This identity match does not determine whether he was the church’s legal purchaser, agent, or financier; the purchase-related S6 candidate remains pending. [第5章 p.128](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_i.md)（L91–93）
