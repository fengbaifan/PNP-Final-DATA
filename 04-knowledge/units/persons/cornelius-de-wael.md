### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记书后索引“Wael, Cornelius de”及第5章 p.122 全名所指的人物。Haskell称他于1657年离开 Genoa，积极从事商业活动，并拥有大量显然待售的画作；他主要与同乡画家及客户往来。作者还说他拥有 Gaulli 的早期作品、其他意大利风俗画家、花卉画家和战争画家所作的小幅画，并以“must have known in Genoa”推断他与 Gaulli 的相识。相关数量、收藏内容和作者对其在重要艺术家生活中作用较小的评价均保留为书内说法；不据此补充生平或逐件作品目录。[第5章 p.122](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_i.md)（L18–19） [书后索引](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-1-Index-MD/UVWXYZ.md)（UVWXYZ.csv#131）

**English:** This card records the person named in the index as “Wael, Cornelius de” and by full name on Chapter 5, p.122. Haskell says that he left Genoa in 1657, engaged in extensive commercial activity, and had a large gallery of pictures apparently for sale; he dealt mainly with compatriots as artists and clients. The author also says that he owned early works by Gaulli and smaller pictures by Italian genre, flower, and battle painters, and infers that he “must have known” Gaulli in Genoa. The collection claims and Haskell’s assessment that de Wael played little part in the lives of more important artists remain attributed to the book; no biography or itemized work catalogue is added.
