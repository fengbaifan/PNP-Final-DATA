## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第6章称Christina在罗马展示的绘画收藏包括其父亲Gustavus Adolfus从布拉格劫掠的作品；第7章再称瑞典军队劫掠布拉格王室收藏并使其女Christina受益，姓名拼作Gustavus Adolphus。两章的拼写异文与来源各自的责任者表述均保留；本卡只登记人物端点，不独立裁决藏品转移或劫掠责任。 [来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_iv.md#L48)；[来源2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_iv.md#L8)

**English:** Chapter 6 says the pictures Christina displayed in Rome included works her father Gustavus Adolfus had looted from Prague; chapter 7 says Swedish troops looted the royal collections in Prague for the ultimate benefit of his daughter Christina and spells his name Gustavus Adolphus. Both spelling forms and each chapter's attribution of agency are preserved. This entry registers the person endpoint without independently adjudicating the collection transfer or responsibility for the looting.
