## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第6章称 Francesco Marucelli 为佛罗伦萨赞助人和收藏家，记其居于西班牙广场、以藏书为主要兴趣，并将大量藏书记录于一部学术著作；他关注当代艺术，撰写部分画家生平，向 Filippo Baldinucci 提供在罗马工作的艺术家资料，为藏书中伟大作家的作品委托制作多种材质的胸像，并喜爱 Theodor Helmbrecker 的作品；Helmbrecker 为其绘制十六幅画，包括乡村生活场景和带有寓意暗示的作品。Haskell 对其赞助趣味的评价属于作者判断。第8章称 Marucelli 兄弟中最有声望的 Francesco 于1703年在罗马去世。

**English:** Chapter 6 identifies Francesco Marucelli as a Florentine patron and collector who lived in the Piazza di Spagna. It says books were his chief passion and that he recorded his substantial library in a learned work; it also notes his interest in contemporary art, his accounts of some painters' lives, information he sent Filippo Baldinucci about artists working in Rome, busts he commissioned of writers represented in his library, and his delight in Theodor Helmbrecker's work. Helmbrecker painted sixteen pictures for him, including scenes of country life and works with a hint of allegory. Haskell's assessment of Marucelli's patronage and taste is attributed to the author. Chapter 8 says that Francesco, the most distinguished of the Marucelli brothers, died in Rome in 1703.
