## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔第6章称 Olimpia Maidalchini 为教皇英诺森十世的弟媳、卡米洛·潘菲利之母，并记她在卡米洛的家族与宫廷事务中发挥影响。第3章称她约于1650年劝说 Theatines 委托 Mattia Preti 绘制教堂后殿，第4章则记 Camillo Massimi 从 Velasquez 处购得她的肖像。书中对其性格及宫廷影响的评价均保留为作者叙述。

**English:** Chapter 6 identifies Olimpia Maidalchini as Innocent X's sister-in-law and Camillo Pamfili's mother, and describes her influence in Camillo's family and court affairs. Chapter 3 says that around 1650 she persuaded the Theatines to commission Mattia Preti to paint their church apse; Chapter 4 records that Camillo Massimi acquired her portrait from Velasquez. The book's characterisations of her and her court influence remain attributed to Haskell.
