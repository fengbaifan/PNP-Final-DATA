## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应索引词头“Rezzonico, Carlo (Pope Clement XIII)”及第9、15章中的Carlo Rezzonico。第9章p.254称他是Aurelio的兄弟并于1758年当选教皇Clement XIII；第15章p.362给出全名，p.363继续称“the Pope”。Q-R.csv将页码录为254、323，而原索引转录列为254、362–363；差异保留并以原索引转录和正文跨章锚点支持身份组。Clement XIII的see-under索引redirect不另立人物；Clement XI既有KU是不同教皇。

**English:** This card represents the index heading “Rezzonico, Carlo (Pope Clement XIII)” and Carlo Rezzonico in chapters 9 and 15. Chapter 9 p. 254 calls him Aurelio’s brother and says he was elected Pope Clement XIII in 1758; chapter 15 p. 362 gives his full name and p. 363 continues with “the Pope.” Q-R.csv records pp. 254 and 323, while the original index transcription lists pp. 254 and 362–363; the discrepancy is retained, with the transcription and cross-chapter body anchors supporting this identity group. The Clement XIII see-under redirect is not a separate person; the existing Clement XI KU is a different pope.
