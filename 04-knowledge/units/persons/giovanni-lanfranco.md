## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 乔万尼·兰弗兰科（1582–1647）是活跃于罗马与那不勒斯的意大利画家。Haskell 用其早期保护、1640 年求取《教皇利奥与阿提拉》委托、按人物计酬及完成工作室既有画稿的事例，讨论画家的职业机会与交易方式。人物与现代同名排球运动员区分。

**English:** Giovanni Lanfranco (1582–1647) was an Italian painter active in Rome and Naples. Haskell discusses professional opportunities and transactions through his early protection, his 1640 request for the Pope Leo and Attila commission, payment per figure, and the completion of existing studio paintings. This person is distinct from the modern volleyball player of the same name.
