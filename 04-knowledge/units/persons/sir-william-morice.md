## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第10章所称Sir William Morice。第290页称他于1730年购买了已完成的其余画作；书后索引以“Morice, Sir William”指向该页。原文限定购买对象为已完成的部分，本卡不扩展为购买未完成作品。中文显示名为项目工作音译。

**English:** This card records the Sir William Morice named in Chapter 10. Page 290 says that in 1730 he bought the remaining pictures that had been completed; the index lists “Morice, Sir William” at that page. The source limits the purchase to completed pictures, and this card does not extend it to unfinished works. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration.
