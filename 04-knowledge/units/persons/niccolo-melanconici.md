## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称 Niccolò Melanconici 是 Luca Giordano 的学生；他同意自费绘制《Abraham》作为试验。书中称该画获认可后，Melanconici 获得整项贝加莫教堂绘画委托，费用为4,000 ducats（含旅行与食宿），并于1693年6月抵达贝加莫。中文名为项目工作音译。〔[第8章，印刷页220–221；OCR L110、113](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md)〕

**English:** Haskell identifies Niccolò Melanconici as a pupil of Luca Giordano who agreed to paint an Abraham at his own expense as an experiment. After the painting met with approval, the book says Melanconici was given the entire Bergamo church commission for 4,000 ducats, including travel, board, and lodging, and arrived in Bergamo in June 1693. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration. ([第8章，印刷页220–221；OCR L110、113](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md))
