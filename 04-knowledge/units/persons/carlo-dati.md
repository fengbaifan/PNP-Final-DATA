**English:** In discussing Cassiano dal Pozzo’s interest in science, Haskell calls Carlo Dati his Florentine disciple and friend, and refers to Dati’s discussion of Cassiano’s religious views and detestation of alchemy. A chapter-four note also says that a portrait of Cassiano formed the frontispiece to Dati’s panegyric.
