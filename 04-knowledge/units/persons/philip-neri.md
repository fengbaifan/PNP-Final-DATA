## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第3章称 Philip Neri 为奥拉托利会（Oratorians）的创立者，记他对绘画与音乐有深切感受，并亲自关注其教堂的装饰。书中还记述他与 Chiesa Nuova（S. Maria della Vallicella）的建设、礼仪空间及 Federico Barocci 祭坛画的关联；早期传记的神意故事和观看画作时的反应均保留为书中转述。

**English:** Chapter 3 identifies Philip Neri as the founder of the Oratorians, describes his deep responsiveness to painting and music, and says that he took a personal interest in decorating his church. The book also connects him with the building of Chiesa Nuova (S. Maria della Vallicella), its devotional setting, and Federico Barocci's altarpiece; stories attributed to early biographers and reports of his response to the painting remain attributed accounts.
