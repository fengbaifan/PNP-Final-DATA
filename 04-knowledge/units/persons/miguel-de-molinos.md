## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本书称 Miguel de Molinos 为西班牙司铎，记其于1664年抵达罗马，并将其广泛影响与每日领圣体、强调静观等教义主张相联系。第3章还讨论其神学观念与另一位艺术家作品主题的相似性，但明确表示无法判定该观念是否源自 Molinos；本卡保留原文的不确定性，不将其写成确定影响关系。中文名为本项目工作译名。[第3章 pp.81–82](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md)（L54–64） [全书索引 M.csv#220](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/M.csv)

**English:** The book identifies Miguel de Molinos as a Spanish priest who arrived in Rome in 1664, and connects his wide influence with teachings including daily communion and an emphasis on passive contemplation. Chapter 3 also discusses a possible similarity between his theological ideas and the subject of another artist’s work, while explicitly saying that it is impossible to determine whether the idea derived from Molinos. This card preserves that uncertainty and does not state a definite influence relationship. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration.
