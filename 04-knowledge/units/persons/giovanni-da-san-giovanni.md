## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第2章注释转述Baldinucci关于Giovanni da San Giovanni创作《夜之战车》、与两名法国人冲突及Bentivoglio赞助其在罗马发展的叙述；第4章又将他列入Giordano II Orsini周围的艺术家与音乐家。轶事保留为哈斯克尔转述的来源链，不作为独立核实的事实。 [来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_iv.md#L218)；[来源2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_i.md#L13)

**English:** A chapter 2 footnote relays Baldinucci's story that Giovanni da San Giovanni worked on the *Carro della Notte*, fought two Frenchmen, and owed his success in Rome to Bentivoglio's patronage. Chapter 4 also lists him among the artists and musicians around Giordano II Orsini. The anecdote remains attributed to Haskell's reported source chain and is not presented as independently verified. [来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_iv.md#L218)；[来源2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_i.md#L13)
