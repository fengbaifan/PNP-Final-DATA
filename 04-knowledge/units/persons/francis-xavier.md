## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 第3章所称 Francis Xavier。第64页称 Gregory XV 下令为 Xavier、Ignatius 和 Philip Neri 举行册封仪式；第84页称 Xavier 于1623年册封后，耶稣堂右横翼礼拜堂改奉他，并安置 Van Dyck 所绘圣像。第85页注4又将礼拜堂保存的手臂圣髑指认为 St Francis Xavier 的手臂；此处仅记录 Haskell 的注释，不表示本轮独立核验圣髑。[S1][S2][S3]

**English:** This unit records Francis Xavier as presented in Haskell’s Chapter 3. Page 64 says Gregory XV decreed the canonisation of Xavier, Ignatius, and Philip Neri; page 84 says that after Xavier’s canonisation in 1623 the right-transept chapel in the Gesù was dedicated to him and received a picture by Van Dyck. Note 4 on page 85 identifies the arm relic kept in the chapel as St Francis Xavier’s; this records Haskell’s note and does not represent independent verification of the relic. [S1][S2][S3]
