## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell在第16、17章以“G. A. Armanni”称一位画家和经纪人，并记其往返于Bologna与Rome、与G. M. Sasso通信及对Edward Poore的评论。本书所读页段没有展开其完整名字；中文转写为暂译。本卡仅登记这组来源局部身份，不与第1章的Vincenzo Armanni合并，也不据姓名首字母补出全名。

**English:** In Chapters 16 and 17, Haskell names a painter and dealer as “G. A. Armanni,” records travel between Bologna and Rome, correspondence with G. M. Sasso, and a comment about Edward Poore. The consulted passages do not expand the initials. The Chinese rendering is a provisional transliteration. This source-local identity is kept distinct from Vincenzo Armanni in Chapter 1; no full name is inferred.
