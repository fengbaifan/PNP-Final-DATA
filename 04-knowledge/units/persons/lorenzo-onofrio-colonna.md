## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔第6章称 Lorenzo Onofrio 是科隆纳家族最有影响的赞助人，记他于1661年与 Maria Mancini 结婚。他持续扩充家族绘画收藏，对神话寓言与风景尤感兴趣，并委托 Claude 创作风景和八幅神话场景；他也雇用 Giovanni Coli 与 Filippo Gherardi 参与家族宫殿的 Lepanto 大画廊装饰。原书仅推测他与部分画作的题材构思密切相关；本卡保留该限定，不把个人委托与家族整体活动混为一谈。

**English:** Chapter 6 identifies Lorenzo Onofrio as the Colonna family's most influential patron and says he married Maria Mancini in 1661. He continued to expand the family picture collection, preferred mythological fables and landscapes, and commissioned Claude to make landscapes and a series of eight mythological scenes. He also employed Giovanni Coli and Filippo Gherardi on the Lepanto gallery decoration in the family palace. Haskell only suggests that Lorenzo was closely connected with the conception of some subjects; this qualification is retained, and his personal commissions are kept distinct from the family's broader activity.
