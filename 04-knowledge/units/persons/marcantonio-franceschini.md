## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Marcantonio Franceschini 为博洛尼亚画家，记述他是 Cignani 的学生、受 Liechtenstein 家族大量委托，并在 Conti 收藏的委托过程中提供画家意见和题材建议。第19章附录把 Conti 的委托文书与 Franceschini 及 Felice Torelli 联系起来；书信及档案材料仅按本书的刊载和转述范围记录。

**English:** Haskell identifies Marcantonio Franceschini as a Bolognese painter and Cignani's pupil, records extensive commissions from the Liechtenstein family, and describes his advice and subject proposals in the commission process for Conti's collection. The Chapter 19 appendix connects Conti commission documents with Franceschini and Felice Torelli; letters and archival materials are recorded only within the book's presentation and reporting.
