## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔书目所列的 Elpidio Benedetti。第7章 p.183 注3 引其1661年葬礼出版物第5页；第21章书目列其为该书责任者。出版物及所引页未独立查阅，本卡不补写生平或外部身份。

**English:** This card records Elpidio Benedetti as named in Haskell’s bibliography. Chapter 7, p.183 n.3 cites p.5 of his 1661 funeral publication, and Chapter 21 lists him as its responsible author. Neither the publication nor the cited page was independently consulted; no biography or external identity is added.

### 身份边界

身份仅限哈斯克尔书目中可追溯的责任者形式；未做外部身份核验。
