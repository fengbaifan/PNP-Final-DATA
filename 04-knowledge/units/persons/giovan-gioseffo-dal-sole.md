## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 全书索引以 “Sole, Giovan Gioseffo dal” 列出人物。第7章将 Dal Sole 列入欧根亲王收藏中的博洛尼亚画家；第8章在商人收藏、绘画委托及创作语境中多次以 Giovan Gioseffo dal Sole 或 Dal Sole 称呼他；第10章写作 Gian Gioseffo dal Sole，并提到他为 Johann Wilhelm 创作的作品。Treccani《百科全书》以 Giovan Gioseffo dal Sole 立目，记其为博洛尼亚画家（1654–1719）。中文名为项目工作译名。[第7章 p.201](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_v.md)（L49–52） [第8章 p.203](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i.md)（L8–9） [第8章 p.214 注](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i_notes_p214_visual-transcription.md)（L1） [第8章 pp.223、225、227](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md)（L142、164、196） [第10章 p.282](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L147、注3 L522） [全书索引 S.csv#169](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/S.csv) [Treccani](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/dal-sole-giovan-gioseffo/)

**English:** The book’s index lists “Sole, Giovan Gioseffo dal.” Chapter 7 includes Dal Sole among the Bolognese painters represented in Prince Eugene’s collection. Chapter 8 names Giovan Gioseffo dal Sole or Dal Sole in contexts involving merchant collections, commissions, and painting. Chapter 10 uses “Gian Gioseffo dal Sole” and mentions works made for Johann Wilhelm. Treccani’s encyclopedia identifies Giovan Gioseffo dal Sole as a painter from Bologna (1654–1719). The Chinese name is a project working transliteration.
