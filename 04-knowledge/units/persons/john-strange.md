## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 约翰·斯特兰奇（1732–1799）是英国外交官、考古与自然史研究者及艺术收藏者；他在威尼斯任职期间委托瓜尔迪创作了一组别墅景观画。

**English:** John Strange (1732–1799) was a British diplomat, antiquarian and natural-history scholar, and art collector. While resident in Venice, he commissioned a group of villa views from Francesco Guardi.
