### 描述

**中文：** 第6章注释提到Berrettoni在一座宫殿中的作品；第7章称Niccolò Berrettoni是Maratta的学生，德尔·卡尔皮奥常到其画室访问，并曾试图说服他赴马德里供职。OCR省略Niccolò的重音符号，S2记录印本作Niccolò。本卡保留姓名依据及书内职业语境，不把关系候选升级为正式师承边。 [来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_iv.md#L71)；[来源2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md#L273)；[来源3](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md#L286)

**English:** A chapter 6 note mentions work by Berrettoni in a palace. Chapter 7 identifies Niccolò Berrettoni as a pupil of Maratta, says Del Carpio visited his studio regularly, and reports that he tried to persuade him to work for the King in Madrid. The OCR omits the accent in Niccolò; S2 records the print reading as Niccolò. This entry records the name and the contexts in the book without turning the relation candidate into a formal apprenticeship edge.
