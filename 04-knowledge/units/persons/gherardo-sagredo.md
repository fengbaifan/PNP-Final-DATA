## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记书后索引“Sagredo, Gherardo”所指人物。第9章称Gherardo是Zaccaria Sagredo的侄子，并引述遗嘱将其列为受遗赠人；同章称Zaccaria死后Gherardo继承收藏。遗嘱转录作“Girardo P.O Sagredio”，保留该原文异写。底特律艺术博物馆的一件绘画馆藏记录也将Gherardo Sagredo列在Zaccaria之后的收藏沿革中，该记录用于身份比对。中文显示名为项目工作音译。 [索引 S.csv#14](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/S.csv) [书内语境](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)

**English:** This card records the person indexed as “Sagredo, Gherardo.” Chapter 9 calls Gherardo a nephew of Zaccaria Sagredo and quotes Zaccaria's will naming him as an heir; the chapter also describes the collection passing to Gherardo after Zaccaria's death. The will transcription reads “Girardo P.O Sagredio,” which is retained as a source variant. A Detroit Institute of Arts painting record also places Gherardo Sagredo after Zaccaria in the collection provenance and is used for identity comparison. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration. [index S.csv#14](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/S.csv) [book passage](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)
