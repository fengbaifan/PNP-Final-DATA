## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 奥林皮娅·阿尔多布兰迪尼是1623年生于罗马的贵族女性，也是阿尔多布兰迪尼家族的重要继承人。她先后与Paolo Borghese和Camillo Pamphili结婚；第二次婚姻使部分家产、艺术品与档案进入潘菲利收藏体系。（S1–S4）

**English:** Olimpia Aldobrandini was a Roman noblewoman born in 1623 and a principal heiress of the Aldobrandini family. She married first Paolo Borghese and later Camillo Pamphili; the second marriage brought parts of the family property, art and archives into the Pamphili collection system. (S1–S4)
