### 描述

**English:** This card records the Frans van Mieris named in Chapter 10. On page 307, while discussing Lady at the Virginals, Haskell says the painting was then attributed to van Mieris; the index lists “Mieris, Frans van” at that page. This is a record of a historical attribution, not the work's current accepted authorship. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration.
