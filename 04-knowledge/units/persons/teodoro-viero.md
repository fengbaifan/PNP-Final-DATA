**English:** This card represents the index entry “Viero, Teodoro” and references to Teodoro Viero/Viero in chapters 13 and 15. Chapter 13 calls him a printer, records his shop opening in 1754, and mentions the 1779 edition of Pietro Monaco’s print collection as posthumously published by Viero. Chapter 15 note 3 says he and Sasso drew up an inventory of Memmo’s collection. These publication, occupation, and inventory relation candidates remain for S6 review.
