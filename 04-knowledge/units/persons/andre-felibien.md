## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡按书末索引的姓名拼写记录 André Félibien；第4章正文用未标重音符号的 Felibien。哈斯克尔称他为法国史家，并评价他与 Bellori 对 Poussin 的古典主义理论表现出趋于教条的推崇。第115页还提到，Félibien 在1647–1650年间身在罗马时，相关素描属于 Massimi；本卡不将这些素描误记为 Félibien 所有。[S1][S2][S3]

**English:** This unit records André Félibien using the name in the book index; Chapter 4 spells it Felibien without the accent. Haskell calls him a French historian and characterizes his and Bellori’s attention to Poussin’s classical theory as increasingly doctrinal. Page 115 also says that the drawings in question belonged to Massimi while Félibien was in Rome between 1647 and 1650; the drawings are not attributed to Félibien as owner. [S1][S2][S3]
