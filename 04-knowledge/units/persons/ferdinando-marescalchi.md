## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 费迪南多·马雷斯卡尔基是政治人物及收藏家，1754年生于博洛尼亚、1816年卒于摩德纳；其收藏曾包含委拉斯开兹的卡米洛·马西米肖像。

**English:** Ferdinando Marescalchi was a politician and collector, born in Bologna in 1754 and deceased in Modena in 1816; his collection included Velázquez’s portrait of Camillo Massimi.
