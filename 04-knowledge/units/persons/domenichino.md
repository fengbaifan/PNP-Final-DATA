## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 多梅尼科·赞皮耶里（1581–1641），通称多梅尼基诺，是出生于博洛尼亚、在罗马和那不勒斯活动的意大利画家。第一章以其罗马任职和那不勒斯主教座堂计酬案例讨论赞助条件；外部馆方资料补充其卡拉奇体系训练及壁画创作背景。

**English:** Domenico Zampieri (1581–1641), known as Domenichino, was an Italian painter born in Bologna and active in Rome and Naples. Chapter 1 discusses patronage through his appointment in Rome and payment for work in Naples Cathedral. Museum sources add context concerning his training within the Carracci circle and his fresco painting.
