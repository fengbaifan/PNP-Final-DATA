## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡按书末索引记录雕塑家 Andrea Ferrerio。第8章称他是 Giuseppe Mazza 的学生，为 Ruffo 制作的 Vigilance 雕像设于费拉拉大主教宫楼梯栏杆，并设计楼梯墙的灰泥装饰；上述作品与身份均按 Haskell 原文记录，不将 Ruffo 的完整身份或其他宫殿事实补入本卡。[S1][S2]

**English:** This unit records the sculptor Andrea Ferrerio under the name in the book index. Chapter 8 calls him a pupil of Giuseppe Mazza, says his statue of Vigilance commissioned by Ruffo stood on the balustrade of the Archbishop’s Palace in Ferrara, and credits him with designing the staircase wall’s stucco decoration. These statements follow Haskell’s account; no fuller identity for Ruffo or other palace facts are added. [S1][S2]
