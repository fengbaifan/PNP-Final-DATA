## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 安尼巴莱·卡拉奇（1560–1609）是生于博洛尼亚、后在罗马活动的画家、素描家和版画家。第一章将他作为博洛尼亚画家进入罗马并引入新画风的先行人物；他的家族合作、教学与法尔内塞赞助经历为这一论述提供背景。

**English:** Annibale Carracci (1560–1609) was a painter, draftsman and printmaker born in Bologna and later active in Rome. Chapter 1 presents him as a pioneer among Bolognese painters bringing a new manner to Rome; his family collaborations, teaching and Farnese patronage provide context for that account.
