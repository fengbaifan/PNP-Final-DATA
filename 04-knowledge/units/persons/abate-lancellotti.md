## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 塞孔多·兰切洛蒂，原名Vincenzo，是意大利奥利韦坦会修士、历史及文献研究作者。《当今》赞扬当代成就，本章借其著作说明巴贝里尼时期的评价；书中1627年“首刊”说与外部版本资料分开保存。

**English:** Secondo Lancellotti, born Vincenzo, was an Italian Olivetan monk and a writer on history and learning. His L’Hoggidì defended contemporary achievement and is cited in chapter 1 on the Barberini period. The chapter’s claim of first publication in 1627 is retained separately from external edition evidence.
