**English:** A Chapter 2 note calls his account the fullest of the travellers’ accounts discussed, says it must date from 1687–1688, and gives a source-reported copy locator in Stockholm. Page 406 says Bjurström discussed Tessin’s taste in art and drawing collection. The page 406 identity follows the index grouping; the shortened reference was not separately verified.
