## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 法国画家，1704年获任罗马法国学院院长；其1708年书信被哈斯克尔用来讨论罗马的绘画赞助。

**English:** A French painter appointed director of the French Academy in Rome in 1704, whose 1708 letter is cited by Haskell in discussing painting patronage in Rome.
