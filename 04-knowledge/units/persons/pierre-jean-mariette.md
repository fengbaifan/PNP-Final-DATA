The index entry M.csv#85 lists pp. 165, 284n, 285, 331, 341, 343n, and 361. Chapter 6, p. 165, presents a statement about Ricci as hearsay attributed to Mariette, explicitly qualified by “j’ai entendu dire”; Chapter 10, p. 285, reports his criticism of the destruction of the frescoes. Chapter 13, p. 341, describes Zanetti’s contact with Mariette and note 3 refers to Mariette’s account in the *Ahecedario*. A Chapter 14 footnote lists Algarotti’s 1751 letter to Mariette, and a Chapter 15 note lists Mariette’s 1768 letter to Temanza. These source claims and relations remain within the wording and evidentiary limits of the original statements; this card establishes no formal relation and does not imply independent consultation of cited originals.
