## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔第7章在 p.189 将 Benedetto Gennari 列为仰慕 Louis XIV 的博洛尼亚画家之一，并保留作者对这种仰慕是否全无利害动机的怀疑。p.196 称 Gennari 来自 Bologna、是 Guercino 之侄，记述其在 England 与 Paris 工作，并于1688年离开 England，后返回 France、最终回到 Italy。本卡按书内叙述登记人物，不补入生卒或未经本书支持的身份细节。[第7章 p.189](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md)（L252–255） [第7章 p.196](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_iv.md)（L52–58） [全书索引 G.csv#34](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/G.csv)

**English:** In Chapter 7, p. 189, Haskell lists Benedetto Gennari among the Bolognese painters who admired Louis XIV, while retaining the author’s suspicion that this admiration was not wholly disinterested. On p. 196, Haskell says Gennari came from Bologna and was Guercino’s nephew, and recounts his work in England and Paris, his departure from England in 1688, and his eventual return to Italy. This card records the book’s account and adds no life dates or identity details unsupported by it. [第7章 p.189](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md)（L252–255） [第7章 p.196](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_iv.md)（L52–58） [全书索引 G.csv#34](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/G.csv)
