### 描述

**中文：** 1663-09-22 致 Antonio Ruffo 信评价罗萨宁愿挨饿也不降低作品质量；评价与画家实际经济状况不能等同。

**English:** His letter of 22 September 1663 to Ruffo says Rosa would rather starve than lower quality. The evaluation does not establish Rosa's actual finances.
