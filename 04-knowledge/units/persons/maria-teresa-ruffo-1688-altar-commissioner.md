## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Lenzo对圣额我略堂的建筑史研究将Maria Teresa Ruffo认定为1688年加尔默罗圣母大理石祭坛的建造委托者，该祭坛用于安置圭尔奇诺1665年绘制的画作。当前仅建立与这一具体行为对应的来源型人物记录；可用资料列有同名修女，尚未确认她对应哪一位，也未确认修会名、家族身份或QID。

**English:** Fulvio Lenzo’s architectural history identifies Maria Teresa Ruffo as the commissioner of the marble altar of the Madonna del Carmine at San Gregorio in 1688, built to house Guercino’s 1665 painting. This source-bound person record represents that specific action. Available sources list homonymous nuns but do not establish which one she was, her religious name, family identity, or a Wikidata item.
