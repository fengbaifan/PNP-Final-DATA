## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 威尼斯画家弗朗切斯科·费代利，通称马焦托；原书图版68a将其列为三幅总督肖像的创作者。

**English:** The Venetian painter Francesco Fedeli, known as Maggiotto; plate 68a lists him as creator of three portraits of doges.
