## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第3章以全名称他为 Pier Donato Cesi 枢机，并记其同意出资完成 Chiesa Nuova；他要求取得与 Farnese 在 Gesù 相同的权利，其本人和家族也获 Oratorian Congregation 事务中的优先权。Haskell 还说其在教堂建造中的实际干预有限。第20章后记以转引的1581年信件说明其自我克制；引文经 Haskell 转述，脚注指向 Bonadonna Russo 1968 年第135页，本批未核读原信。第3章将其与兄弟 Angelo Cesi 区分，并称 Pier Donato 于1586年去世后，Angelo 延续家族赞助。以上均为本书陈述；外部身份与事实未在本批核验。

**English:** Chapter 3 names him Cardinal Pier Donato Cesi and says he agreed to fund completion of Chiesa Nuova; he sought rights at the church comparable to those Farnese held at the Gesù, and his terms provided privileges for him and his family in Oratorian Congregation affairs. Haskell also says Cesi had little involvement in the church's building. The Chapter 20 postscript uses a 1581 letter, quoted second-hand by Haskell, to illustrate Cesi's self-restraint; its footnote cites Bonadonna Russo (1968, p. 135), whose original letter was not checked in this batch. Chapter 3 distinguishes Cesi from his brother Angelo, saying Angelo continued family patronage after Pier Donato's death in 1586. These are reports in the book; external identity and facts were not verified in this batch.
