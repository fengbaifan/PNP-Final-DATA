## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 写作时收藏一幅 Sacchi 为罗马 Capuchin 教堂所作祭坛稿；章中另以 Mahon 1947 为学院研究引用。馆藏状态不延伸到今天。

**English:** Mahon owned a Sacchi Capuchin altarpiece modello at the time of Haskell's account. Mahon 1947 is separately cited on the Academy. The historical holding is not extended to today.
