## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第2章称Louis XIII在1625年遣人代表团离开巴黎前送来一份礼物；相邻句提到他们房间墙上已挂有七幅挂毯，但原文没有在同一分句中明确将两者等同。第7章又称Louis XIII于1643年去世。本卡保留这一语境，不扩大礼物对象的认定。 [来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_iv.md#L8)；[来源2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md#L143)

**English:** Chapter 2 says Louis XIII sent a gift shortly before the Barberini party left Paris in 1625. The adjacent sentence mentions seven tapestries already hanging in their apartments, but the source does not explicitly equate the gift with those tapestries in one clause. Chapter 7 also says Louis XIII was dead by 1643. This entry preserves that context without expanding the identification of the gift.
