## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 奇里亚科·马泰是罗马贵族、收藏家与委托人，与《以马忤斯的晚餐》《逮捕基督》和《施洗者约翰与公羊》的早期历史直接相关。

**English:** Ciriaco Mattei was a Roman nobleman, collector, and commissioner associated with Supper at Emmaus, The Taking of Christ, and John the Baptist with a Ram.
