## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 亚历山德罗·达马谢尼·佩雷蒂·迪·蒙塔尔托是西斯笃五世的母系侄孙、枢机和艺术赞助人。本章以他在教皇死后仍保持赞助活动、接续圣安德烈亚德拉瓦莱教堂工程为例，说明一般趋势中的例外。

**English:** Alessandro Damasceni Peretti di Montalto was a maternal great-nephew of Sixtus V, a cardinal and an arts patron. Chapter 1 treats his continued patronage after the pope’s death and his continuation of Sant’Andrea della Valle as exceptions to the general pattern.
