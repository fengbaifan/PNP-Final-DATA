**English:** Haskell attributes the medal inscription to Lucretius. A BnF catalogue record for *De rerum natura* lists Lucrèce as the author and uses Titus Lucretius Carus in the edition title; this supports identifying the Lucretius named in the chapter as the Roman author. The BnF record is bibliographic, not a personal authority record, and the chapter does not identify the specific edition or passage used for the medal inscription.
