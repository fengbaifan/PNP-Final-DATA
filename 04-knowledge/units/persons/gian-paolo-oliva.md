## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Gian Paolo Oliva 是耶稣会士，也是 Bernini 的亲密朋友；他后来担任耶稣会总会长。第3章记述他于1680年召 Andrea Pozzo 来罗马，并叙述其推动圣依纳爵纪念工程、1681年去世；第6章再次以耶稣会总会长身份提及他。本卡仅据书内交叉语境登记人物，不把友谊、召请或工程记述写成正式关系，也未作外部核验。

**English:** Haskell identifies Gian Paolo Oliva as a Jesuit and one of Bernini's closest friends; Oliva later served as General of the Society of Jesus. Chapter 3 says that in 1680 he summoned Andrea Pozzo to Rome and recounts his efforts concerning the monument to Ignatius and his death in 1681. Chapter 6 again refers to him as General of the Jesuits. This card registers the identity from the book's cross-chapter context; it does not turn friendship, the summons, or the project account into formal relations and makes no external verification claim.

### 本书语境

第3章 p.64 称 Gian Paolo Oliva 为耶稣会士和 Bernini 的亲密朋友；pp.88、91 以 General Oliva 记述其召请 Andrea Pozzo 及圣依纳爵纪念工程。第6章 p.153 再称其为耶稣会总会长，并置于 Bernini 赴巴黎的政治语境。

Chapter 3 (p. 64) names Gian Paolo Oliva as a Jesuit and close friend of Bernini. Pages 88 and 91 refer to him as General Oliva in the accounts of summoning Andrea Pozzo and the Ignatius monument. Chapter 6 (p. 153) again calls him General of the Jesuits in the political context of Bernini's journey to Paris.
