## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第5章称Claude有说法是在罗马展览中看到Flemish画家Goffredo Wals的风景画后前往那不勒斯师从他，原文保留“is supposed to have”限定；第8章收藏叙述中出现拼作“Gioffredo Wals”的形式。本卡保留该拼写异文，不把传闻语气改写为确定师承。 [来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_i.md#L53)；[来源2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i.md#L35)

**English:** Chapter 5 says Claude is supposed to have seen landscapes by the Flemish artist Goffredo Wals at a Roman exhibition and then gone to Naples to study under him; the source's “is supposed to have” qualification is retained. Chapter 8 gives the spelling “Gioffredo Wals” in a collection account. This entry preserves the spelling variant and does not turn the reported story into a certain apprenticeship.
