## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 全书索引人物主项“Juvarra, Filippo”列出第6、7、10章页码；另有子项“as artistic director in Turin”，定位到第10章 pp.296–297。该页正文以全名称他为建筑师，并称其在都灵担任城市艺术总监，随后叙述他为马德里王宫构思装饰方案。第6章以全名记其管理 Pietro Ottoboni 剧场布景，第7章又以全名称其为 Savoy 绘画赞助的艺术决策者。Treccani DBI、瑞典国家博物馆和美国国家美术馆记录在姓名、建筑师身份及1678–1736年日期上相符；后两者列出 Juvara、Ivara 等拼写异形。[人物主项 J，K.csv#23](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/J，K.csv) [索引子项 J，K.csv#24](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/J，K.csv) [第6章 pp.164–165](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_v.md)（L30、36–38） [第7章 p.202](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_v.md)（L59–60） [第10章 pp.296–297](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L330–335、343） [Treccani DBI](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/filippo-juvarra_(Dizionario-Biografico)/) [瑞典国家博物馆人物记录](https://collection.nationalmuseum.se/en/artists/artist/10591/) [美国国家美术馆](https://www.nga.gov/artists/2585-filippo-juvarra)

**English:** The book’s main index entry “Juvarra, Filippo” cites Chapters 6, 7, and 10; a subentry, “as artistic director in Turin,” points to pages 296–297 of Chapter 10. The text on those pages names the architect Filippo Juvarra and describes him as the city’s artistic director in Turin before recounting his scheme for the Royal Palace in Madrid. Chapter 6 names him in connection with Pietro Ottoboni’s theatre scenery, and Chapter 7 again names him as the artistic decision-maker in Savoy’s policy for painting patronage. Treccani’s DBI, the Nationalmuseum, and the National Gallery of Art agree on the identity, architect role, and dates 1678–1736; the latter two list spelling variants including Juvara and Ivara. ### 跨章语境

第6章记其为 Pietro Ottoboni 管理剧场布景及其风格；第7章记其在 Savoy 赞助政策中的角色；第10章将都灵艺术总监与马德里宫廷建筑方案联系起来。三个章节的全名或同页明确指代，与索引主项及都灵艺术总监子项共同支持跨章归并。[第6章 pp.164–165](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_v.md)（L30、36–38） [第7章 p.202](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_v.md)（L59–60） [第10章 pp.296–297](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L330–335、343） [人物主项 J，K.csv#23](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/J，K.csv) [索引子项 J，K.csv#24](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/J，K.csv)
