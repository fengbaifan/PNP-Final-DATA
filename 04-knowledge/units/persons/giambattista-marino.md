## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第2章称那不勒斯诗人 Giambattista Marino 是 Marcello Sacchetti 的密友之一，记他于1623年结束八年巴黎之行返回意大利，在罗马停留一年后前往那不勒斯并很快去世。书中称他热爱绘画、收藏绘画、促成年轻 Nicolas Poussin 来意大利，并曾将 Poussin 作品介绍给 Marcello Sacchetti；第4章记述 Poussin 绘画最早的赞赏者 Marino 委托作品以及其遗赠去向不明。作者对宫廷赞助未果的原因保留为推测。

**English:** Chapter 2 identifies the Neapolitan poet Giambattista Marino as one of Marcello Sacchetti's close friends and says he returned to Italy in 1623 after an eight-year stay in Paris, spent a year in Rome, and soon died in Naples. The book describes his interest in painting and collecting, says he persuaded the young Nicolas Poussin to come to Italy, and records his introduction of Poussin to Marcello Sacchetti. Chapter 4 describes works commissioned from Poussin by Marino, whom it calls the painter's first known admirer, and leaves the recipient of Marino's bequest unidentified. Haskell's explanation for Marino's unsuccessful papal patronage remains conjectural.
