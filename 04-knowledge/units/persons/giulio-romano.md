### 描述

**中文：** 第2章把Giulio Romano列入Cardinal Barberini宫中旧大师绘画的群体性叙述，没有在该段识别具体作品或逐件归属。第10章称他与Castiglione曾受Gonzaga家族大量雇用。第7章转引Brienne以法文Jules Romain将挂毯设计归于该名；本卡依据S3对齐至索引人名，但保留这是被引来源的归属，未作独立作品核验。以上作品及雇佣关系断言留待S6逐条审查。中文名为本项目工作译名。（第2章，印刷页30，Markdown L113–115；第10章，印刷页312，Markdown L21–23；第7章，印刷页186，Markdown L216）

**English:** Chapter 2 includes Giulio Romano in a group-level account of old-master paintings in Cardinal Barberini's palace; that passage does not identify individual works or assign each one to him. Chapter 10 says that he and Castiglione had been much employed by the Gonzaga. Chapter 7 quotes Brienne attributing a tapestry design to “Jules Romain”; S3 aligns this French form with the index entry Giulio Romano, while retaining it as an attribution in a quoted source rather than independent work verification. These work and employment assertions await individual S6 review. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration.
