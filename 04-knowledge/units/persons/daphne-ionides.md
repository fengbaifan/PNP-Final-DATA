## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Daphne Ionides（1896–1966）是英国艺术收藏者。第一版图版目录将她列为《查理一世》肖像的伦敦收藏者；独立馆藏记录也显示她曾收藏并捐赠艺术品。

**English:** Daphne Ionides (1896–1966) was an art collector. The first-edition plate list names her as the London holder of the Portrait of Charles I; independent collection records also document her collecting and donation of artworks.
