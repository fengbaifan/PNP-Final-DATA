**English:** Haskell names Ottavio Vannini as the painter of frescoes in the Del Rosso family chapel and as the family's painter. He says Vannini died the following year, leaving an altar picture for a pupil to complete, and attributes four Old Testament scenes and other pictures to him. Evaluative statements about his style and works remain attributed to Haskell. Person, work, and commission assertions await individual S6 review. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration. (第8章，印刷页212，Markdown L99–101)
