## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称Ottavio Vannini为Del Rosso家族礼拜堂壁画的作者和该家族的画家，并将四幅具名旧约场景及其他绘画归于他。本批为四幅单件场景分别建立S4知识元，并依据同一段文字写入四条限定于Haskell书内归属的created_by正式关系。其关于画风和作品评价的文字仍保留为Haskell的判断；祭坛画、礼拜堂壁画等其他关系候选仍待S6审理。四幅场景的组合候选cand-7527尚未确认为独立作品组，也未登记为KU。中文名为本项目工作译名。（第8章，印刷页212，Markdown L99–101）

**English:** Haskell names Ottavio Vannini as the painter of frescoes in the Del Rosso family chapel and as the family's painter, and attributes four named Old Testament scenes and other pictures to him. This batch registers four individual scenes and adds four formal created_by relations limited to Haskell's account. His evaluative statements about Vannini's style and works remain attributed to Haskell; the altar picture, chapel frescoes, and other relationship candidates remain under S6 review. The four-scene group candidate cand-7527 has not been established as a separate work group or registered as a KU. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration. (第8章，印刷页212，Markdown L99–101)
