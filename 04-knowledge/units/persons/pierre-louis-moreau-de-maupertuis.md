### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记索引“Maupertuis, Pierre-Louis”及第14章p.349所称Maupertuis。Deutsche Biographie的GND 118731998记录以Pierre-Louis Moreau de Maupertuis为规范名，并列出索引与正文中的两种称名。第14章称Algarotti在巴黎遇见Maupertuis；句中“with whom he stayed at Cirey”指向Voltaire，不扩写为Maupertuis的住宿关系。中文名为项目工作音译。

**English:** This card records the person indexed as “Maupertuis, Pierre-Louis” and referred to as Maupertuis on p. 349. Deutsche Biographie's GND 118731998 gives Pierre-Louis Moreau de Maupertuis as the heading and lists both book forms as alternate names. Chapter 14 says Algarotti met Maupertuis in Paris; the clause “with whom he stayed at Cirey” refers to Voltaire and is not extended to Maupertuis. The Chinese display name is a project working transliteration.
