## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称 Giuseppe Valletta 是那不勒斯诗人和品味裁断者，也是 Luca Giordano 与 Francesco Solimena 的赞助人。注释转述其曾帮助 Giordano 处理图像学问题；相关二手引注未独立查阅。中文名为项目工作音译。〔[第8章，印刷页204；OCR L17–18、129（注3）](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i.md)〕

**English:** Haskell describes Giuseppe Valletta as a Neapolitan poet and arbiter of taste, and as a patron of Luca Giordano and Francesco Solimena. A footnote reports that he used to help Giordano with iconographic problems; the cited secondary references were not independently consulted. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration. ([第8章，印刷页204；OCR L17–18、129（注3）](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i.md))
