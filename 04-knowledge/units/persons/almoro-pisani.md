## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡指第9章称为 Pisani 家族最年幼儿子、并在索引列作 Almorò Pisani 的人物。Treccani 的 Pietro Longhi 条目也将 Marina Sagredo 之子称为 Almorò Pisani，并记载其于1766年去世。第13章的 Almorò Albrizzi 是另一人；姓氏、家族角色和外部记载均支持分组。

**English:** This card identifies the person called the Pisani family’s youngest son in Chapter 9 and indexed as Almorò Pisani. Treccani’s Pietro Longhi entry also names Marina Sagredo’s son as Almorò Pisani and records his death in 1766. The Almorò Albrizzi in Chapter 13 is a different person; the surnames, family roles, and external account support separate identity groups.

### 本书语境

**中文：** 第9章 p.255 将 Almorò 置于Pisani家族寓意壁画的在世成员中；p.260称他是私人学院绘画课的对象，并称学院在其去世时结束；p.261称他为业余艺术家。p.261括号中的1740–66按Haskell原文保留为青年时期语境，不作为生卒年。相关关系候选 st-chp9-p260-academy-lessons-end 留待S6。

**English:** On p.255, Chapter 9 places Almorò among the living members represented in a Pisani family allegory. Page 260 describes him as the subject of drawing lessons at a private academy and says it ended at his death; p.261 calls him an amateur artist. The parenthetical 1740–66 on p.261 is retained as Haskell’s context for his youth, not treated as birth and death dates. Relation candidate st-chp9-p260-academy-lessons-end remains for S6.
