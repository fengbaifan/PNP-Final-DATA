## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第10章以 John Sheffield 全名称其为 Buckingham 公爵，并将其任爵起点记为1703年；同段说他是 Bellucci 的早期赞助人之一，且此前已雇用 Sebastiano Ricci。第281页记 Haskell 所述其在女王去世后退出政坛、当时67岁。本卡以书内全名和头衔为身份依据，不将未具名的第三任妻子另行识别，也不把候选关系登记为正式边。 [来源定位：[第10章 p.280](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L61）; [第10章 p.281](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L126）；[书后索引](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-1-Index-MD/B.md)（B.csv#313）]

**English:** Chapter 10 names John Sheffield as Duke of Buckingham and dates his dukedom from 1703; the same passage says he was one of Bellucci’s early patrons and had previously employed Sebastiano Ricci. Page 281 records Haskell’s account that he retired from politics after the queen’s death, aged 67. This card identifies him by the name and title given in the book; it does not identify the unnamed third wife or convert candidate relations into formal edges. ### 名称

书内全名为 John Sheffield；索引采用 Buckingham, John Sheffield, Duke of 形式。中文名为工作音译。

The book gives the personal name John Sheffield; the index files him as Buckingham, John Sheffield, Duke of. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration.

### 身份边界

本卡只指第10章 pp.280–281 所述 John Sheffield。头衔和人物身份依 Haskell 本书记录；本轮未补外部生平或未具名妻子的身份。

This card refers only to the John Sheffield discussed in Chapter 10, pp.280–281. The title and identity follow Haskell’s account; no external biography or identity for the unnamed wife is added.
