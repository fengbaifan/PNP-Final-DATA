### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 第8章 p.218 以全名 Antonio Zanchi 称其为年轻的威尼斯画家，并记述他获选绘制《Moses striking the Rock》。第9章 p.251 以姓氏 Zanchi 提及其在威尼斯装饰艺术中的声望；第10章 p.294 称 Pietro Negri 为 Zanchi 的学生。三处均由书后索引主项 `Zanchi, Antonio` 定位。本卡只登记书内人物身份与相关叙述，不补充外部生平。中文名为项目工作音译。[第8章 p.218](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md)（L84）；[第9章 p.251](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)（L114）；[第10章 p.294](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L295）；[书后索引](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-1-Index-MD/UVWXYZ.md)（UVWXYZ.csv#171）

**English:** In Chapter 8, p.218, Haskell names Antonio Zanchi as a young Venetian painter and says he was selected to paint *Moses striking the Rock*. Chapter 9, p.251, refers to Zanchi by surname among painters successful in Venetian decoration; Chapter 10, p.294, calls Pietro Negri a pupil of Zanchi. The index groups all three references under `Zanchi, Antonio`. This card records the identity and account in the book without adding an external biography. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration.
