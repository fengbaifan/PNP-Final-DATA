## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 老安东·玛丽亚·扎内蒂是威尼斯学者和艺术赞助人，出现在朱塞佩·佐基绘制的、与安德烈亚·杰里尼同坐的双人肖像中。

**English:** Anton Maria Zanetti the Elder was a Venetian scholar and art patron depicted seated with Andrea Gerini in a double portrait by Giuseppe Zocchi.
