## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第一章所述萨基的枢机赞助人。Andrea Sacchi 在其家中于 1637–1640 年间由较低位置升入有作家、诗人和秘书的最高受俸等级。

本卡只确定为该段明确命名的 Cardinal Antonio Barberini；不混同 Francesco，也不从未读材料补写生卒与完整家族谱系。同名 Antonio 的进一步外部权威消歧未开展。

**English:** The named cardinal was Sacchi's patron: during 1637–1640 the painter rose into the highest pensioned household rank alongside writers, poets and secretaries. Antonio is not Francesco; full genealogy and external disambiguation of the Antonio name remain unverified.
