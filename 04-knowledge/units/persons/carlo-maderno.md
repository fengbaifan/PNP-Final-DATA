## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应索引词头“Carlo Maderno”及候选cand-1477、cand-4873。第2章将Maderno置于圣彼得大教堂增建中殿方案的讨论中；第3章称Peretti-Montalto将他带入教堂工程，并称Borromini曾在Maderno与Bernini手下承担较小任务。Postscript提及Hibbard关于Maderno家族宫殿起源的专著。

**English:** This card represents the index entry “Carlo Maderno” and candidates cand-1477 and cand-4873. Chapter 2 names Maderno in its discussion of the proposed nave addition to St Peter’s. Chapter 3 says Peretti-Montalto brought him into a church project and that Borromini had earlier worked on comparatively minor tasks under Maderno and Bernini. The Postscript refers to Hibbard’s monograph on Maderno and the origins of the family palace.
