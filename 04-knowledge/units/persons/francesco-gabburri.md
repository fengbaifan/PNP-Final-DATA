## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell第8章 p.241 注5以 Francesco Gabburri 全名称其为图画鉴赏家；第8章 p.211 与第9章 p.266 注6也以全名提到相关书信。索引 G.csv#4 将这些页码列在同一人物主项下。中文名为本项目工作音译，身份限于书内证据。

**English:** In Chapter 8, p. 241 n. 5, Haskell names Francesco Gabburri as a connoisseur of drawings; Chapter 8, p. 211, and Chapter 9, p. 266 n. 6, also name him in letter references. The index lists these pages under one Francesco Gabburri entry. The Chinese name is a working transliteration, and the identification is limited to the book.
