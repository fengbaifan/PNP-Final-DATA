## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 乔万尼·巴蒂斯塔·阿尔布里齐是威尼斯印刷者和出版者，出版了1745年皮亚泽塔绘图的《耶路撒冷解放》插图本。

**English:** Giovanni Battista Albrizzi was a Venetian printer and publisher who published the 1745 illustrated edition of Jerusalem Delivered with designs by Piazzetta.
