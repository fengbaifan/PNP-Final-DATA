## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 枢机与收藏家，委托扩建巴黎宫殿的画廊及绘制穹顶装饰，也是南特伊等人1659年版画中的肖像对象。

**English:** A cardinal and collector who commissioned the galleries and vault decoration of his Paris palace, and is portrayed in the 1659 engraving by Nanteuil and collaborators.
