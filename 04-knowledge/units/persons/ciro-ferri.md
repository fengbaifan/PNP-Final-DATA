## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 1670 年 S. Agnese 穹顶须交彩色稿并获准后不得擅改；工期四年。1672 年致 Ruffo 信称不收预付以保自由，同时提及事务繁多，不能抹去后半限制。

**English:** Ferri's 1670 S. Agnese contract required an approved coloured design, permission for changes and a four-year duration. His 1672 Ruffo letter rejects advances for freedom but also mentions pressing business; that qualification remains.
