## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell将Giovanni Battista Bellucci另列为一位博洛尼亚商人，称其拥有Crespi的作品及更多dal Sole的作品。同一段此前提到Giovanni Antonio Belloni；“another businessman”明确区分两人。本卡仅记录书内人物身份和收藏陈述，不补充外部生平。〔[第8章，印刷页203](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i.md)；[索引 B.csv#132](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/B.csv)〕

**English:** Haskell names Giovanni Battista Bellucci as another Bolognese merchant and says that he owned works by Crespi and many more by dal Sole. In the same paragraph, “another businessman” distinguishes him from Giovanni Antonio Belloni. This card records only the person and collection statements in the book and adds no external biography. ([第8章，印刷页203](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i.md); [索引 B.csv#132](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/B.csv))

### 名称与身份

- 原书姓名与索引主项一致：Giovanni Battista Bellucci；中文名为本项目工作音译。
- 同段的 Giovanni Antonio Belloni 是不同人物，身份组保持分开。
