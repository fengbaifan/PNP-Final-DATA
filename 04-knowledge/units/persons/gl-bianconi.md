## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 乔万尼·卢多维科·比安科尼（1717–1781）是博洛尼亚医生、古物学者和艺术著述者。其1762年11月22日自德累斯顿寄给菲利波·埃尔科拉尼侯爵的信，批评艺术家传记把卓越才能与疯狂、酗酒或奇特恶习相连。

**English:** Giovanni Ludovico Bianconi (1717–1781) was a Bolognese physician, antiquarian, and writer on art. His letter from Dresden to Marchese Filippo Hercolani, dated 22 November 1762, criticizes biographical tales that associate artistic excellence with madness, drinking, or exceptional vice.
