## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 马尔科·里奇是画家、舞台布景设计者、版画家和素描家；图版47为其《歌剧排练》，图版55b《村景》由巴尔托洛齐依据其原设计刻印。

**English:** Marco Ricci was a painter, stage designer, printmaker and draughtsman; plate 47 is his Opera Rehearsal, while plate 55b is a Village Scene engraved by Bartolozzi after his design.
