## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Frederick IV of Denmark 于1709年到访威尼斯，并记其委托 Rosalba Carriera 创作十二幅粉彩肖像；同段提到 Carlevarijs 为其庆典赛舟作画。此卡只指该人物，不把肖像或赛舟作品并入人物。

**English:** Haskell says Frederick IV of Denmark visited Venice in 1709 and commissioned twelve pastel portraits from Rosalba Carriera. The same passage mentions Carlevarijs’s regatta painting for the celebration in his honour. This unit represents the person only; the portraits and regatta remain separate works.
