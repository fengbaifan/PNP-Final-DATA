## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应索引词头“Mantua, Ferdinando Carlo, Duke of”及第9、10章的Ferdinando Carlo Gonzaga。Haskell称其为曼托瓦末代公爵，记述他在1706年奥地利入侵后避居威尼斯，两年后于帕多瓦去世；第10章将Schulenburg购藏的多数古代作品联系到他的画廊。关于他本人或其近侍可能持有Sagredo图稿的说法是Haskell提出的假设，仍保留其不确定性。既有人物卡中的Ferdinando, Duke of Mantua对应第7章p.175所述、1624年在位且为Vincenzo之子的较早人物；本卡与之分列。中文名为本项目工作音译。

**English:** This card represents the index heading “Mantua, Ferdinando Carlo, Duke of” and the Ferdinando Carlo Gonzaga named in chapters 9 and 10. Haskell identifies him as the last Duke of Mantua, says he sought refuge in Venice after the Austrian invasion in 1706 and died in Padua two years later, and connects most of Schulenburg's purchase of old-master works to his gallery. Haskell's suggestion that Ferdinando Carlo or someone in his entourage may have owned the drawings later assembled by Sagredo remains a hypothesis. The existing KU Ferdinando, Duke of Mantua refers to the earlier duke described on p. 175 as Marie de Medici's nephew and Vincenzo's son in 1624; this card records a separate person. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration.
