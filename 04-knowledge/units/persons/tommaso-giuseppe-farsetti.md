## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 意大利文人、藏书家。Treccani将其规范姓名列为Tommaso Giuseppe Farsetti，记其1720年4月16日生于威尼斯、1791年10月30日卒于帕多瓦。Haskell的索引、缩写署名“T. G. Farsetti”及第15章全名和生年范围共同构成本书内身份锚点；第8章嵌套转引不作为独立核实的作品证据。中文名为项目工作音译。[全书索引 F.csv#28](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/F.csv) [第8章 p.235n](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md)（L435） [第14章 p.347n](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/14_CHP-14_intro.md)（L168–170） [第15章 pp.362n、364](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_i.md)（L38–44） [Treccani《意大利人传记辞典》条目](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/tommaso-giuseppe-farsetti_(Dizionario-Biografico)/)

**English:** An Italian man of letters and bibliophile. Treccani identifies him as Tommaso Giuseppe Farsetti and records his birth in Venice on 16 April 1720 and death in Padua on 30 October 1791. Haskell’s index, the abbreviated attribution “T. G. Farsetti,” and the full name and date range in Chapter 15 provide the book’s identity anchors; the nested quotation in Chapter 8 is not treated as independently verified work evidence. The Chinese name is a working transliteration.
