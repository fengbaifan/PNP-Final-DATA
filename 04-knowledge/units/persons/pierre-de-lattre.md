## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称 Pierre de Lattre 是来自 St Omer 的年轻法国人，也是耶稣会成员；他于1626年进入 S. Andrea 初学院，两年后前往 Collegio Romano，并在那里留至1683年去世。第二句按来源 OCR 转录为“Coliegio Romano”，本卡不将其表述为扫描页校勘。中文名为项目工作音译。〔[第3章，印刷页74；OCR L121–122](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)〕

**English:** Haskell calls Pierre de Lattre a young Frenchman from St Omer and a member of the Society of Jesus. The book says that he entered the novitiate of S. Andrea in 1626, went to the Collegio Romano two years later, and remained there until his death in 1683. The OCR transcription “Coliegio Romano” is retained as transcribed and is not presented as a verified reading of the printed page. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration. ([第3章，印刷页74；OCR L121–122](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md))
