## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 全书索引以 “Rotari, Pietro” 列出人物，指向第295页注3和第297页。第295页注3称 Pietro Rotari 是在德累斯顿工作的威尼斯艺术家之一，1750年抵达，并称他 “at this time” 离开。第297页正文另称他来自[维罗纳](../places/verona.md)，1756年已前往 St Petersburg，六年后在那里去世，并画了数百幅不同题材的作品。两处关于离开和迁居的时间表述保持原样，本卡不推算或调和其精确时间；注3所引 Bellotto 图录未独立查阅。中文名为项目工作音译。[第10章 pp.295、297](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L565、340） [全书索引 Q-R.csv#216](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/Q-R.csv)

**English:** The index lists “Rotari, Pietro” and points to note 3 on p. 295 and the text on p. 297. Note 3 calls Pietro Rotari a Venetian artist working at Dresden, says he arrived in 1750, and reports that he left “at this time.” The p. 297 text separately says that he went from his native [Verona](../places/verona.md) to St Petersburg by 1756, died there six years later, and painted several hundred pictures of different kinds. The departure and migration chronology is retained as stated, without calculating or reconciling exact dates; the Bellotto catalogue cited in note 3 was not independently consulted. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration.
