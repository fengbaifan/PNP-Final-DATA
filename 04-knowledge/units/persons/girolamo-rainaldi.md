## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell在第6章把 Girolamo Rainaldi 列为Innocent X时期接替Bernini、担任首席建筑师的两人之一，并称他规划了Piazza Navona上的家族大宅。第148页将该宅的某些限制归因于Rainaldi的年龄与能力，这是Haskell的评价，不作为独立事实。Treccani《意大利人传记辞典》所载Girolamo Rainaldi（Geronimo）为建筑师，且将Piazza Navona的Palazzo Pamphilj归于他；姓名、职业与建筑对象相合。中文名为工作音译。[第6章书内来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md)

**English:** In Chapter 6 Haskell names Girolamo Rainaldi as one of two architects who replaced Bernini as leading architects under Innocent X, and says Rainaldi planned the family palace on Piazza Navona. On page 148 Haskell attributes limitations of the palace to Rainaldi’s age and limitations; this remains the author’s assessment. Treccani’s *Dizionario Biografico degli Italiani* identifies Girolamo Rainaldi (Geronimo) as an architect and also attributes the Palazzo Pamphilj on Piazza Navona to him, matching the name, profession, and building. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration. [第6章书内来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md)

### 姓名与身份范围

- 书中全名：Girolamo Rainaldi。[第6章书内来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md)
- 外文异名：Treccani DBI列作 Girolamo Rainaldi (Geronimo)。
- 中文显示名：“吉罗拉莫·拉伊纳尔迪”为工作音译，不声明为已核定通行译名。
- 身份边界：Girolamo与第7章另见的Carlo Rainaldi为不同人物；Treccani DBI记Carlo为Girolamo之子，两组不合并。
