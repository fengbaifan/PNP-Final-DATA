## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 安德烈亚·波佐（Andrea Pozzo，1642–1709）是来自特伦托的耶稣会修士、画家、建筑师和透视理论作者，以绘画营造幻觉空间。第一章引传记说明其父母原望他从事文学，后来接受其学画意愿；这是个人经历，不代表所有艺术家庭。[S1、S4–6]

**English:** Andrea Pozzo (1642–1709), from Trento, was a Jesuit brother, painter, architect and writer on perspective who created illusionistic spaces through painting. Chapter 1 cites a biography stating that his parents initially wished him to pursue letters but accepted his desire to paint; this is an individual case, not a claim about all artists' families. [S1, S4–6]
