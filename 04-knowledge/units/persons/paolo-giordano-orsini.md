## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 保罗·乔尔达诺·奥尔西尼（1541–1585）是意大利贵族、军事统帅，1560年起为首任布拉恰诺公爵；《赞助人与画家》图版17c目录将其列为奥塔维奥·莱奥尼肖像的对象。

**English:** Paolo Giordano I Orsini (1541–1585) was an Italian nobleman and military commander, and the first Duke of Bracciano from 1560. The plate 17c entry in Patrons and Painters lists him as the subject of a portrait by Ottavio Leoni.
