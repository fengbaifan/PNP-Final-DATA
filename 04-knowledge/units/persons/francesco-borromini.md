## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔将 Francesco Borromini 描述为17世纪罗马建筑师。第2章称他曾协助 Bernini 修改 Barberini 宫方案；第3章记其为 Chiesa Nuova 建造毗邻 Oratorian 的建筑；第6章记他接手圣若望拉特朗大殿的改建，并参与 Piazza Navona 的 S. Agnese 工程。这里仅概述书中明确的建筑工作，不把竞争关系扩写为未证实的因果，也不作外部核验。

**English:** Haskell presents Francesco Borromini as a seventeenth-century Roman architect. Chapter 2 says he helped Bernini modify the Barberini palace plan; Chapter 3 mentions his design for the building adjoining the Oratorian Chiesa Nuova; Chapter 6 says he took over the remodelling of St John Lateran and worked on S. Agnese in Piazza Navona. This description is limited to the architectural work stated in the book; it does not turn rivalry into an unsupported causal account and makes no external verification claim.

### 本书语境

第2章 p.48 记载 Borromini 曾作为 Carlo Maderno 的助手协助 Bernini 修改 Barberini 宫方案；第3章 pp.75–76 记其设计 Chiesa Nuova 附属建筑。第6章 pp.147–148 以全名 Francesco Borromini 介绍其接手拉特朗大殿改建，并说明旧建筑结构限制了设计；p.149 将他与 S. Agnese 的圆顶及立面工程相连。索引主项和 Innocent X、Scalzi church 子项均以同一全名定位上述书内提及。

Chapter 2, p. 48, says Borromini assisted Bernini as Carlo Maderno's former assistant on the Barberini palace plan; Chapter 3, pp. 75–76, mention his design for the building adjoining the Chiesa Nuova. Chapter 6, pp. 147–148, names Francesco Borromini and says he took over the remodelling of St John Lateran, whose existing structure constrained the design; p. 149 connects him to the dome and façade of S. Agnese. The main index entry and the Innocent X and Scalzi church subentries point to these source mentions under the same full name.
