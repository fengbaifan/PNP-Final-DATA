**English:** A chapter 2 footnote attributes a statement about building as public charity to Pope Gregory XIII and cites it through Baglione and Orbaan. Chapter 3 says Gregory XIII allotted S. Stefano Rotondo to the Jesuits and that Pope Gregory XIII assigned S. Maria della Vallicella to Philip Neri in 1575. This entry preserves the chapter 2 citation chain; the cited texts were not independently checked.
