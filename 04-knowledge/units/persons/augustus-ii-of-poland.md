### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记《赞助人与画家》索引所称的波兰国王奥古斯都二世。索引主项以“Augustus II of Poland”并列其萨克森选帝侯姓名，子项记录对威尼斯画家的赞助语境；第10章p.294直接将Frederick Augustus I与波兰国王Augustus II对应，p.278使用“Augustus the Strong”。Deutsche Biographie与Sächsische Biografie均将其识别为Friedrich August I（August II，the Strong），1670–1733。既有Augustus III人物卡指向其子Friedrich August II，二者保持分立；亲属和赞助关系仍由原始statement承载，待S6审查。中文名为项目工作译名。[全书索引 A.csv#147](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/A.csv) [全书索引 A.csv#148](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/A.csv) [第10章 p.278](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L26–41） [第10章 p.294](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L292–302） [Deutsche Biographie GND 118505084](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/gnd118505084.html) [Sächsische Biografie: Friedrich August I.](https://saebi.isgv.de/biografie/Friedrich_August_I._von_Sachsen_%281670-1733%29)

**English:** This unit records Augustus II of Poland as indexed in *Patrons and Painters*. The main index entry pairs that name with his Saxon electoral identity, while the subentry points to patronage of Venetian artists. Chapter 10, p. 294, directly identifies Frederick Augustus I with the King of Poland Augustus II; p. 278 uses “Augustus the Strong.” Deutsche Biographie and Sächsische Biografie identify him as Friedrich August I (August II, the Strong), 1670–1733. The existing Augustus III unit refers to his son Friedrich August II and remains separate; kinship and patronage claims stay in the source statements for S6 review. The Chinese display name is a project working translation. ### 本章相关内容

第10章p.278将Diziani在Dresden宫廷任职的场景称为Augustus the Strong；p.294则写明Frederick Augustus I同时为萨克森选帝侯与波兰国王Augustus II，并分别描述Dresden的艺术赞助与收藏。本文只以这些内容作为身份与语境锚点，不把书中具体雇佣、购藏或其他关系陈述在本次登记中确立为正式关系。Haskell将继位的Augustus III明确说成Augustus II之子；这一父子身份边界也由两条外部传记支持。[第10章 p.278](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L26–41） [第10章 p.294](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L292–302）
