## 内容

### 人物身份

**中文：** 本卡记录书后索引所列 Girolamo Muziano，以及第3章正文明确称名的画家身份。哈斯克尔称 Cardinal Alessandro Farnese 委托其为耶稣堂主祭坛绘制《The Circumcision》。本卡只保留来源中的姓名和画家角色，不补写生卒、籍贯或其他外部生平。

**English:** This card records the painter indexed as Girolamo Muziano and named in Chapter 3. Haskell says Cardinal Alessandro Farnese commissioned him to paint The Circumcision for the Gesù High Altar. The card preserves only the source’s name and painter role; no dates, birthplace, or other external biography are added.

### 对象边界

**English:** The painter named in the book; the work and commission are recorded separately.
