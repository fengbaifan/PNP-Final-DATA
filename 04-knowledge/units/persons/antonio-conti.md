## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第10章介绍威尼斯贵族 Antonio Conti，记其1713年赴巴黎、1715年赴伦敦结识 Newton、1726年返威尼斯并于1749年去世。其写作和思想影响 Francesco Algarotti，也为绘画中想象力的作用辩护。原书说 Newton 提议 Conti 入选 Royal Society；本卡保留“提议”，不将其改写为实际当选。

**English:** Chapter 10 introduces the Venetian nobleman Antonio Conti, recording his 1713 visit to Paris, his 1715 journey to London and meeting with Newton, his return to Venice in 1726, and his death in 1749. His writing and ideas influenced Francesco Algarotti, and he defended the role of fantasy in painting. Haskell says Newton proposed Conti's election to the Royal Society; this card preserves that as a proposal and does not state that Conti was elected.
