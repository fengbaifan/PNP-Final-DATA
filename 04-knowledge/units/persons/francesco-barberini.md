## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 乌尔班八世的枢机侄辈、圣路加学院保护人。其支持提高学院声望，但有时不在后来的争议中支持学院。

不与萨基雇主 Antonio Barberini 合并；第一章其他仅称 Cardinal Barberini 的通信与举镜逸事，不能都凭同姓自动归给他。

**English:** Urban VIII's cardinal nephew supported the Academy and raised its standing, but did not always back it in later disputes. He is distinct from Antonio; unspecified Barberini correspondents and the mirror anecdote are not all assigned to Francesco.
