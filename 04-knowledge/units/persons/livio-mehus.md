## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称 Livio Mehus 为佛兰德画家，并记德尔罗索兄弟在1677年拥有其三幅画、十二年后这些作品已不在其藏品中。第8章另称费迪南多收藏其三十件作品，赞赏其艺术特点；“去世使他未能直接为费迪南多工作”是 Haskell 的推断，保留为来源判断。中文名为项目工作音译。〔[第8章，印刷页213、240；Markdown L116、351](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i.md)〕

**English:** Haskell identifies Livio Mehus as a Flemish painter and reports that the del Rosso brothers owned three of his pictures in 1677, which had disappeared from their collection twelve years later. Elsewhere the book says Ferdinand owned thirty of his works and describes qualities that appealed to the prince. The claim that Mehus's death prevented him from working directly for Ferdinand is Haskell's inference and remains attributed to the source. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration. ([第8章，印刷页213、240；Markdown L116、351](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i.md))
