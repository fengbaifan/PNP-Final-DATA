**中文：** 本卡将索引主项“Dorigny, Louis”及其“work for Manin”子项映射到同一人物 KU。跨章材料分别使用 Louis Dorigny、Luigi Dorigny 与 Dorigny：第7章称 Louis Dorigny 与多名意大利艺术家前往维也纳，为 Prince Eugene 的 Upper、Lower Belvedere 绘制壁画；第9章 p.250 称法国画家 Dorigny 为 Zenobio 宫绘制神话壁画，并为 Widmann 乡间住宅绘制 Diana 与 Actaeon 壁画；p.251 称 Manin 家族雇用画家 Luigi Dorigny；p.268 则明确以 Louis Dorigny 指称 Manin 家族常用的画家并记其装饰 Passeriano 别墅。索引同一主项及 Manin 子项把这些页码聚合在一起，正文全名及同一 Manin 赞助语境支持将 Louis／Luigi 两种写法归入同一书内人物。本卡不使用外部身份记录，也不扩写脚注未核实的生平。[第7章 p.201](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_v.md)（L49–50） [第9章 p.250](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)（L92、98） [第9章 p.251](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)（L111） [第9章 p.268](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md)（L15） [第9章 p.270](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md)（L28） [书后索引](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-1-Index-MD/D.md)（D.csv#46–47）

**English:** This card maps the index main entry “Dorigny, Louis” and its “work for Manin” subentry to one person KU. Across chapters, the book uses Louis Dorigny, Luigi Dorigny, and Dorigny. Chapter 7 says that Louis Dorigny came from Italy with other artists to paint frescoes in Prince Eugene’s Upper and Lower Belvederes. Chapter 9, p.250 calls Dorigny a French artist engaged to paint mythological frescoes at the Zenobio palace and says that he painted Diana and Actaeon frescoes at the Widmann country house. Page 251 says that the Manin employed the painter Luigi Dorigny; p.268 names Louis Dorigny as a painter they used whenever possible and records his decoration of their Passeriano villa. The shared index entry and Manin subentry aggregate these locations, while the full names and repeated Manin context support treating Louis and Luigi as forms for the same book-local person. No external identity record is used and no biography from unchecked footnotes is added.
