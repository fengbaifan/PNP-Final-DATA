### 本书相关语境

第9章叙述Cordellina在Vicenza与Montecchio的住宅、Tiepolo受邀装饰乡间住宅及其作品；后记称其在1770年开始建造Vicenza宫殿并于十八年后卒于该处。第9章所述Vicenza住宅与后记所述宫殿是否同一建筑，S2候选`cand-8375`仍未裁定；Haskell引述的Puppi文章和Tiepolo书信未独立查阅。关系端点及具体建筑身份不由本人物卡合并。[第9章 p.256](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)（L158–161） [第二版后记 p.405](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/20_CHP-20Postscript.md)（L143–144）

Chapter 9 describes Cordellina’s residences in Vicenza and Montecchio, Tiepolo’s invitation to decorate the country residence, and the resulting works. The postscript says he began building a palace in Vicenza in 1770 and died there eighteen years later. Whether the Vicenza house in Chapter 9 and the palace in the postscript are the same building remains unresolved in candidate `cand-8375`; Puppi’s article and Tiepolo’s cited letter were not independently consulted. This person card does not merge the relationship endpoints or resolve the building’s identity.
