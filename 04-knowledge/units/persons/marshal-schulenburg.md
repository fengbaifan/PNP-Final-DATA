## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 为威尼斯共和国服务的元帅、收藏家与赞助人，委托皮亚泽塔创作及评估收藏。

**English:** A field marshal in Venetian service, collector and patron who employed Piazzetta for artistic commissions and collection assessment.
