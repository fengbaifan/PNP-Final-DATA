## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 保禄五世原名Camillo Borghese，1605–1621年任教皇。本章将他列为乌尔班八世之前的赞助先例，并记录他在1605年授予圣路加学院每年释放一名死刑犯的特权。

**English:** Paul V, born Camillo Borghese, was pope from 1605 to 1621. Chapter 1 presents him as a predecessor in patronage to Urban VIII and records the privilege he granted the Accademia di San Luca in 1605 to release one condemned prisoner annually.
