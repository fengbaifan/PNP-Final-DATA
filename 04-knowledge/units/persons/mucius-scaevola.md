**English:** This card records Mucius Scaevola as named in Haskell's Chapter 9. This card does not turn that comparison into a formal relation between the two persons. [Chapter 9 introduction, print p. 256, L163–L164](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md#L163)
