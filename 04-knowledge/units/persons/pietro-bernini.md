## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 彼得罗·贝尔尼尼是活跃于佛罗伦萨、那不勒斯和罗马的雕塑家与画家，也是 Gian Lorenzo 与 Luigi Bernini 的父亲。

**English:** Pietro Bernini was a sculptor and painter active in Florence, Naples, and Rome, and the father of Gian Lorenzo and Luigi Bernini.
