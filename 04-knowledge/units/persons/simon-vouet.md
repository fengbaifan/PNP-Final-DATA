## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第2章称 Simon Vouet 为 Francesco Barberini 赞助的法国画家之一，并将他与 Caravaggio 的影响及装饰性风格联系起来；Haskell 说他1627年离开罗马时已预示后来带到法国的 grand manner。第4章记他约1614年抵达罗马，是 Cassiano dal Pozzo 首位大规模雇用来创作原创绘画的画家，也是 Cassiano 在罗马最持续支持的画家之一。第4章另称他曾为 Bracciano 公爵夫人 Isabella 画像；该条索引子目指画家本人，不足以识别或登记具体肖像作品。

**English:** Chapter 2 identifies Simon Vouet as one of the French artists patronised by Francesco Barberini and discusses Caravaggio's influence and Vouet's decorative manner. Haskell says that by leaving Rome in 1627 Vouet was already foreshadowing the grand manner he later introduced into France. Chapter 4 dates his arrival in Rome to about 1614 and identifies him as the first artist Cassiano dal Pozzo employed on a large scale to produce original paintings, as well as one of Cassiano's most consistent supporters in Rome. Chapter 4 also says he had painted Isabella, Duchess of Bracciano; the index subentry identifies the artist, not an individually identified portrait work.
