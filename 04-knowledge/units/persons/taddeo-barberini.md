## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Taddeo Barberini 是教皇乌尔班八世的侄子、Antonio 的兄弟；他获授重要政治职位和财富，1627年与 Anna Colonna 成婚，1631年被任命为罗马长官。第2章称他对艺术兴趣有限，第6章在卡斯特罗战争语境中评价其作为总司令的能力。此卡只记录书内人物身份与限定叙述，不作外部核验。

**English:** Haskell identifies Taddeo Barberini as a nephew of Pope Urban VIII and brother of Antonio. He received important political posts and wealth, married Anna Colonna in 1627, and was made Prefect of Rome in 1631. Chapter 2 says he had little interest in the arts, while Chapter 6 assesses his command during the War of Castro. This card records the identity and qualified account in the book, with no external verification.

The bilingual display names appear in the card title. The Chinese name is a transliteration or descriptive rendering for readability; the book's wording is preserved in the source quotations.

### 本书语境

第2章 pp.31–32 将 Taddeo 放入乌尔班八世的家族任用语境，称其为 Antonio 的兄弟和教皇侄子，并记其与 Anna Colonna 的婚姻；pp.47–48 记其任命、罗马长官职衔和对 Barberini 宫邸的控制。第6章 p.146 在卡斯特罗战争段落称其为总司令。

Chapters 2 (pp. 31–32) place Taddeo in Urban VIII's family appointments, identify him as Antonio's brother and the Pope's nephew, and mention his marriage to Anna Colonna. Pages 47–48 record his appointments, the prefecture of Rome, and his control of the Barberini palace. Chapter 6 (p. 146) names him as commander-in-chief in the account of the War of Castro.

### 身份边界

中文：本卡只指人物 Taddeo Barberini，不与 Barberini family 集体卡或其兄弟 Antonio Barberini 人物卡合并。正文称其父亲为 papal armies 的最高统帅但未在该处具名，本卡不推定父亲身份；妻子被称为 Anna Colonna，但本卡不据此新增关系。书中对其性格、艺术兴趣和军事能力的评价属于 Haskell 叙述。本轮未新增正式关系或外部生平。

English: This card refers to Taddeo Barberini as a person and remains distinct from the Barberini family card and his brother Antonio Barberini's person KU. The text says his father held supreme command of the papal armies but does not name him in that passage, so this card does not infer the father's identity. His wife is named as Anna Colonna, but no formal relation is added here. The book's comments on his character, artistic interests, and military ability remain Haskell's account. No formal relations or external biography are added.
