## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡将第4章所称 Andrea Podesta 与第7章注3所写全名 Giovanni Andrea Podesta 记录为同一人物。Haskell在第4章称其为热那亚雕版师；第7章称其为热那亚画家和雕版师。Getty ULAN 与卢浮宫艺术家权威记录均列出与书内形式相符的姓名变体。中文显示名为项目工作译名。有关1661年清单及其与Mazarin联系的内容只保留为Haskell转述，de Cosnac条目及清单原件未独立查阅；本卡不据此建立正式关系边。[第4章 p.97](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_i.md)（L30） [第4章 p.105](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md)（L82–83） [第7章 p.184注3](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md)（L356）

**English:** This card treats the Chapter 4 form Andrea Podesta and the full name Giovanni Andrea Podesta in Chapter 7 note 3 as the same person. Haskell calls him a Genoese engraver in Chapter 4 and a Genoese painter and engraver in Chapter 7. Getty ULAN and the Louvre artist authority both list name variants that match the forms in the book. The Chinese display name is a project working transliteration. The reported role in the 1661 inventory and the reported connection with Mazarin remain attributed to Haskell; de Cosnac’s item and the inventory itself have not been independently consulted, and this card creates no formal relation edge.
