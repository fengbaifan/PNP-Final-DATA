**English:** This card represents the index entry “Cigoli, Ludovico” and the Ludovico Cigoli references in chapters 2 and 4. Chapter 2 note 1 says Cigoli had painted scenes from the story at a palace when it belonged to Scipione Borghese. Chapter 4 places him in Galileo’s artistic context and calls him Galileo’s favourite painter and occasional collaborator. These source statements and relation candidates remain for separate S6 review. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration.
