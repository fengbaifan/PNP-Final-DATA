**English:** Chapter 10 lists Francesco Simonini among the painters of Schulenburg’s portraits and says Simonini painted Schulenburg’s principal battles. Haskell cautiously says that Schulenburg apparently took Simonini on campaigns; this qualification is retained. The card records only the person and context in the book.
