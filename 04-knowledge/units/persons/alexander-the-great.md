## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本书第1章以王室委托颂扬 Alexander the Great 为概括性例子；第8章记述 Sebastiano Ricci 曾受要求创作以他为题的两幅历史画，但方案未实施；第10章提到 Rapparini 将 Johann Wilhelm 与他比较，并把他列为 Pittoni 为 Schulenburg 创作的系列画题材之一。本卡仅归并这些书内具名提及，不确认具体作品身份，也不把比较或题材陈述登记为正式关系。

**English:** Chapter 1 uses royal commissions celebrating Alexander the Great as a general example. Chapter 8 says Sebastiano Ricci was asked to paint two history pictures featuring him, but the proposal came to nothing. Chapter 10 reports Rapparini’s comparison of Johann Wilhelm to Alexander and lists him among subjects in a Pittoni series for Schulenburg. This card consolidates only these named references in the book; it does not identify individual works or record the comparisons or subject references as formal relations.

### 名称

规范英文名依照书内拼写 Alexander the Great。中文显示名采用通行译名“亚历山大大帝”；卡片不补写生卒、地域归属或其他外部生平信息。

The English name follows the spelling in the book. The Chinese display name uses the conventional rendering 亚历山大大帝. No dates, political affiliation, or other external biographical details are added.

### 身份边界

本卡指书中具名的 Alexander the Great，不代表 Ricci 未实施的绘画方案，也不把 Pittoni 系列拆成已确认的单件作品。第1章的王室委托概括性例子不构成具体关系端点。

This card refers to the named Alexander the Great in the book. It does not represent Ricci’s unrealized proposal or treat Pittoni’s series as separately identified works. The general royal-commission example in chapter 1 is not a specific relation endpoint.
