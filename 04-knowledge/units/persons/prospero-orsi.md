## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 普罗斯佩罗·奥尔西是活跃于罗马的意大利画家、怪诞纹饰画家与艺术市场中介，也是卡拉瓦乔的朋友和早期市场引介者。

**English:** Prospero Orsi was an Italian painter, grotesque decorator, and art-market intermediary active in Rome who was also Caravaggio’s friend and early market advocate.
