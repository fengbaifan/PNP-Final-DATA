## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔在第4章称Alessandro Varotari为画家，并以“il Padovanino”称之；他记述Varotari赶赴罗马临摹Titian送往西班牙的《维纳斯的崇拜》。第13章另以Padovanino之名列举Filippo Pinelli收藏中的画家。 [来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md#L83)；[来源2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md#L80)

**English:** In chapter 4 Haskell identifies Alessandro Varotari as a painter and calls him “il Padovanino”; he recounts that Varotari hurried to Rome to copy Titian’s Worship of Venus when it was sent to Spain. Chapter 13 also names Padovanino among the painters represented in Filippo Pinelli’s collection. [来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md#L83)；[来源2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md#L80)
