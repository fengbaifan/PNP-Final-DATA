**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell第10章索引及注释中点名的John Conduitt。第290页注1称一封1729年6月4日致McSwiny的信保存在Conduitt papers；第304页注4另引述他于1730年6月请Smith代购三幅Canaletto画作，却回指第290页注1。S2已标明该引用与日期不吻合，被引信件的身份和来源对应仍未解决；本卡不把两处记录合并为同一信。正文OCR另有“John Conduits”异拼，候选账本保留。中文名为本项目工作音译。 [第10章 p.290 注1；OCR L545](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md) [第10章 p.304 注4；OCR L608](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md) [索引 C.csv#350](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/C.csv)

**English:** This card records John Conduitt named in the Chapter 10 index and notes. Note 1 on page 290 reports a letter to McSwiny dated 4 June 1729 among the Conduitt papers; note 4 on page 304 quotes a June 1730 request for Smith to procure three Canaletto pictures but points back to note 1 on page 290. S2 records that the cross-reference and dates do not match, leaving the identity and provenance of the quoted record unresolved; this card does not merge the two records into one letter. One OCR mention also reads “John Conduits,” retained in the candidate ledger. The Chinese name is a working transliteration.
