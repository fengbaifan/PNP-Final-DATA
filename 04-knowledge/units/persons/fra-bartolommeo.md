**English:** In Chapter 8, p.231, Haskell attributes the S. Marco painting to Fra Bartolommeo and says Ferdinand replaced the church painting with a copy in 1692. Chapter 14, p.348, records Algarotti’s strong impression of Fra Bartolommeo’s art in Florence in 1733. The index locates both passages under one person entry. This card records the person in the book; the S. Marco work and its versions are not folded into the person unit.
