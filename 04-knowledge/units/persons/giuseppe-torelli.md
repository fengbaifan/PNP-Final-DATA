### 本书相关内容

**中文：** 信中称Torelli的兄弟Felice已为一幅已知绘画找到特洛伊题材，并提及Pyrrhus杀害Polyxena及其他构图人物；信件报告题材选择和预期绘制难点，不证明绘画已经完成。原书OCR将Felice转录为“Febee”，S2据页图校读为Felice。该信的原始档案材料未独立查阅；关系候选留待S6。

**English:** The letter says Torelli’s brother Felice had found a Trojan subject for a known painting and mentions Pyrrhus killing Polyxena and other figures in the composition. It reports a subject choice and anticipated difficulty, not completion of the painting. The book OCR transcribes Felice as “Febee”; S2 records a reading of Felice from the page image. The archival original was not independently consulted; the relation candidate remains for S6.
