## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔第2章称“圣彼得本人”，讨论贝尔尼尼华盖中以圣彼得三重冕与钥匙表现教宗制度的方式；第3章又将圣彼得列为Lanfranco穹顶壁画中的人物。本条记录的是使徒圣彼得，与同名的圣彼得大教堂这一地点区分。 [来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_ii.md#L44)；[来源2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md#L79)

**English:** In chapter 2 Haskell refers to “St Peter himself” while discussing the papal symbolism of Bernini’s baldacchino, represented by Peter’s tiara and keys. Chapter 3 also names St Peter among the figures in Lanfranco’s dome fresco. This record identifies the apostle and distinguishes him from St Peter’s Basilica as a place.
