## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 肖像画家和版画设计者，本书图版48b以B. Nazari列作原设计者；曾为舒伦堡绘骑马肖像。

**English:** A portrait painter and print designer named as B. Nazari for the original design of plate 48b, who also painted an equestrian portrait for Schulenburg.
