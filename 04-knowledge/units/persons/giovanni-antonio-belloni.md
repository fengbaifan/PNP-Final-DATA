## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第8章在叙述博洛尼亚商人对艺术的支持时，以全名称Giovanni Antonio Belloni，记其接待流亡中的James III、收藏dal Sole、Torelli、Crespi与Gambarini等人的画作，并送Gambarini赴罗马。紧接着原文另称商人Giovanni Battista Bellucci；本卡保持两人为不同人物。上述收藏与行动仍按S2关系候选留待审查。〔[第8章，印刷页203](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i.md)；[索引 B.csv#118](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/B.csv)〕

**English:** In its account of artistic patronage by Bolognese merchants, Chapter 8 names Giovanni Antonio Belloni and says he gave hospitality to the exiled James III, owned pictures by dal Sole, Torelli, Crespi, and Gambarini, and sent Gambarini to Rome. The text immediately distinguishes another merchant, Giovanni Battista Bellucci; this card keeps them as separate people. The collection and activity claims remain S2 relation candidates for review.
