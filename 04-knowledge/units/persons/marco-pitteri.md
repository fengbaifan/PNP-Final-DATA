### 描述

**中文：** 马尔科·阿尔维塞·皮泰里（1702–1786）是威尼斯刻版家；图版48a的科尔内尔肖像取自朱塞佩·安杰利的设计。

**English:** Marco Alvise Pitteri (1702–1786) was a Venetian engraver; his portrait of Corner in plate 48a is after Giuseppe Angeli’s design.
