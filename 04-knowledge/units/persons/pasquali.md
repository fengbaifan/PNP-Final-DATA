## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 乔万尼·巴蒂斯塔·帕斯夸利是威尼斯印刷者和出版者；《赞助人与画家》图版57b所列戈尔多尼作品第二卷属于其出版版本。

**English:** Giovanni Battista Pasquali was a Venetian printer and publisher; the second volume of Goldoni’s works cited for plate 57b in Patrons and Painters belongs to his edition.
