## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 多梅尼科·切拉托是建筑师及建筑教师，与安德烈亚·梅莫合作整治帕多瓦Prà广场；本书图版63列出他的整治方案。

**English:** Domenico Cerato was an architect and architecture teacher who worked with Andrea Memmo on the reclamation of Prà della Valle in Padua; his proposals appear in plate 63.
