### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 在第9章引用 [Cochin 第三卷](../archives/cochin-volume-iii-sagredo-pictures-1758.md)，并称 Cochin 于1758年写过一份记录 Sagredo 宫当时绘画的记述。该书目语境支持此人物候选与 Cochin, III 的作者同一；本卡不据此扩写生平或其他身份。〔[第9章 p.263](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)（L237）；[p.265 注2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)（L297）〕

**English:** Haskell cites Cochin's third volume in Chapter 9 and says that Cochin wrote an account in 1758 of pictures then in the Sagredo palace. This source context identifies the person candidate with the author of [*Cochin*, volume III](../archives/cochin-volume-iii-sagredo-pictures-1758.md); no broader biography or additional identity claims are added.
