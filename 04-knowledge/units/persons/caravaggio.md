## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Giustiniani 曾购其被拒祭坛画；1600 年《圣保罗归化》《圣彼得殉难》合同要求提交设计。被拒作品未在本章具名，不与后两作机械合并。

**English:** Giustiniani bought a rejected Caravaggio altarpiece. Separately, the 1600 contracts required designs for the Conversion of Saint Paul and Martyrdom of Saint Peter. The unnamed rejected work is not automatically merged with either.
