## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Rosalba Carriera 为威尼斯画家。第10章记述她于1703年绘制鼻烟盒及象牙微型画，Christian Cole 说服她转向粉彩肖像；书中又记她为欧洲访客作肖像，并在18世纪20年代为 Consul Smith 工作。第8章提到她为 Emmanuela Conti 绘制肖像，但该处 OCR 将姓拼作“Garriera”。本卡依据书内跨章同一人物叙述登记身份，保留拼写异文，不作外部核验。

**English:** Haskell identifies Rosalba Carriera as a Venetian painter. Chapter 10 says that in 1703 she was painting snuff-boxes and miniatures on ivory, and that Christian Cole persuaded her to turn to pastel portraits; it also records portrait commissions from European visitors and her work for Consul Smith in the 1720s. Chapter 8 mentions her portrait of Emmanuela Conti, with the surname rendered as “Garriera” in the OCR. This card registers the identity from the book's cross-chapter account, preserves the spelling variant, and makes no external verification claim.

### 本书语境

第10章 p.276 以全名 Rosalba Carriera 介绍她早期绘制鼻烟盒及象牙微型画的经历，并记 Christian Cole 劝其转向粉彩肖像；p.277 记法国收藏家 Crozat 欣赏其粉彩作品。第8章 p.228 记她为 Emmanuela Conti 绘肖像；第10章 p.303 又记 Consul Smith 在18世纪20年代委托她作画。

Chapter 10 (p. 276) names Rosalba Carriera and recounts her early work in snuff-boxes and ivory miniatures and Christian Cole's encouragement of pastel portraiture; p. 277 describes the French collector Crozat's interest in her pastels. Chapter 8 (p. 228) mentions her portrait of Emmanuela Conti, and Chapter 10 (p. 303) records commissions for her from Consul Smith in the 1720s.
