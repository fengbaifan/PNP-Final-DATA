## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第4章明确称其为 Paolo Giordano II Orsini、布拉恰诺公爵，并叙述其音乐、戏剧兴趣及艺术交往。第19章附录列出与其委托 Bernini、Tacca 有关的文件；第20章后记提到一尊以他为主人公的青铜胸像，同时记录对 Bernini 归属的异议。本卡据书内全名和跨章指代登记身份；委托、作品及作者归属仍按 Haskell 的报告范围保留，不视为外部核实。

**English:** Chapter 4 names him Paolo Giordano II Orsini, Duke of Bracciano, and recounts his interests in music and theatre and his artistic associations. Appendix 2 presents documents relating to commissions from him to Bernini and Tacca. The Chapter 20 postscript mentions a small bronze bust identified as him while also reporting objections to its attribution to Bernini. This card registers the identity from the book's full name and cross-chapter references; commissions, works, and attribution remain within Haskell's report and are not treated as externally verified.

Chapter 4 (p. 95) identifies him by full name as Duke of Bracciano. The title of Appendix 2 (p. 387) describes documents relating to his commissions to Bernini and Tacca. The Chapter 20 postscript (p. 401) again names Paolo Giordano II Orsini when discussing a bronze bust and the dispute over its attribution.

中文：本卡仅指 Paolo Giordano II Orsini。已有 `paolo-giordano-orsini` 卡记录 Paolo Giordano I，世代不同，不并合；`duke-bracciano-mulier` 卡所指公爵仍未具名，不能承载本人的已知全名。第19章附录标题的 Paolo Giordano Orsini 与第4、20章具名者按书内引证和同名语境归为同一人。青铜半身像对 Bernini 的归属在后记中被质疑；本卡不将其写为 Bernini 确作。S2 的创作、委托、作品与人物交往断言仍待 S6 审查，本卡不新增正式关系或外部生平。

English: This card refers only to Paolo Giordano II Orsini. The existing `paolo-giordano-orsini` card records Paolo Giordano I, a different generation, and is not merged. The `duke-bracciano-mulier` card refers to an unnamed duke and cannot carry this known full name. The Paolo Giordano Orsini named in the Chapter 19 appendix is matched to the person identified in Chapters 4 and 20 by the book's cross-references and context. The postscript questions the attribution of the bronze bust to Bernini; this card does not state that Bernini made it. S2 claims about commissions, works, and personal associations remain for S6 review. No formal relations or external biography are added.
