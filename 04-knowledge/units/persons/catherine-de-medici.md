## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第7章印刷页174注2所称的 Catherine de’ Medici。注释称她于1559年委托一件亨利二世骑马像，并明确说该像未执行。本条目记录该人物称名与所引语境，不把未完成项目写成已实现的雕像。[第7章 p.174 注2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md)

**English:** This card records the Catherine de’ Medici named in Haskell’s Chapter 7, printed p.174, note 2. The note says that she commissioned an equestrian statue of Henri II in 1559 and explicitly says it was never executed. This entry records the person name and cited context without treating the unrealized project as a completed statue.
