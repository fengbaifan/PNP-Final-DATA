## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Getty ULAN将书中所称的Permoser识别为巴尔塔萨·佩尔莫泽，列为德国雕塑家，生卒年为1651–1732。索引 P.csv#80 与 P.csv#81 均以“Permoser, Balthasar”为主条目；#81 的“Apotheosis of Prince Eugene”是作品子项。两条索引候选合为同一人物身份组，作品另登记。Haskell第7章p.201将一件大理石《神化》作品归于Permoser；作者归属及作品关系按各自来源记录。

**English:** Getty ULAN identifies the Permoser named in the book as Balthasar Permoser, a German sculptor who lived from 1651 to 1732. Index entries P.csv#80 and P.csv#81 share the person heading “Permoser, Balthasar”; “Apotheosis of Prince Eugene” under #81 is a work subentry. The two index candidates are grouped as one person identity, and the sculpture is registered separately. In Chapter 7, p. 201, Haskell attributes a marble Apotheosis to Permoser; the attribution and work relationship remain tied to their respective sources.
