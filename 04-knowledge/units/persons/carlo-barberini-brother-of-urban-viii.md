## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 书后索引 B.csv#41 将 Carlo Barberini 标作 Urban VIII 之兄，并定位至第2章 pp.26、31；同一子项 B.csv#42 的 monuments to 定位至 p.41。第26页记 Urban 与兄长 Carlo 共同购置宫殿；第31页以全名 Carlo Barberini 指称1624、1625年两次地产交易的买方；第41页记其于1630年去世并由 Pope 订制纪念牌。上述全名、亲属称谓和页段相互吻合，支持将两个候选合为一个 source-local 人物 KU。本卡对应 Urban VIII 的兄长，不是其父亲 Carlo Barberini；不据此补充外部生平。[第2章 p.26](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_i.md)（L68）；[第2章 p.31](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_ii.md)（L7–8）；[第2章 p.41](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_ii.md)（L121–123）；[书后索引](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-1-Index-MD/B.md)（B.csv#41–42）

**English:** Index entry B.csv#41 identifies Carlo Barberini as the brother of Urban VIII and locates him at Chapter 2, pp.26 and 31; subentry B.csv#42, “monuments to,” locates p.41. Page 26 says Urban and his brother Carlo jointly bought and furnished a palace. Page 31 names Carlo Barberini as the buyer in two property transactions in 1624 and 1625. Page 41 records his death in 1630 and a memorial plaque commissioned by the Pope. These names, kinship terms and page locations support mapping the two candidates to one book-local person KU. This card refers to Urban VIII’s brother, not his father, who was also named Carlo Barberini; no external biography is added. ### 名称

原书全名为 Carlo Barberini。索引形式为 Barberini, Carlo (brother to Urban VIII)；中文显示名“卡洛·巴贝里尼”为工作音译。

The source gives the full name Carlo Barberini. The index form is Barberini, Carlo (brother to Urban VIII). The Chinese display name is a working transliteration.

### 身份边界

本卡只指索引所标 Urban VIII 之兄 Carlo Barberini。现有 Antonio Barberini 人物卡所记父亲 Carlo Barberini 属不同亲属代际，不映射至本卡。此身份区分依据本书索引及第2章语境；本轮未查阅外部身份记录。

This card refers only to the Carlo Barberini identified by the index as Urban VIII’s brother. The Carlo Barberini recorded as the father in the existing Antonio Barberini card belongs to a different generation and is not mapped here. This distinction follows the book’s index and Chapter 2 context; no external identity authority was consulted.
