## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 全书索引将 François Duquesnoy 的第2、4、7章页码归在同一人物项下。第2章称他为佛兰德雕塑家，记其参与 Bernini 的 Baldacchino 项目并雕刻 St Andrew；第4章称其雕塑最合 Cassiano 的趣味，提到蜡与陶土模型及 St Susanna；第7章则叙述他答应前往巴黎、却在 Livorno 启程前去世。Treccani DBI、Getty ULAN 与美国国家美术馆记录的姓名、雕塑身份和作品语境吻合。Treccani DBI 与 NGA 给1597年生，Getty ULAN 总述为约1594年，ULAN 来源项还列1597年；这一出生年异文予以保留，不据此拆分身份。中文名为项目工作音译。[第2章 p.36](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_ii.md)（L53–54） [第4章 p.104](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md)（L56–58） [第7章 pp.176、179](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md)（L83–89、118–120） [全书索引 D.csv#54](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/D.csv) [Treccani DBI](<https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/du-quesnoy-francois-detto-il-fiammingo_(Dizionario-Biografico)/>) [Getty ULAN 500115495](<https://www.getty.edu/vow/ULANFullDisplay?find=&nation=&role=&subjectid=500115495>) [National Gallery of Art](https://www.nga.gov/artists/2107-francois-duquesnoy)

**English:** The book’s index groups François Duquesnoy’s references on pages 36, 104, 176, and 179 under one person entry. Chapter 2 calls him a Flemish sculptor who worked on Bernini’s Baldacchino and carved the St Andrew; Chapter 4 says his sculpture particularly appealed to Cassiano’s taste and mentions his wax and terracotta models and St Susanna; Chapter 7 recounts that he agreed to go to Paris but died at Livorno before setting sail. Treccani’s DBI, Getty ULAN, and the National Gallery of Art records match the name, sculptor identity, and work context. Treccani and NGA give 1597 as the birth year, while Getty ULAN’s summary gives circa 1594 and its source entries also include 1597; this birth-year variation is preserved and does not split the identity. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration.
