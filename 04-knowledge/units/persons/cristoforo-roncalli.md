### 本书相关语境

第2章以“Pomarancio”称呼Roncalli，第3章使用“Pomerancio”；第4章脚注所讨论的是一封信中Romanelli/Pomarancio的校改以及Nicolson关于抄写者可能写成Romanelli而非Roncalli的推测。后者按Haskell的转述保留为解释，不独立确认为原信内容。作品、旅行、委托和与Giustiniani的关系仍由各自S2陈述限定。[第2章 p.30](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_i.md)（L111） [第3章 p.69](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L49–50） [第4章 p.94 注3](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_intro.md)（L10–13）

Chapter 2 refers to Roncalli as “Pomarancio,” while Chapter 3 uses “Pomerancio.” Chapter 4, note 3, discusses an emendation from Romanelli to Pomarancio in a letter and Nicolson’s suggestion that a copyist may have written Romanelli instead of Roncalli. This remains Haskell’s reported explanation and is not independently confirmed as the letter’s wording. Claims about works, travel, commissions, and Giustiniani remain qualified in their individual S2 statements.
