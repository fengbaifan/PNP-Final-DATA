## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称1575年新教堂奠基石由亚历山德罗·德·美第奇主持，当时称其为佛罗伦萨大主教及大公使节。Treccani与圣座资料确认同名者于1605年当选教宗利奥十一世；此身份对应不验证奠基事件。

**English:** Haskell says Alessandro de’ Medici laid the new church’s foundation stone in 1575, calling him Archbishop of Florence and the Grand Duke’s ambassador. Treccani and the Holy See identify the same Alessandro as elected Pope Leo XI in 1605; this does not verify the foundation event.
