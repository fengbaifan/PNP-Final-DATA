## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记书后索引作“Marchesi, Raffaelo”的人物。正文同一印刷页将律师姓名写作“Raffaele Marchesi”，并称Cerquozzi将部分作品遗赠给他。两种拼写都保留为本书对同一页人物的指称；本卡以索引形式作显示名，不宣称已确定规范拼法。中文显示名为按索引拼写形成的项目工作音译。

**English:** This card records the person indexed as “Marchesi, Raffaelo.” On the same printed page, the body spells the lawyer's name “Raffaele Marchesi” and says that Cerquozzi bequeathed some of his works to him. Both spellings are preserved as the book's forms for the person discussed on that page; the index form is used as the display name without claiming that the standard spelling has been settled. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration based on the index form.
