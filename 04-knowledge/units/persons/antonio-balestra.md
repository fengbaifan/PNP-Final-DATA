**English:** Across Chapters 7–14, Haskell names Antonio Balestra in the context of painting in Venice: he supplied pictures for the Schönborn family and the Buonaccorsi collection, was invited by Johann Wilhelm, and appeared in Algarotti’s proposed venture involving French and Italian painters.
