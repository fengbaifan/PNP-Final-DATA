**English:** This card represents the index entry “Andrea Palladio” and the architect Palladio in chapters 4, 10, 14, and the Postscript. Chapter 10 calls him Venice’s leading architect. Chapter 14 records Algarotti’s admiration for his works and Canaletto’s view conceived according to his scheme. The Postscript connects Joseph Smith’s architectural commissions with the glorification of Palladio’s principles.
