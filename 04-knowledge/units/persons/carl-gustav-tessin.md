## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Count Tessin 是瑞典人，生于1695年；早年旅行时接触 Watteau，后来担任瑞典王室建筑事务主管，1739年任驻法大使并在巴黎形成当代绘画与素描收藏。书中也记载他与威尼斯艺术圈往来、收藏 Tiepolo 等人的作品，并与 Zanetti 交谊。本卡以索引全名作为显示名，正文多称 Count Tessin；内容限于书内转述，不作外部核验。

**English:** Haskell identifies Count Tessin as a Swede born in 1695 who encountered Watteau during his early travels, later served as superintendent of the Swedish royal buildings, and became ambassador to France in 1739, assembling a collection of contemporary drawings and paintings in Paris. The book also records his contacts with the Venetian art world, his purchases of works by Tiepolo and others, and his friendship with Zanetti. The display name follows the full index form while the prose usually says Count Tessin; this card is limited to the book's account and makes no external verification claim.

### 本书语境

第10章 pp.292–293 以 Count Tessin 叙述其瑞典身份、1695年出生、早年旅行、王室职务、驻法经历及收藏；第13章 pp.341–342 将 Swedish Count Tessin 置于 Zanetti 的威尼斯交游与艺术收藏语境，记述他借助 Zanetti 扩充收藏并向 Tiepolo 致意。三个索引候选分别覆盖人物总项、与 Tiepolo 的关系提示及与 A. M. Zanetti the Elder 的关系提示，正文锚点指向同一位瑞典伯爵。

Chapter 10, pp. 292–293, refers to Count Tessin in its account of his Swedish identity, birth in 1695, early travels, royal office, French embassy, and collecting. Chapter 13, pp. 341–342, places the Swedish Count Tessin in Zanetti's Venetian network, recording his use of Zanetti's taste in building his collection and his message to Tiepolo. The three index candidates cover the person entry and its Tiepolo and A. M. Zanetti the Elder cross-references; their body anchors point to the same Swedish count.
