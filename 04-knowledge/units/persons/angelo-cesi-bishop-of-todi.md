## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记索引“Cesi, Bishop Angelo”及第3章p.69所称Angelo Cesi, Bishop of Todi。第2版后记p.400的“Monsignor Angelo Cesi”按S3跨章裁决归入同一人物。第3章p.70称其为“Cardinal Angelo Cesi”，该头衔与Treccani DBI所记1586年枢机推荐未成功相冲突，保留为来源问题，不把该称谓登记为已证头衔。中文名为工作音译。 [C.csv#163](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/C.csv) [第3章第II节 p.69；OCR L42-44](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md) [第3章第II节 p.70；OCR L58](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md) [第2版后记 p.400；OCR L52-53](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/20_CHP-20Postscript.md)

**English:** This card records the person indexed as “Cesi, Bishop Angelo” and called Angelo Cesi, Bishop of Todi, on p.69. The “Monsignor Angelo Cesi” in the second-edition postscript, p.400, is grouped with him under the S3 cross-chapter decision. Chapter 3, p.70, calls him “Cardinal Angelo Cesi”; that title conflicts with the Treccani DBI account of an unsuccessful cardinal nomination in 1586 and is retained as a source issue, not registered as an established title. The Chinese rendering is a working transliteration. **English:** Chapter 3 records Angelo Cesi’s continuation of family patronage at the Chiesa Nuova and a high-altar decoration promise attributed to “Cardinal Angelo Cesi”; the title conflict is retained, and both S2 candidates remain for S6. The sibling wording on p.400 serves as an S3 identity anchor only; this batch creates no formal edge.
