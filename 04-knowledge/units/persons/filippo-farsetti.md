## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Filippo Farsetti 生于1703年，约17或18岁时成为 Padre Lodoli 的学生，曾广泛旅行并在巴黎居住数年；他以成为 abate 回避其身份所附带的政治责任，并把大量时间、精力和财力投入艺术。第15章将其收藏、对古典雕塑和当代艺术的兴趣及其对威尼斯艺术环境的影响相连，同时指出其具体收藏时间与个人目标仍不完全清楚。本卡仅登记书内人物身份和限定叙述，不作外部核验。

**English:** Haskell says Filippo Farsetti was born in 1703, became a pupil of Padre Lodoli at about seventeen or eighteen, travelled widely, and lived for several years in Paris. He avoided the political responsibilities attached to his status by becoming an abate and devoted much of his time, energy, and money to the arts. Chapter 15 connects his collections and interests in classical sculpture and contemporary arts with the Venetian artistic milieu, while noting that the chronology of his collecting and his personal aims remain incompletely known. This card registers the identity and qualified account in the book, with no external verification.

Pages 363–364 discuss his private collections and influence on Venetian Neoclassicism and patronage, while explicitly describing him as a figure whose aims and achievements remain partly obscure. Chapter 10's candidate index gives p. 322 (OCR page marker [Page 22]) for the passage listing him among Lodoli's admirers; Chapter 16's candidate index gives p. 373 (OCR page marker [Page 2]) for the later patronage context.
