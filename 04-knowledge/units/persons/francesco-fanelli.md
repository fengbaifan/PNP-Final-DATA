## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 弗朗切斯科·法内利是出身佛罗伦萨的雕塑家，曾在热那亚经营作坊，1632年迁居伦敦，后获查理一世任命为王室雕塑家。本书图版27a列有他的查理一世肖像。

**English:** Francesco Fanelli was a sculptor from Florence who ran a workshop in Genoa, moved to London in 1632, and was subsequently appointed royal sculptor by Charles I. His portrait of Charles I appears in plate 27a.
