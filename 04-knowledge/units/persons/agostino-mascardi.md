**English:** This card records the Agostino Mascardi named in Chapter 6. Page 157 says that the vogue of The Table was “largely” due to him and that he translated it with a long commentary in 1629; the same page identifies him as a professor of rhetoric at Rome University under Urban VIII. These points are retained as Haskell's account and are not independently verified here, including its causal wording, appointment, or edition history. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration.
