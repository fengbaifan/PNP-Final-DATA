## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Guercino、Rosa、Ferri 及代理人书信中的赞助人/收信人。与刊印这些材料的 V. Ruffo 区分，不能按同姓合并。

**English:** Ruffo appears as patron or recipient in correspondence involving Guercino, Rosa, Ferri and agents. He is distinct from V. Ruffo, the publisher of such materials; shared surnames do not justify merger.
