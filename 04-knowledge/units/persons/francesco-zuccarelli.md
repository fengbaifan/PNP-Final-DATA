## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称弗朗切斯科·祖卡雷利为风景画家，早年受到威尼斯画家安东尼奥·玛丽亚·扎内蒂赏识，并记其曾在英国长期工作、为英国领事约瑟夫·史密斯作画。第13章称阿尔布里齐曾赞助他；第14章记述阿尔加罗蒂为他提出风景画题材。上述职业、赞助和委托内容均限于书内叙述。

**English:** Haskell identifies Francesco Zuccarelli as a landscape painter, admired early in his career by the Venetian painter A. M. Zanetti, and recounts his extended work in England and painting for Consul Joseph Smith. Chapter 13 says Albrizzi patronized him; Chapter 14 describes Algarotti proposing a landscape subject for him. These career, patronage, and commission accounts remain bounded to the book.
