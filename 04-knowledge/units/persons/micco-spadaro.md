## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第5章注释称Micco Spadaro的绘画藏于那不勒斯圣马蒂诺博物馆；该群组与本章另述的Cerquozzi《Masaniello起义》双场景画保持区分。第8章注释另称其人物形象出现在Codazzi作品中，并记载这些画作经过Roomer、Van den Einden及Colonna收藏。本卡记录不同语境，不将其合并为单一作品组或完整收藏清单。 [来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_iii.md#L97)；[来源2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_iii.md#L152)；[来源3](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i.md#L136)

**English:** A chapter 5 note says that pictures by Micco Spadaro are in the Museo di San Martino, Naples; that group remains distinct from the double-scene *Revolt of Masaniello* picture discussed in the same chapter. A chapter 8 note also mentions figures by Spadaro in works by Codazzi and says those pictures passed through the Roomer, Van den Einden, and Colonna collections. This entry records the separate contexts without treating them as one work group or a complete collection inventory.
