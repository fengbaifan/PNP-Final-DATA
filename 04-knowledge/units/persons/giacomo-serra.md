## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第3章 p.70 以 Monsignor Giacomo Serra 全名介绍人物，并称他积极推动年轻画家 Peter Paul Rubens 获得奥拉托利会祭坛画委托，愿意承担300 scudi。第71页记其以撤回资助相劝，神父们随后勉强接受条件。第70页注3另称 Serra 是年轻 Guercino 的赞助人，但 Haskell 引用的 Mahon 尚未独立核读。本卡只据书内姓名与语境登记此人物，不补外部生平。 [来源定位：[第3章 p.70](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L59–60、L158）; [第3章 p.71](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L159）；[书后索引](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-1-Index-MD/S.md)（S.csv#108）]

**English:** Chapter 3, p.70, names Monsignor Giacomo Serra and says he sought to secure the Oratorians’ altarpiece commission for the young Peter Paul Rubens, offering 300 scudi. Page 71 recounts his threat to redirect the money and the fathers’ reluctant acceptance. Note 3 on p.70 also calls Serra a patron of the young Guercino, citing Mahon, which has not been independently consulted. This card registers the person from the name and context in the book and adds no external biography. [Sources: [第3章 p.70](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L59–60、L158）; [第3章 p.71](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L159）; [Index](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-1-Index-MD/S.md) (S.csv#108)]

### 名称

书内称谓为 Monsignor Giacomo Serra，索引形式为 Serra, Mgr Giacomo。中文名为工作音译。

The book calls him Monsignor Giacomo Serra; the index uses Serra, Mgr Giacomo. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration.

### 身份边界

本卡只指第3章 pp.70–71 中的 Monsignor Giacomo Serra。Monsignor 保留为书内称谓，不据此补充任职、具体教区或外部身份。脚注所引 Mahon 未独立查阅。

This card refers only to Monsignor Giacomo Serra in Chapter 3, pp.70–71. Monsignor is retained as the book’s form of address; no office, diocese, or external identity is inferred. Mahon, cited in the footnote, has not been independently consulted.
