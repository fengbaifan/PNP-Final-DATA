## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 在 Prince/Camillo Pamfili 的 Valmontone 工程中绘画，借神谱与带注释的 Virgil 决定神话方案。章中记录预付、材料、食宿以及离开时需获许可。

Alessandro Vasalli 的证词说明优先服务仍须给正常报酬；另有 Innocent X 递画布和 Christina 同乘马车的叙事，前者被作者置于象征性逸事母题中，不能不加限定地当史实。

**English:** Mola worked for Prince/Camillo Pamfili at Valmontone, consulting mythology and an annotated Virgil. Advances, materials, lodging and leave restrictions are recorded. Vasalli's testimony preserves normal fees despite service priority. Innocent X's canvas story is treated as a recurring motif, distinct from Christina's carriage invitation.
