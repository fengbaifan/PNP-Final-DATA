## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第4章具名的Barnabite修士Ambrogio Mazenta。第106页称他是Cassiano重要的通信对象之一，并称其Leonardo手稿收藏在他去世前已散佚、后来由Galeazzo Arconato大部分重建。上述内容保留为Haskell的报告，本卡不补写收藏清单或人物生平。中文显示名为项目工作音译。

**English:** This card records the Barnabite monk Ambrogio Mazenta named in Chapter 4. Page 106 calls him one of Cassiano's notable correspondents and says his Leonardo manuscript collection had been dispersed before his death and later largely reconstructed by Galeazzo Arconato. These points remain Haskell's account; this card adds no collection inventory or biography. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration.
