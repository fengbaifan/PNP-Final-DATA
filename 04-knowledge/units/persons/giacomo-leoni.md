### 本章相关内容

哈斯克尔称莱奥尼帮助开创帕拉第奥运动，并记载他与 Vincenzo Damini 一同从杜塞尔多夫抵达英格兰。抵达关系保留为书内历史地点陈述，不表示其当前所在地或长期居留。

Haskell says Leoni helped inaugurate the Palladian movement and records his arrival in England from Düsseldorf with Vincenzo Damini. The location relation is limited to the historical arrival reported in the book; it does not assert a current location or long-term residence.
