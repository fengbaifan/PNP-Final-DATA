## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 安东尼·弗雷德里克·布伦特（1907–1983）是艺术史家，1947年任考陶尔德艺术学院院长；哈斯克尔感谢他参与研究讨论并指出本书错误。

**English:** Anthony Frederick Blunt (1907–1983) was an art historian appointed director of the Courtauld Institute in 1947; Haskell thanks him for research discussions and for identifying errors in the book.
