## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔第5章称 Famiano Strada 的《De Bello Belgico》第二卷描绘 Alessandro Farnese 为西班牙一方进行的低地国家战役。原文未给其头衔；S3 依据 Treccani《意大利人传记辞典》及该卷题名，将其识别为帕尔马、皮亚琴察和卡斯特罗公爵 Alessandro Farnese。

**English:** Chapter 5 says that the second volume of Famiano Strada's *De Bello Belgico* celebrates Alessandro Farnese's campaigns in the Low Countries on behalf of the Spaniards. Haskell does not give his title here. S3 identifies him as Alessandro Farnese, Duke of Parma, Piacenza and Castro, using Treccani's *Dizionario Biografico degli Italiani* and the title of Strada's second volume.

### 本书语境

第5章 p.137 在罗马西班牙赞助语境中提及该战役，并称 Strada 第二卷出版于1647年；这不表示战役发生于1647年。索引曾将 p.137 列在枢机条下，S3 已把该具名人物拆分为独立候选。

Chapter 5 (p. 137) mentions the campaigns in its account of Spanish patronage in Rome and dates the illustrated second volume to 1647; this does not date the campaigns to 1647. The index placed p. 137 under the cardinal entry, but S3 split the named campaign figure into a separate candidate.
