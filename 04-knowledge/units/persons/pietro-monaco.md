## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 索引主目和“activities as dealer”子目均指向 Pietro Monaco。第13章称他为版画师，并记其于1743年出版一部含55幅版画的集子；Haskell 称，其中相当比例的当代作品属于 Monaco，并认为他几乎可以肯定是在用版画宣传自己的经纪活动。第8章注释另提到 Monaco 于1763年出版的第42、51和89号版画。第13章注2还记一封致 John Strange、日期为1763/4年2月4日的信及17幅拟售作品。以上均按原书表述登记；中文名为工作音译。

**English:** The index main entry and its 'activities as dealer' sub-entry refer to Pietro Monaco. Chapter 13 calls him an engraver and says he published a collection of fifty-five prints in 1743. Haskell says a strikingly high proportion of the contemporary works reproduced belonged to Monaco and that he was almost certainly using the plates to advertise his dealer activity. Chapter 8's note also refers to Monaco's prints published in 1763, nos. 42, 51, and 89. Chapter 13 note 2 reports a letter to John Strange dated 4 February 1763/4 and a list of seventeen pictures offered for sale. These are recorded as the book's account; the Chinese display name is a working transliteration.
