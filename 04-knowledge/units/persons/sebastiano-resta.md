## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应索引词头“Resta, Padre Sebastiano”及第3、5、6、8章中的Sebastiano Resta。第3章称他为奥拉托会学者和收藏家，约1700年选定绘制一组椭圆圣经场景的艺术家；第6章称他为文艺复兴素描研究先驱，并引述其对风景画的意见。第5、8章注释分别引用他的书信。

**English:** This card represents the index entry “Resta, Padre Sebastiano” and Sebastiano Resta in chapters 3, 5, 6, and 8. Chapter 3 calls him an Oratorian scholar and collector and says that he selected artists for a group of oval biblical scenes around 1700. Chapter 6 calls him a pioneer in the study of Renaissance drawings and quotes his view of landscape painting. Notes in chapters 5 and 8 cite his letters.
