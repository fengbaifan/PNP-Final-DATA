## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔在第5章引用Roberto Longhi关于Cerquozzi与Velasquez归属问题的研究，并明确保留对该归属的怀疑；第20章又记载Longhi曾发表一幅Maffeo Barberini肖像候选。第8章以“R. Longhi”署名引用1956年关于Del Rosso收藏的文章；书目给出该文题名与页码，Fondazione Roberto Longhi档案目录将同一篇文章列在Roberto Longhi名下。本卡仅登记同一人物身份；文章未独立阅读，书中作品归属也未由身份匹配核定。 [来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_iii.md#L140)；[来源2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/20_CHP-20Postscript.md#L11)；[来源3](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i.md#L150)；[书目](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/21_CHP-21Bibliography.md#L707)

**English:** In chapter 5 Haskell cites Roberto Longhi’s discussion of Cerquozzi and the attribution to Velasquez, while explicitly retaining doubt about that attribution. Chapter 20 also records that Longhi published a candidate portrait of Maffeo Barberini. Chapter 8 cites a 1956 article on the Del Rosso collection as “R. Longhi”; Haskell’s bibliography supplies its title and pages, and the Fondazione Roberto Longhi archival inventory lists the same article under Roberto Longhi. This card records the identity match only; the article has not been independently consulted, and the book’s artwork attributions are not verified by this identity match.
