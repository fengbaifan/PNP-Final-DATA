### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔在第5章引用Roberto Longhi关于Cerquozzi与Velasquez归属问题的研究，并明确保留对该归属的怀疑；第20章又记载Longhi曾发表一幅Maffeo Barberini肖像候选。这里仅登记书中跨章指称的同一人物，不把其判断转写为本项目已确认的作品归属。 [来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_iii.md#L140)；[来源2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/20_CHP-20Postscript.md#L11)

**English:** In chapter 5 Haskell cites Roberto Longhi’s discussion of Cerquozzi and the attribution to Velasquez, while explicitly retaining doubt about that attribution. Chapter 20 also records that Longhi published a candidate portrait of Maffeo Barberini. This entry registers the same person named across chapters and does not treat either attribution as confirmed by this project.
