## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称 Francesco Fontebasso 是 Sebastiano Ricci 的学生，记其于1762年赴圣彼得堡并装饰冬宫；他也为威尼斯 Santa Maria Assunta 教堂绘制天花板壁画，并与 Gaetano Zompini 为 Zatta 的《但丁》插图本工作。上述活动及评价限于本书所述范围。中文名为项目工作音译。〔[第9章印刷页272；第10章印刷页297；第13章印刷页339；Markdown L54–55、340–341、75–76](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)〕

**English:** Haskell identifies Francesco Fontebasso as a pupil of Sebastiano Ricci and says that he went to St Petersburg in 1762 and decorated the Winter Palace. The book also attributes ceiling frescoes at Santa Maria Assunta in Venice to him and says he worked with Gaetano Zompini on Zatta's illustrated Dante edition. The activities and assessment remain limited to the book's account. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration.
