## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记《赞助人与画家》以Blessed Luigi Gonzaga、S. Luigi Gonzaga指称的人物。梵蒂冈2025年礼仪文本在同一祷文行中并列英文“Saint Aloysius (Gonzaga)”与意大利文“San Luigi (Gonzaga)”，据此将书中Luigi Gonzaga与Aloysius Gonzaga归为同一身份。中文显示名为项目工作音译；本书称号原样保留，不据此改写书内措辞。

**English:** This card records the person named in *Patrons and Painters* as Blessed Luigi Gonzaga and S. Luigi Gonzaga. A 2025 Holy See liturgical booklet places the English “Saint Aloysius (Gonzaga)” and Italian “San Luigi (Gonzaga)” in corresponding lines of the same litany; this supports treating the book's Luigi Gonzaga and Aloysius Gonzaga as one identity. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration, and the book's wording is preserved as written.

### 本书相关内容

**中文：** 本书称该人物为S. Ignazio左横堂一件浮雕所表现的主题，并以the Assumption of the Blessed Luigi Gonzaga称述；这里依原句表述为Luigi Gonzaga升天主题，不据此另造作品题名。第8章及第9章另以S. Luigi Gonzaga指称绘画与modello主题。相应S2关系候选留待S6，不在S4建立作品关系。

**English:** The book names him as the subject of the Assumption bas-relief in the left transept of S. Ignazio; Chapters 8 and 9 also use S. Luigi Gonzaga for the subjects of a painting and a modello. The S2 relation candidate remains for S6; no work relation is created in S4.
