**English:** Haskell describes Daniele Farsetti as Filippo Farsetti's cousin and an amateur painter. He once showed some of his pastels at the S. Rocco exhibition and later inherited and increased Filippo's collection. The family, collection, and patronage statements are retained as the book reports them.
