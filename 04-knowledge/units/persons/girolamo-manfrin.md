## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔在第17章的威尼斯艺术赞助与收藏叙述中，将吉罗拉莫·曼弗林描述为当地重要赞助者和收藏者；他记载曼弗林的画廊曾有四百余幅画作，并组织画家围绕其自拟题材举行竞赛。第20章后记称曼弗林藏品曾包括 Giorgione 的《暴风雨》，同时指出这些作品的来源仍所知甚少。本卡只记录原书叙述。

**English:** In his account of artistic patronage and collecting in Venice, Haskell describes Girolamo Manfrin as an important local patron and collector; he says Manfrin's gallery had held more than 400 pictures and that Manfrin organized competitions for painters around subjects he devised. The Chapter 20 postscript says the collection once included Giorgione's Tempesta while acknowledging that its sources remain poorly known. This card records the book's account only.

### 本书语境

第17章 pp.379–381 记述曼弗林在威尼斯的艺术赞助、收藏、其画廊和画家竞赛；Haskell 称其画廊有四百余幅画作，并称曼弗林曾为画家竞赛拟定题材。第20章 p.410 后记讨论其收藏散佚，称藏品一度包括 Giorgione 的《暴风雨》，并明确指出收藏来源仍所知甚少。

Chapter 17, pp. 379–381, discusses Manfrin's artistic patronage and collecting in Venice, his gallery, and competitions for painters; Haskell says the gallery held more than 400 pictures and that Manfrin devised the competition subjects. The Chapter 20 postscript, p. 410, discusses the dispersal of his collection, says it once included Giorgione's Tempesta, and explicitly notes that its sources remain poorly known.
