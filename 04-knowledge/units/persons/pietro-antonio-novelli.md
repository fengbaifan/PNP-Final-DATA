## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 皮埃特罗·安东尼奥·诺韦利是画家和插图设计者，参与帕斯夸利版戈尔多尼著作的插图设计；本书图版57b署其名。

**English:** Pietro Antonio Novelli was a painter and illustrator who designed illustrations for the Pasquali edition of Goldoni’s works; plate 57b in this book is attributed to him.
