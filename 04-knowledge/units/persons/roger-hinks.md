## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本KU对应Haskell所引Caravaggio专著的作者Roger Hinks。Rijksmuseum图书目录将作者规范形式列为“Hinks, Roger (Roger Packman)”，其书名、London出版地和[1953]出版年与Haskell书目及注释相合。本卡仅确认该书目责任身份，不扩展人物生平，也不声称已阅读该书。

**English:** This unit identifies Roger Hinks as the author of the Caravaggio monograph cited by Haskell. The Rijksmuseum library catalogue records the author form “Hinks, Roger (Roger Packman)”; its title, London publication place and [1953] date match Haskell’s bibliography and note. This card records the bibliographic author identity only; it adds no biography and does not claim the book was read.
