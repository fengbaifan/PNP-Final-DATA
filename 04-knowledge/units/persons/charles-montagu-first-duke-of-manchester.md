## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡将哈斯克尔第10章中的“Lord Manchester”与National Portrait Gallery记录的Charles Montagu、第一代曼彻斯特公爵对齐。NPG将其列为外交官，生卒约为1662–1722，并记载其曾任威尼斯大使及1708年带Marco Ricci和G. A. Pellegrini赴英。Haskell p.278将其使馆年份记为1709；此处保留各来源的原始时间表述，不用外部材料改写书内记录。身份比较及时间差异见过程记录。中文名为项目工作音译。

**English:** This card identifies the “Lord Manchester” in Haskell’s chapter 10 with Charles Montagu, 1st Duke of Manchester, in the National Portrait Gallery record. The NPG describes him as a diplomat, dates him to circa 1662–1722, records his Venice ambassadorships, and says he brought Marco Ricci and G. A. Pellegrini to England in 1708. Haskell’s p. 278 gives 1709 for the embassy; the sources’ dates are preserved separately, and the external record does not overwrite Haskell’s account. See the process record for the identity comparison and chronology difference.
