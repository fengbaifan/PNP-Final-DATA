**English:** This card records the same source-local person named Robert Adam. Chapter 10 lists him among the English visitors welcomed by Consul Smith; Chapter 13 says that during a passage through Venice in 1761 he entrusted Albrizzi with the sale of a book on Diocletian's palace. A Chapter 10 note and Appendix 5 also refer to a letter from James to Robert; neither the letter nor the cited publications was independently consulted. The book's exact title and publication status are not established, and no biography beyond these passages is added.
