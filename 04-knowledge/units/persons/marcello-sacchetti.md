## 内容

### 描述
**中文：** 马尔切洛·萨凯蒂是生于罗马、来自佛罗伦萨商人家族的银行家、商人和艺术赞助人。第一章以他为科尔托纳提供住处说明同乡保护；“佛罗伦萨人”在这里不能直接转写为出生于佛罗伦萨。[S1、S7]
**English:** Marcello Sacchetti was a Rome-born banker, merchant and patron from a Florentine mercantile family. Chapter 1 uses his provision of lodging for Pietro da Cortona to illustrate protection based on shared origins; “Florentine” should not be converted into a Florentine birthplace. [S1, S7]
