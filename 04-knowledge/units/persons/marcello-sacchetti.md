## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 佛罗伦萨赞助人，得知 Pietro 来自 Cortona 后称其为同乡并提供宫中住处；本段不证明其对科尔托纳的所有作品均有委托权。

**English:** The Florentine patron recognized Cortona as a compatriot and offered palace lodging. The passage does not establish control over all Cortona's works.
