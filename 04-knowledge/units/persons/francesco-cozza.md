## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔在第5章 p.131 以全名 Francesco Cozza 指称一名卡拉布里亚人，并记述同一批祭司也雇用他绘制一幅祭坛画。同段将 Cozza 置于与 Sassoferrato 的推荐、共同师承及交往叙述中；“probably”与“almost certainly”保留为原作者限定，四条关系候选均待 S6 逐项审查。Treccani《意大利人传记辞典》条目用于核对人物身份，不据此补入生卒、地点或作品事实。

**English:** On p. 131 of chapter 5, Haskell names Francesco Cozza, calls him a fellow-Calabrian, and says the same priests also employed him on an altar painting. The passage places Cozza in claims about recommending Sassoferrato, their shared pupilage, and their association; the author's qualifiers “probably” and “almost certainly” are retained, and all four relation candidates remain for individual S6 review. The Treccani Dizionario Biografico degli Italiani entry is used to compare identity only; no dates, places, or works are imported from it.
