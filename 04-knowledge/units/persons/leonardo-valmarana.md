## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Leonardo Valmarana 似在1757年为家族首领；第20章后记更正壁画委托者是 Conte Giustino Valmarana，并未否定第9章对 Leonardo 家族身份的这一谨慎说法。其人格描述来自葬礼演说，哈斯克尔明确提示该来源不适合做公正的人格评估。

**English:** Haskell says Leonardo Valmarana seems to have headed the family in 1757. The Chapter 20 postscript corrects the fresco commission to Conte Giustino Valmarana but does not retract the qualified statement about Leonardo's family role. Haskell attributes the character portrait to a funeral oration and warns that it is not an impartial source for character.

### 本书语境

第9章 p.258 的“似为家族首领”是嵌套转述；第20章 p.405 更正的是委托人身份，两者不是同一事实。

The Chapter 9 (p. 258) statement that he “seems” to have headed the family is nested reporting; the Chapter 20 (p. 405) correction concerns the commissioner, a separate fact.

### 类型记录

中文：姓名：原书写作 Leonardo Valmarana；家族身份只按第9章的限定陈述保留。

English: Name: the book writes Leonardo Valmarana; family status is retained only as the qualified Chapter 9 report.

### 身份边界

中文：不登记未经来源支持的出生、死亡、任职或完整谱系；不把家族首领身份改写为委托人身份。

English: Do not add unsupported birth, death, office, or genealogy; do not convert the family-head report into a commissioning role.
