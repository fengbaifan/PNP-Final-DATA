## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第14章所称Francesco Mancini。第351页在概述Algarotti为画廊提出的画家名单时称罗马除Pannini这一特殊情形外只有Mancini；书后索引以“Mancini, Francesco”指向该页。此处身份限于书内所指人物，不据同姓补充生平。中文显示名为项目工作音译。

**English:** This card records the Francesco Mancini named in Chapter 14. On page 351, while summarizing Algarotti's proposed painters for a gallery, Haskell says that in Rome there was only Mancini besides the special case of Pannini; the index identifies the page's Mancini as “Mancini, Francesco.” The record is limited to the person named in the book and adds no biography from the surname. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration.
