## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 意大利画家，受马扎然委托绘制巴黎宫殿上层画廊的穹顶壁画，本书图版26b复制其顶画局部。

**English:** An Italian painter commissioned by Mazarin to paint the vault of the upper gallery of his Paris palace; plate 26b reproduces a ceiling detail attributed to him.
