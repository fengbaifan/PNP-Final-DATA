### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应索引词头“Goya, Francisco”及第7、10章所称“young Goya”。第7章称Luca Giordano的影响后来为Goya所吸收；第10章注释称Corrado Giaquinto对年轻Goya的影响超过其他意大利画家。这里只记录原书的比较与影响判断，不作外部艺术史验证。

**English:** This card represents the index entry “Goya, Francisco” and “the young Goya” in chapters 7 and 10. Chapter 7 says Luca Giordano’s influence was later absorbed by Goya. A chapter 10 note says Corrado Giaquinto’s influence on the young Goya was greater than that of any other Italian painter. These comparisons and influence claims are recorded as Haskell’s statements, without external art-historical verification.
