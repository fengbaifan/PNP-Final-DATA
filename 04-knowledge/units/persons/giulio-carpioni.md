## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell附录中点名的Giulio Carpioni。第393页称，1789年12月10日的匿名拍卖中有若干据称来自Smith收藏的Carpioni画作；Haskell将该拍卖与John Strange联系。拍卖目录未查，书中也明确说归属不能视为定论。索引以“Carpioni, Giulio”列出该人物。中文名为本项目工作音译。 [第19章附录 p.393；OCR L150-151](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/19_CHP-19Appendix.md) [索引 C.csv#99](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/C.csv)

**English:** This card records Giulio Carpioni named in Haskell's appendix. Page 393 says that an anonymous sale on 10 December 1789, which Haskell identifies with John Strange, included pictures by Carpioni said to have come from Smith's collection. The sale catalogue was not consulted, and Haskell explicitly cautions that the attributions are not definitive. The index lists “Carpioni, Giulio.” The Chinese name is a working transliteration.
