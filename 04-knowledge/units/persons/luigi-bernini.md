## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 路易吉·贝尔尼尼是罗马的雕塑家、建筑师和工程技术人员。Treccani《意大利人传记辞典》记载他是 Pietro Bernini 之子、Gian Lorenzo Bernini 的弟弟和学生，并在多项雕塑及建筑工程中协助兄长。

**English:** Luigi Bernini was a sculptor, architect, and technical engineer active in Rome. Treccani’s *Dizionario Biografico degli Italiani* identifies him as the son of Pietro Bernini, the younger brother and pupil of Gian Lorenzo Bernini, and an assistant on several sculptural and architectural projects.
