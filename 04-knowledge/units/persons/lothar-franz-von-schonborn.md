## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记书后索引作“Schönborn, Lothar Franz von”、正文作“Lothar Franz von Schonborn”的第7章人物。Haskell引述一封信，其中Lothar称“Une Souzanne et la Pudiphar”颇合其意，并表示绘画可有裸体但不应淫秽或不雅，还评价Strudel擅长裸体形象。脚注将信件日期标为1708年10月10日并指向Hantsch und Schers第164页；原信及所引刊物未在本轮独立查阅。中文显示名为项目工作音译。

**English:** This card records the Chapter 7 figure indexed as “Schönborn, Lothar Franz von” and spelled “Lothar Franz von Schonborn” in the body. Haskell quotes a letter in which Lothar says that “Une Souzanne et la Pudiphar” would suit his taste, accepts nudity in paintings while excluding obscene or indecent treatment, and describes Strudel as especially capable with nude figures. The footnote dates the letter to 10 October 1708 and points to Hantsch und Schers, page 164; neither the letter nor that cited work was independently consulted here. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration.
