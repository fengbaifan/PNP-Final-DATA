**English:** The page image confirms “peasant life.” Haskell says De Wael dedicated the print set to Roomer; S6 records the dedicator and dedicatee separately, supported by a cataloged dedicatory inscription. Footnote 3 also reports a set in the British Museum; the relation view limits this to Haskell’s report and does not assert a current verified holding. Dedication does not establish the maker or engraver, nor does it imply a commission or ownership by Roomer.
