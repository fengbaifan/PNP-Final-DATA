### 本书相关语境

第8章以Haskell引述呈现Breval对Ferrara画作的评论；第9章两处引用其对Zaccaria Sagredo及其收藏的评价。Haskell所注Breval 1738年版第I卷p.230未独立查阅。第10章记述Breval访问Smith收藏；p.306称其所见小雕像“seemingly an Aesculapio”，续页p.307又称“Priapus”，这里保留两处书内称谓，不裁定雕像身份。[第8章 p.222](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md)（L132–133） [第9章 pp.263、266](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)（L234–236、304–305） [第10章 p.301](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L389） [第10章 pp.306–307](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L441、446）

Chapter 8 presents Breval’s comments on paintings in Ferrara as quoted by Haskell; Chapter 9 quotes two of his assessments of Zaccaria Sagredo and the collection. The cited 1738 edition of Breval, volume I, p. 230, was not independently consulted. Chapter 10 records his visit to Smith’s collection; p. 306 calls a statuette “seemingly an Aesculapio,” while the continuation on p. 307 calls it “Priapus.” Both labels are retained as they appear in the book, without deciding the object’s identity.
