### 描述

**中文：** 第14章印刷页348明确写出Giovanni Bottari全名，并称Algarotti到罗马后不久结识他、与他开始终身通信；同段将Bottari列为罗马一群学者与古物研究者中的成员，但没有将该群体命名为学院或机构。第4、5、7、10、13章的姓氏提及主要处于书目引注语境，作为跨章对齐线索，不在此扩写为传记事实。相关人际及群体断言留待S6审查。中文名为本项目工作译名。（第14章，印刷页348，Markdown L21）

**English:** On p. 348, chapter 14 gives Giovanni Bottari's full name and says that Algarotti met him soon after arriving in Rome and began a lifelong correspondence with him. The same passage places Bottari among a group of scholars and antiquarians in Rome but does not name that group as an academy or institution. Surname mentions in chapters 4, 5, 7, 10, and 13 are chiefly in bibliographic citations; they serve as cross-chapter alignment traces and are not expanded here into biographical claims. The person and group assertions await S6 review. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration. (第14章，印刷页348，Markdown L21)
