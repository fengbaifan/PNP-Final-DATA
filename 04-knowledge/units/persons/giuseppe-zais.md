**English:** The book’s index lists “Zais, Giuseppe.” Chapter 9 p. 261 calls him a landscape painter, and Chapter 10 p. 328 again uses his full name. Appendix 5 p. 393 also names him but leaves open whether the relevant painting activity began before or after 1762. This card preserves the source’s chronological uncertainty and does not turn the question into a settled commission, ownership, or sale fact. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration.
