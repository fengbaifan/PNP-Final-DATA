## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 希皮奥内·卡法雷利·博尔盖塞是保禄五世的外甥、枢机与艺术收藏者，其收藏与建筑赞助构成博尔盖塞画廊的重要早期基础。第一章第 4 页与 Domenichino 争执的 Borghese 枢机，经同书索引可具名为他；第 17 页头部肖像的具体版本仍须另核。（S1–S2、S5、S8）

**English:** Scipione Caffarelli-Borghese was a nephew of Paul V, a cardinal and an art collector whose collecting and architectural patronage formed an important early foundation of the Galleria Borghese. The book's index identifies him as the Cardinal Borghese in the dispute with Domenichino on page 4. The specific portrait version mentioned on page 17 still requires separate identification. (S1–S2, S5, S8)
