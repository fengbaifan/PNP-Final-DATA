## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 拉维尼娅·阿尔贝尔加蒂（Lavinia Albergati）是Orazio Ludovisi之妻，也是枢机Ludovico Ludovisi和Niccolò Ludovisi的母亲。三篇Treccani人物传记分别支持婚姻及母子关系；目前未识别出可确认的独立Wikipedia或Wikidata身份记录。（S1–S3）

**English:** Lavinia Albergati was the wife of Orazio Ludovisi and the mother of Cardinal Ludovico Ludovisi and Niccolò Ludovisi. Three Treccani biographies support these marital and parent-child links; no matching standalone Wikipedia or Wikidata identity record was identified. (S1–S3)
