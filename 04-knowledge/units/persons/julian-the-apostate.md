## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第4章称Cassiano所藏图稿序言可参照1630年在巴黎重印的Julian the Apostate作品集，并由哈斯克尔转述Petau序言为阅读Julian辩护的理由；第7章又称Verrio在Hampton Court的壁画题材取自Julian the Apostate的著作。第4章相关陈述是哈斯克尔对Petau序言的概述，本卡不写成Petau原文已独立核实。 [来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md#L127)；[来源2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_iv.md#L59)

**English:** Chapter 4 says Cassiano's proposed preface to his drawing volumes could use the preface to a 1630 Paris edition of Julian the Apostate's works, and Haskell summarizes Petau's defense of reading Julian. Chapter 7 says the subject of Verrio's Hampton Court fresco was drawn from Julian's writings. The chapter 4 statement is Haskell's summary of Petau's preface and is not presented as independently verified from Petau's text. [来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md#L127)；[来源2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_iv.md#L59)
