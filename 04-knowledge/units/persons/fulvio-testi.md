## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 富尔维奥·泰斯蒂（1593–1646）是服务于埃斯特宫廷的诗人、外交官和宫廷官员，也从事教育及文书工作。第一章引用他致弗朗切斯科·丰塔纳伯爵的信讨论贝尔尼尼的报酬；刊引信件还显示他与贝尔尼尼的交往及对画家、雕塑家才能的评价。各类角色并存，不将其仅归为诗人，也不因接受作品礼物就称其为艺术赞助人。

**English:** Fulvio Testi (1593–1646) was a poet, diplomat and court official in Este service, with additional educational and documentary duties. Chapter 1 uses his letter to Count Francesco Fontana to discuss Bernini's remuneration. The published letter also records his dealings with Bernini and his appraisal of Bernini's abilities as a painter and sculptor. These roles coexist; receiving gifts of art alone does not establish artistic patronage.
