### 本书相关语境

第3章在1680年Oliva召Pozzo赴罗马的叙述后，记录公爵曾召回Pozzo装饰未具名画廊，并向教会当局交涉；第7章在西班牙王位继承战争背景下称Victor Amadeus of Savoy脱离Louis XIV的控制，并叙述他与Juvarra推动Turin艺术赞助。索引将页89、200、202列入同一人物项；对书中九条关系候选仍分别保留其对象、限定和未决端点。[第3章 p.89](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md)（L125–135） [第7章 p.200](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_v.md)（L7–10） [第7章 p.202](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_v.md)（L59–60）

Chapter 3 records the Duke’s recall of Pozzo to decorate an unnamed gallery after Oliva’s 1680 summons to Rome, followed by appeals to church authorities. Chapter 7 describes Victor Amadeus of Savoy breaking from Louis XIV during the War of the Spanish Succession and his and Juvarra’s role in Turin’s artistic patronage. The index places pp. 89, 200, and 202 under one person entry; nine relation candidates retain their individual objects, qualifications, and unresolved endpoints.
