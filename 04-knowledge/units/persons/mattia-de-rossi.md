**English:** This card registers Mattia de’ Rossi under the index and chapter 3, pp.87 and 89 forms. The existing Carlo de’ Rossi card is a different person. The 1676 letter’s “Mattia di Rossi” and “Derossi” forms are mapped here to Mattia de’ Rossi, supported by the Getty ULAN name variants; this identity decision does not verify the letter’s request.
