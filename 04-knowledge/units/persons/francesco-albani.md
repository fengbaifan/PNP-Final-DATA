## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 1639 年一个委托获 1000 lire 中 450 作为定金；同年 Gozadini 为其 Bologna Chiesa de’ Servi 祭坛画承诺画布和群青。两例是否同一合同本章未证，不合并金额。

**English:** One 1639 commission brought an advance of 450 out of 1,000 lire. Gozadini also promised materials for Albani's Servi altarpiece that year. The chapter does not prove these were the same contract, so their terms are not combined.
