## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 在第14章将 Prospero Pesci 与 Mauro Tesi 并列为 Algarotti 于 Bologna 遇到并雇用的艺术家，称 Algarotti 常让他们依据自己的草图作画。第357页注7另列一封1759年9月28日致 Prospero Pesci 的信。此卡仅记录书内姓名和活动语境；中文名为工作音译，不补写生卒、完整生平或外部身份。[第14章书内来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/14_CHP-14_intro.md)

**English:** In Chapter 14 Haskell names Prospero Pesci alongside Mauro Tesi as an artist whom Algarotti encountered and employed in Bologna, often directing them to work from his sketches. Note 7 on page 357 also cites a letter to Prospero Pesci dated 28 September 1759. This card records only the name and context supplied by the book; the Chinese name is a working transliteration, and no dates, full biography, or external identity are added.
