## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记加布里埃尔-朱尔·德·科斯纳克（Gabriel-Jules de Cosnac，伯爵、历史学家）。Haskell第21章书目以“Comte Gabriel-Jules de Cosnac”列出《Les richesses du Palais Mazarin》（Paris, 1885）的责任者；BnF人物权威记录给出其规范名、生卒年月日及地点，并列出“Jules de Cosnac”为被拒姓名形式。中文名为项目工作音译。该书的具体作者关系和书内引用关系留待S6，不由书目形式直接写成正式边。[第21章书目 p.420](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/21_CHP-21Bibliography.md)（L380） [BnF人物权威记录 FRBNF12982918](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb12982918p)

**English:** This card records Gabriel-Jules de Cosnac (Comte; historian). Haskell’s Chapter 21 bibliography lists “Comte Gabriel-Jules de Cosnac” as the responsibility form for *Les richesses du Palais Mazarin* (Paris, 1885). The BnF person authority record gives the authorized name, full life dates and places, and lists “Jules de Cosnac” as a rejected form. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration. The specific author relation to the book and its cited passages remains for S6 review; the bibliographic form is not turned directly into a formal edge.
