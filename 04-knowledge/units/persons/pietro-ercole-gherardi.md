### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录《赞助人与画家》索引所列的 Pietro Ercole Gherardi。第10章p.302注4给出全名；第9章和第13章多处以缩写P. E. Gherardi称呼此人。Haskell称其为Muratori的摩德纳友人，并将一篇Descrizione及多封书信与他相关联；这些作品和通信主张仍按原书证据范围保留，不扩写为独立核实的关系或作品记录。中文显示名为项目工作音译。[全书索引 G.csv#62](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/G.csv) [第9章 p.261 注8](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)（L408） [第9章 pp.271、275注](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md)（L103、L127） [第10章 p.302 注4](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L598） [第13章 p.336](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md)（L41–50、L202）

**English:** This unit records Pietro Ercole Gherardi as listed in the index of *Patrons and Painters*. Chapter 10, p. 302 n. 4, gives his full name; Chapter 9 and Chapter 13 also refer to him as P. E. Gherardi. Haskell describes him as a Modenese friend of Muratori and associates him with a *Descrizione* and several letters. These work and correspondence claims remain within the book’s evidence and are not expanded into independently verified relations or work records. The Chinese display name is a project working transliteration.
