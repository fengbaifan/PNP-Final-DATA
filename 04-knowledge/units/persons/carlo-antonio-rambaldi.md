## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell在第8章称Carlo Antonio Rambaldi为博洛尼亚画家，并记载Raimondo Buonaccorsi于1707年召他与Antonio Dardani绘制长廊拱顶的《埃涅阿斯神化》壁画。原文记录的是受召绘制的任务，不独立证明壁画由Rambaldi完成。

**English:** In chapter 8, Haskell names Carlo Antonio Rambaldi as a Bolognese artist and says Raimondo Buonaccorsi summoned him with Antonio Dardani in 1707 to paint the Apotheosis of Aeneas fresco on the gallery vault. This records the assigned task and does not independently establish that Rambaldi completed the fresco.
