## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔在第20章和第21章书目中以全名提及的Vittorio Casale。第20章称有一部Casale版论著并报告其发现审查意见；第21章将Casale列在Treviso 1973年版的编校信息中。这些是哈斯克尔的书内记载，未独立核查。中文姓名为工作音译。[第20章 p.400](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/20_CHP-20Postscript.md#L48)；[第21章书目 p.432](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/21_CHP-21Bibliography.md#L906)

**English:** This card records Vittorio Casale, named in full in Haskell’s chapter 20 discussion and chapter 21 bibliography. Chapter 20 reports an edition associated with Casale and a subsequent discovery of censor’s comments; chapter 21 lists Casale in the editorial statement for a 1973 Treviso edition. These are reports in Haskell’s book and have not been independently checked. The Chinese name is a working transliteration. [第20章 p.400](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/20_CHP-20Postscript.md#L48); [第21章书目 p.432](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/21_CHP-21Bibliography.md#L906)
