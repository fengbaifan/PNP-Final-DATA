### 描述

**中文：** 热那亚出身画家，与后半世纪 modello 的普及关联；“可能引入”不是确定发明。Gesù 工期约定八年；全身肖像 100 scudi 为章中另一事例。

**English:** The Genoese painter is associated with later use of modelli; possible introduction is not proven invention. Eight years were allowed for the Gesù project, while a 100-scudi full-length portrait is a separate example.
