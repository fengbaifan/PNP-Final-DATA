## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 书中索引以 Giovanni Battista Agucchi 登录此人；第2章称其为 Monsignor Agucchi，记其任 Secretary of State，并称其亦为 Bolognese。哈斯克尔叙述他在1607至1615年退出公职期间草拟艺术理论；第6章又将他列为 Ideal 概念的先行阐述者之一。第20章后记以 Giambattista Agucchi 再称此人。以上限于原书陈述；中文名为工作音译。

**English:** The index identifies this person as Giovanni Battista Agucchi. Chapter 2 calls him Monsignor Agucchi, says he held the office of Secretary of State, and describes him as also a Bolognese. Haskell says he sketched out a theory of art during his retirement from public life between 1607 and 1615; Chapter 6 names him among the pioneers who had elaborated the concept of the Ideal. The Chapter 20 postscript also calls him Giambattista Agucchi. These details are limited to the book's account; the Chinese display name is a working transliteration.
