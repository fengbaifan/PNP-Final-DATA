## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 索引列“Vallisnieri, Antonio”；第15章注2称1772年Boscovich致Vallisnieri的信于1811年刊行。Treccani区分1661–1730年的父亲与其子Antonio（1708–1777），故此处采用junior避免混同；原书未写收信人全名。

**English:** The index lists “Vallisnieri, Antonio”; Chapter 15 n. 2 cites a 1772 letter from Boscovich to Vallisnieri, published in 1811. Treccani distinguishes the father (1661–1730) from his son Antonio (1708–1777), so this card uses junior; Haskell does not give the recipient’s full name.
