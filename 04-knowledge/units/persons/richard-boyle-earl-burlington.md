**English:** Haskell refers to this person as “Lord Burlington” in Chapters 10 and 14, discussing Ricci’s employment in England, Burlington’s later architectural taste and works attributed to him, and plans for the Chiswick house. The National Portrait Gallery identifies this form as Richard Boyle, 3rd Earl of Burlington and 4th Earl of Cork (1694–1753), and describes him as an architect and patron of the arts.
