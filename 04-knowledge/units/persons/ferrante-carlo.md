## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第5章介绍 Ferrante Carlo（索引作 Carlo, Ferrante）：他于1578年生于帕尔马，是律师、诗人，并先后担任两位枢机的秘书；他热衷绘画收藏，与多位艺术家交往并委托作品。第20章后记转述 Nicholas Turner 的研究，指出 Bellori 关于 Lanfranco 的若干著名评论很大程度上源自 Ferrante Carlo 的写作。本卡仅记录原书叙述。

**English:** Chapter 5 identifies Ferrante Carlo (indexed as Carlo, Ferrante): born in Parma in 1578, he was a lawyer and poet who served successively as secretary to two cardinals; he was devoted to collecting pictures, associated with leading artists, and commissioned works. The Chapter 20 postscript reports Nicholas Turner's finding that some of Bellori's famous comments on Lanfranco were closely derived from Ferrante Carlo's writing. This card records the book's account only.

The bilingual display names appear in the card title. The Chinese name is a transliteration or descriptive rendering for readability; the book's wording is preserved in the source quotations.

### 本书语境

第5章 pp.123–124 将 Ferrante Carlo 置于十七世纪艺术赞助与交易语境，记其律师、诗人和秘书经历、对绘画的兴趣、与 Cassiano dal Pozzo 及艺术家的交往、委托和收藏。第20章 p.402 后记明称其写作是 Bellori 若干 Lanfranco 评论的来源，并回指作者在第5章对该人物的叙述。

Chapter 5, pp. 123–124, places Ferrante Carlo in the context of seventeenth-century artistic patronage and dealing, recording his work as a lawyer, poet, and secretary, his interest in pictures, his contacts with Cassiano dal Pozzo and artists, and his commissions and collection. The Chapter 20 postscript, p. 402, explicitly names his writing as a source for some of Bellori's comments on Lanfranco and refers back to the account of him in Chapter 5.

Bellori, Lanfranco, the comments, and the works are not merged into this person's identity. The postscript is Haskell reporting Nicholas Turner, whose study was not independently consulted in this task.
