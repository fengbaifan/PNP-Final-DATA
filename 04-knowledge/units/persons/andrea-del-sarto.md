### 描述

**中文：** 第2章将Andrea del Sarto列入Cardinal Barberini宫中旧大师绘画的举例；第9章又称Manin宫画廊藏有其绘画。第2章为集合层级描述，没有指明具体作品、数量或年代。本卡仅登记人物身份及书内语境。 [来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_i.md#L113)；[来源2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md#L108)

**English:** Chapter 2 includes Andrea del Sarto among the old masters whose paintings were represented in Cardinal Barberini's palace; chapter 9 also says that pictures by him were in the Manin palace gallery. The chapter 2 passage describes an aggregate and identifies no individual work, count, or date. This entry records the person and the contexts in the book.
