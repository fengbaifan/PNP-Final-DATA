## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 克洛德·梅朗是法国版画家，1598年生于阿布维尔，1688年卒于巴黎。本书图版17b列有他的文琴佐·朱斯蒂尼亚尼肖像。

**English:** Claude Mellan was a French engraver, born in Abbeville in 1598 and deceased in Paris in 1688. His portrait of Vincenzo Giustiniani appears in plate 17b.
