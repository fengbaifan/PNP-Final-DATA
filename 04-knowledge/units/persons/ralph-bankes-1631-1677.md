### 描述

**中文：** 17世纪收藏者拉尔夫·班克斯爵士，是杰罗姆·班克斯的兄长；馆方认为他可能委托了弟弟的肖像。

**English:** Sir Ralph Bankes was a seventeenth-century collector and Jerome Bankes’s elder brother; the museum considers him the probable commissioner of Jerome’s portrait.
