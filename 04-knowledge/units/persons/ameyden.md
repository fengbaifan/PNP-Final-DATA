## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 迪尔克·范·阿梅登（1586–1656），又称 Teodoro Ameyden，是出身低地国家、主要在罗马活动的法律工作者、札报作者和历史记录者。第一章引用他关于1642年罗马的记述；其政治代理身份与写作立场须与所记录事实分开判断。

**English:** Dirk van Ameyden (1586–1656), also known as Teodoro Ameyden, was a legal practitioner, newsletter writer and historical chronicler from the Low Countries, active mainly in Rome. Chapter 1 cites his account of Rome in 1642; his political agency and authorial standpoint must be distinguished from the events he recorded.
