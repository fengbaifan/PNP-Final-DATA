## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔以 Angelo Querini 为威尼斯重要赞助人之一，叙述其对 Lodoli 的兴趣、政治活动及晚年文化与收藏生活。第15章将他置于 Alticchiero 别墅和园林的语境；这些评价和经历均按原书叙述登记，不作外部核验。

**English:** Haskell presents Angelo Querini as an important Venetian patron and recounts his interest in Lodoli, political activity, and later cultural and collecting life. Chapter 15 places him in the context of the villa and gardens at Alticchiero; these characterizations and events are recorded as the book's account, not as externally verified facts.
