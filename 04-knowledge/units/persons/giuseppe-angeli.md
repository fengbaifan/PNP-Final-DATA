## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 朱塞佩·安杰利是威尼斯画家，曾负责皮亚泽塔作坊；图版48a的科尔内尔肖像版画取自他的设计。

**English:** Giuseppe Angeli was a Venetian painter who directed Piazzetta’s workshop; the Corner portrait print in plate 48a is after his design.
