## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔明确将十八世纪初的萨克森选帝侯身份与后来波兰国王 Augustus III 相连，并记其三度访问威尼斯及在德累斯顿汇集 Rosalba Carriera 作品。第10章称 Augustus III 为 Augustus II 之子、1733年继位者，并述其购藏；第14章记 Francesco Algarotti 曾为萨克森选帝侯 Augustus 工作，随后为国王购画。本描述仅按原书叙述保留名称与时间语境，不补写外部生平。

**English:** Haskell explicitly identifies the early-eighteenth-century Prince Elector of Saxony with the later Augustus III of Poland, noting three visits to Venice and his collection of Rosalba Carriera's works in Dresden. Chapter 10 calls Augustus III the son of Augustus II and says he succeeded to the throne in 1733, then describes his collecting. Chapter 14 recounts Francesco Algarotti working for Augustus, Elector of Saxony, and later purchasing pictures for the King. This description follows the book's account and adds no external biography.
