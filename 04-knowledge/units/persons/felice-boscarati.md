**English:** This unit records Felice Boscarati as indexed. Chapter 10 identifies him as a local artist, says that after moving to Venice he was taken up by Giorgio Pisani as his painter, and describes his situation after Pisani’s disgrace. The Chapter 20 postscript names him again and reports his trial testimony through Loredana Olivato. The source layers and qualifications are retained as recorded in S2. The Chinese display name is a project working transliteration. ### 本书相关语境

第10章记述Boscarati为Lazzaro Riviera创作四幅寓意画，后来迁居威尼斯并被Pisani接纳为其画家；原文称“official painter”，但S2已限定不能据此推定正式薪职。第20章后记关于绘画政治风波的叙述，包含Haskell对Olivato文章及Boscarati审讯证词的转述，相关材料并未独立查阅。正文及脚注中的委托、展示、亲近关系和观点仍按各条S2陈述限定。[第10章 pp.326–327](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_sec_ii.md)（L216–239） [第20章后记 p.408](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/20_CHP-20Postscript.md)（L174–185）

Chapter 10 says Boscarati painted four allegorical pictures for Lazzaro Riviera, later moved to Venice, and was taken up by Pisani as his painter; Haskell’s phrase “official painter” is not treated as proof of a formal salaried post. The Chapter 20 postscript discusses the political controversy around the pictures and reports Olivato’s account of Boscarati’s trial testimony. The cited article and trial record were not independently consulted. Commissions, display, closeness, and opinions remain qualified in the individual S2 statements.
