## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第16章称 Abate Luigi Celotti 是一位活跃于威尼斯、巴黎与伦敦的艺术品经销者，常建立并解散收藏；报纸上常出现转售艺术品的 discreet announcement。哈斯克尔称他经营的对象包括法国大革命期间从梵蒂冈教皇礼拜堂圣咏书取出的彩饰细密画、古代大师作品及18世纪威尼斯绘画，并据其描述推断他兼有销售才能与独立趣味。书中列其收藏中的八幅 Canaletto、Sebastiano Ricci 牧歌场景及 Tiepolo 素描。

**English:** Chapter 16 describes Abate Luigi Celotti as an art dealer active in Venice, Paris, and London who repeatedly assembled and dispersed collections; newspaper notices often preceded his offers of works for sale. Haskell says the material he handled included illuminated miniatures taken from the Papal Chapel's Choral Books in the Vatican during the French Revolution, old masters, and eighteenth-century Venetian paintings, and infers from his descriptions both salesmanship and individual taste. The book lists eight Canalettos, a pastoral scene by Sebastiano Ricci, and Tiepolo sketches in his collection.
