## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Carlo Cignani 为来自 Bologna 的画家，并记述其作品与委托跨越多个宫廷和城市。第7章称他曾为 Louis XIV 绘制两幅未具名画作，并记一项未继续推进的巴黎邀约；第8章叙述其 Bergamo 教堂中殿委托谈判未果，以及1692年再次受邀时他正绘制 Forlì 主教座堂穹顶。第10章记 Johann Wilhelm 委托他在 Bologna 与 Rome 工作，并提及 Joseph Smith 收藏中的画作与纸本作品；第13章称 Pasquali 于1749年出版记录 Smith 收藏的 Cignani 与 Ricci 作品的图书。

**English:** Haskell identifies Carlo Cignani as a painter from Bologna and discusses his work and commissions across several courts and cities. Chapter 7 says that he painted two unidentified pictures for Louis XIV and records a Paris approach that was not pursued. Chapter 8 recounts the failed negotiation for the Bergamo church nave commission and says that, when approached again in 1692, he was painting the cupola of Forlì Cathedral. Chapter 10 records Johann Wilhelm commissioning him to work in Bologna and Rome and mentions paintings and drawings by him in Joseph Smith's collection; Chapter 13 says Pasquali published a 1749 book recording works by Cignani and Ricci in that collection.
