### 描述

**中文：** J. 托米尔森·希尔是美国收藏家，2019年购得图卢兹《朱迪斯》；该画的卡拉瓦乔归属仍有争议。

**English:** J. Tomilson Hill is an American collector who acquired the Toulouse Judith in 2019; the painting’s attribution to Caravaggio remains disputed.
