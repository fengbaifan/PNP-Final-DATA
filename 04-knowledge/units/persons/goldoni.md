## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 卡洛·戈尔多尼是帕斯夸利版《喜剧集》的作者；《赞助人与画家》图版57b采用该版第二卷的卷首图。

**English:** Carlo Goldoni is the author of the Pasquali edition of The Comedies; plate 57b in Patrons and Painters reproduces the frontispiece to its second volume.
