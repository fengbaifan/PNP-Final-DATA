## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 乔瓦尼·加斯帕雷·巴尔多伊诺是尼斯画家兼工程师，约1590年生、1669年卒，法语资料作Jean-Gaspard Baudoin。研究文献将他与毛里齐奥·迪·萨伏依的保护关系联系起来；本章以Gio. Gasparo Baldoini之名引用其“本府画家”任命。姓名异文、同一保护人及职业语境支持这一身份对应，但尚未取得人物的Wikipedia—Wikidata配对。（S1–S3）

**English:** Giovanni Gaspare Baldoino, also known as Jean-Gaspard Baudoin, was a painter and engineer from Nice, born around 1590 and deceased in 1669. Scholarship identifies him as a protégé of Maurice of Savoy; this chapter cites his appointment as “our painter” under the name Gio. Gasparo Baldoini. Name variants, patron and occupation support the identification, while a matching Wikipedia–Wikidata identity pair remains unconfirmed. (S1–S3)
