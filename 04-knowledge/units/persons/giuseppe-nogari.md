## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称 Giuseppe Nogari 是画家，以受北方画家、尤其伦勃朗影响的‘teste di fantasia’半身想象肖像闻名，并记其为 Marco Foscarini 绘制肖像。书中还记述 Schulenburg 收藏 Nogari 的作品并委托其绘制多件寓意画。以上均为书内记载；中文名为项目工作音译。〔[第9章印刷页200；第10章印刷页316；Markdown L204–206、116–117](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_sec_ii.md)〕

**English:** Haskell identifies Giuseppe Nogari as a painter known for ‘teste di fantasia’, imaginary half-length portraits influenced by Northern artists, especially Rembrandt. The book also reports that he painted a portrait of Marco Foscarini and that Schulenburg collected and commissioned works by him. These statements are recorded as the book's account. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration. ([第9章印刷页200；第10章印刷页316；Markdown L204–206、116–117](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_sec_ii.md))
