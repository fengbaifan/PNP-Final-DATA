## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第4章记 Cassiano dal Pozzo 与 Galileo 的交往，并称 Cassiano 在 Galileo 受宗教裁判所判决约一年后仍以友好口吻致信；Galileo 回信感谢 Cassiano 将其肖像纳入藏书。该章还称 Ludovico Cigoli 是 Galileo 偏爱的画家及偶尔合作对象，并呈现 Galileo 对绘画风格的看法。Haskell 对科学态度与审美的联系属于作者解释；第3章与第10章另有对 Galileo 的提及，均按原书语境限定。

**English:** Chapter 4 recounts Galileo's contact with Cassiano dal Pozzo: about a year after Galileo's condemnation by the Inquisition, Cassiano wrote to him in a friendly spirit, and Galileo thanked Cassiano for including his portrait in the library. The chapter also identifies Ludovico Cigoli as Galileo's favourite painter and an occasional collaborator, and presents Galileo's views on painting. Haskell's connection between a scientific outlook and aesthetic taste remains the author's interpretation. Chapters 3 and 10 contain further references to Galileo, retained within their source contexts.
