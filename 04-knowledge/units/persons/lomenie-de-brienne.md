## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 全书索引以 “Brienne, Lomenie de” 列出人物；第7章正文称其为宫廷侍臣 Loménie de Brienne，并在同一连续叙述中多次以 Brienne 指称。参考书目给出其回忆录作者的完整姓名形式 “Louis-Henri de Loménie Comte de Brienne”。本卡据同书正文、索引和书目将这些姓名形式合并为一个来源人物；中文名为项目工作译名。[第7章 pp.181、184–186](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md)（L142、170–176、205–222） [参考书目](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/21_CHP-21Bibliography_intro.md)（L254–255） [全书索引 B.csv#299](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/B.csv)

**English:** The book’s index lists “Brienne, Lomenie de.” Chapter 7 identifies a courtier as Loménie de Brienne and repeatedly refers to Brienne in the same continuous account. The bibliography gives the full name form “Louis-Henri de Loménie Comte de Brienne” for the author of his memoirs. This card consolidates the forms under one source-local person identity using the book’s body text, index, and bibliography. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration.
