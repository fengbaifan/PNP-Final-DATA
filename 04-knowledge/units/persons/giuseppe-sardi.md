### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录索引S.csv#48的Giuseppe Sardi。第9章p.248称S. Maria del Giglio立面由Antonio Barbaro设计、由Sardi于1675至1683年间建造；p.269另称Longhena与Sardi受聘建造Scalzi新教堂，但没有拆分二人的职责。本卡不把建造角色改写为设计，也不推定具体共同分工。两条直接关系候选均留待S6。中文名为项目工作音译。

**English:** This card records Giuseppe Sardi, indexed at S.csv#48. Chapter 9, p. 248, distinguishes Antonio Barbaro as the designer of the S. Maria del Giglio façade and Sardi as its builder between 1675 and 1683. On p. 269, Haskell also says Longhena and Sardi were employed to build the new Scalzi church, without dividing their roles. This card does not recast the builder as designer or infer a more specific division of work. Both direct relation candidates remain for S6. The Chinese display name is a project working transliteration.
