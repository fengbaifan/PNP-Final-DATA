**中文：** 本卡对应全书索引中的 Domenico Passignani。第2章称Maffeo在Barocci年长后转而选用同乡Passignani，并叙述其提出穹顶装饰方案、后来受委托绘制穹顶壁画，以及绘制 lunettes 中的《圣母生平》场景。以上均保留为Haskell的书内叙述；本卡不补定被动句中未说明的委托方，也不把建议方案视为已实施。[全书索引 P.csv#57](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/P.csv) [第2章 p.26](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_i.md)（L58–61）

**English:** This unit corresponds to Domenico Passignani in the index. Chapter 2 says that Maffeo turned to his compatriot Passignani after Barocci was considered too old, and recounts his proposal for the cupola, his later commission to paint its frescoes, and his paintings and frescoes of Scenes from the Life of the Virgin in the lunettes. These remain Haskell’s book statements. The card does not identify the unnamed agent in the passive commission clause or treat the proposal as executed.
