## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Bernini 一度失去最受青睐雕塑家的地位，Alessandro Algardi 随之获得重要教皇委托；Algardi 制作 Innocent X 与 Donna Olimpia 的胸像，并参与圣彼得大教堂及教皇家族别墅工程。第7章叙述他在 Pamfili 宫廷中的突出地位及 Mazarin 于1648年延揽他的尝试；第15章将 Querini 所藏一件 Hercules 归于 Algardi。

**English:** Haskell says Bernini temporarily lost his place as the most favoured sculptor and Alessandro Algardi received important papal commissions. Algardi made busts of Innocent X and Donna Olimpia and received work at St Peter's and the papal family's villa. Chapter 7 recounts his prominent place at the Pamfili court and Mazarin's 1648 attempt to recruit him; Chapter 15 attributes a Hercules in Querini's collection to Algardi.
