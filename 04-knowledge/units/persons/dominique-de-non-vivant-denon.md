**English:** On p. 371 of Chapter 15, Haskell first names the person Dominique De Non and immediately states that he later became known as Dominique Vivant-Denon. Haskell describes him as an engraver and collector and recounts his roughly five-year stay in Venice, an engraving of Voltaire made at Ferney, and work on Abbé de Saint-Non’s travel book. This card records the two forms used in the book as one person and adds no birth name, life dates, or external biography.
