### 本书相关内容

**中文：** Haskell称Torelli于1645年由帕尔马公爵派往巴黎，此前曾为该公爵工作；其布景和新技术比所服务的歌剧吸引更多注意。第183页称他在Fronde期间财务破产并被监禁数月；此为Haskell书内叙述。脚注4引用Prunières 1913年著作第149页，未独立查阅。四条S2关系候选留待S6，不登记正式关系。

**English:** Haskell says Torelli was sent to Paris by the Duke of Parma in 1645, having worked for him, and that Torelli’s scenery and techniques attracted more attention than the operas they served. Page 183 says he was financially ruined and imprisoned for months during the Fronde; this remains Haskell’s account. Note 4 cites Prunières (1913), page 149, which was not independently consulted. All four S2 relation candidates remain for S6; no formal relation is added.
