## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔第八章注2所述的委托人。该注称他为神父，并称他在画作完成后违约、撤回赠画意图；此卡仅标识书内人物，不延伸至外部身份或生平。

**English:** The commissioner described in Haskell’s Chapter 8, note 2. Haskell calls him a priest and reports that, after the picture was finished, he defaulted and withdrew his intention to give it; this card identifies the person within the book’s account only.
