### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应索引词头“Ciampelli, Agostino”及第2、3章中的Agostino Ciampelli。第2章称其受命为S. Bibiana绘制壁画，并将Pietro da Cortona与他在该教堂的工作联系起来；第3章再次列出该姓名，但OCR作“Ciampelh”。书内关系候选仍待S6审查；中文名为工作音译，OCR异文保留。

**English:** This card represents the index entry “Ciampelli, Agostino” and the Agostino Ciampelli references in chapters 2 and 3. Chapter 2 says he was commissioned to paint frescoes at S. Bibiana and places Pietro da Cortona in the same church context. Chapter 3 names him again, with the OCR reading “Ciampelh”. The source relation candidates remain for S6 review; the Chinese display name is a working transliteration and the OCR variant is retained.
