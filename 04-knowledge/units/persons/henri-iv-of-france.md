### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应索引词头“Henri IV of France”及第2、7章中的Henri IV／Henry IV。第2章记Maffeo Barberini祝贺其长子出生；第7章以Henry IV拼写引用其言论，并在Marie de Medici的艺术委托语境中再次称Henri IV。索引所列跨页与正文姓名及称谓相合。

**English:** This card represents the index heading “Henri IV of France” and Henri IV/Henry IV in chapters 2 and 7. Chapter 2 recounts Maffeo Barberini’s visit to congratulate him on the birth of his eldest son; chapter 7 uses the Henry IV spelling in a reported saying and names Henri IV again in the context of Marie de Medici’s artistic commissions. The index locators and body references support one cross-chapter identity.
