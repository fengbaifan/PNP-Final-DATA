### 描述

**中文：** 舞台设计者，为《拉丁君权凯旋》设计场景，其《极乐世界》由屈塞尔刻成版画。

**English:** A stage designer for La Monarchia Latina Trionfante whose Elysian Fields design was engraved by Küsel.
