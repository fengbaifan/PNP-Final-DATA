## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 毛里齐奥·迪·萨伏依是萨伏依王朝亲王、外交与政治活动者、曾任枢机，也是艺术和文学的保护人。他在罗马和都灵组织文艺活动，1642年退出枢机身份并与侄女Ludovica／Luisa Cristina成婚；本章引用其任命巴尔多伊诺为“本府画家”的例子。任用原件日期仍未核定。（S1、S4–S8）

**English:** Maurice of Savoy was a dynastic prince, diplomat, political figure, former cardinal, and patron of the arts and letters. He organized cultural activity in Rome and Turin, relinquished his cardinalate in 1642 and married his niece Ludovica, also known as Luisa Cristina. The chapter cites his appointment of Baldoino as his painter; the original appointment date remains unverified. (S1, S4–S8)
