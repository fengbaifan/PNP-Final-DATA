## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第5章称 Carlo de’ Rossi 为 Salvator Rosa 在罗马的主要赞助人和银行家，也称其为有才华的音乐家、作曲家 Luigi 的兄弟。Haskell 记述 Rosa 曾将4000 scudi存入其处，De’ Rossi 按6%付息，并建立了 Rosa 作品的私人收藏；第6、7章继续使用其姓名叙述作品和收藏语境。所有内容均限于书内记载。

**English:** Chapter 5 identifies Carlo de’ Rossi as Salvator Rosa's principal patron in Rome and his banker, and calls him a talented musician and brother of the composer Luigi. Haskell reports that Rosa deposited 4,000 scudi with him, that De’ Rossi paid six per cent interest, and that he assembled a private collection of Rosa's works. Chapters 6 and 7 refer to him again in accounts of a painting and a collection; the card stays within the book's reports.
