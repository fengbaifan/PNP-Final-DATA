**English:** This card groups the index main entry “Baroni, Leonora” with the Chapter 4 body candidate “Leonora Baroni” as one cross-chapter person in the book. Chapter 4 calls her a singer and says Paolo Giordano arranged publication of poems written by admirers in her honour. Its note lists a 1639 publication at Bracciano, but the poetry collection was not independently consulted. Chapter 7, p.180 says that Mazarin chose the singer Leonora Baroni as his mistress and uses her success in discussing the rise of operatic stardom; p.181 says that Mazarin initially failed to bring her to Paris and calls her his former mistress. The relationship account and assessment of her success remain Haskell’s statements in the book and are not independently verified or expanded. This card does not register the collection, Milton, Paolo Giordano, or Mazarin as formal relations.
