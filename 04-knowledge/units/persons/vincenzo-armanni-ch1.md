## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 温琴佐·阿尔曼尼（1608–1684）是古比奥出身的法学研究者、秘书、历史与谱系编纂者及书信作者。他在致卡米洛·潘菲利的信中提出瓦尔蒙托内装饰题材，属于文献作者和学者，不能与近名画家Vincenzo Armanno混同。[S1–S4]

**English:** Vincenzo Armanni (1608–1684), from Gubbio, was a scholar of law, secretary, compiler of historical and genealogical materials, and letter writer. His letter to Camillo Pamfili proposes subjects for decoration at Valmontone. He is distinct from the similarly named painter Vincenzo Armanno. [S1–S4]
