## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 在第10章具名 Lazzaro Riviera，称他委托 Felice Boscarati 绘制四幅寓意画，以表现一种理想的哲学与教育体系；Haskell 又称 Riviera 发行了解释这些版画的小册子。注3将小册子指认为 La Educazione Virile，并称法文、意大利文两种版本于1773年在 Verona 出版；这两种小册子未在本轮独立查阅。此卡保存 Haskell 的归属，不补写外部生平。[S1][S2]

**English:** Haskell names Lazzaro Riviera in Chapter 10 and says that he commissioned Felice Boscarati to paint four allegorical pictures illustrating a kind of ideal philosophical and educational system. Haskell also says Riviera issued a pamphlet to explain the engravings. Note 3 identifies the pamphlets as La Educazione Virile and states that French and Italian versions were published in Verona in 1773; neither version was independently consulted in this pass. This unit preserves Haskell’s attribution and adds no external biography. [S1][S2]
