## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Giovanni Battista Pamphilj Aldobrandini（1648–1709）是Camillo Pamphilj和Olimpia Aldobrandini之子，继承家族产业与头衔，并延续父母发起的艺术项目。

**English:** Giovanni Battista Pamphilj Aldobrandini (1648–1709) was the son of Camillo Pamphilj and Olimpia Aldobrandini. He inherited the family estate and titles and continued artistic projects begun by his parents.
