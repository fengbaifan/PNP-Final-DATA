## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记书后索引作“Stazio, Andrea”、第9章脚注具名的Andrea Stazio。Haskell称他遗留近200达克特用于S. Stae的后续营建与装饰，部分款项用于合唱席画作；同一脚注明确说遗嘱没有说明是谁委托这些画作，故不将Stazio写作画作委托人。中文显示名为项目工作音译。

**English:** This card records Andrea Stazio, indexed as “Stazio, Andrea” and named in a Chapter 9 footnote. Haskell says he left nearly 200 ducats for further building and decoration at S. Stae, part of which was spent on choir pictures. The same note explicitly says the will does not identify who commissioned those pictures, so this card does not name Stazio as their commissioner. The Chinese display name is a project working transliteration.
