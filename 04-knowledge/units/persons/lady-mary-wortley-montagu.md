## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第10章以Mary Wortley Montagu具名，记述她认为Smith自述为她服务时的自我夸耀令人难以忍受；第14章又以Lady Mary Wortley Montagu具名，叙述Algarotti周旋于她与Lord Hervey的示好。两处姓名与称谓共同支持同一人物身份。 [来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md#L392)；[来源2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/14_CHP-14_intro.md#L25)

**English:** Chapter 10 names Mary Wortley Montagu and says she found Smith’s self-congratulation over a service to her overwhelming. Chapter 14 names Lady Mary Wortley Montagu while recounting Algarotti’s handling of her and Lord Hervey’s advances. The two forms refer to the same person in the book.
