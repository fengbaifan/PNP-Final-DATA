**English:** This card records the architect Agostino Barelli. Chapter 7, section IV, p.193, says that he came to Munich in 1662, built a church for the Theatines, and built the central block of Nymphenburg Palace; it does not attribute the entire palace to him. The index has two same-name subentries for these activities, both pointing to the person named in the passage. The Chinese name is a working transliteration.
