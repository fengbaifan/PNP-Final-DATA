## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 查尔斯·福克斯是马拉塔约1680年肖像的描绘对象；Mezzetti目录将他的生卒年记为1659–1713。本书图版31a记该画为伊尔切斯特伯爵所藏。

**English:** Charles Fox is the sitter in Maratta’s portrait of about 1680; Mezzetti’s catalogue gives his dates as 1659–1713. Plate 31a records the painting in the Earl of Ilchester’s collection.
