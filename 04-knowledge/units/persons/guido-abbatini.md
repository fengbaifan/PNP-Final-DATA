## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 圭多·乌巴尔多·阿巴蒂尼是从事绘画与马赛克的艺术家，短期师从朱塞佩·切萨里，后来与贝尔尼尼合作。本书图版9署名其为《巴贝里尼宫》卷首图作者。

**English:** Guido Ubaldo Abbatini was a painter and mosaicist who briefly studied under Giuseppe Cesari and later collaborated with Bernini. Plate 9 names him as the author of the frontispiece to Aedes Barberinae ad Quirinalem.
