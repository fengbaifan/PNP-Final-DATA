## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 安东尼·莫里斯·克拉克（1923–1976）是艺术史家、画家和收藏者，研究十八世纪罗马艺术，曾任大都会艺术博物馆欧洲绘画策展人。

**English:** Anthony Morris Clark (1923–1976) was an art historian, painter and collector specializing in eighteenth-century Rome, and a curator of European paintings at the Metropolitan Museum of Art.
