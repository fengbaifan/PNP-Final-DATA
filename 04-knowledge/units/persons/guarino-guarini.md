## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记书内具名人物 Guarino Guarini，中文名采用项目工作音译。Haskell称 Theatines 为重建圣安娜—皇家教堂召来本会成员 Guarini；他于1662年抵达并开始教堂工程，工程于1669年中断，约五十年后才完成。第7章后文又称 Adelaide Enrichetta 未能如愿吸引 Guarini。以上仅记录 Haskell 的叙述；未独立核验其生平或相关事件，约五十年的工程完成时间不换算为确切年份。[第7章 p.186](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md)（L219）；[第7章 p.193](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_iv.md)（L15）

**English:** This card records the person named Guarino Guarini in Haskell’s account; the Chinese form is a project transliteration. Haskell says that the Theatines summoned Guarini, a member of their order, for the rebuilding of the church of St Anne-la-Royale. He arrived in 1662 and began work on the church; the work was interrupted in 1669 and completed about fifty years later. A later passage says that Adelaide Enrichetta failed to attract him. These are Haskell’s reports; Guarini’s biography and the events have not been independently verified, and the approximate completion interval is not converted into a precise year. [第7章 p.186](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md)（L219）; [第7章 p.193](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_iv.md)（L15）

- 第7章 p.186 称 Theatines 召来本会成员 Guarini 参与重建圣安娜—皇家教堂；原文称其于1662年抵达，随后开始教堂工程。Haskell称工程于1669年中断，并“约五十年后”才完成；本卡保留约数，不推算完成年份。[第7章 p.186](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md)（L219）
