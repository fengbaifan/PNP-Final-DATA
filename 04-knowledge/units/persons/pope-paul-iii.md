## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第2章称Paul III曾召集特伦托会议，并记述Guglielmo della Porta为其制作的陵墓被移至圣彼得大教堂的另一壁龛；第4章再次以OCR形式“Paul Ill”提及该陵墓与Urban VIII陵墓相对。本条使用第2章明确的Paul III作为姓名依据，保留第4章OCR字形。 [来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_ii.md#L125)；[来源2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_ii.md#L128)；[来源3](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md#L55)

**English:** Chapter 2 says Paul III summoned the Council of Trent and describes Guglielmo della Porta’s tomb for him being moved to another niche in St Peter’s. Chapter 4 refers again to that tomb opposite the tomb of Urban VIII, with the OCR reading “Paul Ill.” This entry uses the clear form in chapter 2 as its name evidence and preserves the chapter 4 OCR reading.
