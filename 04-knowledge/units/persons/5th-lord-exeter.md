## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称第五代埃克塞特勋爵是大规模的绘画收藏者和赞助人；他在1680至1685年间两次赴意大利，并在所到城市委托绘画。第7章还叙述其跨城市购藏和委托活动，并称其对英国收藏史具有重要作用。

**English:** Haskell describes the 5th Lord Exeter as a collector and patron on a far larger scale. He visited Italy twice between 1680 and 1685 and commissioned paintings in every city he visited. Chapter 7 also recounts his collecting and commissions across Italian cities and calls his role in the history of English collecting highly significant.
