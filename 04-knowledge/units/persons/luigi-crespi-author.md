### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记书目中署名Luigi Crespi、并在注释中简称L. Crespi的作者。书目列有其1769年《Vite de’ Pittori bolognesi non descritte nella ‘Felsina Pittrice’》；哈斯克尔以L. Crespi p.211转述Giuseppe Maria Crespi在佛罗伦萨停留两年的说法，同时给出1709年最多八个月及Zanotti暗示多次到访的不同叙述。被引书页未独立查阅；本卡不把争议时长写作已核事实，也不与画家Giuseppe Maria Crespi合并。

**English:** This card records the author named Luigi Crespi in the bibliography and cited in notes as L. Crespi. The bibliography lists his 1769 *Vite de’ Pittori bolognesi non descritte nella ‘Felsina Pittrice’*. Haskell attributes to L. Crespi, p. 211, a two-year Florence stay for Giuseppe Maria Crespi, while also giving a maximum of eight months during 1709 and noting Zanotti's implication of multiple visits. The cited pages were not independently consulted; the disputed duration is not treated as verified fact, and this author is kept distinct from painter Giuseppe Maria Crespi.
