**English:** This card records the source-local person indexed as “Massimo, Padre” and named in the chapter as “Padre Massimo.” Haskell describes him as a Capuchin and a pupil of Brusasorci, and says that he had painted altar pictures. The historical full name is not supplied, and this record is not merged with other people named Massimo.
