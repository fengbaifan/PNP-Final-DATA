## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应索引词头“Rode, Bernhard”及第14章的Rode与Postscript中的Bernhard Rode。第14章称Algarotti为德国画家Rode提供介绍信，并预计他会受到Tiepolo影响；Postscript记录早期作者将一幅画归于Rode，同时转述Marianne Roland-Michel认为更可能出自意大利艺术家。作者归属争议按原文保留；相关书内关系候选留待S6审查。

**English:** This card represents the index entry “Rode, Bernhard” and the references to Rode in chapter 14 and Bernhard Rode in the Postscript. Chapter 14 says Algarotti provided an introduction for the German painter Rode and expected him to be influenced by Tiepolo. The Postscript reports an attribution to Bernhard Rode by earlier writers alongside Marianne Roland-Michel’s view that an Italian artist was more likely. The attribution dispute is retained as stated; the book’s relation candidates remain for S6 review.
