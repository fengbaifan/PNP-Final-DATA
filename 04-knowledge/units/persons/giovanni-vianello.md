## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔第16章称 Dr Giovanni Vianello 为 Chioggia Cathedral 的 canon，并将他可能属于 Sasso 与 della Lena 的交往圈明确保留为推测。书中记其于1793年刊行 Rosalba 日记版本、拥有200余幅绘画，并在1790年出版绘画目录；作者同时说其是否为画商尚不确定。第16章还记录 Vianello 收藏的 Francesco Guardi 作品及其对 Guardi 风格的评价。上述内容均按哈斯克尔的叙述、判断和限定登记。

**English:** In Chapter 16, Haskell identifies Dr Giovanni Vianello as a canon of Chioggia Cathedral and qualifies his possible membership in the circle of Sasso and della Lena as uncertain. The book says he brought out an edition of Rosalba's journal in 1793, owned over 200 pictures, and published a catalogue of his paintings in 1790; Haskell explicitly leaves uncertain whether he was a dealer. Chapter 16 also records works by Francesco Guardi in Vianello's collection and his assessment of Guardi's style. These details retain Haskell's narrative, judgements, and qualifications.
