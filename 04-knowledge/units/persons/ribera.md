### 描述

**中文：** 胡塞佩·德·里贝拉是画家，创作了本书图版32a所列、卡波迪蒙特博物馆收藏的《醉西勒诺斯》。

**English:** Jusepe de Ribera was a painter who created The Drunken Silenus, held by the Museo di Capodimonte and listed as plate 32a in the book.
