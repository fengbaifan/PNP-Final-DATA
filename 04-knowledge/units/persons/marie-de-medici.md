## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Marie de Medici 是亨利四世之妻，并认为她的宫廷活动推动意大利艺术影响进入巴黎。第7章记她召 Franz Pourbus the Younger 入巴黎、营建新宫，并于1621年委托 Rubens 为长廊绘制她与亨利四世生平的寓意场景；第2章也提及 Rubens 为二人创作的寓意组画。第7章关于意大利艺术家的论述称，唯一明确存世的她的委托是 Orazio Gentileschi 的《Public Felicity triumphant over Dangers》。以上均按原书的记述和范围保留。

**English:** Haskell identifies Marie de Medici as the wife of Henri IV and says her court helped Italian artistic influence take hold in Paris. Chapter 7 records her summoning Franz Pourbus the Younger to Paris, building a new palace, and commissioning Rubens in 1621 to paint allegorical scenes from her life and Henri IV's in its galleries; Chapter 2 also mentions Rubens's cycle for the couple. Within Chapter 7's discussion of Italian artists, Haskell says the only certain surviving commission from her is Orazio Gentileschi's Public Felicity triumphant over Dangers. The book's wording and scope are preserved here.
