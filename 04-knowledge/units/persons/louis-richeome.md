## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称 Louis Richeôme 为法国耶稣会士，并记述他于1611年写作一部献给 Aquaviva、涉及 S. Andrea 与 S. Vitale 绘画的书。第3章后续再次以 Richeôme 指称该人。此卡只登记书内具名人物身份；作者对其观点的概括和评价继续按原书发言者、语气及限定理解，不据此补写生卒或完整生平。[第3章书内来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)

**English:** Haskell identifies Louis Richeôme as a French Jesuit and says that in 1611 he wrote a book dedicated to Aquaviva concerning paintings in S. Andrea and S. Vitale. The continuation refers to the same person as Richeôme. This card registers only the named person in the book; Haskell's summaries and assessments of his views retain their original speaker and qualification, and no dates or full biography are added. [第3章书内来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)

### 姓名与身份

- 书中全名：Louis Richeôme。[第3章书内来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)
- 中文显示名：“路易·里肖姆”为工作音译，不声明为已核定通行译名。[第3章书内来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)
- 书内身份：法国耶稣会士；Haskell称其于1611年写作一部献给Aquaviva的书。原书此处未列书名。[第3章书内来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)
