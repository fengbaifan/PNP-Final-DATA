**English:** This card groups cand-1586 (index M.csv#125) and cand-1587 (index M.csv#126) as one person. Haskell uses “Elector Max Emmanuel” on p. 193 and p. 278, and “Maximilian of Bavaria” on p. 277 for the 1704 commission. Deutsche Biographie GND 11857941X lists Max Emanuel and Maximilian II. Emanuel under one authority record and records him as Elector of Bavaria from 1679. No matching person KU exists in the project, so one card receives both candidate mappings. The Chinese display name is a project working transliteration.
