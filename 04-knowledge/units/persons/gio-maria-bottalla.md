## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 乔万尼·马里亚·博塔拉是受朱利奥·萨凯蒂保护、接受科尔托纳指导的画家；本书列其《以扫与雅各相会》。

**English:** Giovanni Maria Bottalla was a painter protected by Giulio Sacchetti and placed under Pietro da Cortona’s guidance; the book lists his Meeting of Esau and Jacob.
