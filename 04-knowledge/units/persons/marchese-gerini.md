## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 安德烈亚·杰里尼是佛罗伦萨侯爵和艺术赞助人，曾支持朱塞佩·佐基；图版58a描绘他与安东·玛丽亚·扎内蒂。

**English:** Andrea Gerini was a Florentine marquess and art patron who supported Giuseppe Zocchi; plate 58a portrays him with Anton Maria Zanetti.
