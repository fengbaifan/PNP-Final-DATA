### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔在第4章称 Paganino Gaudenzi 为学者，记述 Cassiano dal Pozzo 于1647年请他寄来肖像，数周后又收到肖像副本。第4章注2将二人的往来书信定位为1641至1647年。Treccani《意大利人传记辞典》和《瑞士历史词典》所列姓名、年代及出生和去世地点与该人物锚点相符。此卡登记人物身份；书内肖像、书信和其他关系候选仍待 S6 审查。中文名为项目工作音译。[第4章 p.109](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md)（L122） [第4章 p.117 注2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md)（L270） [Treccani DBI](<https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/paganino-gaudenzi_(Dizionario-Biografico)/>) [瑞士历史词典](<https://hls-dhs-dss.ch/it/articles/015853/2007-09-05/>)

**English:** In Chapter 4, Haskell identifies Paganino Gaudenzi as a scholar, recounts Cassiano dal Pozzo’s 1647 request for his likeness, and describes the later receipt of a portrait copy. Note 2 locates their correspondence between 1641 and 1647. The name, dates, and birth and death places in Treccani’s *Dizionario Biografico degli Italiani* and the *Historical Dictionary of Switzerland* match this person anchor.
