## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡依 Haskell 书末索引所列姓名与 Duke of Urbino 头衔记录 Francesco Maria della Rovere。第2章称其无嗣之子先于他去世；Haskell 将公爵去世日期记为1631年4月28日，并称其后立即出现取得 Urbino 的行动，但没有指明行动者。书中还称老公爵将私人物品留给女儿 Vittoria della Rovere；此说不扩展为全部公爵收藏的继承。[S1][S2][S3]

**English:** This unit records Francesco Maria della Rovere under the name and Duke of Urbino title given in Haskell’s index. Chapter 2 says his heirless son died before him; Haskell dates the duke’s death to 28 April 1631 and says immediate steps followed to take possession of Urbino, without naming those who acted. The book also says the old duke left his private possessions to his daughter Vittoria della Rovere; this is not extended to the whole ducal collection. [S1][S2][S3]
