## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录索引中的 Paul Fréart de Chantelou。第4章称其为Poussin的朋友并叙述其将一套手稿与摹画带到巴黎；第6章称其为Poussin的巴黎赞助人；第7章称其为Surintendant des Bâtiments派往罗马的代理人。中文展示名为项目工作音译。[全书索引 C.csv#172](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/C.csv) [第4章 p.106](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md)（L96） [第6章 p.146](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md)（L3–4） [第7章 p.176](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md)（L83–84）

**English:** This unit records Paul Fréart de Chantelou as indexed. Chapter 4 calls him Poussin’s friend and says he took a set of a manuscript version and copied drawings to Paris; Chapter 6 calls him Poussin’s Parisian patron; Chapter 7 identifies him as the agent sent to Rome by the Surintendant des Bâtiments. The Chinese display name is a project working transliteration. ### 本书相关语境

第4章把Chantelou与Poussin的友谊及其携带Cassiano手稿和摹画副本赴巴黎的叙述相连；第6章记录Poussin致巴黎赞助人Chantelou的信；第7章记载他受未具名的Surintendant des Bâtiments派遣赴罗马，并承担招募画家、雕塑家、建筑师及其他工匠的任务，后来护送Poussin及几位未具名画家返程。以上均按各条S2陈述及其原有语境限定。[第4章 p.106](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md)（L96） [第6章 p.146](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md)（L3–4） [第7章 p.176](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md)（L83–84）

Chapter 4 connects Chantelou with Poussin’s friendship and his carrying Cassiano’s manuscript-and-drawing copy to Paris; Chapter 6 records Poussin’s letter to his Parisian patron; Chapter 7 describes his mission to Rome on behalf of an unnamed Surintendant des Bâtiments, including recruitment and his later escort of Poussin and a few unnamed painters. These claims retain the qualifications recorded in their individual S2 statements.
