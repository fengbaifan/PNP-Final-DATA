## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 保罗·博尔盖塞是十七世纪意大利贵族、博尔盖塞家族成员，也是奥林皮娅·阿尔多布兰迪尼的第一任丈夫。现有短条目对其生年只给1622／1624的范围；本卡保持该不确定性，并与1904–1985年的同名人物区分。（S1–S3）

**English:** Paolo Borghese was a seventeenth-century Italian nobleman of the Borghese family and the first husband of Olimpia Aldobrandini. The available short biography gives his birth only as 1622/24; this card preserves that uncertainty and distinguishes him from the namesake who lived from 1904 to 1985. (S1–S3)
