**English:** This card records the person indexed as Felice Brusasorci. Chapter 8, section II, p.216, calls Padre Massimo a Capuchin pupil of Brusasorci and uses “Veronese” as a regional adjective; it supplies no date or place of training, and the adjective does not establish birthplace or residence. The full name comes from the index. The Chinese name is a working transliteration.
