## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔将枢机托马索·鲁福置于意大利地方赞助语境，记述他1706年获枢机职、曾在拉文纳任特使并前往费拉拉；书中又以他在费拉拉汇集绘画、在多座任职城市委托作品为例。第8章叙述正文的 OCR 多处写作“Russo”，而同章后注所引费拉拉手稿题名写作“Ruffo”；本卡依书内手稿题名与索引规范为 Ruffo，并保留这一拼写差异。以上均为原书记述，本轮没有外部核验。

**English:** Haskell presents Cardinal Tommaso Ruffo in the context of provincial patronage in Italy. The account says he became cardinal in 1706, served as legate in Ravenna before going to Ferrara, assembled pictures there, and commissioned works in cities where he served. The Chapter 8 narrative OCR reads ‘Russo’ several times, while the Ferrara manuscript title quoted in the note reads ‘Ruffo’; this card uses Ruffo from that title and the index while preserving the spelling discrepancy. These remain the book's reports; no external verification was made in this batch.

### 本书语境

第8章 p.221 叙述其枢机身份、拉文纳特使任职及赴费拉拉；接续段落使用 Ruffo 和 Russo 两种拼写，并叙述其绘画收藏及委托。p.224 后注引 Biblioteca Ariostea 手稿题名，称其为 Cardinal Tommaso Ruffo。

Chapter 8 (p. 221) recounts his cardinalate, legation in Ravenna, and move to Ferrara; the continuation uses both Ruffo and Russo and describes his picture collection and commissions. A note on p. 224 quotes a Biblioteca Ariostea manuscript title naming him Cardinal Tommaso Ruffo.
