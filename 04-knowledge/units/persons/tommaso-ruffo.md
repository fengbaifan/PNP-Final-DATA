## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔将枢机托马索·鲁福置于意大利地方赞助语境，记述他1706年获枢机职、曾在拉文纳任特使并前往费拉拉；书中又以他在费拉拉汇集绘画、在多座任职城市委托作品为例。第8章 p.221 页图首处清楚印作 “Cardinal Tommaso Ruffo”，相应 OCR L115 误作 “Russo”；同章后注所引费拉拉手稿题名也作 “Ruffo”。本卡保留 OCR 异读并注明页图核对结果。以上均为原书记述，本轮没有外部核验。

**English:** Haskell presents Cardinal Tommaso Ruffo in the context of provincial patronage in Italy. The account says he became cardinal in 1706, served as legate in Ravenna before going to Ferrara, assembled pictures there, and commissioned works in cities where he served. The scan of Chapter 8 p.221 clearly prints “Cardinal Tommaso Ruffo”; the corresponding OCR line L115 misreads the surname as “Russo.” The manuscript title quoted in the note on p.224 also reads “Ruffo.” This card preserves the OCR variant and records the image check. These remain the book's reports; no external verification was made in this batch.
