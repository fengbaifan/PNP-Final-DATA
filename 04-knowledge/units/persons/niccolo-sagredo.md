## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记书后索引“Sagredo, Niccolò”所指人物。第6章以全名Niccolò Sagredo标识一段1661年的引文；第9章称同名人物为Doge，并称其是年轻Zaccaria的叔父，另将一组Borgognone战景归于Doge Niccolò。Treccani人物条目中的Niccolò Sagredo也于1675年当选威尼斯总督，与书中姓名、职位和年份相合。中文显示名为项目工作音译。 [索引 S.csv#15](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/S.csv) [书内语境](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)

**English:** This card records the person indexed as “Sagredo, Niccolò.” Chapter 6 identifies the speaker of a 1661 quotation as Niccolò Sagredo. Chapter 9 calls a Niccolò a Doge and uncle of the young Zaccaria, and separately attributes a group of Borgognone battle scenes to Doge Niccolò. Treccani's biographical entry for Niccolò Sagredo also records his election as Doge of Venice in 1675, matching the book's name, office, and year. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration. [index S.csv#15](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/S.csv) [book passage](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)
