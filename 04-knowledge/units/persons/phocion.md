## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡只汇集哈斯克尔在阿尔蒂基耶罗祭坛段落及《福基翁之葬》引注题名中使用的Phocion指称。法语称谓和“Querini的特别英雄”均作为哈斯克尔的表述保留；本卡不补写传记或外部身份核验结果。

**English:** This unit consolidates Haskell’s references to Phocion in the Alticchiero altar passage and in the cited title *Funeral of Phocion*. The French epithet and the description as Querini’s particular hero are recorded as Haskell’s wording; no biography or external identity verification is added.
