**中文：** Haskell称 Daniele IV Dolfin 是 Daniele III 的兄弟，二人于1729年同年去世；Daniele IV 曾在土耳其战争中担任指挥官，并参加伯罗奔尼撒及1715年的战事。来源对兄弟关系及军职的记载留待 S6 审查；中文名为项目工作音译。〔[第9章印刷页252；Markdown L119–122](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)〕

**English:** Haskell identifies Daniele IV Dolfin as the brother of Daniele III; both died in 1729. He describes Daniele IV as a commander in the Turkish wars, serving in the Peloponnese and again in 1715. The source's family and military role statements await S6 review. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration. ([第9章印刷页252；Markdown L119–122](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md))
