## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔将其称为法国画家 Jacques Courtois，并说明意大利人称他为 Giacomo Borgognone；他于1657年加入耶稣会，早年从第戎来到意大利，后来在 S. Andrea 初修院与 Casa Professa 继续绘画。书中称他与兄弟 Guglielmo 为 Oliva 作画，并记其于1676年去世，未能完成耶稣教堂看台装饰。本卡只记录书内身份与限定叙述；关于其妻之死的杀人说法仅作为传闻保存，不当作事实，也未作外部核验。

**English:** Haskell identifies him as the French painter Jacques Courtois and says Italians called him Giacomo Borgognone. He entered the Society of Jesus in 1657, having come to Italy from Dijon as a youth, and later continued painting at the S. Andrea novitiate and the Casa Professa. The book says he and his brother Guglielmo painted for Oliva and records his death in 1676 before the intended tribune decoration of the Gesù could be completed. This card records the source-defined identity and qualified account; the allegation concerning his wife's death is preserved only as a rumour, not as fact. No external verification was made.

### 本书语境

第3章 pp.78–79 将 Jacques Courtois 与意大利名 Giacomo Borgognone 明确相连，叙述他从第戎来到意大利、1657年加入耶稣会并继续作画；p.79 说他与兄弟 Guglielmo 为 Oliva 作画。第3章 pp.87–88 再以 Giacomo Borgognone 及其兄弟 Guglielmo 的作品和教堂委托讨论其耶稣会艺术活动；p.79 记其于1676年去世，未能完成 Gesù 看台工程。

Chapter 3, pp. 78–79, explicitly connects Jacques Courtois with the Italian name Giacomo Borgognone, recounts his move from Dijon to Italy and entry into the Society of Jesus in 1657, and says he continued to paint. Page 79 says he and his brother Guglielmo painted for Oliva. Pages 87–88 revisit Giacomo Borgognone and the work of the brothers in Jesuit commissions; page 79 records his death in 1676 before the Gesù tribune project was completed.
