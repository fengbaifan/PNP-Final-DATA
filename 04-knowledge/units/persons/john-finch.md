## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 约翰·芬奇是医生和外交官，委托卡洛·多尔奇绘制托马斯·贝恩斯肖像；贝恩斯是他的终身伴侣。

**English:** John Finch was a physician and diplomat who commissioned Carlo Dolci’s portrait of Thomas Baines, his life-long companion.
