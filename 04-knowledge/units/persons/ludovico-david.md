## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称 Ludovico David 当时身在罗马，是一位“Swiss painter”；他从一位同事处听说贝加莫教堂所需的14幅画，并提出独自承担全部画作或与其他画家分担。原文记录的是 David 的提议，委员会没有采用其方案。中文名为项目工作音译；来源措辞不另行证明出生地或国籍。〔[第8章，印刷页220；OCR L106](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md)〕

**English:** Haskell describes Ludovico David as a “Swiss painter” then in Rome. After hearing from a colleague about the fourteen pictures needed for the Bergamo church, David offered either to paint them all or to share the scheme with other artists. The source records David's proposal, which the committee did not adopt. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration; the source wording does not independently establish birthplace or nationality. ([第8章，印刷页220；OCR L106](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md))
