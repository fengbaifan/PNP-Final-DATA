**中文：** 与 Titian 捡画笔逸事及 1533 年授予多项荣衔相关。前者叙事可信度与后者授衔记载不得混同。

**English:** The chapter links Charles V with Titian's brush anecdote and the grant of several honours in 1533. The credibility of the anecdote and the grant record must remain distinct.
