## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第2章注8提到“艺术家的儿子 Domenico”多年后写下同一场合的另一种叙述；第7章 p.191 明称 Domenico 为 Bernini 之子，并称 Del Carpio 从其子处购得一幅 Bernini 自画像素描。同页注3的引文形式为 “Domenico Bernino”，书后索引以 “Bernini, Domenico” 定位 p.58 注、p.85 与 p.191。本卡记录 Haskell 书内的姓名和角色，不补充外部生平或文献身份。〔[第2章 p.58 注8](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_iv.md)；[第7章 p.191](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md)；[索引 B.csv#141](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/B.csv)〕

**English:** Chapter 2, note 8, refers to “the artist’s son Domenico,” who wrote another account of the occasion many years later. Chapter 7, p.191, explicitly identifies Domenico as Bernini’s son and says Del Carpio acquired one of Bernini’s self-portrait drawings from him. The note on that page cites “Domenico Bernino,” while the index entry “Bernini, Domenico” points to p.58 n., p.85, and p.191. This card records the name and role in Haskell’s account and adds no external biography or bibliographic identity. ([第2章 p.58 注8](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_iv.md); [第7章 p.191](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md); [索引 B.csv#141](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/B.csv))
