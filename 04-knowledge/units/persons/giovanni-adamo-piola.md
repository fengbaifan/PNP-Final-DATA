## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 列支敦士登亲王约翰·亚当·安德烈亚斯一世（1657–1712）是庄园经营者、金融管理者和艺术收藏赞助人。1690年2月3日刊本书信确认他是保罗·吉罗拉莫·皮奥拉的委托人，并允许画家自选题材。

**English:** Johann Adam Andreas I, Prince of Liechtenstein (1657–1712), was an estate administrator, financial organizer, art collector, and patron. A printed letter of 3 February 1690 identifies him as Paolo Gerolamo Piola’s patron and records that the painter could choose the subject.
