## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记第15章 p.367 提及的 Leonardo Venier。Haskell称 Canova 于1781年受委为 Marchese Poleni 制作一尊雕像，雕像是为 Venier 制作；原句没有说 Venier 本人就是委托人。中文名为项目工作音译。

**English:** This card records Leonardo Venier as named on page 367 of Chapter 15. Haskell says that Canova was commissioned in 1781 to make a statue of the Marchese Poleni for Venier; the passage does not identify Venier himself as the commissioner. The Chinese name is a project transliteration.

### 本书相关内容

**中文：** 原文使用“for Leonardo Venier”，并称 Canova 受委制作雕像；本卡只保留“为 Venier 制作”的书内说法，不推断法律委托关系。 [15章 p.367 L36](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_ii.md#L36)

**English:** The source says “for Leonardo Venier” and that Canova was commissioned to make the statue. This card preserves the stated beneficiary context and does not infer who legally commissioned the work. [15章 p.367 L36](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_ii.md#L36)
