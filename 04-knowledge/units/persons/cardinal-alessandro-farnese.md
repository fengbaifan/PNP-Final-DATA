## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔第3章称枢机亚历山德罗·法尔内塞以个人赞助主导罗马耶稣会 Gesù 教堂的建设；1568年接手赞助后，他指定 Vignola 为建筑师并任用自己的画家，把教堂视作个人与家族项目。第20章后记援引 Ackerman 时仍称其为支持 Vignola 的 Farnese camp 领袖。

**English:** Chapter 3 describes Cardinal Alessandro Farnese as the personal patron behind the Jesuit church of the Gesù in Rome. After assuming its patronage in 1568, he employed Vignola as architect and his own painters, treating the church as a personal and family project. Chapter 20's postscript, reporting Ackerman, again names him as the Farnese-camp supporter of Vignola.
