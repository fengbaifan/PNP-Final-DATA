## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 乔万尼·巴蒂斯塔·皮亚泽塔是画家、素描家和插图设计者，也为舒伦堡提供购藏评估及估价服务。

**English:** Giovanni Battista Piazzetta was a painter, draughtsman and illustrator who also assessed acquisitions and valued artworks for Schulenburg.
