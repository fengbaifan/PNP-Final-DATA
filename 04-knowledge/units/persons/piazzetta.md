## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 乔万尼·巴蒂斯塔·皮亚泽塔是画家及书籍插图设计者；章前图版目录列有他的两幅绘画和1745年《耶路撒冷解放》插图末页。

**English:** Giovanni Battista Piazzetta was a painter and book illustrator; the front-matter plate list includes two of his paintings and the final illustration in the 1745 Jerusalem Delivered.
