## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡将全书索引主项及其三个相关子项归入同一人物：索引写作 “Castiglione, Giovanni Benedetto”，正文第4章称他为热那亚画家 Giovanni Benedetto Castiglione。第4章在讨论 Cassiano 的文化影响时提及其图画，并明确保留“无法确认 Cassiano 曾直接关注他”的限定；第5章提到由其出版者献出的 Diogenes 版画；第8章称其为动物题材专家；第9章记述其素描集及作品在十八世纪的接受，并称他曾在曼图亚任宫廷画家二十余年。中文名为本项目工作译名。[第4章 p.114](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md)（L173–174） [第5章 p.124及注释](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_i.md)（L38–49） [第8章 p.207](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i.md)（L44） [第9章 pp.266–267](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)（L309–321） [全书索引 C.csv#126–129](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/C.csv)

**English:** This card groups the book’s main index entry and its three related subentries under one person. The index gives the name “Castiglione, Giovanni Benedetto,” and Chapter 4 calls him the Genoese painter Giovanni Benedetto Castiglione. In discussing Cassiano’s cultural influence, Chapter 4 mentions his drawings while explicitly qualifying that Cassiano is not known to have taken a direct interest in him. Chapter 5 mentions a print of Diogenes dedicated by his publisher; Chapter 8 calls him an animal specialist; and Chapter 9 describes his set of drawings and their reception in the eighteenth century, and says that he had been court painter at Mantua for over twenty years. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration.
