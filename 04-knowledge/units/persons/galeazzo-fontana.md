## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 加莱亚佐·丰塔纳（Galeazzo Fontana）是弗朗切斯科·丰塔纳之子。家族史记载其于1657年获准加入马耳他骑士团，后任两位大团长的侍酒官、骑士团常备团火枪手队长及大海军上将副官。

**English:** Galeazzo Fontana was the son of Francesco Fontana. A family history records that he was admitted to the Order of Malta in 1657 and later served as cupbearer to two grand masters, captain of 120 musketeers in the Order’s standing regiment, and lieutenant to the Grand Admiral.
