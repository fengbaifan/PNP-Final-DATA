### 本书相关内容

**中文：** Haskell称圣保罗大教堂穹顶及汉普顿宫王后寝宫天顶交由Thornhill绘制；Moor Park乡间住宅由Sir James Thornhill与Giacomo Leoni先后建造。Styles原拟委托Thornhill装饰Moor Park，争执后改交Amigoni与Francesco Sleter。四条S2关系候选均留待S6，不新增正式关系。

**English:** Haskell says the dome of St Paul’s and the Queen’s Bed Chamber ceiling at Hampton Court were given to Thornhill to paint; Sir James Thornhill and Giacomo Leoni built Moor Park in succession. Styles had planned to entrust Thornhill with its decoration, then reassigned the work to Amigoni and Francesco Sleter after a quarrel. All four S2 relation candidates remain for S6; no formal relation is added.
