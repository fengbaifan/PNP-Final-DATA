## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本书第8章称 Felice Torelli 是画家：第203页把他的作品列入 Giovanni Antonio Belloni 的藏画群，第227页记其提出一幅特洛伊历史题材画；第19章附录又将 Stefano Conti 委托给 Torelli 的文书列入卢卡馆藏档案。第14章 p.348 只写姓氏 Torelli，并由书后索引归在 Felice Torelli 主项下。上述均为书内记载；作品身份、委托文件内容及完成状态依原书范围保留。

**English:** Chapter 8 identifies Felice Torelli as a painter: p. 203 includes his pictures in Giovanni Antonio Belloni’s collection, and p. 227 records a Trojan-history composition he proposed. The Chapter 19 appendix also lists documents for commissions from Stefano Conti to Torelli in the Lucca repository. Chapter 14 p. 348 gives only the surname Torelli, which the book’s index places under Felice Torelli. These are source-local accounts; the identities of the pictures, the contents of the commission documents, and completion status remain limited to what the book establishes.
