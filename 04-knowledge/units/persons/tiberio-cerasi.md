## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 蒂贝里奥·切拉西是罗马法学家、教皇财政机构财务总管与礼拜堂委托人；1600年签约委托卡拉瓦乔为切拉西礼拜堂创作两幅侧壁画。

**English:** Tiberio Cerasi was a Roman jurist, Treasurer-General of the Apostolic Camera, and chapel patron who contracted Caravaggio in 1600 to make two lateral paintings for the Cerasi Chapel.
