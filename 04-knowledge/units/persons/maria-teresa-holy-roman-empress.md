## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第13章所称Empress Maria Teresa。第335页称1745年版《耶路撒冷》题献给她，并将其奢华品味与统治时期Schönbrunn的装饰相联系；书后索引列名“Maria Teresa, Holy Roman Empress”。本卡只记录来源中的身份称名和语境，不扩展人物生平或独立核实相关历史判断。中文显示名为项目工作音译。

**English:** This card records the Empress Maria Teresa named in Chapter 13. Page 335 says that the 1745 edition of the Gerusalemme was dedicated to her and associates her taste for luxury with decorations at Schönbrunn during her rule; the index lists “Maria Teresa, Holy Roman Empress.” This entry records the source's naming and context without adding a biography or independently verifying the historical claims. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration.
