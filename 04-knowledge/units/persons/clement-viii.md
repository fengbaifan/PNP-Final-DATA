## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 克勉八世本名伊波利托·阿尔多布兰迪尼，是出身阿尔多布兰迪尼家族的教会法学者，1592年至1605年任教皇。此卡因家族卡已采纳其成员身份而建立，重点承担人物消歧和家族关系端点，不展开完整教宗史。（S1–S4）

**English:** Clement VIII, born Ippolito Aldobrandini, was a canon lawyer from the Aldobrandini family who served as pope from 1592 to 1605. This card was created because his family membership had already been adopted in the family entry; it provides a disambiguated person endpoint rather than a complete history of his pontificate. (S1–S4)
