## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 阿马德奥·斯瓦耶尔（1727–1791）是威尼斯的德裔商人、学者和收藏家；图版59a收录卡诺瓦所作的斯瓦耶尔肖像。

**English:** Amadeo Swajer (1727–1791) was a Venetian merchant, scholar and collector of German origin; plate 59a reproduces his portrait by Canova.
