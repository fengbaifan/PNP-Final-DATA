## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 卡特里娜·库基亚罗尼与乔瓦尼·泰利之女；奥塔维奥·莱奥尼的继女。RISD Museum将其登记为1617年一幅烛光素描的肖像对象。

**English:** Daughter of Caterina Cucchiaroni and Giovanni Telli, and stepdaughter of Ottavio Leoni. The RISD Museum identifies her as the sitter in a 1617 candlelit drawing.
