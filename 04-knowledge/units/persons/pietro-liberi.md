## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第7章称 Pietro Liberi 为当时威尼斯最成功的画家之一，记他于1658年应利奥波德·威廉大公之邀赴维也纳一年并获授 Count Palatine。第8章记他于1660年承接贝加莫 Santa Maria Maggiore 的绘画委托，作品《洪水》经返修后安置，另一画稿遭拒后合同取消。对其艺术质量及委托失败原因的说法保留为 Haskell 的评价和推断。

**English:** Chapter 7 calls Pietro Liberi one of the most successful Venetian painters of his day and says that he went to Vienna for a year in 1658 at the invitation of Archduke Leopold Wilhelm, receiving the title Count Palatine in return. Chapter 8 records his 1660 commission for Santa Maria Maggiore in Bergamo: The Flood was repainted and installed, while the contract was cancelled after a sketch for a second picture was rejected. Haskell's assessment of the painter and his inference about the commission's failure remain attributed judgements.
