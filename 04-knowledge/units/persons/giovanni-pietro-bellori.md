目前只登记本章可支持的评论与引用角色；第六章学院职位、《名人传》出版和风格解释暂停。

**English:** Bellori is cited on Maratta's contribution to higher artistic fees and Del Carpio's commissions. Only those comments and references are registered; chapter 6's offices, publication history and stylistic interpretation are excluded.
