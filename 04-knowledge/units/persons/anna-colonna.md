## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 第2章所称 Anna Colonna。第31页先称 Taddeo 有富裕贵族妻子的前景；第32页随后称，考虑 Colonna 家族的西班牙关联后，Contestabile 之女 Anna 被选作 Don Taddeo 的新娘。原文只记“被选作新娘”，不证明婚礼举行；Contestabile 的个人身份也未给出。本卡不补写外部家世。[S1][S2]

**English:** This unit records the Anna Colonna named in Haskell’s Chapter 2. Page 31 first says that Taddeo had the prospect of a rich and aristocratic wife; page 32 then names Anna, daughter of the Contestabile, as the bride chosen for Don Taddeo after hesitation over the Colonna family’s Spanish affiliations. The source records her selection as a bride, not a completed marriage, and does not identify the Contestabile personally. No external family history is added. [S1][S2]
