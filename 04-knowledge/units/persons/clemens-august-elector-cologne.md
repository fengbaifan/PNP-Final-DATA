## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称 Clemens August 为科隆大主教选侯，记他曾雇用 Amigoni、常访威尼斯、拥有 Rosalba Carriera 的粉彩画，并于约1730年后为其庇护的多所教堂委托祭坛画。亲属、作品和委托陈述保留原书范围与限定；中文名为项目工作音译。〔[第10章，印刷页295–296；OCR L316、319](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)〕

**English:** Haskell identifies Clemens August as Archbishop-Elector of Cologne and says that he employed Amigoni, frequently visited Venice, owned pastels by Rosalba Carriera, and began commissioning altar paintings for churches under his patronage soon after 1730. The family, work, and commission statements remain within the scope and qualifications of the book. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration. ([第10章，印刷页295–296；OCR L316、319](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md))
