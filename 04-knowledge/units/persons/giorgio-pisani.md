## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔将 Giorgio Pisani 列为威尼斯 Barnabotti（贫困贵族）之一，记述他于1780年当选圣马可检察官、随后遭逮捕，并在其任职与相关画作的政治争议中扮演委托人和赞助者。第20章后记补述 Boscarati 在审讯中的说法，称 Pisani 对所委托画作题材兴趣不大；该说法保留为哈斯克尔转述。

**English:** Haskell places Giorgio Pisani among the Barnabotti, Venice's poorer nobles, and recounts his election as Procuratore di San Marco in 1780, his subsequent arrest, and his role as commissioner and patron amid political controversy over his appointment and related pictures. The Chapter 20 postscript adds Haskell's report of Boscarati's testimony that Pisani showed little interest in the subjects of the pictures he commissioned.

### 本书语境

第10章 pp.325–327 连续叙述 Pisani 的改革主张、1780年任职、庆典图像、委托版画、逮捕及 Boscarati 的处境；第20章 p.408 后记回顾相关画作引发的政治风波，并转述 Boscarati 在审讯中的说法。

Chapter 10 (pp. 325–327) follows Pisani's reform proposals, his 1780 appointment, imagery associated with the celebrations, a print commission, his arrest, and Boscarati's situation. The Chapter 20 postscript (p. 408) revisits the political uproar around the pictures and reports Boscarati's testimony.
