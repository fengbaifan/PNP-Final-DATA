**English:** This card records the French sculptor indexed as Edmé Bouchardon. Chapter 14 says that Algarotti provided Bouchardon with a letter of introduction as an example of his efforts to encourage foreign artists to study in Italy. Note 3 lists letters dated 25 October 1750 and 23 April 1752 but does not identify which, if either, concerned Bouchardon; the letters were not independently consulted.
