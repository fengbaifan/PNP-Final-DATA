## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell在第9章以全名 Maria Labia 指称Labia家族的一名成员，称其为家族的“doyenne”，并在讨论Tiepolo的宴饮题材时提到她拥有珍爱的珠宝收藏。关于她与Cleopatra珍珠赌约的对应，以及Tiepolo有意恭维她，均是Haskell在同段提出的解释，不作为Maria本人的自述或直接证词。中文名“玛丽亚·拉比亚”为工作音译。[第9章书内来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)

**English:** In Chapter 9 Haskell names Maria Labia as a member of the Labia family, calls her its “doyenne,” and mentions her valued jewelry collection while discussing Tiepolo’s banquet subject. Haskell’s proposed identification of Maria with Cleopatra in the pearl-wager story, and his inference that Tiepolo intended a compliment, remain authorial interpretations rather than Maria’s testimony. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration. [第9章书内来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)
