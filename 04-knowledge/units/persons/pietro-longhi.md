## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔第10章称 Pietro Longhi 在威尼斯社会受到重视；第13章称 Remondini 以复制出版推广其作品。第17章称他主导 Teodoro Correr 收藏中的18世纪画家部分；后记提到1776年售画目录列有归于他的两幅“conversation”作品，同时提醒目录与作品归属并不确定。以上均为原书的记述或评价。

**English:** Chapter 10 says Pietro Longhi was highly regarded in Venetian society; Chapter 13 says Remondini popularised his work through reproduction. Chapter 17 says he dominates the eighteenth-century artists in Teodoro Correr's collection. The appendix notes two 'conversation' pictures attributed to him in the 1776 sale list, while warning that the catalogue and attributions are not definitive. These are the book's reports and assessments.
