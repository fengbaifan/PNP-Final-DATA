## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 奥拉齐奥·真蒂莱斯基（1563–1639）是生于比萨的画家，曾训练女儿阿尔泰米西娅，后在查理一世宫廷工作。本书图版25列有他的《公共幸福战胜危难》。

**English:** Orazio Gentileschi (1563–1639) was a painter born in Pisa who trained his daughter Artemisia and later worked at the court of Charles I. His Public Felicity triumphant over Dangers appears in plate 25.
