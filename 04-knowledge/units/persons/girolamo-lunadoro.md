## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡将Haskell书目中的责任者形式“Lunadoro”对应为Girolamo Lunadoro。Treccani《意大利人传记辞典》以该全名立传，并将《Relatione della corte di Roma》归于他；不将其与侄子Romolo Lunadoro混同。本卡只记录完成本批文献身份判断所需的信息。

**English:** This card resolves the responsibility form “Lunadoro” in Haskell’s bibliography to Girolamo Lunadoro. Treccani’s Italian biographical dictionary identifies him under this full name and attributes *Relatione della corte di Roma* to him; he is distinct from his nephew Romolo Lunadoro. This card records only the information needed for the current bibliographic identity decision.
