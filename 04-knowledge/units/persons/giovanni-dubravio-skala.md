## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记书后索引作“Skala, Giovanni Dubravio”、第15章具名的Giovanni Dubravio Skala。Haskell在讨论Waldstein雕像可能对象时写作“Giovanni Dubravio Skala, perhaps”；同一问句称其来自Pilsen，并提出他是否为坎布雷同盟之后首位在Padua学习的德国学生。雕像对象与学生身份均保留原文的试探语气。中文显示名为项目工作音译。

**English:** This card records Giovanni Dubravio Skala, indexed as “Skala, Giovanni Dubravio” and named in Chapter 15. When discussing a possible subject for a Waldstein statue, Haskell writes “Giovanni Dubravio Skala, perhaps”; the same question says he was from Pilsen and asks whether he was the first German student to study at Padua after the League of Cambrai. Both the statue identification and the student description retain the source's tentative wording. The Chinese display name is a project working transliteration.
