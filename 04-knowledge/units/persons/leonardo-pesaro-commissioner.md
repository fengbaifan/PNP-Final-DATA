### 描述

**中文：** Giovanni Pesaro的侄子，委托Baldassare Longhena制作Giovanni的纪念碑，并照料其建造工程。此条按委托角色与亲缘语境限定。

**English:** Nephew of Giovanni Pesaro, he commissioned Baldassare Longhena to make Giovanni’s monument and oversaw its construction. This record is limited to that commission and kinship context.
