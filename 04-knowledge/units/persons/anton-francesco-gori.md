## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡将第10章的“A. F. Gori”与第13章的“Francesco Gori”登记为同一位佛罗伦萨古物学家。Treccani《意大利人传记辞典》将其规范姓名记为Anton Francesco Gori；其Zanetti条目也将Dactyliotheca的拉丁文说明归于Anton Francesco Gori。第10章所述Smith邀请Gori协助重印Guicciardini，并承诺为Gori的Museum Etruscum寻找英国订阅者；第13章则称他撰写了拉丁文commentary。版年差异和关系候选不在本卡解决。

**English:** This card identifies chapter 10’s “A. F. Gori” and chapter 13’s “Francesco Gori” as the same Florentine antiquary. Treccani’s *Dizionario Biografico degli Italiani* gives his canonical name as Anton Francesco Gori; its entry on Zanetti also attributes the Latin description in the *Dactyliotheca* to him. The chapter 10 account of Smith inviting Gori to assist with a Guicciardini reprint and promising to find English subscribers for the *Museum Etruscum*, and chapter 13’s account of the Latin commentary, are retained within their source contexts. The publication-date discrepancy and relation candidates are not resolved in this card.
