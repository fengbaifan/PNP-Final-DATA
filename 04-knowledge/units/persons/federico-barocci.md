## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第2章称Maffeo Barberini曾请求Federico Barocci为其私人房间送画；原文记录的是请求，不据此推断画作已经送达或完成。第3章称Oratorians于1582年委托Barocci为Visitation小堂创作祭坛画，并希望取得更多作品；同章还将Borromini与Barocci相隔多年并置，比较二人对特定反宗教改革感受性的表达。委托、作品及比较断言留待S6逐条审查。中文名为本项目工作译名。（第2章，印刷页26，Markdown L56–58；第3章，印刷页69，Markdown L49；第3章，印刷页76，Markdown L142）

**English:** Chapter 2 says Maffeo Barberini asked Federico Barocci to send a picture for his private apartments; the passage records a request and does not establish that a picture was sent or completed. Chapter 3 says the Oratorians commissioned Barocci in 1582 for an altarpiece in the Chapel of the Visitation and wanted further works. The same chapter compares Borromini with Barocci, many years earlier, as interpreters of a specified Counter-Reformation sensibility. Commission, work, and comparison assertions await individual S6 review. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration. (第2章，印刷页26，Markdown L56–58；第3章，印刷页69，Markdown L49；第3章，印刷页76，Markdown L142)
