## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell在第5章将Cornelius de Stael称为dealer，转述他于1663年向客户表示，与画家交易十分麻烦，自己更愿意购买已经完成的画作。第5章注6又引用一封1663年6月9日由de Stael致Don Antonio Ruffo的信。此卡只保留本书可追溯的人物身份范围；不补写生卒年、出生地或其他传记信息。原信、V. Russo刊本及其身份均未在本任务中独立查阅。[第5章 p.122](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_i.md)（L25） [第5章 p.122 注6](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_i.md)（L137）

**English:** In Chapter 5 Haskell calls Cornelius de Stael a dealer and reports his 1663 explanation to a client that dealing with painters was troublesome and that he preferred buying pictures already painted. Note 6 in the same chapter cites a letter from de Stael to Don Antonio Ruffo dated 9 June 1663. This card is limited to the person identity traceable in the book; it adds no birth or death dates, birthplace, or other biography. The letter, V. Russo's publication, and Russo's identity were not independently consulted for this task.
