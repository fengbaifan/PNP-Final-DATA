**中文：** Haskell在第2章通过注3将“法国怀疑者”的引语归于Gabriel Naudé；第4章称Naudé等人为Cassiano的亲密朋友，并称Naudé是Cassiano的好友、曾为其收藏中的肖像撰写拉丁铭文；第7章转述Naudé在1638年对部分绘画可能流向Lord Arundel的判断。以上身份和语境均按书中转述记录，涉及人物或作品的断言留待S6逐条审查。中文名为本项目工作译名。（第2章，印刷页33，Markdown L27–28；第2章，注3，Markdown L155；第4章，印刷页107，Markdown L106–107；第4章，印刷页108–109，Markdown L117–120；第7章，印刷页177，Markdown L95）

**English:** Haskell identifies the speaker of a French quotation in chapter 2 as Gabriel Naudé in note 3. Chapter 4 names Naudé among the French libertins, describes them as close friends of Cassiano, calls Naudé Cassiano's great friend, and says Naudé wrote Latin inscriptions for portraits in Cassiano's collection. Chapter 7 reports Naudé's 1638 expectation that some pictures might go to Lord Arundel. These are recorded as Haskell's account; person and work assertions await individual S6 review. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration. (第2章，印刷页33，Markdown L27–28；第2章，注3，Markdown L155；第4章，印刷页107，Markdown L106–107；第4章，印刷页108–109，Markdown L117–120；第7章，印刷页177，Markdown L95)
