### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录索引S.csv#45的Tommaso de’ Santi。第9章p.263称他为Zaccaria Sagredo的仆人并受托照料收藏；p.264引述的遗嘱写作“Tomaso de Santi”。这段遗嘱文字由Haskell转引，原始公证文书未在本项目中独立查阅。正文称遗产受益侄子为Gherardo，遗嘱引文则拼作Girardo；两种形式的身份对应仍待S3处理，本卡不替其合并。中文名为项目工作音译。

**English:** This card records Tommaso de’ Santi, indexed at S.csv#45. Chapter 9, p. 263, identifies him as Zaccaria Sagredo's servant and says he was entrusted with the collection; the quoted will on p. 264 spells his name “Tomaso de Santi.” Haskell reproduces the will passage, and the notarial original has not been independently consulted in this project. The narrative names the nephew Gherardo, while the quoted will spells the name Girardo; their identity correspondence remains for S3 review and is not merged here. The Chinese display name is a project working transliteration.
