### 描述

**中文：** 被 Marchese Costaguti 的家庭总管视作适合其妹妹的配偶；婚姻关系仅涉及这位未具名总管的妹妹，不是侯爵之妹。

**English:** Costaguti's household steward considered Carlone a suitable husband for the steward's sister. She was not the marchese's sister.
