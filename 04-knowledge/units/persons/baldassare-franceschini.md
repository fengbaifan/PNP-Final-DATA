## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 巴尔达萨雷·弗兰切斯基尼，别称“沃尔泰拉人”，是画家和素描家；本书图版37c和66分别收录其阿尔洛托题材绘画与路易十四寓意画。

**English:** Baldassarre Franceschini, known as il Volterrano, was a painter and draughtsman; plates 37c and 66 reproduce his Arlotto painting and an allegory concerning Louis XIV.
