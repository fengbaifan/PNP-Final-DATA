## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 佛兰德画家、学者与外交官；本书讨论其新教堂委托和大型工程，并列出《希律王宴会》。

**English:** A Flemish painter, scholar and diplomat; the book discusses his Chiesa Nuova commission and large projects and lists The Feast of Herod.
