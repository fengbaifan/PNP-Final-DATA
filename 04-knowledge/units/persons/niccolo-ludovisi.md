## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 尼科洛·卢多维西（Niccolò Ludovisi，1610–1664）是Orazio Ludovisi与Lavinia Albergati之子、格列高利十五世的侄子，也是枢机Ludovico Ludovisi的弟弟。其后任Piombino亲王；家族关系依据Treccani传记及已核对的身份记录。（S1–S3）

**English:** Niccolò Ludovisi (1610–1664) was the son of Orazio Ludovisi and Lavinia Albergati, nephew of Pope Gregory XV, and younger brother of Cardinal Ludovico Ludovisi. He later became Prince of Piombino. The family links are supported by the Treccani biography and the aligned identity records. (S1–S3)
