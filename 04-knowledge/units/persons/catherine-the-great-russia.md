## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第10章以Catherine购买《埃及艳后宴会》说明她对威尼斯艺术的欣赏；第20章后记则称作者此前关于她本人购买该画的假定现在看来过于自信。本卡记录书内人物身份和作者跨章判断变化，不将购买行为写作已确认事实。 [来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md#L349)；[来源2](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/20_CHP-20Postscript.md#L157)

**English:** Chapter 10 cites Catherine's purchase of Tiepolo's *Banquet of Cleopatra* as evidence of her admiration for Venetian art. The chapter 20 postscript says the author's earlier assumption that Catherine herself bought the painting now seems over-confident. This entry records the person and the change in the author's assessment without presenting the purchase as established fact.
