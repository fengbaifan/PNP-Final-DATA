**English:** The index of Haskell’s book lists the name “Bentivoglio, Enzo.” Chapter 2 identifies Enzo as one of Ippolito’s younger brothers, says that he served for a time as an “ambassador” from Ferrara to Rome, and describes him and Guido as strongly interested in art. This card records the person identified within the book and adds no fuller personal name, dates, or biography unsupported by the source.
