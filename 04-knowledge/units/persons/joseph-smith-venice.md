## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Joseph Smith 是联结威尼斯与外部世界的重要人物，也是其时代重要的艺术赞助人。书中记述他约1675年出生，18世纪初作为商人与经营者定居威尼斯，并于1744年成为英国领事；第9章记其向 Sagredo 继承人购买绘画，第19章附录讨论其收藏与皇家收藏的关系。此卡依据书内叙述登记身份，不作外部核验。

**English:** Haskell presents Joseph Smith as an important link between Venice and the outside world and as a leading art patron of his day. The book reports that he was born around 1675, settled in Venice in the early eighteenth century as a businessman and merchant, and became British Consul in 1744. Chapter 9 notes his purchases from the Sagredo heirs, while the Chapter 19 appendix discusses his collections and their relation to the Royal Collection. This card registers the identity from the book's account and makes no external verification claim.

### 本书语境

第9章 p.264 称其为英国领事 Joseph Smith，并记其向 Sagredo 继承人购买作品；第10章 pp.299–300 以全名介绍其来历、威尼斯商贸活动及1744年任英国领事；第19章附录5讨论其收藏与 George III 所购皇家收藏。

Chapter 9 (p. 264) names British Consul Joseph Smith among the buyers from the Sagredo heirs. Chapter 10 (pp. 299–300) gives his full name, background, commercial activity in Venice, and 1744 appointment as British Consul. Appendix 5 in Chapter 19 discusses his collections and the works bought by George III for the Royal Collection.

### 身份边界

中文：本卡对应第10章以“CONSUL JOSEPH SMITH”为题的连续人物叙述，以及第9章领事购藏和第19章皇家收藏附录中的同一 Joseph Smith。五条索引候选覆盖其传记、与 McSwiny、Rosalba Carriera 的关联、Sagredo 购藏及皇家收藏语境；第19章同处出现 Giuseppe Smith 与 Joseph Smith 的目录形式，按后文明确的威尼斯国王领事语境归组。此处仅登记人物身份，不把原书所述购买、委托、收藏和交往改作正式关系或外部核实；相关 S2 断言仍待 S6 审查。

English: This card represents the continuous account headed “CONSUL JOSEPH SMITH” in Chapter 10 and the same Joseph Smith in Chapter 9's consular purchase context and Chapter 19's Royal Collection appendix. The five index candidates cover his biography, index links to McSwiny and Rosalba Carriera, the Sagredo purchases, and the Royal Collection context. Chapter 19 uses both Giuseppe Smith and Joseph Smith in catalogue forms; they are grouped here in the surrounding context that explicitly identifies the deceased Joseph Smith as His Majesty's Consul at Venice. This card registers identity only: the book's reports of purchases, commissions, collections, and associations are not made into formal relations or externally verified facts, and relevant S2 claims remain for S6 review.
