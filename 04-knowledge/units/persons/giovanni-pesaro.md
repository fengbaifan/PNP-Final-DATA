## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 威尼斯贵族、外交官与政治人物，1658至1659年任威尼斯总督。其遗嘱为本人纪念碑建造拨款。

**English:** A Venetian nobleman, diplomat, and political figure who served as Doge of Venice from 1658 to 1659. His will funded the construction of his funerary monument.
