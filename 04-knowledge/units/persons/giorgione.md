### 描述

**中文：** Haskell第7章以Giorgione指称此人。Treccani说明Giorgione是画家Giorgio da Castelfranco的通称，并指出Barbarelli姓氏有误。此卡登记人物身份，不核定书中或其他来源对单件作品的归属。

**English:** Haskell refers to this person as Giorgione in Chapter 7. Treccani identifies Giorgione as the name used for the painter Giorgio da Castelfranco and notes that the surname Barbarelli is erroneous. This card records the person’s identity without determining the attribution of any individual work in the book or elsewhere.
