## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡按Haskell书中“Duke of Richmond”称谓记录与McSwiny英国名人墓葬画系列相关的人物。第10章p.287称McSwiny代表Lord March筹划该系列，并说Lord March于1723年成为里士满公爵；p.288注2记一封1727年McSwiny致公爵的信；p.289–291继续叙述同一系列及公爵购藏。索引主项与“and McSwiny’s British Worthies”子目相连，支持把两条索引候选及对应正文提及归入同一书内人物组。所引段落没有提供该人的完整个人姓名，本卡保留头衔作工作名称；具体购藏清单按Haskell所述保留不确定性。中文名为描述性工作译名。

**English:** This card records the person whom Haskell refers to as the Duke of Richmond in the McSwiny British Worthies sequence. Chapter 10 p. 287 says McSwiny acted for Lord March, who became Duke of Richmond in 1723; note 2 on p. 288 cites a 1727 letter from McSwiny to the Duke; and pp. 289–291 continue with the same series and the Duke’s acquisitions. The index main entry and its “and McSwiny’s British Worthies” subentry support grouping the two index candidates and their corresponding body references as one source-local person. The cited passages do not give his full personal name, so the title is retained as a working label; Haskell’s uncertainty about the exact acquired pictures is preserved. The Chinese name is a descriptive working rendering.
