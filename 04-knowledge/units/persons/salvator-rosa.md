## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 画家，利用展览、喝彩者和工作室现画销售扩大选择空间。拒收预付并主张按成品质量定价，是本章依据书信与传记提炼的具体行为。

不愿依委托作画不等于拒绝朋友建议，宣称只为满足自己也不是没有经济依赖的证明。“没有真正追随者”须与章末 Ferri、Luti 的局部相似做法并读；与 Schildersbent 没有成员证据。

**English:** Rosa used exhibitions, supporters and studio stock, rejected advances and argued for prices based on finished quality. Refusing commissions did not preclude friends' advice or prove economic independence. His lack of real followers must be read alongside Ferri's and Luti's partial parallels; Schildersbent membership is unproved.
