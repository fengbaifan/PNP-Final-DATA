## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 1624 年为 Piacenza 的 S. Antonino 绘壁画，获按艺术需要扩展题材的自由，并在合同中承诺使用上等颜料。

**English:** For his 1624 fresco work at S. Antonino in Piacenza, Gavasetti could expand the prescribed subjects as artistic needs required and undertook to use fine pigments.
