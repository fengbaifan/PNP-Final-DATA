**English:** The book’s index lists “Zompini, Gaetano” as a person entry and repeats the same name in a Zanetti-related subentry. Chapter 9 identifies Gaetano Zompini as a painter and uses his full name when describing a proposal he put forward. Chapter 13 lists him again by his full name and continues to refer to his work by surname on p. 344. The body text and index provide cross-chapter identity anchors for one person. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration.
