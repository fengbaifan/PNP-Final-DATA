## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 威尼斯史学者，研究18世纪威尼斯社会与思想史，著有安德烈亚·梅莫研究和威尼托史学论文；曾为哈斯克尔提供讨论和帮助。

**English:** A historian of Venice who studied its eighteenth-century society and thought, Torcellan wrote on Andrea Memmo and other Venetian historical subjects and assisted Haskell through discussions and other help.
