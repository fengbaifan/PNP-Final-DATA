### 本书相关内容

**中文：** Haskell称Smith于1770年去世时，公证人Uccelli记录其宫殿内有数百幅画；清单仅列主题而不列作者。该段未命名宫殿，Archivio di Stato记录及C. A. Levi的再版未独立查阅；关系候选留待S6。

**English:** Haskell says that when Smith died in 1770, the notary Uccelli recorded several hundred pictures in his palace; the list gives subjects, not artists. The passage does not name the palace, and neither the Archivio di Stato record nor C. A. Levi’s reprint was independently consulted. The relation candidate remains for S6.
