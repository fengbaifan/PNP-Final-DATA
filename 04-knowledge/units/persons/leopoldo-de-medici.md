**中文：** Jacopo Salviati 1662-07-22 信的枢机收信人，讨论购 Claude 作品及对高身份者不预定价格的做法。

**English:** The cardinal received Salviati's letter of 22 July 1662 about buying Claude's pictures and not fixing prices for high-status clients beforehand.
