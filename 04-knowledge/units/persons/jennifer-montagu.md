## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 索引及第2章注释所称 Jennifer Montagu。第52页注2引述她关于 Barberini 天顶画阐释的文章，并转述她对 Rosichino（1640）、Girolamo Teti（1642）等人解释的区分；“四时代”说法则归于一位未具名观察者。第56页注4中，Haskell 感谢她指出 Tessin 关于 Barberini 收藏的记述并展示影印件。本卡仅记录书内学术提及，不补写其外部身份或生平，所引文章未独立查阅。[S1][S2][S3]

**English:** This unit records Jennifer Montagu as named in Haskell’s index and Chapter 2 notes. Note 2 on page 52 cites her article on interpretations of the Barberini ceiling and reports her distinction between readings by Rosichino (1640), Girolamo Teti (1642), and others; the possible Four Ages interpretation is attributed to an unnamed observer. In note 4 on page 56, Haskell thanks her for pointing him to Tessin’s account of the Barberini collection and showing him a photocopy. This unit records only the book’s scholarly references, adds no external identification or biography, and does not independently consult the cited article. [S1][S2][S3]
