## 内容

### 人物身份

**中文：** 本卡记录书后索引所列 Niccolò Circignani dalle Pomarance，以及第3章正文称作 Nicolo Circignani dalle Pomarance、后记简称 Circignani 的画家身份。第3章称一位未具名校长委托他制作 S. Stefano Rotondo 的殉道题材壁画；后记 p.399 再次以 Circignani 指称该组壁画。章间身份对应以索引页码和正文互文为依据；不补写生卒、籍贯或其他外部生平。

**English:** This card records the painter indexed as Niccolò Circignani dalle Pomarance, named in Chapter 3 as Nicolo Circignani dalle Pomarance, and referred to as Circignani in the postscript. Chapter 3 says an unnamed rector commissioned him to make the martyrdom frescoes at S. Stefano Rotondo; the postscript again identifies that cycle as painted by Circignani. The cross-chapter identity rests on the index locator and the book’s repeated reference; no dates, birthplace, or other external biography are added.
