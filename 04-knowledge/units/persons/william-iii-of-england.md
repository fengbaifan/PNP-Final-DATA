## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应索引词头“William III of England”以及第7章、第10章中的William III。第7章将他置于Verrio服务的政治语境；第10章p.280记William Bentinck为其亲信，p.289注2将William III列入1741年出版物的题名人物。p.280转录写作William Ill，印本页图确认是III；该转录差异不形成另一人物。

**English:** This card represents the index heading “William III of England” and William III in chapters 7 and 10. Chapter 7 places him in the political context of Verrio’s service; chapter 10 p. 280 names William Bentinck as his favourite, and note 2 on p. 289 lists William III among the subjects in the 1741 publication. The p. 280 text transcription reads William Ill, while the printed page image confirms III; this transcription difference does not indicate another person.
