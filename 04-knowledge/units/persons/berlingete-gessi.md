## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 1647-07-10 致 Don Cesare Leopardi d’Osimo 的信说明绷框、打底材料和群青通常由委托人承担；原信由 Gualandi 刊引。OCR Berlingete 经 PDF 印刷页 13 核对为 Berlingero；文件路径沿用，显示名已纠正。

**English:** His letter of 10 July 1647 to Don Cesare Leopardi d'Osimo, cited through Gualandi, discusses patrons normally supplying stretchers, priming and ultramarine. Printed page 13 corrects OCR Berlingete to Berlingero; the stable path retains its old spelling.
