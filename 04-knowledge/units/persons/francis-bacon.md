## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应索引词头“Bacon, Sir Francis”，连接第4、10、15章的人名提及。第4章注释转引Peiresc 1635年法文信件时写作Baccon；第10章称Bacon是Lodoli喜爱的作者之一；第15章写到Querini图书室中的Bacon半身像，并将“启蒙运动奠基者”及Lodoli特别敬仰者的评价归于Haskell。转引中的Baccon作为原拼异文保留；此卡不独立核验信件或评价。中文名为通行工作译名。

**English:** This card represents the index heading “Bacon, Sir Francis” and the name occurrences in chapters 4, 10, and 15. Chapter 4's note transcribes Baccon in a French letter by Peiresc dated 1635; chapter 10 names Bacon among Lodoli's favourite authors; and chapter 15 describes a bust of Bacon in Querini's library, attributing to Haskell the characterization of him as a founder of the Enlightenment and Lodoli's particular hero. The transcribed form Baccon is retained as a source variant; the letter and Haskell's evaluation are not independently verified here. The Chinese name is the project's working rendering.
