## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应索引词头“Vecchia, Pietro della”及第7、13、16章中Pietro della Vecchia的提及。第7章称Archduke Leopold Wilhelm曾赞助他，并称其声誉部分依赖对Giorgione作品的伪作；第13章提到Pinelli收藏中有其绘画；第16章记载Vianello最喜爱他的作品，原Markdown将名转录为“Pietrp”。作者对伪作和作品归属的判断保留为来源表述。

**English:** This card represents the index entry “Vecchia, Pietro della” and references to Pietro della Vecchia in chapters 7, 13, and 16. Chapter 7 says Archduke Leopold Wilhelm patronised him and that part of his reputation depended on forgeries of Giorgione. Chapter 13 mentions his pictures in Pinelli’s collection. Chapter 16 records Vianello’s special preference for his work; the source Markdown transcribes the name there as “Pietrp.” The author’s statements about forgery and attribution remain attributed to the book.
