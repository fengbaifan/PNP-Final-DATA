## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第13章称 Don Pietro Antonio Toni 是一位出生于 Modena 附近、后定居 Venice 的神父、收藏家与艺术品经销者。哈斯克尔记述他与当时多位艺术家交往，尤其与 Sebastiano Ricci 亲近；Ricci 生病时 Toni 曾照料他，Ricci 后来赠予 Toni 版画与一件《东方三博士来朝》modello。Toni 还曾为年轻的 Pietro Antonio Novelli 提供建议与赞助，并在1748年去世时将收藏留给他；他也为 Zaccaria Sagredo、Zanetti、Consul Smith 等收藏者及外国访客充当代理人。

**English:** Chapter 13 identifies Don Pietro Antonio Toni as a priest born near Modena who later settled in Venice, a collector, and an art dealer. Haskell recounts his contacts with leading artists, especially his closeness to Sebastiano Ricci: Toni cared for Ricci during an illness, and Ricci later gave him prints and a modello of an Adoration of the Magi. Toni also advised and patronised the young Pietro Antonio Novelli, to whom he left his collections when he died in 1748; he acted as an agent for collectors including Zaccaria Sagredo, Zanetti, and Consul Smith, as well as foreign visitors.
