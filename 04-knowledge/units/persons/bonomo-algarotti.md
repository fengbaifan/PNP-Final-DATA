## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔第14章称 Bonomo 为 Francesco Algarotti 的长兄，称他有鉴赏力，并认为他对 Francesco 的影响相当大但难以评估。书中记述 Bonomo 购买了两人共同收藏中的许多绘画，协助落实 Francesco 的计划，并向他提供意大利社交与艺术消息；第14章注释和第15章称 Bonomo 于1776年去世。索引以“Algarotti, Bonomo”列名；本卡按本书所见称谓登记这位兄长，不与 Francesco Algarotti 合并。相关亲缘、通信、藏品和委托事实仍保留来源限定。

**English:** Chapter 14 identifies Bonomo as Francesco Algarotti's elder brother, describes him as a man of taste, and says his influence on Francesco was considerable but difficult to evaluate. The book reports that Bonomo bought many of the pictures in their joint collection, helped put Francesco's plans into practice, and kept him informed about social and artistic news in Italy; a Chapter 14 note and Chapter 15 report that Bonomo died in 1776. The index form is Algarotti, Bonomo. This card registers the brother as named in the book and keeps him distinct from Francesco Algarotti; kinship, correspondence, collection, and commission claims remain source-bounded.

Chapter 14 introduces the elder brother and describes their joint collection and his help with Francesco's plans; its note reports that Bonomo died in 1776. Chapter 15 again identifies him as Francesco's brother when explaining that the pictures began to disperse after 1776. The book supplies no other given name for Bonomo.
