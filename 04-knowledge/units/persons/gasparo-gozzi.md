## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Gasparo Gozzi 是 Goldoni 的热心崇拜者，并在威尼斯贵族圈中依附于较开明的贵族；第10章另以 Gaspare Gozzi 拼写，记他批评建筑装饰、编辑《Osservatore Veneto》，为 Goldoni 辩护并评论 Pietro Longhi 与 Tiepolo。哈斯克尔还概述 Gozzi 以寓言讨论绘画的自然性与劳作者、1782年访问 Padua 的 Arena 礼拜堂后倡议以公共艺术表现公民德行，以及他为 Giorgio Pisani 撰写颂词。

**English:** Haskell calls Gasparo Gozzi an ardent admirer of Goldoni and describes him as dependent on more open-minded Venetian nobles. Chapter 10 also spells the name Gaspare Gozzi and recounts his criticism of architectural ornament, editorship of Osservatore Veneto, defence of Goldoni, and writing on Pietro Longhi and Tiepolo. Haskell also discusses Gozzi's fable about natural images and working people, his 1782 visit to the Arena Chapel in Padua and proposal for public art representing civic virtues, and his tribute for Giorgio Pisani.
