## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡按书末索引姓名记录 Marchese Girolamo Durazzo。第8章称其为热那亚主要贵族家族成员；该家族长期收藏本地艺术，并从博洛尼亚、那不勒斯等地获得绘画。哈斯克尔明确说 Luca Giordano 为 Durazzo 家族作四幅画，约1704年 Francesco Solimena 又为该家族补充两幅旧约题材大画；本卡不把家族委托改写为 Girolamo 本人的逐件委托。[S1][S2]

**English:** This unit records Marchese Girolamo Durazzo under the name in the book index. Chapter 8 identifies him as a member of a leading Genoese patrician family that had long collected local art and obtained paintings from Bologna and Naples. Haskell says Luca Giordano painted four canvases for the Durazzo family and that Francesco Solimena added two large Old Testament histories around 1704; the family’s commissions are not recast as individual commissions by Girolamo. [S1][S2]
