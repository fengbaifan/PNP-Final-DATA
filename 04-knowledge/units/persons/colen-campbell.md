**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell第10章点名的Colen Campbell。第309页说，十一幅画中有两幅描绘Campbell的作品；紧接着称这一系列主要依据《Vitruvius Britannicus》的版画。原文没有说Campbell绘制了这两幅画。索引列出“Campbell, Colen”，并有“Vitruvius Britannicus”子项。中文名为本项目工作音译。 [第10章 p.309；OCR L469-471](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md) [索引 C.csv#17](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/C.csv)

**English:** This card records Colen Campbell named in Chapter 10. Page 309 says that two of eleven pictures portray works by Campbell and then says that the series was based primarily on engravings from *Vitruvius Britannicus*. The source does not say that Campbell made the two pictures. The index lists “Campbell, Colen” with a “Vitruvius Britannicus” subentry. The Chinese name is a working transliteration.
