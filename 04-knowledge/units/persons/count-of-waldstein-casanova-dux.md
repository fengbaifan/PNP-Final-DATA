## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记 Haskell 在第15章称为 “the Count of Waldstein” 的特定书内人物：他是 Casanova 在 Dux 的保护人。书末索引也仅列出 “Waldstein, Count of”，未给出名字。Memmo 曾询问他是否愿意为 Prà della Valle 的一座雕像出资；这是可能性问题，不证明他承诺出资、付款或委托作品。[书末索引 p.473](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/22_CHP-22Index.md)（L3448）；[第15章 p.367](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_ii.md)（L33–34）

**English:** This card registers the source-local person Haskell calls “the Count of Waldstein” in Chapter 15: Casanova’s patron at Dux. The book index likewise lists only “Waldstein, Count of” and supplies no given name. Memmo asked whether he might be willing to pay for a statue for the Prà della Valle; this is a question about possible support, not evidence of a pledge, payment, or commission. [书末索引 p.473](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/22_CHP-22Index.md)（L3448）; [第15章 p.367](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_ii.md)（L33–34）
