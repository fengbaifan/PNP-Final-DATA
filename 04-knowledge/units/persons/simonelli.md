## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 尼科洛·西莫内利是莫拉的朋友、收藏家和业余画商，与莫拉共同创作本书图版65a所列双人漫画。

**English:** Niccolò Simonelli was Mola’s friend, a collector and an amateur dealer, and co-created the double caricature listed as plate 65a with Mola.
