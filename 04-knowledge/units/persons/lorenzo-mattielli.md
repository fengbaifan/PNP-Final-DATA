## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应索引词头“Lorenzo Mattielli”及第7、14章中的雕塑家Lorenzo Mattielli。第7章称他与Domenico Antonio Parodi为Prince Eugene的大厅和花园制作雕像；第14章将布吕尔府邸中的海神喷泉归于Mattielli。作品归属仍按原书表述留待关系阶段审查。

**English:** This card represents the index entry “Lorenzo Mattielli” and the sculptor Lorenzo Mattielli in chapters 7 and 14. Chapter 7 says he and Domenico Antonio Parodi made statues for Prince Eugene’s hall and gardens. Chapter 14 attributes the Neptune fountain at Brühl’s houses to Mattielli. These work attributions remain as stated in the book and await relation review.
