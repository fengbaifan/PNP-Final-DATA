## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应索引词头“Medici, Cosimo III de'”及第8章、第20章后记中的Cosimo III。第8章明确称Grand Prince Ferdinand为Cosimo III之子；后记以相同姓名回顾Haskell早期对Cosimo III的评价，并转述Klaus Lankheit的重新评估。本卡只记录书内跨章身份，不把Haskell或被引学者的评价改写为本项目独立判断。

**English:** This card represents the index heading “Medici, Cosimo III de'” and Cosimo III in chapter 8 and the chapter 20 postscript. Chapter 8 explicitly names him as Grand Prince Ferdinand’s father; the postscript returns to Cosimo III by the same name and reports Klaus Lankheit’s reassessment of his role. The card records the book’s cross-chapter identity and does not recast Haskell’s or the cited scholar’s evaluations as independent project findings.
