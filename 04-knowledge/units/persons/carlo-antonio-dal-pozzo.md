**English:** Haskell identifies Carlo Antonio dal Pozzo as Cassiano dal Pozzo's younger brother, with scientific and artistic interests and patronage that may have been nearly as significant as Cassiano's. The postscript calls him Cassiano's heir as well as a notable collector and patron. The book also notes his interest in Poussin's bird paintings. Family and work relations await S6 review.
