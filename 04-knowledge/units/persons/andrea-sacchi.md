## 内容

### 描述
**中文：** 安德烈亚·萨基是十七世纪在罗马工作的画家，也从事建筑设计。第一章以他在小安东尼奥·巴贝里尼家户中的等级变化说明画家的服务地位；后文所称祭坛画“稿”须与后来图录的复制品判断区分。[S1–S2、S7]
**English:** Andrea Sacchi was a seventeenth-century painter active in Rome who also undertook architectural design. Chapter 1 uses his changing rank in Antonio Barberini the Younger's household to illustrate an artist's position in service. The later reference to an altarpiece modello must be distinguished from the subsequent identification of that object as a reduced replica. [S1–S2, S7; linked work record]

### 评价与研究
- Bellori、Passeri为早期生平与艺术评价的重要记述者；本轮通过百科／专业传记及作品图录了解其说法，未重读原版全书。[S5、S7]
- 构图集中、较少人物与Cortona的比较属于艺术理论评价，不推出每幅画固定六人或八人的规则。[S5、S7]
- Ann Sutherland Harris，*Andrea Sacchi*（1977）；Giovanni Incisa della Rocchetta，〈Notizie inedite su Andrea Sacchi〉（1924）；D’Avossa的遗嘱研究（1985）。书目由S5、S7核见，未声称研究原文全读。
