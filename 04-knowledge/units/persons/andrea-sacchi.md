## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 1637–1640 年在 Antonio Barberini 家户中居较低等级，1640 年进入最高受俸等级的画家。

p.12 脚注给其 modello 实例，限定同页正文“博洛尼亚画家没有模型稿”的概括；不因正文绝对措辞删除这个反例。

**English:** Sacchi held a lower rank in Antonio Barberini's household during 1637–1640 and entered its highest pensioned rank in 1640. The modello example in the page 12 note qualifies the same page's sweeping generalization about Bolognese painters.
