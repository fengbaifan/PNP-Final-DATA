## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Niccolò Cassana 为热那亚画家，并记其自1698年起在威尼斯担任 Grand Prince Ferdinand 的代理人，也为其作画及担任艺术顾问，数次赴佛罗伦萨为王子及家人绘制肖像。第7章提到 Queen Anne 雇用他为肖像画家，但主文所记1714年卒年带问号，注释转述 Ratti 的1713年说法，并称其来英时间及是否卒于英格兰不明；卡片保留这些异文和不确定性。

**English:** Haskell identifies Niccolò Cassana as a Genoese artist and says that from 1698 he acted as an agent in Venice for Grand Prince Ferdinand, worked for him as a painter and artistic adviser, and visited Florence several times to paint portraits of the Prince and his household. Chapter 7 mentions Queen Anne employing him as a portrait painter, but its 1714 death date is queried; a note reports Ratti's 1713 date and says that the date of Cassana's arrival in England and whether he died there are unknown. These variants and uncertainties are retained.
