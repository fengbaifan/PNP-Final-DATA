### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记索引主项 “Peterborough, 3rd Earl of” 所指的书内人物。中文显示名按爵位翻译；书内材料没有给出其个人姓名，本卡不补写。第7章 p.200 注1称，正文里的 Admiral “Pekemburgh” 可能是第三代彼得伯勒伯爵，并在这一可能识别的语境中记录其1707年从西班牙抵达意大利、次十年再次长期停留，以及确曾向画家 Giovan Gioseffo dal Sole 委托若干画作。注释中的试探识别不视为已证实身份：正文人物候选 cand-7285 仍为 S3 undecided，本卡与之保持分开。OCR 转录中的 “tbe” 与 “ofPeterborough” 已按 S2 记录依据 PDF 物理页38校正为 “the” 与 “of Peterborough”；这是字面校勘，不构成人物身份依据。[第7章 p.200 注1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_v.md)（L63；PDF物理页38） [全书索引 P.csv#92](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/P.csv)

**English:** This card records the in-book person listed in the index as “Peterborough, 3rd Earl of.” The Chinese display name translates the title; the book does not supply a personal name, which is not added here. Chapter 7, p. 200, note 1 says Admiral “Pekemburgh” in the text may possibly be the 3rd Earl of Peterborough, and in that tentative identification context reports his arrival in Italy from Spain in 1707, a longer return in the following decade, and commissions of pictures from Giovan Gioseffo dal Sole. The proposed identification is not treated as established: the body candidate cand-7285 remains S3 undecided and separate from this card. The OCR readings “tbe” and “ofPeterborough” are recorded in S2 as corrected to “the” and “of Peterborough” against PDF physical p. 38; this is textual correction, not identity evidence.
