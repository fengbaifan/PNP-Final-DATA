### 本书相关语境

第4章将Angeloni置于Cassiano dal Pozzo的艺术与学术讨论中，并列其奖章收藏和Annibale Carracci素描；同页注释称同时代人曾多次提及他。第6章介绍其收藏、写作及与Bellori相关的语境；Haskell对Bellori亲缘身份使用“was said to be”的转述限定。本卡不把这些来源陈述提升为已独立核验的关系。[第4章 p.114](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md)（L173、291） [第6章 p.158](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_iv.md)（L22–26）

Chapter 4 places Angeloni in its discussion of Cassiano dal Pozzo’s artistic and scholarly circle and mentions his medal collection and Annibale Carracci drawings; a note on the same page reports that contemporaries often referred to him. Chapter 6 discusses his collecting and writing in connection with Bellori; Haskell qualifies the kinship claim about Bellori with “was said to be.” These source statements are not presented here as independently verified relations.
