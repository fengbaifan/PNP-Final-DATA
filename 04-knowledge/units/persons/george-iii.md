## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 全书索引将人物列为 “George III, King of England”。第10章称 Consul Smith 于1762年向 King George III 出售图书及多数绘画；第15章也记 Smith 最好的绘画于当年由 George III 获得。附录5明确提出售出全部收藏还是部分选择仍无法定论；本卡保留 Haskell 对该问题的限定，不将交易范围扩写为已确定事实。[第10章 pp.300、310](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L369、488） [第15章 p.361](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_i.md)（L11） [附录5 pp.391–393](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/19_CHP-19Appendix.md)（L117–120、149） [第20章后记 p.406](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/20_CHP-20Postscript.md)（L160–162） [全书索引 G.csv#48](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/G.csv)

**English:** The book’s index lists the person as “George III, King of England.” Chapter 10 says that Consul Smith sold books and most of his pictures to King George III in 1762; Chapter 15 likewise says Smith’s best paintings were acquired by George III that year. Appendix 5 states that the evidence cannot establish whether the whole collection or only a selection was sold. This card preserves Haskell’s qualification and does not present the scope of the transaction as settled.
