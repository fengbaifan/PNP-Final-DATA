## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 意大利艺术史家、展览策展人与大学教授，长期参与威尼斯艺术史研究和展览工作；曾协助哈斯克尔并于1962年介绍他认识拉里萨·萨尔米娜。

**English:** An Italian art historian, exhibition curator and university professor, active in Venetian art-historical research and exhibitions; he assisted Haskell and introduced him to Larissa Salmina in Venice in 1962.
