## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应索引词头“Poleni, Marchese Giovanni”及第10章明载的Marchese Giovanni Poleni。第10章称他为Smith在Padua的熟人、大学教授、工程师和建筑师；注4转述1747年两封致Smith的信，称Poleni曾请Smith向Visentini取得绘图，但原信未在本项目中查阅，请求是否完成亦未据此确认。第15章记Canova于1781年受委为Marchese Poleni制作雕像。索引另列p.201与p.367；当前S2人物提及映射对应p.301及p.367，p.201仅作为索引定位保留，不单独作为本次身份判断依据。中文名为本项目工作音译。

**English:** This card represents the index heading “Poleni, Marchese Giovanni” and the Marchese Giovanni Poleni named in chapter 10. Haskell describes him as Smith’s acquaintance in Padua, a university professor, engineer, and architect. Note 4 reports two 1747 letters to Smith and says Poleni asked Smith to obtain drawings from Visentini; the letters were not consulted for this project, and fulfillment of the request is not inferred. Chapter 15 reports that Canova was commissioned in 1781 to make a statue of Marchese Poleni. The index also lists p. 201 and p. 367; the current S2 person mentions map to pp. 301 and 367. The p. 201 locator is retained as an index reference and is not used alone as identity evidence. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration.
