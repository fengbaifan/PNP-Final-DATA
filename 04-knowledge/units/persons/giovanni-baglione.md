### 本书相关内容

第3章分别记载Baglione因《The Resurrection》画作的报酬问题向教宗投诉，以及他于1602年为耶稣堂右横翼礼拜堂绘制祭坛画。S3把两处作品锚点合为一个作品组，依据为同一画家、复活题材、耶稣会教堂语境与年代相容；p.84未重复题名，p.66也未明说地点，因此不推定祭坛画的题名出处、陈列状态或投诉年份。第5章记有Divine Love与Caravaggio的Earthly Love之间的竞争叙述。相关关系候选保留待S6审查。

Chapter 3 separately records Baglione’s payment complaint concerning a picture called The Resurrection and his 1602 altarpiece for the Gesù’s right transept chapel. S3 groups the two work references based on the same painter, resurrection subject, Jesuit church context, and compatible chronology. The p. 84 passage does not repeat the title, and p. 66 does not name the location, so the unit does not infer the title’s source, display status, or the complaint year. Chapter 5 also records the account of Divine Love in competition with Caravaggio’s Earthly Love. Relation candidates remain for S6 review.
