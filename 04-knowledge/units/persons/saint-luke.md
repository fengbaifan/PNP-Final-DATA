## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 同一人物锚点在第1章节日语境、第3章福音书语境及第8章圣路加节／礼拜堂奉献语境中均称 St Luke。此处只合并书内同一圣徒指称，不补写传记或外部身份事实。

**English:** Haskell refers to St Luke in the chapter 1 feast-day context, chapter 3 Gospel context, and chapter 8 feast-day and chapel-dedication context. This unit groups the same saint named in the book and adds no biography or externally verified identity facts.
