## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第10章以 Crown Prince Frederick of Prussia (later the Great) 称其早年身份，并称他曾向 Schulenburg 寻求一位年轻 castrato；第14章称 Frederick the Great 是 Algarotti 的宫廷赞助人之一，记录他于1740年12月授 Algarotti 伯爵头衔，也记述 Algarotti 为其和宫廷顾问讨论柏林发展艺术中心的计划。第10章 p.295 又将德累斯顿十字教堂遭轰炸一事归于 Frederick the Great；此处保留原书的归因，不作外部核验。

**English:** Chapter 10 calls him Crown Prince Frederick of Prussia (later the Great) when recounting his request to Schulenburg for a young castrato. Chapter 14 describes Frederick the Great as one of Algarotti's court patrons, reports that he made Algarotti a Count in December 1740, and says Algarotti advised him and his courtiers about plans to make Berlin a major centre for the arts. Chapter 10 (p. 295) also attributes the bombardment of Dresden's Church of the Cross to Frederick the Great; this card preserves the book's attribution without external verification.
