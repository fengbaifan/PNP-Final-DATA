## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 鲁道夫·维特科尔（1901–1971）是艺术史家，1956–1969年任哥伦比亚大学艺术史教授；本书导言转述沃特豪斯对其十八世纪意大利绘画论断的反驳。

**English:** Rudolf Wittkower (1901–1971) was an art historian and professor of art history at Columbia University in 1956–1969; the book’s introduction reports Waterhouse’s challenge to his account of eighteenth-century Italian painting.
