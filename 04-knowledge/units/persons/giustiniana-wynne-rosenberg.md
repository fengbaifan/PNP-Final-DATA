## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔第10章以 Giustiniana Wynne 记述她在 Lodoli、Andrea Memmo 与英国领事 Joseph Smith 的求爱叙事中的位置，并称她后来反复成为18世纪威尼斯赞助人与作家之间的联系线索。第15章称她是 Angelo Querini 的亲密朋友之一，记述她于1762年与奥地利大使 Count Rosenberg 结婚，并以 Mme Rosenberg 转述她对 Alticchiero 的描述；该章注释以 Giustiniana Wynne-Rosenberg 为《Alticchiero》（威尼斯，1787）的作者名。以上均按本书叙述和引文范围登记，未作外部核验。

**English:** In Chapter 10, Haskell names her as Giustiniana Wynne in the courtship narratives involving Lodoli, Andrea Memmo, and British Consul Joseph Smith, and says she repeatedly appears as a link among eighteenth-century Venetian patrons and writers. Chapter 15 calls her one of Angelo Querini's close friends, reports her 1762 marriage to Austrian Ambassador Count Rosenberg, and presents her account of Alticchiero under the form Mme Rosenberg. Its note names Giustiniana Wynne-Rosenberg as the author of Alticchiero (Venice, 1787). These details are registered within the book's account and citations; no external verification was made.
