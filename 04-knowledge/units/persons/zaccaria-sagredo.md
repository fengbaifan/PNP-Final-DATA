## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Zaccaria Sagredo（1572–1647）是威尼斯贵族、圣马可检察官及军事将领；科雷尔馆藏目录所录Faldoni版画以他为肖像对象。此人不同于1653–1729年以收藏家著称的同名者。

**English:** Zaccaria Sagredo (1572–1647) was a Venetian nobleman, Procurator of Saint Mark, and military commander. The Museo Correr catalogue identifies him as the sitter in Faldoni’s print. He is distinct from the namesake collector who lived from 1653 to 1729.
