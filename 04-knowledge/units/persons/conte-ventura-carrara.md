**English:** In Chapter 1 note 2, Haskell identifies Conte Ventura Carrara as the addressee of a letter by Carlo Quarisinini dated 11 July 1696 and cites Bottari, volume V, page 186. The OCR transcription reads Quarisinini; S2 records the printed-form correction Quarismini. Neither the original letter nor Bottari’s publication was independently consulted for this task. This card records only the recipient identity traceable in the book and adds no other biography. [第1章；印刷页13；注2；OCR L512-L513](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/01_CHP-1.md)
