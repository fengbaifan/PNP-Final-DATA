## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 1916年刊载的圭尔奇诺书信及编辑注释将一位与Don Antonio Ruffo有亲缘关系的修女委托者称为D.a Maria Ruffo，并认定她是其侄女。现有材料尚未把这一称名与修女名Suor Illuminata作无歧义的同一认定，也不应与Duchess of Sora Maria Ruffo合并。

**English:** A 1916 publication of Guercino’s letters and its editorial notes identify the nun who commissioned the altarpiece as D.a Maria Ruffo, a niece of Don Antonio Ruffo. The available evidence does not unambiguously equate this name with the religious name Suor Illuminata, and she should not be merged with Maria Ruffo, Duchess of Sora.
