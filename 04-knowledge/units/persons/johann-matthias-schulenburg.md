**English:** Chapter 10 identifies Johann Matthias Schulenburg by full name as a man from a Saxon family, recounts his 1715–16 defence of Corfu and continued service to the Venetian Republic, and places him among foreign collectors in Venice. The Chapter 20 postscript revisits his collection and later scholarly disagreement. This card registers the person as presented in the book; it does not recast the account as externally verified fact.
