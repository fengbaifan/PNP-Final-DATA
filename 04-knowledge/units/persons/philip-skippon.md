**English:** This card records the person listed as Philip Skippon in the book’s index; the Chinese name is a project transliteration. Chapter 4, note 3, says that Cassiano’s unnamed nephew took Skippon round the collection in 1663 and that Skippon noted many bird paintings. Haskell explicitly says that Skippon did not record whether the paintings were by Poussin; the passage identifies no individual painting. The chapter 4 introduction and a chapter 6 note also cite different pages by Skippon. These are Haskell’s references and do not indicate that Skippon’s works were independently consulted. This card adds no external biography or identity verification.
