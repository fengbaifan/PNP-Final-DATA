The book discusses a modello and a letter addressed to Paolo Giordano in Chapter 4; a monument, sculptural work, and an unapproved plan to travel to England in Chapter 7; and a letter signed by Tacca in the Chapter 19 appendix. The appendix text is reproduced by Haskell; the cited archival manuscript has not been independently consulted. Proposed work is not treated as completed.
