**English:** This card records the Prato bookseller Nicola Mazzoni named in Chapter 13's footnote. The footnote spells the name “Niccola Mazzoni,” while the index entry is “Mazzoni, Nicola”; both book forms are preserved without selecting a standard spelling. Haskell says the cited letter was published by Nuti in 1941; this card does not imply independent consultation of the letter. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration.
