**中文：** Elizabeth Orna是信息管理顾问、作者及索引编制者；《赞助人与画家》第一版序言致谢她编制该书索引。

**English:** Elizabeth Orna was an information management consultant, author and indexer. The first edition preface of Patrons and Painters thanks her for compiling its index.
