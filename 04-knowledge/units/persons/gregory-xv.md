## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 1621–1623 年在位。与本章 Cardinal Ludovisi 为同一人；确认学院章程，其当选吸引同乡多梅尼基诺重返罗马。

**English:** Pope in 1621–1623, Gregory XV is the chapter's Cardinal Ludovisi. He confirmed Academy statutes, and his election drew his compatriot Domenichino back to Rome.
