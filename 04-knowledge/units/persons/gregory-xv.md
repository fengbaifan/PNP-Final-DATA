## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 格列高利十五世，原名亚历山德罗·卢多维西（Alessandro Ludovisi，1554–1623），是出身博洛尼亚、于 1621–1623 年在位的教皇。第一章将他与侄子卢多维科分别叙述，并讨论其当选对多梅尼基诺返罗马的作用及其对圣路加学院章程的确认。外部资料补入教育、任职与亲缘，具体日期异文保留。

**English:** Gregory XV, born Alessandro Ludovisi (1554–1623), was a pope from Bologna who reigned from 1621 to 1623. Chapter 1 distinguishes him from his nephew Ludovico and discusses his election in relation to Domenichino's return to Rome and his confirmation of the Accademia di San Luca's statutes. External sources supply education, offices and family information; conflicting dates remain explicit.
