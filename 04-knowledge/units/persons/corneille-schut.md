**English:** This card records Corneille Schut, indexed as “Schut, Corneille” and named in Chapter 8. In discussing Roomer's collection, Haskell lists Schut among the makers of works owned by Roomer and says the collection included several hundred works by the named artists. That number describes the works collectively and is not a count of Schut's individual works. The Chinese display name is a project working transliteration.
