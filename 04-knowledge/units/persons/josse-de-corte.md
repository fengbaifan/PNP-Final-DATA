### 描述

**中文：** 雕塑家。为乔万尼·佩萨罗总督纪念碑制作小天使、总督雕像和四件寓意像。

**English:** A sculptor. For the monument to Doge Giovanni Pesaro, he made the small angels, the doge’s statue, and four allegorical figures.
