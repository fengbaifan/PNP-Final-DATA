### 描述

**中文：** 在威尼斯活动的艺术商人，与收藏家亚伯拉罕·休谟通信，向其介绍和经办购藏。

**English:** An art dealer active in Venice who corresponded with the collector Abraham Hume about potential purchases and acquisitions.
