## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔第5章称 Giambattista Ricciardi 为 Salvator Rosa 最亲密且挚爱的朋友，并称他是诗人；Rosa 长期为他绘画并向他寻求建议。哈斯克尔还说 Ricciardi 居于另一座城市，不能提供 Rosa 所求的个人赞助。第7章在1669年书信语境中称他为 Rosa 忠实且长期相伴的朋友。

**English:** Chapter 5 calls Giambattista Ricciardi Salvator Rosa's closest and much-loved friend and identifies him as a poet; Rosa continued painting for him and sought his advice. Haskell also says Ricciardi lived in another town and could not provide the personal patronage Rosa wanted. Chapter 7, in the context of a 1669 letter, calls him Rosa's loyal and long-suffering friend.
