## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 朱塞佩·佐基是画家，曾获安德烈亚·杰里尼的支持，并绘制图版58a所列扎内蒂与杰里尼的双人肖像。

**English:** Giuseppe Zocchi was a painter supported by Andrea Gerini and the creator of the double portrait of Zanetti and Gerini listed as plate 58a.
