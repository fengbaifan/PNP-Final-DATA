## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应全书索引中的 Christian Cole。第10章以全名叙述其在1703年担任英国驻威尼斯大使第一秘书、遇见Rosalba Carriera并劝她转向粉彩肖像；注1另转述早期传记作者称一位“certo signor Colle inglese”首先提出该建议。注释所引传记未独立查阅，故该说法仍按Haskell的转述记录。[全书索引 C.csv#331](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/C.csv) [第10章 p.276](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L9–10） [第10章 p.276 注1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L492）

**English:** This unit corresponds to Christian Cole in the index. Chapter 10 names him as the British Ambassador’s first secretary in Venice in 1703 and says that he encountered Rosalba Carriera and persuaded her to turn to pastel portraits. Note 1 separately reports earlier biographers’ attribution of the advice to a “certo signor Colle inglese.” The cited biographies were not independently consulted, so that attribution remains Haskell’s report. [全书索引 C.csv#331](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/C.csv) [第10章 p.276](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L9–10） [第10章 p.276 注1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L492）
