## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 第6章以全名记载 Maria Mancini，称她为 Cardinal Mazarin 的侄辈女性亲属（niece；原文未说明具体亲属支系），并称她于1661年与 Lorenzo Onofrio Colonna 结婚。作者称她是 Louis XIV 的 first love，并描述她到罗马后的社会位置；第156页记其要求 ‘separatione di letto’，以及 Haskell 所述她于1672年离开丈夫。本卡只登记本书可支持的人物身份与叙述，不补出生、死亡、具体亲属支系或外部生平。[第6章 p.155](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md)（L106–107）；[第6章 p.156](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md)（L116–117）

**English:** Chapter 6 names Maria Mancini and calls her Cardinal Mazarin’s niece, without specifying the kinship line; Haskell says she married Lorenzo Onofrio Colonna in 1661. He also calls her Louis XIV’s first love and describes her social position after arriving in Rome. Page 156 records her demand for a ‘separatione di letto’ and Haskell’s account that she left her husband in 1672. This card registers only the identity and account supported by the book; it adds no birth or death dates, specific kinship line, or external biography. [Chapter 6, p.155](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md) (L106–107); [Chapter 6, p.156](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md) (L116–117)

### 名称

中文显示名“玛丽亚·曼奇尼”为原书姓名 Maria Mancini 的工作音译。第6章 p.155 L106 使用书内全名 Maria Mancini；索引 M.csv#46 以反序形式 Mancini, Maria 列出该人物。

The Chinese display name 玛丽亚·曼奇尼 is a working transliteration of the source name Maria Mancini. Chapter 6, p.155, L106, gives the full name Maria Mancini; index entry M.csv#46 lists the reversed form Mancini, Maria.

### 身份边界

本卡对应 Haskell 第6章的 Maria Mancini，不与同书其他 Mancini 人物按姓氏合并。原书称谓支持 “niece”，但未在此处说明具体亲属支系；本轮没有查阅外部身份记录，也不将 Haskell 的叙述写成独立核实的传记事实。

This card identifies the Maria Mancini named in Haskell’s Chapter 6 and does not merge her with other people named Mancini in the book. The source says “niece” but does not specify the kinship line. No external identity record was consulted, and Haskell’s account is not presented as independently verified biography.
