## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡依第2章正文拼写记录 Ippolito Bentivoglio；书末索引将同一定位项写作 Bentivoglio, Ippotilo，保留这一拼写差异。哈斯克尔称 Bentivoglio 家族来自 Ferrara，并称 Ippolito 是其主要成员；1598年 Ferrara 归于教廷后，他支持 Cesare d’Este 的继承主张以尝试维护城市独立，哈斯克尔称这些主张“相当可疑”。原文称其弟 Guido 与 Enzo 支持教会一方。本卡不补写外部家世。[S1][S2]

**English:** This unit uses the Chapter 2 body spelling Ippolito Bentivoglio; the book index spells the corresponding entry Bentivoglio, Ippotilo, and that variation is retained. Haskell says the Bentivoglio came from Ferrara and calls Ippolito its principal member. After Ferrara devolved to the papacy in 1598, he supported Cesare d’Este’s succession claim in an attempt to maintain the city’s independence; Haskell describes those claims as “rather dubious.” The source names his younger brothers Guido and Enzo as supporting the Church. No external family history is added. [S1][S2]
