**English:** This card groups the index candidate “Peri, Pellegrino” with the body candidate “Pellegrino Peri” in note 1 on page 122. The index form and the full name in the book match, so the S3 identity group is registered as one book-local person. Page 122 says that he lived near the statue of Pasquino in the second half of the seventeenth century and employed the young Gaulli on his arrival in Rome; the same sentence says that Gian Andrea Carlone owed Peri a debt and refused to repay it. Note 1 on page 122 further reports that Carlone attacked Peri in 1666 and that Peri reported the theft of two pictures from his shop in 1681. These latter claims are Haskell’s reports citing Pascoli and Bertolotti; those cited works were not independently consulted, so this card records the author’s account rather than independently verified biographical facts. 本卡仅对应 Haskell 与索引具名的 Pellegrino Peri。正文称他居住在 Pasquino 雕像附近并雇用 Gaulli；注释所述袭击、借款与失窃信息均经由作者转引，未核读原始研究。两幅被盗画作未具标题，也未逐幅对应两位画家；不扩张其作品清单或身份资料。

This card refers only to the Pellegrino Peri named by Haskell and the index. The text says that he lived near the statue of Pasquino and employed Gaulli; the attack, loan, and theft details in the note are author-reported citations whose sources were not consulted. The two stolen pictures are untitled and are not mapped one-to-one to the two artists; no catalogue or further identity details are inferred.
