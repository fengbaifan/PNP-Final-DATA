## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记书中提及的 Cyril Humphris。第20章后记称，初版出版后，一件 Paolo Giordano II Orsini 青铜胸像进入 Humphris 在伦敦的持有；原文未给出转移日期。中文名为项目工作音译。

**English:** This card registers Cyril Humphris as named in the book. The chapter 20 postscript reports that, after the first edition, a small bronze bust of Paolo Giordano II Orsini came into Humphris’s possession in London; no transfer date is supplied. The Chinese name is a project transliteration.

### 本书相关内容

**中文：** 本卡保存 Haskell 关于 Humphris 持有状况的书内报告；对应 `held_by` 关系已从作品卡登记，不代表所有权或当前持有。Bernini 归属仍是来源转述的学术争议，未建立作品—人物归属边。[第20章后记 p.401](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/20_CHP-20Postscript.md#L85)

**English:** This card records Haskell’s report of Humphris’s possession; the corresponding `held_by` relation is recorded from the work card and does not assert ownership or current possession. The Bernini attribution remains a reported scholarly dispute and is not encoded as a work-person relation. [第20章后记 p.401](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/20_CHP-20Postscript.md#L85)
