## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 科雷乔是意大利文艺复兴画家安东尼奥·阿莱格里的通称，取自其故乡。他主要在帕尔马创作壁画、祭坛画及其他宗教和神话绘画，以仰视透视和明暗处理著称。第一章将赴帕尔马观看其壁画作为赞助人资助艺术家学习旅行的例子，没有指定具体壁画或旅行者。生年、训练经历与罗马旅行仍有证据限制。（S1、S4、S6–S8）

**English:** Correggio is the name commonly used for the Italian Renaissance painter Antonio Allegri, derived from his native town. He worked chiefly in Parma on frescoes, altarpieces and other religious and mythological paintings, becoming known for foreshortening and the handling of light and shade. Chapter 1 uses visits to his frescoes in Parma as an example of patron-funded study travel without identifying a particular fresco or traveller. His birth year, training and possible journey to Rome remain subject to evidential limits. (S1, S4, S6–S8)
