### 描述

**中文：** 皮埃尔·罗森贝格是研究十七、十八世纪法国与意大利艺术的艺术史家，曾任卢浮宫院长；本书新版序言感谢他提供建议和帮助。

**English:** Pierre Rosenberg is an art historian of seventeenth- and eighteenth-century French and Italian art and a former director of the Louvre; the revised-edition preface thanks him for advice and assistance.
