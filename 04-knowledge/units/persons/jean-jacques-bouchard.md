## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本书称 Jean-Jacques Bouchard 为法国 libertin，并记他出席巴贝里尼宫歌剧演出；第4章将他列入 Cassiano dal Pozzo 的法国友人圈。这里仅按本书指称登记人物，不补入出生、死亡或其他传记事实。

**English:** Haskell identifies Jean-Jacques Bouchard as a French libertin present at an opera performance in the Barberini palace and later includes him among Cassiano dal Pozzo’s French friends. This unit records the person as named in the book and adds no unsupported dates or biography.

Chapter 2 p. 56 places Bouchard at an opera performance in the palace. Chapter 4 pp. 107–108 discusses his association with Cassiano and distinguishes the restrained “official” conversation from the private papers described in the account.
