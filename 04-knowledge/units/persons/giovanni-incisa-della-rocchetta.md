**English:** This card records Giovanni Incisa della Rocchetta, identified by the signature of his 1966 article and its self-reference to his 1925 *Roma* article. Chapter 6 and Haskell’s bibliography use “Incisa della Rocchetta” with the initial G.; the 1966 author line and self-reference resolve this source-local identity. No biography is inferred.
