## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应索引主项 Giacomo Guarana 在第9、10、13、15章的提及。第9章称其追随Tiepolo并为宫殿绘制壁画；第10章称他为Ricci与Tiepolo的学生，并报告其于1762年为莫斯科绘制《伊菲革涅亚的祭献》；第13章将他列入Remondini推广的艺术家；第15章又在新一代历史画家语境中提及他。第10、15章Markdown转录出现Guaraña，索引与第9、13章作Guarana；转录异形不另建人物。中文名为本项目工作音译。

**English:** This card represents the index entry Giacomo Guarana across chapters 9, 10, 13, and 15. Chapter 9 calls him a follower of Tiepolo and says that he painted frescoes in the palace. Chapter 10 describes him as a pupil of Ricci and Tiepolo and reports that in 1762 he painted a Sacrifice of Iphigenia for Moscow. Chapter 13 includes him among artists popularised by the Remondini, and chapter 15 mentions him among a younger generation of history painters. The chapter 10 and 15 Markdown transcriptions use Guaraña, while the index and chapters 9 and 13 use Guarana; these source forms are retained as variants of this person. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration.
