## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应索引词头“Anne, Queen of England”及第7、10章中的Queen Anne。第7章记Verrio领取来自Queen Anne的养老金，并称她雇用Niccolò Cassana为肖像画家；第10章p.281的OCR写作“Queen Arme”，该段随后以“she”指称其去世。姓名归组依据索引、第7章明名与第10章语境；OCR异文保留，相关关系断言仍待S6审查。

**English:** This card represents the index entry “Anne, Queen of England” and Queen Anne references in chapters 7 and 10. Chapter 7 mentions a pension from Queen Anne for Verrio and says she employed Niccolò Cassana as a portrait painter. The OCR at chapter 10 p.281 reads “Queen Arme”; the passage then refers to her death. The grouping follows the index, the explicit chapter 7 name, and the chapter 10 context. The OCR variant is retained, and the relation candidates remain for S6 review.
