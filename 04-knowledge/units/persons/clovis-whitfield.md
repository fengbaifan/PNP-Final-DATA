**English:** This card registers Clovis Whitfield as named in the book. The chapter 20 postscript refers to him by surname as having discovered and analysed Agucchi’s programme for the painting; the chapter 21 bibliography gives the corresponding article under Clovis Whitfield’s full name. The programme and article have not been independently consulted. The Chinese name is a project transliteration.
