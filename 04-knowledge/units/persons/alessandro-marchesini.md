## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Alessandro Marchesini 为维罗纳画家，记述他居住在威尼斯、曾在博洛尼亚 Carlo Cignani 的学校学习，并受 Stefano Conti 任命为代理人。他替 Conti 催办画家委托、定期报告、转交预付款、推荐艺术家并提出改画建议；1725年 Conti 再向他征询。书信及艺术家评价均按哈斯克尔的叙述和引文范围保留。

**English:** Haskell describes Alessandro Marchesini as a Veronese painter who lived in Venice, studied at Carlo Cignani's school in Bologna, and was appointed Stefano Conti's agent. Marchesini pressed artists over delayed commissions, reported regularly to Conti, distributed advances, recommended artists, and proposed alterations; Conti sought his advice again in 1725. Accounts of letters and artists' opinions remain bounded to Haskell's narrative and quotations.
