## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称 Gaspare Diziani 是 Sebastiano Ricci 的学生，1717年赴德累斯顿为 Augustus the Strong 绘制舞台布景；他后来也赴罗马，为 Cardinal Ottoboni 制作四十小时敬礼仪式的舞台布景。相关委托和工作关系按书内语境保留；中文名为项目工作音译。〔[第6章印刷页165；第10章印刷页278、294；Markdown L40、30–31、293](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)〕

**English:** Haskell identifies Gaspare Diziani as a pupil of Sebastiano Ricci, says he went to Dresden in 1717 to paint stage scenery for Augustus the Strong, and later went to Rome to produce a set for the Forty Hours ceremony for Cardinal Ottoboni. The commissions and working relations are retained within the book's context. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration. ([第6章印刷页165；第10章印刷页278、294；Markdown L40、30–31、293](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md))
