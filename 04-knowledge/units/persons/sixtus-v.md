## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 西斯笃五世，世俗名Felice Peretti，1585–1590年任教皇。第一章把他与1580年代对艺术社会作用的认识联系起来；其侄孙蒙塔尔托在他死后继续赞助艺术家的例外仍保留原章语境。

**English:** Sixtus V, known in secular life as Felice Peretti, was pope from 1585 to 1590. Chapter 1 associates him with an awareness of art’s social role in the 1580s and discusses his great-nephew Montalto’s continued patronage after his death as an exception to the general pattern.
