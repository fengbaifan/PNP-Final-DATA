## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Alessandro Peretti-Montalto 的教皇叔辈；作者亦将 1580 年代对艺术社会作用的认识与其联系。后者是史学解释。

**English:** Sixtus V was Alessandro Peretti-Montalto's papal uncle. Haskell also associates him with understanding art's social role in the 1580s, an interpretative claim.
