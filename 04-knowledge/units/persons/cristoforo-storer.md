## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称 Cristoforo Storer 是委员会最初选择的画家，并以“Swiss artist”称呼他，记其师从 Ercole Proccaccini、曾与贝加莫显要家族密切接触，且于1654年2月受委托绘制《The Levites》。这里仅记录书内识别和叙述；“Swiss artist”保留原文表述，不另推出生地或国籍。中文名为项目工作音译。〔[第8章，印刷页216；OCR L23–25](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md)〕

**English:** Haskell identifies Cristoforo Storer as the artist first chosen by the committee, calls him a “Swiss artist,” notes his pupilage under Ercole Proccaccini and close contacts with leading Bergamo families, and says he was commissioned in February 1654 to paint The Levites. This record is limited to the book's identification and account; “Swiss artist” is retained as the source's wording and is not expanded into a birthplace or nationality claim. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration. ([第8章，印刷页216；OCR L23–25](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md))
