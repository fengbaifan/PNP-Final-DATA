## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 罗马教会枢机与教廷外交官。Treccani记载他生于1594年4月21日，1623年受任驻巴黎教廷使节，1626年1月获任枢机，曾任博洛尼亚使节至1631年，1661年11月10日去世。Haskell第3章的“Cardinal Bernardino”与第5章再次出现的同一称谓，按本书语境及传记身份记录对齐为Bernardino Spada。中文名为项目工作音译。[第3章 p.75](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L125–135） [第5章 p.139](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_iii.md)（L95–101） [Treccani《意大利人传记辞典》条目](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/bernardino-spada_(Dizionario-Biografico)/)

**English:** A Roman ecclesiastic and papal diplomat. Treccani records his birth on 21 April 1594, appointment as papal nuncio in Paris in 1623, appointment as cardinal in January 1626, service as legate of Bologna through 1631, and death on 10 November 1661. Haskell’s “Cardinal Bernardino” in Chapter 3 and the repeated title in Chapter 5 are aligned to Bernardino Spada on the basis of the book context and the biographical authority record. The Chinese name is a working transliteration.
