## 内容

### 描述
**中文：** 彼得罗·奥托博尼（1667–1740）是威尼斯出生的枢机、剧本文字作者与艺术音乐赞助人，亚历山大八世的侄孙。第一章所指特雷维萨尼的保护人已与这一身份对应，不能混同同名教皇。[S1、S5、S7]
**English:** Pietro Ottoboni (1667–1740) was a Venetian-born cardinal, librettist and patron of art and music, and a grandnephew of Alexander VIII. Trevisani's patron in Chapter 1 is identified with this cardinal, not with the pope who shared his name. [S1, S5, S7]
