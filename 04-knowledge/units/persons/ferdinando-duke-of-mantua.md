## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记索引主项“Mantua, Ferdinando, Duke of”所指的人物。第7章 p.175 明称 Marie de Medici 的侄子 Ferdinando 于1624年已为曼托瓦公爵；同章叙述其重视法国宫廷对其称谓、与父亲 Vincenzo 同为收藏家及赞助人、雇用多位意大利艺术家，并提出赠送 Baglione 带来的十幅 Apollo 与 Muses 题材画作。第1章 p.14 的索引定位页记载一位曼托瓦公爵于1617年向 Guido Reni 订画；索引将该页与第175页列在同一人物主项下，作为跨章定位依据。索引另列 p.10，但该页当前可读正文没有找到对应的公爵提及，故不以该定位增加人物事实。卡片仅保留 Haskell 的书内称谓和叙述，不推定外部全名、生卒年或更精确的朝代身份。[第1章 p.14](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/01_CHP-1_sec_ii.md)（L81） [第7章 p.175](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md)（L64–65） [书后索引](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-1-Index-MD/M.md)（M.csv#60）

**English:** This card records the person named in the index main entry “Mantua, Ferdinando, Duke of.” Chapter 7, p.175 explicitly calls Ferdinando a nephew of Marie de Medici and says that by 1624 he was Duke of Mantua. The passage recounts his concern with his form of address at the French court, describes him and his father Vincenzo as collectors and patrons, says that he employed leading Italian artists, and reports his proposal to send ten Apollo-and-Muses pictures brought by Baglione. Chapter 1, p.14 describes a Duke of Mantua commissioning a painting from Guido Reni in 1617; the index places that page and p.175 under one person entry, which supplies the cross-chapter locator. The index also lists p.10, but the currently readable text on that page contains no corresponding mention of the duke, so that locator is not used to add a biographical fact. The card preserves Haskell’s names and account without inferring an external full name, dates of life, or a more precise regnal identity.
