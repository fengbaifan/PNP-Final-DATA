## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本书以 Luca Carlevarijs／Carlevarijs 指称威尼斯画家，并记其绘制城市景观及为来访使节记录入城场景。索引中的 regatta 子目与正文中的 Luca Carlevarijs 属同一人物；相关具体作品分别登记为作品，不并入人物卡。

**English:** Haskell refers to the Venetian painter as Luca Carlevarijs or Carlevarijs, describing his city views and his work recording visiting ambassadors’ entries into Venice. The regatta index subentry and the prose reference to Luca Carlevarijs identify the same person; the individual works remain separate units.

### 本书语境

第8章 p.227–228 引述 Marchesini 对 Carlevarijs 景观画的评价；第10章 pp.276–277 记其受托记录 Lord Manchester 入城并绘制 Frederick IV 庆典赛舟；第13章 p.332 将其《Le Fabriche e Vedute di Venetia》（1703）列为面向旅行者的图书插图出版物。

Chapter 8 pp. 227–228 quotes Marchesini on Carlevarijs’s views. Chapter 10 pp. 276–277 records the Lord Manchester entry commission and the regatta painting for Frederick IV. Chapter 13 p. 332 names his Le Fabriche e Vedute di Venetia (1703) as an illustrated publication designed for travellers.
