## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第13章将 Antonio Zatta 列为18世纪威尼斯三位重要出版商之一。哈斯克尔称他在耶稣会争议中积极支持耶稣会，并曾因提议出版针对其对手的文本而与 Inquisitori 发生冲突；同时记述他出版的范围由礼仪小册子扩展至意大利经典，目录显示其为当时威尼斯多产的出版商。哈斯克尔称 Zatta 支持当时不流行的 Dante，并赞助一部相隔两个世纪才再现的插图版《神曲》；他写作的前言被评价为鲜明，常有机智讽刺。

**English:** Chapter 13 lists Antonio Zatta among three important Venetian publishers of the eighteenth century. Haskell says he actively supported the Jesuits during political controversies and encountered the Inquisitori after proposing to publish texts attacking their opponents; his publishing ranged from ceremonial pamphlets to Italian classics, and his catalogues present him as a prolific publisher in Venice. Haskell says Zatta supported Dante when that view was unfashionable and sponsored an illustrated edition of the Commedia, the first to appear for two centuries; his prefaces are described as vivid and sometimes witty.
