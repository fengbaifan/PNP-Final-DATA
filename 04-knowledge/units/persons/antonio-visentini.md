## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 全书索引以“Visentini, Antonio”列出第10章注释与正文及第13章的页码。第10章 p.308 以全名记载 Antonio Visentini 长期为 Joseph Smith 担任建筑师和书籍插图者；p.306 称 Smith 保留的原始绘稿多为 Visentini 所作，p.309 记其续写 Gallacini 的建筑批评。第13章再次提到 Pasquali 支持 Visentini 对巴洛克建筑的批评。Treccani DBI、意大利文化遗产总目录及威尼斯学院美术馆记录在姓名、威尼斯活动语境和1688–1782年日期上吻合；美术馆使用变体名 Antonio Maria Visentini。中文名为项目工作音译。[全书索引 UVWXYZ.csv#119](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/UVWXYZ.csv) [第10章 p.306](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L437） [第10章 pp.308–309](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)（L464–478） [第13章 p.338](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md)（L65–66） [Treccani DBI](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/antonio-visentini_(Dizionario-Biografico)/) [意大利文化遗产总目录（ICCD）](https://catalogo.beniculturali.it/Agent/7304fc00c8dfda8cd628ce9554df755d) [威尼斯学院美术馆目录](https://www.gallerieaccademia.it/opera/fantasia-architettonica/)

**English:** The book’s index lists “Visentini, Antonio” across the notes and text of Chapter 10 and the text of Chapter 13. Chapter 10 names Antonio Visentini as an architect and book illustrator employed by Joseph Smith for more than fifteen years; it says that most of the original drawings retained by Smith were by Visentini and records his continuation of Gallaccini’s architectural criticism. Chapter 13 again mentions Pasquali’s support for Visentini’s criticism of Baroque architecture. Treccani’s DBI, the Italian national cultural heritage catalogue, and the Gallerie dell’Accademia records agree on the identity, Venetian context, and dates 1688–1782; the museum uses the variant name Antonio Maria Visentini. The Chinese display name is a project working transliteration.
