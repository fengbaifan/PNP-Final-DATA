### 描述

**中文：** 曼图亚驻罗马代理人，1621-10-09 致公国大臣信称若要及时得到好画必须送来定金。大臣未具名。

**English:** Mantua's agent in Rome wrote to the unnamed ducal minister on 9 October 1621, insisting on a deposit to obtain good paintings promptly.
