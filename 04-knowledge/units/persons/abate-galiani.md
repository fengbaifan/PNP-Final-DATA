**English:** This card records the person named by Haskell as Abate Galiani. On p. 318, Haskell describes *Della Moneta* as his treatise and says it appeared in 1750. The record preserves the name form used in the book and does not infer a fuller personal name, dates, birthplace, or other biography. The Chinese display name transliterates the surname.
