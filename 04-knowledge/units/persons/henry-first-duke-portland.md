**中文：** Haskell将此人物称作 Henry，并记他是 William Bentinck 之子，1709年继承 Portland 爵位、1716年成为第一代公爵。他委托 Sebastiano Ricci 为城镇宅邸及 Bulstrode Park 乡间住宅礼拜堂作画。中文展示名按书后索引的头衔译出；来源未给出更完整的名字。〔[第10章印刷页279–280；Markdown L49、52–55](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)〕

**English:** Haskell identifies the person as Henry, son of William Bentinck, who succeeded to the Portland title in 1709 and was created first Duke in 1716. He commissioned Sebastiano Ricci to paint for his town house and the chapel at his country house at Bulstrode Park. The displayed name follows the index title; the source does not provide a fuller given name. ([第10章印刷页279–280；Markdown L49、52–55](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md))
