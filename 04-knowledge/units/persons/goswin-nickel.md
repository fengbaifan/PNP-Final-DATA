## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称 Goswin Nickel 为比利时耶稣会总会长，并记其曾建议将秘鲁遗产用于装饰罗马 Gesù 教堂的一座小堂。相关建议及西班牙方面的传闻均按书内叙述保留；中文名为项目工作音译。〔[第3章印刷页78、91；Markdown L24、160](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md)〕

**English:** Haskell identifies Goswin Nickel as the Belgian Superior General of the Jesuits and reports his proposal to use a Peruvian legacy to decorate a chapel in the Gesù in Rome. The proposal and the subsequent Spanish rumours are retained as the book's account. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration. ([第3章印刷页78、91；Markdown L24、160](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md))
