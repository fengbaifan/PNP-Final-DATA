## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记书后索引“Leopold Wilhelm, Archduke”及第7章 p.193 正文所指人物。Haskell称他曾任 Netherlands 总督，返回时带回在那里购得的画作，其中部分来自 Charles I 的画廊；作者还说他尤其喜爱16世纪早期威尼斯画家、赞助 Pietro della Vecchia，并说服 Pietro Liberi 于1658年前往 Vienna。Haskell记载大公于1662年去世，并将全部财产留给侄子 Emperor Leopold I。以上均保留为书内叙述；本卡不将收藏、赞助、邀请或遗赠内容写为正式关系，也不补外部生平。[第7章 p.193](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_iv.md)（L8–12） [书后索引](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-1-Index-MD/L.md)（L.csv#56）

**English:** This card records the person named in the index as “Leopold Wilhelm, Archduke” and in the Chapter 7 passage on p.193. Haskell says that he had been Governor of the Netherlands and returned with pictures he had acquired there, some from Charles I’s gallery. The author also says that he especially favoured early sixteenth-century Venetian painters, patronized Pietro della Vecchia, and persuaded Pietro Liberi to go to Vienna in 1658. Haskell records the Archduke’s death in 1662 and says that he left all his possessions to his nephew, Emperor Leopold I. These remain statements in the book; this card does not register collecting, patronage, recruitment, or bequest as formal relations and adds no external biography. ### 身份边界

本卡只对应索引主项及第7章 p.193 的 Archduke Leopold Wilhelm。Emperor Leopold I 是其侄子，保持为独立人物；Prince Eugene、Pietro della Vecchia、Pietro Liberi、Charles I 及 Netherlands、Vienna、Prague 也分别保持独立。文中所述抢掠、收藏来源和艺术赞助均按 Haskell 的叙述范围保留。

This card refers only to the Archduke Leopold Wilhelm in the index and the Chapter 7 passage. Emperor Leopold I is identified as his nephew and remains a separate person. Prince Eugene, Pietro della Vecchia, Pietro Liberi, Charles I, the Netherlands, Vienna, and Prague also remain distinct. Accounts of looting, collection provenance, and artistic patronage are kept within Haskell’s stated scope.
