## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第7章称John Closterman为肖像画家，并叙述Shaftesbury于1699年派他赴罗马寻找可制作一组美德雕像的雕塑家；Closterman寄来Domenico Guidi的审慎与正义图稿，并曾为Shaftesbury绘制全身肖像。原书索引拼作Clostermann, John，正文拼作Closterman；本卡保留这项拼写差异，不据此拆分人物。〔[第7章，印刷页198](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_iv.md)；[索引 C.csv#321](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/C.csv)〕

**English:** Chapter 7 calls John Closterman a portrait painter and says Shaftesbury sent him to Rome in 1699 to find a sculptor for a series of Virtues. Closterman sent drawings of Prudence and Justice by Domenico Guidi and had already painted a full-length portrait of Shaftesbury. The index spells the surname Clostermann, while the chapter text spells it Closterman; the difference is retained and does not split the person. ([第7章，印刷页198](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_iv.md); [索引 C.csv#321](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/C.csv))
