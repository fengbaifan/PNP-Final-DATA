## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应索引词头“Rezzonico, Aurelio”及第9章p.254所称Aurelio。Haskell称其为Lodovico之父，记其1751年任Procuratore di S. Marco并购入Bon家族的一座宫殿。同页另称其兄弟Carlo当选教皇Clement XIII；该兄弟是不同人物身份组，相关关系候选留待S6。

**English:** This card represents the index heading “Rezzonico, Aurelio” and Aurelio named on chapter 9 p. 254. Haskell identifies him as Lodovico’s father and says he became Procuratore di S. Marco in 1751 and bought a palace from the Bon family that year. The same page names his brother Carlo as Pope Clement XIII; Carlo remains a distinct person identity group, and the relationship candidate awaits S6.
