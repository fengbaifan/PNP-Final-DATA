## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 弗朗切斯科·彼得鲁奇（约1660–1719）是沃尔泰拉诺的学生，作为宫廷画家和复制画家为托斯卡纳大公子费迪南多工作，也为版画提供绘图。

**English:** Francesco Petrucci (c. 1660–1719) was a pupil of Volterrano who worked as a court painter and copyist for Grand Prince Ferdinand of Tuscany and also supplied drawings for prints.
