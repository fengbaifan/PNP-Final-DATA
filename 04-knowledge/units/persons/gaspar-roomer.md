**中文：** 在那不勒斯经商的佛兰德商人与收藏家，馆方认为他可能委托鲁本斯绘制《希律王宴会》。

**English:** A Flemish merchant and collector based in Naples, identified by the gallery as the probable commissioner of Rubens’s Feast of Herod.
