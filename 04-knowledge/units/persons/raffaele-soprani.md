## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 中文显示名为本项目工作音译。Haskell第21章书目采用“Raffaello Soprani”这一姓名形式，著录经Carlo Giuseppe Ratti修订增注的1768年版。Treccani DBI以Raffaele Soprani为规范名；Biblioteca Braidense目录将Raffaello列为Raffaele的异名形式。此处仅记录同一人物的姓名对齐与书目身份依据，不表示已读Haskell所引著作的内容。

**English:** The Chinese display name is a working transliteration. Haskell’s Chapter 21 bibliography uses the name form “Raffaello Soprani” for the 1768 edition revised and expanded by Carlo Giuseppe Ratti. Treccani DBI uses Raffaele Soprani as the standard name, and the Biblioteca Braidense catalogue lists Raffaello as a variant of Raffaele. This records the name alignment and bibliographic identity evidence only; it does not indicate that the cited work has been read.
