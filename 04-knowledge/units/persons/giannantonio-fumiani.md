## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 乔瓦尼·安东尼奥·富米亚尼是威尼斯画家，擅长透视建筑构图，也从事舞台绘景；图版40b为其《撒迦利亚被石击》。

**English:** Giovanni Antonio Fumiani was a Venetian painter specializing in architectural perspective who also painted theatrical scenery; plate 40b reproduces his Stoning of Zechariah.
