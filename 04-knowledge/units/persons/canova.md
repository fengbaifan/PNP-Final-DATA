## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 雕塑家，亦从事绘画；曾得到沃尔帕托支持，本书列有他的斯瓦耶尔肖像。

**English:** A sculptor who also practised painting; he received support from Volpato, and the book lists his portrait of Swajer.
