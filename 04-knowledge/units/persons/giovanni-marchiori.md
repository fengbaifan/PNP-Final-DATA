## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第14章具名的雕塑家Giovanni Marchiori。第356页称Algarotti为他取得柏林一座教堂人物雕像的委托；书后索引以“Marchiori, Giovanni”指向该页。此卡不独立核实委托后续或雕像身份。中文显示名为项目工作音译。

**English:** This card records the sculptor Giovanni Marchiori named in Chapter 14. Page 356 says that Algarotti obtained for him a commission to produce figures for a church in Berlin; the index lists “Marchiori, Giovanni” at that page. This card does not independently verify the commission's outcome or identify the figures. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration.
