## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡按书末索引姓名记录 Count Camillo Carandini。第5章称他是 Modenese 伯爵、倾向西班牙一方，并将他列为 Cerquozzi 此类绘画的收藏者之一。第5章注2在其姓名后列出1619–1663；本卡保留为原书所列日期，不据此补充其他传记事实。[S1][S2][S3]

**English:** This unit records Count Camillo Carandini under the name in the book index. Chapter 5 describes him as a Modenese count aligned with the Spanish cause and names him among collectors of this type of Cerquozzi picture. Note 2 on page 137 lists 1619–1663 after his name; the dates are retained as printed, without adding further biographical claims. [S1][S2][S3]
