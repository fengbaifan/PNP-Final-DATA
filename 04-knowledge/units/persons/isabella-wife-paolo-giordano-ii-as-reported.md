## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应Haskell第4章印刷页97所称的Paolo Giordano II之妻Isabella。原书本段只给出名“Isabella”，没有提供全名或外部身份标识。第4章印刷页98注3另称Paolo Giordano I之妻Isabella de' Medici，并报告她被勒死；本卡依这两项不同的婚姻关系保留书内人物区分，不补出此处Isabella的姓氏或生平。

**English:** This card represents the Isabella whom Haskell identifies on printed page 97 as the wife of Paolo Giordano II. The passage gives only the name “Isabella,” with no full name or external identifier. On printed page 98 note 3, Haskell separately names Paolo Giordano I's wife as Isabella de' Medici and reports that she was strangled. The two source-described identities are kept distinct by their different marital relations; no surname or biography is supplied for this Isabella.
