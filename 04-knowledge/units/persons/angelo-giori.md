### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记书后索引 G.csv#97“Giori, Angelo”所指的 Angelo Giori。索引将他定位于第2章第41、59页；两处正文均重复其全名，构成跨章身份锚点。第41页中，Haskell称他曾在 Camerino 任校长，听说有晋升机会后步行到 Rome；后来监督 Urban VIII 墓的工程，被作者称为 Barberini 家族的 protégé，并是 Claude 的特别赞助人，拥有 Claude 的七幅画。作者在同段使用 “rather mean” 这一评价，本卡将其保留为作者措辞，不作客观人格事实。第59页称在 Bernini 完成 Urban VIII 墓时，Giori 致信遭流放的枢机，建议他们向艺术家致贺；“who was in charge” 的责任范围未说明。以上均是 Haskell 的书内叙述，不据脚注补充外部生平。[第2章 p.41](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_ii.md)（L125） [第2章 p.59](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_vii.md)（L14） [书后索引](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-1-Index-MD/G.md)（G.csv#97）

**English:** This card records the Angelo Giori named in index entry G.csv#97. The index locates him on pages 41 and 59 of Chapter 2, where the text repeats his full name and provides cross-chapter identity anchors. On page 41, Haskell says that Giori had been a schoolmaster in Camerino and walked to Rome after hearing of opportunities for preferment; he later supervised work on Urban VIII’s tomb, is described by the author as a Barberini protégé, and is called Claude’s special patron who owned seven of Claude’s pictures. Haskell also uses the evaluative phrase “rather mean”; this card preserves it as the author’s characterization and does not treat it as an objective personality fact. Page 59 says that, when Bernini completed Urban VIII’s tomb, Giori wrote to the exiled Cardinals suggesting that they congratulate the artist; the scope of “who was in charge” is unspecified. These are Haskell’s statements in the book; no external biography is added from cited footnotes. ### 身份边界

本卡只对应索引及第2章两处具名的 Angelo Giori。第41页注5、注6所引材料未在本轮独立核读；不推定他与 Barberini 的正式职务、亲缘或法律关系，也不补充“负责”一词的行政范围。Bernini、Claude、Sacchi、Urban VIII、枢机、Camerino 与 Rome 均为独立对象。

This card refers only to the named Angelo Giori in the index and the two Chapter 2 passages. The materials cited in notes 5 and 6 on page 41 have not been independently consulted. No formal office, kinship, or legal relationship with the Barberini is inferred, and the administrative scope of “in charge” is not expanded. Bernini, Claude, Sacchi, Urban VIII, the Cardinals, Camerino, and Rome remain separate objects.
