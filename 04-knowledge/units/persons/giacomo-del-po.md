**English:** This card registers Giacomo del Po from the index and the chapter 7, p. 200 passage. Haskell says that he, Solimena, and Paolo de Matteis each produced canvases to decorate Daun’s palace in Vienna. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration. Neither the canvas group nor individual artist-to-canvas assignments are identified; this card does not infer an external identity or specific work attribution.
