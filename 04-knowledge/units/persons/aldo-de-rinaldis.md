## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡仅登记 Accademia dei Georgofili 图书馆目录所列 Aldo De Rinaldis，责任范围为转录并注释1939年 Rosa—Ricciardi 书信集。第6、7章的姓氏式引注与该版本书目记录相连。本记录不扩写生平，不称其为Rosa原信作者，也不把“转录并注释”改写成未经来源支持的编辑或编纂关系。

**English:** This unit records only the Aldo De Rinaldis named by the Accademia dei Georgofili catalogue as transcriber and annotator of the 1939 Rosa–Ricciardi letter collection. The surname references in Chapters 6 and 7 connect to that edition. No biography is added, he is not described as the author of Rosa’s letters, and “transcribed and annotated” is not recast as an unsupported editor or compiler role.


### 身份边界

身份仅限该书目责任项；未发现足以建立更广泛人物身份的权威标识。
