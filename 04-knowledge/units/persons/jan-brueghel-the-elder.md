### 本书相关内容

**中文：** Haskell将其列入Roomer藏画的开放作者名单；数百件是多人作品总量，不归给该人物个人。同一名单另有Abram Breughel相关记录，但本候选的别称由ULAN对应Jan Brueghel the Elder；没有依据把两张人物卡合并。

**English:** Haskell includes him in an open list of makers represented in Roomer's collection; several hundred is the collective total, not an individual count. Abram Breughel is represented elsewhere in the book, but ULAN resolves this candidate's nickname to Jan Brueghel the Elder; there is no basis for merging the two person records.
