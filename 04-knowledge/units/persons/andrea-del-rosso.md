## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第8章称 Andrea 为德尔·罗索兄弟中的长兄，生于1640年，36岁时获授与稍年轻的 Lorenzo 共同拥有的面粉专卖特许；他后来成为参议员，并任教皇邮政总局局长。Haskell 记其经常将画作借予罗马 S. Salvatore in Lauro 的展览，并称其于1715年去世。第8章另述兄弟的祖父 Andrea：祖父在1642年时已距去世两年，p.212 又记其建造家族礼拜堂；该祖父与本卡长兄是两代同名人物。

**English:** Chapter 8 identifies Andrea as the eldest of the del Rosso brothers, born in 1640. At age 36 he received the lucrative flour monopoly shared with the slightly younger Lorenzo; both later became senators, and Andrea served as director-general of the papal postal services. Haskell says Andrea frequently lent pictures to exhibitions at S. Salvatore in Lauro in Rome and reports that he died in 1715. The chapter separately identifies the brothers' grandfather, also named Andrea: in 1642 he was two years from death, and p. 212 continues the account with his family chapel. The grandfather is a different generation and person from this card's subject.
