## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 泰奥多尔—埃内斯特·科涅克是商人、慈善家及收藏者，与妻玛丽—路易丝·杰共同收藏艺术品；其1928年遗赠给巴黎市的收藏成为科涅克—杰博物馆的基础。

**English:** Théodore-Ernest Cognacq was a merchant, philanthropist and collector who collected art with his wife Marie-Louise Jaÿ; his 1928 bequest to the City of Paris formed the basis of Musée Cognacq-Jay.
