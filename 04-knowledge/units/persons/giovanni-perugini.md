## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Turin 宫廷所保护的画家；1676 年萨伏依代表将其行为与“画家多少有些疯狂”的观念相连，是被引评价。

**English:** Protected by the Turin court, Perugini appears in a Savoy representative's 1676 comments connecting his behaviour with supposed artistic madness. That is an attributed judgement.
