## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡依 Haskell 书末索引的 “Styles, Benjamin” 展开姓名；第10章正文称其为 Mr Styles。Haskell 称他是 Amigoni 在英国第三位且最重要的赞助人，并记述其 Moor Park 乡间宅邸由 Thornhill、后由 Giacomo Leoni 建造；书中没有分明两位建筑师各自负责的阶段。Haskell 又称 Styles 与 Thornhill 争执后，把装饰任务交给 Amigoni 与 Francesco Sleter。此卡仅记录书内称谓和叙述，不补写外部身份或生平。[S1][S2][S3]

**English:** This unit expands the name from Haskell’s index entry “Styles, Benjamin”; the Chapter 10 narrative calls him Mr Styles. Haskell describes him as Amigoni’s third and most important English patron and says his country house at Moor Park was built by Thornhill and later Giacomo Leoni; the book does not specify the phases undertaken by each architect. Haskell also says that, after Styles quarrelled with Thornhill, he gave the decoration job to Amigoni and Francesco Sleter. This unit records only the book’s name form and account and adds no external identification or biography. [S1][S2][S3]
