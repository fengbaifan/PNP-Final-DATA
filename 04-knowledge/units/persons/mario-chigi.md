## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔在第6章 p.154 称 Don Mario Chigi 为教皇之弟；索引进一步标为教皇亚历山大七世之弟。正文转述 Baldinucci 对 Salvator Rosa 画作争议的记载，并说 Don Mario 的介入使画家免于在监狱解释画意。哈斯克尔还把 Don Mario 列为其对《Fortune》讽刺解读明显影射的人物，并称红衣主教 Flavio Chigi 是其子。介入、亲缘与影射均作为原书关系候选保留，尚未写成正式关系；“double irony”是作者的修辞判断。

**English:** On p. 154 of chapter 6, Haskell calls Don Mario Chigi the Pope’s brother; the index specifies Alexander VII. Recounting Baldinucci on the controversy over Salvator Rosa’s picture, Haskell says Don Mario’s intervention spared the painter from having to explain the image in prison. Haskell also names Don Mario among those most obviously implicated in his reading of the picture as a satire on Fortune, and identifies Cardinal Flavio Chigi as his son. The intervention, kinship, and implication remain source-attributed relation candidates, not formal relations; “double irony” is Haskell’s rhetorical interpretation.

### 名称

中文显示名及英文显示名见卡片标题；原书称谓 Don Mario Chigi 按来源引文保留，不在本卡推定其为正式贵族头衔。

The bilingual display names appear in the card title. The source form “Don Mario Chigi” is preserved in the quotation; this card does not treat Don as a verified formal noble title.

### 身份核对

Treccani《意大利人传记辞典》第88卷 Salvator Rosa 条目[PDF](https://static.treccani.it/export/sites/default/catalogo/catalogo_prodotti/grandi_opere/biografia_nazionale/pdf/DBI88_Rosa_Salvatore.pdf)（访问于 2026-10-17）提到 1659 年 Rosa 展出讽刺 Chigi 教廷裙带关系的《Fortune》，并称 Mario Chigi（教皇之弟）为 Rosa 辩护、使其免受争议指控。该姓名、教皇亲缘及同一画作争议与本书 p.154 的 Don Mario Chigi 相合；此来源用于身份比对，不把外部生平字段或精确关系写入卡片。

### 身份边界

中文：本卡仅指第6章 p.154 的 Don Mario Chigi／索引所称亚历山大七世之弟。不要与其子 Cardinal Flavio Chigi 或其他 Chigi 家族成员合并。

English: This card refers to Don Mario Chigi in chapter 6 p. 154, indexed as Alexander VII’s brother. Keep him distinct from his son Cardinal Flavio Chigi and other members of the Chigi family.
