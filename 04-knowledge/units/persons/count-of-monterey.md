## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Count of Monterey 是17世纪上半叶意大利最显著的西班牙赞助人。第5章称他于1630年任罗马大使并接待 Velasquez；第7章接续称他于1631年调往 Naples 并任总督，购买绘画、继续雇用在罗马接触的艺术家，也为 Domenichino、Lanfranco 与 Ribera 提供委托或赞助。

**English:** Haskell calls the Count of Monterey the most conspicuous Spanish patron in Italy during the first half of the seventeenth century. Chapter 5 identifies him as the ambassador in Rome who welcomed Velasquez in 1630. Chapter 7 continues the account with his transfer to Naples as viceroy in 1631, his purchases of paintings, his continued employment of artists he had met in Rome, and commissions or patronage involving Domenichino, Lanfranco, and Ribera.
