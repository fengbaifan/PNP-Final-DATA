## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应索引词头“Walpole, Horace”及第9、10、14章中的Horace Walpole。第9章称他于1739至1741年在意大利旅行并听闻Sagredo收藏拟出售；第10章称他讥称Joseph Smith为“威尼斯商人”；第14章称Algarotti曾计划为他购入一幅Veronese作品，原书并未说该计划已实现。

**English:** This card represents the index entry “Walpole, Horace” and Horace Walpole in chapters 9, 10, and 14. Chapter 9 says that he heard of the proposed sale of the Sagredo collection while travelling in Italy between 1739 and 1741. Chapter 10 says he jeered at Joseph Smith as the “Merchant of Venice.” Chapter 14 says Algarotti planned to acquire a Veronese for him; the text does not say that the plan was carried out.
