**English:** This card records the Chapter 10 figure indexed as “Schönborn, Friedrich Karl” and also called “Frederick Karl” in the body. Haskell places his death in 1746 and says the Würzburg bishopric passed to Karl Philip von Greiffenklau after a short successor interval; a footnote, citing Von Freeden, identifies him as the addressee of Johann Philip Franz's letter of 12 July 1723 introducing Pellegrini. The letter was not independently consulted in this work; these details remain limited to Haskell's account. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration.
