## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记第6章和第7章具名的枢机 Girolamo Colonna，中文名采用项目工作音译。第6章称他是 Filippo Colonna 之子，并说父子在本世纪上半叶大幅重建和装饰 Colonna 家族宫殿；该句没有区分 Girolamo 的个人贡献。第7章称 Mazarin 曾陪同 Girolamo 前往西班牙，随后 Girolamo 担任家族所募军团的队长以收复意大利北部 Valtellina，原文未给出旅程日期。以上仅记录 Haskell 的书内叙述，未作外部人物或事件核验。[第6章 p.155](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md)（L105）；[第7章 p.180](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md)（L132–133）

**English:** This card records Cardinal Girolamo Colonna, named in chapters 6 and 7; the Chinese name is a project transliteration. Chapter 6 identifies him as Filippo Colonna’s son and says that the two substantially rebuilt and decorated the Colonna family palace during the first half of the century; the sentence does not distinguish Girolamo’s individual contribution. Chapter 7 says that Mazarin accompanied Girolamo to Spain, after which Girolamo served as captain of a regiment raised by the family to reconquer Valtellina in northern Italy. The passage gives no date for the journey. These remain Haskell’s reports and have not been externally verified. [第6章 p.155](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md)（L105）; [第7章 p.180](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_i.md)（L132–133）
