## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记书后索引作“Schmidt, Georg Friedrich”、第20章后记具名的Georg Friedrich Schmidt。Haskell转述Maria Santifaller的研究，称Algarotti的大多数肖像源自Schmidt于1752年在柏林制作的版画；同段另称Schmidt此前为Algarotti蚀刻过歌手Felice Salimbeni的肖像。本卡仅记录书内识别与归因，不独立核验相关版画或研究结论。中文显示名为项目工作音译。

**English:** This card records Georg Friedrich Schmidt, indexed as “Schmidt, Georg Friedrich” and named in Chapter 20's postscript. Haskell reports Maria Santifaller's finding that most portraits of Algarotti derived from a print Schmidt made in Berlin in 1752; the same passage separately says Schmidt had etched a portrait of the singer Felice Salimbeni for Algarotti. This records the book's identification and attribution without independently verifying the prints or the reported scholarship. The Chinese display name is a working transliteration.
