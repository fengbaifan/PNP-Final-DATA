## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 全书索引以 “Brustolon, Andrea” 为人物主项，指向第337与340页；第13章正文两处以 Brustolon 指称同一雕刻者。哈斯克尔称《Dactylografa Smithiana》含有由 Brustolon 雕刻的100幅图版，并特别指出他依据 Pietro Antonio Novelli 的图稿刻制了一幅 Perseus 与 Medusa 题材尾页图。第340页则说 Canaletto 的杜卡尔仪式图稿“拟由” Brustolon 雕刻，故只记录计划。以上为书内叙述，未对外部生平或原书实物作核验。中文名为本项目工作音译。[第13章 pp.337、340](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md)（L54、56、87） [全书索引 B.csv#310](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/B.csv)

**English:** The book’s index lists “Brustolon, Andrea” as a person entry and points to pp. 337 and 340; Chapter 13 refers to the same engraver by surname in both places. Haskell says the *Dactylografa Smithiana* contained 100 plates engraved by Brustolon and singles out an endplate of Perseus and the Head of Medusa, engraved after a drawing by Pietro Antonio Novelli. On p. 340, Haskell says Canaletto’s drawings of Ducal ceremonies were “to be engraved” by Brustolon, which records a plan rather than proof of completion. These are statements in the book; no external biography or copy of the illustrated book was consulted. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration.
