## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应索引词头“Galgario, Fra (Vittore Ghislandi)”及第6、7章中的Fra Galgario／Vittore Ghislandi。第6章称Fra Galgario与威尼斯密切相关、居于Bergamo，并拒绝Ottoboni召其赴罗马；第7章将Vittore Ghislandi列入Prince Eugene收藏中的画家，并在注释提及其肖像。索引将两种称谓置于同一词头。

**English:** This card represents the index entry “Galgario, Fra (Vittore Ghislandi)” and Fra Galgario / Vittore Ghislandi in chapters 6 and 7. Chapter 6 says Fra Galgario was closely associated with Venice, lived in Bergamo, and refused Ottoboni’s call to Rome. Chapter 7 lists Vittore Ghislandi among painters represented in Prince Eugene’s collection and mentions his portrait in a note. The index places both names under one entry.
