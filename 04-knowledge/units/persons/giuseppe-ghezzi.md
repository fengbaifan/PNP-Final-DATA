### 描述

**中文：** 受西班牙大使邀请乘车，加入 Arcadia，并由 Clement XI、Innocent XIII 及帕尔马公爵授予不同职位/荣衔的艺术家。不同授予者及职务逐项区分。

**English:** Ghezzi received a carriage invitation from the Spanish ambassador, joined Arcadia, and received different offices or honours from Clement XI, Innocent XIII and Parma's duke. Grantors and appointments remain distinct.
