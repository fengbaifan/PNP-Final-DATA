## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录第7章注释中的萨伏依国王Carlo Emanuele III。Treccani人物辞典将他识别为Vittorio Amedeo II之子；该身份依据用于区分两位君主。Haskell关于他购得Prince Eugene收藏“大部分”的说法仍是原书经引文转述，未由此处的身份核对独立验证。中文名为项目工作音译。

**English:** This card records Carlo Emanuele III of Savoy, named in a Chapter 7 note. Treccani's biographical dictionary identifies him as the son of Vittorio Amedeo II; this identity evidence distinguishes the two rulers. Haskell's report that he bought “much of” Prince Eugene's collection remains a second-hand account and is not independently verified by the identity check. The Chinese display name is a project working transliteration.
