**English:** This card records the person named in index entry C.csv#171, “Chandos, James Brydges, 1st Duke of,” and by full name on Chapter 10, p.281. Haskell calls Brydges Bellucci’s most important English patron, says that he became the first Duke of Chandos in 1719, served eight years as Paymaster to the Forces, acquired Canons in 1713, and entertained friends there at concerts organized by Handel and Dr Pepusch. Haskell also says that he employed Bellucci with Bagutti and Francesco Sleter to decorate the chapel. The account of his fortune and the assessment “most important patron” remain attributed to Haskell. This card registers the person and does not turn offices, commissions, the house, or concerts into formal relations.
