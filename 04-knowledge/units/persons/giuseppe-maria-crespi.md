### 描述

**中文：** 朱塞佩·马里亚·克雷斯皮是意大利画家，曾为卢卡商人斯特凡诺·孔蒂绘制朱庇特题材画作。

**English:** Giuseppe Maria Crespi was an Italian painter who made a painting of Jupiter for the Lucca merchant Stefano Conti.
