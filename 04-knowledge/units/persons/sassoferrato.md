## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 乔万尼·巴蒂斯塔·萨尔维以萨索费拉托之名活动，擅长宗教画；本书图版21a、21b均列其作品。

**English:** Giovanni Battista Salvi, known as Sassoferrato, was a painter of religious subjects whose works are listed as plates 21a and 21b in the book.
