## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应索引词头“Zanetti, Girolamo”及候选cand-2861、cand-9280。第9、10、14章多处以全名引用其1743年文字或《Elogio》；第10章p.306称他曾评论其兄弟Antonio Maria为Joseph Smith复制宝石所得报酬及Smith古物。既有Anton Maria Zanetti the Elder人物卡与Girolamo为不同人物。

**English:** This card represents the index entry “Zanetti, Girolamo” and candidates cand-2861 and cand-9280. Chapters 9, 10, and 14 cite his writings from 1743 or his Elogio. On p. 306, chapter 10 says that he commented on his brother Antonio Maria’s payment for copying gems for Joseph Smith and on Smith’s antiquities. The existing person card for Anton Maria Zanetti the Elder represents a different person.
