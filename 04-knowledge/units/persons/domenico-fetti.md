### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应索引印本词头Fetti, Domenico，并与第7、9、18、19章的Domenico Fetti／Fetti提及相连。第7章将其列为曼图亚公爵Ferdinando雇用的艺术家；第9章把Fetti列入一份古代大师名单；第18章以他为某类独立、内省型艺术家的例外，这是Haskell的评价；第19章仅报告1789年拍卖清单中被说成来自Smith收藏的若干Fetti作品，不据此确认作者或原藏。CSV与Markdown索引转录写作Feti，印本页图核为Fetti；校勘依据见03过程记录。中文名为本项目工作音译。

**English:** This card represents the printed index heading Fetti, Domenico and its Domenico Fetti/Fetti mentions in chapters 7, 9, 18, and 19. Chapter 7 lists him among artists employed by Ferdinando, Duke of Mantua; chapter 9 includes Fetti in a list of old masters; chapter 18 presents him as an exception to a type of individual and withdrawn artist, an assessment by Haskell; and chapter 19 reports pictures by Fetti said to have come from Smith's collection in an 1789 sale, without establishing their authorship or provenance. The CSV and Markdown index transcriptions read Feti; the printed index image was checked as Fetti, as documented in the processing record. The Chinese name is a project working transliteration.
