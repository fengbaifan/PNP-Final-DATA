## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 提交绘画/素描方案供检查，某些合同禁止获准后擅改；必要性因时期、项目与画家声誉而异，不能设为所有历史委托必经关卡。

**English:** Patrons sometimes inspected painted or drawn designs before execution. Some contracts prohibited changes after approval without permission. Requirements varied by period, commission and reputation, so this was not an obligatory stage for every painting.
