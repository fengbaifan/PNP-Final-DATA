## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第8章称 Andrea del Rosso 获授有利可图的面粉专营权，并与 Lorenzo 共享；脚注将其记为佛罗伦萨城市及领地面粉收入的 Appalto Generale，自1676年6月1日起九年。本卡将正文与脚注所述授予事项登记为一个程序身份组，相关法令档案仍是独立对象。

**English:** Chapter 8 says Andrea del Rosso received a lucrative flour monopoly and shared it with Lorenzo. A footnote identifies it as the Appalto Generale of flour revenues for the City and Domain of Florence for nine years from 1 June 1676. This card registers the concession described in the text and note as one procedure identity group; the decree archive remains a separate object.
