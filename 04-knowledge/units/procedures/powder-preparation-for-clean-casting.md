## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 一封书中转录的信称，用于洁净铸造的粉末由一种 OCR 读作“dalco”、印本疑似为“talco”的材料充分焙烧后细筛制成；材料读法仍有疑问。

**English:** A letter quoted in the book reports that powder for clean casting was made by thoroughly burning and finely sifting a material read by OCR as “dalco” and possibly printed as “talco”; the material reading remains uncertain.
