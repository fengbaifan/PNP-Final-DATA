## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第13章将领先的当代画家有时制作、主要供雕版师复制的绘画列为出版赞助的第二种路径，并与复制既有名作、绘制书籍插图底稿和艺术家自行制版分别区分。

**English:** Chapter 13 identifies pictures sometimes produced by leading contemporary painters primarily for reproduction by an engraver as a second route of publishing patronage, distinct from reproducing established masterpieces, preparing drawings for book illustrations, and artists making their own engravings.
