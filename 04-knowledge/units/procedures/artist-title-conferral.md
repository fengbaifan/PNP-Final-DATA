## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 以骑士、伯爵和宫廷/教会职务承认艺术家身份；章中区分较早特殊恩宠与较晚常规化。

**English:** Knighthoods, comital titles and court or ecclesiastical offices could honour artists. Haskell contrasts exceptional early favours with their more routine later use; this is a historical pattern, not a uniform appointment procedure.
