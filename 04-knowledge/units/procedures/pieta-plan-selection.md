## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第9章称，Pietà 理事会似乎采用过一种做法：向最受看重且构想合适的画家征求方案，再择优选择。哈斯克尔以 A similar practice seems 限定其判断；本卡保留这种推测语气，不把它扩展为已证实、固定或普遍适用的制度。

**English:** Chapter 9 says that a similar practice seems to have been followed at the Pietà: the governors were to seek plans from highly regarded painters with suitable ideas and choose the best. Haskell’s phrase A similar practice seems qualifies the claim; this card does not turn it into a verified, fixed, or universal rule.
