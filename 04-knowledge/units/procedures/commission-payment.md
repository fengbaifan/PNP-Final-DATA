## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 先预付一部分，再按进度/完工支付或大型壁画月付；比例、材料和奖励由实例限定。lire、scudi、ducats 保留原币种，不相加或换算。

**English:** Payments could include an advance followed by instalments and a balance, or monthly payments for fresco work. Advance proportions, material costs and rewards depended on particular agreements. Amounts in lire, scudi and ducats must not be added or converted without further evidence.
