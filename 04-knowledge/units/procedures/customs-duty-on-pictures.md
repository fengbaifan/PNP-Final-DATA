## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第8章称宗教题材画作适用的海关税低于世俗题材；第16章在艺术品跨境运输语境下称海关手续棘手、必要时须设法规避。本卡只记录索引将两处归入的画作关税／海关事项，不推定税率、法域或固定操作步骤。

**English:** Chapter 8 says sacred pictures paid lower customs duty than profane ones. In the context of transporting art across borders, Chapter 16 says customs were troublesome and had to be evaded if possible. This card records only the picture-duty and customs topic grouped by the index; it infers no rate, jurisdiction, or fixed procedure.
