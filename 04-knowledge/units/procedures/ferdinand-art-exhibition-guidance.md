## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第8章称费迪南德借助艺术展览引导当时佛罗伦萨绘画方向，并在 Corpus Domini 游行期间展出其认可艺术家的作品；他还曾计划展出 Cassana 的肖像以影响本地画家。1706年较大型展览是另一个 event 身份组，本卡不与该事件合并。

**English:** Chapter 8 says Ferdinand used art exhibitions to guide the direction of painting in Florence, showing works by artists he approved during Corpus Domini processions; he also planned to exhibit a portrait by Cassana to influence local painters. The larger exhibition of 1706 is a separate event identity group and is not merged into this procedure.

### 本书语境

第8章 p.240；哈斯克尔把展览描述为费迪南德引导绘画方向的工具，并区分较随意的节庆展示与1706年更大规模的展览。

Chapter 8 (p. 240); Haskell describes exhibitions as an instrument Ferdinand used to guide painting and distinguishes casual festival displays from the larger exhibition of 1706.

### 身份与范围边界

**中文：** 只登记展览作为引导绘画的做法；不将一次具体展览、展览中的作品或参与者关系并入程序卡，相关正式关系留待 S6。

**English:** Register only the practice of using exhibitions to guide painting. A particular exhibition, its works, and participant relations remain distinct and await S6 review.
