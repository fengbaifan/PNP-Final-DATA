## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Rosa 在信中声称只在热情驱动时作画；本卡保留自我表述及 Haskell 对其意义的解释，不将灵感量化为事实字段。

**English:** Inspiration and enthusiasm form part of Rosa's explanation of artistic work and Haskell's interpretation of his position. These are attributed statements, not measurable causes proved for every painting.
