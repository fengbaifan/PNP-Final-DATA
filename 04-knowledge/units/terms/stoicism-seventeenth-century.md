## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语以书后索引 S.csv#199 的 Stoicism in 17th century 为锚，限于哈斯克尔第5章 pp.143–144 对斯多葛主义的讨论。作者称这一哲学自16世纪后期在欧洲传播，并对思想家、艺术家的伦理与图像产生影响；“几乎取代基督教信念”明确限定为 some daring thinkers 的情形。哈斯克尔还将 Rosa 信中的 Stoic 比喻解释为对斯多葛主义的暗指，称 Rosa 研究斯多葛作者以寻找绘画主题，并以《Regulus》说明其对艺术题材的影响。以上均为本书的历史概括或作者解释，不作为完整的斯多葛学派史，也不扩展为独立核实的普遍历史结论。[第5章 p.143](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_iv.md)（L15–16）；[第5章 p.144](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_iv.md)（L26）；[书后索引 S.csv#199](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-1-Index-MD/S.md)

**English:** This term is anchored to the index entry S.csv#199, “Stoicism in 17th century,” and is limited to Haskell’s discussion on pp.143–144 of Chapter 5. The author says the philosophy spread in Europe from the late sixteenth century and influenced the ethics and imagery of thinkers and artists; the claim that it “virtually replaced” Christianity as a guiding creed is explicitly limited to some daring thinkers. Haskell also interprets a Stoic metaphor in a letter by Rosa as an allusion to Stoicism, says Rosa studied Stoic writings for artistic themes, and uses Regulus to describe their influence on his treatment of subjects. These are the book’s historical summary and the author’s interpretation, not a comprehensive history of Stoicism or independently verified general conclusions.
