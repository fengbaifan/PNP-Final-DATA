## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录 Haskell 对卡尔米内学院第一套天顶装饰方案之拟议主题的举例：天使拯救炼狱中的灵魂。原文将其作为八幅周围画布所表现的多种教义主题之一；它不是八幅画布的完整题材清单，也不确定对应哪一件存世作品或具体人物形象。

**English:** This term records Haskell’s example of a proposed subject in the first ceiling scheme for the Scuola del Carmine: angels rescuing souls in purgatory. The passage presents it as one of various doctrinal subjects for the eight surrounding canvases, not as a complete list or an identified surviving work or specific figure.
