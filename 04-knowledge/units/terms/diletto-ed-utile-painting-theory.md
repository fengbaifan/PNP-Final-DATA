## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录Haskell所称旧有的 *diletto ed utile*（愉悦与效用）绘画理论。Haskell把绘画教化作用与精神休憩的说法称为这一旧理论的时新改编；本卡保留这一来源限定关联，不推断术语的起源、首次使用者或完整思想史。

**English:** This term records the older theory of *diletto ed utile* (pleasure and usefulness) in painting as Haskell describes it. Haskell calls the account of painting’s didactic function and mental relaxation a fashionable adaptation of that older theory. This card preserves that source-bounded connection without asserting the term’s origin, first user, or a complete intellectual history.

### 概念辨析

- 与Haskell所述Zanetti the Younger后出的有用艺术与赞助论证分开；原书说明其联系和先后，不把二者合并为同一术语。
- 与 *dilettante*（业余艺术爱好者／实践者）一词分开；词形相近不表示对象相同。
