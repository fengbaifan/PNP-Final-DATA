## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔将一个家族的政治野心列为艺术赞助变化因素的书内解释；同页随后谈及巴贝里尼扩张私人领地的计划。该卡表达作者的叙述和解释，不把政治动机或行动范围提升为独立核实的历史结论，也不与巴贝里尼家族这一 family 对象合并。

**English:** This term records Haskell’s account of a family’s political ambitions as a factor in changes to artistic patronage; the same page later discusses a Barberini scheme to extend private dominions. It preserves the author’s narrative and interpretation without treating motive or scope as independently verified, and remains distinct from the Barberini family as a family entity.
