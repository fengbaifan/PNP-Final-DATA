## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语表示 Haskell 在第8章所述比加里壁画中的天主教寓意主题。原文称此主题由Papacy代表；该概念在本项目中与教廷制度这一机构知识元分开，不延伸为对天主教教义的完整定义。

**English:** This term represents the Catholic Religion as the allegorical subject in Bigari’s fresco as described by Haskell in Chapter 8. The text says it is represented by the Papacy. This source-local concept remains distinct from the Papacy as an institution and does not define Catholic doctrine generally.
