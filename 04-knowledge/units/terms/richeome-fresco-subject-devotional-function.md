## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所转述的 Richeôme 观点：壁画题材应成为敬虔注意力的集中对象，并可像讲道一样阅读。该术语只指本段具名论述；具体壁画题材未在此列明，也不构成所有宗教艺术的普遍规则。中文名称为描述性译名。 [第3章书内来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L35; print p. 68）

**English:** This card records the view attributed by Haskell to Richeôme: fresco subject matter should focus devotional attention and may be read like a sermon. The term is limited to the named argument in this passage; no particular fresco subject is identified, and the statement is not treated as a rule for all religious art. The Chinese name is a descriptive translation. [第3章书内来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L35; print p. 68）
