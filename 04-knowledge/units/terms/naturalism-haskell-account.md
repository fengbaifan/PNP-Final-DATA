## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell在第4章和第6章对Naturalism／naturalism的两处艺术史用法：一处与古典绘画路径比较，一处评价其极端形式；不建立普遍定义或外部年代边界。

**English:** This unit records Haskell’s two uses of Naturalism/naturalism in Chapters 4 and 6: a comparison with the classical-painting path and a judgment about its extreme form. It does not establish a universal definition or external date boundary.
