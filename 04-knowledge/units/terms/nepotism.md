## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本章指新教皇将亲友客户带入有利职位的安排；私人领地式任用已改变，收入和赞助地位仍紧密依赖统治更替。

**English:** The chapter describes papal appointments and patronage benefiting relatives, friends and clients. Changes in access to fiefs and income affected this practice, but the resulting privileges were conditional and could change under a successor.
