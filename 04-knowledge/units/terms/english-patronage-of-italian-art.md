## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔第7章关于英国方面赞助意大利艺术及延请意大利艺术家的书内主题。第177页讨论英国方面争取意大利主要艺术家的早期努力；第7章后段转向17世纪后半的英国王室、赴意大利旅行者及 Shaftesbury 所鼓励的艺术赞助。这些场景在书中分属不同阶段和参与者，不构成单一机构或连续政策。

**English:** This term records Chapter 7’s account of English patronage of Italian art and efforts to recruit Italian artists. The chapter first discusses earlier English attempts to secure leading Italian artists, then turns to English royal patronage, travellers to Italy in the later seventeenth century, and the artistic patronage welcomed and stimulated by Shaftesbury. These episodes involve different periods and actors and do not constitute one institution or continuous policy.
