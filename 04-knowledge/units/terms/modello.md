## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本章使用的预备油画稿/模型概念，可用于向赞助人展示设计、辅助大型壁画施工。需与一般素描相区分；“在画布上展示设计”也不必等同独立收藏性小幅草图。

Haskell 指出上半世纪并非普遍强制，后半世纪更常见。科尔托纳若干所谓 modello 的归属受争议，盖乌利从热那亚引入做法是可能解释，不立为确定创新者关系。

**English:** A modello could serve as a preliminary painted design, especially for fresco planning and approval. It is distinct from a drawing or a design laid out directly on the final canvas. Haskell describes increased later use, disputed Cortona examples and only a possible Genoese contribution through Gaulli; he does not establish universal use or a single inventor.
