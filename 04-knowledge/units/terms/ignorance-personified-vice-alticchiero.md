## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell在阿尔蒂基耶罗祭坛语境中将Ignorance列为拟人化恶德之一，并称这些恶德在浮雕中得到表现。本卡记录来源中的概念用法，不将其等同于一件具体雕塑或独立人物。

**English:** In Haskell’s account of the Alticchiero altar, Ignorance is one of the personified vices, and the vices are reported as depicted in a bas-relief. This card records the source’s conceptual use; it does not identify a particular sculpture or individual person.
