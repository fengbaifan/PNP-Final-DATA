## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔所述乔尔达诺在西班牙宫殿与教堂装饰中引入的一种绘画类型。原书没有给出正式风格名称；“多彩、奔放、或许流于轻易、但技艺高超、轻盈而富于幻想”等均是哈斯克尔的描述。

**English:** This term records the unnamed type of painting that Haskell says Giordano introduced through his decoration of Spanish palaces and churches. The book supplies no formal style name; “colourful,” “exuberant,” “facile perhaps,” skilfully composed, airy, and fantastical are Haskell’s characterizations.
