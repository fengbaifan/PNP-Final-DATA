## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell在第10章p.281所述的威尼斯历史画家群体及其英格兰语境。Haskell称约1720年前十年这些画家在英格兰获得广泛支持，并提到富裕、显要与时尚领袖雇用画家装饰新宅。他以Pellegrini、Ricci和Bellucci的英国作品说明其叙述，但没有给出穷尽性的成员名单。本卡是原书语境中的描述性术语，不代表正式组织或固定成员名册。该页随后提到“更具冒险精神的威尼斯艺术家”及Düsseldorf、London、Paris的短期中心地位；此处不据相邻措辞断言两组成员完全相同。

**English:** This card records the group of Venetian history painters and its English context as described by Haskell on p.281 of Chapter 10. He says that these painters received wide support in England during the decade before about 1720 and that wealthy, influential, and fashionable patrons employed painters on new mansions. Pellegrini, Ricci, and Bellucci illustrate the account, but Haskell does not provide an exhaustive membership list. This is a descriptive term in the book’s context, not a formal organization or fixed roster. The page later mentions “more adventurous Venetian artists” and the brief standing of Düsseldorf, London, and Paris as centres; the adjacent wording does not establish that the two descriptions have identical membership.
