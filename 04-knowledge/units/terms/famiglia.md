## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本章的 prince’s famiglia 包括侍从、官员、受俸者和艺术家，表示家庭服务组织而非血缘家庭；其内部有地位/酬劳阶梯。

**English:** In the patronage context, famiglia includes household staff, office-holders and pensioned artists at different ranks. It denotes a household establishment rather than only blood relatives.
