## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 家户（famiglia）在本章指围绕王公或枢机组织的侍从、职员与受俸艺术家群体，并有等级及待遇差别。成员身份来自服务安排，不能据此认定血亲、共同姓氏或现代法人雇用关系。

**English:** In this chapter, famiglia denotes the establishment of courtiers, officials and pensioned artists around a prince or cardinal, with differentiated ranks and rewards. Membership derives from arrangements of service and does not establish kinship, a shared surname or employment by a modern corporation.
