## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔对乔尔达诺较暗那不勒斯绘画方式的描述。哈斯克尔把这种绘画方式作为乔尔达诺在赴西班牙以前留下的风格痕迹来叙述，并称提香作品的影响使其中“大部分”残余消退；此处的因果与评价均依原书归属。

**English:** This term records Haskell’s description of Giordano’s darker Neapolitan manner. Haskell presents it as a stylistic trace of Giordano’s earlier practice and says that the impact of Titian’s paintings led him to abandon “most” of its remaining vestiges; the causal account and evaluation remain attributed to the book.
