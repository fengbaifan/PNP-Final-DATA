## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔注4所述 1775 年拒绝获取威尼斯 patrician 身份机会的外省贵族集合称谓。注释未列出个人姓名、人数或集合边界；这里保留为来源局部群体术语，不构成人物名录。 [[来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md#L363)]

**English:** This card records the source-local collective term for provincial nobles whom Haskell’s note 4 says declined the opportunity to acquire Venetian patrician status in 1775. The note supplies no individual names, count, or membership boundary; this is not a roster of persons.

### 身份边界

该术语仅限注释所称的外省贵族集合，不与已具威尼斯贵族身份的家族或个人合并。

This term is limited to the provincial-noble group named in the note and is not merged with families or persons already holding Venetian patrician status.
