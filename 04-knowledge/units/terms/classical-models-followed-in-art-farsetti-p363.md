### 描述

**中文：** 本术语只记录哈斯克尔第15章 p.363 所说 Algarotti 与其他人关于追随古典范例之必要性的理论。其他倡导者未具名，原文也未将此命题表述为单一学派、固定名称的条约或普遍适用的规范；本卡不与一般新古典主义或其他来源中的理论自动合并。[第15章 p.363](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_i.md)（L32）

**English:** This term records only the theories described by Haskell on page 363 about the need to follow classical models, attributed to Algarotti and others. The other advocates are unnamed, and the passage does not present the claim as a single school, a named treatise, or a universal rule. This card is not automatically merged with Neoclassicism in general or with theories in other sources. [Chapter 15, p. 363](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_i.md) (L32)
