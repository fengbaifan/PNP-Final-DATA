## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔对路易十四胸像的解释：伯尼尼借此塑造个人君主制的理想化、庄严形象。这里保留作者对该件作品的阐释，不将其扩展为一般政治概念定义。

**English:** This term records Haskell’s interpretation of the Louis XIV bust: Bernini used it to build an idealised, hieratic image of personal monarchy. It preserves the author’s reading of this work and does not define personal monarchy as a general political concept.
