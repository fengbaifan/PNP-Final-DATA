## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔书中“art exhibitions”作为面向公众的绘画展示活动这一一般概念。第1章将展览写作罗马艺术市场中日益重要的实践，并称 Rosa 反复用展览建立声誉；第5章讨论其逐渐扩展、装饰性作用和历史影响；第8章举出 S. Salvatore in Lauro 的展览，第10章区分威尼斯不同地点的随意展示与较有组织的展览。书后索引将 `cand-0132`（S. Bartolommeo）、`cand-0134`（San Salvatore in Lauro）和 `cand-0137`（Piazza S. Marco）列在同一术语主项下；本卡不把这些地点或个别活动并入术语。

**English:** This card records “art exhibitions” as the general practice of displaying pictures to a public in Haskell's account. Chapter 1 calls exhibitions a growing feature of Rome's art market and says Rosa repeatedly used them to build his reputation; Chapter 5 discusses their extension, decorative use, and historical influence; Chapter 8 mentions exhibitions at S. Salvatore in Lauro, and Chapter 10 distinguishes casual display at one Venetian site from more systematic exhibitions elsewhere. The book index places `cand-0132` (S. Bartolommeo), `cand-0134` (San Salvatore in Lauro), and `cand-0137` (Piazza S. Marco) under the same term entry. The venues and particular activities remain separate.
