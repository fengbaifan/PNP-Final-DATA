## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本章用以描述低收入画家可能为经销商批量制作的作品类型；其生活状况一部分是作者重构，不能普遍归给具名画家。

**English:** Haskell discusses devotional pictures among the works made in quantity by cheaper painters for dealers. This describes a kind of production and use, not an inventory of identifiable objects.
