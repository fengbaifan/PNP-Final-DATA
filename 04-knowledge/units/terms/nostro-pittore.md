## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** “本府画家”是本章引用毛里齐奥任用巴尔多伊诺时的称谓。原语nostro pittore字面为“我们的画家”，在王公发话语境中表达依属；不能仅从这个短语推定全国统一官职、多人共同雇用或艺术家的人身所有权。（S1、S4、S5）

**English:** “Our painter” is the wording cited for Maurice of Savoy’s appointment of Baldoino. In this princely context it expresses affiliation to the appointing patron; the phrase alone does not establish a standardized national office, multiple employers or ownership of the artist. (S1, S4, S5)
