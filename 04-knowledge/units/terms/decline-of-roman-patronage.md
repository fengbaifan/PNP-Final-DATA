## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第6章以“The Decline of Roman Patronage”为章题，并称罗马艺术整体遭遇具有持久影响的打击；正文将经济、政治、瘟疫、饥荒、死亡及审美变化等列为因素，也记述 Innocent X 时期仍有“what patronage there was”。此卡记录作者的章节框架和书内用法，不把“衰退”处理为经独立验证、量化或意味着赞助完全停止的历史结论。

**English:** Chapter 6 is headed “The Decline of Roman Patronage” and says that the arts in Rome suffered a blow with lasting consequences. The narrative lists economic, political, plague, famine, death, and fashion-related factors, while also referring to “what patronage there was” under Innocent X. This unit records the author’s chapter framing and source usage; it does not treat “decline” as an independently verified, quantified, or total cessation of patronage.

### 本书语境

p.146 标题给出“衰退”框架；同页叙述称多种因素共同造成艺术变化。p.147 提到卡斯特罗战争及后续财政和政治背景，并称教宗本人仍安排当时尚存的赞助。

The p. 146 heading supplies the decline framing, and the same page attributes change in the arts to multiple factors. Page 147 discusses the War of Castro and later financial and political circumstances, while saying the Pope directed the patronage that remained.
