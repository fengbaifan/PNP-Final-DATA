## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语项记录哈斯克尔对 Flaminio Corner 祭坛设计偏好的转述：教堂祭坛应使用一致的大理石，并具有相同形状和尺寸。该项指来源所述的个人偏好与信条，不证明威尼斯教堂普遍采用了这一方案，也不表示每项赞助都按此实施。

**English:** This term records Haskell’s account of Flaminio Corner’s altar design preference: church altars should use matching marble and share the same shape and size. It represents a source-reported personal preference and creed; it does not establish general adoption by Venetian churches or implementation in every act of patronage.
