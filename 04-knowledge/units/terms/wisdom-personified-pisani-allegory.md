## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡仅记录哈斯克尔第9章关于图版44寓意画中被称作 Wisdom 的图像主题。原文先称 Faith、Hope、Charity 为 theological virtues，随后另列 Wisdom。本卡依该来源语境记录Wisdom；不据此界定更广义神学分类，也不推断它与其他作品中同名概念的身份同一性。

**English:** This card records only the iconographic subject called “Wisdom” in Haskell’s Chapter 9 description of the Plate 44 allegory. The source first names Faith, Hope, and Charity as the theological virtues, then lists Wisdom separately. The card remains scoped to this source context; it does not define broader theological classifications or determine whether this Wisdom is identical to the concept named in another work.
