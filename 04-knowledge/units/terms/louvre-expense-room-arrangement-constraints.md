### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔对科尔贝尔实际行政顾虑的描述：他关注开支及房间的具体安排。书中此处没有指认某一份确定的房间方案。

**English:** This term records Haskell’s description of Colbert’s practical administrative concerns: expense and the detailed arrangement of rooms. No particular room plan is identified here.
