## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 对《Le Fabriche e Vedute di Venetia》《Il Gran Teatro delle Pitture e Prospettive di Venezia》及 Marieschi 1741 年 views 所述的预期受众类别。原文称这些出版物面向旅行者；这不证明具体个人实际阅读、旅行者的国籍或群体成员身份。[[来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md#L12)]

**English:** This card records the intended readership category Haskell assigns to *Le Fabriche e Vedute di Venetia*, *Il Gran Teatro delle Pitture e Prospettive di Venezia*, and Marieschi’s 1741 views. The text says these publications were designed for travellers; it does not establish that particular people read them, the travellers’ national origins, or membership in a specific group.

### 身份边界

这是与三类来源所述出版物相联系的受众类别，不等同于实际到访威尼斯的旅行者、外国旅行者、具名读者或实际接受群体。原文没有说明这些受众均为外国人，也没有给出成员、国籍或访问日期。

This is an audience category attached to the publications named in this source passage. It is distinct from people who actually visited Venice, foreign travellers as a general category, named readers, or a demonstrated reception group. The passage does not say that all intended readers were foreign and identifies no members, nationalities, or visit dates.
