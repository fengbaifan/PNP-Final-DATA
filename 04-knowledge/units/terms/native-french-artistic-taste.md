## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔对“本土审美”的描述：第187页所述批评认为意大利建筑方案过于繁复、过于巴洛克，不合本土趣味；第188页称一种排斥意大利式繁饰的本土趣味已经形成。此处不推定法国公众意见一致。

**English:** This term records Haskell’s description of “native taste.” Reported criticism on p. 187 calls Italian architectural proposals too elaborate and too Baroque for that taste; p. 188 says a native taste had developed that found Italian floridity repugnant. The account does not establish a uniform public opinion.
