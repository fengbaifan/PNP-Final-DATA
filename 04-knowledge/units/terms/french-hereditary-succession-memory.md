## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔用于比较的法国王权继任观念：国王继承其敬重的父亲与祖父，并无意抹去前代记忆。该卡保存原书比较语境，不扩展为所有法国王室成员的个人态度。

**English:** This term records Haskell’s contrastive account of French royal succession: a king succeeded a revered father and grandfather and did not seek to erase their memory. The card preserves the book’s comparative context without generalizing to every member of the royal family.
