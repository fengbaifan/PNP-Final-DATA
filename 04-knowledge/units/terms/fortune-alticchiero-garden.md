## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** “Fortune”是哈斯克尔列于阿尔蒂基耶罗花园其他祭坛语境中的主题词。本卡保留原词，因为本段未充分区分抽象理想、拟人形象或具体祭坛对象；它也不指另有其名的 Salvator Rosa 绘画。

**English:** “Fortune” is a theme named by Haskell in the context of the other altars at Alticchiero. The original term is retained because this passage does not fully distinguish an abstract ideal, a personification, or a particular altar object. This is not the separate painting by Salvator Rosa.
