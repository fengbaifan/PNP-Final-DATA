## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔在第13章对 Zanetti 品味及相近欧洲业余鉴赏家品味的解释：洛可可与新古典风格在此语境中并不冲突；哈斯克尔又称 Francesco Algarotti 是保留这种风格综合的人之一，同时指出 Algarotti 比 Zanetti 更担忧表面上的品味矛盾。这里记录的是哈斯克尔对具体人物和语境的解释，不把两种风格并成普遍流派，也不把个别审美陈述扩展为人物的完整艺术理念。[第13章 p.343](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md)（L113）

**English:** This term records Haskell’s Chapter 13 interpretation of Zanetti’s taste and that of similar European amateurs: in this context Rococo and Neoclassical styles were not in conflict. Haskell also describes Francesco Algarotti as one of the men who retained this synthesis, while saying that Algarotti was more troubled than Zanetti by apparent contradictions in taste. The card preserves Haskell’s interpretation of these people and this passage; it does not turn the styles into a general movement or extend the remarks into a complete account of either man’s artistic ideas. [Chapter 13, p. 343](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md) (L113)
