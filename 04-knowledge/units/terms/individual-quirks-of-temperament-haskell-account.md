## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔第6章对“个体性情特殊倾向”的概括性用法。它是作者所列的解释因素类别；该段未指明哪些人物具有此类倾向，不能转化为任何个人的性格事实。

**English:** This term records Haskell’s general use of “individual quirks of temperament” in Chapter 6. It is an explanatory category in the author’s account; the passage names no individuals and does not establish a character trait for any person.
