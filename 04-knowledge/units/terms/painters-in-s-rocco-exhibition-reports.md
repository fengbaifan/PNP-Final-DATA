## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语项归集 Haskell 所引圣罗科展览报道中对参展画家的概括性称谓：Thomas Martyn 的文字称“当时威尼斯画派的画家”在 Scuola 展示作品；1762年《Nuova Gazzetta Veneta》提及古代与在世画家的其他作品；正文另称每年有若干画家参加。这里记录的是来源中的群体称谓，不是有名册的组织或一个可还原成员名单的历史社团。不同报道中的画家不据此视为完全相同的一组人。〔[第10章 p.330 注4](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_sec_ii.md)（L265–267）；[p.331](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_sec_ii.md)（L270）〕

**English:** This term groups the collective descriptions of exhibiting painters in reports cited by Haskell about [S. Rocco](../events/s-rocco-annual-exhibition.md). Thomas Martyn's text refers to “painters of the present Venetian school” showing their work at the Scuola; the 1762 *Nuova Gazzetta Veneta* mentions other works by old and living painters; the main text says that several artists took part each year. This is a source-defined collective label, not a named organization or a recoverable membership list. Painters in the different reports are not assumed to be exactly the same group. ([Chapter 10, p. 330, note 4](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_sec_ii.md) (lines 265–267); p. 331 (line 270).)

### 概念边界

- **适用范围：** Haskell 所引报道及其本人对[圣罗科年度展示](../events/s-rocco-annual-exhibition.md)的概括。
- **不适用边界：** 不指单一画家、完整参展名册或正式画家团体；不把各引文提及的全部画家合并为同一批成员。
