## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本条目记录 Haskell 第8章 p.231 对“Florentine Renaissance”的具体书内用法。该短语指其对伟大佛罗伦萨文艺复兴的艺术史表述；本卡不把它等同于佛罗伦萨城市，也不将其扩展为未限定的一般“Renaissance”时期。Haskell 称威尼斯绘画传统长期被视为它的对立面，但未说明持有这一看法的人群；此处保留为 Haskell 的历史叙述，不据此确定外部时期边界或独立艺术史结论。[来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md#L241)

**English:** This unit records Haskell’s specific in-book use of “Florentine Renaissance” in Chapter 8, p. 231. The phrase denotes his art-historical reference to the great Florentine Renaissance; this unit does not identify it with the city of Florence or expand it into an unqualified general Renaissance period. Haskell says the Venetian painting tradition had long been considered its antithesis, without identifying who held that view. This preserves Haskell’s historical account and does not establish external period boundaries or an independent art-historical conclusion.
