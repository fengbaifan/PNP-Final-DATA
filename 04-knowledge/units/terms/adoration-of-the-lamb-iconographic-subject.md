## 内容

### 定义与范围

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔在第3章 p.84 所称的羔羊崇拜图像主题。哈斯克尔称 Gaulli 在耶稣堂后殿恢复了这一主题，并指出在罗马，继 S. Prassede 与 S. Maria Maggiore 的马赛克之后，数百年间很少再见到这一主题。他描述的图像元素包括封印之书、七个印、七支火炬、二十四位长老、持棕枝的青年以及吹号和焚香的天使。此术语指图像主题本身，不指 Gaulli 的后殿壁画、两处较早的马赛克或拱顶原拟表现的基督受难主题。上述年代与艺术史表述均为哈斯克尔的书内陈述，未在本卡独立核验。来源：[第3章 p.84](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md#L76)。

**English:** This term records the iconographic subject Haskell calls the Adoration of the Lamb (Chapter 3, p. 84). Haskell says Gaulli revived the subject in the Gesù apse and notes that in Rome it had hardly been seen since the mosaics at S. Prassede and S. Maria Maggiore many centuries earlier. He describes its imagery as including the sealed book with seven seals, seven torches, twenty-four elders, youths with palms, and angels with trumpets and thuribles. The term denotes the subject itself, not Gaulli’s apse fresco, the two earlier mosaics, or the glorification of Christ’s suffering proposed for the vault. These chronological and art-historical statements remain Haskell’s account and have not been independently verified here. Source: [Chapter 3, p. 84](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md#L76).
