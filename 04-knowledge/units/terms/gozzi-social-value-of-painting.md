## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录Haskell对Gasparo Gozzi绘画论述的概括：Gozzi先以建筑、祭坛和绘画代表生产国的荣誉回应批评，随后试探性地强调艺术的社会价值，包括就业、资金流通和家庭生计。原书索引子目将此话题列在“Gozzi, Gasparo”之下；本卡表示其来源限定的论述主题，不是Gozzi人物本身，也不指称一篇题名未明的文章或书信。

**English:** This term records Haskell’s account of Gasparo Gozzi’s discussion of painting: Gozzi first answered criticism by invoking buildings, altars, and pictures as the producing country’s claim to glory, then tentatively stressed the arts’ social value in employment, circulating money, and family livelihoods. The book index lists this topic under “Gozzi, Gasparo.” This card represents that source-bounded position, not Gozzi as a person or an unidentified article or letter.
