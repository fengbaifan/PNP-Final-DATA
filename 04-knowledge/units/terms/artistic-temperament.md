## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 关于艺术家异常性、灵感与独立性的历史观念。本章区别 Vasari 暗示、十七世纪认可、后世固定化，不能当作临床性格诊断。

**English:** Haskell traces ideas about artists' exceptional behaviour and inspiration through Vasari, seventeenth-century examples and later formulations. These are historical representations of temperament, not clinical diagnoses of the people discussed.
