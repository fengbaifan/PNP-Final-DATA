## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔第7章关于英国对意大利艺术影响的特定历史主题。他将1700年前的影响与德国、奥地利涌来的委托相比，称其几乎可忽略；随后说下一个世纪这一影响变得重要到需要回顾其早期阶段。这是哈斯克尔对章节范围与历史分量的概括，不是量化的委托统计或独立确认的文化因果结论。相邻叙述还称查理二世对当代意大利艺术不感兴趣；该人物陈述不扩展为所有英国赞助者的共同态度。

**English:** This term records Chapter 7’s specific historical topic of English impact on Italian art. Haskell compares the impact before 1700 with the stream of commissions from Germany and Austria, calling it almost negligible, and then says it became important enough in the next century to warrant an outline of earlier stages. This is Haskell’s summary of the chapter’s scope and historical significance, not a quantified commission count or an independently verified causal conclusion. The adjacent account says Charles II showed no interest in contemporary Italian art; that person-specific statement is not generalized to all English patrons.
