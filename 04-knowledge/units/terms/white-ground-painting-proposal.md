## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录在绘画上使用白色底层、而非较常见红棕色底层的提议。Haskell依据一封致Eustachio Zanotti的1756年书信转述该想法，并认为信中暗示它刚刚出现；原信未独立查阅，本文不认定其发明者或确切提出时间。

**English:** This term records the proposal that artists paint on white grounds rather than the more usual reddish-brown grounds. Haskell attributes the report to a 1756 letter to Eustachio Zanotti and says it implies the idea had only just occurred; the letter was not independently consulted, and no inventor or exact inception date is asserted.
