## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录 Haskell 在教宗政府叙述中使用的 Secretary of State 职位称谓。第2章称 Monsignor Agucchi 担任这一有影响力的职位；第6章称一位未具名外来者被任命担任该职，并称教宗与已任命的国务秘书掌握着 Camillo Pamfili 相关安排中的重大政治权力。本卡使用“教宗政府”作为区分语境的描述性限定；原书未提供该职位的正式意大利语名称、完整职能或未具名任职者身份。职位与任职人物、任职事件及教宗政府机构分开记录。[第2章 p.28](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_i.md#L87)；[第6章 p.147](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md#L14)

**English:** This term records the title Secretary of State as Haskell uses it in his account of papal government. Chapter 2 says that Monsignor Agucchi held this influential post. Chapter 6 says that an unnamed outsider was brought into the office and that the Pope and the already appointed Secretary of State retained serious political authority in arrangements concerning Camillo Pamfili. “In the Papal Government” is a descriptive contextual qualifier used here; the book supplies no formal Italian title, complete account of the office’s functions, or identity for the unnamed officeholder. The office is kept distinct from its holders, appointments as events, and the papal government as an institution. [Chapter 2, p. 28](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_i.md#L87); [Chapter 6, p. 147](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md#L14)

### 名称与范围

原书使用英文称谓 *Secretary of State*。中文名称和“in the Papal Government”均为区分本书语境的描述性表达，不声称为该职务正式历史名称；卡片不补写任期制度或未见于所引段落的行政权限。
