## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 由枢机继任承接的教堂，区别于新建教堂和私人墓所；古老性与枢机赞助构成作者解释。

**English:** The chapter distinguishes a cardinal's titular church, potentially passing to a successor, from new foundations and private burial projects. This institutional setting helps explain patterns of decoration and patronage.
