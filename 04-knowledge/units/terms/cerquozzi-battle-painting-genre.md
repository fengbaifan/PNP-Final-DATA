## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔在切尔奎齐讨论中对战事绘画题材的表述：切尔奎齐主要专擅此类绘画，哈斯克尔并将其与 *Bambocciate* 作等级评价比较。此卡限于该段的书内分类与评价，不把它外推为普遍艺术史等级。

**English:** This term records Haskell’s account of battle painting in the discussion of Cerquozzi: Haskell says Cerquozzi chiefly specialized in this subject and compares it evaluatively with the *Bambocciate*. The card preserves this passage-specific classification and judgement rather than stating a general art-historical hierarchy.
