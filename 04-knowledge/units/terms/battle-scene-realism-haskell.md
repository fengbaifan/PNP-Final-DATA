## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语只记录哈斯克尔对切尔奎齐战事场景的“现实感”评价；原文将其部分联系到战事场景中的疏离、反英雄式混乱和远处硝烟。它不是对某件画作的独立鉴定，也不是此处已经定义的普遍艺术运动。

**English:** This term records Haskell’s quoted characterization of the “realism” of Cerquozzi’s battle scene, which he partly connects with detachment, anti-heroic confusion, and distant smoke. It is neither an independent authentication of a particular painting nor a general artistic movement defined by this passage.
