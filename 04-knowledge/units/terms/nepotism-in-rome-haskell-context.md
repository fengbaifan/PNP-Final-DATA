### 描述

**中文：** 记录 Haskell 在第1、2、6章讨论的罗马教皇亲族任用语境：教皇亲属及依附者的职位、收入和赞助机会与政治更替相关；第6章又提及克雷芒十世试图减少过度的 nepotism。不同章节为作者的历史叙述，不合并成一条具体任命关系。

**English:** This unit records Haskell’s Roman papal context for nepotism across Chapters 1, 2, and 6: offices, income, and patronage opportunities for papal relatives and clients are connected to political succession, and Chapter 6 notes Clement X’s attempt to reduce excessive nepotism. These passages remain the author’s historical account and are not merged into a specific appointment relation.
