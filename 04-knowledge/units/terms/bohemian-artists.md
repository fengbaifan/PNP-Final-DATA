## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 用以描述罗马部分北方艺术家的非正式社交与生活传统；不是按标签即可确定成员的制度组织。

**English:** Haskell describes an informal milieu of northern artists and their sociability. The description is not by itself the name of a formal organization or proof of an individual's membership.
