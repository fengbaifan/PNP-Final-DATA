## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称罗森贝格不信任寓意表达，认为绘于天花板的寓意难以理解，而雕塑中的寓意较容易把握，因为人物较少。此术语记录第15章 p.371 对寓意作为表现方式的讨论，不把罗森贝格的个人反应推广为普遍观看经验，也不指向某一特定作品。

**English:** Haskell says Mme Rosenberg distrusted allegory: she found allegorical ceilings difficult to understand and sculpture easier because it involved fewer figures. This term records the discussion of allegory as a representational mode on p.371; it does not generalize her response to all viewers or identify a particular work.
