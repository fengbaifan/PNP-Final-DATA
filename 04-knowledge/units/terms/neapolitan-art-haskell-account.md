## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔在第7章所述的那不勒斯艺术领域：他称当地视觉艺术兴盛、绘画带有特定文化印记，并在注释中以“Neapolitan art”指称哈拉赫的赞助对象。第172页还把蒙特雷的行为与当地艺术活动相联系。这里保留哈斯克尔不同段落的用语与评价，不把该领域定义为单一画派、固定组织或完整作品集合。

**English:** This term records the field of Neapolitan art described in Haskell’s Chapter 7: he says the visual arts flourished locally, describes a distinctive imprint on painting, and uses “Neapolitan art” for the object of Harrach’s patronage in a footnote. Page 172 also connects Monterey’s conduct with local artistic activity. The card preserves Haskell’s wording and evaluations across these passages; it does not define a single school, formal organization, or complete body of works.
