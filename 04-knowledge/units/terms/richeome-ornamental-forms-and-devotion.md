## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所引 Richeôme 对装饰形式的特定判断。该段列及假壁柱、柱式、大理石祭坛、檐口、基座、绘画、浮雕和建筑装饰；“不触及敬虔”是 Richeôme 的立场，不转写为本项目的普遍评价，也不将类别归到某一具体作品或教堂。中文名称为描述性译名。 [第3章书内来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L34-L35; print p. 68）

**English:** This card records the specific judgment by Richeôme quoted by Haskell about ornamental forms. The passage mentions fake pilasters, columns, marble altars, cornices, pedestals, paintings, reliefs, and architectural ornaments. “Not touching devotion” is Richeôme's position, not a general assessment adopted here; the category is not assigned to a particular work or church. The Chinese name is a descriptive translation. [第3章书内来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L34-L35; print p. 68）
