## 内容

### 描述与范围

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔对 S. Vitale 壁画组题材的概括：画面几乎全都涉及酷刑。它是来源限定的图像主题概念，不代表每幅壁画都描绘同一具体施刑事件，也不提供受难者或单幅场景的完整清单。

**English:** This term records Haskell’s characterization of the S. Vitale fresco cycle: its subjects were almost exclusively concerned with torture. It is a source-bound iconographic topic; it does not identify a single historical torture event for each fresco or supply a complete list of victims or individual scenes.

### 身份边界

本术语只指向 S. Vitale 壁画组的题材概念，不是壁画组、教堂地点或某一具体受刑事件。原书将其与较早的 S. Stefano Rotondo 壁画作代际比较；两组作品保持分立。

This term refers only to the subject concept in the S. Vitale fresco cycle, not to the fresco group, the church, or a specific torture event. Haskell compares the cycle with the earlier frescoes at S. Stefano Rotondo; the two groups remain distinct.
