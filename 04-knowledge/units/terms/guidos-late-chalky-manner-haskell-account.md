## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔第10章注1对圭多·雷尼晚期画风的描述。哈斯克尔推测Crespi想到的是其非常晚期的“chalky”画风，并评价这种画风将吸引18世纪鉴赏家；“chalky”保留为原文描述，不在此确定具体材料或技法含义。

**English:** This term records Haskell’s description of Guido Reni’s late manner in chapter 10, note 1. Haskell infers that Crespi had in mind Reni’s very late “chalky” manner and says it would attract eighteenth-century connoisseurs. “Chalky” is retained as Haskell’s wording without assigning a specific material or technical meaning.
