## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔关于17世纪法国对意大利艺术敌意的叙述：第187页称这种敌意不只基于种族情感，第188页又称相关批评与敌意随法国人的自信增长而增强。这里保留作者的章节解释，不将其推广为全体法国人的一致态度。

**English:** This term records Haskell’s account of French hostility toward Italian art in the seventeenth century. Chapter 7 says the hostility rested on more than racial feeling and later connects stronger criticism with growing French self-assurance. The card preserves Haskell’s account without treating it as a uniform view held by all French people.
