## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡只记录哈斯克尔第9章p.256所述的法律职业及律师群体的社会经济位置：Sandi家族的财富被说成与极为有利可图的法律职业紧密相关；哈斯克尔转述Goldoni回忆录称18世纪威尼斯律师的社会地位仅次于贵族；Carlo Cordellina被称为当时的leading advocate。本卡不将职业概念当作机构，不推定Sandi家族成员从事法律工作，也不把转述说法当作已独立核实的事实。 [第9章 p.256](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md#L158)

**English:** This unit records only the legal profession and lawyers' social-economic position in Haskell's Chapter 9, p. 256: the Sandi family's fortunes are said to be closely linked to the lucrative legal profession; Haskell reports Goldoni's Memoirs as saying that lawyers in eighteenth-century Venice ranked second only to the nobility; and Carlo Cordellina is called the leading advocate of the day. The unit treats the profession as a term, not an institution, does not infer that Sandi family members practiced law, and does not present the reported Memoirs passage as independently verified. [Chapter 9, p. 256](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md#L158)

### 名称

本卡以 “profession of the law” 与 “lawyers” 记录哈斯克尔第9章的社会职业用语；描述性名称不表示某个具名法律机构。

### 基本信息

范围限于哈斯克尔第9章 p.256 的法律职业及律师群体。Goldoni 回忆录的社会地位说法仅按哈斯克尔转述记录，未独立查阅该回忆录。

### 概念辨析

法律职业是概念类别，不等同于法律机构；本页没有说 Sandi 家族成员本人从事法律工作。
