## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔注2转引的意大利报纸报道中所称青年画家。报道称其中一些是蒂埃波罗的学生，但没有列出姓名或人数；言论经报纸及哈斯克尔转述，未在此独立核验。 [[来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md#L361)]

**English:** This card records the young artists mentioned in the Italian newspaper report reproduced in Haskell’s note 2. The report says some were Tiepolo’s pupils but gives no names or count; the statement is mediated through the newspaper and Haskell and is not independently verified here.

### 身份边界

本组为来源局部的青年画家集合称谓，不转写为具名人物或固定学生名册。

This group is a source-local collective term for young artists, not a set of named persons or a fixed pupil roster.
