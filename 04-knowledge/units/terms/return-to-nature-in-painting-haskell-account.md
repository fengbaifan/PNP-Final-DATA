### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell对Carracci及其Bolognese追随者“回归自然”的书内表述。该语句保留为来源限定的绘画倾向，不与自然主义术语合并，也不扩写为已核实的风格史结论。

**English:** This unit records Haskell’s in-book phrase that Carracci and his Bolognese followers “turned back to nature.” It remains a source-limited tendency in painting, distinct from the term Naturalism and not expanded into a verified art-historical conclusion.
