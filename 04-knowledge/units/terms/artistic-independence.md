## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Rosa 所争取的免于既定委托束缚、依据灵感和成品质量作画的权利，其他局部相似例保留；不是实际完全脱离市场。

**English:** Rosa sought freedom from advance commitments and justified his choices through inspiration and the quality of finished work. Some other painters adopted partly similar practices; this does not mean that they were free from economic dependence.
