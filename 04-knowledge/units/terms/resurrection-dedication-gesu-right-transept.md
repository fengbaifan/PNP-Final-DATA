## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录Haskell所述耶稣堂右横翼礼拜堂的“复活”奉献对象。书中未给出这一奉献的日期；本对象与Baglione的同名绘画分开。中文名称为描述性工作译名。

**English:** This term records the Resurrection as the dedication of the right-transept chapel of the Gesù in Haskell's account. The passage gives no date for this dedication. It is distinct from Baglione's painting of the same name. The Chinese name is a descriptive working translation.
