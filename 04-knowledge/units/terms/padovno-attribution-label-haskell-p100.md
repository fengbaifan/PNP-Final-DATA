## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔转述目录时出现的“Padov.no”归属标记及其讨论中出现的“Padovano”形式。标记本身不是人物身份；哈斯克尔报告目录编者曾将其解释为 Alessandro Varotari，随后提出更可能指 Ottavio Leoni。两种解释均保留为来源中的竞争判断，不将标记与任一人物合并。

**English:** This term records the attribution label “Padov.no” in Haskell’s report of the inventory and the related form “Padovano” discussed in the note. The label is not itself a person identity. Haskell reports the inventory authors’ interpretation as Alessandro Varotari, then argues that Ottavio Leoni is more likely. Both remain competing source-level interpretations; the label is not merged with either person.
