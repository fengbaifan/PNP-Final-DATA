## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔在第10章和结论中使用的“genre”／“genre painting”范畴。第10章 p.314 的用例涉及日常人物、乞丐和动物题材的藏画；p.316 又以该词描述诺加里的作品处理，并讨论其在威尼斯部分进步圈子中的支持。结论 p.384 将其纳入对克雷斯皮艺术取向的评价。该卡记录的是哈斯克尔书内用法，不把一处题材例子扩成完整定义，也不声称所有时期和语境中的风俗画均属同一类型。

**English:** This card records Haskell’s use of “genre” and “genre painting” in Chapter 10 and the conclusion. The Chapter 10, p.314 passage applies the term to collection pictures involving ordinary people, beggars, and animals; p.316 also uses it for Nogari’s treatment of subjects and discusses support in some progressive Venetian circles. The conclusion on p.384 includes genre painting in Haskell’s assessment of Crespi’s artistic outlook. This card records Haskell’s in-book usage; it does not turn one set of subjects into a complete definition or claim that genre painting has the same scope in every period and context.
