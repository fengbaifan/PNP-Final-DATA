### 描述

**中文：** 作者认为其对抬高创造者角色的作用后来不再主导思辨；这是 Haskell 的思想史概括，不预设为研究维度。

**English:** Haskell argues that Platonism's earlier elevation of the creator no longer dominated later speculation. This is his intellectual-historical interpretation, not a predetermined dimension of the present research.
