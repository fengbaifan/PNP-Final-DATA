## 内容

### 描述
**中文：** 哈斯克尔所述科尔托纳巴贝里尼宫天顶中央的神意人格化形象。该概念限于此处图像角色，不与一般神学意义的神意或天顶作品本身合并。
**English:** The personified figure of Divine Providence at the centre of Cortona’s Barberini Palace ceiling in Haskell’s account. This unit is limited to the iconographic role and remains distinct from general theological uses of Providence and from the ceiling work itself.
