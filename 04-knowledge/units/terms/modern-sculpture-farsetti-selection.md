## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell在第15章p.362用“modern sculpture”描述Farsetti所选四尊雕像所代表的类别。它是该段落中的来源限定用语，不在此定义近代雕塑的一般年代、风格或边界；与意大利启蒙思想及威尼斯新古典主义运动保持概念区分。

**English:** This card records Haskell’s use of “modern sculpture” for the category represented by Farsetti’s four selected statues on p.362. It is a source-bound term in that passage and does not define the general period, style, or boundaries of modern sculpture. It remains distinct from the Enlightenment in Italy and the Venetian neo-classical movement.
