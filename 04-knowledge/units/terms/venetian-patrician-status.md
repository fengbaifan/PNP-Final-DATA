## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔注4提到的威尼斯 patrician 身份，作为外省贵族可能取得的一种身份地位。来源说相关群体在 1775 年拒绝这一机会；本卡不据此补写授予程序、法律规则或群体成员。 [[来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md#L363)]

**English:** This card records Venetian patrician status as a standing that provincial nobles could acquire, as mentioned in Haskell’s note 4. The source says that the group declined the opportunity in 1775; this card adds no admission procedure, legal rule, or membership list.

### 身份边界

本组是身份地位术语，不等同于威尼斯共和国、贵族家族或取得该身份的个人。

This group is a status term, distinct from the Venetian Republic, patrician families, and individuals who acquired the status.
