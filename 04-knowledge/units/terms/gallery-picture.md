## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 可在收藏、出售、交换中流动的绘画；可兼有建筑装饰功能，不能等同所有画均脱离地点限制。

**English:** Movable paintings collected for galleries could be bought, exchanged or arranged in decorative schemes. Their mobility does not mean that they were free of architectural or compositional requirements.
