## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语对应哈斯克尔索引项“Indecency and the erotic”，汇集书中涉及裸体表现、情色内容及其道德边界的不同讨论。第7章谈及德意志王侯对裸体画的需求、对作品是否构成不雅的判断，以及女性裸体在意大利绘画中的历史位置；第17章另谈 Manfrin 以自拟情色题材组织绘画竞赛。索引项将不同作品、人物和时代的相关页码归在一起，不表示它们组成单一流派或共同立场。

**English:** This term corresponds to Haskell’s index heading “Indecency and the erotic,” which gathers distinct discussions in the book concerning nude representation, erotic subject matter, and moral boundaries. Chapter 7 addresses German princely demand for nudes, judgments about whether paintings were obscene, and the historical role of the female nude in Italian painting; Chapter 17 separately discusses Manfrin’s competitions for pictures on erotic themes of his own devising. The index groups page references about different works, people, and periods; it does not imply one artistic movement or shared position.
