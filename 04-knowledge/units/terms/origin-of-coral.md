## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本条记录哈斯克尔所称的题材名“Origin of Coral”。第4章将其称为Claude《珀耳修斯与珊瑚起源的海景》的题材，并说Massimi已拥有Poussin据此绘制的素描。注5另提一幅由Pietro da Cortona绘制、Cassiano曾经拥有的“Origin of Coral”画作；哈斯克尔称该画对17世纪鉴赏家的确切意义并不清楚。这里记录的是来源中的题材概念，不把Claude画、Cortona画与Poussin素描合并为同一作品。

**English:** This unit records the subject name “Origin of Coral” as used by Haskell. Chapter 4 identifies it as the subject of Claude’s A Coast View with Perseus and the Origin of Coral and says Massimi already owned a Poussin drawing of it. Note 5 separately mentions a painting by Pietro da Cortona, once owned by Cassiano, “of the Origin of Coral”; Haskell says its precise significance to seventeenth-century connoisseurs is unclear. This is the source-named subject concept; the Claude painting, Cortona painting, and Poussin drawing are not treated as the same work.
