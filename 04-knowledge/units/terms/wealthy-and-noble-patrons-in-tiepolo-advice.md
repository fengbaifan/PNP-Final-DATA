## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔正文及注2所呈现的富有者与贵族作为画家大型作品的预期受众。正文将话语归于蒂埃波罗，注2说明其来自 1762 年意大利报纸报道；这不证明所有此类人士实际委托了作品。 [[来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md#L130)]

**English:** This card records the wealthy and noble audience for large-scale works in Haskell’s body text and note 2. The body attributes the advice to Tiepolo, while note 2 locates it in a 1762 Italian newspaper report; this does not establish that all such people commissioned works.

### 身份边界

本组是来源话语中的社会受众类别，不与威尼斯贵族术语、具名赞助人或实际委托者合并。

This group is a social audience category in the reported statement, distinct from the Venetian aristocracy term, named patrons, and actual commissioners.
