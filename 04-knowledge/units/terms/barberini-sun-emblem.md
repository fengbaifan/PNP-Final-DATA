## 内容

### 描述
**中文：** 哈斯克尔所述巴贝里尼太阳纹章这一跨作品符号概念。在萨基壁画中，作者还将太阳解释为智慧的惯用象征；这些象征用途不使太阳、智慧人格化形象或各作品中的太阳图像成为同一对象。
**English:** The Barberini sun emblem as a cross-work symbolic concept in Haskell’s account. For Sacchi’s fresco, the author also describes the sun as a conventional emblem of Wisdom; these symbolic uses do not equate the sun with personified Wisdom or with any individual depiction.
