## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔转述的一种解释：伯尼尼在巴黎的失败“常被说成”标志意大利艺术在法国声望的崩塌。卡片保留“常被说成”的转述限定，不将其改写为无归属的历史事实。

**English:** This term records an interpretation reported by Haskell: Bernini’s failure in Paris is “always said” to mark the collapse of Italian artistic prestige in France. The attribution is preserved rather than restated as an unqualified historical fact.
