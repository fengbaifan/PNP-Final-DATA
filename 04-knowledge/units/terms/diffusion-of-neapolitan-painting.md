## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录 Haskell 对那不勒斯绘画传播及其跨地域影响的概述。第7章称 Daun 是那不勒斯绘画传入中欧的主要推动者之一；第8章称其传播远及西班牙帝国疆界之外，并以跨出本地城市的赞助人收藏说明它在17世纪末于意大利各地获得的重要性。上述范围和评价均保留为 Haskell 的叙述；本卡不补全传播路线、作品清单或因果链。[第7章 p.200](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_v.md#L15) [第8章 p.210](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i.md#L80) [第8章 p.213](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i.md#L117)

**English:** This term records Haskell’s account of the diffusion and wider reach of Neapolitan painting. Chapter 7 calls Daun one of the principal agents through whom it was introduced into Central Europe. Chapter 8 says it spread beyond the Spanish Empire and uses patrons’ collections beyond their native cities to illustrate the importance it had acquired across Italy by the late seventeenth century. These scope and evaluative claims remain attributed to Haskell; this unit does not supply a complete route, work list, or causal chain. [Chapter 7, p. 200](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/07_CHP-7_sec_v.md#L15) [Chapter 8, p. 210](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i.md#L80) [Chapter 8, p. 213](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i.md#L117)
