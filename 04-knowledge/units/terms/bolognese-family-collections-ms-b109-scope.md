## 内容

### 描述
**中文：** 本术语记录Haskell第8章p.203注5中“Belloni、Bellucci以及博洛尼亚家族的收藏”这一来源限定的复数范围。它是手稿主题的集合性表述，不是一个具体家族，不给出完整成员名单，也不证明B.109穷尽记录所有相关收藏。

**English:** This term records the source-defined plural scope in Haskell’s Chapter 8 p.203 note 5: collections associated with Belloni, Bellucci, and Bolognese families. It is a collective description of the manuscript’s subject, not a particular family. It supplies no complete membership list and does not establish that B.109 exhaustively records all relevant collections.
