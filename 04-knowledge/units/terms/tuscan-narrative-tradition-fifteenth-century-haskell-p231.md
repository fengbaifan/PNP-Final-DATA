## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语卡只记录Haskell在第8章p.231对《La Burla》风格与题材灵感的来源局部表述：他认为作品的“朴素而古雅”更多承接十五世纪托斯卡纳叙事传统。该标签不定义整个托斯卡纳传统，不预设其成员、作品清单或单一文献来源；这是Haskell的比较性艺术史判断，不是本项目独立确认的影响史事实。

**English:** This term card records only Haskell’s source-local description on Chapter 8, p. 231 of the stylistic and subject inspiration for La Burla: he says its “homely and archaic” simplicity owed more to fifteenth-century Tuscan narrative. The label does not define the Tuscan tradition as a whole or prescribe members, a work list, or a single textual source. It represents Haskell’s comparative art-historical judgment, not an independently verified influence history.
