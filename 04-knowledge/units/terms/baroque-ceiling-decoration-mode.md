## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔对巴洛克天花板装饰方式的书内描述。第2章称 Pietro da Cortona 的天花板开启了巴洛克装饰的新篇章；第7章称 Antonio Verrio 将一种此前未见于英格兰的装饰类型带入当地，并描述整面天花板的幻觉式壁画和仿佛向天空敞开的墙面。作品“平庸”及其风格影响是哈斯克尔的评价。本卡不把这些表述转写成独立的流派定义、时期边界或已确认的首创结论。

**English:** This term records Haskell’s account of Baroque ceiling decoration. Chapter 2 says Pietro da Cortona’s ceiling opened a new chapter in Baroque decoration; Chapter 7 says Antonio Verrio introduced into England a type not previously seen there and describes illusionistic frescoes across whole ceilings and walls that seemed to open to the sky. The judgment that the work was “mediocre” and its stylistic effect remain Haskell’s evaluations. This card does not turn those statements into an independently established movement definition, period boundary, or verified priority claim.
