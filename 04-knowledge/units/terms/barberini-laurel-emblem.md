## 内容

### 描述
**中文：** 哈斯克尔所述巴贝里尼月桂纹章这一跨作品符号概念。第2章先将华盖柱叶辨认为月桂、称其为另一项家族纹章，后又称其在科尔托纳天顶中成为重要主题。各处图像出现仍属不同作品语境。
**English:** The Barberini laurel emblem as a cross-work symbolic concept in Haskell’s account. Chapter 2 identifies the baldacchino’s column leaves as laurel and another family emblem, then describes it as an important theme in Cortona’s ceiling. The depictions remain distinct work contexts.
