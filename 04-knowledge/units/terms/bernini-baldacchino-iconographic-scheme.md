## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语限定于 Haskell 对贝尔尼尼巴尔达基诺图像方案的叙述。他称该方案的图像内容相对简单；又说 Urban VIII 可能直接参与构思，并确保自己与方案紧密联系。后一判断保留原文的“可能”与作者归属，不推断具体设计分工，也不将该方案等同于巴尔达基诺这一作品本身。[第2章 p.35](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_ii.md#L43) [第2章 p.35](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_ii.md#L46)

**English:** This term is limited to Haskell’s account of the iconographic scheme of Bernini’s baldacchino. He calls its imagery relatively simple; he also says Urban VIII probably took a direct part in outlining the scheme and made sure he was closely identified with it. The latter claim retains the source’s qualification and attribution. The scheme is not treated as identical to the baldacchino itself, and no specific division of design work is inferred.
