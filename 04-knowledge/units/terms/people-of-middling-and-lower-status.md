## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录 Haskell 借引 Giulio Mancini 的意大利语短语 *uomini di stato mediocre e di stato basso* 所指的中等与较低社会地位人群。第5章将其与富有者和贵族相区别，并转述 Mancini 关于购画议价及房间陈列的建议；随后称 bambocciate 的早期支持可能来自这一类别，并推断 Van Laer 的主要客户可能包括匿名的中等社会地位者。原书分别使用“有理由相信”和“必须假定”等限定；本卡保留这些作者推断，不据此建立固定阶层制度或成员名单。[第5章 p.130](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_i.md#L110)；[第5章 p.132](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_iii.md#L7)；[第5章 p.136](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_iii.md#L38)

**English:** This term records the middling and lower-status people denoted by Haskell’s Italian phrase *uomini di stato mediocre e di stato basso*, cited through Giulio Mancini. Chapter 5 distinguishes them from the rich and the aristocratic and reports Mancini’s advice on negotiating picture prices and hanging pictures in the home. Haskell then says that support for the bambocciate may have begun with this category and infers that Van Laer’s main clientele may have included anonymous people of middling status. The book marks these claims with qualifications such as “there is reason to believe” and “we must assume”; this card preserves those inferences and supplies neither a fixed estate system nor a membership list. [Chapter 5, p. 130](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_i.md#L110); [Chapter 5, p. 132](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_iii.md#L7); [Chapter 5, p. 136](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_iii.md#L38)

### 名称与范围

原书短语为 *uomini di stato mediocre e di stato basso*；第5章 p.136 另以 *uomini di stato mediocre* 指代 Van Laer 的匿名客户。此卡仅表达 Haskell 及其所引 Mancini 的书内社会类别，不把该类别当作具体人物、家族或正式组织。
