## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡将 Haskell 第2章所说绘画应追求的理想，与第6章所称 Bellori 1664年讲演的“The Ideal in art”视为同一书内概念；第6章明确说 Bellori 的判断标准基于由 Agucchi 等人先行阐述的这一概念。Haskell举古代作品、Raphael 和年轻的博洛尼亚追随者为理想典范，但这些段落没有给出概念定义或完整理论边界。本卡不补写一般美学定义。

**English:** This card treats the ideal that Haskell says painting should pursue in Chapter 2 as the same in-book concept as the “Ideal in art” named as the subject of Bellori's 1664 lecture in Chapter 6. Chapter 6 explicitly says Bellori's criterion rested on the concept already elaborated by Agucchi and others. Haskell names ancient works, Raphael and young Bolognese protégés as exemplars, but these passages supply no definition or full theoretical boundary. The card adds no general aesthetic definition.
