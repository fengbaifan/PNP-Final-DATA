## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语表示哈斯克尔在第13章 p.343 所定义的一类欧洲业余鉴赏家：Zanetti 的品味与其他相近鉴赏家的品味兼具学识与奇想。原文随后以 “these men” 回指这一群体，并称洛可可与新古典风格对他们而言并不冲突。这里是来源中的类别，不是正式组织；该段没有给出完整成员名单或固定边界。[第13章 p.343](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md)（L112–113）

**English:** This term records a category in Haskell’s Chapter 13, p. 343: Zanetti and similar European amateurs whose taste combined scholarly and fanciful elements. Haskell then refers to this group as “these men” and says that Rococo and Neoclassical styles were not in conflict for them. This is a source-defined category, not a formal organization; the passage supplies neither a complete membership list nor a fixed boundary. [Chapter 13, p. 343](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/13_CHP-13_intro.md) (L112–113)
