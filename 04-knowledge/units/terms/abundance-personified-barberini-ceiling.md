## 内容

### 描述
**中文：** 哈斯克尔所述科尔托纳巴贝里尼宫天顶中的丰饶人格化形象。书中转述解释者将其联系到年轻枢机安东尼奥，并提到其后方的粮仓；此为来源所载图像解释，不与该历史人物等同。
**English:** The personified figure of Abundance in Cortona’s Barberini Palace ceiling as described by Haskell. The book reports interpreters linking the figure to the younger Cardinal Antonio and mentioning granaries behind it; this is an attributed iconographic interpretation, not an identity between the allegory and the historical person.
