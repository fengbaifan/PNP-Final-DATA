## 内容

### 描述
**中文：** 哈斯克尔在科尔托纳巴贝里尼宫天顶描写的翻滚巨人群像。作者称其营造正在发生冲突的印象，并将观者卷入教皇对抗邪恶力量的寓意阐释；该群像是作品中的图像主题，不作为历史人物群体。
**English:** The group of tumbling giants described by Haskell in Cortona’s Barberini Palace ceiling. The author says they convey an impression of conflict and interprets the visitor as drawn into the Pope’s war against evil; the group is an iconographic subject, not a historical collective.
