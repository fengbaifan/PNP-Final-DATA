## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 特定保护人服务（servitù particolare）是本章所述艺术家持续为某保护人服务的紧密关系，可包括月俸、住处、作品另付酬与学习旅行资助。服务优先权和排他劳动不是同一条件；艺术家进入家户也不意味着成为血亲。

**English:** Servitù particolare describes an artist's close, continuing service to a particular patron. It could include an allowance, lodging, separate payment for works and support for study travel. Priority of service is distinct from exclusive labour, and household membership does not imply kinship.
