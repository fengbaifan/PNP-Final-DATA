## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 记录哈斯克尔所述阿尔蒂耶里宫壁画中的女性仁慈寓意形象。作者称其为壁画高潮，并说明其与教皇名号有关的双关及金色光照；这些均按作者的图像解读保留。

**English:** This unit records the female personification of Clemency in the Altieri palace fresco. Haskell calls it the fresco’s climax and notes a pun on the pope’s name and its golden light. These details remain the author’s iconographic reading.
