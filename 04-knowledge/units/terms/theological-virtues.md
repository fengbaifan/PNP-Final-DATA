## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔第9章第255页称围绕圣母出现的三项神学美德：Faith、Hope和Charity。中文题名为项目描述性译名。原文在同一句另列Wisdom，本卡不将Wisdom纳入这三项范围，也不把三项概念等同于个别人物或某一件作品。

**English:** This term records the three theological virtues that Haskell names around the Virgin in Chapter 9, p. 255: Faith, Hope, and Charity. “神学美德” is a descriptive Chinese rendering used by the project. Haskell names Wisdom separately in the same sentence; it is outside this three-part scope. The concepts are not equated with individual persons or with the artwork itself.
