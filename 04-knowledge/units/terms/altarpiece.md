## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 用于教堂祭坛语境的绘画，位置、尺寸、照明和图像主题影响其委托；本章与私人画廊画相对照。

**English:** In this chapter, paintings intended for church altars are discussed through their location, dimensions, lighting and iconographic requirements. Their commissioning conditions differ from those of movable pictures for private galleries.
