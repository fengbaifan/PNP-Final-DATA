## 内容

### 描述
**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔对圣彼得大殿整体装饰方案的解释：他认为完成后的方案使整座教堂获得新意义，各要素在风格与主题上相互呼应，并联结宗教意象、教宗权势与巴贝里尼家族。此为来源限定的解释性概念，不等同于巴尔达基诺等单件作品，也不将作者阐释提升为独立核实事实。

**English:** This unit records Haskell’s interpretation of the integrated decorative programme at St Peter’s Basilica: he says the completed scheme gave the whole church new meaning, its elements echoed one another in style and theme, and the ensemble joined religious imagery with papal and Barberini themes. This is a source-local interpretive concept, distinct from individual works such as the baldacchino; it does not present Haskell’s interpretation as independently verified fact.
