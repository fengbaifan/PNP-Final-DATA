## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔第6章以“艺术整体”概括其所述受影响的艺术领域。此处是“艺术”这一宽泛对象范围，不等同于另行登记的“罗马艺术赞助的衰退”分析框架，也不据此限定具体艺术门类或量化影响。

**English:** This term records the broad artistic sphere that Haskell describes as affected in Chapter 6. It refers to “the arts” as a whole, distinct from the separately registered analytical framing of the decline of Roman patronage, and does not specify particular art forms or quantify the effect.
