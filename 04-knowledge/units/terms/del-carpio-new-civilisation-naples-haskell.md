## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔将 Del Carpio 的改革描述为给“新文明”奠定基础的说法。原文预测这一文明将很快使那不勒斯重回知识欧洲前沿；这是作者的未来性判断，不表示该结果已经发生。

**English:** This term records Haskell’s description of Del Carpio’s reforms as laying the foundations for a “new civilisation.” The passage predicts that this civilisation would soon return Naples to the forefront of intellectual Europe; this is the author’s forecast, not a claim that the outcome had already occurred.
