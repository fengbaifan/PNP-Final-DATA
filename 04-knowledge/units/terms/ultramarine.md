## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本章合同中特别讨论费用承担的颜料。不同委托可由画家或赞助人供应，不设单一成本规则；其他古色名保留在相关材料安排中。

**English:** Ultramarine appears in contracts assigning pigment costs either to patrons or to painters. The allocation varied by agreement; other named colours and materials must not be collapsed into this single pigment.
