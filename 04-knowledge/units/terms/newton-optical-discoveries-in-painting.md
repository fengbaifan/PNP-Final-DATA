## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录Haskell在绘画讨论中所称的牛顿光学发现，以及Algarotti希望艺术家研习这些发现的书内语境。它不同于第10章所述的色彩和谐概念；本卡不评价牛顿光学理论本身，也不推定具体艺术家的采纳。

**English:** This term records Newton’s optical discoveries as discussed in Haskell’s account of painting and Algarotti’s wish that artists study them. It is distinct from the color-harmony concept in Chapter 10; this card does not assess Newtonian optics itself or infer adoption by any particular artist.
