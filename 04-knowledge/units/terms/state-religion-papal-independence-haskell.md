## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔对“Gallican”原则的解释：路易十四希望建立尽可能独立于教皇影响的国教。此处表达的是被归于国王的目标，不是该目标已实现或已成制度的断言。

**English:** This term records Haskell’s explanation of “Gallican” principles as Louis XIV’s wish to establish a state religion as independent as possible from papal influence. It records an attributed objective, not an implemented policy or institutional fact.
