## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔第8章所述的南意大利封建土地所有者这一社会类别。索引将“feudal landowners”列在 Naples 条目下；正文举出若干家族名称，并称这类土地所有者在西班牙统治下兴盛、给予当地艺术学校大力支持。原书没有给出完整成员表，也没有把该类别定义为正式组织；所列家族仅是段落中的例子，不构成完整名册。

**English:** This term records the social category of southern Italian feudal landowners described in Chapter 8. The index lists “feudal landowners” under Naples; the passage names several families and says that these landowners prospered under Spanish rule and gave lavish support to a local school of art. Haskell supplies neither a complete membership list nor a formal organization; the named families are examples in the passage, not an exhaustive roster.
