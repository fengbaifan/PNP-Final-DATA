### 描述

**中文：** 本章讨论艺术家神秘光环、特殊性及未获承认天才的观念史；不同世纪和未成型状态要区分。

**English:** Haskell distinguishes a mystique surrounding exceptional creators from later ideas about misunderstood genius. The chapter treats these as historically changing ideas, not a fully formed doctrine shared by every period.
