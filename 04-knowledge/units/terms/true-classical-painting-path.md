## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语限于 Haskell 第4章所说的“真正古典绘画之路”。他称1630年代巴贝里尼所培养的高巴洛克风格在一些人看来与这一路径相对，并以早期矫饰主义和自然主义作比较；对巴贝里尼支持的艺术家团队，他说只有 Sacchi 与 Poussin 保持该路径。原文的“在一些人看来”及“仅限该团队”均予保留，不将本术语扩写成正式流派定义。[第4章 p.104](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md#L59)

**English:** This term is limited to the “straight and narrow path of true, classical painting” described in Haskell’s Chapter 4. He says the 1630s high Baroque style cultivated by the Barberini appeared to some to oppose this path, as earlier Mannerism and Naturalism had; of the Barberini-sponsored team, he says only Sacchi and Poussin had kept to it. The source’s “appeared” qualification and team-specific scope are retained, and the term is not expanded into a formal movement definition. [Chapter 4, p. 104](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_ii.md#L59)
