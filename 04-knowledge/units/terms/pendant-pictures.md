## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 成对绘画指有意作为一对构思、彼此在题材、构图或布置上呼应的画作。本章用它解释对称装饰对委托尺寸及构图的影响；术语的成立不等于已经识别任何特定实物组合。[S1–S6]

**English:** Pendant pictures are conceived as a pair, with correspondences in subject, composition or display. Chapter 1 uses this concept to explain how symmetrical decoration affects commissioned dimensions and composition; defining it does not identify a particular surviving pair. [S1–S6]
