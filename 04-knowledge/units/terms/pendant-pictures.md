## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 委托中成对配置画作以适应对称装饰，影响尺寸乃至构图；非特定一对作品名称。

**English:** Paintings could be planned or selected as pairs to meet requirements of symmetry, dimensions and composition. The concept is separate from the identification of any particular pair of surviving works.
