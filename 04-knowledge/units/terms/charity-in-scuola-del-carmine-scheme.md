## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录 Haskell 所述卡尔米内学院第二套主厅装饰方案中的 Charity。原文称周围画布拟表现启迪兄弟会成员的美德，并将 Charity 与 Faith、Hope 并列。该记录指抽象美德主题，不指某幅已确认存世的画布或一位被人格化的人物。

**English:** This term records Charity as named in Haskell’s account of the second decoration scheme for the Scuola del Carmine’s main room. He says the surrounding canvases were to depict virtues that should inspire the Brethren and names Charity alongside Faith and Hope. This unit denotes the abstract virtue as a proposed subject, not an identified surviving canvas or a personified figure.

### 本章相关内容

Haskell says Tiepolo’s descriptions of the virtues drew on illustrations in Ripa’s iconographic manual. The manual’s edition and the relation between its illustrations and any particular finished canvas are not identified here.
