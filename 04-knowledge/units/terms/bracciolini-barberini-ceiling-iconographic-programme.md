## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔对 Francesco Bracciolini 为巴贝里尼宫大礼堂穹顶设计的图像纲领的叙述。作者称 Bracciolini 拟定的方案由 Pietro da Cortona 执行，旨在颂扬巴贝里尼家族；之后又比较宫殿穹顶的新主题、文学性和宫廷赞助语境。此条记录设计方案这一概念对象，不是穹顶壁画本身、诗稿档案或抽象的巴洛克风格。

**English:** This term records Haskell’s account of Francesco Bracciolini’s iconographic programme for the great hall ceiling of the Barberini palace. The author says Bracciolini drew up the programme and Pietro da Cortona executed it as a hymn to the family’s glory; the discussion then compares the ceiling’s novel theme, literary character, and courtly patronage context. The unit denotes the design scheme, not the ceiling fresco itself, a manuscript archive, or an abstract Baroque style.
