## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第2章与第8章在罗马艺术语境中使用的“Roman classicism”及“classicist doctrines”。第2章称萨基逐渐转向拉斐尔和罗马古典主义寻求灵感；第8章称鲁福相对不受17世纪后半叶在罗马增强的古典主义教条影响。此卡保留哈斯克尔的来源限定用法，不将其扩写成正式流派边界，也不推定它与洛多利论述中的威尼斯建筑古典主义或新古典主义相同。 [第2章 p.46](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_iv.md#L34) [第8章 p.210](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_i.md#L74)

**English:** This unit records Haskell's use of “Roman classicism” and “classicist doctrines” in the Roman artistic context of Chapters 2 and 8. Chapter 2 says Sacchi increasingly turned to Raphael and Roman classicism for inspiration; Chapter 8 says Ruffo was comparatively unaffected by classicist doctrines gaining force in Rome in the second half of the seventeenth century. The unit preserves Haskell's source-bounded usage without defining a formal school or asserting identity with the Venetian architectural classicism discussed through Lodoli or with Neo-classicism.
