## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔对意大利式繁饰的审美表述：其称法国本土趣味已发展到排斥意大利式繁饰的程度。这里记录作者的来源限定用语，不把它归纳为一般性民族审美事实。

**English:** This term records Haskell’s aesthetic characterization of Italian floridity: he says native French taste had developed to find it repugnant. The card preserves this source-specific formulation rather than presenting a general fact about national taste.
