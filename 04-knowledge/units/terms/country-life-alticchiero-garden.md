## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** “Country Life”是哈斯克尔列出的阿尔蒂基耶罗园林祭坛主题之一。此卡限于这一来源用例；它不概括一般乡村生活，也不指定某一祭坛或存世对象。

**English:** “Country Life” is one of the ideals Haskell lists in connection with the other altars in the Alticchiero garden. This unit is limited to that source use; it does not generalize about rural life or identify a particular altar or surviving object.
