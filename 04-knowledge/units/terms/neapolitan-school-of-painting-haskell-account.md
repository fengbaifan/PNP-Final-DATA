## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本术语记录哈斯克尔在第7、8章提及的那不勒斯绘画学派。第7章以蒙特雷的赞助语境称其为“Neapolitan school”；第8章称那不勒斯有一所得到当地土地所有者支持的艺术学校，并把它与罗马相比。原书没有给出正式机构名称或完整成员表，因此本卡表达地区艺术传统，不把它升格为机构，也不与那不勒斯艺术这一较宽领域合并。

**English:** This term records the Neapolitan school of painting mentioned in Chapters 7 and 8. Chapter 7 calls it the “Neapolitan school” in the context of Monterey’s patronage; Chapter 8 describes a local school of art supported by landowners and compares it with Rome. Haskell supplies neither a formal institutional name nor a complete membership list, so this card represents a regional artistic tradition, not an institution, and keeps it distinct from the broader field of Neapolitan art.
