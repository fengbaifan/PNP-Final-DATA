### 描述
**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 在奥托博尼语境中使用的“traditional Cardinal-nephew”角色表述，限于该段对其奢华趣味及仅能享有该角色两年的描述，不扩展为所有时期的制度定义。
**English:** This card records Haskell’s phrase “traditional Cardinal-nephew” in the Ottoboni passage. Its scope is limited to the passage’s description of extravagant tastes and the brief period in which Ottoboni could enjoy the role; it is not a general institutional definition.
