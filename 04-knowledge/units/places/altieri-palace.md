## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 记录哈斯克尔在第4章和第6章所称的 Altieri 家族新宫殿。第4章提到 Massimi 受托监督其装饰；第6章称宫殿正在建造，并叙述主厅壁画及未完成的装饰计划。来源没有给出可核实的现存建筑身份或正式建筑名。

**English:** This unit records the new Altieri family palace described by Haskell in Chapters 4 and 6. Chapter 4 says Massimi was asked to supervise its decoration; Chapter 6 describes the palace under construction, the principal-hall fresco, and an unfinished decorative programme. These passages do not establish the identity or formal name of a surviving building.
