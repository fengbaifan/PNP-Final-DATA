## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 马扎然宫上层的巴洛克画廊，保留罗马内利及作坊绘制的穹顶装饰，属于法国国家图书馆黎塞留馆址。

**English:** The Baroque upper gallery of the Palais Mazarin, preserving vault decoration painted by Romanelli and his workshop within the BnF Richelieu site.
