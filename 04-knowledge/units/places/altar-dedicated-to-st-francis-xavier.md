## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔为《Death of St Francis Xavier》指出的祭坛目的地，称其奉献给圣方济各·沙勿略。它与祭坛画作品、S. Andrea al Quirinale高祭坛及教堂空间分别识别；不据此确认祭坛现存、精确建筑位置或画作实际安装。

**English:** This unit records the altar destination Haskell names for *The Death of St Francis Xavier* and says it was dedicated to that saint. It remains distinct from the painting, the High Altar of S. Andrea al Quirinale, and the church space. The passage does not establish that this altar survives, its precise architectural location, or actual installation of the painting.
