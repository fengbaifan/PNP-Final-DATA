**中文：** 哈斯克尔第10章 p.279 将其称为 Lord Manchester 位于 Arlington Street 的一处宅邸，并称 Pellegrini 与 Marco Ricci 受命装饰他的宅邸。原书没有给出正式建筑名或门牌；具体画家与宅邸的对应关系仍待证。

**English:** In chapter 10, p. 279, Haskell refers to a residence of Lord Manchester on Arlington Street and says Pellegrini and Marco Ricci were set to decorate his mansions. The book gives no formal building name or street number; the artist-to-residence assignments remain unresolved.
