## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 仁慈山会教堂是那不勒斯仁慈山会建筑群内的宗教建筑。《七件善事》为早期教堂创作，今天仍安置在教堂主祭坛。本卡表示建筑和作品的物理位置，不代替同名慈善机构。

**English:** The Church of Pio Monte della Misericordia is the religious building within the institution's Naples complex. Caravaggio's *Seven Works of Mercy* was made for the early church and remains installed at the high altar. This unit represents the building and the painting's physical location, separately from the charitable institution.

本卡只表示教堂建筑。同名的[仁慈山会](../institutions/pio-monte-della-misericordia.md)是委托与保管主体；[《七件善事》](../works/caravaggio-seven-works-mercy.md)是建筑内部的独立作品。建筑分期不用于推断作品在后期重建中的移动细节。
