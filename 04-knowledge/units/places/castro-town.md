**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔在卡斯特罗战争叙述中称为“Castro”的小镇。第6章称该镇位于教皇领地以内；此处保留书内的历史地理表述，不据此推定现代行政归属或具体现址。

**English:** This card records the small town Haskell calls “Castro” in his account of the War of Castro. Chapter 6 places it within papal territory. This preserves the book’s historical geographical wording without inferring a present-day jurisdiction or a more precise location.

### 本章相关内容

哈斯克尔称该镇属于帕尔马公爵，并称一系列财政争端随后导致该公爵遭绝罚、镇子迅速被占领。原文没有指明执行绝罚或占领的行动者；这些表述仅作为作者所述的历史语境保留。

Haskell describes the town as belonging to the Duke of Parma and reports that financial disputes were followed by the Duke’s excommunication and the town’s rapid occupation. The passage does not name the actors who carried out the excommunication or occupation; these statements are retained as the author’s account.
