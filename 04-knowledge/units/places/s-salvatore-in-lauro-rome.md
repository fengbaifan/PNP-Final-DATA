## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记罗马的S. Salvatore in Lauro教堂建筑，与内部回廊候选`cand-6053`分开；哈斯克尔将常规画展描述为在教堂回廊举办，不把回廊并入教堂建筑。罗马教区名录确认堂区及其地址。〔[第5章 p.128](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_i.md)（L91–93）；[罗马教区名录](https://www.diocesidiroma.it/phpenti/ente/?ID=29)〕

**English:** This unit records the church building of S. Salvatore in Lauro, Rome, distinct from the cloisters candidate `cand-6053`. Haskell places the regular picture exhibition in the church’s cloisters; they are not merged into the church-building entity. The Diocese of Rome directory identifies the parish and its address.
