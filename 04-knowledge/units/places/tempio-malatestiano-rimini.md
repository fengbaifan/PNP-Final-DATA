## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell在第九章将里米尼的 Tempio Malatestiano 作为比较参照，讨论百合圣母堂立面对人的赞颂及神圣空间中的表现；本卡仅记录原书所指对象和比较语境。

**English:** In chapter 9, Haskell uses the Tempio Malatestiano at Rimini as a comparison when discussing the glorification of humanity in the façade of S. Maria del Giglio and its sacred setting. This card records only the source-named place and comparison context.
