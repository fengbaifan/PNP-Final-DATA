## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 记录哈斯克尔所称 Altieri 宫殿主厅。原书称主厅天顶到1673年已准备装饰；该日期不表示整座宫殿或装饰计划已经完工。

**English:** This unit records the principal hall of the Altieri palace. Haskell says its ceiling was ready for decoration by 1673; this does not mean that the palace or its decorative programme was complete.
