**English:** This card records the family palace near the Rialto described in Chapter 15. On page 363, Haskell calls it Palazzo Farsetti and describes it as the main centre for people absorbing theories about following classical models. The continuous passage supports treating these references as one source-local place. It does not identify a present-day address, use, or institutional identity for a surviving building.
