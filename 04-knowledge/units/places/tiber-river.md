## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 台伯河是哈斯克尔在本书中点名的河流地理特征。第2章以它定位萨凯蒂兄弟的宫殿；第4章把泰斯塔自尽的地点叙为此河；第5章称此河屡次决堤，洪水冲过罗马低地。原书未给出河段或各次洪水的具体年份。

**English:** The Tiber is a named river in Haskell’s account. Chapter 2 uses it to locate the Sacchetti brothers’ palace; Chapter 4 places Testa’s reported suicide in the river; and Chapter 5 says it repeatedly burst its banks and flooded Rome’s low-lying areas. The book gives neither a river section nor dates for individual floods.
