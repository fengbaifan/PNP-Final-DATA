## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 所称 S. Ignazio 教堂的后殿空间。第3章 p.74 记载 Pierre de Lattre 曾在后殿内墙绘制仿祭坛；p.89 又将 Pozzo 后来的壁画组定位于祭坛席与后殿。本卡仅表示建筑空间，不把仿祭坛、Pozzo 壁画或后殿并为同一对象，也不补充现状或精确边界。[第3章 p.74](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)；[第3章 p.89](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md)。

**English:** This unit records the apse space Haskell names in the church of S. Ignazio. Chapter 3, p. 74, says Pierre de Lattre painted an imitation altar on its inner wall; p. 89 places Pozzo’s later frescoes in the tribune and apse. This card represents the architectural space and keeps the imitation altar, Pozzo’s frescoes, and the apse distinct. It adds no claim about present condition or precise boundaries. [Chapter 3, p. 74](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md); [Chapter 3, p. 89](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md).
