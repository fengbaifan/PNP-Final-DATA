## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔关于奎里纳尔圣安德烈堂内圣母小堂的叙述。哈斯克尔称该小堂是最后装饰的一处，并转述一份1691年耶稣会文书称其“affatto rustica”；他接着称红衣主教 Ottoboni 接手小堂并委托 Ludovico David 制作《圣母诞生》《贤士朝拜》和《逃往埃及》祭坛画，费用来自 Pamfili 亲王留下的资金。哈斯克尔还说 Pamfili 继承人保留并后来行使更改小堂装饰的权利。以上均为书中叙述；本卡不确认作品是否完成或存世、小堂的正式名称及现状。

**English:** This unit records Haskell’s account of the chapel dedicated to the Madonna at S. Andrea al Quirinale. He calls it the last chapel to be adorned and reports that a Jesuit document described it as “affatto rustica” in 1691. He then says Cardinal Ottoboni took over the chapel and commissioned altarpieces by Ludovico David depicting the Nativity, the Adoration of the Magi, and the Flight into Egypt, paid for with funds left by Prince Pamfili. Haskell also says Pamfili’s heirs reserved and later exercised the right to change the chapel’s decoration. These are statements in the book; this unit does not establish whether the works were completed or survive, or the chapel’s formal name or present condition.

### 名称与身份边界

- “Chapel of the Madonna at S. Andrea al Quirinale”是依据原书“that dedicated to the Madonna”形成的描述性名称；来源没有给出正式建筑名称。
- 本小堂与 S. Andrea al Quirinale 教堂本体、主礼拜堂及来源提及的圣方济各·沙勿略小堂分立。教堂入口见[奎里纳尔圣安德烈堂](s-andrea-al-quirinale.md)。
