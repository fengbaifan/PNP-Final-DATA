**English:** The index has the heading “St Petersburg, Venetian artists in,” with references to pp. 297–298. Chapter 10, p. 297, says [Pietro Rotari]([Pietro Rotari](../persons/pietro-rotari.md)) went to St Petersburg by 1756 and died there six years later. On p. 298, Haskell reports that Bernardo Bellotto planned to go to the city; the same page says the *Banquet of Cleopatra* was transferred to St Petersburg in the 1760s and interprets this as a reflection of Europe’s changing balance of power and the diffusion of Venetian art. This card records the city as represented in the book; it does not identify the city with the Russian political entity or with an institution. The interpretation of the painting’s transfer is attributed to Haskell and is not treated as proof of its specific cause.
