## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称贝尔尼尼对圣彼得大殿交叉部的改造，并在转引欧文·拉文时将其描述为历时演变、最终未完全实现的过程。此卡把 crossing 作为来源限定的建筑内部空间记录，与圣彼得大殿整体和其中的作品区分；原书未说明精确空间边界，亦未独立查阅拉文原文。 [S1–S2]

**English:** Haskell discusses Bernini’s transformation of the crossing of St Peter’s and, while quoting Irving Lavin, describes it as an evolution over time that was never fully realized. This card records the crossing as a source-local interior architectural place, distinct from the basilica as a whole and from works within it. The source gives no exact spatial boundaries, and Lavin’s publication was not independently consulted. [S1–S2]
