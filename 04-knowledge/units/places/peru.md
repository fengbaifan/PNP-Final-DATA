## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell 将秘鲁列为灰泥像群所表现地区之一，并置于耶稣会传教活动的地域叙述中。该处没有把某一尊像对应到该地区，也未界定现代政治边界。

**English:** Haskell names Peru among the regions represented collectively by the stucco group in his account of Jesuit missionary reach. The source does not assign an individual figure to Peru or define a present-day political boundary.
