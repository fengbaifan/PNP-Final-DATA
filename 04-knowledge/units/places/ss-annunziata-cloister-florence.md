## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称礼拜堂位于Annunziata回廊，并称画作悬挂于回廊的lunettes。注5目录题名写作“Chiostro del Monastero de' Padri della SS. Nonziata di Firenze”；保留扫描可读拼写，不据外部资料校正。

**English:** Haskell locates the chapel in the Annunziata cloisters and says pictures were hung in the cloister lunettes. Note 5 gives the catalogue wording “Chiostro del Monastero de' Padri della SS. Nonziata di Firenze”; the scan's readable spelling is retained without external normalization.
