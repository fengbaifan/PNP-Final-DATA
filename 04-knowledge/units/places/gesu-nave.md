## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录耶稣堂的中殿这一建筑空间。图版14图注将作品置于该中殿；第3章 p.83 又称十六尊灰泥像位于中殿窗龛。此空间与耶稣堂整体及其中作品分别建模。

**English:** This card records the nave as an architectural space within the Gesù. The Plate 14 caption locates the work there, and chapter 3, p. 83 places the sixteen stucco figures in its window niches. The space remains distinct from the church as a whole and from works in it.
