## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称1706年展览在圣路加节举行于圣母领报修道院回廊内、奉献给圣路加的礼拜堂。对象范围限于书内所指礼拜堂，不与修道院整体或回廊合并。

**English:** Haskell places the 1706 St Luke's Day exhibition in a chapel dedicated to St Luke within the Annunziata cloisters. This unit is limited to the chapel named in the book and remains distinct from the monastery and its cloister.
