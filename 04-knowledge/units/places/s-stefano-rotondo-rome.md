## 内容

### 建筑地点身份

**中文：** 本卡记录第3章及后记共同指称的罗马 S. Stefano Rotondo 教堂建筑。第3章称其为耶稣会分得的一座早期基督教建筑；后记称 Circignani 的殉道壁画位于该耶稣会教堂。教堂建筑为 place，壁画组为独立 work；本卡不补写现代地址、建筑沿革或现存状况。

**English:** This card records the church building in Rome referred to in both Chapter 3 and the postscript as S. Stefano Rotondo. Chapter 3 calls it an early Christian building allotted to the Jesuits; the postscript places Circignani’s martyrdom frescoes in that Jesuit church. The church is a place and the fresco cycle is a separate work. No modern address, building history, or present condition is added.

### 对象边界

**中文：** 教堂建筑地点；殉道壁画组另作为 work，不与建筑合并。

**English:** The church building as a place; its martyrdom fresco cycle is a separate work.
