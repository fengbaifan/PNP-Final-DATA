## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔在多章中以 Vicenza 指称一座城市：第9章将其与 Valmarana 家族别墅和 Cordellina 宅邸相连，第14章记 Algarotti 曾旅行至此，第20章称 Valmarana 别墅在其附近。

**English:** Across several chapters Haskell uses Vicenza as a city: Chapter 9 associates it with the Valmarana family villa and a Cordellina house, Chapter 14 says Algarotti travelled there, and Chapter 20 places the Valmarana villa nearby.
