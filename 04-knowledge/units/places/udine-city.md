## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记第9章 p.252 所称的 Udine 城市。哈斯克尔称其为 “the seat of the bishopric”，并记 Dionisio Dolfin 获任后赴此居住。p.253 又把主教座堂与新大主教宫置于该城市语境中。本卡只表示城市，不与主教区、主教座堂、宫殿或内部作品合并；本次未补城市边界、坐标或现代行政信息。[第9章 p.252](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)（L120–122）[第9章 p.253](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)（L125）

**English:** This unit records the city of Udine as named on p.252 of Chapter 9. Haskell calls it “the seat of the bishopric” and says that Dionisio Dolfin went to live there after receiving the post. Page 253 places the Cathedral and the new Archiepiscopal Palace in the same city context. This unit denotes the city and keeps it distinct from the bishopric, Cathedral, palace, and interior works; no city boundary, coordinates, or present-day administrative information was added.
