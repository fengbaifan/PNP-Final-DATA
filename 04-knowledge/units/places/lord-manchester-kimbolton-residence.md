## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔第10章 p.279 将其称为 Lord Manchester 位于 Huntingdonshire 金博尔顿的一处宅邸，并说 Vanbrugh 为他重建了该宅邸。原书未给正式建筑名或重建年份；本卡只指这处宅邸，不与金博尔顿城镇或两处宅邸的集合称呼合并。

**English:** In chapter 10, p. 279, Haskell identifies a residence of Lord Manchester at Kimbolton in Huntingdonshire and says Vanbrugh rebuilt it for him. The book gives no formal building name or rebuilding date; this card represents that residence alone, not the town of Kimbolton or the collective reference to both mansions.
