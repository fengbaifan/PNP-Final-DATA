## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell在耶稣堂装饰委托及中殿壁画叙述中所指的主拱顶空间，包括合同范围中的窗龛。它与穹顶、祭坛席拱顶及拱顶上的绘饰作品分别识别。

**English:** This unit records the main vault space referred to by Haskell in the Gesù decoration commission and his account of the nave fresco, including the window recesses in the commission scope. It is distinguished from the dome, the tribune vault and the decorative works on the vault.
