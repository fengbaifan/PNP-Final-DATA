## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡表示Haskell为《圣安德烈殉难》指出的奎里纳尔圣安德烈堂高祭坛这一室内目的地。该段只记作品的预定用途地点；不据此识别一件独立祭坛艺术品，也不声称画作实际安装或仍存于此。

**English:** This unit represents the High Altar of S. Andrea al Quirinale as the interior destination Haskell names for The Martyrdom of St. Andrew. The passage identifies an intended destination; it does not identify a separate altar artwork or establish that the painting was installed or survives there.
