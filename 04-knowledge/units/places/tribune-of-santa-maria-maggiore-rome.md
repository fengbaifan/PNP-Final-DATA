## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔所称罗马圣母大殿的祭坛席空间。第6章印刷页162把它作为克勉九世拟改建方案的目标；该处没有说明祭坛席的精确建筑边界、完整平面或现状。本卡不把未实施的方案写成已完成的空间改造。

**English:** This unit records the tribune space of Santa Maria Maggiore in Rome as named by Haskell. Chapter 6, printed p.162 identifies it as the target of Clement IX’s proposed remodelling; the passage does not define its precise architectural boundaries, full plan, or present condition. This card does not treat the unexecuted proposal as a completed alteration.
