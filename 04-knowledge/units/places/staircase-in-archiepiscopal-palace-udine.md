## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔所述、蒂埃波罗在乌迪内新总主教宫楼梯天花板上开始壁画工作的内部空间。原书只称楼梯天花板，不说明该楼梯的建筑细节、确切空间范围或现存状态。 [[来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md#L125)]

**English:** This card records the interior space where Haskell says Tiepolo made a melodramatic start on the ceiling of the staircase in the new Archiepiscopal Palace at Udine. The account gives no architectural detail, exact spatial boundary, or present condition for the staircase.

### 身份边界

本组只对应来源所述宫殿楼梯空间；不把未具名的楼梯壁画与 Abraham 场景或《所罗门的审判》合并。

This group represents only the staircase space described in the source; the untitled staircase fresco is kept separate from the Abraham scenes and The Justice of Solomon.
