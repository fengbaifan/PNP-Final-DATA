## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 墨西拿圣额我略堂右侧横翼北墙上的历史小堂，供奉加尔默罗圣母。建筑史研究记载，Maria Teresa Ruffo于1688年委托建造此处大理石祭坛，以安置圭尔奇诺1665年绘制的画作；确切安置日期未载。谱系页列有两位同名修女，具体委托者身份未定，因此不建立人物关系。教堂于1908年地震后被拆除。（S1–S3）

**English:** A historic chapel on the north wall of the right transept of the Church of San Gregorio in Messina, dedicated to the Madonna del Carmine. Architectural research records that Maria Teresa Ruffo commissioned its marble altar in 1688 to house Guercino’s painting made in 1665; the exact installation date is not stated. A genealogy lists two nuns with that name, so the individual patron remains unresolved and no person relation is asserted. The church was demolished after the 1908 earthquake. (S1–S3)
