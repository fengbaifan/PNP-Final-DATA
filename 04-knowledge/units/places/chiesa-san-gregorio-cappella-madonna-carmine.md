## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 墨西拿圣额我略堂右侧横翼北墙上的历史小堂，供奉加尔默罗圣母；小堂设有大理石祭坛及其龛位。教堂在1908年地震后被拆除。意大利语教堂条目将该环境记作Maria Teresa Ruffo于1688年资助；谱系页另列两条在San Gregorio修院的同名人物记录，尚不能确定具体资助者，故不建立人物关系。（S1–S2）

**English:** A historic chapel on the north wall of the right transept of the Church of San Gregorio in Messina, dedicated to the Madonna del Carmine and furnished with a polychrome-marble altar and its niche. The church was demolished after the 1908 earthquake. The Italian church entry says the space was patronized by Maria Teresa Ruffo in 1688; a genealogy page separately lists two women of that name as nuns at San Gregorio, so the specific patron cannot yet be identified and no person relation is asserted. (S1–S2)
