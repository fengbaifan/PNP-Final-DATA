### 描述

**中文：** 本卡将哈斯克尔第9章中“威尼斯的耶稣会教堂”与同一叙述中的 Santa Maria Assunta 作为书内同一地点记录。该身份范围限于本书，不推断现代建筑身份、现存边界或现今状态。

**English:** This unit records the Jesuit church in Venice and Santa Maria Assunta in the same Chapter 9 narrative as one source-local place. The identity is limited to the book; no modern building identity, surviving boundary, or present condition is inferred.
