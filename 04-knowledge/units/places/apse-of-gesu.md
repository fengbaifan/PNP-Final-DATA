## 内容

### 空间身份

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔在第3章称为耶稣堂 apse 的内部建筑空间。第66页称，Cardinal Alessandro Farnese 去世前已有以马赛克覆盖后殿的计划。同段另行称述 Farnese 将祭坛席与 High Altar 的装饰保留给家族。本卡保留原书分别命名的对象，不据此断言它们边界互不重叠，也不补写现状。[第3章 p.66](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L8–9）

**English:** This unit records the interior architectural space Haskell calls the Gesù’s apse. On p. 66 he says plans had been made to cover it with mosaics before Cardinal Alessandro Farnese died. In the same passage, he separately names the tribune and High Altar as objects whose adornment Farnese reserved to his family. This card preserves the source’s distinct names without asserting non-overlapping boundaries or adding a present-day condition. [第3章 p.66](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md) (L8–9)

### 对象边界

后殿与已登记的[祭坛席地点卡](tribune-of-gesu.md)分别对应原书的 apse 与 tribune；二者的精确空间关系未在本卡建立。[罗马耶稣堂](gesu-rome.md)作为主建筑身份入口。
