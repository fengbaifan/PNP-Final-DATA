## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡将 Haskell 第6章 p.148 未具名的罗马别墅与 Villa Doria Pamphilj 作身份对齐。原文称 Camillo Pamfili 在流放期间负责由 Algardi 建造一座罗马名园，但没有给出正式名称。罗马首都督察局将 Villa Doria Pamphilj 记为 Pamphilj 家族的乡间庄园，并记载 Camillo 的营建活动及 Algardi 等艺术家在园区内参与工程。名称、人物、艺术家和地点语境相合，足以支持 S3 身份判断；不将 Haskell 对 Algardi 的表述扩写为其独自设计整个庄园。[第6章 p.148](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md)（L34–35） [罗马首都督察局：Villa Doria Pamphilj](https://sovraintendenzaroma.it/node/2148)

**English:** This card aligns the unnamed Roman villa in Haskell’s Chapter 6, p. 148, with Villa Doria Pamphilj. Haskell says that Camillo Pamfili was responsible during his exile for the construction by Algardi of a major Roman villa, without supplying its formal name. The Capitoline Superintendence identifies Villa Doria Pamphilj as the Pamphilj family’s country estate and records Camillo’s building activity and the participation of Algardi and other artists in the complex. The name, patron, artist, and place context support the S3 identity match; Haskell’s wording is not expanded into a claim that Algardi alone designed the whole estate.[第6章 p.148](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md)（L34–35） [罗马首都督察局：Villa Doria Pamphilj](https://sovraintendenzaroma.it/node/2148)

### 名称与范围

- **名称：** Villa Doria Pamphilj；书中未给出该名称，采用罗马文化遗产记录中的地点名称。[罗马首都督察局：Villa Doria Pamphilj](https://sovraintendenzaroma.it/node/2148)
- **地点性质：** 罗马的历史乡间庄园。外部记录将 Casino del Bel Respiro 列为园区中的单体建筑；本卡不等同于该 Casino。[罗马首都督察局：Villa Doria Pamphilj](https://sovraintendenzaroma.it/node/2148)
- **对象边界：** 与第6章 p.148 随后所称、位于 Collegio Romano 对面的宫殿分别建模；该宫殿另映射至既有 Palazzo Doria-Pamfili KU。[第6章 p.148](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md)（L34–35）
