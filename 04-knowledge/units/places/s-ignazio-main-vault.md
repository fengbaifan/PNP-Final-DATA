## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 S. Ignazio 教堂整面拱顶这一建筑空间。Haskell转引的传记叙述指向在此整面绘画的计划；其后作者叙述称拱顶此前曾有灰泥装饰，Pozzo于1688年开始新的壁画工程。此空间与旧灰泥装饰、后来壁画及其模型分属不同对象。[第3章 p. 90](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md#L147)

**English:** This unit records the architectural space of the entire vault of S. Ignazio. The biographical passage quoted by Haskell refers to the plan to paint the whole vault; Haskell’s narrative then says it had previously been decorated with stucco and that Pozzo began the fresco project in 1688. The space is distinct from the earlier stucco decoration, the later fresco, and its modello. [Chapter 3, p. 90](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md#L147)

### 空间边界

“整面拱顶”是描述性地点名称，指壁画工程所覆盖的建筑空间；它不是 S. Ignazio 教堂本体或拱顶上的任何一件作品。[第3章 p. 90](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md#L147)
