## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记 Haskell 多章叙述中作为地理地点使用的名称 Germany。S3 将第4章关于 Paolo Giordano II 服役地点的候选判为新 place 身份；其他章节的同名指称涉及画工迁移、作品流向、赞助者所在地域、旅行及收藏转运。本卡只记录书中这些地点指称及其限定，不据此补定现代边界、历史主权、具体路线或未列出的地点，也不把 Southern Germany 候选并入本组。第10章 p.295 的君主比较保留为 Haskell 的作者性表述；不据此建立政治实体或关系。[S1：第4章，印刷页96，正文行21](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/04_CHP-4_sec_i.md) [S4：第10章，印刷页293，正文行288–289](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md) [S5：第10章，印刷页295，正文行316](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/10_CHP-10_intro.md)

**English:** This unit records Germany as a geographic place name used across Haskell’s chapters. S3 classified the Chapter 4 candidate for the setting of Paolo Giordano II’s military service as a new place identity. Other chapters use the same place name in accounts of artisan migration, the destination of artistic output, patronage, travel, and collection transfer. This unit records those source-defined references and their limits; it does not add modern boundaries, historical sovereignty, specific routes, or unnamed destinations, and it does not merge the separate Southern Germany candidates. The comparison of rulers on p.295 remains Haskell’s authorial statement and does not create a political entity or relation.
