## 内容

### 描述
**中文：** 哈斯克尔以旧式拼写 Sinigaglia 称呼亚得里亚海沿岸的一处港口。Treccani《意大利百科全书》将 Senigallia 与 Sinigaglia 并列为同一地名。本卡采用书中拼写并记录规范对应；外部来源只支持名称与地点身份，不核实布拉乔利尼滞留事件。
**English:** Haskell uses the spelling Sinigaglia for a port on the Adriatic coast. The Treccani Enciclopedia Italiana lists Senigallia and Sinigaglia as the same place name. This unit preserves the book’s form and records the normalized identity; the external source supports the name and place identity only, not Bracciolini’s reported stay.
