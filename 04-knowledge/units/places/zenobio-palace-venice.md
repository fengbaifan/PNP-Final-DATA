## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell第9章所述、位于Carmini的泽诺比奥宫。原书称泽诺比奥家族从莫罗西尼家族购得此宫，并称其在1703年接近完成彻底改造；这些表述不证明现今产权或建筑现况。[第9章 p.250](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)（L92）

**English:** This card registers the Palazzo Zenobio described in chapter 9, at the Carmini. Haskell says the Zenobio family had bought the palace from the Morosini and that its transformation was nearing completion by 1703; this does not establish present ownership or condition. [Chapter 9, p. 250](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md) (L92)
