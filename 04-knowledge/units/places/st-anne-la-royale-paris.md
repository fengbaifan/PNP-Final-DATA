## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell称作St Anne-la-Royale的教堂建筑地点。第7章记载Mazarin遗嘱中的重建款项用途及心脏安葬指示，并称Guarini开始教堂工程；后续中断和约五十年后完成的施事者没有指明。遗嘱指示不等于本段独立证明心脏实际安葬。

**English:** This card records the church building named by Haskell as St Anne-la-Royale. Chapter 7 describes a rebuilding purpose for money left in Mazarin’s will, a direction concerning the burial of his heart, and Guarini’s beginning of work on the church. The agents responsible for the later interruption and completion are not named. The direction in the will is not independent proof here that the heart was actually buried there.
