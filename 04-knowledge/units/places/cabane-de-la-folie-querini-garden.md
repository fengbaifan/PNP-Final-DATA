## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔将“cabane de la folie”描述为一间茅草屋，处于第15章所述奎里尼园林的语境中。此卡依据原书的对象描述登记；园内精确位置与建筑现存状态均未确定。

**English:** Haskell describes the “cabane de la folie” as a thatched hut in the context of Querini’s garden in chapter 15. This card follows the source’s object description; its precise position within the garden and whether the structure survives are not established.
