## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录安杰洛·奎里尼位于阿尔蒂基耶罗庄园中的花园。哈斯克尔在同段将花园与住宅区分，称园林轮廓规整，其内灌木和植物呈现“愉悦的杂陈”；他将主园林解释为哲学家生活方式的完整寓意。该卡仅指花园，不代表住宅或整个庄园，也不推断园林的现存状态。

**English:** This unit represents the garden within Angelo Querini’s Alticchiero estate. Haskell distinguishes the garden from the house, describing a severely regular outline containing an “agreeable confusion” of bushes and plants; he interprets its main part as a complete allegory of the philosopher’s way of life. The unit covers the garden only, not the house or the wider estate, and does not assert that the garden survives.
