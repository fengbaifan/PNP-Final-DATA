## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔称 Angelo Querini 将余生大部分时间用于建造和装饰其位于 Alticchiero、距 Padua 一两英里的乡间住宅；他说明该住宅在写作时早已被夷平。此地点卡按原书的 villa／country house 与其园林所构成的庄园语境登记，不认定其现存建筑或遗址。

**English:** Haskell says Angelo Querini devoted most of his remaining life to building and decorating his country house at Alticchiero, a mile or two from Padua; the author reports that it had long since been razed by the time of his account. This place unit follows the villa/country-house and garden-estate context in the book and does not assert a surviving building or ruin.

### 本书语境

第15章 pp.369–370 主要依据 Giustiniana Wynne-Rosenberg 的记述介绍 Alticchiero：住宅以简朴、实用为特点，园林则由规整布局、植物、纪念性构件及雕塑组成。Haskell 将住宅与园林都置于 Querini 的哲学和审美表达中。

Chapter 15 pp. 369–370 describes Alticchiero chiefly through Giustiniana Wynne-Rosenberg’s account: the house was presented as simple and convenient, while the garden combined formal outlines, plants, monuments, and sculpture. Haskell treats both the house and garden as expressions of Querini’s philosophical and aesthetic interests.

### 类型记录

中文：类型：place。原书以 Alticchiero 指该乡间住宅地点，并在同段区分 villa 与 gardens；此卡采用庄园级范围，未另行登记单件建筑或花园构件。

English: Type: place. Haskell uses Alticchiero for the country-house site and distinguishes the villa from its gardens in the narrative; this card uses the estate-level scope and does not register individual structures or garden features.
