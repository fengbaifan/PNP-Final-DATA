## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡指第6章记述的 Cardinal Flavio Chigi 宫殿：Haskell 称它位于 Piazza SS. Apostoli，Flavio 于1662年向 Colonna 家族一支购入，并雇 Bernini 建造或重建。原书提到 Mei 的顶棚壁画、Canini 的其他顶棚、Gaulli 的神话画和肖像，以及大量古代雕像；p.155 说 Bernini 到 Alexander VII 在位近十年后才开始重建。名称为位置和人物限定的描述性工作名。[第6章 p.154](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md)（L91–100）；[第6章 p.155](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md)（L102–110）

**English:** This card identifies Cardinal Flavio Chigi's palace described in Chapter 6. Haskell locates it in Piazza SS. Apostoli, says Flavio bought it from a branch of the Colonna family in 1662, and says he employed Bernini to build or rebuild it. The book mentions ceiling paintings by Mei, other ceilings by Canini, mythological paintings and portraits by Gaulli, and numerous antique statues; p.155 says Bernini began rebuilding only after Alexander VII had been pope for nearly ten years. The name is a descriptive working label based on place and patron. [第6章 p.154](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md)（L91–100）; [第6章 p.155](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md)（L102–110）
