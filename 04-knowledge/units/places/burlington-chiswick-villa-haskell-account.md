## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录索引所列“house at Chiswick”、并由哈斯克尔第10章注7称为伯灵顿勋爵别墅的建筑地点。哈斯克尔称别墅始于1725年；其关于Ricci作品从伯爵城中住宅转移至此的说法明确为推测。此卡不据此确定现代建筑实体或完整产权史。

**English:** This card records the “house at Chiswick” in the index, called Lord Burlington’s villa in Haskell’s chapter 10, note 7. Haskell says the villa was begun in 1725; his suggestion that Ricci’s works were transferred there from the Earl’s town house is explicitly probable rather than certain. This card does not identify a modern building entity or establish a complete ownership history.
