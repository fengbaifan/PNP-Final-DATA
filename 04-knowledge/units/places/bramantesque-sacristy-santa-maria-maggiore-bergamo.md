## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本地点知识元记录 Haskell 第8章 p.215 所述贝加莫 Santa Maria Maggiore 教堂内的祭衣间。原书称其以新的布拉曼特式增设于15世纪末。本卡将祭衣间作为独立内部空间（place），与教堂整体及内部绘画、雕塑等作品区分；原书关于它使人更难理解原有平面布局的判断保留为 Haskell 的表述，不转写为已核验的建筑效果。

**English:** This place unit records the sacristy inside Santa Maria Maggiore in Bergamo, as described on p. 215 of Haskell’s Chapter 8. The book says it was added in a new Bramantesque style toward the end of the fifteenth century. The sacristy is treated as a distinct interior space (place), separate from the church as a whole and from its paintings, sculptures, and other works. Haskell’s assessment that it made the original plan harder to appreciate remains attributed to him rather than presented as a verified architectural effect.
