## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell第13章中与Remondini印刷商行设立地相关的Bassano。Treccani词条将Bassano del Grappa定位于Vicenza省，并提到当地的Remondini雕版印刷业；Treccani《意大利人传记辞典》也将该印刷企业及其生产置于Bassano。与第2章明确写作Bassano di Sutri的Viterbo地点不是同一实体。

**English:** This unit records the place called Bassano in Haskell’s chapter 13 account of the Remondini firm’s establishment. Treccani identifies Bassano del Grappa in the province of Vicenza and notes the Remondini printmaking business there; Treccani’s *Dizionario Biografico degli Italiani* also places the firm and its production at Bassano. This is distinct from the Viterbo place explicitly called Bassano di Sutri in chapter 2.
