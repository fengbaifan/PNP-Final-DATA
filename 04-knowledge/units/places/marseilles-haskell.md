### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell第7章所称的马赛。原书注释报告一件Guercino画作位于Marseilles，另一处正文称Gregorio de Ferrari赴Marseilles为Noailles作画；两处不指认具体馆藏机构或画作的当前所在地。

**English:** This unit records Marseilles as named in Haskell’s Chapter 7. A note reports a Guercino painting at Marseilles, while a separate passage says Gregorio de Ferrari went there to paint for Noailles. Neither passage identifies a specific holding institution or establishes the painting’s present location.
