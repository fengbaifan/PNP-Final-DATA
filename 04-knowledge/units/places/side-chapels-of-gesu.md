## 内容

### 空间身份

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔在耶稣堂早期装饰叙述中统称的侧礼拜堂群。第66页以复数 side-chapels 指称这些内部空间，并称主装饰工程停滞后，耶稣会转而集中于此。此为集合性地点记录；原文未列全体成员，本卡不补列具体礼拜堂或认定群内成员。[第3章 p.66](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L9–11）

**English:** This unit records the collective group of chapels Haskell refers to as the Gesù’s side-chapels in his account of the church’s early decoration. On p. 66 he uses the plural “side-chapels” for these interior spaces and says the Jesuits turned their efforts to them after the main decoration stalled. The source does not enumerate the complete set; this card does not identify specific members. [第3章 p.66](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md) (L9–11)

### 对象边界

这是原书复数总称对应的集合性地点，不替代已单独登记的具体礼拜堂卡，也不据此建立具体成员或包含关系。[罗马耶稣堂](gesu-rome.md)仅作身份导航入口。
