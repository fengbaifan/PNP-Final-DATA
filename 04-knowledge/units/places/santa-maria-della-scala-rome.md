## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 罗马阶梯圣母堂是罗马教堂建筑。《圣母之死》的原定安置地点；作品被拒后离开。

**English:** Santa Maria della Scala is a church building in Rome and the intended site for Death of the Virgin, which left the church after being rejected.
