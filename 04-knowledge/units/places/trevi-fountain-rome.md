## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第8章 p.221 提及 Trevi Fountain，将其作为定位 Cardinal Tommaso Ruffo 晚年居住的罗马宫殿的相对地标。本卡只记录原书点名的喷泉，不补充坐标、地址或其他建筑沿革。

**English:** Chapter 8, p. 221, names the Trevi Fountain as the relative landmark locating the Rome palace where Cardinal Tommaso Ruffo lived in old age. This card records only the fountain named in the book and adds no coordinates, address, or building history.

### 本书相关内容

该喷泉在本段作为罗马宫殿的相对定位地标。原文称宫殿位于喷泉对面；本卡不将喷泉与宫殿合并，也不将位置关系表述为宫殿属于喷泉。

The fountain appears as a relative landmark for a Rome palace. The text places the palace opposite the fountain; this card keeps the two places distinct and does not describe the palace as part of the fountain.
