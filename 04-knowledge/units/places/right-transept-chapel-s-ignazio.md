## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记Haskell第3章p.93所称S. Ignazio右侧耳堂内的礼拜堂。Haskell称该礼拜堂于1749年完工，并在同句提到Filippo della Valle与Pietro Bracci制作《天使报喜》大理石浅浮雕。原文未给礼拜堂专名或独立奉献名称；本卡不补外部身份或现存状态。

**English:** This card records the chapel in the right transept of S. Ignazio described by Haskell in Chapter 3, p. 93. He says it was completed in 1749 and, in the same sentence, mentions the marble bas-relief of the Annunciation made by Filippo della Valle and Pietro Bracci. The passage supplies no proper chapel name or separate dedication; no external identity or present condition is asserted.
