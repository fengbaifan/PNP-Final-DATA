## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录馆藏目录标示的单体地点“Unteres Belvedere”（Lower Belvedere）；中文名为项目工作译名。哈斯克尔第7章 p.201 将 Upper 与 Lower Belvederes 并列为意大利画家绘制壁画的地点。本卡与既有“two Belvederes”集合候选区分，不指认具体房间、单件壁画或当前展陈位置。

**English:** This card records the singular place labeled “Unteres Belvedere” (Lower Belvedere) in the museum catalog; the Chinese name is a project translation. Haskell’s Chapter 7, p. 201 names the Upper and Lower Belvederes together as sites where Italian painters came to paint frescoes. This card is distinct from the existing pair candidate “two Belvederes”; it does not identify a specific room, fresco, or current display location.
