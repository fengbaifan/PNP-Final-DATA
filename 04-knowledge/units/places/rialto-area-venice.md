### 描述

**中文：** 第10章景观叙述中的宽泛里亚托地点；与具名的里亚托广场Campo di Rialto分开，不指定建筑或精确点位。

**English:** The broader Rialto locality named at the far end of the Chapter 10 panorama; distinct from the specifically named Campo di Rialto and not resolved to a building or exact point.
