## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 坎诺斯位于 Edgware。哈斯克尔记载詹姆斯·布里奇斯于1713年取得此处的旧伊丽莎白时期庄园，并在随后数年将其改建为大型宫殿；其注释称坎诺斯于1747年拆毁。这里仅记录该书的历史叙述。

**English:** Canons at Edgware. Haskell reports that James Brydges acquired the old Elizabethan manor in 1713 and transformed it into a large palace over the following years; his note states that Canons was demolished in 1747. This records the book's historical account only.
