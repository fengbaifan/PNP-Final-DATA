## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 切拉西礼拜堂是礼拜堂。位于人民圣母堂，现存两幅侧壁画与原委托对象分开。

**English:** The Cerasi Chapel is a funerary chapel in Santa Maria del Popolo; the two extant lateral paintings are distinguished from the original commission objects and first versions.
