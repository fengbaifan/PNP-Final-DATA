## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 佛罗伦萨位于意大利托斯卡纳的阿尔诺河畔，是中世纪贸易、金融及文艺复兴艺术的重要城市。第一章用其说明 Barberini、Sacchetti 的地域背景与艺术家获得保护的联系，也将其列为 Bonati 的学习旅行目的地。城市身份不等于共和国、大公国或家族身份。（S1–S3、S6、S8）

**English:** Florence is a city on the River Arno in Tuscany, Italy, important in medieval trade and finance and in Renaissance art. Chapter 1 uses it to explain the regional backgrounds of the Barberini and Sacchetti and their connections with artistic patronage, and names it as a destination on Bonati's study journey. The city is distinct from a republic, grand duchy or family. (S1–S3, S6, S8)
