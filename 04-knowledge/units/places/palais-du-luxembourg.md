## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔索引中题作 “Luxembourg, Palais du” 并指向第7章 p.174 的宫殿。哈斯克尔称 Marie de Medici 于1611年命其建筑师 Salomon de Brosse 为她建造一座大型宫殿；Marie 曾索取 Palazzo Pitti 的图样，希望以其为范本，但哈斯克尔称新宫采用了全然不同的设计。宫殿建在她此前购入的另一座宫殿旧址上；早先建筑与新建的 Palais du Luxembourg 分开记录。工程延迟所涉“内部问题”未在此处识别，本卡不据此认定建成日期、具体设计特征或现今状态。

**English:** This unit records the palace indexed by Haskell as “Luxembourg, Palais du” and referred to in Chapter 7, p.174. Haskell says that in 1611 Marie de Medici ordered her architect Salomon de Brosse to build a large palace for her. Marie requested plans of Palazzo Pitti in the hope of using it as a model, but Haskell says the new palace was designed on wholly different lines. It was built on the site of an earlier palace she had bought; the earlier building and the new Palais du Luxembourg remain distinct. The “internal problems” delaying the work are not identified here. This unit does not establish a completion date, specific design features, or present condition.
