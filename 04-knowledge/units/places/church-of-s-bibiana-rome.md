**English:** In Chapter 2, Haskell refers to a small church as S. Bibiana and reports that Pope Urban VIII had it completely rebuilt after the reported discovery of a saint’s body. This unit records the church as a place, distinct from the reconstruction and decoration project and the interior statue. The remains’ identity, the place’s external identity, and its present state are not verified here.
