## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 基吉—奥德斯卡尔基宫是罗马宫殿建筑。《圣保罗归化》第一版的保管地点；不等于委托发生地。

**English:** Palazzo Chigi-Odescalchi is a palace in Rome and the current custodial location of the first Conversion of Saint Paul; it is not treated as the place where the commission occurred.
