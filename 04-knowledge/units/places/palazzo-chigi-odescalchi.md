## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 基吉—奥德斯卡尔基宫是罗马宫殿建筑。《圣保罗归化》第一版的保管地点；不等于委托发生地。

**English:** Palazzo Chigi-Odescalchi is a palace in Rome and the current custodial location of the first Conversion of Saint Paul; it is not treated as the place where the commission occurred.

### 17世纪的宫殿身份与来源日期

**中文：** 第5章p.140注1将Chigi购入的建筑称作“the palace (later Palazzo Odescalchi) at SS. Apostoli”，并报1661年；第6章p.154称Cardinal Flavio Chigi于1662年从Colonna家族一支购入Piazza SS. Apostoli的宫殿。两处的买方、卖方和地点对应同一宫殿，因此本卡保留这段历史称名；两个年份按各自原文并列，未作消解。[第5章p.140注1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_iii.md)（L154）；[第6章p.154](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md)（L96）

**English:** Chapter 5, p.140 n.1 calls the building bought by the Chigi “the palace (later Palazzo Odescalchi) at SS. Apostoli” and gives 1661. Chapter 6, p.154 says Cardinal Flavio Chigi bought the palace at Piazza SS. Apostoli from a branch of the Colonna family in 1662. The buyer, seller, and site identify the same palace; both source dates are retained without resolution. [Chapter 5, p.140 n.1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_iii.md) (L154); [Chapter 6, p.154](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md) (L96)

### 建筑、内部空间与作品

本卡只记录当前作品链所需的委托、原定安置、历史安置或现存地点；建筑与管理机构、城市和内部独立作品不合并。

**中文：** 第6章p.154还描述宫殿有贯穿上两层的巨型壁柱立面，并列举Mei的寓意画、Canini的其他天花画、Gaulli的神话画与肖像及大量古代雕像。此处保留Haskell对建筑陈设的原文记录；Canini、Gaulli和古代雕像的未名作品组仍待身份与端点核实，沿用`bl-0067`–`bl-0069`。已有Mei壁画关系不变，本段不新增边。[第6章p.154](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md)（L96–97）

**English:** Chapter 6, p.154 also describes a façade with colossal pilasters running through the top two floors, and lists Mei allegories, other ceilings by Canini, Gaulli mythological paintings and portraits, and numerous antique statues. This preserves Haskell’s account of the palace contents. The unidentified Canini, Gaulli, and antique-statue groups remain unresolved as identities and endpoints under `bl-0067`–`bl-0069`. Existing Mei fresco relations are unchanged; this passage adds no edge. [Chapter 6, p.154](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md) (L96–97)
