## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell第10章注5中称作Chatsworth的乡间宅邸地点。原书将其称为德文郡公爵的乡间宅邸，并报告Ricci为伯灵顿勋爵绘制的两幅画在此。本卡限于该来源所指地点，不据此断言现时产权、管理关系或画作现今的实际存放位置。

**English:** This unit records the country-house place called Chatsworth in Haskell’s chapter 10, note 5. The book describes it as the country house of the Dukes of Devonshire and reports two paintings made by Ricci for Lord Burlington there. This unit is limited to the source-referred place and does not assert present ownership, management, or the paintings’ current physical location.
