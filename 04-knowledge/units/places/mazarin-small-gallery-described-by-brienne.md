## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Brienne回忆中Mazarin住所新公寓内的一处小画廊。她的叙述称画廊悬有Scipio题材的羊毛挂毯；原书没有给出画廊专名。该空间不与第7章较早记载的上层画廊合并。

**English:** This card records the small gallery in the new apartments of Mazarin’s residence described in Brienne’s memoir. The account says that a wool tapestry depicting Scipio hung there; the book gives no proper name for the gallery. This space is kept separate from the upper gallery described earlier in Chapter 7.
