## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔以“at Richelieu”指称黎塞留城堡所在地点。本卡保留原文中的地点指称，将其与黎塞留枢机及城堡建筑分开；不扩展为现代行政区划或外部地理身份。

**English:** Haskell uses “at Richelieu” for the locality of the Cardinal's chateau. This card preserves that source-level place reference, distinct from Cardinal Richelieu and the building; it does not add modern administrative boundaries or an external geographic identity.
