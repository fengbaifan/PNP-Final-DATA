## 内容

### 空间身份

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔所称耶稣堂祭坛席的拱顶空间。第80页称该拱顶留给Borgognone，未纳入盖乌利装饰委托；第81页另称盖乌利仅绘制“vault above it”的一小部分。本卡不推断该绘饰的精确范围、现状或独立作品边界。

**English:** This unit records the vaulted space Haskell calls the vault of the Gesù tribune. On p. 80 he says it was kept for Borgognone and excluded from Gaulli’s decoration commission. On p. 81 he separately reports that Gaulli painted only a small fraction of “the vault above it.” This card does not infer the painted area’s exact extent, present condition, or a distinct artwork boundary.

### 对象边界

- 该拱顶与较大的[耶稣堂祭坛席空间](tribune-of-gesu.md)分立：本卡记录其中的拱顶区域，parent place 保留原书所称的 tribune 整体空间。
- 与盖乌利的[耶稣堂穹顶与拱顶装饰委托](../works/gaulli-gesu-dome-and-vault-decoration-commission.md)、其穹顶、主拱顶及后殿 conch 绘饰分别建模；不将空间与工程或单件绘饰合并。
