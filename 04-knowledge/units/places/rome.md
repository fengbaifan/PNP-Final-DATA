### 描述

**中文：** 罗马（Rome；意大利语 Roma）位于台伯河流域，是第一章讨论教皇赞助、家户、教堂委托和艺术市场的城市空间。历史上的教皇国首都与今日意大利首都涉及不同政治时期；城市不等同于教廷、梵蒂冈或单个赞助人。[S1–4、S7]

**English:** Rome (Italian: Roma), on the Tiber, is the urban setting for the chapter's papal patronage, households, church commissions and art market. Its roles as a capital of the Papal States and of modern Italy belong to different periods. The city is distinct from the Holy See, Vatican City and individual patrons. [S1–4, S7]
