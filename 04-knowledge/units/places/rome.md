## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第一章的城市与教皇赞助中心。作者以教皇更替、家户、同乡引介、教堂与工作室交易解释多样赞助条件，强调艺术家社会地位不能用单一定义概括。

原文 richest city in Italy 指意大利，不扩大为欧洲。城市是地理知识对象，不预设为涌现后的领域；第六章论述暂不进入本章样例。

**English:** Rome is the chapter's urban centre of papal patronage. Haskell explains varied conditions through papal succession, households, compatriot introductions, churches and studio transactions, stressing that no single definition captures artists' social position. Richest city in Italy is not extended to Europe. The city is a geographical object, not a predetermined emergent domain; chapter 6 remains outside the sample.
