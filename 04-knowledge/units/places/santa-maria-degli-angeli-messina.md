## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 墨西拿天使圣母教堂是墨西拿已不存的教堂／委托地点。《牧羊人朝拜》为该教堂方济各会小兄弟会而作，现藏博物馆。

**English:** Santa Maria degli Angeli in Messina was a church and commissioning site for Adoration of the Shepherds, painted for its Franciscan community and now held by a museum.
