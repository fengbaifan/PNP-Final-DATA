## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本地点知识元按哈斯克尔在第9章中的称呼，记录威尼斯的 Pietà 教堂。作者称其在18世纪上半叶建造，并叙述1745年决定重建、1754—1755年 Tiepolo 在椭圆形穹顶绘制《信仰的胜利》；另一处又将穹顶壁画说成教堂的委托。卡片保留来源名称，不推定其完整的意大利语正式名称。

**English:** This place unit records the church in Venice that Haskell calls the Pietà in Chapter 9. He describes it as erected during the first half of the eighteenth century, reports the decision to rebuild it in 1745, and says Tiepolo painted The Triumph of Faith on its oval dome in 1754–55; elsewhere he refers to Tiepolo’s dome frescoes in the church. The card preserves Haskell’s source wording and does not infer a full Italian formal name.

### 本书语境

**原书语境：** 第9章 p.272 将教堂置于威尼斯宗教建筑与官方赞助讨论中：作者回述其14世纪的收容所前身、威尼斯总督与元老院的赞助，继而叙述1745年重建、Massari 的建筑设计及立面迟至约150年后才完成。第9章 p.202 另将 Tiepolo 为该教堂穹顶作画作为画家酬金比较的一例。

**Book context:** Chapter 9, p. 272 discusses the church in the context of Venetian religious architecture and public patronage. Haskell recounts the fourteenth-century refuge on the site, patronage by the Doge and Senate, the 1745 rebuilding decision, Massari’s architectural role, and the façade’s much later completion. On p. 202 he also cites Tiepolo’s dome frescoes there when comparing artists’ fees.

### 身份边界

中文：**对象边界：** 本卡记录教堂这一 place 身份，不等同于旧址上的收容所／慈善机构、椭圆穹顶或 Tiepolo 的《信仰的胜利》壁画作品。哈斯克尔仅以 Pietà 称呼教堂；未凭本轮证据补写完整意大利语正式名称，也未将同址旧机构与后来教堂合并。

English: **Identity boundary:** This card denotes the church as a place, distinct from the earlier refuge or charitable institution on the site, the oval dome, and Tiepolo’s fresco The Triumph of Faith. Haskell calls the church the Pietà; this registration does not supply a full Italian formal name or merge the earlier institution with the later church.
