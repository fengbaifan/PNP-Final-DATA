## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 在第3章 p.89 所称 S. Ignazio 教堂的祭坛席空间。原文将 Pozzo 的壁画组定位于祭坛席和后殿，未说明空间的精确边界或现状。本卡仅表示建筑内部空间，不将其与位于其中的壁画组混为一体。[第3章 p.89](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md)。

**English:** This unit records the tribune space in the church of S. Ignazio named by Haskell in Chapter 3, p. 89. He locates Pozzo’s fresco cycle in the tribune and apse but gives no precise spatial boundaries or present condition. This card represents the architectural interior, distinct from the fresco group located there. [Chapter 3, p. 89](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_iv.md).
