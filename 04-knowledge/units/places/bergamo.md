## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Bergamo 是本书多章及书后索引所指的城市。S3 将索引主项与正文中 Codazzi 所称 patria、Santa Maria Maggiore 所在城市，以及 Zaccaria Sagredo 的任职地点合并为同一城市身份组。此卡只登记地名及书中城市指称，不据此扩写现代行政边界、历史政治归属或城市沿革，也不将人物履历、教堂或任职事件并入城市实体。[书后索引 B.csv#325](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-2-Index-CSV/B.csv) [第5章 p.153 注6](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_iii.md) [第8章 p.223](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md) [第9章 p.263](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)

**English:** Bergamo is a city named across the book and its index. S3 groups the index heading with the city references in Codazzi’s quoted patria, the location of Santa Maria Maggiore, and the place of Zaccaria Sagredo’s governorship. This unit records the place name and the book’s use of it as a city; it does not add modern administrative boundaries, a full political history, or a city chronology, and it keeps biographies, the church, and office-holding events separate from the city entity.
