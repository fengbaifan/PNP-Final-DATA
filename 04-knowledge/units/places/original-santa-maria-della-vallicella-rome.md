## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell第3章所述被拆除的较小S. Maria della Vallicella教堂。原书称教宗格列高利十三世于1575年将其分配给腓力·内里；该堂位于Rome，后因被认为过小而拆除，由同名较大的教堂取代，后者常称Chiesa Nuova。本卡所指旧堂与已登记的后继教堂Chiesa Nuova分别识别；原书没有提供精确拆除年份或旧堂建筑的其他身份信息。

**English:** This card records the smaller S. Maria della Vallicella church described in Haskell’s chapter 3, which was later demolished. Haskell says Pope Gregory XIII assigned it to Philip Neri in 1575 and places it in Rome. The church was considered too small and was pulled down to make way for a larger building of the same name, often called Chiesa Nuova. This earlier church is distinct from the registered successor church, Chiesa Nuova; Haskell gives no precise demolition year or further identity details for the earlier building.
