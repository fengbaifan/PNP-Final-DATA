### 描述

**中文：** 本书中 France 作为国家地点，出现在 Bernini 访问耶稣会教堂、威尼斯艺术家赴法及意大利艺术在法国传播等语境。本卡汇集原书对法国这一地点的跨章指称，不把历史国家称谓规范成现代政权或固定边界。

**English:** In Haskell’s account, France is a country-place in passages about Bernini’s visits to Jesuit churches, Venetian artists travelling there, and the circulation of Italian art. This unit groups the book’s cross-chapter references to France without normalizing the historical name to a modern polity or fixed boundary.
