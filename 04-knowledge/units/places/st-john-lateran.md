## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本地点知识元记录 Haskell 所称的 St John Lateran，即书中称为罗马主教座堂的建筑。第6章 p.147 描述其当时仍保留大量中世纪面貌，并与 Urban VIII 装饰的圣彼得大殿对照；这些是 Haskell 的历史叙述，不表示当前建筑状态。

**English:** This place unit records the building Haskell calls St John Lateran, which he describes as the Cathedral of Rome. Chapter 6, p. 147, portrays it as retaining much of its medieval character at that time and contrasts it with Urban VIII’s decoration of St Peter’s. These are Haskell’s historical statements, not claims about the building’s present condition.
