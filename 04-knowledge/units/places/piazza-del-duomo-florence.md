## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 第8章把 Piazza del Duomo 记作 Ferdinand 将加比亚尼《逃往埃及》向公众展示的地点。仅记录哈斯克尔的书内称谓，不补充现代广场身份、建筑边界或展示日期。

**English:** Chapter 8 names the Piazza del Duomo as the place where Ferdinand put Gabbiani's Flight into Egypt on public view. This unit records Haskell's wording only and adds no modern site identification, architectural boundary, or display date.
