## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第15章所述阿尔蒂基耶罗宅邸中的图书室。原文称该空间存有藏书，并记有培根胸像；本卡仅表达来源中的历史空间语境，不断言其现今存续或位置。

**English:** This unit records the library room in the Alticchiero house described in chapter 15. Haskell says that it held books and mentions a bust of Bacon there. The card preserves the historical setting in the source and makes no claim about the room’s present survival or location.
