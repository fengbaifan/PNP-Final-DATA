## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Young’s Wood 是哈斯克尔所述阿尔蒂基耶罗花园中、距住宅一段距离的野趣区域。文中称其树木经过布置，枝叶间可见柱子和散置的葬礼瓮；这些物件没有逐件识别。中文显示名“青年林”是便于阅读的暂译；英文名称取自原书，并指向诗人 Edward Young。

**English:** Young’s Wood is a wild area in Haskell’s account of the Alticchiero garden, at some distance from the house. Its trees were artfully sited, with columns and burial urns visible through their branches; the objects are not individually identified. The Chinese display name is a descriptive rendering; the English name follows the book and refers to the poet Edward Young.
