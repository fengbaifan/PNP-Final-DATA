## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔将 S. Vitale 称为罗马耶稣会所拥有的第三所教堂，并描述为一座早期基督教建筑；他还记载 Clement VIII 将其交予耶稣会。本卡记录来源所述的历史身份和归属，不推定现代产权、现存状态或精确地址。

**English:** Haskell identifies S. Vitale as the third church the Jesuits owned in Rome, describes it as another early Christian building, and says Clement VIII gave it to the Society. This card records the historical identity and attribution in the source without asserting present-day ownership, physical condition, or a precise address.
