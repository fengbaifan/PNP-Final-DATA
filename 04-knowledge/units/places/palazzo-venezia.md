## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 罗马的宫殿，巴托尼《威尼斯的胜利》1737年完成后悬挂于此，当时用作威尼斯使馆。

**English:** A palace in Rome where Batoni’s The Triumph of Venice hung after its completion in 1737, when the building served as the Venetian embassy.
