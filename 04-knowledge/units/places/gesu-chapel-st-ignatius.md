## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本书将耶稣堂圣依纳爵礼拜堂列为 Gaulli 拱顶装饰工程覆盖的内部空间之一；后文又叙述画家于1685年完成耶稣堂左横翼、圣依纳爵祭坛上方的绘画。本卡登记礼拜堂空间，不将其与祭坛或绘画合并。

**English:** Haskell names the Chapel of St Ignatius as one of the internal spaces beneath the transept vaults included in Gaulli’s decoration commission. He later says that in 1685 Gaulli completed work in the Gesù’s left transept above the altar of St Ignatius. This unit records the chapel separately from its altar and paintings.

### 名称

中文显示名及英文显示名见卡片标题；中文名为描述性工作译名，非原书正式题名。

### 地点信息

- 地点性质：耶稣堂内部礼拜堂。\n- 所在建筑：罗马 Gesù 教堂；本卡不复写建筑卡的基本信息。\n- 空间语境：第3章将圣依纳爵与圣方济各·沙勿略礼拜堂列在耶稣堂横翼拱顶之下；后文提及左横翼圣依纳爵祭坛上方的绘画。\n- 原书没有在此为礼拜堂给出独立的建成年份或完整空间测绘。

- Place type: an internal chapel within the Gesù.\n- Parent building: the Gesù in Rome; this card does not duplicate the church card’s general description.\n- Spatial context: Chapter 3 names the chapels of St Ignatius and St Francis Xavier beneath the Gesù’s transept vaults and later mentions painting above the altar of St Ignatius in the left transept.\n- The cited passages do not provide a separate construction date or complete architectural survey.

### 身份边界

中文：本卡为礼拜堂建筑空间，不是其祭坛、祭坛画或拱顶绘画；不与耶稣堂建筑本体合并，也不与另一座 S. Ignazio 教堂的圣依纳爵礼拜堂混为一处。

English: This unit is the chapel space, not its altar, altarpiece, or vault paintings. It remains distinct from the Gesù church building as a whole and from the chapel of St Ignatius at the separate church of S. Ignazio.
