## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell将四幅画作的礼拜堂称为 Portland 位于 Bulstrode Park 的乡间宅邸之 chapel。本卡只记录书内可区分的建筑内部空间，不把礼拜堂等同于宅邸或庄园，也不声称其现存身份已核实。

**English:** Haskell identifies the chapel for the four paintings as belonging to Portland’s country house at Bulstrode Park. This card records the interior space as distinct from the house and estate; it does not claim that the surviving architectural identity has been verified.

### 本章相关内容

礼拜堂是 programme 的原定场所语境。Haskell以天主教堂作比较，并未说明该礼拜堂属于天主教机构。

The chapel is the stated destination context for the programme. Haskell compares the programme to one suitable for a Catholic church but does not say this chapel belonged to a Catholic institution.
