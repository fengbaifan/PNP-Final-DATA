## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell所述耶稣堂穹顶空间，包括其所提及的灯笼和帆拱；该空间与主拱顶、祭坛席拱顶以及其上的绘饰作品分别识别。

**English:** This unit records the Gesù dome space in Haskell's account, including the lantern and pendentives he mentions. The space is distinguished from the main vault, the tribune vault and the decorative works on these surfaces.
