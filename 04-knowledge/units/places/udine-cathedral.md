## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔第9章所称的 Udine Cathedral。第251页将唱诗席列为彩色大理石或灰泥帷幔状装饰的 Manin 风格实例；第268页记载 Manin 家族于1706年提出装饰该堂唱诗席、耳堂和主祭坛的方案。本卡只记录原书所述地点及范围，不补正式意大利语名称、建筑年代、现存状况，也不把提议写成已完成的委托或装饰。该主教座堂与 Udine 城市及附近的 Cappella della Purità 分别建模。[第9章 p.251](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md#L112) [第9章 p.268](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_sec_ii.md#L14)

**English:** This unit records the Udine Cathedral named in Chapter 9. On p. 251 Haskell identifies its choir as an instance of the Manin style of coloured-marble or stucco decoration imitating draperies. On p. 268 he reports that the Manin offered in 1706 to decorate the choir, transept, and High Altar. This unit records only the place and scope described in the book; it supplies no formal Italian name, building date, or present condition, and does not treat the offer as a completed commission or decoration. The Cathedral remains distinct from the city of Udine and the nearby Cappella della Purità.
