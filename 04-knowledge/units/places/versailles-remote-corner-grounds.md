### 描述

**中文：** 本地点卡记录哈斯克尔所述路易十四骑马像被放置于凡尔赛园地最偏远角落。原文未给出精确位置；该记录只指历史安置地点，不表示作品现址。

**English:** This place card records Haskell’s account that Louis XIV’s equestrian statue was placed in a remote corner of the Versailles grounds. The exact location is unnamed; this is a historical placement, not a statement of the work’s present location.
