## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 哈斯克尔将欧根亲王相关的两座 Belvederes 归于 Lucas von Hildebrandt，并称它们见证了欧根亲王对建筑的热情。本卡保留成对对象，不分别识别两座建筑，也不将其与现存建筑对应。

**English:** Haskell attributes the two Belvederes associated with Prince Eugene to Lucas von Hildebrandt and describes them as testimonials to Eugene's passion for building. This card preserves the pair as a single source-local object group without identifying each building or matching them to specific surviving structures.
