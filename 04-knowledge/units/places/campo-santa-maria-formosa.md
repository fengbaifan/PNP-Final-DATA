## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记威尼斯的 Campo di S. Maria Formosa 广场。哈斯克尔第10章 p.327 称该场所是 Giampiccoli 版画所描绘的景观，并说 Pisani 的宫邸位于该广场，但没有指出是哪一栋建筑。威尼斯市政府现行地点页使用 Campo Santa Maria Formosa 这一名称并提供官方平面图；这只支持当代名称与地点范围，不据此推定18世纪地界或 Pisani 宫邸身份。本卡与同名教堂、未识别宫邸分开。

**English:** This unit records Campo di S. Maria Formosa as a named square in Venice. Haskell's chapter 10, p. 327, identifies it as the view depicted in Giampiccoli's print and says Pisani's palace stood in the campo, without identifying the building. The Comune di Venezia uses the current name Campo Santa Maria Formosa and provides an official plan; this supports the present-day place name and area only, not an eighteenth-century boundary or Pisani palace identity. This unit is distinct from the church and the unidentified palace.
