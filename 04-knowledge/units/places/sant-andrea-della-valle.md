## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 谷地圣安德烈堂是罗马戴蒂尼会的教堂，自十六世纪末起分阶段营建，先后涉及格里马尔迪、德拉·波尔塔、马代尔诺和赖纳尔迪等人的工作。第一章强调亚历山德罗·佩雷蒂—蒙塔尔托接续前人已经开始的工程。建筑空间与穹顶壁画、祭坛画、雕像和墓碑分别成对象；创始捐赠、续建资助与各小堂委托也须区分。（S1、S4、S6–S12）

**English:** Sant’Andrea della Valle is a Theatine church in Rome, built in stages from the late sixteenth century with work involving Grimaldi, Della Porta, Maderno and Rainaldi. Chapter 1 stresses Alessandro Peretti-Montalto’s continuation of an existing building project. The church as a space is distinct from its dome frescoes, altarpieces, sculptures and funerary monuments; the initial bequest, later construction funding and individual chapel commissions also require separate treatment. (S1, S4, S6–S12)
