## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第10章注2提及的Great Witley地名。注释称原Canons礼拜堂搬迁后完整保留为Great Witley的堂区教堂；所指礼拜堂的具体建筑范围尚未解决，“to this day”仅保留为原书的时间表述，不表示当前状态已核实。

**English:** This card records the place name Great Witley mentioned in Haskell’s chapter 10, note 2. The note says the former Canons chapel survived intact as the parish church of Great Witley after its transfer. The chapel’s precise physical scope remains unresolved, and “to this day” is retained as the source’s wording rather than a present-day status check.
