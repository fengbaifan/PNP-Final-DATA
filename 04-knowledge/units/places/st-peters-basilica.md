## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本章由教皇直接监督装饰的重要教堂，也是 Lanfranco 祭坛画申请和 Ghezzi 圣俸事例的所在地。与 Fabbrica 管理机构区分。

**English:** The chapter presents this major church as subject to direct papal supervision of decoration, and as the setting for Lanfranco's altarpiece request and Ghezzi's benefice. It is distinct from the Fabbrica administration.
