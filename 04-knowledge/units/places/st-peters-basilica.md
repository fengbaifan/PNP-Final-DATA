## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 圣彼得大殿（St Peter's Basilica）是现位于梵蒂冈的宗教建筑，经历多期设计与装饰。第一章讨论教皇直接监督其装饰、兰弗兰科申请祭坛画委托，以及盖齐获得圣俸；建筑与 Fabbrica 营造管理机构分别登记。[S1–3、S6]

**English:** St Peter's Basilica, now in Vatican City, is a religious building shaped by successive campaigns of design and decoration. Chapter 1 discusses direct papal supervision, Lanfranco's request for an altarpiece commission and Ghezzi's benefice. The building is recorded separately from the Fabbrica administration. [S1–3, S6]
