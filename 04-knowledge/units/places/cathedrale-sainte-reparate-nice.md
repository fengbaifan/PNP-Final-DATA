## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 尼斯旧城的圣雷帕拉塔主教座堂；法国文化部遗产目录将圣埃洛伊与圣若翰洗者、圣安德烈的1661年画作定位在该堂Sainte-Rose-de-Lima小堂。教堂官网另记同一小堂一件Saint Éloi画作，署名B. Baldoino、年代1646；两项记录是否同一实物待考。（S1–S3）

**English:** A cathedral in the old town of Nice. The French Ministry of Culture’s heritage record locates the 1661 painting of Saint Eloi between Saint John the Baptist and Saint Andrew in its Sainte-Rose-de-Lima chapel. The cathedral website separately describes a Saint Éloi painting in that chapel as “B. Baldoino, 1646”; whether the records refer to the same object remains unresolved. (S1–S3)
