## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称Manin家族在Passeriano有一处乡间宅邸。本卡用地点和家族描述作工作名称，不把它与威尼斯大运河上的Manin宫殿或Manin家族合并，也不声称已识别其现存建筑身份。

**English:** Haskell identifies a Manin family country house at Passeriano. This descriptive project name keeps it separate from the Manin palace on Venice’s Grand Canal and from the Manin family as an entity; it does not claim an identification of a surviving building.

Haskell称宅邸入口由两座柱廊翼通向，并将其描述为仿自Bernini为St Peter’s所作的porticos。来源没有指明被仿的具体设计、现存构件或对应关系的精确性质；该关系保留为S6待证候选。

Haskell says the approach ran through two colonnaded wings adapted from Bernini’s porticos for St Peter’s. The source does not identify the exact design, any surviving feature, or the precise nature of the adaptation; the relation remains an S6 pending candidate.
