## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应书后索引 M.csv#57 所列 Venice 的 Manin palace。第9章将它称为 Manin 家族位于大运河上的宫殿；页下注释所引1708年通信题名写作 Palazzo di S.E. il Signor Co: Manin in Venezia。原书另述 Passeriano 的 Manin country house，本卡与该乡间宅邸及 Manin 家族实体分开。此处不据原书描述推定宫殿的现代正式名称、现存状态或具体地址。[第9章 p.251](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md)；[书后索引 M.csv#57](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-1-Index-MD/M.md)

**English:** This card identifies the Manin palace in Venice listed under index entry M.csv#57. Chapter 9 calls it the Manin family’s palace on the Grand Canal; the title cited in footnote 3 for a 1708 letter reads Palazzo di S.E. il Signor Co: Manin in Venezia. Haskell separately describes the Manin country house at Passeriano, which is kept distinct from this palace and from the Manin family as an entity. The account does not establish a current formal building name, survival status, or precise address. [第9章 p.251](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md); [书后索引 M.csv#57](../../../02-sources/03-Index/03-1-Index-MD/M.md)
