## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡对应第3章所称 Chapel of the Visitation。该段将此小堂置于 Chiesa Nuova 的局部叙述中，并称一幅祭坛画预定安置于此。中文显示名为工作译名；此地点卡与该祭坛画作品卡分立，不推断确切空间结构或今日状态。 [第3章书内来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L49; print p. 69）

**English:** This card represents the Chapel of the Visitation named in Chapter 3. The local passage places it in the Chiesa Nuova context and says an altar painting was intended for it. The Chinese display name is a working translation. This place unit remains distinct from the altar-painting work and does not infer the chapel's precise spatial layout or present-day status.
