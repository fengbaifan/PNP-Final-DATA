## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔第8章所述位于费拉拉的总主教宫，即 Cardinal Tommaso Ruffo 于当地任职期间使用的宫殿。原书称该建筑于1710年由罗马建筑师 Tommaso Mattei 为 Ruffo 建造、毗邻一座未具名的座堂，Ruffo 的画廊收藏存放于此。脚注所引手稿题名写作 “Palazzo Vescovale di Ferrara”；本卡记录的是哈斯克尔的叙述，未独立查阅该手稿或核实建筑现状。

**English:** This unit records the Archbishop’s Palace in Ferrara described in Haskell’s chapter 8, used by Cardinal Tommaso Ruffo while he served there. Haskell says the palace was built for Ruffo in 1710 by the Roman architect Tommaso Mattei, adjoined an unnamed cathedral, and housed Ruffo’s gallery. The manuscript title cited in a footnote includes “Palazzo Vescovale di Ferrara.” This card records Haskell’s account; the manuscript has not been consulted directly and the building’s present condition has not been verified.

### 名称与身份边界

- “费拉拉总主教宫（Archbishop's Palace of Ferrara）”是据原书称谓形成的描述性名称；“Palazzo Vescovale di Ferrara”见于哈斯克尔转引的手稿题名，不据此声称已核对手稿原件。
- 本建筑与 Ruffo 在罗马、特雷维喷泉对面的住宅是不同地点；也不与费拉拉总主教职位或教区机构合并。

### 本章相关内容

Haskell’s footnote says the account of the palace is based on a manuscript held by the Biblioteca Ariostea in Ferrara and gives a title containing “Palazzo Vescovale di Ferrara.” The manuscript is a cited source, not one consulted for this registration. Haskell also says the palace remained as described at the time of his research; this is not an independently verified statement about its present condition.
