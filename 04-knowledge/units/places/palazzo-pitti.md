## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 佛罗伦萨的宫殿建筑，帕拉蒂纳画廊位于其中，本书多件图版作品以此为收藏地点。

**English:** A palace in Florence housing the Galleria Palatina and named as the collection location of several works illustrated in the book.
