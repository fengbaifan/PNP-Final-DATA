## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡指哈斯克尔第2章所述温琴佐·朱斯蒂尼亚尼在圣路易法国教堂附近的宫邸。该描述性名称是项目暂拟名称；书中此处未给出建筑正式名称。宫邸与教堂、巴萨诺乡间住宅及其中收藏分别识别。

**English:** This unit identifies Vincenzo Giustiniani’s palace near S. Luigi dei Francesi as described in Haskell’s Chapter 2. The descriptive title is a project label; the passage does not supply a formal building name. The palace is distinguished from the church, the country house at Bassano and the collection housed in it.
