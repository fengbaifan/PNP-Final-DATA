## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡登记哈斯克尔注1所称、靠近乌迪内主教座堂的 Cappella della Purità。注释称 1759 年 Daniele Dolfin 召回蒂埃波罗及其子 Gian Domenico 前往乌迪内装饰该堂；召回和装饰目的不证明装饰实际完成。 [[来源1](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/09_CHP-9_intro.md#L360)]

**English:** This card records the Cappella della Purità near Udine Cathedral as named in Haskell’s note 1. The note says that in 1759 Daniele Dolfin recalled Tiepolo and his son Gian Domenico to Udine to decorate the chapel; the recall and stated purpose do not establish that the decoration was completed.

地点身份限于哈斯克尔注释所指礼拜堂；不推定其现存建筑身份、历史沿革或装饰现状。

The place identity is limited to the chapel named in Haskell’s note; no surviving architectural identity, building history, or present decoration is inferred.
