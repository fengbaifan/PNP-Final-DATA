## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell称作Chiesa Nuova穹顶的建筑内部空间。第3章的跨页句将“cupola”与“Chiesa Nuova”连为同一处建筑空间与教堂的组成关系。穹顶与整座教堂、穹顶上的具体壁画及其预备图稿分别建模；本书段落未说明穹顶的尺寸、建造年代或当前状态。

**English:** This unit records the architectural interior space Haskell calls the cupola of Chiesa Nuova. The sentence continuing across pages 76–77 identifies the cupola as a space belonging to that church. The cupola remains distinct from the church as a whole, the frescoes on it, and preparatory cartoons for it. This passage does not give the cupola's dimensions, construction date, or present condition.
