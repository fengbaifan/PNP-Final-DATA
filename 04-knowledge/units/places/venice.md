## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 学习色彩、图书出版与艺术家来源的地点；与罗马家庭画业传承的比较为作者论述。

REV-016 边界复核：本章这些出处使用城市、籍贯和行程含义，保持 place。它们没有独立陈述“威尼斯共和国”的政府或政治行为，故不从城市名自动建立政治实体卡；后续若出现共和国作为行动主体，应另用 institution。

**English:** Venice appears as a place of colour study, publication and artistic origin; its comparison with Roman family workshops is Haskell's argument. These passages concern the city, origins and travel, not independent governmental actions by the Republic of Venice. The city remains a place; a political actor would require a distinct institution entry.
