## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 威尼斯位于意大利东北部威尼托地区的泻湖之中，历史城区以岛屿、运河和桥梁组织空间。它曾是威尼斯共和国的都城，但本卡记录城市地点。第一章将其作为出版、艺术学习、旅行及艺术家来源的地点，并讨论其家庭画业与罗马的差异。（S1–S5、S8、S10）

**English:** Venice is a city in the lagoon of the Veneto region in northeastern Italy, with a historic centre organised around islands, canals and bridges. It was the capital of the Republic of Venice, but this entry describes the city as a place. Chapter 1 uses Venice as a location of publishing, artistic study, travel and artistic origins, and compares its family painting workshops with those of Rome. (S1–S5, S8, S10)
