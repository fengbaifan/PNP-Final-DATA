## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡以描述性名称登记圣彼得大殿后殿左侧的壁龛。Haskell称保罗三世墓被移至此处，并把它与后殿右侧、为Urban VIII墓建造的壁龛区分开来。原文没有给出建筑空间的正式名称、具体移置日期或其当前状态；本卡不将壁龛视为独立艺术作品。

**English:** This unit uses a descriptive name for the niche on the left side of the apse at St Peter’s Basilica. Haskell says the tomb of Paul III was moved there and distinguishes it from the niche built on the right side of the apse for Urban VIII’s tomb. The text provides no formal architectural designation, exact relocation date, or current status; the niche is recorded as a place, not an independent artwork.
