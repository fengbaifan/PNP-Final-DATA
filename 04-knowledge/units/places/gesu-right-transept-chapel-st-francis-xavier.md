## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本书先在 Gaulli 委托范围中提及耶稣堂圣方济各·沙勿略礼拜堂，后说明右横翼礼拜堂先后奉献于救世主、复活和圣方济各·沙勿略。两处指向同一右横翼礼拜堂；本卡登记建筑空间及其奉献沿革，不混同祭坛画或独立绘画。

**English:** Haskell first names the Chapel of St Francis Xavier in the Gesù among the spaces beneath Gaulli’s transept vaults, then describes a right-transept chapel successively dedicated to the Saviour, the Resurrection, and St Francis Xavier. The passages identify the same chapel; this unit records the space and its dedication history separately from altarpieces and paintings.

### 名称

中文显示名及英文显示名见卡片标题；中文名为描述性工作译名，非原书正式题名。

The bilingual display names appear in the card title; the Chinese name is a descriptive working title, not a formal title from the book.

### 地点信息

- 地点性质：耶稣堂右横翼内部礼拜堂。\n- 奉献沿革：原奉献于救世主，后改奉复活；1623年圣方济各·沙勿略列圣后改奉该圣人。\n- 早期陈设：第3章称 Baglione 于1602年为此礼拜堂绘制祭坛画，并称礼拜堂内曾置一幅 Van Dyck 绘制的圣人像；作品各自保持独立对象。\n- 后续 patronage：本书称 Monsignor Negroni 于17世纪60年代后期取得礼拜堂赞助权，并强调他对本礼拜堂装饰的主张。

- Place type: an internal chapel in the right transept of the Gesù.\n- Dedication history: first dedicated to the Saviour, then to the Resurrection, and after St Francis Xavier’s canonization in 1623 to that saint.\n- Earlier furnishings: Haskell says Baglione painted an altarpiece for the chapel in 1602 and that a picture of the saint by Van Dyck was placed there; each work remains a separate object.\n- Later patronage: Haskell says Monsignor Negroni acquired the chapel’s patronage in the late 1660s and asserted authority over its decoration.

### 身份边界

中文：cand-5237 的 Gaulli 合约段与 cand-5300 的奉献沿革段指同一礼拜堂，合为一张地点 KU。礼拜堂不是耶稣堂本体、祭坛、Baglione 或 Van Dyck 作品；不与 S. Andrea al Quirinale 的圣方济各·沙勿略礼拜堂合并。

English: Candidate cand-5237’s Gaulli-commission passage and cand-5300’s dedication history refer to the same chapel and share one place KU. The chapel is distinct from the Gesù building, its altar, and works by Baglione or Van Dyck; it is also distinct from the Chapel of St Francis Xavier at S. Andrea al Quirinale.
