## 内容

### 空间身份

**中文：** 本卡记录原书所称耶稣堂的祭坛席空间。哈斯克尔说 Cardinal Alessandro Farnese 将该空间及 High Altar 的装饰专留给家族；他去世后，装饰工程须得到继承人许可。中文名称是描述性译名，英文原词保留用于身份定位。本卡不据此补充精确建筑边界或装饰现状。[第3章 p.66](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L8–9）

**English:** This unit records the architectural space Haskell calls the Gesù’s tribune. He says Cardinal Alessandro Farnese reserved its decoration, together with that of the High Altar, for his family; after Farnese’s death, work required the heirs’ permission. The Chinese title is descriptive, and the source’s English term is retained for identification. No precise architectural boundary or present decorative state is inferred. [第3章 p.66](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/03_CHP-3_sec_ii.md)（L8–9）

### 对象边界

祭坛席是[罗马耶稣堂](gesu-rome.md)内部空间，不与教堂建筑、High Altar 或其装饰作品合并。正文链接仅作导航；祭坛席与建筑、装饰计划及相关人物之间的正式关系留待 S6 审查。
