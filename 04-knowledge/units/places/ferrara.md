## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 费拉拉（Ferrara）是本书多章直接称作 city 或 town 的城市地点。第2章以其作为1598年接管叙述中的城市，第8章又以其作为相关驻地语境。此卡只登记城市地点身份；政治转移、家族沿革、任职及收藏等陈述仍按原书语境保留为独立S2候选，不在本卡扩写为完整城市史。[第2章 p.25](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_i.md) [第2章 p.48](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_iv.md) [第8章 p.221](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md)

**English:** Ferrara is a city-place that the book directly calls a city or town in multiple chapters. Chapter 2 uses it as the city in an account of the 1598 takeover, while Chapter 8 uses it as a setting for later offices. This card registers only the city-place identity; statements about political transfer, family history, offices, and collections remain separate S2 candidates in their source context and are not expanded into a city history here. [第2章 p.25](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_i.md) [第2章 p.48](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/02_CHP-2_sec_iv.md) [第8章 p.221](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/08_CHP-8_sec_ii.md)
