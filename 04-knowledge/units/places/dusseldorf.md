## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录哈斯克尔注3所列城市Düsseldorf。该注称复数形式的Gaulli图稿位于Berlin与Düsseldorf，但未给馆藏机构、档号、数量或单件图稿与城市的对应关系。本卡只登记城市级地点，不识别收藏机构或解决图稿组身份。

**English:** This card records the city of Düsseldorf named in Haskell’s note 3. The note says plural Gaulli drawings are in Berlin and Düsseldorf but provides no holding institution, catalogue identifier, count, or mapping between an individual drawing and either city. This card registers only the city-level place and does not identify a collection or resolve the drawing-group identity.
