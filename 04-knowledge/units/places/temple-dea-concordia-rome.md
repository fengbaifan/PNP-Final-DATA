## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡表示哈斯克尔提及的德阿·协和神庙地点。卡片保留原书“in Rome”的定位及其作为柱群来源的说法，不据此确认神庙的具体历史身份、遗址或后续迁移。 [第15章来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_i.md)

**English:** This card represents the Temple of the Dea Concordia named by Haskell. It preserves the book's wording that it was in Rome and was the source of the columns, without confirming the temple's specific historical identity, site, or later transfers. [Chapter 15 source](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_i.md)
