## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本书以 Dresden 指称同一城市地点，并将其置于萨克森选侯及波兰国王宫廷、城市建筑与艺术收藏的历史语境中。此卡登记城市本身，不与 Dresden 宫廷、画廊或具体建筑混为一体。

**English:** Haskell uses Dresden for the same city in accounts of the Saxon electoral and Polish royal court, urban architecture, and art collecting. This unit represents the city itself and keeps it distinct from the Dresden court, gallery, and individual buildings.

### 本书语境

第10章 p.277 注5报告 Colloredo 入城画位于 Dresden；p.294 叙述 Augustus II 的宫廷与城市建设；全书其他章节继续以 Dresden 指涉城市及相关目的地。

Chapter 10 p. 277 n. 5 reports the Colloredo entry picture in Dresden. Page 294 discusses Augustus II’s court and the city’s development; other chapters use Dresden for the city and destinations associated with it.

English: “Dresden” also appears as contextual shorthand for the court or collection location in particular statements; the city unit does not absorb the court institution, gallery, or buildings.
