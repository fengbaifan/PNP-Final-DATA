## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡指哈斯克尔第3章中位于 Chiesa Nuova 旁、称为 Oratorio 的建筑。原书称奥拉托里会委托 Borromini 建造该建筑以容纳图书馆和宗教音乐会，并记载 Virgilio Spada 选他为建筑师；书中还说 Borromini 于1637年受请设计并开始该工程。这些是本书的历史叙述，不确认建筑的完工时间、现存状况或当前名称与用途。

**English:** This unit records the building described in Haskell’s chapter 3 as adjoining Chiesa Nuova and referred to as the Oratorio. Haskell says the Oratorians commissioned Borromini to build it for a library and religious-music concerts, and that Virgilio Spada selected him as architect. Haskell also says Borromini was asked to design it and began work in 1637. These are statements in the book; this unit does not establish the building’s completion date, present condition, current name, or use.

### 名称与身份边界

- 原书第3章使用“Oratorio”一词；英文 KU 名称为描述性名称，因为来源没有给出更具体的建筑正式名称。
- 本建筑与 Chiesa Nuova 教堂及 Oratorians 机构分别登记。来源只记其毗邻 Chiesa Nuova，没有提供更精确的位置。

### 本章相关内容

Haskell associates the building with the Oratorians’ library and religious-music concerts. He reports Virgilio Spada’s choice of Borromini as architect, Borromini being asked to design the Oratorio in 1637, and his beginning work that year. The statements remain S2 relation candidates pending individual S6 decisions; the dates are not used here to assert completion.
