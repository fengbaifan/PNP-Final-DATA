## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 马扎然在巴黎营建的宫殿建筑群，包含上下两层收藏画廊，部分建筑后来由图书馆使用。

**English:** A palace complex developed by Mazarin in Paris, including two superposed collection galleries; parts of the buildings later came into library use.
