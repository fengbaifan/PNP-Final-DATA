## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell第2章中作为Giustiniani乡间宅邸所在地出现的Bassano di Sutri。Treccani《意大利百科全书》1930年词条将该地列为维泰博省的Comune；该历史条目用于区分相近地名，不代表当前行政名称或辖区。本卡不把Haskell所称乡间宅邸与词条所述的Giustiniani宫殿合并。

**English:** This unit records Bassano di Sutri as the place where Haskell locates Giustiniani’s country house in chapter 2. A 1930 entry in Treccani’s *Enciclopedia Italiana* identifies it as a comune in the province of Viterbo; this historical entry helps distinguish similarly named places and does not establish a current administrative name or jurisdiction. The country house in Haskell is not identified with the Giustiniani palace described in that entry.
