### 描述

**中文：** 本卡表示哈斯克尔所称别墅花园这一地点对象。原书没有给花园专名、边界、植物种类或单独设计者；“威尼托最受称道的景观之一”保留为哈斯克尔的评价，不当作地理定位事实。 [第15章来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_i.md)

**English:** This card represents the villa gardens described by Haskell as a place associated with the house. The passage supplies no garden name, boundary, plant species, or separate designer. The claim that its rarities were among the Veneto's most celebrated sights remains Haskell's evaluation, not a geographic-location fact.
