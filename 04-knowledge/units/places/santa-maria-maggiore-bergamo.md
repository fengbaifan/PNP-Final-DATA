## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本地点知识元记录贝加莫的 Santa Maria Maggiore 教堂。哈斯克尔称其营建始于1137年，之后历经数世纪断续施工；书中描述其主体结构、门廊、钟楼、祭衣间及内部装饰在不同时期陆续变化。本卡以教堂建筑作为地点实体，不把其内部空间、建筑构件或祭坛画并入本体。

**English:** This place unit records the church of Santa Maria Maggiore in Bergamo. Haskell says work began in 1137 and continued fitfully over several centuries; the book describes changes to its structure, porticoes, campanile, sacristy, and interior decoration at different times. The card denotes the church building as a place, separate from its interior spaces, architectural components, and altarpieces.

### 本书语境

**原书语境：** 第8章 p.215 将教堂置于贝加莫由外地艺术家参与的收藏与赞助讨论之后，先述其自1137年起的建筑变化，再转入1653年以后围绕中部交叉空间展开的装饰工程。该历史过程为地点定位证据，不表示相关作品或空间就是教堂本体。

**Book context:** Chapter 8, p. 215 places the church after a discussion of collecting and patronage involving artists from outside Bergamo. Haskell first outlines building changes from 1137 and then turns to the decoration programme begun after 1653 around the central crossing. This history anchors the place identity; it does not make the works or interior spaces identical with the church building.
