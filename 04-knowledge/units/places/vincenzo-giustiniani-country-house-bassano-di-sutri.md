## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录Haskell第2章中与Vincenzo Giustiniani相关的一处乡间宅邸。书中说该宅邸位于Bassano di Sutri，并称Giustiniani曾帮助设计；原文未给宅邸专名，也未说明其为唯一设计者。本卡不把宅邸等同于该镇另有记载的宫殿，不据此确立所有权或建筑作者身份。

**English:** This unit records a country house mentioned in connection with Vincenzo Giustiniani in Haskell’s chapter 2. The book locates it at Bassano di Sutri and says Giustiniani helped design it; it gives no proper name for the house and does not call him its sole designer. This unit does not identify the house with another palace reported at the town or establish ownership or sole authorship.
