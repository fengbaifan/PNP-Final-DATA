## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡记录 Haskell 用来定位 The Beaneater 的 Colonna gallery。它作为来源限定的画廊空间单独表示；原书未给出具体建筑、房间、城市或所述地点时间，不据同一姓氏将其并入科隆纳家族宫殿。[第5章 p.134](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_iii.md)（L26）

**English:** This card records the Colonna gallery that Haskell names as the location of The Beaneater. It represents the source-bounded gallery space; the book does not identify a specific building, room, city, or date for this location. The shared Colonna name does not merge it with the Colonna family palace. [Chapter 5, p. 134](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/05_CHP-5_sec_iii.md) (L26)
