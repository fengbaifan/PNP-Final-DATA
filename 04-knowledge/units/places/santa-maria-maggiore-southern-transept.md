## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Haskell称马西莫神父受托绘制的《无辜者屠杀》计划置于贝加莫圣母玛利亚大殿南耳堂右墙。本卡记录原文所指的教堂内部空间与计划位置，不把“计划放置”说成已经安装；耳堂、建筑本体和绘画分别登记。

**English:** Haskell says that Padre Massimo’s commissioned Massacre of the Innocents was intended for the right wall of the southern transept of Santa Maria Maggiore in Bergamo. This card records the interior space and planned location; it does not claim that the painting was installed there. The transept, the church building, and the painting are separate records.

### 名称

- 原文空间称谓：the southern transept。
- 贝加莫圣母玛利亚大殿由上下文限定；本卡名称为描述性定位名。
