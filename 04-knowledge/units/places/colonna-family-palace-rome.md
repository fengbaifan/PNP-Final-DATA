## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡指第6章 p.155 所称的科隆纳家族宫殿。Haskell 明确说它位于“卖给 Chigi 的那座宫殿”附近，并将其重建、装饰归于 Filippo Colonna 与其子 Cardinal Girolamo Colonna，时间为本世纪上半叶。原书未给出建筑正式名称，本卡使用描述性工作名。[第6章 p.155](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md)（L102–110）

**English:** This card identifies the Colonna family palace described on p.155 of Chapter 6. Haskell explicitly places it near “the one sold to the Chigi” and attributes its rebuilding and decoration during the first half of the century to Filippo Colonna and his son, Cardinal Girolamo Colonna. The book does not supply a formal building name; this card uses a descriptive working name. [第6章 p.155](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/06_CHP-6_sec_i.md)（L102–110）
