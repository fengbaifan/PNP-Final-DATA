## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** 本卡表示乡间别墅两端被分别描述的小型方形亭体，按建筑空间地点归 place；它们通过两层翼部连接至主楼。原书未提供单独名称或尺寸。 [第15章来源](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_i.md)

**English:** This card represents the small square pavilions described at the two ends of the country house. They are treated as architectural place units and are connected to the main block by the two-storey wings. The passage gives no individual names or dimensions. [Chapter 15 source](../../../02-sources/02-Markdown/15_CHP-15_sec_i.md)
