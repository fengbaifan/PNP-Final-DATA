## 内容

### 描述

**中文：** Domenichino 与 Carracci 等画家的出发地，也是 Ricci/Gozadini/Albani 委托事例所在地；地点相同不自动形成艺术影响。

**English:** The city is a point of departure for painters such as Domenichino and the Carracci and the location of Ricci, Gozadini and Albani commission examples. Shared location does not automatically establish artistic influence.
