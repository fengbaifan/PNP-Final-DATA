De Dominici, IV, pp. 87, 188 and 428, and Bologna, p. 164. Both Luca Giordano and Solimena had a number of admirers in Venice. Besides the Labia, who are discussed separately in Chapter 9, the Procuratore Canale, Flaminio Corner, the Giovanelli and the Widmann all owned pictures by one or other of them—de Dominici, IV, p. 428, and the engravings of Pietro Monaco published in 1763, Nos. 42, 51 and 89. Early in the nineteenth century the French traveller Valéry (I, p. 342) said that the Palazzo Contarini contained ‘quatre des meilleurs tableaux de Luc Giordano, parmi lesquels l’Enée emportant son père Anchise.’ For Neapolitan artists in Rome see Mattia Loret.
